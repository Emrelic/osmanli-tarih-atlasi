Ayudha Hanedanı (Kannauj)

783 – 815 krallik guney-asya Başkent: Kannauj

Indrayudha (783'te hüküm sürüyor) ve Pala Dharmapala'nın tahta oturttuğu Çakrayudha ile Kannauj tahtı; Pratihara II. Nagabhata 815 civarı Çakrayudha'yı yendi.

🗺 Haritada aç

Kronoloji

  • 783 Indrayudha Kannauj'da hüküm sürüyor (Jinasena 783)
  • 815 Nagabhata II Çakrayudha'yı yenip Kannauj'u aldı

Kaynakça

  • R. C. Majumdar (ed.), The History and Culture of the Indian People, Vol. IV: The Age of Imperial Kanauj, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Bombay 1955 (archive.org: ageofimperialkan04bhar, tam metin okundu), «Rise and fall of the Pratihara empire»: «The first two lines of the verse tell us that in that year Indrayudha was ruling in the north»
  • «Dharmapala placed on the throne a new ruler Chakrayudha, whose name-ending shows that he was probably connected with the ruling family.»
  • «Nagabhata defeated Chakrayudha and conquered his kingdom.»
