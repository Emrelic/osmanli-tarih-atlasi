Kus (Napata–Meroe) Krallığı

MÖ 749 – 349 krallik misir-sudan Başkent: Napata → Meroe

DUZELTME: aday f=MO ~780 (Alara) teyit edilemedi; kaynaklar Alara/Kashta ile 2. Kus Krallığı'nın ~MO 750 (Teb ele geciş) civarında olustugunu verir (Napata donemi 730-400). t: Aksum seferi/Meroe'nin dusus ~MS 330-350. Kesinlik yuzyil, guven dusuk. MO ~590 Meroe'ye tasinma.

Kronoloji

  • MÖ 590 Meroe Krallığı (Meroitik evre): başlangıç (Meroe itself was destroyed between 320 and 350 by an expedition that was dispatched by Aeizanes, king of Aksum (Britannica))
  • 349 Meroe Krallığı (Meroitik evre): sona erer (Meroe itself was destroyed between 320 and 350 by an expedition that was dispatched by Aeizanes, king of Aksum (Britannica))
  • MÖ 689 Taharka (yaklasik MO 690-671)
  • MÖ 589 Napata yagmalandi, baskent Meroe'ye tasindi (yaklasik MO 590)
