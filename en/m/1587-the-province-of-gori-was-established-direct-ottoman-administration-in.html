The province of Gori was established — direct Ottoman administration in western Kartli

1587

In 1578 the Georgian princes of the Gori and Muhran districts had submitted to the Ottomans. In 1587 the province of Gori was established in western Kartli, and the region was placed under direct Ottoman administration. The fortress of Gori, lost during the revolt of the Georgian Simon, was retaken in 1601; Ottoman administration ended in 1603 with Shah Abbas's campaign.
