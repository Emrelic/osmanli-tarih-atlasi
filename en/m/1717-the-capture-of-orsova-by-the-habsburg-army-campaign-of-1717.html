The capture of Orsova by the Habsburg army (campaign of 1717)

1717

In the campaign of 1717, General Splényi arrived before Orsova on 27 August and took the town easily; Orsova, which Mercy had been unable to take in 1716, thus passed into Habsburg hands. Because the surrounding Danube-bank cells (including the Đerdap section on the Serbian side) depend on the nearest settlement point, Orsova, they are painted with its owner. The date has been taken as a year only; the exact day is unconfirmed.
