Pencik Law — laying of the foundation of the Janissary Corps

1361

After the conquests in Rumelia, on the advice of Karamanlı Kara Rüstem of the ulama, the pencik system, which provided for setting aside for the state one fifth of the prisoners of war or its equivalent in money, was put into force. Through those chosen from these prisoners being raised with Turkish families and then taken into the army, the nucleus of a permanent infantry force directly attached to the sultan — the Janissary Corps — was formed. The transformation of a frontier beylik based on tribal cavalry into a state with a central army began with this arrangement.
