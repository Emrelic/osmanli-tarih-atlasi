Iltutmish campaigned against Bengal and brought Ghiyath al-Din Khalji, who had declared his independence, to submission

13 January 1225

The Delhi Sultan Iltutmish set out in 622 (1225) on a campaign against Bengal, where Ghiyath al-Din Khalji had declared his independence, and brought him to submission; the following year he conquered Ranthambhor and Mandor.
