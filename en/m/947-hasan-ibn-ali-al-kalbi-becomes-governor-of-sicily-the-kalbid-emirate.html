Hasan ibn Ali al-Kalbi becomes governor of Sicily: the Kalbid Emirate begins

947

The Fatimid caliph al-Mansur-Billah appointed Hasan ibn Ali al-Kalbi governor of Sicily to suppress the Banu Tabari revolt (335/947); this appointment began the Kalbid period, which lasted ninety years as a semi-independent rule.
