Drake anchors in a harbor on the California coast

17 June 1579

Drake, who entered a harbor at about 38°30'N on 17 June 1579, stayed until 23 July; he is believed to have claimed the region in the name of England as 'Nova Albion'. The location of the harbor is disputed.
