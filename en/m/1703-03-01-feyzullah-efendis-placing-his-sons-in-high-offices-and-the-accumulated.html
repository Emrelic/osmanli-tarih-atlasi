Feyzullah Efendi's placing his sons in high offices and the accumulated reaction

1 March 1703

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Feyzullah Efendi, Seyyid, the şeyhülislam obtained from Mustafa II an unprecedented decree that his son Fethullah Efendi would become şeyhülislam after him, and placed his relatives in high offices at a young age. These practices created wide discontent among the ilmiye class, the soldiers and the people of Istanbul; combined with rumours that the capital would be moved to Edirne, they prepared the ground for the Edirne Incident of 1703.
