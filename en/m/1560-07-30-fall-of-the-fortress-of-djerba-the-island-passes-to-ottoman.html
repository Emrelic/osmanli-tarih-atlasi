Fall of the Fortress of Djerba: the island passes to Ottoman administration

30 July 1560

After Piyale Pasha's naval victory of 14 May 1560, the Crusader forces shut themselves in the fortress they had built on the island; the forces of the Beylerbeyi of Tripoli Turgut Pasha besieged it by land. The siege, which lasted two months, ended on 30 July 1560 with the fall of the fortress, and the island was attached to the beylerbeyilik of Tripoli. Between the naval victory and the conquest of the fortress there are seventy-seven days; the island effectively changed hands not on 14 May but on 30 July.
