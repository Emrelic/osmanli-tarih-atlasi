The surrender of the fortress of Kilia to the Russians

24 October 1790

In the campaign of 1790 the fortress of Kilia at the mouth of the Danube was besieged on 4 October (old style) and nine days later surrendered to General Gudovich. The fortress was returned to the Ottomans by the Treaty of Jassy of 1792.
