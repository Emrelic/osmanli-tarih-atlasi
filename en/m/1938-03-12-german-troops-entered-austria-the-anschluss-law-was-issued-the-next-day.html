German troops entered Austria — the Anschluss law was issued the next day

12 March 1938

Schuschnigg's call for a plebiscite led Hitler to intervene; the German army entered Austria on 12 March 1938, and on 13 March the incorporation of Austria into the Reich was enacted (on the map the chains turn on 13 March).
