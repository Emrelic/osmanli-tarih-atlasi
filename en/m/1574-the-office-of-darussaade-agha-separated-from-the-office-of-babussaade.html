The office of Darüssaade agha separated from the office of Bâbüssaâde agha and became an independent post

1574

Habeşî Mehmed Agha, making use of the powers given to him, had the office of Dârüssaâde agha separated from the office of Bâbüssaâde (Enderun) agha and took the title of Dârüssaâde agha. This post, which was the head of the harem agas, carried an authority extending from the supervision of the Haramayn foundations to the palace treasury and other affairs of state. From the seventeenth century on, black eunuchs (karaağalar) prevailed over white eunuchs (akağalar) in the post.
