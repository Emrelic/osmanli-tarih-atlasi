Occupation of Komotini — the loss of Western Thrace

27 May 1920

Western Thrace, which after Mudros first came under the control of the Entente troops, was left to Greece by the decision of the San Remo conference and the Greek army entered Komotini. The region, retaken from Bulgaria in 1913, became the last territory the empire lost in Rumelia. The status of the Turkish population in the region would be regulated three years later at Lausanne by the minority regime. With this occupation the districts of Komotini, Xanthi, Dedeagach and Soufli of Western Thrace passed to Greece.
