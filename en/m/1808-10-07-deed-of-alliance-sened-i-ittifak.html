Deed of Alliance (Sened-i İttifak)

7 October 1808

A document signed between the palace and the powerful provincial notable families, under the leadership of Alemdar Mustafa Pasha, the notable of Rusçuk who had put Mahmud II on the throne; while the notables pledged loyalty to the sultan and military support, the state recognized their existence and rights. Since it was the first example in Ottoman history of the sultan's authority being limited by a document, some historians regard it as the distant beginning of the constitutional tradition; in practice it became ineffective within a few months.
