The dismissal of Koca Yûsuf Pasha and his appointment to the governorship of Trabzon

3 May 1792

Three months after the peace became final, Selim III dismissed Koca Yûsuf Pasha, who had been grand vizier twice since the beginning of the war, on 3 May 1792 and on the same day appointed him to the governorship of Trabzon and the guardianship of Anapa. The cadre that had conducted the war thus left office.
