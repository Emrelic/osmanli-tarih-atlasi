Duke Ferdinand I of Parma died: the duchy passed to French administration

9 October 1802

Ferdinand, who had ceded the duchy to France by the Treaty of Aranjuez (1801), died on 9 October 1802; Parma and Piacenza came under French administration (official annexation 1808).
