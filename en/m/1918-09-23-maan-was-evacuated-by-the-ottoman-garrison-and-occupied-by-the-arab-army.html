Maan was evacuated by the Ottoman garrison and occupied by the Arab Army

23 September 1918

According to Allenby's despatch, the troops of the Ottoman 2nd Army Corps retreating from the Hejaz evacuated Maan on 23 September 1918 and the Arab Army occupied the town; the Arab Army then pursued the rear of the retreating garrison as far as Jerdun.
