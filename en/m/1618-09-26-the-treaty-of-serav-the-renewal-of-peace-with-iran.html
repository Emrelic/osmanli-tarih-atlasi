The Treaty of Serav — The Renewal of Peace with Iran

26 September 1618

The Treaty of Serav, reached with Iran after the defeat at Pul-i Shikasta, largely confirmed the terms of the 1612 Treaty of Nasuh Pasha; the annual amount of silk to be sent by the Safavids was reduced from two hundred loads to one hundred loads. Thus the second phase of the eastern wars that had continued since 1603 came to a close. The peace would not be long-lived; with the loss of Baghdad in 1623, the war would flare up again.
