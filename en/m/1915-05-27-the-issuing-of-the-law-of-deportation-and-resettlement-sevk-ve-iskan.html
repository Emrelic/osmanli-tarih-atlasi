The Issuing of the Law of Deportation and Resettlement (Sevk ve İskân / Tehcir)

27 May 1915

After the Van revolt and the Russian advance, the government issued a temporary law permitting army commanders to transfer to other regions the population judged to be disturbing security behind the front, and the sultan ratified it on 27 May 1915. As a result of the implementation, the greater part of the Armenian population in the eastern and southern vilayets of Anatolia was sent in the direction of Syria and Mosul; during the transfer, very great loss of life occurred through disease, hunger and attacks. The decision has remained the heaviest and still today the most contested question of the last years of the Ottomans.
