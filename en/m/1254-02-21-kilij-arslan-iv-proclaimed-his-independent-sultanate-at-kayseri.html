Kilij Arslan IV proclaimed his independent sultanate at Kayseri

21 February 1254

After the deaths of Jalal al-Din Karatay and Kayqubad II, Kaykaus II and Kilij Arslan IV fell into disagreement; Kilij Arslan proclaimed himself independent sultan at Kayseri in 652 (1254); the next year he was defeated in the battle of Ahmedhisarı.
