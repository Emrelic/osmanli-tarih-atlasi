Failure of the siege of Rhodes

28 July 1480

By order of Mehmed the Conqueror, the fleet of 160 vessels under the command of Mesih Pasha besieged Rhodes in the spring of 885 (1480), and the island remained under blockade for three months. The Knights of Saint John, led by the grand master Pierre d'Aubusson, resisted fiercely despite heavy losses; when the last great assault, made on 20 Jumada al-Awwal 885, was also repulsed, the fleet withdrew on 3 August. After this failure the city's walls were renewed from the foundations and made the strongest fortification in Europe, and the island could be taken only in 1522 by Süleyman the Lawgiver.
