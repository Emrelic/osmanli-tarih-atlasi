Sudan campaign began

20 July 1820

Mehmed Ali's aim was threefold: to recruit soldiers for his army, to reach the gold of Sennar, and to destroy the remnant of the fugitive Mamluks. The force under the command of his son İsmail Pasha descended southward following the Nile.
