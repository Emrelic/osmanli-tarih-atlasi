At the invitation of Rastislav, Cyril and Methodius come to Moravia; the Moravians become Christian

863

As a result of Great Moravia's alliance with Byzantium against Frankish influence, the two Greek monks Cyril and Methodius, sent from Constantinople, came to Moravia in 863 and laid the foundation of the Slavic liturgy.
