The First Battle of İnönü: The First Victory of the Regular Army

10 January 1921

The newly formed regular army stopped the Greek forces advancing from the direction of Bursa at the İnönü positions and compelled them to withdraw. This success, limited in scale in military terms, was very great in its political consequences: the Ankara government was invited to the London Conference, the way was opened to the Treaty of Moscow with Soviet Russia, and the Law of Fundamental Organisation (Teşkilât-ı Esâsiyye Kanunu) was adopted. Confidence in the regular army in domestic public opinion was also consolidated by this victory.
