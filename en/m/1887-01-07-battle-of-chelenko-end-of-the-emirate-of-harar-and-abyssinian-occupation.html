Battle of Chelenko — end of the Emirate of Harar and Abyssinian occupation

7 January 1887

When Dâvûd became imam of Aussa, Harar, which lay in the midst of the lands ruled by the Oromo, again became an independent emirate and maintained this state until the Abyssinian occupation of 1887. Emir Abdullah, proclaiming jihad, met the Abyssinians with 5,000 men at Chelenko (Shelenko) on 7 January 1887; realizing he would be defeated, he fled to the Ogaden region in southern Harar. The battle showed the imbalance between the Abyssinian army, equipped with firearms, and the forces of the emirate, and eliminated one of the last independent Muslim emirates beyond the Red Sea. Harar, Jijiga and Dagahbur were thus joined to Abyssinia.
