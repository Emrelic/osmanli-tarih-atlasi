Chișinău is first mentioned in a decree of the voivode of Moldavia

1420

The name of Chișinău first appears in a decree of 1420 of the Moldavian voivode Alexandru. The city remained for a long time a small Moldavian town; when it passed to Russia together with Bessarabia in 1812, it became the provincial centre.
