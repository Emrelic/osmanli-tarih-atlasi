Šibenik, caught between the Šubić counts and Venice, became attached to Venice

1 March 1322

Šibenik, caught between the Croatian magnates, the Šubić counts of Bribir, and Venice, gave itself to Venice on 1 March 1322 and remained under Venetian rule until 1357.
