The Victory of Turnadağ — The Dulkadir Principality Became an Ottoman Vassal

13 June 1515

Ottoman forces defeated Alâüddevle Bozkurt, the Dulkadir ruler who had taken an equivocal stance during the Chaldiran campaign, at the locality of Ördekli between Göksun and Andırın; Alâüddevle was killed in this battle. The principality was not annexed at this date: after Alâüddevle, Selim the Grim installed Şehsuvaroğlu Ali Bey at the head of the Dulkadir principality, and the realm centred on Maraş and Elbistan continued as a principality subject to the Ottomans. The buffer on the Mamluk frontier thus came under Ottoman control, and the way was opened for the Egyptian campaign two years later; annexation would take place only after the killing of Ali Bey.
