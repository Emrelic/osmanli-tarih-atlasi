France took Ouagadougou: the Mossi Kingdom came under French rule

1 September 1896

French troops under Voulet entered Ouagadougou, the Mossi capital; the Mogho Naba fled and the kingdom was placed under French protection. Other settlements lost on the same date: Tenkodogo.
