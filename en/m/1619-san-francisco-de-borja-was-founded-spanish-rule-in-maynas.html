San Francisco de Borja was founded — Spanish rule in Maynas

1619

Diego Vaca de Vega founded San Francisco de Borja below the Pongo de Manseriche gorge of the Marañón River and thereby bound the Maynas region to Spanish rule. The town became the point of departure of the Jesuit mission network in the upper Amazon that would last for the following century.
