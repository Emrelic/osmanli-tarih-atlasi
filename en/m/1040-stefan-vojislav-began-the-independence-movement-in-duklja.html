Stefan Vojislav began the independence movement in Duklja

1040

Stefan Vojislav's first revolt in 1036 had been suppressed and he had been taken to Constantinople; upon the revolt of Peter Delyan in Macedonia he fled in 1040 and took the lead of a new liberation movement in Duklja. Although Byzantium suppressed Delyan, it could not renew its authority over Duklja, and the region remained independent for a long time.
