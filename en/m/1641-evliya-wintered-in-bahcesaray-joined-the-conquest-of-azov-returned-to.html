Evliya wintered in Bahçesaray, joined the conquest of Azov, returned to Istanbul

1641

After the Azov campaign he wintered in Bahçesaray, the center of the Crimean Khanate. In spring he was present at the capture of Azov and nearly sank in a storm while returning by sea.
