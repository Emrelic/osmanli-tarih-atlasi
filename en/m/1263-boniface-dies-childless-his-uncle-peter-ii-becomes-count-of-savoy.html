Boniface dies childless; his uncle Peter II becomes Count of Savoy

1263

When Boniface, the young son of Amadeus IV, died childless in 1263, Peter II ('the Little Charlemagne') became count, contrary to his father's will and in place of his brother Thomas III, son of Thomas II.
