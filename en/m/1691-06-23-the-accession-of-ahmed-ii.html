The Accession of Ahmed II

23 June 1691

Upon the death of Süleyman II, his forty-nine-year-old brother Ahmed II ascended the throne at Edirne. His reign coincided with the hardest years of the Holy League wars; two months after his accession, the Grand Vizier Fâzıl Mustafa Pasha was martyred at Slankamen. On the other hand, the mâlikâne system put into practice in his period became one of the most important arrangements that changed the long-term structure of Ottoman finance.
