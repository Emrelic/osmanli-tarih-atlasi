The Oghuz of Arslan Yabgu, son of Seljuk Beg, together with the Samanid Isma'il, defeated the Karakhanid Ilig Khan Nasr

3 August 1003

The army under Arslan Yabgu, sent to help the Samanid Isma'il al-Muntasir, who came to Jand in 393 (1003), defeated the Karakhanid commander Subashi Tegin at Kuhak; Ilig Khan Nasr, who had come to Samarkand, was routed in a night attack (Shawwal 393 / August 1003). The following year (Shawwal 394 / August 1004) Ilig Khan was defeated again in the vicinity of Samarkand.
