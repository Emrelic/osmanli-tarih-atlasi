Qutb al-Din Aybak fell from his horse and died while playing chaugan; he was buried at Lahore

4 November 1210

Qutb al-Din Aybak, the founder of the Delhi Sultanate, died after falling from his horse during the game of chaugan, of which he was very fond, and was buried at Lahore (1210). He was succeeded by his son-in-law Iltutmish (1211).
