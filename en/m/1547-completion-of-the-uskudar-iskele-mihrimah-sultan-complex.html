Completion of the Üsküdar İskele (Mihrimah Sultan) Complex

1547

Construction of the complex at Üsküdar İskele Square began in the early 1540s and was completed in 1547. Of the two great mosques Mihrimah Sultan had Mimar Sinan build, it is chronologically the first; the second (Edirnekapı) would be completed about twenty years later.
