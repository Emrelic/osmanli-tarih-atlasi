The Arrival of the Entente Fleet before Istanbul and the De Facto Occupation of the City

13 November 1918

After the Armistice of Mudros, an allied fleet of more than sixty vessels made up of British, French, Italian and Greek ships anchored before Dolmabahçe, and Istanbul came effectively under occupation. After four hundred and sixty-five years the capital had for the first time passed under the control of foreign soldiers. The words 'They will go as they came', said to have been spoken on the same day by Mustafa Kemal Pasha on landing at Haydarpaşa and seeing the scene, would become one of the symbolic sentences of the National Struggle.
