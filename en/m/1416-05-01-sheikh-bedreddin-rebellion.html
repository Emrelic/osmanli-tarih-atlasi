Sheikh Bedreddin rebellion

1 May 1416

The religious and social movement that developed around Sheikh Bedreddin, a great jurist and mystic who had served as military judge (kazasker) during the Interregnum, broke out simultaneously with the uprisings of Börklüce Mustafa at Karaburun and Torlak Kemal at Manisa. The rebellions were suppressed bloodily; Bedreddin was captured in Deliorman and executed at Serres. The event left a deep mark on literature and historiography as the earliest great social and mahdist uprising in Ottoman history.
