Aristonicus was defeated; the province of Asia was established from the lands of Pergamum

128 BC

Aristonicus, who claimed the throne as Eumenes III, was defeated and captured by a Roman army (129); by the settlement of Manius Aquillius the former kingdom of Pergamum became the province of Asia. The province's tax system (publicani) was a great source of revenue for Rome.
