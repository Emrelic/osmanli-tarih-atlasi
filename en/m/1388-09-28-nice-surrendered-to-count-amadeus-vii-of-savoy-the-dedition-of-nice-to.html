Nice surrendered to Count Amadeus VII of Savoy (the 'dédition' of Nice to Savoy)

28 September 1388

During the succession dispute among the Angevin heirs of Provence, Nice and its surroundings (the County of Nice) came under the dominion of Count Amadeus VII of Savoy on 28 September 1388; Nice remained in the Savoy/Sardinian dynasty until 1860 (apart from French occupations).
