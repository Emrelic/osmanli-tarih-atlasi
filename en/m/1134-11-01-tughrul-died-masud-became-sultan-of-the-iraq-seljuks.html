Tughrul died; Mas'ud became sultan of the Iraq Seljuks

1 November 1134

Mas'ud, who had won the throne of rulership in Hamadan, succeeded Tughrul upon his death in Muharram 529 (November 1134); he later defeated and captured Caliph al-Mustarshid-Billah and, by eliminating the emirs in 542 (1147), became a powerful ruler.
