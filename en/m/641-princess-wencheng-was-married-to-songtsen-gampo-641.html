Princess Wencheng was married to Songtsen Gampo (641)

641

After Songtsen Gampo attacked Songzhou and again requested marriage from the Tang, Taizong gave him Princess Wencheng; as Li Daozong escorted the bride, Songtsen came with his army to Baihai and met her at the River Source, and upon returning to his country he had a city built for the princess, made the people give up painting their faces, and wished to send noble children to the Tang school.
