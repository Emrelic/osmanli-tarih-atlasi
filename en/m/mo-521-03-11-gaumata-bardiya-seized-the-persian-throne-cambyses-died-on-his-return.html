Gaumata (Bardiya) seized the Persian throne; Cambyses died on his return from Egypt

11 March 521 BC

According to the Behistun inscription, the priest Gaumata proclaimed himself Bardiya (Smerdis), son of Cyrus, and came to the throne in March 522; Cambyses died in Syria. Darius and six nobles killed Gaumata on 29 September 522.
