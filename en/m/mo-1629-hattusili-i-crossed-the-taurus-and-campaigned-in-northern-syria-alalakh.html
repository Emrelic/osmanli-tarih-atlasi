Hattusili I crossed the Taurus and campaigned in northern Syria; Alalakh was destroyed, the kingdom of Aleppo (Yamhad) was attacked

1629 BC

Hattusili I (c.1650-1620) crossed the Taurus and campaigned in northern Syria; Alalakh was destroyed and the kingdom of Aleppo (Yamhad) was attacked, but Aleppo was not taken. The annexation of Kizzuwatna (Cilicia) did not occur with this campaign: Kizzuwadna later went back and forth between Hatti and Mitanni, and the Hittite annexation is at the start of the New Kingdom.
