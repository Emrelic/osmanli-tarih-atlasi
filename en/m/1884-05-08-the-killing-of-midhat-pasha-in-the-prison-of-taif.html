The Killing of Midhat Pasha in the Prison of Taif

8 May 1884

Midhat Pasha, the architect of the Kanûn-ı Esâsî and the symbolic name of the First Constitutional Era, had been found guilty by the Yıldız Court in connection with Abdülaziz's death and, his death sentence commuted to exile, had been sent to Taif. According to the TDV record, he was strangled to death in his cell on the night of 7-8 May 1884, and the official reason was announced as the 'şîrpençe' disease. His death became the symbol of the liquidation of constitutional opposition in the reign of Abdülhamid II.
