The surrender of Qunfudhah to British ships

18 July 1916

On the bombardment of British warships the Ottoman garrison surrendered; the Idrisi flag was raised over the town, and a few days later an Idrisi garrison was installed. In August, at the protest of Sharif Husayn, Britain forced the Idrisi to withdraw.
