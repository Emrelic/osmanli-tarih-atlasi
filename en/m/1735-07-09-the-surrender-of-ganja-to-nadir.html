The surrender of Ganja to Nadir

9 July 1735

Nadir, who had defeated the Ottoman army at Arpaçay, a few weeks later captured Ganja, surrounded by strong walls and three moats. The city, which the Ottomans had taken in 1725, became Iranian again; Karabakh was left to Iran by the treaty of 1736.
