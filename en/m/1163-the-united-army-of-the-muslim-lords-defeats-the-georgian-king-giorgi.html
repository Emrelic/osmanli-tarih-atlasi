The united army of the Muslim lords defeats the Georgian King Giorgi; Giorgi is forced to return Ani

1163

The great army formed at the initiative of Sultan Tughril and Atabeg Shams al-Din Eldiguz inflicted a heavy defeat in 558/1163 on King Giorgi of Georgia, and Giorgi was forced to return Ani.
