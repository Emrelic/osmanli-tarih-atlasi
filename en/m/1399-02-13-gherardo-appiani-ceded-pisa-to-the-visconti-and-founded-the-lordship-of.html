Gherardo Appiani ceded Pisa to the Visconti and founded the lordship of Piombino

13 February 1399

Gherardo Appiani, lord of Pisa, ceded the city to Gian Galeazzo Visconti, ruler of Milan, for 200,000 florins. He reserved for himself the lordship of Piombino, made up of Piombino, Suvereto, Scarlino and the islands of Elba, Pianosa and Montecristo.
