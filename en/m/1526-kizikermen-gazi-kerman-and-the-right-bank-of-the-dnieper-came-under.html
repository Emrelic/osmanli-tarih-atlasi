Kızıkermen (Gazi Kerman) and the right bank of the Dnieper came under direct Ottoman control

1526

The fortress of Kızıkermen and the right bank of the Dnieper, which the Crimean Khanate had held since the middle of the fifteenth century, came at this date under the direct control of the Ottoman State; the fortress was turned into an Ottoman forward post at the northern end of the steppe. Unlike the territory of the Crimean Khanate around Bahçesaray and Or Kapı, the administration of Kızıkermen lay directly with the Ottomans.
