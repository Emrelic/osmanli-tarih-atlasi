Muhammad, restored to the throne, was killed; Mawdud became Ghaznavid sultan

1041

Muhammad, who had been placed on the throne in Mas'ud's place, was killed; Mas'ud's son Mawdud defeated his uncle Muhammad and his supporters and became head of the Ghaznavid state in the same year (432/1041).
