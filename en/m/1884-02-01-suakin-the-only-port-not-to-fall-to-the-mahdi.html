Suakin — The Only Port Not to Fall to the Mahdi

1 February 1884

From February 1884, while all the regions of eastern Sudan other than Suakin passed to the dominion of Muhammad Ahmad al-Mahdi, the port stood under the military protection of a British force of three battalions under General Graham. This was the beginning of a period in which the de facto and legal situations diverged: although the coast was in fact under British military control, its administrative tie would be taken from Egypt in 1885 and attached directly to the vilayet of Yemen, that is, to the Sublime Porte — that arrangement lasted until it ended in 1888 with France's occupation of Zeila.
