Togan Arslan, Emir of Erzen, attacked Dvin, killed the Shaddadid Manuchihr, and annexed the city to his lands

24 April 1118

Despite the raids of the Artuqid Ilghazi and of Qizil Arslan, Emir of Erzen-Bitlis, on Dvin, the Shaddadids held the city until 1105; Qizil Arslan's successor Togan Arslan attacked Dvin in 512/1118, killed Manuchihr, and annexed the city to his lands.
