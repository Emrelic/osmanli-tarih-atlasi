Ghiyath al-Din Kaykhusraw I took Antalya from Latin rule

5 March 1207

Ghiyath al-Din Kaykhusraw besieged the fortress but could not seize it because help came from Cyprus, and placed it under blockade; at last, with the support of the Greeks who were dissatisfied with Latin rule, he conquered Antalya on 5 March 1207 (603/1207). Antalya became an important port of Seljuk maritime trade.
