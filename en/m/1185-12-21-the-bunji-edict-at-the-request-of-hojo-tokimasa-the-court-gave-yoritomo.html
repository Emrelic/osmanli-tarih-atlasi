The Bunji Edict: at the request of Hojo Tokimasa, the court gave Yoritomo the authority to appoint shugo and jito throughout the country (Bunji 1/11/28)

21 December 1185

After Yoshitsune's opposition to Yoritomo, at the end of December 1185 the court, on Hojo Tokimasa's petition, granted Kamakura the authority to appoint and dismiss shugo (military-police governors) and jito (land/tax stewards) in every province. This is regarded as the moment when the bakufu gained permanent administrative authority in the provinces (Bunji kuni-jito). (Kyushu: a region where shugo/jito were applied.)
