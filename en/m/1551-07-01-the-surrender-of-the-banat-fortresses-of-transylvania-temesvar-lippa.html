The surrender of the Banat fortresses of Transylvania (Temesvár, Lippa, Lugos, Karánsebes) to Ferdinand, King of Hungary

1 July 1551

In 1551 the Habsburg army under General Castaldo entered Transylvania; Queen Isabella and her son were forced to renounce their rights to the Hungarian throne. With Castaldo's approval, in mid-July 1551 Martinuzzi sent Endre Báthory to take over the fortresses of Temesvár, Lippa, Solymos, Karánsebes, Lugos, Becse, Becskerek and Csanád in Ferdinand's name. The southern fortresses of Transylvania, which was under Ottoman protection, thus passed into Habsburg hands; Süleyman the Lawgiver's campaigns of 1551–1552 are the response to this.
