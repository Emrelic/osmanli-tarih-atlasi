Bayram Khwaja gained independence — birth of the Qaraquyunlu Beylik

1351

Bayram Khwaja, regarded as the first bey of the Qaraquyunlu, gained his independence by killing Akchasakal Hüseyin Bey, ruler of the Sutayids. He took Mosul and gave its administration to his brother Birdi Khwaja.
