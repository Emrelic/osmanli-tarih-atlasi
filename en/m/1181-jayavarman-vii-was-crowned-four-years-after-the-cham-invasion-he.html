Jayavarman VII was crowned four years after the Cham invasion; he rebuilt Angkor, surrounding it with walls and a moat (Angkor Thom)

1181

Jayavarman VII, who had defeated the invasion of 1177 and restored the country, was crowned in 1181 and rebuilt the capital with the moat and walls of present-day Angkor Thom. His reign (1181 to about 1220) is the period in which the Khmer state reached its widest extent; the annexation of Champa took place in this reign.
