The death of Mehmed IV at Edirne

6 January 1693

Mehmed the Hunter, who was deposed in 1687, was brought to Edirne during Süleyman II's 1689 Hungarian campaign and also witnessed the reign of his other brother, Ahmed II. He died in this city, which he loved very much. His body was brought to Istanbul and buried in the tomb of his mother, Turhan Sultan, next to the Yeni Cami.
