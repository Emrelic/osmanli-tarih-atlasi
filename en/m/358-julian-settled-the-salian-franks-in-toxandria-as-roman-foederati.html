Julian settled the Salian Franks in Toxandria as Roman foederati

358

In 358 the Caesar Julian defeated the Salian Franks and allowed them to settle on Roman territory in Toxandria (on today's Dutch–Belgian border). It marks the beginning of the settlement, under Roman protection, of the region in which the Frankish kingdom would arise.
