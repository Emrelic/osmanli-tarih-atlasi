The Mongols under Yashmut take Mayyafariqin after a long siege; Mardin resists for eight months

1257

In 1257, on his way to the Syrian campaign, Hülegü charged a unit under Yashmut with taking Mardin and Mayyafariqin; Mayyafariqin fell after a long siege, and fortified Mardin held out for more than eight months.
