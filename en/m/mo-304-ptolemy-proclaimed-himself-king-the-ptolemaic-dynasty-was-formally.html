Ptolemy proclaimed himself king; the Ptolemaic dynasty was formally established

304 BC

After Antigonus and Demetrius took the royal title (306 BCE), Ptolemy also took the royal title; Egypt came to be known as an independent Hellenistic kingdom. The presentation of the dynasty as pharaohs of Egypt also hastened reconciliation with the native priestly class.
