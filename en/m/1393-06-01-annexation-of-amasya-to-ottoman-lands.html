Annexation of Amasya to Ottoman lands

1 June 1393

The emir of Amasya, besieged during Bayezid the Thunderbolt's operation in Central Anatolia, surrendered the city to the Ottomans, and Bayezid entered the city in 1393. The sultan left its administration to his son Mehmed Çelebi; as a princely sancak, Amasya thereafter became one of the most important training grounds of the Ottoman dynasty. This choice was to prove decisive in the later Interregnum, and Çelebi Mehmed was to conduct his struggle to reunify the state from precisely this Amasya base.
