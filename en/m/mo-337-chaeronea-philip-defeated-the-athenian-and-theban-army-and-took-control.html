Chaeronea: Philip defeated the Athenian and Theban army and took control of Greece

337 BC

In August 338 on the plain of Chaeronea the Athenian-Theban army was routed by a cavalry charge commanded by the young Alexander; a Macedonian garrison was placed in Thebes, while Athens was spared with leniency. The victory ended the independence of the Greek cities and opened the way to the League of Corinth.
