The Latin Emperor Henry died at Thessalonica

11 June 1216

The Latin Emperor Henry died suddenly at Thessalonica on 11 June 1216 before reaching the age of forty; in his reign of ten years he had strengthened the Latin Empire. In July of the same year Pope Innocent III also died; the wind of the Fourth Crusade had died down.
