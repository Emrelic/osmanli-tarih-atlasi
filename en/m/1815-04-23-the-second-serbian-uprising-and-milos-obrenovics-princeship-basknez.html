The Second Serbian Uprising and Miloš Obrenović's Princeship (Başknez)

23 April 1815

Against the heavy measures applied after the suppression of the first uprising, a new rebellion broke out under the leadership of Miloš Obrenović; this time the Sublime Porte preferred settlement to force. By the agreement made, Miloš was recognised with the title of 'başknez' (chief knez), and the authority to collect taxes and conduct internal affairs was left to the Serbian knezes. This first nucleus of de facto autonomy became the starting point of the process extending to the firman of 1830 and independence in 1878.
