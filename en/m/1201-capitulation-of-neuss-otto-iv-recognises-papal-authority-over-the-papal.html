Capitulation of Neuss: Otto IV recognises papal authority over the papal territories

1201

By the capitulation of Neuss in 1201, the German emperor Otto IV accepted papal authority over the region extending from Radicofani to the pass of Ceperano (the Exarchate of Ravenna, the Pentapolis, the March of Ancona, the duchy of Spoleto, the lands of Matilda, the county of Bertinoro). For centuries part of these lands remained only a papal aspiration; the actual annexation of places such as Ravenna, Ancona, Bologna and Ferrara dates to the sixteenth century.
