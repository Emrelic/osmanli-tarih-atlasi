The Capture of Tabriz — The Seizure of Azerbaijan

28 July 1725

Tabriz, besieged again after the unsuccessful attempt of a year earlier, surrendered to the Ottoman army after a hard-fought battle that went on street by street. Merend, Maragha and Sovukbulak (Mahabad), attached to the province of Tabriz, also came under Ottoman administration. In the same year Lori, Ganja, Ardabil and Khurramabad were also taken, and the regions that fell to the Ottoman share by the Istanbul Mukāsemenâme were effectively occupied. This extent reached in the east was the farthest frontier since the era of the Treaty of Ferhad Pasha; however, holding it placed a heavy burden on the army and the treasury.
