Ausgleich (Compromise): the Hungarian Diet adopted Law 1867:XII — the Austro-Hungarian dual monarchy

29 May 1867

The reconvened Hungarian Diet adopted Law 1867:XII, regulating common affairs (foreign policy, the army, and the finances of these), by 209 votes to 89. The Habsburg monarchy was transformed into a dual structure consisting of two sovereign halves (the Austrian Empire and the Kingdom of Hungary); Hungary became an equal partner.
