Ali Kuşçu in Istanbul

1472

Ali Kuşçu, the last great representative of the Samarkand observatory, became a professor (müderris) at the Hagia Sophia Madrasa at the invitation of the Conqueror; he founded the mathematical-astronomical tradition in Istanbul.
