British Fraser expedition landed at Alexandria

17 March 1807

In response to the Ottoman rapprochement with France, Britain sent a landing force to Egypt. Mehmed Ali was at that time in Upper Egypt dealing with the Mamluks; he returned swiftly and organized the defence.
