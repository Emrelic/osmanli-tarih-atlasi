The construction of the Sa'dabad Pavilion

1 June 1722

According to the TDV article on 'Sâdâbâd', on the bank of the Kağıthane stream a large entertainment and promenade complex consisting of a pavilion, canals, fountains and kiosks inspired by French palace architecture was built. Sa'dabad, which became the most fashionable entertainment place of the period, was immortalised in the poems of the poet Nedim. The complex was largely destroyed during the Patrona Halil Rebellion of 1730.
