Isma'il ibn Ahmad defeated his elder brother Nasr near Samarkand and took him prisoner, leaving him in his post

16 May 888

In the year 275 (888) Nasr was defeated and taken prisoner near Samarkand; Isma'il treated him well and sent him to Samarkand. With Nasr's death (279/892) sovereignty passed to Isma'il.
