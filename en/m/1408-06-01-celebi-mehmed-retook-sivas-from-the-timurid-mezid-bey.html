Çelebi Mehmed retook Sivas from the Timurid Mezid Bey

1 June 1408

After the Timurid invasion the administration of Sivas had been left to Mezid Bey, who was subject to Timur. Çelebi Mehmed at Amasya sent his commander Bayezid Pasha to take the city, and after fierce resistance Sivas passed for the second time into the hands of the Ottoman dynasty. Mehmed's share thus reached its former frontier in the east, six years after the Battle of Ankara.
