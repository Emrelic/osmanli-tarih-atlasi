Peace of Basel: Spain ceded the east of Hispaniola (Santo Domingo) to France: Santo Domingo

22 July 1795

The peace signed at Basel on 22 July 1795 between Spain and the French Republic ceded the Spanish part of the island of Hispaniola (Santo Domingo, Santiago de los Caballeros, Higüey) to France in return for the lifting of the French occupation in the Pyrenees.
