Britain transferred the Arctic Archipelago to Canada

1 September 1880

By decision of the British Order in Council, all the islands in the north of North America and the rights over them were transferred to the Dominion of Canada. The decision was the last step following the transfer of Rupert's Land completed in 1870, and established on paper the present northern boundaries of Canada. The transfer was made without consulting the Inuit communities living in those lands, and actual administration was not established for decades.
