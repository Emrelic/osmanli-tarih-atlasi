Tabarka taken from the Genoese — end of the Lomellini coral concession

20 June 1741

The island of Tabarka, held by the Genoese Lomellini family, was a Christian settlement where coral was harvested. When Ali Pasha of the Husaynid dynasty learned of the family's attempt to dispose of the island, he sent a force and seized it on 20 June 1741; hundreds of people were taken captive. From this date Tabarka came under the administration of the Regency of Tunis.
