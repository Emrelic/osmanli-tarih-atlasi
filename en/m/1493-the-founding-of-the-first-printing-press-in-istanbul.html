The founding of the first printing press in Istanbul

1493

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Matbaa, the Jewish immigrants from Spain, the brothers David and Samuel ibn Nahmias, founded in Istanbul the first printing press to print in Hebrew letters. This went on record as the first printing venture operating in Ottoman lands; printing in Turkish-Arabic script would become possible much later, in the 18th century.
