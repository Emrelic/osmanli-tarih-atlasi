Kilij Arslan II divides his country among his eleven sons

14 April 1184

In his old age Kilij Arslan II divided the country among his sons; Tokat fell to the share of his eldest son Rukn al-Din Suleyman Shah. The Seljuk realm was divided into eleven parts.
