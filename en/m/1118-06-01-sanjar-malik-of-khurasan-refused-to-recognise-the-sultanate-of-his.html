Sanjar, Malik of Khurasan, refused to recognise the sultanate of his nephew Mahmud and proclaimed his own sultanate

1 June 1118

After the death of Muhammad Tapar (18 April 1118), his son Mahmud was proclaimed sultan by the caliph on 6 May 1118; his uncle Sanjar did not recognise him and proclaimed his own sultanate in June 1118.
