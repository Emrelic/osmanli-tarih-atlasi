Conquest of Durrës

17 August 1501

During the Ottoman-Venetian war of 1499-1503, forces under the command of Mehmed Bey took Durrës (Draç), Venice's last great Albanian port on the Adriatic. After Shkodër (1479) and Vlorë (1417), with this conquest the Albanian coastal strip came entirely under Ottoman control.
