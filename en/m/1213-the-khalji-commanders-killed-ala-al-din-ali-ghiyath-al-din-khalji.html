The Khalji commanders killed Ala al-Din Ali; Ghiyath al-Din Khalji became sultan of Lakhnawti

1213

On the death of Izz al-Din in 1211, Ali took the throne with the title Ala al-Din; when his mental balance was disturbed, the Khalji commanders removed him (1213). Ghiyath al-Din Khalji, the last ruler of the Khaljis of Lakhnawti, had a great embankment built against the floods of the Ganges and Brahmaputra.
