Beginning of construction of the Kırkçeşme waterway line

1554

Mimar Sinan, at Süleyman the Lawgiver's order, examined bringing water to Istanbul from the Belgrade forests and reported that it was feasible; construction of the great water installation began in 1554 and was finished before 1563. The TDV describes this structure as Sinan's "most important work in terms of engineering and also the largest in volume."
