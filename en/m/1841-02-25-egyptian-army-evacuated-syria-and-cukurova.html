Egyptian army evacuated Syria and Çukurova

25 February 1841

The occupation of nine years ended; Ibrahim Pasha's army returned to Egypt by way of Sinai. On the map the broad belt extending from Aleppo to Gaza and from Adana to Crete passes again to direct Ottoman administration. Other settlements lost on the same date: Urfa, Maraş, Tarsus, Antioch, Candia (Crete), Hama, Damascus, Jerusalem, Jaffa, Nablus.
