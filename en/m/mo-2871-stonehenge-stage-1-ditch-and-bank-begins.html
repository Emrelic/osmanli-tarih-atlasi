Stonehenge (Stage 1: ditch and bank): begins

2871 BC

A circular earthen monument 110 m in diameter (bank + ditch, Aubrey Holes); cremation burials. The whole of Stage 1 begins and ends between 3100–2920 / 2965–2755 cal BC; stones are erected in the later stages.
