Tekish died; his son Ala al-Din Muhammad became Khwarazm-Shah (1200-1220)

1200

With the death of Tekish in 1200 his son Ala al-Din Muhammad succeeded him; he entered into a struggle with the Ghurid sultans Shihab al-Din Mu'izz al-Din and Ghiyath al-Din Shams al-Din.
