Christmas Island transferred from Britain to Australia

1 October 1958

Britain's Christmas Island (Transfer to Australia) Order in Council 1958 entered into force on 1 October 1958; sovereignty of the island passed from the British Colony of Singapore to the Commonwealth of Australia. Flying Fish Cove is the island's settlement and port center.
