The Emirate of Tokat is given by the Ilkhanids to Mu'in al-Din Suleyman Parwana after Köse Dağ

1243

The Ilkhanids, who dominated the Anatolian Seljuk state after the Battle of Köse Dağ, gave the Emirate of Tokat to Mu'in al-Din Suleyman Parwana; in 1276 Tokat was under the administration of Nur al-Din Jibril.
