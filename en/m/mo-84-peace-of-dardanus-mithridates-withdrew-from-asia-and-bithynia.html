Peace of Dardanus: Mithridates withdrew from Asia and Bithynia

84 BC

In the Peace of Dardanus made with Sulla, Mithridates agreed to evacuate all his conquests (Asia, Bithynia, Cappadocia, Paphlagonia) and to hand over 70 ships and 2,000-3,000 talents. However, the dominance of Sulla's enemies in Rome led to the Second Mithridatic War.
