Aqquyunlu Uzun Hasan eliminated the Ayyubid branch of Hasankeyf

1462

Hasankeyf and Siirt, one of the last two surviving branches of the Ayyubid dynasty on the Upper Tigris (together with Hama), were annexed in 1462 by the Aqquyunlu ruler Uzun Hasan, ending Ayyubid rule.
