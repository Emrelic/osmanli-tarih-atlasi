Adad-nirari I defeated Hanigalbat (the Mitanni remnant) and made it a vassal

1294 BC

Adad-nirari I, grandson of Aššur-uballit I, defeated the kingdom of Hanigalbat, the remnant of Mitanni struck by the Hittites, and reduced it to vassalage; in the time of the next king, Shalmaneser I, the country was turned into an Assyrian province. Assyria extended as far as the Euphrates.
