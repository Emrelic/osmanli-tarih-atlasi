Exile of Dey Hussein — the effective end of the Regency of Algiers

9 August 1830

In accordance with the terms of surrender, the last dey of Algiers, Hussein, together with his family and treasure, was taken out of the city and sent to Naples. The administration of the Regency, which had been attached to Selim I (the Grim) in 1519 and for three hundred years was the strongest Ottoman naval base in the Mediterranean, thus came to an end.
