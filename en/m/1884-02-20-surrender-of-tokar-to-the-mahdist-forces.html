Surrender of Tokar to the Mahdist forces

20 February 1884

The besieged Tokar garrison entered negotiations with the Mahdist forces on 19 February 1884 and surrendered the city the next day, on 20 February. The news reached the British force at Trinkitat on 24 February; Graham's relief operation had come too late.
