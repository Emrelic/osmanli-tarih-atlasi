Emir Süleyman crossed to Anatolia: Bursa came into his hands

1 March 1404

Emir Süleyman, hearing of Isa Çelebi's defeat, crossed to Anatolia with a strong army together with Çandarlı Ali Pasha and seized Bursa; records of March 1404 show that Karadeniz Ereğli too was under his rule. Çelebi Mehmed could not resist Süleyman, who soon came to dominate both Rumelia and the greater part of Anatolia. On the map, from this date until Çelebi Mehmed's retaking of Bursa (1410) the two shores appear in a single colour, in Emir Süleyman's share.
