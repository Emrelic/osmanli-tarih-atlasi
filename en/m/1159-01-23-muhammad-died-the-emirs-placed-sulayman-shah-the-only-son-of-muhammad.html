Muhammad died; the emirs placed Sulayman Shah, the only son of Muhammad Tapar, on the throne

23 January 1159

After the death of Mas'ud (1152), Malikshah and then his elder brother Muhammad were placed on the throne; when Muhammad died young in 554 (1159), the emirs placed Sulayman Shah on the throne.
