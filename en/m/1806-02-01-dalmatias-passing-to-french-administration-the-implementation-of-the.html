Dalmatia's passing to French administration — the implementation of the Treaty of Pressburg (Bratislava)

1 February 1806

The Treaty of Pressburg (Bratislava/Pressburg) of 26 December 1805 forced Austria to give up Istria, Dalmatia and all the Adriatic islands. France effectively took over the administration in February 1806; Zadar remained the seat of the governor-general (proveditor), and between 1806 and 1810 this office was held by the Venetian Vincenzo Dandolo. Dalmatia was added to the Illyrian Provinces after the 1809 Treaty of Schönbrunn. The first Austrian administration, which had lasted since 1797, thus came to an end.
