Nadir's threat to the Russian envoy Golitsyn: if the Caspian provinces and Derbent were not given up he would come to terms with the Ottomans

31 May 1734

Nadir, who had defeated the Ottoman army before Revan, rejected the Russian offer of aid and announced that if Russia did not at once return the Caspian coastal provinces and Derbent beyond the Treaty of Rasht he would make a separate peace with the Ottomans. St Petersburg, on the eve of a new Ottoman war, backed down so as not to push Iran to the other side.
