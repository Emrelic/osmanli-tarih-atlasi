The Crusaders took Antioch; Bohemond founded the Principality of Antioch

3 June 1098

The Crusaders took Antioch (through the treachery of Fīrūz, a commander of Yaghi-Siyan); Bohemond founded the Principality of Antioch, and Alexandretta also remained within the principality's borders.
