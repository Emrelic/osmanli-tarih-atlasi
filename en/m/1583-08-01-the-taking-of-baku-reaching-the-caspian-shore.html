The taking of Baku — reaching the Caspian shore

1 August 1583

As the last link of the Shirvan operation, the port of Baku on the western shore of the Caspian Sea was taken. The naphtha wells around the city were, even in that period, a known source of revenue, and the port was the point of export for Iranian silk coming via the Caspian. The Ottoman State thus became, for the first and only time in its history, a littoral power on the Caspian Sea.
