The Transcaspian government and army evacuated Ashgabat; the Bolsheviks returned to Ashgabat

15 July 1919

After the departure of British troops in April 1919, the Bolsheviks took Merv in May 1919; the Transcaspian forces withdrew to Ashgabat, and the city was evacuated by the government and army on 15 July 1919. The front was held for a time near Kizil Arvat, then withdrawn to Krasnovodsk.
