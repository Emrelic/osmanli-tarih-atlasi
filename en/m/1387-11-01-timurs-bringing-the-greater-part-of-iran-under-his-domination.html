Timur's bringing the greater part of Iran under his domination

1 November 1387

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Timur, Timur, who from the early 1380s eliminated one by one the post-Ilkhanid local dynasties such as the Sarbadars, the Kartids and the Muzaffarids, effectively brought the greater part of Iran under his domination during the 'three-year campaign' of 1386-1388; the final blow to the Muzaffarid dynasty would be dealt in 1393. The region was thus again bound to a single central authority after a century of fragmentation.
