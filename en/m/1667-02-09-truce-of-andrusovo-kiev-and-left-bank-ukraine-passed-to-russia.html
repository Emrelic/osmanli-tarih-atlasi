Truce of Andrusovo — Kiev and Left-Bank Ukraine passed to Russia

9 February 1667

The Truce of Andrusovo, which ended the Polish-Russian War of 1654-1667, secured a 13.5-year ceasefire; Russia gained Smolensk, the Chernigov-Seversk region, Ukraine on the left bank of the Dnieper, and — initially for two years, but in practice permanently — the city of Kiev. This treaty laid the legal foundation for the division of the Ukrainian lands between Poland and Russia along the Dnieper (and for Kiev's remaining in Russia).
