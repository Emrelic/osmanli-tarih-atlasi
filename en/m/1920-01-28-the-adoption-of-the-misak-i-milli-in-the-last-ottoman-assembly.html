The Adoption of the Misâk-ı Millî in the Last Ottoman Assembly

28 January 1920

The text of the Ahd-i Millî, signed by 121 deputies in the last Ottoman Meclis-i Meb'ûsan, stipulated the indivisible integrity of the lands remaining within the Mudros line and inhabited by a Muslim-Turkish majority, a plebiscite in the Arab vilayets and Western Thrace, the abolition of the capitulations, and full political and financial independence. The text was adopted unanimously on 17 February 1920 and announced to the world. This declaration was as much the political programme of the National Struggle as the map of the borders to be defended at Lausanne; it was the direct reason for the Entente states' officially occupying Istanbul on 16 March.
