The appointment of Cezayirli Gazi Hasan Pasha as grand vizier and commander-in-chief

3 December 1789

After the routs in Wallachia and the loss of fortresses, Cezayirli Gazi Hasan Pasha, the aged commander of the fleet, was appointed grand vizier and commander-in-chief on 3 December 1789. Three separate entries of TDV give this day in the same way. His grand vizierate would last less than four months.
