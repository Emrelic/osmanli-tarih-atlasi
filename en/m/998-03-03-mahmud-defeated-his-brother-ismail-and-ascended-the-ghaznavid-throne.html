Mahmud defeated his brother Isma'il and ascended the Ghaznavid throne

3 March 998

Mahmud, who was at Nishapur, refused to recognize his father's testament and took action; the armies of the two brothers met in Rabi I 388 (March 998), and Mahmud defeated Isma'il and ascended the Ghaznavid throne.
