Postumus founded the separatist Gallic Empire in Gaul

260

Upon Valerian's capture by the Parthians (260), Postumus proclaimed an empire in Gaul with the Rhine armies; Gaul, Britannia, and most of Hispania broke away from Rome until 274. Gallienus was unable to put an end to the secession.
