Özi Returned from the Russian Occupation: Handed Over after the Fortress Was Demolished under the Peace of 1739

12 December 1739

The Russians had taken Özi on 11 July 1737. Under the peace made with Russia in 1739, the fortress was handed back to the Ottomans after its fortifications had been demolished; the Ottomans held Özi until 1788, before the Treaty of Jassy.
