Khubilai crossed the Yangtze and besieged Ezhou (Wuchang) (1259)

27 September 1259

In the campaign against the Song, Khubilai crossed the Huai in the 8th month and reached Huangpi; he then ferried his army across the Yangtze in three columns to the south bank, came before Ezhou in the 9th month and the next day (庚戌) besieged the city; the Song general Lü Wende, coming from Chongqing, entered the city by night and tightened the defense.
