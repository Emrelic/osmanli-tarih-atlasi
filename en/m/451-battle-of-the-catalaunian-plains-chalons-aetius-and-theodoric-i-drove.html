Battle of the Catalaunian Plains (Châlons): Aetius and Theodoric I drove Attila out of Gaul

451

In 451 Attila entered Gaul and besieged Orléans; the coalition of Aetius and the Visigothic king Theodoric I turned him back on the Catalaunian Plains (Theodoric being killed). The Huns' advance in Western Europe thus came to a halt.
