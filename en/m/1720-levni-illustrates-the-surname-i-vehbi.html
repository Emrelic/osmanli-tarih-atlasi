Levni illustrates the Surname-i Vehbi

1720

The palace painter Levni illustrated with 137 miniatures the Surname-i Vehbi, which recounts the circumcision festivities of Ahmed III's princes in 1720. By not confining the festivities, unlike earlier examples, to the Hippodrome alone but depicting them in different corners of the city, he began a new approach in Ottoman miniature art.
