Mithridates invaded the province of Asia; the Ephesian Vespers (massacre of 80,000 Romans)

87 BC

At the beginning of the First Mithridatic War (89/88), Mithridates invaded Bithynia and the province of Asia; on the order he gave at Ephesus, Roman citizens were massacred in large numbers. Athens also went over to the Pontic side (Aristion).
