Nadir Shah's Iraq campaign — the last Ottoman-Iranian war began, Kirkuk fell

25 February 1743

Nadir Shah, who had stayed eighteen months in Dagestan and Georgia, his demand that Jaʿfarism be recognised as the fifth school having been rejected by the Ottomans, began a new campaign against the Ottomans in 1743 and entered Kirkuk with a large army. He could not repeat the same success at Mosul; he was forced to lift the siege. This war is the last Ottoman-Iranian war, which ended with the 1746 Treaty of Kerden.
