The Council of Lithuania (Taryba) signed the declaration of independence in Vilnius

16 February 1918

In Lithuania, under German occupation since 1915, the Council of Lithuania, working under German military control, signed the declaration of independence in Vilnius. Germany had encouraged this step during the Brest-Litovsk negotiations; real sovereignty did not come until the German withdrawal in November 1918. Settlements joining the new state on the same date: Kaunas, Šiauliai.
