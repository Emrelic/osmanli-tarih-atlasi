Baltacı Mehmed Pasha and the story of Tsarina Catherine

21 July 1711

The popular account that attributes the salvation of the Russian army besieged on the Prut to the gifts/bribe Catherine I sent to Baltacı Mehmed Pasha has been repeated for centuries.
