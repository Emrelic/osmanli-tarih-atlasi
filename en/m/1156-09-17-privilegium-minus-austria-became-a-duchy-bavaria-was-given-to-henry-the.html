Privilegium Minus — Austria became a duchy, Bavaria was given to Henry the Lion

17 September 1156

By the judgment of the princes in Regensburg, Austria was raised to a duchy and special rights were granted to Henry Jasomirgott; in the same settlement Henry returned Bavaria to the emperor, and the emperor in turn gave Bavaria to Henry the Lion as a fief.
