The Sicilian Expedition ended in disaster: the Athenian army was destroyed before Syracuse

412 BC

The Sicilian Expedition that Athens had launched in 415 BC, after Syracuse could not be besieged, ended in the retreat of Nicias and Demosthenes with a surrender on the bank of the Assinarus river (autumn 413 BC). Athens lost its army and most of its fleet.
