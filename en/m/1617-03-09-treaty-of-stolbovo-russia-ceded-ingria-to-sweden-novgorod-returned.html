Treaty of Stolbovo — Russia ceded Ingria to Sweden, Novgorod returned

9 March 1617

Russia ceded the Neva shore and part of Karelia (Yam, Koporye, Ivangorod, Oreshek) to Sweden and was cut off from the Baltic coast; Sweden returned Novgorod. This situation lasted until Peter I's Great Northern War.
