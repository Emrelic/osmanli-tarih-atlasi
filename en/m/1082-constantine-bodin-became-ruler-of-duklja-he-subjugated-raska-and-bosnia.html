Constantine Bodin became ruler of Duklja; he subjugated Raška and Bosnia

1082

Constantine Bodin, son of Michael (c. 1050-82, who had received the title of king from Pope Gregory VII and raised the bishopric of Bar to an archbishopric), ascended the throne around 1082 and conquered Raška and Bosnia and placed his own candidates there. Thus Duklja embraced almost all the lands inhabited by the Serbs; the state quickly dissolved through dynastic quarrels.
