Dahir, Raja of Sind, died facing Muhammad b. al-Qasim; Rur (Aror), Brahmanabad and Multan were conquered

20 June 712

Dahir, the ruler of Sind who fought the Umayyad commander Muhammad b. al-Qasim, died on 10 Ramadan 93 / 20 June 712; after this battle Rur (Aror), Brahmanabad and Multan were conquered and all of Sind fell into Umayyad hands.
