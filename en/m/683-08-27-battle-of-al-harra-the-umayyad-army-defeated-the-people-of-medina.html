Battle of al-Ḥarra: the Umayyad army defeated the people of Medina

27 August 683

Muslim had set up camp at al-Ḥarra, northeast of Medina; in the battle fought between the people of Medina and the Umayyad forces on 27 Dhū'l-Ḥijja 63 (27 August 683), the people of Medina were defeated.
