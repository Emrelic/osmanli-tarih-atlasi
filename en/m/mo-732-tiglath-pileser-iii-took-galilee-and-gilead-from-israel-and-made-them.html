Tiglath-Pileser III took Galilee and Gilead from Israel and made them Assyrian provinces

732 BC

When Pekah's Israel took a stand against Assyria together with Damascus, Tiglath-Pileser III turned northern Israel (Galilee, Gilead, the Megiddo region) into Assyrian provinces and carried out deportations; Hoshea replaced Pekah. The Kingdom of Israel was reduced to Samaria and the surrounding hill country.
