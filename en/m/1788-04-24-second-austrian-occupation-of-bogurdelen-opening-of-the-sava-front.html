Second Austrian occupation of Böğürdelen: opening of the Sava front

24 April 1788

After Austria's entry into the war alongside Russia on 9 February 1788, the Sava line became a front again and Böğürdelen passed to Austrian rule on 24 April 1788. The fortress had earlier been ceded to Austria without a fight on 17 August 1717 and held until the Treaty of Belgrade (1739); at that time its population had declined so much that only thirty-four Christian houses remained in the town and no Muslims. In the Ottoman period of 1739-1788 the population had risen again to 1,500-2,000. The second occupation would end with the Treaty of Sistova (4 August 1791) and the fortress would be returned to the Ottomans.
