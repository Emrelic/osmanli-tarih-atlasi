Leonhard, the last Count of Gorizia, died: Lienz and the Puster valley passed to Habsburg Austria (Tyrol)

12 April 1500

Leonhard, the last representative of the counts of Görz (Gorizia), died at Lienz on 12 April 1500. In accordance with a succession agreement made earlier, the forecounty comprising Lienz and the Puster valley passed to Maximilian I and was annexed to Tyrol.
