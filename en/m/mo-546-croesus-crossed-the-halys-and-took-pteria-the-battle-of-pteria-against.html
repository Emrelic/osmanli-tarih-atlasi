Croesus crossed the Halys and took Pteria; the Battle of Pteria against Cyrus ended indecisively

546 BC

Trusting the Delphic oracle, Croesus crossed the Halys, besieged Pteria and expelled the Cappadocian Syrians. When the bloody battle with Cyrus proved inconclusive, Croesus returned to Sardis and gathered allied armies; Cyrus, by a sudden march, defeated him at Thymbra and Sardis fell.
