Kilij Arslan II rebuilds Aksaray and makes it a military base under the name Dar al-Zafar

14 September 1170

Kilij Arslan II made the city of Aksaray, which he rebuilt in 566 (1170-71) and named Dar al-Zafar, a military base; he had mosques, zawiyas, madrasas and a caravanserai built, and settled there the ghazis and scholars he had brought from Azerbaijan.
