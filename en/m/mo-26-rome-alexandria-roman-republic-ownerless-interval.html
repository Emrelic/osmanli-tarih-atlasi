Rome, Alexandria: Roman Republic → ownerless interval

26 BC

At the points Rome and Alexandria, ownership passes from the Roman Republic to the ownerless interval. Transition source information: death of Theodosius 17 January 395 (consulship of Olybrius and Probus): Socrates Scholasticus, Eccl. Hist. 5.26 (NPNF, newadvent.org/fathers/26015.htm); Marcellinus Comes s.a. 395: Arcadius and Honorius take over the empire divided only by their places of residence.
