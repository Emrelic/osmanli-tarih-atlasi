Sanjar marched from Balkh to Khwarazm and defeated the army of Atsiz, who had declared independence

1138

Atsiz declared his independence against Sanjar, who was disturbed by Atsiz's expansionist policy and by his shedding the blood of Muslims in the places he had seized; Sanjar marched from Balkh to Khwarazm and inflicted a heavy defeat on his army (1138).
