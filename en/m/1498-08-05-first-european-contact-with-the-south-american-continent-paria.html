First European contact with the South American continent (Paria)

5 August 1498

Columbus, noticing the freshwater current of the Orinoco, sensed that the 'island' he was on at the coast of Paria was in fact a large landmass; he obtained pearls from the natives. The date is within the range 5–11 August.
