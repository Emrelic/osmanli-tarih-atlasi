Li Cunxu was killed at Luoyang by rebellious soldiers

15 May 926

During the military mutiny at Yedu (Weizhou), the garrison commander Guo Congqian attacked the Xingjiao Gate in the capital Luoyang with his unit; Li Cunxu, defending, was struck by an arrow and died at noon in the corridor of the Jiangxiao Hall.
