The Sultanate of Demak's overthrow of Majapahit and its domination of the Javanese coast

1527

According to the TDV article on Demak, the Sultanate of Demak, the first independent Muslim state of Java, was founded by Raden Patah in the last quarter of the fifteenth century; according to Javanese sources, the declining Hindu-Buddhist Kingdom of Majapahit was brought to an end by Demak's attacks. In 1527 Demak conquered Tuban, which Chinese sources mention as an important port for the Majapahit rulers since the eleventh century — this is the year in which the last remnants of Majapahit were also attached to Demak, and it is a turning point in the process of the Islamization of most of Java.
