Alexander reached Persepolis; the city and treasury fell into Macedonian hands

329 BC

Alexander, who passed the Persian Gates after Susa, reached Persepolis at the end of January 330; the city surrendered without resistance. Achaemenid rule at Persepolis ends this year (the palace was burned in May 330).
