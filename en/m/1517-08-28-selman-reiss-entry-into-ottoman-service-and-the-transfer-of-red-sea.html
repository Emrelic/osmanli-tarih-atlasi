Selman Reis's entry into Ottoman service and the transfer of Red Sea policy

28 August 1517

Selman Reis, who had defended Jeddah against the Portuguese, came to Cairo and appeared before Selim the Grim; although he was detained for a time, he was summoned to Istanbul in 1519 and undertook to prepare thirty galleys and several galliots against the Portuguese threat. This meeting is the beginning of the inheritance of Red Sea and Indian Ocean policy from the Mamluks and its attachment directly to Istanbul. The Ottomans were now a power that, besides being a Mediterranean state, also conducted oceanic policy.
