Lesbos and Ainos passed to the administration of the Gattilusio family

1355

The Byzantine Emperor John V Palaiologos gave Lesbos to his Genoese brother-in-law Francesco Gattilusio; Ainos also came under the administration of the Gattelusi family in the same years.
