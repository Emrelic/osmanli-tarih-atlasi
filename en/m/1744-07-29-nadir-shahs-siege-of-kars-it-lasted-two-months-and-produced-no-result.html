Nadir Shah's siege of Kars — it lasted two months and produced no result

29 July 1744

Sāfī Mirza, a Safavid prince who had taken refuge with the Ottomans, was claiming rights to the Iranian throne; according to the plan, the seraskier of Kars would take him by way of Tabriz to Isfahan and place him on the throne, and the Ottomans had opened three fronts against Iran. Thereupon Nadir Shah marched on Kars and besieged the fortress. He obtained no result from the siege, which lasted two months.
