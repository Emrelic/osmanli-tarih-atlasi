The grand vizierate of Koca Ragıp Mehmed Pasha

28 February 1757

Ragıp Pasha, known as a poet and diplomat, was brought to the office of grand vizier by Osman III (he continued in office upon the death of Osman III in October 1757, taking a new seal from Mustafa III). While the Seven Years' War continued, he followed a balanced foreign policy that kept the Ottoman State out of the conflicts in Europe. During his grand vizierate, which would last six years, he also gave importance to cultural activities such as founding libraries.
