Kim Bang-gyeong and Hindu destroyed the Sambyeolcho on Jeju (Tamna); the rebellion came to an end

16 May 1273

Kim Bang-gyeong, who landed on Jeju in the spring of 1273 with 160 ships and more than ten thousand soldiers, together with Hindu and Hong Da-gu, killed six leaders including Kim Won-yun and separated 1,300 surrendered rebels onto ships; the native people of Jeju were left in place. The Sambyeolcho rebellion thus came to an end.
