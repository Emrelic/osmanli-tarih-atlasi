The Kingdom of Georgia divided into three — Kartli, Kakheti and Imereti with its center at Kutaisi

1490

The united Kingdom of Georgia, ruled by the Bagratid dynasty, was formally divided into three kingdoms after a long internal struggle: Kartli, centered on Tiflis, in the east; Kakheti in the northeast; and Imereti, with its center at Kutaisi, in the west. The TDV article on Georgia confirms the division — 'Georgia was divided into three kingdoms (Kartli, Kakheti, Imereti) and five principalities'. The division was decisive in the Ottoman-Safavid rivalry of the following century: according to the 1555 Treaty of Amasya, Imereti, Dadiani, Guria and Daveli were left to the Ottomans; Kartli, Kakheti and Mosuk to the Safavids. That is, the fact that on the map western and eastern Georgia appear attached to different masters arises from this division. Selim the Grim, while still governor of Trabzon, had in 1508 subjected the Kingdom of Imereti (Açıkbaş) to obedience and tribute.
