The Sekban-ı Cedid army was established

15 August 1808

Alemdar Mustafa Pasha had a new army equipped in the European manner established in place of the abolished Nizam-ı Cedid. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Sekbân-ı Cedîd, this force, made up of three regiments, was regarded by the Janissaries as a direct threat and was eliminated in a Janissary uprising only a few months after its founding.
