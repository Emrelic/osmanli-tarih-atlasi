The Georgian King David marches on Erzurum; he turns back in the face of popular resistance

1184

The Georgian King David marched on Erzurum, defeated Nasir al-Din Muhammad and caused him to withdraw into the city; faced with the courage and determination of the Turkish population, he plundered the surroundings and withdrew (580/1184-85).
