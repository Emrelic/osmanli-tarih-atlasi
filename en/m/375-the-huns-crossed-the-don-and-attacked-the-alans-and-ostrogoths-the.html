The Huns crossed the Don and attacked the Alans and Ostrogoths: the beginning of the Great Migration

375

According to Ammianus, in 375 the Huns crossed the Don and defeated the Alans, then destroyed the kingdom of the Ostrogothic king Ermanaric; the Visigothic (Tervingi) people then pressed up against the Danube. The age of Hunnic domination in Europe thus began.
