Abu'l-Hasan Ali Arslan Khan died; the Karakhanids continued in two branches, the Ali and Hasan lines

3 January 998

Abu'l-Hasan Ali died at the end of Muharram 388 (January 998); his son Toghan Khan Abu Nasr Ahmad became great khagan. From this date the Karakhanids lived on in two branches: the Ali line (Ilig Khan Nasr, Toghan Khan Ahmad, Arslan Khan Mansur, Muhammad) and the Hasan line (Harun Bughra Khan).
