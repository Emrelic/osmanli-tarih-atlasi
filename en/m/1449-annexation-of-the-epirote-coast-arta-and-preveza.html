Annexation of the Epirote coast: Arta and Preveza

1449

Nineteen years after the surrender of Ioannina in 1430, Arta and Preveza, on the coastal part of the Despotate of Epirus, also came under Ottoman administration. The Ambracian Gulf opening onto the Ionian Sea was thus brought under control, and Ioannina's sea connection was completed. Preveza is the key point of the bay which, ninety years later, was to give its name to Barbarossa's celebrated victory.
