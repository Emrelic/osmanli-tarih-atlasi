New Hebrides became independent: the Republic of Vanuatu was founded

30 July 1980

The New Hebrides Condominium, under the joint sovereignty of France and the United Kingdom, became independent on 30 July 1980 under the name Vanuatu. The capital Port Vila on the island of Efate and the settlement of Teouma passed from condominium administration to Vanuatu.
