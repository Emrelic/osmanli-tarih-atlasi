The Kipchaks, together with the rebellious Pechenegs, besieged Adrianople

1078

In 1078 the Kipchaks, together with the Pechenegs who had rebelled against Byzantium, laid siege to Adrianople; they subsequently raided Byzantium in 1083-1096 and 1109-1114.
