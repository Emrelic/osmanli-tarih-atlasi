Battle of Legnano: the Lombard League defeats Frederick I

29 May 1176

After Frederick launched his fifth Italian expedition in 1174, the Lombard League was formed against him and the fortress of Alessandria was raised. When Henry the Lion, duke of Saxony, failed to send help, Frederick was defeated at Legnano on 29 May 1176; he was thought to have been wounded and killed. He withdrew to Pavia and began peace negotiations with Pope Alexander III.
