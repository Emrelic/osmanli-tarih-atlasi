Kara Arslan, Artuqid ruler of Mardin, accepts Ilkhanid rule

1260

Mardin suffered famine and plague during the Mongol siege of more than eight months; Kara Arslan, who came to the head on the death of Najm al-Din Ghazi, recognised Mongol rule in return for the restoration to the Artuqids of Dara, Khabur, Nusaybin and Ras al-Ayn, and was confirmed by Hülegü as ruler of Mardin in 659/1261.
