Poland's advance of the Dnieper line — the founding of Kremenchuk

1571

Poland-Lithuania founded a new frontier town at the central Dnieper crossing, the passage point of Crimean raids; twenty-five years later a fortress was added there. Thus Poland's line of control descended to the northern bank of the Zaporozhian steppe, and for the first time this crossing of the river was tied to the continuous garrison of a state. In the following century the town would pass into the Hetmanate order as a Cossack regimental centre.
