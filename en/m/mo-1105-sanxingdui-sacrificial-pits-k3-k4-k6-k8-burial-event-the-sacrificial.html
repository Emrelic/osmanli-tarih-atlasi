Sanxingdui sacrificial pits (K3, K4, K6, K8) — burial event: the sacrificial pits (K3/K4/K6/K8) are buried (AMS)

1105 BC

Pits no. 3, 4, 6 and 8 (the newly opened ones) were buried between 1201 and 1012 BCE with 95.4% probability; end of the Shang. Joining fragments show that pits 1, 2, 3, 4, 7 and 8 were buried at the same time.
