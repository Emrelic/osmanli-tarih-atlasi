Battle of Călugăreni: Koca Sinan Pasha's Wallachian campaign

23 August 1595

With the loss of all three voivodeships, the grand vizier Koca Sinan Pasha crossed the Danube with a great army and marched to bring Wallachia back to obedience. Since Michael the Brave knew he could not resist in open battle, he awaited the army at Călugăreni, at the marshy crossing of the Neajlov, and struck by surprise the forces squeezed into the narrow terrain. After the attack, unable to stand against the numerically superior Ottoman army, he withdrew north into the mountains; Sinan Pasha occupied Bucharest and Târgoviște and tried to bring Wallachia under direct administration. Călugăreni was therefore for the Ottomans not a defeat but an ambush that delayed the completion of the campaign; but the fact that Michael had been able to preserve his army made it possible for him to launch a counteroffensive a few weeks later with help coming from Transylvania.
