Peace of Prague: Austria kept its lands other than Venetia, Prussia took Schleswig-Holstein

23 August 1866

The Treaty of Prague of 23 August 1866 ended the Seven Weeks' War; Austria suffered no occupation and its border did not change (Venetia was ceded to Italy by the Treaty of Vienna of 3 October 1866; see the separate entry).
