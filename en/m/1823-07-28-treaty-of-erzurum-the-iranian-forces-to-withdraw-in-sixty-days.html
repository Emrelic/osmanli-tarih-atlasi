Treaty of Erzurum — the Iranian forces to withdraw in sixty days

28 July 1823

The war, which lasted two years, ended with the peace treaty signed at Erzurum. Under the treaty the Iranian forces were to withdraw within sixty days from the lands they had occupied. The treaty drew no new frontier, restoring the pre-war situation; the frontier disputes between the two states would turn again into conflict in 1833-1842 and be settled only in 1847 by a second treaty, again signed at Erzurum. The closing of the eastern front was the precondition for the empire's being able to give all its weight to the Greek revolt.
