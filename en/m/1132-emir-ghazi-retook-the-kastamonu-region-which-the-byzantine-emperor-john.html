Emir Ghazi retook the Kastamonu region, which the Byzantine Emperor John Komnenos had occupied

1132

Taking advantage of the absence of Emir Ghazi on the Çukurova campaign, Emperor John Komnenos occupied the Kastamonu region; Emir Ghazi retook it in 1132.
