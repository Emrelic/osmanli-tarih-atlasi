Ala al-Din Kayqubad takes Erzincan and abolishes the Erzincan branch of the Mengujekids

1228

Kayqubad, who moved against the Mengujekids, who wanted independence, added Erzincan to the Anatolian Seljuk lands in 625/1228; Malik Muzaffar al-Din, lord of Şebinkarahisar of the dynasty, was given Kırşehir as iqta' and, together with some places, Efsus as mulk.
