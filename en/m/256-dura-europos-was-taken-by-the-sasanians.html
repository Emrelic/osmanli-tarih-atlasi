Dura-Europos was taken by the Sasanians

256

The Roman frontier garrison of Dura-Europos on the bank of the Euphrates fell after a long siege and was abandoned; the Sasanian siege tunnels and Roman countermines are archaeologically documented. The city was never resettled.
