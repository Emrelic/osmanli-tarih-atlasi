Tumanbay's raid on Cairo and three days of street fighting

27 January 1517

Tumanbay, who had withdrawn to Upper Egypt, suddenly entered Cairo on the night of 27-28 January with about seven thousand men, began resistance inside the city and left the Ottoman troops in an extremely difficult position. In fierce street clashes lasting three days, the Mamluks temporarily held most of the city. The resistance could be broken only when the Ottoman artillery and reinforcements came into play.
