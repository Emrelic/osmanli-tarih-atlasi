A new wave of plague began in Istanbul and its surroundings

1 June 1552

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Tâun, one of the 16th-century epidemics affecting the Islamic world occurred between 1552 and 1566 and was especially effective in Istanbul, Anatolia, Rumelia, Egypt and North Africa. Expanding trade routes and increasing population mobility are counted among the factors that facilitated the spread of epidemic diseases in the empire's most powerful period.
