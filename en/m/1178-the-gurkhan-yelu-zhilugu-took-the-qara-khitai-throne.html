The Gurkhan Yelu Zhilugu took the Qara Khitai throne

1178

After the Qara Khitai ruler Zhentian (1164-1177), Yelu Zhilugu took the throne as gurkhan (1178-1211); from 1204 he played an active role in the struggle between the Khwarazmshahs and the Ghurids, and in 1211 he was overthrown by his son-in-law, the Naiman Kuchlug.
