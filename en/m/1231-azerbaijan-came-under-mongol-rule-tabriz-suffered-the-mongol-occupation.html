Azerbaijan came under Mongol rule — Tabriz suffered the Mongol occupation

1231

With the collapse of Jalāl al-Dīn Khwarazmshah, Tabriz suffered the Mongol occupation in 1231; in the same year Mongol rule covered Northern and Southern Azerbaijan (this information concerns the region as a whole, it is not specific to the city). In Shirvan (Baku, Shamakhi) the Shirvanshahs continued to exist as a local dynasty under Mongol sovereignty; on the map these two places are shown in the Shirvanshah colour (there was Mongol/Ilkhanid vassalage).
