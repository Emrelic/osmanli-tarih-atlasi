Attila entered Italy; Aquileia was destroyed

452

In 452 Attila entered northern Italy from beyond the Alps, razed Aquileia, and plundered the Po plain; he withdrew because of disease and the Eastern Roman counterattack. The refugees of Aquileia formed the nucleus of Venice in the lagoons.
