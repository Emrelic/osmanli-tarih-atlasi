Jalal al-Din was defeated at Yassıçimen by the Anatolian Seljuk Sultan Ala al-Din Kayqubad I

1230

Jalal al-Din, who was struggling with the Caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah, the Georgians and the Ismailis, fell out with Ala al-Din Kayqubad I and lost the Battle of Yassıçimen in 1230; he took refuge in the vicinity of Amid.
