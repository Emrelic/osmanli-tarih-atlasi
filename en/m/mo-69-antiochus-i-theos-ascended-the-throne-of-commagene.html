Antiochus I Theos ascended the throne of Commagene

69 BC

Antiochus I, king of Commagene, had the tomb-sanctuary on Mount Nemrut built, uniting Persian and Macedonian ancestry. The kingdom was a buffer between Rome and Parthia.
