The Conquest of Esztergom and Székesfehérvár

10 August 1543

In the Hungarian campaign that Süleyman the Lawgiver led in person, after a siege of two and a half weeks, Esztergom, the religious centre of the Hungarian Kingdom and an archiepiscopal city, came under Ottoman rule. Then Székesfehérvár, the coronation city of the Hungarian kings, was also taken, and the defensive belt of the province of Buda was completed. Esztergom would remain in Ottoman hands until 1595 as the key fortress of the Danube line.
