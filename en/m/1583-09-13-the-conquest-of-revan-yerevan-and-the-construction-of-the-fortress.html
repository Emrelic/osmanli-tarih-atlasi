The conquest of Revan (Yerevan) and the construction of the fortress

13 September 1583

Ferhad Pasha, who at Kars received the order to go to Revan and build a fortress there, marched on Revan, and the city was taken under Ottoman control in the early days of Ramadan 991 (mid-September 1583). In Revan, which holds the northern gate of the Aras valley, a new and large fortress was built; the city was made the seat of a beylerbeylik. Standing in the middle of the army road between Kars and Tabriz, Revan was the logistical backbone of the eastern front. The fortress was built so solidly that for the following two hundred years it was the constant target of the Ottoman-Iranian wars.
