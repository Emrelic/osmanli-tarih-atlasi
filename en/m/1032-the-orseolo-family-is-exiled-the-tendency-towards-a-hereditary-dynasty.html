The Orseolo family is exiled; the tendency towards a hereditary dynasty is definitively broken

1032

In 1032 the Orseolo family was expelled from Venice entirely and the doge Domenico Flabanico was called to the throne; this was the first institutional step against the dogeship becoming a dynasty.
