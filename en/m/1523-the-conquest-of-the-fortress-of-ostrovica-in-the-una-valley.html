The conquest of the fortress of Ostrovica in the Una valley

1523

Ostrovica, held by the Frankopans and other Croatian nobles, stood on a steep ridge linking the northern and southern parts of the Una valley. It passed to Ottoman administration in 1523 and housed an Ottoman garrison until 1878. The fortress gained its real importance after Karlowitz (1699): because of its proximity to the Venetian and Austrian frontier it was enlarged and became the seat of the captaincy of Stara Ostrovica; the town of Kulen Vakuf was founded at its foot at the beginning of the eighteenth century.
