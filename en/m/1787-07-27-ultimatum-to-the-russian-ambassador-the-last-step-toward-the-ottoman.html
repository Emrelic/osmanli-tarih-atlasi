Ultimatum to the Russian ambassador — the last step toward the Ottoman–Russian War of 1787–1792

27 July 1787

The tension that continued after Russia's annexation of Crimea in 1783 broke when it was understood that Russia's consulate in Alexandria was inciting the Mamluk beys in Egypt against the Ottomans. According to the TDV article on Abdülhamid I, the reisülküttab Süleyman Feyzi Efendi delivered a seven-article ultimatum to the Russian ambassador in Istanbul on 27 July 1787. The decision for war was taken without waiting for Russia's reply and was declared within a few weeks; the aim of the war was the recovery of Crimea.
