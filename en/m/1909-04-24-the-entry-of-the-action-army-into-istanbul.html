The entry of the Action Army into Istanbul

24 April 1909

The Action Army, which set out from Salonica and was made up mostly of units loyal to the Committee of Union and Progress, entered Istanbul to suppress the 31 March incident. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on the Hareket Ordusu, command was assumed by Mahmud Şevket Pasha and the chief of staff was Captain Mustafa Kemal. After brief clashes, order was restored in the city.
