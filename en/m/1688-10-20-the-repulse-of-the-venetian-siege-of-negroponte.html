The Repulse of the Venetian Siege of Negroponte

20 October 1688

Morosini, who turned to the Aegean after seizing the Morea, tried to take Negroponte with a siege lasting more than three months; however, in the face of the fortifications strengthened in the winter of 1687-1688 and the resistance of the garrison, he was forced to withdraw after suffering heavy losses. This failure halted Venice's advance in the Aegean and shook Morosini's military prestige. Negroponte would also remain in Ottoman hands at Karlowitz.
