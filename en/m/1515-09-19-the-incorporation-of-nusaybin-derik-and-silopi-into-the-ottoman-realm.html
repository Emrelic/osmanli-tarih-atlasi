The incorporation of Nusaybin, Derik and Silopi into the Ottoman realm — the southern branch of Diyarbekir

19 September 1515

After the surrender of Âmid and the establishment of the beylerbeyilik of Diyarbekir, Nusaybin, Derik and Silopi, south of the Mardin plain, also passed under Ottoman administration when İdrîs-i Bitlisî won over the Kurdish beys of the region. These places were organised as the nahiye and kaza centres of the new beylerbeyilik; some, such as Cizre, were left to their local beys with the status of yurtluk-ocaklık. Thus the Eastern Anatolian campaign that had begun after Chaldiran reached Upper Mesopotamia.
