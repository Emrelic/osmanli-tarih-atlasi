The Zirid emir al-Mu'izz left Kairouan for Mahdiyya; Kairouan fell to the Hilalis

10 March 1057

Under the siege of the Banu Hilal, al-Mu'izz b. Badis left Kairouan in 449 (1057) for Mahdiyya, where his son Tamim was governor; the undefended Kairouan was plundered and burned by the Bedouin.
