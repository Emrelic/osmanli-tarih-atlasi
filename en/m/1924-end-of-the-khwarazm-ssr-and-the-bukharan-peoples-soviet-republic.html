End of the Khwarazm SSR and the Bukharan People's Soviet Republic through national delimitation: founding of the Uzbek and Turkmen SSRs

1924

By the Soviet decision of 'national delimitation' (natsionalno-territorialnoe razmezhevanie), the Khwarazm Soviet Socialist Republic and the Bukharan People's Soviet Republic were abolished; their territories were divided between the newly established Uzbek and Turkmen Soviet Socialist Republics (the east of Khiva to Uzbekistan, the west to Turkmenistan). The TDV `harizm` entry: '1924’te Hîve Hanlığı’nın doğu kesimleri Özbekistan Sovyet Sosyalist Cumhuriyeti’ne, batı tarafı da Türkmenistan Sovyet Sosyalist Cumhuriyeti’ne bırakıldı.' — it gives only the year.
