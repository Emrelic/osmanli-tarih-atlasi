Qajar Muhammad Hasan Khan declared independence; took Mazandaran and Gilan

1750

Upon the deposition of the Afsharid Suleiman Shah, the Qajar chief in Astarabad, Muhammad Hasan Khan, declared independence; besides Astarabad and Gorgan, he also brought Mazandaran and Gilan under his administration.
