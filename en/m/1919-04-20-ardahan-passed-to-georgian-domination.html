Ardahan passed to Georgian domination

20 April 1919

Ardahan, evacuated after Mudros, was occupied by Georgian forces on 20 April 1919, in the days when the South-West Caucasus Government was being dissolved by the British. Georgian domination would last until 23 February 1921.
