Salamis (29 September 480) and Plataea: the Persian invasion was repulsed

478 BC

After Xerxes captured and burned Athens, the Persian fleet was routed in the straits of Salamis through Themistocles' trap (29 September 480); the following year at Plataea the Greek land army under Pausanias defeated Mardonius and ended the invasion. Thus Aegean Greece was freed from Persian domination.
