Corinth united with Argos (Argos-Corinth isopoliteia)

391 BC

During the Corinthian War (395-387 BC) Corinth formed a union of common citizenship with Argos; an Argive garrison remained in Corinth. Sparta had this union broken up by the King's Peace (386).
