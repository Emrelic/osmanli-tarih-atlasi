The first treaty between Rome and Carthage (Polybius 3.22)

508 BC

Polybius transmits, with its text, the treaty made in the year of the first consuls between Rome and Carthage, which assigned the coasts of Latium to Rome and delimited Carthage to western Sicily and Libya. Rome is named as master of Latium.
