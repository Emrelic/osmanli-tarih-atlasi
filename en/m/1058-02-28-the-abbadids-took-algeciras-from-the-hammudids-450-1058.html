The Abbadids took Algeciras from the Hammudids (450/1058)

28 February 1058

Muhammad al-Mahdi, son of Qasim, and his grandson Qasim al-Wathiq governed Algeciras until it was seized by the Abbadids (Taifa of Seville) (450/1058); the rule of the Hammudid dynasty in Algeciras thus came to an end.
