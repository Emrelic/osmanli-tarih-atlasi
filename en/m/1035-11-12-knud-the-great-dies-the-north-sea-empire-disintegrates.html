Knud the Great dies; the North Sea Empire disintegrates

12 November 1035

Knud (Cnut), who united Denmark, England and Norway under a single throne, died on 12 November 1035; he had been king of Denmark since 1018 and of England since 1016. Norway was his from 1028 to 1035; the union did not last long after his death.
