Ishbi-Erra broke away from Ur III at Isin; the Dynasty of Isin was founded

2016 BC

Ishbi-Erra, a general of Ibbi-Sin, founded an independent dynasty at Isin as the Ur III state collapsed. Isin, as a dynasty bearing the title "king of Sumer and Akkad" that continued the cultural legacy of Ur, was one of the chief powers of southern Mesopotamia for about 220 years.
