Founding of the fortified camp of Sidi Bel Abbès

12 June 1843

On 12 June 1843 Bugeaud ordered General Bedeau to establish a fortified camp with a ditch and walls at Sidi Bel Abbès; construction began on 18 June and in November of the same year a battalion of the Foreign Legion settled there. The camp became a permanent base controlling the interior plain between Oran and Tlemcen.
