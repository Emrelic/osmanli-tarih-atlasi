The Social War ended: Byzantium, Chios, Rhodes and Cos broke away from the Second Athenian League

354 BC

The revolt that began in 357 BC ended when the Athenian commanders were defeated at Chios and Persian support turned against Athens, so that Athens accepted peace; four important members gained their independence. The League weakened and was left defenceless before Macedonia.
