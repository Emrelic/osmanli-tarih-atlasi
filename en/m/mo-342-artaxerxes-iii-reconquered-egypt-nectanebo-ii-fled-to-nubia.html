Artaxerxes III reconquered Egypt; Nectanebo II fled to Nubia

342 BC

When Artaxerxes III marched on Egypt after taking Sidon, the defense of Pelusium collapsed; Nectanebo II, without waiting to be besieged at Memphis, fled to Upper Egypt and Nubia. Egypt became a Persian satrapy for the second time; the fate of the last native pharaoh is unknown.
