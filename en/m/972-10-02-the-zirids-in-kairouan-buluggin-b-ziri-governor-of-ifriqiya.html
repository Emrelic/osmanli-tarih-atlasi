The Zirids in Kairouan (Buluggīn b. Zīrī governor of Ifriqiya)

2 October 972

In the name of the Fatimid caliph, Buluggīn b. Zīrī governed Ifriqiya; the Zirids (972-1057) ruled Kairouan (TDV gives only the year; the day used does not rest on this source).
