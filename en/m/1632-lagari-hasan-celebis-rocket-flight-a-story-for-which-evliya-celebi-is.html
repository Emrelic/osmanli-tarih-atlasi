Lâgarî Hasan Çelebi's rocket flight — a story for which Evliya Çelebi is the only source

1632

According to Evliya Çelebi's Seyahatname, Lâgarî Hasan Çelebi built a seven-armed rocket apparatus with fifty okka of gunpowder at Sarayburnu in the presence of Murad IV, and mounted the rocket saying 'My sultan, I commend you to God, I am going to speak with the prophet Jesus'. According to the account, Hasan Çelebi rose about 250 metres, glided down to the sea with devices resembling a falcon's wings, swam back to the sultan, and in return received a sipahi appointment with a daily pay of seventy akçe.
