The Austrian State Treaty was signed in Vienna — the four-power occupation is ending

15 May 1955

The US, USSR, Britain, France and Austria signed the State Treaty in the Belvedere Palace on 15 May 1955; the treaty came into force on 27 July 1955, the last occupation troops left on 25 October 1955, and the Austrian Parliament passed the constitutional law on permanent neutrality on 26 October 1955.
