Timur took Kandahar from the Kartids and gave it to his grandson Pir Muhammad

1383

Kandahar, which had been held since 1281 by the Kartids of Herat, vassals of the Ilkhanids, was seized during Timur's Khorasan campaigns and given to his grandson Pir Muhammad; the city was thereafter governed by Timurid governors.
