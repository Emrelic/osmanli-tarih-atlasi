Muhammad, son of Arslan Shah, imprisoned his father and ascended the Kirman Seljuk throne

1 September 1142

In Safar 537 (September 1142) Muhammad, son of Arslan Shah, imprisoned his father and ascended the throne; Malik Muhammad Shah, who was fond of learning and astrology, had a mosque, a madrasa and a bimaristan built.
