Galați is first mentioned in a document of a voivode of Moldavia

1445

Stephen II, voivode of Moldavia, exempted two carts of Humor Monastery bringing fish from Galați from customs duty. This document is the first definitely known written testimony to Galați; the settlement may earlier have existed as a fishing village on the Danube bank.
