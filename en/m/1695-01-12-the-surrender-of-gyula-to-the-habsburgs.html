The surrender of Gyula to the Habsburgs

12 January 1695

The besieged Gyula signed a capitulation on 21 December 1694; on 12 January 1695 the garrison marched out and Habsburg troops entered. Until Karlowitz (1699) the fortress was in Habsburg, not Ottoman, hands.
