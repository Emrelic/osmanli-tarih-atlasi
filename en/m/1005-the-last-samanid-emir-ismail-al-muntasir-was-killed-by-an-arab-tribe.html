The last Samanid emir, Isma'il al-Muntasir, was killed by an Arab tribe near Merv; the Samanids passed into history

1005

Isma'il, who crossed the Oxus to escape the Karakhanid forces in Transoxiana, was killed by an Arab tribe with which he took refuge in the desert he entered near Merv (395/1005); with this the Samanid state came to an end.
