Peace of Apamea: Eumenes II gained for Pergamum the Thracian Chersonese, Phrygia, Lydia and most of Mysia

187 BC

After his defeat at Magnesia, Antiochus III signed peace with Rome in 188: he gave up the north of the Taurus Mountains. Most of these lands were given to Rome's ally Eumenes II's Pergamum (the Thracian Chersonese, Greater Phrygia, Lydia, Mysia, part of Ionia).
