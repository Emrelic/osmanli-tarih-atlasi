Mahmud made his last Indian campaign against the Jats; he built a river fleet of 1,400 boats at Multan

11 February 1027

To punish the Jats on both banks of the Indus, who had attacked him, Mahmud had a river fleet of 1,400 boats built at Multan, and defeated the Jats in the battle on the Indus (418/1027).
