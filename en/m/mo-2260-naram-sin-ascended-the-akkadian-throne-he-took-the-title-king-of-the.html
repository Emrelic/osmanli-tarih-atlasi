Naram-Sin ascended the Akkadian throne; he took the title "king of the four quarters of the world"

2260 BC

Naram-Sin (c. 2254-2218), grandson of Sargon, presided over the zenith of the Akkadian state. He became the Mesopotamian ruler who for the first time used the title of deified king for himself and the epithet "king of the four quarters".
