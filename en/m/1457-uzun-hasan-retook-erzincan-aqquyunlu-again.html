Uzun Hasan retook Erzincan: Aqquyunlu again

1457

Uzun Hasan defeated the Qaraquyunlu and brought Erzincan again under Aqquyunlu rule. The city remained within Aqquyunlu borders also after the Battle of Otlukbeli in 1473.
