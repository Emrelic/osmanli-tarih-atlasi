Suleyman Shah I, the founder of the Anatolian Seljuks, conquered Tarsus

1082

Suleyman Shah I conquered Tarsus in 475/1082; the city was taken by the Crusaders in 490/1097 and afterwards changed hands among the Crusaders, Byzantium, and the Armenians of the region.
