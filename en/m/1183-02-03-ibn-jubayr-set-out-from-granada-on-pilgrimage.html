Ibn Jubayr set out from Granada on pilgrimage

3 February 1183

The Andalusian secretary Ibn Jubayr set out from Granada with the intention of making the pilgrimage and sailed from Tarifa and Ceuta to Alexandria on a Genoese ship. His journey lasted 2.5 years and is the source of his work called the Rihla.
