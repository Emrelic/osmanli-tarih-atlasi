The surrender of Derbent to Iran — the end of thirteen years of Russian occupation

8 May 1735

The keys of Derbent were handed over to the Iranian side; some Russian troops remained in the city a little longer. Thus the Russian occupation that began in 1722 ended without a single major battle, owing to Russia's wish to keep Nadir as an ally before a new Ottoman war.
