Los Millares: the settlement ends

2166 BC

A Copper Age walled settlement and megalithic necropolis in southeastern Iberia; the wall circuits at the beginning of the 3rd millennium, the 'forts' after ca. 2500 BCE, abandonment around ca. 2200 BCE.
