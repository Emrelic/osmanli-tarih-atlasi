San Francisco de Quito was founded — Spanish order in the northern Inca capital

6 December 1534

The Inca general Rumiñahui burned Quito, the capital of the north, before leaving it to the Spaniards. Sebastián de Benalcázar founded San Francisco de Quito over the ruins; thus, after Cuzco, the second centre of the Inca state was also bound to Spanish rule, and the conquest of the Northern Andes was directed from here.
