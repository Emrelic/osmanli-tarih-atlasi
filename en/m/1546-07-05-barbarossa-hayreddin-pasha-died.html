Barbarossa Hayreddin Pasha died

5 July 1546

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Barbaros Hayreddin Pasha, after a short illness he died in Istanbul on 6 Jumada al-Awwal 953 (5 July 1546). The death of Barbarossa, who was buried in the tomb adjoining the madrasa he himself had built at Beşiktaş, was seen as the close of an era in the Mediterranean supremacy established by the Ottoman navy.
