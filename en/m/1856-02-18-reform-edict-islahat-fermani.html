Reform Edict (Islahat Fermanı)

18 February 1856

The edict, proclaimed before the Paris peace negotiations, extended the principles of the Tanzimat and granted non-Muslim subjects broad rights of equality, including state office, military service and the opening of schools; the organization of the communities was rearranged. With the Treaty of Paris signed afterwards, the Ottoman State was formally admitted to the family of European public law and its territorial integrity was placed under the joint guarantee of the great powers — a guarantee that in practice would become a new door for interventions.
