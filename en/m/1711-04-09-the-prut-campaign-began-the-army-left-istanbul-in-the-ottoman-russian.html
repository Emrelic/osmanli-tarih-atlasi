The Prut campaign began — the army left Istanbul in the Ottoman–Russian War of 1710–1711

9 April 1711

Russia's attack on Ottoman lands, Tsar Peter's failure to abide by the provisions of the Treaty of Istanbul of 1700, and the incitements of the Crimean Khan Devlet Giray had led Ahmed III to declare war on Russia. According to the TDV article on Ahmed III, this war, later known as the Prut campaign, effectively began with the departure of the Ottoman army from Istanbul on 9 April 1711. The army under Baltacı Mehmed Pasha joined the Crimean forces and advanced toward the River Prut; the campaign would end in July with the Russian army being hemmed in on the banks of the Prut.
