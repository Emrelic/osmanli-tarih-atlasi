The child Frederick is crowned king of Sicily at Palermo (later Frederick II)

17 May 1198

After the death of Henry VI in 1197, his three-year-old son Frederick was crowned at Palermo on 17 May 1198; with the death of his mother Constance, the Norman heiress, he came under the guardianship of Pope Innocent III.
