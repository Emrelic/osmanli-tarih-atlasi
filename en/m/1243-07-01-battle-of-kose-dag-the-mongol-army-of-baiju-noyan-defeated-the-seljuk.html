Battle of Köse Dağ: the Mongol army of Baiju Noyan defeated the Seljuk army; the Seljuks agreed to pay the Mongols an annual tribute

1 July 1243

On the view of the young commanders, Sultan Kaykhusraw II left Sivas behind and advanced to the heights of Köse Dağ; according to Ibn Bibi, the Mongol army of Baiju Noyan routed the Seljuk army on 11 Muharram 641 (1 July 1243). The Mongols plundered Sivas and Kayseri; according to the peace, the Seljuks were to give 3,600,000 dirhams, 10,000 sheep, 1,000 cattle and 1,000 camels annually (Ilkhanid vassalage began).
