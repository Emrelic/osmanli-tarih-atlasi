Ala al-Din Muhammad took Ghazna and gave it to his son Jalal al-Din; the atabegs of Fars and Azerbaijan submitted

1215

Ala al-Din Muhammad, who in the same year took Ghazna and gave it to his son Jalal al-Din, defeated the Atabeg of Fars, Sa'd b. Zangi, and the Atabeg of Azerbaijan, Uzbek, and secured their submission; the Khwarazmshahs established their dominion over a vast area extending from the banks of the Syr Darya to Iraq-i Arab.
