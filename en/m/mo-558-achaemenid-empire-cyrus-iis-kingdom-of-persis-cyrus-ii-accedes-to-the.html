Achaemenid Empire / Cyrus II's kingdom of Persis: Cyrus II accedes to the Persian throne (559)

558 BC

559: beginning of Cyrus II's reign (the reign limit given by the Pasargadae article); 550: overthrow of the Median king Astyages and the unification of Iran (Schmitt); 330: the end of Darius III.
