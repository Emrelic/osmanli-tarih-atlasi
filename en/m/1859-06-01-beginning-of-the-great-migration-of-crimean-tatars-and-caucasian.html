Beginning of the great migration of Crimean Tatars and Caucasian peoples into Ottoman lands

1 June 1859

After Sheikh Shamil was taken captive by the Russians in 1859, a large number of Circassian and Nogay immigrants from the Caucasus began to migrate to Ottoman lands together with the Crimean Tatars. This wave of migration continued with varying intensity until after the Ottoman-Russian War of 1877-78.
