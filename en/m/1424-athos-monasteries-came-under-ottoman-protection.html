Athos monasteries came under Ottoman protection

1424

In the years when Thessalonica was under siege, the monastic republic of Mount Athos (Aynaroz) on the eastern prong of Chalkidiki applied to Murad II, asked for protection and accepted Ottoman rule. The monks' rights of property and worship were guaranteed; in return for tax, the monasteries were left free in their internal affairs. This arrangement became the longest-lived example of the relationship of protection that the Ottomans established with Orthodox institutions, and it lasted five centuries.
