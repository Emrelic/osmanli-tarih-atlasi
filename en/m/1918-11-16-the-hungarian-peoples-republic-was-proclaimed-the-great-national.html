The Hungarian People's Republic was proclaimed: the Great National Assembly declared Hungary an independent people's republic

16 November 1918

Charles issued a proclamation of withdrawal from Hungarian politics on 13 November 1918; on 16 November the Great National Assembly of the Hungarian National Council proclaimed the 'People's Decree' and made Hungary an independent people's republic, with Károlyi becoming prime minister and provisional president; 400 years of Habsburg rule formally ended. (The Aster Revolution of 31 October had formed the government; the republic was proclaimed on this day.)
