Fulad Sutun, master of Shiraz, had the khutba read in the name of Toghril Beg

1054

Abu Mansur Fulad Sutun, who was struggling against the Buyid al-Malik al-Rahim, became master of Shiraz and in January 1054 had the khutba read in the name of the Seljuk sultan Toghril Beg; the Oghuz had advanced as far as Shiraz in 1052-53.
