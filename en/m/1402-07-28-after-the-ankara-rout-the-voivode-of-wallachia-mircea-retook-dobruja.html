After the Ankara rout the Voivode of Wallachia Mircea retook Dobruja: Silistra, Constanța, Babadag, Isaccea

28 July 1402

Bayezid the Thunderbolt's defeat by Timur at Ankara shook Ottoman rule along the Danube. The Voivode of Wallachia Mircea took advantage of this gap and entered Dobruja again; Silistra, Constanța, the Babadag area and Isaccea on the Danube remained in his hands throughout the Interregnum years. Mircea supported Musa Çelebi against Çelebi Mehmed. He held Silistra until his death in 1418.
