Tepe Yahya: Period VII begins (Iranica; provisional table)

5499 BC

A mound with a long stratigraphic sequence in southeastern Iran; Period VII (ceramic Neolithic, 5500-4500), Period IVC2 (3100-2800) a large building complex with Proto-Elamite tablets and Susa III-type material; IVB 2400-2000; IVA 1800-1400; III 800-500; II 500-275; I 200 BCE-300 CE.
