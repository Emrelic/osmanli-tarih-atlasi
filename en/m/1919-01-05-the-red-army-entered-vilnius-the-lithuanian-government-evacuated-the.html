The Red Army entered Vilnius — the Lithuanian government evacuated the city

5 January 1919

The Lithuanian government evacuated Vilnius as the Red Army approached; Soviet forces entered the city and in February 1919 the Lithuanian-Belorussian Soviet Republic (Litbel) was proclaimed from there. The city would be taken by Polish forces in April 1919.
