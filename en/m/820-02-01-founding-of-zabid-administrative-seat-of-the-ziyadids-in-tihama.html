Founding of Zabīd: administrative seat of the Ziyadids in Tihāma

1 February 820

By order of Caliph al-Ma'mūn, Muhammad ibn Abdallah ibn Ziyād founded the city of Zabīd in Sha'bān 204 (February 820); he had palaces built and canals dug. The city became the administrative seat of the Ziyadids, who were semi-independent of the Abbasids.
