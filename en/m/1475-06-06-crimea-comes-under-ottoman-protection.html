Crimea comes under Ottoman protection

6 June 1475

The fleet under the command of Gedik Ahmed Pasha conquered the southern coastal cities of Crimea, beginning with Caffa, which was in Genoese hands; these coasts were made a direct Ottoman sancak. The Crimean Khanate, under Mengli Giray, became a privileged vassal state recognizing Ottoman suzerainty and was for three centuries a cornerstone of northern policy; the Black Sea had now effectively become an Ottoman inland sea. Other places annexed on the same date: Bakhchysarai, Kerch, Azak.
