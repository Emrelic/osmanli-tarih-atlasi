The Achaean League was refounded: Patrae, Dyme, Tritaea, Pharae

279 BC

In Achaea under Antigonid control, four cities (Dyme, Patrae, Tritaea, Pharae) refounded a federal league in 280 BC; in the following years Aegium and other cities joined. Under Aratus's leadership the League became a great power of the Peloponnese.
