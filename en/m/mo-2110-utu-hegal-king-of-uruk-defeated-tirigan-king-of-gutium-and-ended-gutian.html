Utu-hegal, king of Uruk, defeated Tirigan, king of Gutium, and ended Gutian dominion

2110 BC

Utu-hegal, king of Uruk, defeated and captured Tirigan, the Gutian king who ruled Sumer, on the bank of the Tigris; the Gutians withdrew from Sumer and Sumerian rule was re-established in the south under the leadership of Uruk. Utu-hegal's general Ur-Nammu would soon found the Ur III dynasty.
