The Aghlabid fleet besieged Dubrovnik; the city was saved by the Byzantine fleet

867

A fleet of the Aghlabids, who ruled Sicily, besieged Dubrovnik in 867; the city was saved from a siege lasting fifteen months with the help of the Byzantine fleet. With this victory Byzantium consolidated its superiority in the Adriatic.
