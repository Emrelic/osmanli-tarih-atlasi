Konya Ereğli under Egyptian occupation — the Taurus mouth of the Konya–Adana road

13 September 1832

Ereğli, on the road to Konya of the Egyptian army that crossed the Gülek Pass, came under the occupation of Ibrahim Pasha in 1832 and, when it was evacuated with the Kütahya arrangement of 1833, was again attached to the province of Karaman. On the map it is the sourced intermediate point of the occupation line between Adana and Konya; no city-level testimony could be found for Ulukışla and Karapınar.
