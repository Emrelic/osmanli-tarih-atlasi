Tughrul Shah, son of Muhammad Shah, became ruler of Kirman

27 June 1156

After Malik Muhammad Shah, his son Tughrul Shah ascended the throne on 6 Jumada I 551 (27 June 1156); he is remembered as a compassionate and just ruler and died in 565 (1170).
