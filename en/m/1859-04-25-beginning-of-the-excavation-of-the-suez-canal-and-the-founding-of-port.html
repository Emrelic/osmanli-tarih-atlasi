Beginning of the excavation of the Suez Canal and the founding of Port Said

25 April 1859

The excavation works of the Lesseps project, which the Sublime Porte had approved on 5 January 1856, began on 25 April 1859; the canal would be completed ten years later, on 16 November 1869. At the Mediterranean end of the excavation, a hut village of five houses set up for the workers on the coastal strip between Lake Manzala and the sea formed the nucleus of Port Said. The city was given this name, meaning 'Port of Said,' in 1860 by the officials of the canal company in honor of the then governor of Egypt, Said Pasha. Thus a new port was born on the Ottoman map on a coastal strip where there had previously been no settlement.
