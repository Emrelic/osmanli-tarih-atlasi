Sechín Alto: Sechín Alto: latest Haldas date

1009 BC

A large ceremonial complex of the Moxeke/Haldas period in the Casma valley; the oldest known structures are ~1600–1400 BC, the painted façade of Moxeke B ~1450/1400, and dates of the Haldas period (1400–1000) extend to ~1100–920.
