The fourteenth session at Jassy — Russia's renunciation of its claim to indemnity

7 January 1792

At the fourteenth meeting on 7 January 1792 the Russian plenipotentiary Bezborodko gave up the claim to indemnity and declared that peace had been concluded. It was decided that the text of the treaty would be signed three days later.
