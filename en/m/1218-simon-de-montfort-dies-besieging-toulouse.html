Simon de Montfort dies besieging Toulouse

1218

Simon de Montfort, commander of the Albigensian Crusade, had entered Toulouse in 1215, but died in 1218 while besieging the city. Command of the crusade passed to his son Amaury; Raymond VII, son of Raymond VI, became head of the county in 1222.
