Piri Reis expands the Kitab-i Bahriye and presents it to Süleyman the Lawgiver

1526

Piri Reis expanded the Kitab-i Bahriye, partly in verse form, and presented it to Süleyman the Lawgiver in 1526. In this form the work became widespread as the most comprehensive sailing guide of Ottoman seafaring.
