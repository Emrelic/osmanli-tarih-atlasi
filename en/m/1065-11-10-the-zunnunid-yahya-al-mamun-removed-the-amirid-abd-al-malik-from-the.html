The Zunnunid Yahya al-Ma'mun removed the Amirid Abd al-Malik from the throne; Valencia passed to the Zunnunids

10 November 1065

The ruler of Toledo, the Zunnunid Yahya al-Ma'mun, removed the Amirid ruler Abd al-Malik from his throne (8 Dhu'l-Hijja 457 / 10 November 1065); between 457 and 462 (1065-1070) the Zunnunids ruled Xativa and Valencia, which were in Amirid hands.
