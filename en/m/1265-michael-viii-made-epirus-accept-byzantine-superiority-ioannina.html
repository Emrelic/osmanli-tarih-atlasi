Michael VIII made Epirus accept Byzantine superiority (Ioannina)

1265

Michael Palaiologos took part of Macedonia from the Bulgarians in 1264 and, by seizing Ioannina in 1265, made the Despotate of Epirus accept the empire's sovereignty.
