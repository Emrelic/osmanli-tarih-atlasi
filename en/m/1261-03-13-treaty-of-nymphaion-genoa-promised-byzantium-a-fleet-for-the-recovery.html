Treaty of Nymphaion: Genoa promised Byzantium a fleet for the recovery of Constantinople

13 March 1261

By the treaty signed at Nymphaion on 13 March 1261 between Michael VIII and Genoa, the Genoese merchants would become heirs to Venice's former privileges if Constantinople were recovered.
