Battle of Merv: the Uzbeks heavily defeated, Merv and Herat taken

2 December 1510

Shah Ismail, having completed the Aqquyunlu inheritance in the west, marched east to Khorasan and heavily defeated the Uzbeks before Merv; Shaybani Khan died on the battlefield. Merv and Herat came under Safavid rule, and the Safavid State attained borders extending from the Euphrates to the Oxus. Thus the process that began with the entry into Tabriz in the summer of 1501 was completed in nine years: the Aqquyunlu inheritance passed entirely to Safavid administration, and the Ottoman State found itself neighbor, to its east, to a second state of its own size.
