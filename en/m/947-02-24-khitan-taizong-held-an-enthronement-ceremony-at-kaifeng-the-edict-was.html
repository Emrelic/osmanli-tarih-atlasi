Khitan Taizong held an enthronement ceremony at Kaifeng; the edict was issued under the name 'Great Liao'

24 February 947

After Kaifeng was entered by the vanguard force of Zhang Yanze, the Khitan ruler held a ceremony in the main palace on the first day of the 2nd month: the Chinese officials lined up in Chinese dress and the Khitans in Khitan dress, the edict was issued with the wording 'Great Liao, Huitong year 10', and a general amnesty was proclaimed.
