The Thirty Years' Peace: Athens lost its land empire (Boeotia, Megara, Achaea)

445 BC

After the Boeotians' defeat of the Athenian army at Coronea (447), then the revolts of Euboea and Megara and the Spartan invasion of Attica, Athens signed the Thirty Years' Peace. Athens gave up Megara, Nisaea, Pegae, and Troezen and Achaea in the Peloponnese; it kept only its naval empire.
