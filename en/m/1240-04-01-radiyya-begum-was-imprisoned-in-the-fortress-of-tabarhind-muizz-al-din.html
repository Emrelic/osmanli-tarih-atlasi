Radiyya Begum was imprisoned in the Fortress of Tabarhind; Muizz al-Din Bahram Shah was proclaimed sultan

1 April 1240

Radiyya's most loyal man, Yaqut al-Habashi, was killed when she campaigned against the rebel Governor of Tabarhind, Ikhtiyar al-Din Altuniyya, and the Turkish commanders; Radiyya was captured and imprisoned in the Fortress of Tabarhind (Ramadan 637 / April 1240). When the news reached Delhi, Iltutmish's son Muizz al-Din Bahram Shah was proclaimed sultan.
