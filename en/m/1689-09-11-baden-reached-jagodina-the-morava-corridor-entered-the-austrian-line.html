Baden reached Jagodina — the Morava corridor entered the Austrian line

11 September 1689

After the defeat at Batočina (30 August) the Ottoman army withdrew southward; Baden reached Jagodina on 11 September 1689 and built a bridge over the Morava. The Austrian line extended to Niš by way of Jagodina–Ćuprija–Paraćin, while Alaca Hisar (Kruševac) remained outside the line.
