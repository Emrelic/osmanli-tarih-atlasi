Cyaxares (Umakištar) ascended the Median throne

624 BC

According to the tradition of Herodotus, Cyaxares reigned for forty years, united Media and set out on campaign against Assyria. The alliance with Babylon (614) and the capture of Nineveh (612) fall within his reign; in these years Media reached its greatest extent.
