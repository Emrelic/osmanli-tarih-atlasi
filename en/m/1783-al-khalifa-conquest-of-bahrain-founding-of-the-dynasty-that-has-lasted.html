Al Khalifa conquest of Bahrain: founding of the dynasty that has lasted to the present day

1783

Nasr Al Mazkur, the Zand administration's governor of Bahrain, besieged Zubarah on the Qatar peninsula; the Utub forces under the command of Ahmad b. Muhammad b. Khalifa broke the siege and, launching a counterattack, took Bahrain the same year. The TDV's record is brief: "Bahrain came under the rule of the Al Khalifa of the Utub tribe in 1783." After this victory Ahmad b. Muhammad was called "al-Fatih" (the Conqueror), and the dynasty he founded has ruled the island to the present day.
