Contest of the two kings: Esztergom under Habsburg protection, Szeged with Szapolyai

1530

Esztergom, which had changed hands between the two kings since 1526, came under Habsburg protection after the archbishop moved north with his archive and treasury. From the same year until 1541, Szeged remained under the sovereignty of Szapolyai.
