The Later Liang made Qian Liu 'King of Wuyue'

907

Following the edict of Zhu Wen's accession, the military governor of Zhejiang, Qian Liu, received the title 'King of Wuyue'; in the same edict the governor of Henan, Zhang Quanyi, was made 'King of Wei'.
