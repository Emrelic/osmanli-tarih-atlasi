The Seljuk vizier Sahib Ata Fakhr al-Din Ali dies near Akşehir

22 November 1288

Sahib Ata, who served Kaykawus as deputy of the sultanate (na'ib-i saltanat) (657/1259), was imprisoned in Osmancık Castle in 670/1271-72 and reappointed vizier in 674/1275-76; after the campaign against the Karamanids with the allied Seljuk-Mongol army at the end of 686 / beginning of 1288, he died in the village of Nadir, dependent on Akşehir, on 25 Shawwal 687 / 22 November 1288 and was buried in his tomb at Konya.
