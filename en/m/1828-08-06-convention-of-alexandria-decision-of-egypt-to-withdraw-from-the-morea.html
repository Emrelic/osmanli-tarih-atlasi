Convention of Alexandria: decision of Egypt to withdraw from the Morea

6 August 1828

Mehmed Ali, who had lost his fleet at Navarino, came to terms directly with the British admiral without consulting Istanbul and agreed to withdraw his forces from the Morea. A governor's making an independent agreement with the great powers was a harbinger of the future crisis.
