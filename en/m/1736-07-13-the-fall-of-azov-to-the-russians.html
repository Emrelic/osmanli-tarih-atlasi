The fall of Azov to the Russians

13 July 1736

At the end of the attack that began in March 1736, the fortress of Azov at the mouth of the Don fell into Russian hands. The same summer the Russians crossed Or Kapı and entered the Crimea, took Gözleve and Kılburun, and caused destruction at Bahçesaray and Akmescid. Azov remained in Russia by the peace of 1739, but its fortifications were demolished. The sources differ on the day of the fall: TDV gives this date together with Gözleve, Or Kapı and Kılburun; Russian sources say 19 June (30 June Gregorian), and another source says 1 July.
