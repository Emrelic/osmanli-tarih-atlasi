Work began on opening the Tongji Canal (the first branch of the Grand Canal)

14 April 605

More than a million men and women were conscripted from the commanderies of Henan; the Tongji Canal was dug, connecting the Gu and Luo waters from the Western Park to the Yellow River and the Yellow River to the Huai from Banzhu.
