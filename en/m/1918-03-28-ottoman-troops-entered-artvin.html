Ottoman troops entered Artvin

28 March 1918

Ottoman troops, who crossed the 1914 border on 25 March, marched on the Three Sanjaks (Elviye-i Selase), whose pre-1878 border Brest-Litovsk gave back, and entered Artvin on 28 March 1918. Artvin had been left to Russia in 1878 as part of the sanjak of Batum; the TDV entry on Artvin writes the return only as 'March 1918', the day is given by Mustafa Sarı.
