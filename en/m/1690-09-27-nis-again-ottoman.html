Niš again Ottoman

27 September 1690

Fazıl Mustafa Pasha set out on campaign in July 1690; after Şehirköy (Pirot), Passarowitz and Güvercinlik (Golubac) were taken, Niš, which was besieged, fell into Ottoman hands on 27 September 1690 (TDV semendire). At the same time Vidin had also been retaken. Other settlements returning from the Habsburgs to the Ottomans on the same day in the atlas: Jagodina and Ürküp (Prokuplje) — the Niš day was taken because no day at place level could be found (TDV semendire; declared approximation; Röder 1842 writes that the vizier passed through Jagodina on 8-24 September, the contradiction was not resolved).
