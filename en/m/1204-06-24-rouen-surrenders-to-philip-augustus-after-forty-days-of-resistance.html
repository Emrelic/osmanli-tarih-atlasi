Rouen surrenders to Philip Augustus after forty days of resistance

24 June 1204

The siege of Château-Gaillard from September 1203 to April 1204 decided the fate of Normandy; Rouen, tied to England by trade, surrendered on 24 June 1204 after holding out for forty days.
