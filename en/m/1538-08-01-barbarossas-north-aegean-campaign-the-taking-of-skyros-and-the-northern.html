Barbarossa's North Aegean campaign: the taking of Skyros and the Northern Sporades

1 August 1538

On the way to Preveza, the fleet took over one by one the North Aegean islands that had taken refuge with Venice after the conquest of Constantinople. Skyros, north of Negroponte, and Skiathos, Skopelos and Alonnisos, which hold the mouth of the Gulf of Thessaloniki, thus came under Ottoman administration. These islands closed the last North Aegean gap, which had remained in Venetian hands for sixty-eight more years even though Negroponte had been taken in 1470.
