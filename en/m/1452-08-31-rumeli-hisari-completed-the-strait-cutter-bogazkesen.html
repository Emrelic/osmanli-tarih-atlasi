Rumeli Hisarı completed — the 'Strait-Cutter' (Boğazkesen)

31 August 1452

With the fortress completed, according to the sources, in an extraordinarily swift construction lasting four to five months, control of the Bosphorus passed entirely to the Ottomans; the route of aid from the Black Sea to Constantinople was closed. It is regarded as the de facto first step of the siege.
