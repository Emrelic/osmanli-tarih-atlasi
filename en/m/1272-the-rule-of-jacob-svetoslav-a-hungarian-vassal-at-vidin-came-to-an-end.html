The rule of Jacob Svetoslav, a Hungarian vassal, at Vidin came to an end

1272

In June 1266 a Hungarian army took Vidin and destroyed the region; Jacob Svetoslav, of Rus origin, remained in power as a vassal of Hungary with the title of Bulgarian tsar until 1272, after which Vidin weakened and did not recover for a long time.
