Midhat Pasha's Najd Campaign: The Re-establishment of Ottoman Dominion in al-Hasa

20 April 1871

Midhat Pasha, the governor of Baghdad, exploiting the struggle for the throne within the Saudi family, sent troops to the western shore of the Persian Gulf and attached the region of al-Hasa to the vilayet of Basra under the name of the sancak of Najd. With Abdullah b. Faysal appointed mutasarrıf, Ottoman dominion was established on paper. The campaign aimed at halting the spread of British influence in the Gulf and in the same year led to Qatar's also coming under Ottoman control.
