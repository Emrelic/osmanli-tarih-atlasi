Yue Fei and Zhang Xian were tried in the Court of Judicial Review (Dali); Yue Fei was sentenced to death

27 January 1142

On the 戊寅 day of the 10th month Yue Fei and Zhang Xian were thrown into the Dali Prison and interrogated; on the 癸巳 day of the 12th month Yue Fei was sentenced to death in the Dali Court, his son Yue Yun and Zhang Xian were executed in the marketplace, and their families were exiled to Guangnan.
