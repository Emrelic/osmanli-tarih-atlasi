Ban Non Wat: Neolithic 1 phase: rice farmers settle

1799 BC

A moated site, the longest continuous sequence in Southeast Asia from the Neolithic to the end of the Iron Age; ~700 burials with 75 radiocarbon dates (shell, charcoal, rice, bone). Bronze Age: BA1 1050-1000, BA2 1000-900, BA3 900-800, BA4 800-700, BA5 700-420 BCE; Neolithic 2 1250-1050.
