Sheikh Edebali — the founding link between the ahi organization and the Ottoman dynasty

1326

Edebali, who founded a zawiya at Bilecik and was also a sheikh of the ahi organization, according to tradition interpreted Osman Gazi's famous dream; Osman Gazi then married Edebali's daughter Malhun Hatun. Edebali is also regarded as the first Ottoman kadi and mufti.
