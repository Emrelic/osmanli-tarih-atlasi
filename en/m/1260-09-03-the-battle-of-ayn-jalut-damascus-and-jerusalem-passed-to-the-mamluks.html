The Battle of ʿAyn Jālūt: Damascus and Jerusalem passed to the Mamluks

3 September 1260

Qutuz defeated the Mongols at ʿAyn Jālūt (25 Ramadan 658 / 3 September 1260); the greater part of Syria, Damascus and Jerusalem fell into the hands of the Mamluks, and the Ayyubid dynasty came to an end.
