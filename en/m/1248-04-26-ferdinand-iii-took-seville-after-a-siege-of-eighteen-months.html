Ferdinand III took Seville after a siege of eighteen months

26 April 1248

After years of wars and a siege of eighteen months, King Ferdinand III of Castile captured Seville (646/1248); some 400,000 people were forced to leave the region, and the Great Mosque was converted into a church.
