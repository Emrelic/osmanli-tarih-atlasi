The grand vizier-commander Koca Yûsuf Pasha set out on campaign with absolute authority

22 February 1788

In the days when the two-front war opened, the grand vizier and commander-in-chief Koca Yûsuf Pasha set out from Istanbul at the head of the army on 22 February 1788. Abdülhamid I gave him, in the words of TDV, a broad independence of the degree of the Köprülüs, that is, absolute authority. The direction and conduct of the war were thus left entirely in the hands of the commander.
