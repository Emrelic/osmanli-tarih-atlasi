The Crusaders took Antioch: the Principality of Antioch was founded

3 June 1098

Antioch, which had withstood a long siege, fell into the hands of the Crusaders through the treachery of Firuz, a commander of Yaghi-Siyan. The Principality of Antioch that was established included Alexandretta (Iskenderun).
