Takarkori rock shelter (Tadrart Acacus): ends

3949 BC

A rock shelter in the Tadrart Acacus in the Fezzan of Libya: a continuous stratigraphy extending from Late Acacus hunter-gatherer-fishers (LA1 first occupation) to Early, Middle and Late Pastoral herders (Green Sahara).
