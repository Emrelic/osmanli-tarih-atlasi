Act of Abjuration (Plakkaat van Verlatinghe) — the Netherlands declared independence from Spain

26 July 1581

While the Eighty Years' War continued, the States General (Staten-Generaal) of the Dutch provinces belonging to the Union of Utrecht, by the Act of Abjuration adopted on 26 July 1581, declared King Philip II of Spain a 'tyrant' and renounced allegiance to him; this document is regarded as the founding declaration of the de facto independent United Provinces (the Dutch Republic). Spain's final recognition would await Westphalia in 1648, but the actual break dates from this day.
