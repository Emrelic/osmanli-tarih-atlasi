King István I dies; a period of turmoil in the Árpád dynasty

1038

After István's death in 1038, forty troubled years lasting until 1077 began; Hungary was drawn into the struggle between empire and papacy. In the Árpád dynasty there were twenty-three changes of king until 1301.
