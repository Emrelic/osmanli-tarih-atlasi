Ahmose campaigned into Lower Nubia and suppressed the Nubian bowmen

1524 BC

After defeating the Asiatics, Ahmose sailed up the river south to Khent-hen-nefer and suppressed the Nubian bowmen. This campaign is the first step in the process of annexing Lower Nubia, which had been in the hands of Kerma since the Middle Kingdom fortress line, to Egypt.
