Doge Pietro II Orseolo subjugated the Dalmatian cities and became "Duke of Dalmatia"

1000

Setting out with a large fleet at the invitation of the Dalmatians, Orseolo defeated the Croats and the Narentines, cleared the Adriatic of piracy, and received the tribute and oaths of allegiance of the Dalmatian cities; he took the title "Duke of Dalmatia". Zadar swore fealty to the doge in 1000.
