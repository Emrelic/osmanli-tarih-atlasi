The recapture of Orsova by Thököly and the Ottoman forces

15 July 1689

After the capture of Vetislan, Thököly and the Ottoman pashas marched on Orsova, which Veterani had occupied in 1688. After insignificant skirmishes, Thököly entered Orsova on 15 July 1689. Heissler, who had come on news of the danger, saw that the force facing him was well equipped with munitions and ships, and withdrew to protect Transylvania. Herbeville's attack before the town in August also failed to retake Orsova.
