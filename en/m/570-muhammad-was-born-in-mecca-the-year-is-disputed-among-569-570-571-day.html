Muhammad was born in Mecca (the year is disputed among 569/570/571; day: 12 Rabi' al-awwal, Monday)

570

According to Muslim belief, Muhammad, the final prophet of God, was born in Mecca shortly after the Event of the Elephant. Historical record: the TDV gives the day as Monday, 12 Rabi' al-awwal; the year differs among the sources: 569 (Hamidullah), 570 or 571 (dating of the Event of the Elephant) and 571 (Falaki); Watt also says "about AD 570".
