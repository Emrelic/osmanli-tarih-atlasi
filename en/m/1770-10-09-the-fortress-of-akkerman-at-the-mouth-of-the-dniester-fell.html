The fortress of Akkerman at the mouth of the Dniester fell

9 October 1770

Shortly after the fall of Bender, Akkerman at the mouth of the Dniester also fell into the hands of the Russian forces. The fortress was returned to the Ottomans by the Treaty of Küçük Kaynarca (1774).
