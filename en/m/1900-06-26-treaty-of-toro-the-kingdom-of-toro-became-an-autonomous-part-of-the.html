Treaty of Toro — the Kingdom of Toro became an autonomous part of the British Uganda Protectorate

26 June 1900

After the fall of Bunyoro in 1899, the neighbouring Kingdom of Toro formalized the British protection it had been under since 1896 within the framework of the 1900 Uganda Agreement; the kingdom, including its capital Fort Portal, acquired the status of an autonomous local administration within the British Uganda Protectorate.
