Al-Hajj Umar took Hamdallahi — end of the State of Macina

16 May 1862

The allied forces of Macina, in which Bina Ali, who had fled from Segou, had taken refuge, were defeated; Hamdallahi, the center of Macina, fell into the hands of al-Hajj Umar, and Bina Ali and Ahmadu Ahmadu were executed.
