The Druze-Maronite Civil War in Mount Lebanon and the Damascus Events

30 May 1860

The clashes that erupted in May 1860 with the killing of two Druze near Sidon turned into the bloodiest Druze-Maronite civil war in the mountain and spread to Damascus in July, resulting in the deaths of thousands of Christians. Upon France's landing of troops, the Sublime Porte sent Fuad Pasha to the region with extraordinary powers; a harsh punitive operation was conducted. The events provided a pretext for European intervention and were the direct reason for Lebanon's acquiring a special status.
