Pasargadae, Susa: Empire of Alexander the Great (Argead nominal) → ownerless interval; Alexander IV was killed

309 BC

Alexander IV (323-310 BC), the last king of the Argead kingship, was killed together with his mother Roxane; the Empire of Alexander entry ends with this year. The later owner of Pasargadae and Susa has not been sourced. Babylon, Uruk and Hamadan pass to Seleucus before this date.
