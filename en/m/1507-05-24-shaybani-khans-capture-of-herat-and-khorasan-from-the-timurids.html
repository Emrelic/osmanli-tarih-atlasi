Shaybani Khan's capture of Herat and Khorasan from the Timurids

24 May 1507

According to the TDV article on Shaybani Khan, after taking Andijan, Fergana, Hisar, Kunduz and Balkh between 1503 and 1505, the Uzbek ruler Shaybani Khan also captured the Timurid capital Herat in 913 (1507); the Encyclopaedia Iranica article on Khorasan gives the exact date of this conquest — the city fell on 8 Muharram 913 (20 May 1507), and a week later, on 15 Muharram (27 May), the khutba was read in Shaybani Khan's name and the conquest became official. All of Khorasan, including Mashhad, passed from the Timurids to the Shaybanids within the same year; this meant that the Timurid dynasty, after Transoxiana, had lost its last great stronghold in Iran as well.
