The surrender of the Gyula (Göle) fortress — Pertev Pasha's Temesvár frontier campaign

1 September 1566

During the Szigetvár campaign of Süleyman the Lawgiver, Pertev Pasha was appointed commander to take Göle (Gyula), one of the fortresses on the frontier of the province of Temesvár; all the forces of the beylerbeyi of Temesvár and the sancakbeyi of Belgrade were placed under his command. Pertev Pasha, who left Istanbul in May 1566 with Janissaries, silahdars and gunners, captured Göle and several fortresses around it on 1 September 1566 after a siege of more than a month. The timars and zeamets of those who had distinguished themselves in the battle were increased.
