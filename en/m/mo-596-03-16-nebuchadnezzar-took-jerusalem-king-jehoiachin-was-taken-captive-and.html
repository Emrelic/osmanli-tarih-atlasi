Nebuchadnezzar took Jerusalem; King Jehoiachin was taken captive and Zedekiah took his place

16 March 596 BC

The Babylonian army besieged "the city of Judah" and took it on 2 Addaru; King Jehoiachin was taken captive to Babylon and was replaced by Zedekiah (Mattaniah), chosen by Babylon. The city came under Babylonian sovereignty for the first time.
