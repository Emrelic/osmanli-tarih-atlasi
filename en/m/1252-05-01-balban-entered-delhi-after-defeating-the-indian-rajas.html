Balban entered Delhi after defeating the Indian rajas

1 May 1252

Ulugh Khan Balban defeated the Indian rajas, and especially the strong army of the raja Chahar Deva, and entered Delhi in May 1252; in March 1253 he fell from favor and was forced to withdraw to his iqtas.
