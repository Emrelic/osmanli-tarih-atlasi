Turan Shah II, with the support of the Salghurids, defeated and killed Arslan Shah II and took the Kirman throne

1177

Arslan Shah II, who had come from Yazd and ascended the throne for the third time, died on the battlefield against Turan Shah II, who had obtained the support of the Salghurids (572/1177); the Atabeg Qutb al-Din placed his younger son Yavlak on the throne, and Turan Shah had his eyes put out with a hot iron and took the throne.
