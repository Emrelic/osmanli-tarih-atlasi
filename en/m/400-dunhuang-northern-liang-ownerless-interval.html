Dunhuang: Northern Liang (北涼) → ownerless interval

400

At Dunhuang, ownership passes from Northern Liang (北涼) to the ownerless interval. Transition source information: Jin shu 87 (Liang Wuzhao wang Li Xuansheng/Hao zhuan): in Long'an 4 the Jinchang side summoned six commanderies and chose Li Hao; 'Li Hao was established in Long'an 4, until Song Shaodi Jingping 1' (end of text 423); Wei shu 99 the same. Hou Han/ZZTJ end 421 (Mengxun takes Jiuquan) — the text says 'took Jiuquan' (Jin shu 129) but the year has not been checked.
