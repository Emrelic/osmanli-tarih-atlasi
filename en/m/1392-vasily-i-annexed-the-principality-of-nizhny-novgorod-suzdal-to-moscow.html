Vasily I annexed the Principality of Nizhny Novgorod-Suzdal to Moscow

1392

With a yarlyk obtained from Khan Tokhtamysh, Vasily I attached Nizhny Novgorod, Gorodets and Suzdal to Moscow; Moscow's opening to the Volga and Oka basins began.
