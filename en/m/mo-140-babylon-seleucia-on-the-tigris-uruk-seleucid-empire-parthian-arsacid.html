Babylon, Seleucia (on the Tigris), Uruk: Seleucid Empire → Parthian (Arsacid) Empire

140 BC

At the points Babylon, Seleucia (on the Tigris), Uruk, ownership passes from the Seleucid Empire to the Parthian (Arsacid) Empire. Transition source information: Mithradates I reaches Seleucia in July 141 and Uruk in October 141 | Source: Lendering, J., Livius.org: Mithradates I (Bagayasha page); footnote: van der Spek 1997/8: 171 | https://livius.org/articles/person/bagayasha | «In July 141, he captured Seleucia and in October he reached Uruk in southern Babylonia.»
