Kilij Arslan II takes Niksar from the Danishmendids and adds it to Seljuk rule

1175

In the summer of 1175 Kilij Arslan II seized Sivas, Niksar, Comana, Tokat and the other Danishmendid lands; Niksar, one of the Danishmendid centres where Yaghibasan lay buried in the cemetery of his madrasa, passed into Seljuk hands.
