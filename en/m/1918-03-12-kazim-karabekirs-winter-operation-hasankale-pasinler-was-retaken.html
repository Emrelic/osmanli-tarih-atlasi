Kâzım Karabekir's winter operation: Hasankale (Pasinler) was retaken together with Erzurum

12 March 1918

After the disintegration of the Russian army, the Ottoman forces under Kâzım Karabekir Pasha, who went into operation in the east, retook Erzincan, Erzurum and Hasankale, east of Erzurum, despite the severe winter conditions; the advance extended to Kars on 25 April 1918.
