Theodosius I died; the empire was permanently divided in two, and Egypt remained in the Eastern Roman Empire (Arcadius)

17 January 395

On the death of Theodosius, his sons took over the empire, Arcadius the East and Honorius the West; although Egypt was the principal supplier of Rome's grain needs, it remained a province of the Eastern Empire (Byzantium) and in the following centuries became the grain source of Constantinople.
