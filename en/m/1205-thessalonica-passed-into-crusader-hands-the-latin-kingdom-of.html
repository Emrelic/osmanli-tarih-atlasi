Thessalonica passed into Crusader hands: the Latin Kingdom of Thessalonica

1205

Twenty years after the Fourth Crusade the Crusader army captured Thessalonica and made the city the center of the Latin Kingdom of Thessalonica (1205–1225); the Orthodox churches (St Demetrius, Hagia Sophia) were converted into Catholic churches.
