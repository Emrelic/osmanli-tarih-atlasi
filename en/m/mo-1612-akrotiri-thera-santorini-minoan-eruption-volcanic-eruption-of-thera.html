Akrotiri (Thera/Santorini) — Minoan eruption: volcanic eruption of Thera (Santorini)

1612 BC

Akrotiri (a Thera settlement with Minoan culture) was buried under tephra by the volcanic eruption; the eruption was dated by radiocarbon on an olive branch and on the Akrotiri seed assemblage.
