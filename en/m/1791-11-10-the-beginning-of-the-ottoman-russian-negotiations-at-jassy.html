The beginning of the Ottoman-Russian negotiations at Jassy

10 November 1791

The Ottoman and Russian delegations held their first session at Jassy on 10 November 1791. Sixteen meetings would be held over two months, and the questions of indemnity and frontiers would be settled in these sessions.
