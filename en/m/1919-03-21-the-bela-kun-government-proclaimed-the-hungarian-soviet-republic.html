The Béla Kun government proclaimed the Hungarian Soviet Republic

21 March 1919

When the Entente's Vix note (20 March) demanded the withdrawal of Hungarian troops, the Károlyi government withdrew; the communists, merged with the Social Democrats, founded the Soviet Republic on 21 March 1919. The Republic lasted 133 days (there is also a statement in TDV saying 121 days); the map shows this period under the Hungarian Regency entry.
