The definitive passing of Drežnik and the županija of Drežnik to Ottoman administration

1592

Drežnik, held by the Frankopans since 1323, had been held temporarily by the Ottomans in 1578. In 1592 the fortress and the whole županija of Drežnik definitively came under Ottoman administration; Bihać also fell in the same year. In the eighteenth century Drežnik was attached to the captaincy of Ostrožac.
