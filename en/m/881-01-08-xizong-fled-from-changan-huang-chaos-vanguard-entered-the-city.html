Xizong fled from Chang'an; Huang Chao's vanguard entered the city

8 January 881

Xizong fled through the Jinguang Gate with the 500-man Shence guard under the eunuch Tian Lingzi; toward the afternoon the vanguard general Chai Cun entered Chang'an, and Zhang Zhifang met the rebels at Bashang.
