Harun Bughra Khan took Samarkand; the Qarakhanids entered Transoxiana for the first time

992

Harun Bughra Khan, summoned by the secret agreement of Abu Ali al-Simjuri and Fa'iq al-Khassa, captured Samarkand in early 992 and then marched on Bukhara; he died at Koçkarbaşı on his way back to Kashgar.
