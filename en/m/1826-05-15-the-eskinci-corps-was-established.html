The Eşkinci Corps was established

15 May 1826

Mahmud II had a new unit established, to be drilled in the European manner with soldiers selected from within the Janissary corps. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on the Eşkinci Ocağı, the unit began drill on 12 June 1826, but the Janissaries saw this as the end of their own corps. The rebellion that broke out three days later also ended the short life of the Eşkinci Corps.
