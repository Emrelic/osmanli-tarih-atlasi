Tongguan fell: Ge Shuhan was taken prisoner and the road to Chang'an was opened

10 July 756

Ge Shuhan, commander of the Tang army that had been defeated by Cui Qianyou at Lingbao (6th month, 8th day), had withdrawn to Tongguan; the next day he was taken prisoner by his own officer Huoba Guiren and handed over to the rebels; the pass was left undefended and the capital fell into panic.
