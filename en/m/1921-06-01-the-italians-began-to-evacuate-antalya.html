The Italians began to evacuate Antalya

1 June 1921

According to the TDV article on Antalya, the Italian occupation that had continued since 29 April 1919 ended when the Italians began to evacuate the city on 1 June 1921. Antalya and its surroundings were thus freed from occupation without waiting for the military outcome of the National Struggle.
