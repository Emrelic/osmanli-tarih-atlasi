The Istanbul Observatory was demolished by hatt-ı hümâyun

22 January 1580

Under the influence of a fatwa to the effect that observatories bring ill fortune to countries and of opposition in the palace, the Istanbul Observatory was demolished with all its instruments by a hatt-ı hümâyun dated 4 Dhu al-Hijja 987 (22 January 1580). According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, this was the short-lived end of the institution founded by Taqi al-Din al-Rasid.
