The Annazid Sa'di had the khutba read at Hulwan in the name of Ibrahim Yinal

1 August 1046

After the death of Abu'l-Shawk (April 1046), Sa'di b. Abu'l-Shawk, who had taken offense at his uncle Muhalhil, joined the Seljuk commander Ibrahim Yinal with a group from the Shazanjan tribe and had the khutba read in his name at Hulwan (August 1046); thus the Annazid land was opened to Seljuk influence.
