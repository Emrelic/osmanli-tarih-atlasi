Withdrawal of the Egyptian army from Anatolia completed through Gülek

15 June 1833

After the Kütahya arrangement the Egyptian forces evacuated Anatolia; toward mid-June the whole army crossed through the Gülek Pass into Çukurova. The İçil coast (Mersin, Silifke) returns to Ottoman administration; the muhassıllık of Adana and Syria remain with Egypt.
