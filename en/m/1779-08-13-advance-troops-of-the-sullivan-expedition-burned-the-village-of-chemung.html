Advance troops of the Sullivan expedition burned the village of Chemung (Chemong)

13 August 1779

On the evening of 12 August 1779 the Sullivan army set out from Tioga on a night march and reached the village of Chemung at dawn the next morning; the villagers had fled beforehand. The troops burned about twenty structures in the village and destroyed the surrounding cornfields.
