Selim III was deposed

29 May 1807

Under the pressure of the Kabakçı Mustafa rebellion, Selim III was deposed with a fatwa obtained from the şeyhülislam. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Selim III, this decision was the moment when the reform period of eighteen years effectively ended; the Nizam-ı Cedid army was also abolished within a short time. Selim began to be held under guard in Topkapı Palace.
