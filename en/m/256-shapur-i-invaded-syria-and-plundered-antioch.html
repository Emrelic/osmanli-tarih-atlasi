Shapur I invaded Syria and plundered Antioch

256

Shapur I made a great campaign into Rome's province of Syria, plundered dozens of cities including Antioch, and settled the captives in Iran. The record of this campaign is in the ŠKZ inscription at Naqsh-e Rustam.
