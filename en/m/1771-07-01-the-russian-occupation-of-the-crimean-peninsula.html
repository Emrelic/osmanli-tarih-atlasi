The Russian Occupation of the Crimean Peninsula

1 July 1771

The Russian armies, which had seized Bucak a year earlier, crossed Or Kapı (Perekop) under the command of Prince Dolgorukov and invaded the whole of the Crimean peninsula. The khan was deposed and the pro-Russian Sahib Giray was put at the head, taking the first step toward severing the khanate from the Ottomans. The loss of Crimea, for three centuries the northern shield of the Ottomans, was the beginning of the process of independence in 1774 and of direct annexation in 1783.
