Tarsus surrendered to Byzantium: Cilicia came under Byzantine rule

16 August 965

Mopsuestia (Misis) surrendered in July 965 and Tarsus a month later, on 16 August 965, on humane terms, and again became a Christian city. Thus the region of Cilicia was annexed to Byzantium.
