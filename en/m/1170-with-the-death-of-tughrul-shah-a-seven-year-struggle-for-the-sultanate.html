With the death of Tughrul Shah a seven-year struggle for the sultanate began among his sons; Bahram Shah ascended the throne

1170

The four sons of Tughrul Shah, who died in 565 (1170) — Arslan Shah, Turkan Shah, Turan Shah and Bahram Shah — entered a struggle for the sultanate lasting seven years; the youngest, Bahram Shah, ascended the throne and had Turkan Shah killed; Kirman was devastated and an age of interregnum began.
