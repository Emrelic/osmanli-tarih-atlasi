Pippin donated the Exarchate of Ravenna to the papacy

756

The Frankish king Pippin defeated Aistulf and gave the Exarchate of Ravenna and the Pentapolis to the papacy; thus Ravenna was taken from the Lombards and joined the nucleus of the Papal State. Byzantium was unable to oppose this donation.
