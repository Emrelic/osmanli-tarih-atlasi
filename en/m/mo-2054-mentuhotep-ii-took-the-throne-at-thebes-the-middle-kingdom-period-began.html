Mentuhotep II took the throne at Thebes; the Middle Kingdom period began and war against Herakleopolis intensified

2054 BC

Mentuhotep II's long reign (51 years) is regarded as the beginning of the Middle Kingdom. In the first half of his reign Thebes was at war with Herakleopolis; in his 14th year, when Herakleopolitan forces violated the sacred cemetery at Abydos, the king sent an army north. The actual unification of Egypt came much later, around the 39th year of his reign (the entry below).
