The Fatimid caliph al-Mu'izz appointed Buluggin governor of the Maghrib and settled him in Kairouan; the Zirid period began

972

The Fatimid Caliph al-Mu'izz li-Din Allah appointed Buluggin b. Ziri to the governorship of the Maghrib and settled him in Kairouan (361/972), and left the region after transferring the administrative centre of the state to Egypt.
