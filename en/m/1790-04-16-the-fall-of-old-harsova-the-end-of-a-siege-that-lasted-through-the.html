The fall of Old Hârșova — the end of a siege that lasted through the winter

16 April 1790

After a hard siege that lasted through the winter, Old Hârșova fell into the hands of Austrian forces on 16 April 1790. In the same days the Ottoman side's landing at Calafat from Vidin across the Danube was also repulsed.
