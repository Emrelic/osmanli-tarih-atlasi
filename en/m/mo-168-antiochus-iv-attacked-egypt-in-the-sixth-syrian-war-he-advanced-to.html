Antiochus IV attacked Egypt; in the Sixth Syrian War he advanced to Memphis and Naucratis

168 BC

In response to Ptolemy VI's preparations to recover Coele-Syria, Antiochus IV entered Egypt and came down to the Nile from Pelusium; he defended his claim to Coele-Syria and treated the people well at Naucratis. The war ended a year later with Rome's intervention, without destroying Egypt but weakening the state.
