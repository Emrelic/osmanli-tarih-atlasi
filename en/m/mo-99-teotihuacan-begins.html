Teotihuacan: begins

99 BC

First settlement in the northeast of the Basin of Mexico, the Patlachique phase (100 BCE–1 CE); within a century it becomes a city of ~20,000 inhabitants; Tzacualli phase (1–100 CE) monumental construction.
