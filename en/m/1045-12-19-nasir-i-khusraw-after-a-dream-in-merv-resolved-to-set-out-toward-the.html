Nasir-i Khusraw, after a dream in Merv, resolved to set out toward the qibla

19 December 1045

Nasir-i Khusraw, an official at the Ghaznavid and Seljuk court, through a dream he saw in Jumada II 437 renounced wine and worldly things and resolved to set out in the direction of the qibla. In Sha'ban he asked to be relieved of his post and set out toward Nishapur.
