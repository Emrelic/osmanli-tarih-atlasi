The Fourth Crusade took and sacked Zadar (Zara)

15 November 1202

The Crusaders, unable to pay their debts to Venice, took Zadar (Zara), a Dalmatian port that the Hungarian king had seized a few years earlier, on the proposal of Doge Dandolo in return for the postponement of the debt; the Christian city was sacked on 15 November 1202. This event is the first link of the chain that turned the direction of the campaign toward Constantinople.
