Li Congke burned himself at Luoyang with his family: the Later Tang came to an end

11 January 937

When the Later Jin emperor Shi Jingtang reached Heyang, Li Congke, the last emperor of the Later Tang, burned himself in the early morning hours in the Xuanwu Tower together with his mother, the Empress Dowager Cao, and his family; the Jin emperor entered Luoyang and found his bones among the ashes.
