The Council of State (Şura-yı Devlet) was established

5 March 1868

The Meclis-i Ahkâm-ı Adliyye was divided in two, and the Şûrâ-yı Devlet, handling administrative affairs, and the Dîvân-ı Ahkâm-ı Adliyye, dealing with judicial affairs, were established. According to the TDV article on 'Şûrâ-yı Devlet', Midhat Pasha was appointed to the first presidency of the institution, modelled on the French Conseil d'État; the aim was the separation of the legislative, executive and judiciary from one another.
