The Cham fleet attacked and plundered Angkor by way of the Mekong and the Tonle Sap; the Khmer king Tribhuvanaditya was killed

1177

The Cham king Jaya Indravarman, whose land campaign had been inconclusive, attacked from the sea in 1177; guided by a shipwrecked Chinese, the fleet reached the mouth of the Mekong and from there the Great Lake (Tonle Sap), took Angkor by surprise and plundered it. This catastrophe, which followed twenty years of internal turmoil, shook the Khmer kingdom.
