The Vandals, Alans, and Suebi crossed into Spain: Hispania was partitioned

409

The Vandal, Alan, and Suebic groups who crossed the Pyrenees from Gaul in autumn 409 divided the peninsula among themselves in 411: the Hasdings and Suebi took Gallaecia, the Silings Baetica, and the Alans Lusitania and Carthaginiensis. By crushing the Silings and Alans (416–418), the Visigoths prepared the Vandals' crossing to Africa.
