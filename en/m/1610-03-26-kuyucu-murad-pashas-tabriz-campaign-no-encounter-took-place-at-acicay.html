Kuyucu Murad Pasha's Tabriz campaign — no encounter took place at Acıçay

26 March 1610

The grand vizier Kuyucu Murad Pasha, who had suppressed the Celali revolts and secured calm in Anatolia, marched by way of Erzurum, Hasankale, Chaldiran and Khoy against Shah Abbas, who was reported to be waiting in Tabriz. The two armies came face to face at the locality of Acıçay north of Tabriz, but no battle took place; correspondence continued. With the approach of winter and a shortage of munitions and provisions, the pasha withdrew to Diyarbekir by way of Ahlat, Van and Bitlis, and died there the following summer while preparing a new campaign (5 August 1611).
