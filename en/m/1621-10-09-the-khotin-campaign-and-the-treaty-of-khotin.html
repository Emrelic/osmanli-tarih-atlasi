The Khotin Campaign and the Treaty of Khotin

9 October 1621

The fortified camp that Chodkiewicz established before Khotin could not be overcome by the great army commanded in person by Osman II, and no result was obtained from assaults lasting months. By the treaty signed on 9 October the Dniester river was accepted as the frontier, and the Poles undertook to prevent Cossack raids. Although it was presented as a victory, the campaign, which failed to yield a result, reinforced the young sultan's distrust of the Janissaries and his intention to purge the corps.
