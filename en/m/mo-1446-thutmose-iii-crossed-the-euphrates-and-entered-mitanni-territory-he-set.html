Thutmose III crossed the Euphrates and entered Mitanni territory; he set up a boundary stele in Naharin

1446 BC

In year 33 Thutmose III advanced from Retenu and crossed the Euphrates, plundered the towns of Naharin, and set up a boundary stele east of the river; he placed a second beside his father's stele. This campaign is the farthest point of Egyptian influence in the north and shows the threshold of direct conflict with Mitanni.
