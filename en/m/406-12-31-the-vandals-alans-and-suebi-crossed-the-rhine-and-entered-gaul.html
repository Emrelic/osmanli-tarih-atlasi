The Vandals, Alans, and Suebi crossed the Rhine and entered Gaul

31 December 406

On the traditional date of 31 December 406 (over the frozen Rhine), the Vandals, Alans, and Suebi entered Roman Gaul; the Rhine defense collapsed. Their subsequent crossing into Spain (409) tore away essential provinces of the Western Roman Empire.
