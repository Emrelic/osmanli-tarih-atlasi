The ahidname of the peace with Russia: Kabarda neutral, Azov to be demolished

12 December 1739

The ahidname of the peace with Russia was drawn up on 12 December 1739. The fortifications of Azov were to be demolished and an empty buffer zone left between the two states; Kabarda was to be dependent neither on Russia nor on the Ottomans (art. 6). Özi and Kılburun were left to the Ottomans.
