A Roman army landed in Hispania: the first Roman base at Emporion

217 BC

After Hannibal's march into Italy, Gnaeus Cornelius Scipio landed at Emporion in 218 BC and began Rome's presence in Hispania along the Ebro; the peninsula became the second front of the Second Punic War.
