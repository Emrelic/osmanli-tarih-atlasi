Jin vanguards reached Yangzhou: Gaozong fled from Yangzhou to Zhenjiang

23 February 1129

When news came that the Jin cavalry was approaching, Emperor Gaozong put on his armor and fled in haste to Zhenjiang; the Jin army crossed the Yangzi Bridge the same day, and the next day the vanguards reached Guazhou; as the Taimiao tablets were being moved, the tablet of Taizu was lost; on the 戊辰 day of that month the Jin burned Yangzhou.
