Erlitou (Erlitou culture, Yanshi): Phase IV ends

1519 BC

The type settlement of the Erlitou culture in Yanshi County, Henan; a large center with phases I-IV. Series-fitted 14C dates of the phases: I 1750-1680, II 1680-1610, III 1610-1560, IV 1560-1520 BCE.
