A Mongol caravan of 450 men was killed at Otrar; Ala al-Din Muhammad rejected Genghis Khan's demand for compensation and had the envoys killed

1218

After the killing of a Mongol trade caravan of 450 men at Otrar, Genghis Khan demanded the surrender of the culprits and compensation for the goods; Ala al-Din Muhammad rejected the offer and had the envoys killed (1218). Genghis devoted 1219 to military preparations.
