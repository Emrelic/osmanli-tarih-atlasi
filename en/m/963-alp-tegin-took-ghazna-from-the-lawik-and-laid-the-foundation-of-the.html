Alp Tegin took Ghazna from the Lawik and laid the foundation of the Ghaznavid state

963

Alp Tegin, commander of the Samanid army of Khurasan, failed in 961 in his attempt to place his own candidate on the Samanid throne and withdrew to Ghazna with a small force; he removed the local Lawik dynasty and seized the city (Dhu'l-Hijja 351 / January 963).
