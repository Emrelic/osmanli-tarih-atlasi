Odai Yamamoto I (Aomori) — among the oldest pottery sites in Japan: invention of the oldest pottery (pre-Jomon/early)

13799 BC

At Soto-ga-hama, undecorated pottery sherds (46 pieces) together with stone tools; among the earliest fired clay vessels of the Japanese archipelago and East Asia. The soot/charcoal residue on the sherds was dated by AMS.
