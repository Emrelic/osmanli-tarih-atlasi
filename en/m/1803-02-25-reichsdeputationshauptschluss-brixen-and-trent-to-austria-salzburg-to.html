Reichsdeputationshauptschluss: Brixen and Trent to Austria, Salzburg to the electorate of Ferdinand, Grand Duke of Tuscany

25 February 1803

In accordance with the final decision that abolished the ecclesiastical principalities of the Holy Roman State, the prince-bishoprics of Brixen and Trent were joined to Austria; the Prince-Archbishopric of Salzburg was secularized and given as an electorate to Ferdinand, Grand Duke of Tuscany. By this decision Trento (Bishopric of Trent) passed to Austria.
