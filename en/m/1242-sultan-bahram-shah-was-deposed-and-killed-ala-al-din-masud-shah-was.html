Sultan Bahram Shah was deposed and killed; Ala al-Din Mas'ud Shah was placed on the throne

1242

When Sultan Muizz al-Din Bahram Shah was deposed and killed in 1242, the nobles decided to make Ala al-Din, one of the three princes they had taken from the Qasr-i Safid Prison, sultan with the title Mas'ud Shah.
