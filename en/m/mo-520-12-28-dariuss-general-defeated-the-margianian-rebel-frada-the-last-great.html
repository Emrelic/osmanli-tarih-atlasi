Darius's general defeated the Margianian rebel Frada; the last great uprising was suppressed

28 December 520 BC

Dâdarši, satrap of Bactria, marched 300 km across the Karakum Desert and defeated the Margianian rebel Frada on 28 December 521. In the first regnal year of Darius all the revolts had thus been suppressed in nineteen battles.
