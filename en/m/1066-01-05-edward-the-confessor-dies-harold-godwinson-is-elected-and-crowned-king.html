Edward the Confessor dies; Harold Godwinson is elected and crowned king at London

5 January 1066

Edward the Confessor, who could not attend the consecration of Westminster Abbey, died on 5 January 1066; as he died he recommended Harold as successor. Harold was at once elected and crowned; Northumbria, although it first refused, submitted to him. William, citing Edward's will and Harold's oath of about 1064, claimed the throne and prepared an invasion.
