Russia took Nöteborg (Oreshek) from Sweden — the first Neva fortress of the Great Northern War

22 October 1702

The armies of Peter I besieged and took Nöteborg (Oreshek in Russian), at the outlet of Lake Ladoga, and renamed it Schlüsselburg ('Key Fortress'); this opened the way to the Neva and to the founding of St. Petersburg in 1703.
