The second loss of Belgrade — Prince Eugene's siege

18 August 1717

After the rout at Petrovaradin and the loss of Temesvár, Prince Eugene besieged Belgrade; when the relief army that came to save the city was also routed, the fortress surrendered. The city, which had been retaken in 1690 after Karlowitz, was thus lost again and the Danube line of defense collapsed. Belgrade could be recovered only twenty-two years later, by the 1739 Treaty of Belgrade. Other settlements lost on the same date: Smederevo. The atlas also assigns the areas of Hisarcık (Grocka) and Yagodina (Jagodina) to the Habsburgs on this day (a regional day; the day at place level is not in the source, declared approximation); Mehadia, however, passes to the Habsburgs on 27 August 1717 (Bánlaky 0019/1715).
