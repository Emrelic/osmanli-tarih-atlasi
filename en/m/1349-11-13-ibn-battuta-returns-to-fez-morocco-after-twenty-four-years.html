Ibn Battuta returns to Fez, Morocco, after twenty-four years

13 November 1349

Ibn Battuta, who reaches Morocco by sea and land by way of Tunis and Algeria, appears before the Marinid Sultan Abu Inan; later he will set out on the Andalusian and trans-Saharan Mali journey, and the Rihla will be written in Fez in 1355.
