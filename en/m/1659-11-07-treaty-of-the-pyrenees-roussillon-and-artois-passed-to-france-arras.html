Treaty of the Pyrenees: Roussillon and Artois passed to France: Arras

7 November 1659

The Treaty of the Pyrenees, signed on 7 November 1659 between France and Spain on the Isle of Pheasants (île des Faisans) on the Bidasoa, ceded Roussillon, Northern Cerdagne and Artois to France. Perpignan had been under de facto French occupation since 1642 and Arras since 1640; the atlas writes the break of sovereignty on the day of the treaty.
