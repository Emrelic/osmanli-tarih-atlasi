Seleucus I made a treaty with Chandragupta Maurya in India; Arachosia, Gedrosia and Paropamisadae were ceded to the Mauryas

304 BC

Seleucus marched into India to recover Alexander's Indian satrapies, but came to terms with Chandragupta and exchanged part of his eastern lands for 500 war elephants. The treaty moved the western frontier of the Maurya Empire to the Hindu Kush and supplied Seleucus with elephants for the West.
