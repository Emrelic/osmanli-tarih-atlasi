Northern Kyushu (Uki-Kunden Shell Midden), arrival of rice cultivation: begins

1009 BC

A Bayesian estimate for the arrival of rice cultivation in northern Kyushu in Japan (end of Jomon / beginning of Yayoi); the model's point of origin is the Uki-Kunden Shell Midden (the oldest burnt rice date in the dataset).
