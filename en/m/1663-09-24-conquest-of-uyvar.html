Conquest of Uyvar

24 September 1663

Uyvar (Nové Zámky), the key fortress of northern Hungary, was taken; the expression 'as strong as a Turk before Uyvar' passed into European languages. After Uyvar, Nitra and Léva also surrendered after brief resistance; De Souches retook both in the spring of 1664.
