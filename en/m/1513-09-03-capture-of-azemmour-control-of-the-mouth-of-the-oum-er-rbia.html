Capture of Azemmour: control of the mouth of the Oum Er-Rbia

3 September 1513

The Portuguese army under the command of Jaime, Duke of Braganza, entered Azemmour at the mouth of the Oum Er-Rbia river on 3 September 1513 without encountering resistance. The taking of the city gave control both of the river mouth and of the grain route opening to the interior, and the next year opened the way for the founding of the fortress of Mazagan, 12 km to the southwest. Azemmour remained in Portuguese hands for twenty-eight years.
