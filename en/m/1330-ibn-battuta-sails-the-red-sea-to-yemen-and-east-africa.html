Ibn Battuta sails the Red Sea to Yemen and East Africa

1330

After the pilgrimage he reaches Zabid from Jeddah on a stormy voyage, goes to Aden; from there he sails to Zeila, Mogadishu, Mombasa and Kilwa, and crosses to Hormuz by way of Dhofar.
