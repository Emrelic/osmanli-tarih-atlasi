Annexation of the Teke province: annexation of Antalya

1392

The Teke province on the Mediterranean coast was annexed to Ottoman lands. The Tekeids are a branch separated from the Hamidids: Feleküddin Dündar Bey had left Antalya, which he had conquered, to his brother Yûnus Bey, and around 1321 a separate principality had arisen. For this reason Antalya and its surroundings are shown on the map after 1321 not in the colour of the Hamidids but in that of the Tekeids. The principality was re-established after the Battle of Ankara and was definitively liquidated only in 1423.
