Battle of Salamis: the Greek fleet routed the Persian fleet

479 BC

The Greek fleet under Themistocles defeated the Persian fleet in the narrow strait at Salamis and gained naval supremacy. Xerxes returned to Asia with most of the land army, while Mardonius remained in Greece.
