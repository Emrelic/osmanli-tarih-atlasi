Khanaqin entered the British line

6 December 1917

After the Hamrin operation, the British III Corps settled on 6 December 1917 on an advanced line extending from Khanaqin to Qizil Ribat; Khanaqin remained thereafter in British hands and became the head of the road to Iran.
