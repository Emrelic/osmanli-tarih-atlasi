Alemdar Mustafa Pasha entered Istanbul with his army

19 July 1808

Alemdar Mustafa Pasha, the ayan of Rusçuk, came before Istanbul with his army with the aim of restoring Selim III to the throne. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Alemdar Mustafa Pasha, his entry into the city was received by Mustafa IV himself; however, within a few days the balance of power within the palace would change completely. This entry began the chain of events that resulted both in Selim's death and in a new accession.
