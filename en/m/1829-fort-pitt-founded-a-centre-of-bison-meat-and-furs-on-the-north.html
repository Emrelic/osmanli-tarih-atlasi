Fort Pitt founded — a centre of bison meat and furs on the North Saskatchewan

1829

The Hudson's Bay Company founded Fort Pitt as a provisioning post on the banks of the North Saskatchewan, between Fort Carlton and Fort Edmonton. The post's main trade was in pemmican and other bison products; the Cree, Assiniboine and Blackfoot peoples frequented it.
