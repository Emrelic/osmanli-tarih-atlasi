Kursk liberated by the Red Army

8 February 1943

Kursk was recaptured at the end of the Soviet winter offensive (the Kursk-Kharkov axis that followed the Voronezh-Kastornoye operation); in July of the same year the city's name was given to the Battle of Kursk.
