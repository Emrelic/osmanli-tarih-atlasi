At Mignano Pope Innocent II recognises Roger II's kingdom

25 July 1139

After Anacletus's death in January 1138, Roger recognised Innocent II; on 25 July 1139 at Mignano (near Montecassino) the pope invested Roger as king of Sicily, prince of Capua and duke of Apulia, and confirmed the sub-fiefs granted to his sons. Roger declared himself a vassal of the papacy.
