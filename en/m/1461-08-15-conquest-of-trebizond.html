Conquest of Trebizond

15 August 1461

At the end of the arduous Black Sea campaign that the Conqueror conducted simultaneously with army and fleet, the Empire of Trebizond surrendered; with the liquidation of this last remnant of the Byzantine world in Anatolia, the entire southern coast of the Black Sea came under Ottoman dominion. The ending of the Komnenos dynasty strengthened the Conqueror's claim to be 'ruler of two seas and two lands'. Other places annexed on the same date: Giresun, Rize.
