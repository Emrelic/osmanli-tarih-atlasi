Süleyman the Lawgiver Set Out on the German Campaign — A Challenge to Charles V

25 April 1532

After the inconclusive outcome of the siege of Vienna and Ferdinand's renewed siege of Buda, the sultan set out from Istanbul, this time to draw the emperor Charles V himself into a pitched battle. In August the army would be delayed by the resistance of the small fortress of Güns, and since the emperor did not come out to fight, the campaign would remain limited to raids. The campaign prepared the ground for the first peace made with the Habsburgs the following year.
