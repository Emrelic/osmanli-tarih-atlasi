Karaferye (Veria) came under Ottoman rule

8 May 1387

In the same year as the surrender of Salonica in 1387, Karaferye, in the interior of Lower Macedonia, also came under Ottoman rule. The city was the junction point of the road between Salonica and Thessaly.
