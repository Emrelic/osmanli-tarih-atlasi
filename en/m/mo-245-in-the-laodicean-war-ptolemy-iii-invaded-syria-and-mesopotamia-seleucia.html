In the Laodicean War Ptolemy III invaded Syria and Mesopotamia; Seleucia Pieria passed into Ptolemaic hands

245 BC

Ptolemy II died on 28 January 246 BCE; Ptolemy III attacked the Seleucid state in order to protect his sister Berenice. He was welcomed at Seleucia Pieria and at Antioch; the Babylonian Chronicle (BCHP 11) reports that his army reached Babylon from December 246 BCE. The Adulis inscription claims that he conquered the lands west of the Euphrates, Cilicia, Pamphylia and Thrace. Seleucia Pieria remained in Ptolemaic hands for many years.
