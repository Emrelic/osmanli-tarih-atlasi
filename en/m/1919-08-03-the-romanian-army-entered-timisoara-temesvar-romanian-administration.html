The Romanian army entered Timișoara (Temeşvar) — Romanian administration was established in the city

3 August 1919

On the morning of 3 August 1919, the first units of the Romanian army under the command of Colonel Virgil Economu entered Timișoara, which Serbian troops had entered on 14 November 1918 and French troops in early December, and Romanian administration was established in the city.
