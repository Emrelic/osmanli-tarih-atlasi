Ibrahim Shah of the Sutayids took control of Diyarbekir

1343

After the Ilkhanate collapsed, Ibrahim Shah of the Sutayids took control of the Diyarbekir region, which had been the scene of struggles between the Jalayirids and the Chobanids; his rule lasted until 1353.
