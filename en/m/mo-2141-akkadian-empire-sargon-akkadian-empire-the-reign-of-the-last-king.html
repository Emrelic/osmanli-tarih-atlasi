Akkadian Empire (Sargon): Akkadian Empire: the reign of the last king Shudurul ends

2141 BC

Sargon (Šarru-kīn) defeats Lugalzagesi and founds the first extensive Mesopotamian empire centered on Akkade; its peak in the time of Naram-Suen; the last ruler is Shudurul.
