Meeting at the Gökmeydan in Aleppo with the Abbasid Caliph al-Mutawakkil III

29 August 1516

The Abbasid caliph of Cairo, al-Mutawakkil III, who had fallen prisoner at Marj Dabiq together with the Mamluk army, was brought before the sultan at the Gökmeydan in Aleppo, and Selim the Grim showed him great respect. The caliphate of Cairo, a symbolic office in the shadow of the Mamluk sultans since 1261, passed in practice under Ottoman protection with this meeting. The caliph would later be taken to Istanbul with the sultan.
