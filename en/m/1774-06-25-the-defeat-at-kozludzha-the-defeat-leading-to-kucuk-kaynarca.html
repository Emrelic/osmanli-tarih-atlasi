The Defeat at Kozludzha — The Defeat Leading to Küçük Kaynarca

25 June 1774

The Ottoman forces gathered before Shumla were defeated at Kozludzha by the combined columns of Suvorov and Kamensky, and the greater part of the army was dispersed. With this defeat there was no force left to resist in the Balkans; the Russian vanguards besieged Shumla and the grand vizier's headquarters came under threat. The sitting down at the table at Küçük Kaynarca and the acceptance of extraordinarily heavy terms before a month had passed are a direct result of this defeat.
