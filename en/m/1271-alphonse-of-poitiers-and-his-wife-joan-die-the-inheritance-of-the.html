Alphonse of Poitiers and his wife Joan die: the inheritance of the county of Toulouse passes to the French crown

1271

When Raymond VII died in 1249, the inheritance fell to his daughter Joan; Joan was married to Alphonse, brother of Louis IX. With the deaths of the two in 1271, the extensive inheritance of the counts of Toulouse passed to the French crown.
