Babadag and its surroundings pass to the Ottomans: Mircea and his son Mihail defeated

1416

After defeating the Voivode of Wallachia Mircea and his son Mihail, Çelebi Mehmed brought Babadag and its surroundings in Northern Dobruja under Ottoman rule. Babadag was a town bearing the name of Saltuk Baba and founded with a thirteenth-century Turkmen settlement. The surrounding Silistra, Constanța and the other Dobruja fortresses, however, remained in Wallachia until 1419; that is, Babadag appears for three years as an island (exclave) cut off from Ottoman land.
