Mehmed the Conqueror assigns Okmeydanı to the archers

1 June 1453

Mehmed the Conqueror, who had set up his headquarters on the Kasımpaşa ridges during the conquest of Constantinople, had also used the place as a training ground for archers; after taking the city he permanently assigned the area to the archers. This assignment is the beginning of Okmeydanı becoming the center of the Ottoman archery tradition that would last for centuries.
