Knud IV (Saint Knud) is murdered in St Alban's Church at Odense

10 July 1086

Knud IV, king of Denmark since 1080, was murdered on 10 July 1086 in St Alban's Church at Odense while preparing a new expedition to England; he was canonised in 1100.
