Damascus surrendered

16 June 1832

The city surrendered without fighting. Ibrahim Pasha tried to win local support by proclaiming a tax order and the equality granted to non-Muslims; the same policies would a few years later be the cause of revolts against him. Other places joined in the same days: Beirut, Tripoli of Syria, Sidon.
