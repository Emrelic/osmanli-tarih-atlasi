Debrecen Declaration of Independence — the Habsburg dynasty was dethroned, Kossuth became head of state

14 April 1849

In the Great Reformed Church the Hungarian Diet declared that the Habsburg-Lorraine dynasty had been dethroned and that Hungary was an independent state; Kossuth was elected 'governor-president'.
