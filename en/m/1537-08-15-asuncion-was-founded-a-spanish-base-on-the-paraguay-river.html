Asunción was founded — a Spanish base on the Paraguay River

15 August 1537

After the Río de la Plata expedition failed to hold on at Buenos Aires, the Spaniards withdrew upriver, and Juan de Salazar de Espinosa founded Nuestra Señora de la Asunción, relying on the alliance formed with the Guaraní communities. For a century the town remained the centre of Spanish rule in the Río de la Plata basin.
