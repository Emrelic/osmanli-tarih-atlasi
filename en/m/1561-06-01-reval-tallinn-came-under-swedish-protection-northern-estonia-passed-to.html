Reval (Tallinn) came under Swedish protection — Northern Estonia passed to Sweden

1 June 1561

As the Livonian Confederation disintegrated, the city council of Reval offered allegiance to King Eric XIV of Sweden in 1561; Northern Estonia remained with Sweden until the Great Northern War (1721).
