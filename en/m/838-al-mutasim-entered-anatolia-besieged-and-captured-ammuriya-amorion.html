Al-Mu'tasim entered Anatolia, besieged and captured Ammuriya (Amorion)

838

Caliph al-Mu'tasim entered Anatolia in 838 with a large army; marching by way of Ankara, he besieged and captured Ammuriya, one of the great cities of Anatolia, near Afyon.
