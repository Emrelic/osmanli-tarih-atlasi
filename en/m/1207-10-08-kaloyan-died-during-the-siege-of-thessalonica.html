Kaloyan died during the siege of Thessalonica

8 October 1207

The Bulgarian Tsar Kaloyan died on 8 October 1207, in a manner still unexplained, while making a new assault on Thessalonica. With this death the harsh period of Bulgarian-Latin conflicts closed; the throne of Tarnovo passed to Boril.
