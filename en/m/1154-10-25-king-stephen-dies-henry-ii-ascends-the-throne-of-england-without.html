King Stephen dies; Henry II ascends the throne of England without encountering any resistance

25 October 1154

In 1153 Henry had wrung from Stephen, with the help of the Church and the partisans of his mother Matilda, the recognition of his right of succession to England; when Stephen died on 25 October 1154, he put forward his claim without opposition. The Angevin Empire united the land extending from England to the Pyrenees.
