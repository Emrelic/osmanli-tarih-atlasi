Battle of Adrianople: Kaloyan defeated and captured the Latin Emperor Baldwin

15 April 1205

During the uprisings that broke out upon the capture of Constantinople by the Latins, the Latin army before Adrianople was routed by the joint Byzantine-Bulgarian forces; Emperor Baldwin was captured and taken to Tarnovo. This defeat was the first great blow to the Latin Empire.
