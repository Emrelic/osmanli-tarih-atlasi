Adranos (Orhaneli) campaign — Osman Bey's last campaign

1 September 1323

TDV, Orhan entry records the campaign against Adranos Castle in the autumn of 1323 as Osman Bey's last campaign. The tekfur of Adranos had been a member of the alliance of tekfurs formed against the Ottomans at Dimbos twenty years earlier; this campaign meant the settling of that account. The exact day of the fortress's conquest is unknown; the operation was continued by Orhan Bey.
