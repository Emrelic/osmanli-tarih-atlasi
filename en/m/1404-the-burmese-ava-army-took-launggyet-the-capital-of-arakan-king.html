The Burmese (Ava) army took Launggyet, the capital of Arakan; King Narameikhla fled to Bengal

1404

The 1404 campaign of Prince Minrekyawswa on behalf of King Minkhaung of Ava entered Arakan and occupied Launggyet; the Arakan king Narameikhla fled to Bengal (Gaur), and the Burmese left Anawrahtaminsaw in his place.
