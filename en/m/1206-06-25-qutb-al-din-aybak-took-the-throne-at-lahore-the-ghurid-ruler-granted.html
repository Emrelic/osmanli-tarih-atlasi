Qutb al-Din Aybak took the throne at Lahore; the Ghurid ruler granted him the title of sultan

25 June 1206

On the death of the Ghurid Muizz al-Din, Aybak took the throne at Lahore with the title 'Qutb al-Din'. The Ghurid ruler Ghiyath al-Din Mahmud confirmed this de facto situation and sent him the title of sultan and a parasol (chatr) as the emblem of sovereignty. In Aybak's time the borders of the sultanate extended from the Punjab to Bihar and from Kashmir to the central Indian plateau.
