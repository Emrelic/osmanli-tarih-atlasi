Anchorage founded as the center of the Alaska Railroad

1915

After Congress passed the Alaska Railroad Act on 12 March 1914, the Alaska Engineering Commission established the railroad construction camp at Ship Creek in the spring of 1915; a tent city arose on the shore, and in April 1915 the post office gave the new town the name Anchorage. The city became the center of the Alaska Railroad extending north to Fairbanks.
