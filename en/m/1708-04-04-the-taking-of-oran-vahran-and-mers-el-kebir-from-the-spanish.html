The Taking of Oran (Vahran) and Mers el-Kébir from the Spanish

4 April 1708

The forces of the beylerbeyi of the Ocak of Algiers captured Oran, which had been in the hands of a Spanish garrison for two centuries, and its port Mers el-Kébir, after a long siege. It was the greatest success won against Spain by the Western ocaks under Ottoman protection in the Western Mediterranean, and it secured the western flank of Algiers. The city would be occupied by the Spanish again in 1732, but would be definitively retaken in 1792.
