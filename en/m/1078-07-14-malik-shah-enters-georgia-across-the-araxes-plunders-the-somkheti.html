Malik-Shah enters Georgia across the Araxes, plunders the Somkheti region and takes Ioane captive

14 July 1078

When King Giorgi II defeated Savtegin and recovered some places, Sultan Malik-Shah again set out on campaign and entered Georgia across the Araxes (471/1078-79); he plundered the Somkheti region and took Ioane captive, and returned, leaving Savtegin there with reinforcements. The emir Ahmad inflicted a heavy defeat on the king at Kouel and retook Kars.
