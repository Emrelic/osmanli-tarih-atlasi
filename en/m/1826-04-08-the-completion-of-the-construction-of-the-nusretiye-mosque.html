The completion of the construction of the Nusretiye Mosque

8 April 1826

The construction of the sultanic mosque that Mahmud II had built at Tophane was finished on 8 April 1826; the building replaced the mosque of the Tophane Barracks that was reduced to ashes in the great fire of 24 February 1823, and its construction had begun in June 1823. Its architect is Krikor Amira Kalfa of the Balyan family; it is regarded as the most showy example of the Empire style in Ottoman mosque architecture. On 14 May 1826 the minarets were demolished as far as the lower balcony and rebuilt so as to be higher; the name "Nusretiye" (victory) of the mosque was given in commemoration of the abolition of the Janissary Corps in June of the same year, but it is known among the people as the Tophane Mosque.
