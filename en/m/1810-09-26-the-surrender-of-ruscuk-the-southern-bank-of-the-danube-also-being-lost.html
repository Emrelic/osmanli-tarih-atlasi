The surrender of Rusçuk — the southern bank of the Danube also being lost

26 September 1810

Rusçuk resisted the long and bloody siege of the Russian army under Count Kamensky; it surrendered on condition that the garrison and the civilian population leave the city in safety. The fall of the strongest fortress-city on the southern bank of the Danube turned the course of the war in Russia's favour.
