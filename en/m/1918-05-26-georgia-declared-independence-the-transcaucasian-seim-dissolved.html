Georgia declared independence — the Transcaucasian Seim dissolved

26 May 1918

Georgia, emerging from the Transcaucasian Seim formed after Brest-Litovsk, declared independence and the Democratic Republic of Georgia was founded. Together with Kutaisi, western Georgia came under the administration of this new state. With this declaration Sukhumi, Poti, Zugdidi, Gori, Telavi and Dusheti passed to the Democratic Republic of Georgia.
