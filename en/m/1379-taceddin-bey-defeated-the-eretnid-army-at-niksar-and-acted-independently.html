Taceddin Bey defeated the Eretnid army at Niksar and acted independently

1379

In the spring of 1379, Taceddin Bey defeated the forces of Qadi Burhan al-Din and of the Eretnid Bey Alaeddin Ali, who had attacked Niksar. He ceased paying taxes to the Eretnid Beylik and acted independently from the year 781 (1379).
