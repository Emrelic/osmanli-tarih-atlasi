The first Althing of Iceland convenes at Þingvellir

930

The Althing, which according to tradition was founded around 930, was Iceland's national assembly and judicial body; according to tradition, the 'Ulvljots-lovene', modelled on the Gulating law, was adopted at the first Althing and the first lawspeaker (lovsigemann), Ravn Høngsson, was elected. The Landnám (settlement) period extends roughly from 870 to 930.
