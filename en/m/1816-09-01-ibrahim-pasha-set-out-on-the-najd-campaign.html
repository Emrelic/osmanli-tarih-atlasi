Ibrahim Pasha set out on the Najd campaign

1 September 1816

Ibrahim Pasha, who took over the command upon the death of his brother Tosun, embarked on a heavy march in the desert that lasted two years. The army, advancing well by well and eliminating the tribes one by one, closed in on the Saudi capital.
