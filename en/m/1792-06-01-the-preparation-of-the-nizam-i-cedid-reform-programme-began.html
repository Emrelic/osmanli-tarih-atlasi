The preparation of the Nizam-ı Cedid reform programme began

1 June 1792

Selim III had a comprehensive programme of renewal prepared in line with the memoranda he requested from statesmen and foreign experts. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Nizâm-ı Cedîd, this programme was not limited to founding a new army; it turned into a wide reform movement also covering finance, provincial administration and diplomacy. The programme would find both supporters and opponents over the following fifteen years.
