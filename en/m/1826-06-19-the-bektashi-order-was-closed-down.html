The Bektashi order was closed down

19 June 1826

Simultaneously with the abolition of the Janissary Corps, the Bektashi order, known for its religious ties with the corps, was also banned. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Bektaşîlik, while some sheikhs of minor lodges were executed, the sheikh of the Hacı Bektaş lodge was exiled to Amasya and the lodges were handed over to Naqshbandi administration. The order, despite this heavy blow, continued its organisation secretly.
