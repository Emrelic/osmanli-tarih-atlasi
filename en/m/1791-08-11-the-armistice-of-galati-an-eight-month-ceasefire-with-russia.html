The Armistice of Galați — an eight-month ceasefire with Russia

11 August 1791

An eight-month armistice between the Ottoman State and Russia was signed on 11 August 1791; the documents were ratified the same day under the heading 'essential article and armistice'. The fighting on the Russian front, which had lasted four years, thus ceased, and the framework of the peace that would be signed at Jassy five months later was established.
