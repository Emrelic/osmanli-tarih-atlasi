Heraclius overthrew Phocas and ascended the throne

5 October 610

Heraclius, son of the exarch of Africa, came to Constantinople with the fleet; Phocas was seized and killed on the morning of 5 October 610, and Heraclius became emperor. His reign is remembered for the Persian war, the Arab conquests and the foundations of the 'theme' order.
