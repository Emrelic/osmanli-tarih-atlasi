Ashurnasirpal II crossed the Euphrates and took tribute from Carchemish and the Neo-Hittite/Aramaean states

876 BC

On his western campaign Ashurnasirpal II crossed the Euphrates, reached Carchemish, Patina and the Phoenician coast, and collected tribute. This campaign marks the beginning of the process by which the Neo-Hittite and Aramaean city-states were bound to Assyria.
