Annexation of the Touat, Gourara and Tidikelt oases to France

21 February 1901

The belt of oases of Touat, Gourara and Tidikelt in southwestern Algeria was officially attached to France on 21 February 1901. This junction of caravan routes, over which the Moroccan sultan nominally claimed rights — including Adrar, Timimoun, Reggane and Beni Abbès — was thus joined to Algeria; this is the beginning of the border dispute between Morocco and French Algeria.
