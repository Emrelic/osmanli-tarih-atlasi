Navarino raid

20 October 1827

The Ottoman-Egyptian fleet, which was suppressing the Greek revolt, was burned at Navarino by the combined British-French-Russian fleet, which entered the harbour without a declaration of war; nearly sixty ships and thousands of sailors were lost. The raid, which European diplomacy brushed aside as an 'untoward event', effectively guaranteed Greek independence and opened the way to the Ottoman-Russian war of 1828–29.
