Ottoman army crossed the Euphrates

21 April 1839

Mahmud II drove his army to the Syrian frontier to recover what had been lost at Kütahya. The Prussian officer Moltke, who was with the army as adviser, objected to the choice of position and to the early attack; his objections were not heeded.
