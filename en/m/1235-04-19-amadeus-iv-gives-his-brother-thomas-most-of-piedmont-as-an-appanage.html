Amadeus IV gives his brother Thomas most of Piedmont as an appanage

19 April 1235

After a quarrel with his brothers, Amadeus IV on 19 April 1235 invested his brother Thomas (Thomas II, count of Flanders) as lord of the whole of Piedmont from Avigliana downwards; this is the foundation of the Piedmont branch of the House of Savoy.
