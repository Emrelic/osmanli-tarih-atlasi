The taking over of Alexandria from the French — Menou's capitulation

31 August 1801

General Menou, commander-in-chief of the Army of the Orient, was besieged in Alexandria on 17 August; on 21 August the British took the fort of Marabout near the city, and on 31 August Menou signed the capitulation of the army. The return of the French troops to France began on 2 September. Thus the occupation that had begun with the landing at Alexandria in June 1798 ended in the city where it had begun.
