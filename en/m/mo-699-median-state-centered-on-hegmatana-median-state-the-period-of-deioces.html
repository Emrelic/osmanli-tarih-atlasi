Median State (centered on Hegmatana): Median state: the period of Deioces begins (Herodotean tradition)

699 BC

The political unity/state of the Medes: the Herodotean tradition begins it with Deioces (c. 700-647); the alternative Grantovskiy chronology, based on Assyrian sources, places the Median revolt at c. 672 and Deioces at c. 672/671. The last Median king Astyages was overthrown by Cyrus in 550 BCE (Median-Persian war 553-550).
