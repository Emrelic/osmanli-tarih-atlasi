The Cimmerians sacked Sardis; Gyges died

651 BC

The Cimmerian raid, after Gordion (696/695), also reached Lydia and sacked the lower city of Sardis. Assyrian sources report that the Lydian king Gugu (Gyges) sought Assyrian help against the Cimmerians; Gyges died during or after these wars.
