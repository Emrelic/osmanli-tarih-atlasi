Tibetan armies entered Chang'an; they placed the puppet emperor Cheng Hong on the throne

18 November 763

After the Tibetan army seized Jingzhou and descended on the capital region, Emperor Daizong fled to Shaanzhou; the Tibetans entered Chang'an, proclaimed Prince Cheng Hong emperor, and plundered the treasury. The forces that Guo Ziyi rallied at Shangzhou and the resistance begun by the youths of Chang'an, who beat drums on Zhuque Avenue, shook the Tibetan army.
