Dawud, Artuqid ruler of Hisn Kayfa, dies; his son Fakhr al-Din Qara Arslan succeeds him

1 August 1144

Dawud, who had allied with the Mardin Artuqid Timurtash against Zengi, Atabeg of Mosul, died on 1 August 1144; his son Fakhr al-Din Qara Arslan came to an understanding with the Anatolian Seljuk Sultan Mas'ud I against Zengi.
