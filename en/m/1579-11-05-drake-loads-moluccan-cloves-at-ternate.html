Drake loads Moluccan cloves at Ternate

5 November 1579

The Golden Hind, having crossed the Pacific, arrived at Ternate on 5 November 1579; trade was carried on with the Sultan. Then a grounding occurred on the Celebes shoals (10 January 1580).
