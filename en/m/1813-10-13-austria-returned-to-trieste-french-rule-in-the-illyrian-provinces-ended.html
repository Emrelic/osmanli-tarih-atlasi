Austria returned to Trieste: French rule in the Illyrian Provinces ended

13 October 1813

Austrian troops and the British fleet entered Trieste in October 1813; according to Treccani, the city returned to Austria on 13 October 1813. The French garrison withdrew to the castle of San Giusto, the terms of capitulation were signed on 29 October, and it left the castle on 8 November. The map shows the city's return (13 October).
