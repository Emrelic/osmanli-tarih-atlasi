Nanda dynasty (Magadha): Dhana Nanda in Magadha (age of Alexander)

325 BC

The nine Nandas (Mahapadma and eight sons) in Magadha; the last (Dhana Nanda) was on the throne in 326 BC; in ~321 BC sovereignty passed to the Maurya. The date of the founding is not given in the source.
