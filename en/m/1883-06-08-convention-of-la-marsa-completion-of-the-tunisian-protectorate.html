Convention of La Marsa: completion of the Tunisian protectorate

8 June 1883

The Treaty of Bardo (Qasr al-Said) of 12 May 1881 had left Tunisia's foreign affairs to France but the internal administration to the bey; two years later the Convention of La Marsa, signed on 8 June 1883, also placed financial and administrative reforms under French control and completed the protectorate. The Ottoman State maintained its claim of sovereignty over Tunisia and never recognized the occupation, but could take no actual counter-step. Thus Tunisia, an Ottoman ocak since 1574, formally came under French protection.
