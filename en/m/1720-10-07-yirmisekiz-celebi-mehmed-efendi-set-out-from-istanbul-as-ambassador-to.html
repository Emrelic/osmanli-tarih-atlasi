Yirmisekiz Çelebi Mehmed Efendi set out from Istanbul as ambassador to France

7 October 1720

Mehmed Efendi, commissioned by Ahmed III and Damad İbrahim Pasha, set out with his son Said Efendi on the merchant galleon allotted by the French ambassador. Because of the epidemic in Marseille, the route was changed to Toulon, then to an overland and canal route through France.
