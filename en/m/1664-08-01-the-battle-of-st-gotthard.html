The Battle of St. Gotthard

1 August 1664

The Ottoman forces that crossed the Raab river were stopped by the allied European army under Montecuccoli and suffered heavy losses. The battle is regarded as a turning point in military history in that it showed clearly for the first time the superiority of the musket fire of disciplined infantry over the Ottoman cavalry charge. Despite the defeat, the Ottomans managed to obtain advantageous terms in the Treaty of Vasvár signed nine days later.
