The Hadramawt coast (Mukalla) came under British protection

1 May 1888

A protection treaty was signed between Britain and the Yafi'i (Kuaiti) rulers, who controlled the Hadramawt coast including Shihr and Mukalla; the British assumed complete responsibility for the external relations of the Yafi'i administration on the coast.
