Battle of Cumae (Kyme): Syracuse defeated the Etruscan fleet, and Etruscan sea power was broken

473 BC

At the call for help of Cumae, Hieron I, Tyrant of Syracuse, routed the Etruscan fleet off Cumae; Hieron celebrated the victory by dedicating a helmet at Delphi (the Etruscan helmet mentioned by Livy). Etruscan dominance in Campania was shaken.
