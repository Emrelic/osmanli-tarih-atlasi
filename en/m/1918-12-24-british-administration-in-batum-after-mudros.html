British administration in Batum after Mudros

24 December 1918

Under the Armistice of Mudros of 30 October 1918, the Ottoman State was obliged to withdraw from Batum; the city was held by British units on 24 December 1918. Batum, made the centre of an independent sancak in April 1918, remained under British control from this date until July 1920.
