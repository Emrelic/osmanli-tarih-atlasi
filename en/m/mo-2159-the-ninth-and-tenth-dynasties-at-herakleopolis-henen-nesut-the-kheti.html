The Ninth and Tenth Dynasties at Herakleopolis (Henen-nesut): the Kheti dynasty ruled Northern Egypt

2159 BC

In the First Intermediate Period the dynasty at Herakleopolis filled the vacuum left by Memphis; loyal nomarchs such as Tefibi, governor of Asyut in Middle Egypt, fought on the side of this kingdom against the south (Thebes). The Asyut tomb inscription describes the advance of the Southerners north from Elephantine and their being repulsed as far as the 'southern boundary'. Egypt was thus divided into two main powers, Herakleopolis (North) and Thebes (South).
