After Ipsus, Ptolemy occupied Coele-Syria; Seleucus objected (the root of the Syrian Wars)

300 BC

After the Battle of Ipsus (301 BCE), Seleucus, Lysimachus and Cassander agreed and gave all of Syria to Seleucus; Ptolemy, however, occupied Coele-Syria with his army and kept it. This dispute gave rise to six Syrian Wars over the next two centuries. Antiochus III later defended this claim, as Polybius reports.
