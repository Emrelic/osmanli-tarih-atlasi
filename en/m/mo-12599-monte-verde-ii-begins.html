Monte Verde II: begins

12599 BC

An open-air camp preserved under peat on the banks of the Chinchihuapi Creek in south-central Chile (MV-II); it is the main reference site for the early settlement of the Americas. Because this was a short-term camp, the interval is an 'uncertainty of the time of occurrence', not the duration of settlement.
