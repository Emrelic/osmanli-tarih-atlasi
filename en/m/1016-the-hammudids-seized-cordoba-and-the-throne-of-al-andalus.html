The Hammudids seized Cordoba and the throne of al-Andalus

1016

The Shi'i Hammudids, who claimed descent from Ali, took advantage of the impotence of the Umayyad dynasty to seize Cordoba and the throne of the caliphate in 1016; Cordoba remained under their rule between 1016 and 1022.
