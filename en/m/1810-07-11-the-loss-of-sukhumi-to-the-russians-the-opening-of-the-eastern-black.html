The loss of Sukhumi to the Russians — the opening of the eastern Black Sea coast

11 July 1810

In the Ottoman-Russian war of 1806-1812 the Russian fleet turned toward the eastern Black Sea coast and took Sohumkale, on which Ottoman domination in Abkhazia rested. The fortress was the centre of contact with the Caucasian tribes and of coastal trade. With its loss Russia settled permanently on the eastern shore of the Black Sea for the first time; Ottoman influence in the Caucasus was squeezed into the area around Anapa and Poti.
