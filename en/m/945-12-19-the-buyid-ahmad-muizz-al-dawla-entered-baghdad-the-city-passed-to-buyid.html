The Buyid Ahmad (Muʿizz al-Dawla) entered Baghdad; the city passed to Buyid rule

19 December 945

Ahmad entered Baghdad on 11 Jumādā I 334 (19 December 945); the caliph al-Mustakfī made him amīr al-umarāʾ, and the city passed to the rule of the Buyids (Kufa also came under Buyid rule (945-1055); for Kufa the day of neighbouring Baghdad was taken as the basis).
