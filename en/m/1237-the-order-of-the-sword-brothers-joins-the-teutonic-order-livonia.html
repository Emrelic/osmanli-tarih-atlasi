The Order of the Sword Brothers joins the Teutonic Order; Livonia becomes a province of the order

1237

In 1237 the Order of the Sword Brothers was merged with the Teutonic Order; Livonia became a province with its own master, subordinate to the grand master. Two years earlier the Knights of Dobrzyń had also joined the order.
