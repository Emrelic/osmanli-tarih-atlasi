Japanese forces occupied the undefended island of Labuan — British Malaya (Straits Settlements) lost its island in northern Borneo

1942

During the Borneo campaign, a small Japanese force landed on the undefended island of Labuan and seized it; the island remained under Japanese military occupation from January 1942 to September 1945. The day varies in the sources as 1 or 3 January.
