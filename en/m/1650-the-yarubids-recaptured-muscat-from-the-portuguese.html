The Ya'rubids recaptured Muscat from the Portuguese

1650

Nasir ibn Murshid, leader of the Ya'rubid dynasty of Oman, recaptured Muscat from the Portuguese in 1650; this Omani port, held by Portugal since 1507, passed to the Ya'rubids.
