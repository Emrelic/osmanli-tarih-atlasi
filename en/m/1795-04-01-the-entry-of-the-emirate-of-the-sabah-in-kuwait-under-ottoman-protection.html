The entry of the emirate of the Sabah in Kuwait under Ottoman protection

1 April 1795

According to the TDV article on Kuwait, the harbour had been founded by the Utub tribe coming from Najd at the beginning of the 18th century, and the Sabah family had taken over the administration toward the end of the century. In the same years that al-Hasa passed into Saudi hands, the emirate, which had no choice but to lean on Basra in the face of Wahhabi pressure, came under Ottoman protection. The tie would be formalised in 1871 by Midhat Pasha's Basra arrangement, and Kuwait would be counted as one of the three kazas of the sancak of Basra; Iraq's later territorial claim also rests on this record.
