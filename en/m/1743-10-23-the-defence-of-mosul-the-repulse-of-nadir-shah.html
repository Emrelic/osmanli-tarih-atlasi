The Defence of Mosul — The Repulse of Nadir Shah

23 October 1743

Nadir Shah, who came before Mosul after taking Kirkuk and Erbil, bombarded the city for forty days and mounted successive general assaults. The defence conducted by the governor Hüseyin Pasha al-Jalili, who also armed the townspeople, compelled the Iranian army to lift the siege with heavy losses. Mosul's holding out prevented the loss of the whole of Iraq and consolidated the influence of the Jalili family in the city for a century.
