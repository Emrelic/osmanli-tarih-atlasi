Governor Otermín abandoned Santa Fe and marched toward Isleta

21 August 1680

After a nine-day siege, Governor Antonio de Otermín ordered the abandonment of Santa Fe on 21 August 1680 and that same morning led the Spanish inhabitants on a march toward Isleta; Santa Fe fell into the hands of the rebel Pueblo forces.
