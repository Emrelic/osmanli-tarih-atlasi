Mieszko I accepts Christianity; the beginning of the Polish state

966

Mieszko I, the first historical ruler of the Piast dynasty, accepted Christianity according to the Latin rite in 966 (according to TDV, from 967); the first bishopric was founded at Posen (Poznań) in 968 at the latest.
