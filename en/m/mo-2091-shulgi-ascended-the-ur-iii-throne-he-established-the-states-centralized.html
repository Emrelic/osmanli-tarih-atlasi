Shulgi ascended the Ur III throne; he established the state's centralized administrative system

2091 BC

Shulgi (c. 2094-2047), son of Ur-Nammu, centralized the Ur III state during his 48-year reign: a system of tax and livestock deliveries (Puzrish-Dagan), military reform, city governorships and a new calendar. Ur III is regarded as the bureaucratically most developed early state in Mesopotamian history.
