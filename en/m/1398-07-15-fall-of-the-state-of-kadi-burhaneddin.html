Fall of the State of Kadı Burhaneddin

15 July 1398

Kadı Burhaneddin, who fell prisoner to the Aqquyunlu ruler Qarayuluk Osman Bey, offered to be released in return for the surrender of Sivas, but the people of the city resisted, and the sultan was executed before the walls. Although the notables of Sivas installed his son Alaeddin Ali Çelebi at the head, in the face of Aqquyunlu pressure they asked help from Bayezid the Thunderbolt; the Ottoman forces under the command of Süleyman Çelebi defeated Qarayuluk and became master of the city. The State of Kadı Burhaneddin thus passed into history, and Sivas, Tokat, Niksar and Kayseri were joined to Ottoman lands. Other places joined on the same date: Kırşehir, Çorum.
