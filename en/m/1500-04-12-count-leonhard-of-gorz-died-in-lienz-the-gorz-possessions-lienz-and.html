Count Leonhard of Görz died in Lienz: the Görz possessions (Lienz and East Tyrol) passed to the Habsburg Maximilian I

12 April 1500

When Leonhard, the last count of the dynasty of the counts of Görz, died in Lienz on 12 April 1500, the Görz possessions passed to the House of Habsburg (King Maximilian I) in accordance with the inheritance contract (Erbvertrag) he had signed shortly before his death; East Tyrol (Lienz) was administered together with the Tyrolean Land.
