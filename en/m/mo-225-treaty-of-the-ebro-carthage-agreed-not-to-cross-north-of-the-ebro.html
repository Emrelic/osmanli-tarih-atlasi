Treaty of the Ebro: Carthage agreed not to cross north of the Ebro

225 BC

Hasdrubal, who continued the conquest of Hispania begun by Hamilcar, made the Ebro Treaty with Rome: Carthage would not cross the Ebro in arms. Hannibal's attack on Saguntum in 219 was justified on the interpretation of this treaty.
