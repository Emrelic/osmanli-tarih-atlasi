Turgut Reis became beylerbeyi of Tripoli of Barbary — the province expanded eastward to Tobruk and Derna

1556

After the conquest of Tripoli of Barbary in 1551, Turgut Reis came to Tripoli with the title of beylerbeyi in Jumada I 963 (March 1556). During his nine-year governorship he worked on the development and fortification of the province, carried Ottoman administration south to Fezzan, and expanded the frontiers eastward, taking Tobruk and Derna on the Barca coast. Thus the province of Tripoli of Barbary came to include the coastal strip extending to the Egyptian border.
