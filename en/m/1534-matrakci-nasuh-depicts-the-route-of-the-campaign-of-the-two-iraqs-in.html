Matrakçı Nasuh depicts the route of the Campaign of the Two Iraqs in the Beyan-i Menazil

1534

Matrakçı Nasuh, who took part in Süleyman the Lawgiver's first Iranian (Two Iraqs) campaign of 1534, depicted in the miniature-map technique every halting place along the army's route from Istanbul to Tabriz and from there to Baghdad. This work was collected in the book called Beyan-i Menazil-i Sefer-i Iraqayn and is the most original example of the menzilname genre among the Ottomans.
