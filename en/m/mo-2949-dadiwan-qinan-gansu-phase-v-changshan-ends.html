Dadiwan (Qin'an, Gansu): Phase V (Changshan) ends

2949 BC

The oldest dry-farming (millet) settlement on the Western Loess Plateau; Dadiwan I (early Neolithic, Laoguantai type) → Yangshao phases → Changshan. The first millet activity ~5800-5300 BCE.
