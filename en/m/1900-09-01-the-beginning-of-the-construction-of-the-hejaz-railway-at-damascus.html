The Beginning of the Construction of the Hejaz Railway at Damascus

1 September 1900

Construction of the line, ordered by the irade of Abdülhamid II dated 2 May 1900, began on 1 September 1900 with an official ceremony held at Damascus. The project, built entirely with donations collected from the Islamic world and the labour of Ottoman engineer-soldiers, reduced the pilgrims' journey from Damascus to Medina from forty days to five. It was both a work of prestige reinforcing the spiritual authority of the caliphate and a strategic investment strengthening military control over the Hijaz.
