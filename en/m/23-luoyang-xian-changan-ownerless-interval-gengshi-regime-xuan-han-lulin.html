Luoyang, Xi'an (Chang'an): ownerless interval → Gengshi Regime (Xuan Han / Lulin)

23

At the points Luoyang, Xi'an (Chang'an), ownership passes from the ownerless interval to the Gengshi Regime (Xuan Han / Lulin). Transition source information: Hou Han shu 11 (Liu Xuan): uprising against Mang in Chang'an; in the 9th month Gongbin Jiu killed Mang at Jiantai; Gengshi went to Luoyang, Shentu Jian/Li Song sent couriers from Chang'an, and in the 2nd month of year 2 (24) Gengshi marched west (to Chang'an) from Luoyang. End limit: Hou Han shu 1a, Jianwu 1, 9th month: Chimei entered Chang'an, Gengshi fled to Gaoling.
