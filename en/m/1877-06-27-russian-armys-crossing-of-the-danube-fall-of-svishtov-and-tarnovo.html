Russian army's crossing of the Danube: fall of Svishtov and Tarnovo

27 June 1877

The Russians, who with the beginning of the war had entered Romanian territory and drawn that principality to their side, advanced in two columns, one toward Dobruja and the other toward Bucharest. The Danube was crossed between Ruse and Nikopol, and Svishtov was taken on 27 June and Tarnovo, the old Bulgarian capital, on 1 July. Nikopol, whose evacuation had been ordered but not carried out, also surrendered after resisting for a while. The breaching of the first line of defense within a week caused great panic in Istanbul; there were even rumors that the seat of the sultanate would be moved to Bursa, and the conduct of the war was handed over to a military council set up in the capital.
