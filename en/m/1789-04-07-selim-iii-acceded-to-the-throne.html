Selim III acceded to the throne

7 April 1789

Upon the death of Abdülhamid I, his nephew Selim III came to the throne. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Selim III, the new sultan, who had been interested in the reform of European armies since his princedom, soon after his accession began to have a radical programme of renewal prepared. This programme would within a few years be known by the name Nizam-ı Cedid.
