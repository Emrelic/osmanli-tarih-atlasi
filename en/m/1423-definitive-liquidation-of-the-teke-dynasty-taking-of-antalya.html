Definitive liquidation of the Teke Dynasty: taking of Antalya

1423

The Beylik of the Tekeoğulları, which had been re-established through Timur's reviving it after the Battle of Ankara, came to an end when the Ottoman forces under Hamza Bey besieged Antalya; Osman Çelebi, the last important man of the beylik, was eliminated. The land of Teke on the Mediterranean coast thus definitively joined the Ottomans after a separation of twenty-one years. The beylik had ruled in Antalya since 1321 as the branch that split from the Hamidids.
