Conquest of Rhodes and the Dodecanese by the Knights of St. John (Hospitallers)

15 August 1310

The TDV's Rhodes entry records that the island passed into the hands of the Knights of St. John (Hospitallers/Isbitariyye) in 1309; the standard Hospitaller history (see J. Riley-Smith, The Knights Hospitaller) details that the siege began in the summer of 1306 and that the city of Rhodes, which long resisted with Byzantine reinforcements, fell to the forces of Grand Master Foulques de Villaret only on 15 August 1310. From this date until the Ottoman conquest in 1522 the island would be the headquarters of the knights, and the Dodecanese around it passed from Byzantium to Hospitaller rule in the same process.
