Ibn Saud defeated the Rashidi emir: Qasim passed from the Emirate of Shammar to the Saudis

13 April 1906

Abd al-Aziz b. Saud, who had retaken Riyadh in 1902, defeated and killed the Rashidi emir Abd al-Aziz b. Mit'ab on 13 April 1906. After this battle he took the Qasim region (Buraydah, Unayzah); thereafter the Rashidis ruled only at Ha'il and its surroundings.
