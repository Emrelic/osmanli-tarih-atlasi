End of the local dominance of the Harfush family in Baalbek

1850

The influence of the Harfush family, which had ruled Baalbek as a small local dynasty since 1521 (after the rebellion of Janbirdi al-Ghazali), came to an end with the new administrative arrangement of the Sublime Porte; the city was reduced to an ordinary district (kaza) centre attached to the province of Damascus.
