Mursili I was assassinated; the Hittite kingdom was weakened by internal strife

1589 BC

On his return from Babylon, Mursili I was killed by his brother-in-law Hantili and Zidanta. Struggles over the throne and the new Hurrian/Mitanni power left the kingdom weak for half a century; Telepinu was able to establish order only in c. 1525 BC.
