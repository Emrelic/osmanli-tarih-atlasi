Crimean War began

4 October 1853

The crisis that began with the question of the holy places and Russia's claim of protection over Orthodox subjects turned into open conflict with the Ottoman declaration of war; after the raid at Sinop, Britain and France (later Piedmont) entered the war on the Ottoman side. With the Allied armies landing in Crimea and taking Sevastopol, the war was won with the Treaty of Paris in 1856; however, the first foreign loan taken during the war years (1854) drew the empire into a spiral of financial dependence that would last half a century.
