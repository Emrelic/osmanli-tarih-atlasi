Buğra Qara Khaqan Hasan, ruler of Kashgar, had the khutba read in Malikshah's name; the Western and Eastern Karakhanids acknowledged Seljuk suzerainty

16 March 1089

When Malikshah turned towards the Eastern Karakhanid lands and came to Uzgand, Bughra Qara Khaqan Hasan, ruler of Kashgar, declared his obedience and had the khutba read in Malikshah's name; with the capture of Ahmad Khan, the Western Karakhanids too had been made subject to the Seljuks (482/1089).
