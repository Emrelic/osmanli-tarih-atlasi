Magnesia ad Sipylum: Rome and Pergamum defeated Antiochus III

189 BC

In the winter of 190/189 at Magnesia, Lucius Scipio and Eumenes II routed the army of Antiochus III. The Peace of Apamea (188) made after the victory led to Antiochus's giving up the north of the Taurus.
