Suleyman Shah II ends the Saltukid dynasty and grants Erzurum as iqta' to his brother Tughril Shah

25 June 1202

Suleyman Shah II, the Anatolian Seljuk Sultan, who came to Erzurum during the Georgian campaign, imprisoned Malik-Shah and abolished the Saltukid principality (2 Shawwal 598 / 25 June 1202) and gave Erzurum to his brother Mughith al-Din Tughril Shah, Malik of Elbistan. The Saltukid lands remained in Tughril Shah's hands until 1225.
