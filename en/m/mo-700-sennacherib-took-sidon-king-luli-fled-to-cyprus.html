Sennacherib took Sidon; King Luli fled to Cyprus

700 BC

In 701 Sennacherib subjugated the Phoenician cities: Luli (Elulaeus), king of Sidon, fled to Cyprus, and the dependent cities of Sidon and Tyre passed to Assyria. With these conquests the western coast was secured before the Judean campaign.
