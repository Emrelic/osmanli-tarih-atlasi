Hüsrev Pasha's rebuilding of the fortress of Gülanber in Shahrizor — the beylerbeylik of Shahrizor

16 March 1630

Grand Vizier Hüsrev Pasha, who set out on the Baghdad campaign, turned to Shahrizor after staying a long time in Mosul because of heavy rains. The rebuilding of the fortress of Gülanber, which had been demolished on Shah Abbas's order during his invasion, was begun; Shahrizor was again made the seat of a beylerbeylik, and Arnavud Mustafa Pasha was left in the fortress as beylerbeyi. Hüsrev Pasha brought the tribes of the region into obedience, took the fortress of Mihriban, and on 5 May 1630 near this fortress inflicted heavy losses on the Safavid army.
