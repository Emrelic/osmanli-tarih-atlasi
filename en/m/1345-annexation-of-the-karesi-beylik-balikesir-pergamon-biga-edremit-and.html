Annexation of the Karesi Beylik: Balıkesir, Pergamon, Biga, Edremit and Erdek

1345

TDV, Orhan entry (İnalcık) places the annexation in 735 (1334-35): upon the death of Demirhan Bey, the Karesi ghazis and Hacı İlbey invited Dursun Bey to the throne and asked Orhan for help. Orhan first marched on Balıkesir; when Dursun Bey, who went to a peace negotiation, was killed by an arrow shot from the Pergamon fortress, the beylik was joined entirely to Ottoman territory. In the same operation Kirmasti and Mihaliç submitted, Gölyazı was taken, and Ulubat, which had earlier been left on terms of safe-conduct (eman), was definitively seized. Orhan appointed Süleyman Pasha to the sancak of Karesi and made Biga the frontier centre — the seafaring cadre for the crossing to Rumelia was thus gained. Note: the TDV entry Karesioğulları gives the date 746 (1345) for the same event; the encyclopaedia contradicts itself. Other places joined on the same date: Çanakkale.
