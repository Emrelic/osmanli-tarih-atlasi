Besni passed to the Mamluks during Timur's Sivas and Malatya campaign

1 August 1400

Besni, which Bayezid the Thunderbolt had taken in 1398 together with Sivas, Darende and Malatya, fell into the hands of the Mamluks in 1400 as Timur took Sivas and Malatya. Timur also took Besni on 27 September 1400 after a fierce siege; when he withdrew from that region Besni came again under Mamluk rule, and remained with the Mamluks (except for a Dulkadirid interlude) until Selim the Grim took it in August 1516.
