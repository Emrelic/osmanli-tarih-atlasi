The ulama of Cairo proclaimed Mehmed Ali governor

May 1805

The tradesmen and ulama of Cairo, overwhelmed by the burden of taxation, rose under the leadership of the naqib al-ashraf Umar Makram, declared that they did not recognize the governor Hurşid Pasha, and seated Mehmed Ali in the office of governor. The choice of a governor by the people is a rarely encountered event in Ottoman history.
