The execution of Kemankeş Mustafa Pasha

31 January 1644

Kemankeş Mustafa Pasha, who had been grand vizier since the time of Murad IV and had earned the treasury a significant surplus with his financial reforms, was strangled and executed after his rivals in the palace influenced Ibrahim and he was accused of raising a Janissary uprising. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on 'Kemankeş Mustafa Pasha', his execution took place on 21 Dhu al-Qa'da 1053 / 31 January 1644: Kemankeş Kara Mustafa Pasha was strangled by the executioner Kara Ali in the Hocapaşa Bazaar and buried in his own tomb at Çarşıkapı. His death is remembered as a striking example of the instability of the grand vizierate in the period, and it ended a short period of strict reform.
