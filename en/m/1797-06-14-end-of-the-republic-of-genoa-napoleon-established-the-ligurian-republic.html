End of the Republic of Genoa — Napoleon established the Ligurian Republic

14 June 1797

In place of the Republic of Genoa, which had been handed over to a provisional government in early June 1797 under the pressure of Napoleon in the Italian campaign, the 'Ligurian Republic' was proclaimed on 14 June 1797 with a constitution on the French model; the centuries-old Genoese state effectively became one of France's satellite states. The republic would be annexed directly to France in 1805, and ceded to the Kingdom of Sardinia-Piedmont by the 1815 Congress of Vienna.
