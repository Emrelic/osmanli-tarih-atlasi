San Sebastián de Colima was founded — New Spain opened to the Pacific coast

25 July 1523

Gonzalo de Sandoval, one of Cortés's commanders, subdued the Colima valley in western Mexico and founded the town of San Sebastián de Colima. The town became one of New Spain's first bases on the Pacific coast and in later years supplied the shipyards of the South Sea exploration expeditions.
