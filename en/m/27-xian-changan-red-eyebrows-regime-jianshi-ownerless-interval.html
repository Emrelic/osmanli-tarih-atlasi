Xi'an (Chang'an): Red Eyebrows Regime (Jianshi) → ownerless interval

27

At the points Xi'an (Chang'an), ownership passes from the Red Eyebrows Regime (Jianshi) to the ownerless interval. Transition source information: Hou Han shu 1a, Jianwu 1: Chimei entered Chang'an and burned the palace; in the same month Deng Yu entered Chang'an and carried the tablets of the 11 emperors to Gao Miao (a brief Han entry). Chimei's departure from Chang'an (entity end t=0027) was not read in this round.
