Rome took Lycia and Caria from Rhodes; Delos was declared a free port

166 BC

In the Third Macedonian War, Rhodes's attempt at mediation between Rome and Perseus was punished: the Senate took Lycia and Caria from Rhodes and declared them free, gave Delos to Athens and turned it into a duty-free port. Rhodes's trade revenue fell sharply.
