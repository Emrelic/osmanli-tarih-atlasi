The Sublime Porte accepted the fait accompli: firman of the governorship of Egypt

3 July 1805

The Sublime Porte, lacking the power to uphold its own candidate in Egypt, sent Mehmed Ali a firman of the governorship of Egypt with the rank of vizier. On the map, from this date Egypt passes to the tributary (light-toned) layer: legally Ottoman territory, in practice the domain of the Kavalalı dynasty. Other settlements passing to the tributary layer on the same date: Cairo, Alexandria, Damietta, Asyut, Aswan, Ibrim, Suez, Reşîd (Rosetta).
