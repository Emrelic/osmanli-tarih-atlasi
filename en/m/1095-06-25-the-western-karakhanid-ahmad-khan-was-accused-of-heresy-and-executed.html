The Western Karakhanid Ahmad Khan was accused of heresy and executed; Qilich Tamghach Khan Mas'ud I took his place

25 June 1095

Ahmad ibn Khidr, whom Malikshah had restored to his country, was accused of heresy by the ulama, tried, and sentenced to death on 18 Jumada al-Akhira 488 (25 June 1095); his cousin Mas'ud I ibn Muhammad took his place (1095-1097).
