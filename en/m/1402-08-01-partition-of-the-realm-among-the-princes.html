Partition of the realm among the princes

1 August 1402

After the Ankara disaster, Süleyman Çelebi crossed to Rumelia and proclaimed his sultanate in Edirne; İsa Çelebi held Bursa, while Çelebi Mehmed held the Amasya-Tokat-Sivas region. On the map these three shares are shown in separate colours.
