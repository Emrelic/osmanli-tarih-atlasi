League of the Three Emperors (second) — secret understanding over the Straits, Bosnia-Herzegovina and Eastern Rumelia

18 June 1881

Austria-Hungary, Germany and Russia signed a secret treaty in Berlin. The parties accepted that the territorial status quo of 'Turkey in Europe' could be changed only by the joint decision of the three. They regarded the closure of the Straits to warships as a common principle. By an additional protocol Austria-Hungary received the right to annex Bosnia-Herzegovina whenever it wished; it was agreed that the union of Bulgaria and Eastern Rumelia would not be opposed. The treaty replaced the first league of 1873, was renewed in 1884, and expired in 1887.
