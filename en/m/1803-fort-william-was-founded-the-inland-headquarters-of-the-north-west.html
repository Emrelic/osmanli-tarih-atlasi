Fort William was founded — the inland headquarters of the North West Company

1803

When Grand Portage remained on United States territory, the North West Company moved its inland headquarters to the north of Lake Superior and founded Fort William. The post was the annual meeting place where the canoes coming from Montreal and the furs coming from the interior met, and it became the heart of the company's continent-wide network.
