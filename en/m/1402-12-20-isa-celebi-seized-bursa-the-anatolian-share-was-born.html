Isa Çelebi seized Bursa — the Anatolian share was born

20 December 1402

Isa Çelebi, who had withdrawn to the Karesi region after the Ankara disaster, seized Bursa while Timur was at İzmir. In the former capital, plundered and burned by Timur's soldiers, he proclaimed his rulership and had coins struck in his own name; in January 1403 he was recognized in Anatolia with the title 'ruler of the Turks'. On the map this share, extending along the Asian shore of the Marmara, appears in Isa Çelebi's own colour.
