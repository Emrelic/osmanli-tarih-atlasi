Alexios IV Angelos was crowned as co-emperor

1 August 1203

Alexios, under the protection of the Crusaders, was crowned as co-emperor with his father Isaac II in Hagia Sophia on 1 August 1203. He could not fulfil the promises he had made to the Crusaders.
