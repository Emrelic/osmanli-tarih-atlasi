Adad-nirari II ascended the throne; the Neo-Assyrian recovery began

910 BC

After a decline lasting about a century and a half, Adad-nirari II began to gather the Assyrian lands together again; he campaigned against the Aramaeans and the Babylonian frontier. The Neo-Assyrian Empire was born under his successors Ashurnasirpal II and Shalmaneser III.
