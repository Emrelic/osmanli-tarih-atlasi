The loss of Shirvan — Shamakhi, Baku and Derbent

1607

The province of Shirvan on the Caspian shore, won by Özdemiroğlu Osman Pasha's campaigns of 1578-1585, had provided the Ottoman treasury with important revenue as the main source of the silk trade. Shah Abbas's operation turning northward liquidated this province by taking Shamakhi, Baku and Derbent in succession. The loss was not only territorial: the route by which Iranian silk flowed to Europe through the Ottomans also passed out of control, and the Safavid State began to market its silk via the Indian Ocean.
