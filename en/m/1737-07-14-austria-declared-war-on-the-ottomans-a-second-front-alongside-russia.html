Austria declared war on the Ottomans — a second front alongside Russia

14 July 1737

Austria, Russia's ally, although peace negotiations had been agreed at Nemirov, attacked Ottoman lands in three columns in June 1737; the emperor's official declaration of war is dated 14 July 1737. Seckendorf took Niš in July, but the Austrian Bosnian operation was halted at Banja Luka in August. This front was closed by Austria's loss in the Treaty of Belgrade of 1739.
