Pisa was sold to Gian Galeazzo Visconti (Gherardo d'Appiano)

1399

The Appiano family's signoria of Pisa was sold through Gherardo to Gian Galeazzo Visconti, Duke of Milan; Pisa remained attached to the Duchy of Milan in 1399-1402. The day is not in the source (year-level 1399).
