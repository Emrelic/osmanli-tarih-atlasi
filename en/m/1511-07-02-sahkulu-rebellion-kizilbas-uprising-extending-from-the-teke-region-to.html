Şahkulu Rebellion — Kızılbaş uprising extending from the Teke region to Sivas

2 July 1511

Şahkulu Baba Tekeli, who revealed his rebellion in 1510 under the influence of Safavid propaganda in Anatolia, had stormed the bazaar of Antalya in March 1511 and in April defeated Ottoman forces in and around Kütahya. In the last clash he engaged in near Sivas with the grand vizier Hadım Ali Pasha, both sides suffered heavy losses; both the grand vizier and the rebel leader lost their lives. This uprising, which shook the state's authority in Anatolia, laid bare the vacuum of power in the last years of Bayezid II and became the strongest justification for Prince Selim's march on the throne.
