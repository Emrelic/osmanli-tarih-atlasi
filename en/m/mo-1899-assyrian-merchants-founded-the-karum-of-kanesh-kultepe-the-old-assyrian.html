Assyrian merchants founded the karum of Kanesh (Kültepe); the Old Assyrian trade network extended to Anatolia

1899 BC

The merchants of the city of Aššur founded karums (trading colonies) in Anatolia, centred on Kanesh, for the trade in copper, tin and textiles. The tens of thousands of cuneiform tablets found at Kültepe mark the beginning of writing and written history in Anatolia.
