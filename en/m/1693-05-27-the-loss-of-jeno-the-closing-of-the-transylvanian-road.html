The loss of Jenő — the closing of the Transylvanian road

27 May 1693

Jenő, taken in 1658, was a fortress holding the link between Várad and Timișoara. When its surroundings were cut off immediately after the fall of Várad, it too was lost. Thus the province of Timișoara was completely isolated from the north and continued to exist until Karlowitz as the only Hungarian territory remaining in Ottoman hands.
