Chagatai Khan died in May 1241, seven months before Ogedei

1 May 1241

Chagatai Khan, the second son of Genghis Khan, died in May 1241 (in 1242 according to another account); he left the throne to his grandson Qara Hulagu. Chagatai was the supreme enforcer of the Mongol law (yasa) and had received as the core lands of the khanate named after him the region from the Uyghur country to Samarkand and Bukhara; the independent khanate was founded by Alghu Khan twenty years after his death.
