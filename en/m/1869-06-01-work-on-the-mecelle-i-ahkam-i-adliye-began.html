Work on the Mecelle-i Ahkam-ı Adliye began

1 June 1869

The Mecelle Society, formed under the presidency of Ahmed Cevdet Pasha, head of the Dîvân-ı Ahkâm-ı Adliyye, set about preparing a civil code based on Hanafi jurisprudence. According to the TDV article on the 'Mecelle-i Ahkâm-ı Adliyye', the last part of the work, consisting of sixteen books and 1851 articles, was completed on 15 September 1876 and came into force; it is regarded as the first codified form of Islamic law.
