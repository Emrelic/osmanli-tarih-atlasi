Clovis is baptized by Bishop Remigius at Reims (Christmas)

25 December 496

After his victory against the Alamanni, Clovis went to Reims and, on Christmas Day 496, was baptized by Saint Remigius together with three thousand Franks. The baptism turned the eyes of the Catholic population in the Burgundian and Visigothic kingdoms toward Clovis.
