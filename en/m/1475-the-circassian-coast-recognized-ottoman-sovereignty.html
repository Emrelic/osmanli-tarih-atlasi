The Circassian coast recognized Ottoman sovereignty

1475

The Genoese colonies on the Black Sea coast, whose connection with the Mediterranean had been cut after the conquest of Constantinople, were taken under Ottoman administration by order of Mehmed the Conqueror, in 1475 by Gedik Ahmed Pasha and in 1479 by Kasım Pasha. The communities living on the shore between Taman and Sochi, whom Ottoman sources call the "Lower Circassians", recognized Ottoman sovereignty; the coast thereafter remained under Ottoman sovereignty for three centuries. This is a recognition of sovereignty: the tribes continued their own order, and the places the Ottomans in fact held were coastal fortresses such as Anapa, Sudjuk, Gelincik, Sukhum and Poti.
