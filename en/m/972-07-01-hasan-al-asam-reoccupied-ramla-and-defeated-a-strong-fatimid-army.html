Hasan al-A'sam reoccupied Ramla and defeated a strong Fatimid army

1 July 972

The Qarmatian leader Hasan al-A'sam reoccupied Ramla in Ramadan 361 (July 972) and defeated a strong Fatimid army; a year later he attacked Egypt by land and sea and besieged Cairo once more, but was defeated by al-Aziz Billah.
