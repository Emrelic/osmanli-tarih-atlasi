Egypt was divided in two: the Hyksos (Fifteenth Dynasty) ruled Lower and Middle Egypt from Avaris, and the Seventeenth Dynasty at Thebes ruled Upper Egypt

1649 BC

The 'foreign rulers' (Hyksos) of Canaanite origin, settled at Avaris in the Eastern Delta, founded the Fifteenth Dynasty and ruled Lower Egypt and Middle Egypt as far as Cusae (Qusiya), while the native Seventeenth Dynasty at Thebes held Upper Egypt. South of Thebes the kingdom of Kerma stood as an independent power. That Avaris was the Hyksos capital is confirmed in Breasted by Ahmose's siege narrative.
