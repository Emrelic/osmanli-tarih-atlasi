In the Judean campaign Sennacherib met the Kushite-Egyptian army at Eltekeh

700 BC

During the Assyrian king Sennacherib's campaign in Canaan, the Kushite-led Egyptian army came to the aid of Judah and fought the Assyrians at Eltekeh; the Assyrian army withdrew without taking Jerusalem. Josephus relates that Sennacherib, on hearing that the Kushite king Tirhakah was coming with a great army, left Pelusium. This is the first direct clash between Kush-Egypt and Assyria.
