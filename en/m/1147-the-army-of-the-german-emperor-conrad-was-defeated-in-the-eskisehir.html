The army of the German Emperor Conrad was defeated in the Eskişehir region; King Louis of France suffered heavy losses on the road to Antalya

1147

During the Second Crusade the armies of Conrad and King Louis of France crossed from Constantinople into Anatolia; the German army, which had advanced as far as the Eskişehir region, was heavily defeated by the forces of Sultan Mas'ud, and the emperor returned to Constantinople with very few troops; the French army, finding that Central Anatolia could not be crossed, suffered heavy losses from Turkish attacks while moving through Western Anatolia toward Antalya.
