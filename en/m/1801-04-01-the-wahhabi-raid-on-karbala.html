The Wahhabi Raid on Karbala

1 April 1801

Wahhabi forces rising from Najd entered Karbala, plundered the city, destroyed the tomb of Imam Husayn and killed more than three thousand people. The kölemen governors of the Ottoman State in Iraq were unable to prevent the raid; the event aroused great reaction both in Iran and in Istanbul. This attack was the declaration that the Saudi movement had ceased to be an oasis emirate and had become a regional power threatening the holy cities of the Ottomans.
