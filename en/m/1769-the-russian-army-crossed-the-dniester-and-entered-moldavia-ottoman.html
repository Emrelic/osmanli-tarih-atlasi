The Russian army crossed the Dniester and entered Moldavia (Ottoman–Russian War of 1768–1774)

1769

About six months after the declaration of war, the main Russian army assembled in Podolia under Prince Golitsyn. At the call of Dosoftei, metropolitan of Moldavia, to take Moldavia under Russian protection, Golitsyn crossed the Dniester and tried to take Khotin before marching on Iași. This first attempt failed, and the army returned to Podolia because of difficulties of provisioning; Khotin fell only in September 1769.
