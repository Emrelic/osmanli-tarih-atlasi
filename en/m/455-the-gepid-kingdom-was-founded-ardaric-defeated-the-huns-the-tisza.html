The Gepid Kingdom was founded: Ardaric defeated the Huns, the Tisza-Transylvania region came under Gepid rule

455

At Nedao the revolt led by the Gepid Ardaric broke up the Hun union (Britannica 455, Maenchen-Helfen 454); Ardaric founded the Gepid Kingdom on the Middle Danube, with its center at Sirmium. The kingdom was destroyed in 567 by the Avar+Lombard alliance (year precision).
