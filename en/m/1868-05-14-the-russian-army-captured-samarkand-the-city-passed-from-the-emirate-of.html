The Russian army captured Samarkand — the city passed from the Emirate of Bukhara to the Governorate-General of Turkestan

14 May 1868

Russian forces captured Samarkand, held by the Emirate of Bukhara, and attached the city to the Turkestan governorate-general. Samarkand had been a city that lived in Bukhara's shadow in the era of the Uzbek khans; under Russian administration it became a new stop in the Russian advance in Central Asia.
