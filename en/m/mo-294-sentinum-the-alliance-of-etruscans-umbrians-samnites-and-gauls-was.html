Sentinum: the alliance of Etruscans, Umbrians, Samnites and Gauls was defeated by Rome

294 BC

This battle at Sentinum in the Third Samnite War destroyed the last great anti-Roman alliance in Italy; the Etruscan cities were forced one by one into alliance with Rome.
