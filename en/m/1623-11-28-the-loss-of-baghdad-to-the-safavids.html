The Loss of Baghdad to the Safavids

28 November 1623

As a result of the negotiations that Bekir Subaşı, who had rebelled in Baghdad, entered into with the Safavids, his son Derviş Mehmed, who was defending the inner citadel, surrendered the city to the forces of Shah Abbas. The loss of one of the symbolic cities of the caliphal heritage and of the greater part of Iraq aroused great indignation in Istanbul. Retaking Baghdad would be the sole aim of Ottoman eastern policy for fifteen years.
