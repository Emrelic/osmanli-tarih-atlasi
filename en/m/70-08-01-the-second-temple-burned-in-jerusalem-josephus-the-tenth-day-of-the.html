The Second Temple burned in Jerusalem (Josephus: the tenth day of the month of Lous/Ab; Britannica: August 70)

1 August 70

Titus withdrew to the Antonia tower and resolved to assault the Temple the next morning; according to Josephus, by fate, on the tenth day of the month of Lous (Ab), the anniversary of the day the king of Babylon burned the Temple, the Temple caught fire when a Roman soldier set the golden window alight with a piece of burning material. Britannica writes that the Romans broke through the last defenses by August 70 and destroyed the Second Temple.
