Treaty of Benevento: Pope Adrian IV recognises William I's kingdom

18 June 1156

The tension between King William I and the papacy was resolved on 18 June 1156 by the treaty signed at Benevento after negotiations conducted by Roland Bandinelli (later Alexander III) and Maio; the treaty repeated the relations of Roger II's time and added new concessions.
