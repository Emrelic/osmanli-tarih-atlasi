Slab Grave culture, Mongolia: the Slab Grave culture begins

1099 BC

An Early Iron Age culture recognized by rectangular graves with stone slabs; it emerges from the tradition of decorated graves and replaces the Deer Stone–Khirigsuur Complex (a genetic population change). Deer stones and khirigsuur stones were reused in the construction of slab graves.
