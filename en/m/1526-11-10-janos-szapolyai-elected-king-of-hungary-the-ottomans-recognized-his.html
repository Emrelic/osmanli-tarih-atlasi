János Szapolyai elected king of Hungary — the Ottomans recognized his kingdom on condition of his being a vassal

10 November 1526

To the Hungarian throne left vacant by the death of the king at Mohács, part of the nobles elected János Szapolyai, voivode of Transylvania; Szapolyai was crowned at Székesfehérvár. The Ottomans, who had evacuated Buda, recognized Szapolyai's kingdom on condition that it be subordinate to themselves, in order to keep the region as a buffer. On the map, places dependent on Szapolyai appear as subordinate to the Ottomans from this date.
