Russia's first fortress in the Caucasus: Terek

1563

Temryuk, the Kabardian prince, oppressed by Crimean raids and slave raids, sent envoys to Moscow in 1557 and 1559 and married his daughter to Tsar Ivan IV. The Russians, having signed a treaty with some Kabardian princes, founded their first fortress in the Caucasus on the bank of the Terek river in 1563. In a letter he sent in 1570 the Ottoman sultan demanded the demolition of the Russian fortresses in Kabarda; the fortress was demolished in 1574. This is a matter of an alliance and a fortress, not a transfer of territory — for this reason it shows no change on the map.
