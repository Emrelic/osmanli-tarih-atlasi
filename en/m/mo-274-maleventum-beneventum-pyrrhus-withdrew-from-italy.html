Maleventum (Beneventum): Pyrrhus withdrew from Italy

274 BC

Pyrrhus's renewed attack on Rome after returning from his Sicilian campaign failed before Manius Curius Dentatus at Maleventum; Pyrrhus returned to Epirus in 275. He left a small garrison at Tarentum; Rome remained without a rival in Italy.
