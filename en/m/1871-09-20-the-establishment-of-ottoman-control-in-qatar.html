The Establishment of Ottoman Control in Qatar

20 September 1871

Upon the invitation of Sheikh Qasim b. Thani, who was under British pressure, Ottoman forces landed on the Qatar peninsula, and in the autumn of 1871 Ottoman control was established in the region. Qatar was organised as a kaza attached to the sancak of Najd, and the sheikh was appointed kaymakam. This move was the most concrete gain of the forward policy that Midhat Pasha conducted in the Gulf against England; the Ottoman flag flew on the peninsula until 1913.
