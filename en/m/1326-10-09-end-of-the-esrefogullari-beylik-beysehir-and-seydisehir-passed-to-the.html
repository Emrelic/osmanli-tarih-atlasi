End of the Eşrefoğulları Beylik — Beyşehir and Seydişehir passed to the Hamidids

9 October 1326

Demirtaş, son of Emir Chobān, governor-general of Anatolia for the Ilkhanate, moved against the Turkmen beyliks that had declared their independence, entered Beyşehir, and had Eşrefoğlu Süleyman Shah, who had once complained of him, put to death (9 October 1326). The Eşrefoğulları Beylik, which had been founded in the region of Beyşehir and Seydişehir at the end of the thirteenth century, thus came to an end. The TDV article on the Hamidids gives what followed: Beyşehir, Seydişehir, Akşehir and Doğanhisar, which belonged to the Eşrefoğulları eliminated by Demirtaş, were joined to the borders of the Hamid beylik. In the same year the Hamidid Dündar Bey was also handed over to Demirtaş and killed. These lands would pass to Ottoman administration in 1381 when they were sold to Murad I for 80,000 gold pieces.
