Prozorovsky occupied Or Kapı — Russian forces remained at Or Kapı until the annexation

3 December 1776

When General Prozorovsky, who was marching to place Şahin Giray on the Crimean throne, reached the Perekop line, Devlet Giray, the kaymakam and the Tatars had withdrawn from the city; Prozorovsky occupied the fortress of Or and the Perekop line without a fight. The Russians settled in Or Kapı and remained there until the annexation of Crimea in 1783.
