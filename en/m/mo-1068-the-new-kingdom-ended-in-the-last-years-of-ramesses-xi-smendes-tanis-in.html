The New Kingdom ended: in the last years of Ramesses XI, Smendes (Tanis) in the Delta and the High Priest of Amun at Thebes were effectively independent

1068 BC

In the last period of Ramesses XI the central state collapsed: at Thebes the High Priest of Amun Herihor controlled Upper Egypt, and in the Delta at Tanis Nesbanebdjed (Smendes) ruled; the Tale of Wenamun records this division (Wenamun's journey from Herihor to Smendes). Smendes eventually founded the Twenty-first Dynasty; Nubia also left Egyptian control in this turmoil.
