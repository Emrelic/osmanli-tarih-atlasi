The British evacuation of Eskişehir

20 March 1920

Eskişehir, which had been under British occupation since January 1919, was, according to the TDV article on Eskişehir, evacuated by the British on 20 March 1920. A few months later, in June 1920, the city became the centre of the Western Front established by the Grand National Assembly.
