Suleyman Shah I campaigns in Cilicia (Çukurova) and conquers Tarsus, Adana, Ayn Zarba and Mopsuestia

1083

Suleyman Shah, who took the title of sultan in 1082, in the same year campaigned in Cilicia (Çukurova) and conquered the cities and fortresses of Tarsus, Adana, Ayn Zarba and Mopsuestia (1082-1083); then, at the invitation of the people of Antioch, he marched on Antioch with his sons Kilij Arslan and Dawud.
