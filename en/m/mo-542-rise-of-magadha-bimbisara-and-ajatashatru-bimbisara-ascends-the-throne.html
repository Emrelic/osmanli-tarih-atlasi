Rise of Magadha (Bimbisara and Ajatashatru): Bimbisara ascends the throne of Magadha

542 BC

The Cambridge History of India (1922), following the Geiger chronology based on the Sinhalese chronicles, gives the reigns of Bimbisara as 543-491 BCE and of Ajatashatru as 491-459 BCE; the rise of Magadha begins with the conquest of Anga (Bimbisara, c. 600 BCE), and the supremacy over Kashi-Kosala-Videha is attributed to Ajatashatru.
