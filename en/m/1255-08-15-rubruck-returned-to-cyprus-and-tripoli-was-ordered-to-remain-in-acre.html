Rubruck returned to Cyprus and Tripoli, was ordered to remain in Acre

15 August 1255

Coming to Ayas by way of Derbent, Armenia and the Sultanate of Rum, he reaches Tripoli via Cyprus and Antioch; the Franciscan superior obliges him to stay in Acre, and he writes his report to King Louis.
