The Granting of the First Ahidname to the Netherlands

6 July 1612

Cornelis Haga, the envoy of the United Dutch Republic, succeeded in obtaining from Ahmed I an ahidname of the same scope as those given to France and England. The Netherlands thus gained the right to open an embassy in Istanbul, to open consulates at Ottoman ports, and to trade under its own flag. This ahidname symbolises the entry into the Ottoman market of the northern maritime powers that replaced Venice in Mediterranean trade.
