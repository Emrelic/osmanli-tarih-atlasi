The Zaporozhian Cossacks founded the first Sich on the island of Khortytsia

1552

Prince Dmytro Vyshnevetsky founded the first Zaporozhian Sich on the island of Little Khortytsia in the Dnieper. The fortress was besieged and destroyed by the Crimean Tatars in 1558; the Sich later moved to the islands to the south. Term: «Sich» (Ukrainian Sich) is the central camp of the Zaporozhian Cossacks on the Dnieper islands, fortified with logs and ditches; it is the headquarters, the seat of the assembly (Rada) and the centre of the elected head of the host (koshovyi), not a state capital.
