La Blanca (San Marcos, Pacific Guatemala): Middle Preclassic regional centre, Mound 1: the decline of La Blanca

599 BC

After the collapse of the Early Preclassic political centre of Ojo de Agua (Chiapas), La Blanca was a regional centre for about 300 years in the Middle Preclassic (Conchas phase, ~900–600 BC); at its peak it was larger than 200 ha, with Mound 1, 25 m high (one of the oldest pyramid-temples of Mesoamerica). It declined for unknown reasons at ~600 BC.
