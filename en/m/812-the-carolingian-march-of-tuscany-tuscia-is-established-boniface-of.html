The Carolingian March of Tuscany (Tuscia) is established: Boniface of Bavaria becomes count of Lucca

812

The March of Tuscany (Tuscia), which arose in the Carolingian period as a military and political institution, was granted in 812 in the form of a countship to Boniface of Bavaria as a continuation of the Lombard duchy of Lucca (Boniface 812-23). In the second half of the ninth century Adalbert I, count of Lucca (845-98), became margrave of Tuscia; in the mid-eleventh century the march passed to the Attoni, lords of Canossa.
