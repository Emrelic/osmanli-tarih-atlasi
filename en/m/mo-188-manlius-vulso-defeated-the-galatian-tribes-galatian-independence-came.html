Manlius Vulso defeated the Galatian tribes: Galatian independence came under Roman supervision

188 BC

After Magnesia, Gnaeus Manlius Vulso invaded the land of Galatia and routed the Tolistoagii and Tectosages at Olympus and Magaba; by treaty the Galatians agreed not to attack their neighbours. Galatia remained largely independent but was bound to the Roman-Pergamene balance.
