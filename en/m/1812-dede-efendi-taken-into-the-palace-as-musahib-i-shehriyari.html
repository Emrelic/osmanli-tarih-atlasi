Dede Efendi taken into the palace as musahib-i shehriyari

1812

Dede Efendi, who drew the sultan's attention in the time of Selim III with a composition in the Buselik makam, was taken in 1812 by Mahmud II among the "musahib-i shehriyari" (the sultan's private companions of the assembly) and soon afterward rose to the post of chief muezzin.
