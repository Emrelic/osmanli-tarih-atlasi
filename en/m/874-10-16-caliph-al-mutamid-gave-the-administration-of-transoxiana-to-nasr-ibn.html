Caliph al-Mu'tamid gave the administration of Transoxiana to Nasr ibn Ahmad; his brother Isma'il became governor of Bukhara

16 October 874

In the year 261 (874-75) the administration of Transoxiana was granted by decree to Nasr ibn Ahmad; in the same year Nasr, at the request of the people of Bukhara, appointed his brother Isma'il ibn Ahmad governor of Bukhara. Some scholars regard this date as the foundation of the state.
