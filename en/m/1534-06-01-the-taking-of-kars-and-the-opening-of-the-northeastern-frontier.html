The Taking of Kars and the Opening of the Northeastern Frontier

1 June 1534

As the vanguard column of the Campaign of the Two Iraqs turned eastward from Erzurum, the fortress of Kars, which was held by the Georgian atabegate and had lain in ruins since Timur, was taken over without encountering resistance. Since the city held the pass between the arc of the Aras and Çıldır, it was regarded as the key to the northern gate opening onto Iran. This gain became the first link of the beylerbeyilik of Kars, to be established thirty years later, and of the Caucasus line.
