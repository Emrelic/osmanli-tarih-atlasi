The Treaty of Vasvár

9 August 1664

The treaty signed despite the defeat at St. Gotthard stipulated that Uyvar and Várad would remain with the Ottomans, that Transylvania would be left neutral under Ottoman protection, and that Austria would pay a 'gift' of 200,000 kuruş. This peace of twenty years' duration is one of the rare examples of the side that lost the war winning at the table. The discontent that the treaty created in Habsburg public opinion would feed the Central Hungarian movement and, indirectly, the 1683 Vienna campaign.
