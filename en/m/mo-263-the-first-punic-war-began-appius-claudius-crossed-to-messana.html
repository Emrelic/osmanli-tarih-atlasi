The First Punic War began: Appius Claudius crossed to Messana

263 BC

When the Mamertines of Messana, after Carthage, also asked Rome for help against Hieron, king of Syracuse, the Roman consul Appius Claudius Caudex crossed to Sicily in 264 and took Messana from the Carthaginian garrison. Rome thus entered a war outside Italy for the first time.
