Uzun Hasan's end of the Qaraquyunlu State

1 April 1469

According to the Uzun Hasan entry of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, the Aqquyunlu ruler Uzun Hasan defeated and killed the Qaraquyunlu ruler Jahan Shah in the Bingöl-Sancak region on 12 Rabi' al-Akhir 872 / 10 November 1467; Jahan Shah's son Hasan Ali was defeated at Merend in September 1468 and killed before Hamadan in April-May 1469, and the Qaraquyunlu State was brought to a definitive end. The lands passed under Aqquyunlu rule; over the following decade the state would expand as far as the interior of Iran.
