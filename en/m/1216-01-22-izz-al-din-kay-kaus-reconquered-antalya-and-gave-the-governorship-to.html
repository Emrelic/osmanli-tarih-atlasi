Izz al-Din Kay Ka'us reconquered Antalya and gave the governorship to Mubariz al-Din Ertokush

22 January 1216

Antalya, which was in the hands of the Cypriots, was reconquered by Izz al-Din Kay Ka'us on 22 January 1216, and the governorship was again given to Mubariz al-Din Ertokush; the city became the centre of the Seljuk Mediterranean fleet.
