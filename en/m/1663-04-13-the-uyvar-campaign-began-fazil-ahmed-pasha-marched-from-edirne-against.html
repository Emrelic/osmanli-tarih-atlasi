The Uyvar campaign began — Fâzıl Ahmed Pasha marched from Edirne against Austria

13 April 1663

After Austria attacked Transylvania and took the fortresses of Székelyhíd and Kolozsvár, Istanbul decided on a campaign against the Habsburgs. Grand Vizier Köprülüzâde Fâzıl Ahmed Pasha set out from Edirne on 13 April 1663. At Belgrade an Austrian request for peace arrived, but in the two meetings at Osijek the Ottoman terms were rejected. At the council in Buda the objective was also set as Uyvar.
