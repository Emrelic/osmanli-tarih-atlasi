Patrona Halil Rebellion

25 September 1730

Failure on the Iranian front, taxes and anger at the luxury of the palace circle turned into a great uprising under the leadership of Patrona Halil, a bath attendant. Grand Vizier İbrahim Pasha was killed, Ahmed III was deposed and the Tulip Era came to an end; the rebels' rule over the city for months strikingly demonstrated the fragility of central authority.
