The Italian occupation of Massawa — the loss of the western shore of the Red Sea

5 February 1885

The port of Massawa, the centre of the province of Habeş, was occupied by Italy, which took advantage of the turmoil in which the Mahdist rebellion covered Sudan; this formed the nucleus of Italian Eritrea. Suakin in the north and the Dahlak archipelago offshore were also lost in the same process. Thus Ottoman domination on the western shore of the Red Sea, which had lasted since the time of Selim the Grim, came to an end, and the sea became waters foreign on both of its sides.
