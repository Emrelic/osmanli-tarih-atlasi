The death of the last sultan, Mehmed VI Vahdeddin, at San Remo

16 May 1926

Mehmed Vahdeddin, the last Ottoman sultan, who settled in the Italian city of San Remo after leaving Istanbul, died on 16 May 1926 before learning that the caliphate congress (13 May 1926), at which he had declared that he had never renounced his rights to the sultanate and the caliphate, had convened. He was in severe financial difficulty; he owed 60,000 liras to the tradesmen of San Remo, and the creditors who heard of his death had the furnishings of his house seized.
