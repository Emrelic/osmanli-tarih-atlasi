The Erzurum Congress: The Decision of National Resistance of the Eastern Vilayets

23 July 1919

The congress convened by the Society for the Defence of the Rights of Eastern Anatolia (Şarkî Anadolu Müdafaa-i Hukuk Cemiyeti), after being postponed under British pressure, opened with fifty-six delegates, and Mustafa Kemal Pasha, who had resigned from the army, was elected its president. The congress declared that within the national borders the homeland was a whole, that mandate and protectorate could not be accepted, and that if the government dissolved the nation would take its own destiny into its hands. These decisions would form the nucleus of the text of the Misâk-ı Millî six months later.
