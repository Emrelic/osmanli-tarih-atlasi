Nicomedes I brought the Galatians into Anatolia

277 BC

Nicomedes I, King of Bithynia, against his brother Zipoetes and the pressure of Seleucus, brought the Galatian (Celtic) tribes across the Bosphorus and took them into his service; the Galatians later settled in Central Anatolia; the Tectosages branch made Ankara (Ancyra) its capital (TDV «Ankara»: «between 278-189 BC it became the capital of the Tectosages branch of the Galatians who came from Thrace»). The date of the crossing is 278/277.
