Palmyra (Zenobia) invaded Egypt; the Roman general Probus was defeated

270

The Palmyrene general Zabdas, acting in the name of Zenobia, entered Egypt with an army of seventy thousand; the Roman general Tenagino Probus recovered Alexandria for a time, but was defeated and shut himself in the fortress of Babylon, and committed suicide when captured. For a year or two Egypt was counted as part of the Palmyrene state in the name of Zenobia.
