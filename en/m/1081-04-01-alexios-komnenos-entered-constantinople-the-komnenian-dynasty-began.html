Alexios Komnenos entered Constantinople: the Komnenian dynasty began

1 April 1081

The troops of Alexios, whom the military aristocracy had proclaimed emperor, entered Constantinople on 1 April 1081. Alexios's reign (1081-1118) passed amid Norman, Pecheneg and Crusader threats.
