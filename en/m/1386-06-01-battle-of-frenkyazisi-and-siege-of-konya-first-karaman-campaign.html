Battle of Frenkyazısı and siege of Konya — first Karaman campaign

1 June 1386

Murad I himself set out on campaign against the Karamanid Alaeddin Ali Bey, who, although his son-in-law, was disturbed by the Ottoman expansion in western Anatolia; in the battle fought at the place called Frenk Yazısı, the trained and experienced Ottoman army defeated the Karamanid forces. Then the fortress of Konya was besieged, but with the pardoning of Alaeddin Bey the beylik was not entirely abolished. This campaign is the opening act of the long reckoning between the Ottomans and Karaman, which would last a century and end only with the definitive annexation in 1468.
