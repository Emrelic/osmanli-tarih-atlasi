With the death of Charles III, Provence passed to King Louis XI of France under his will: Aix-en-Provence

11 December 1481

Charles III, the last count of Provence of the House of Anjou, made his will in Marseille on 10 December 1481 and died on 11 December 1481; the County of Provence passed into the hands of the king of France. The union was legally finalized in 1486 (the accession of the Estates of Provence).
