The Peace of Kraków — the Teutonic Order state became the Duchy of Prussia

8 April 1525

Albrecht of Brandenburg, Grand Master of the Teutonic Knights, signed the Peace of Kraków when he could find no help against Poland. The order state (Ordensstaat) became a hereditary and secular duchy, under Polish suzerainty, passing to the Brandenburg dynasty. The centre was Königsberg; in 1544 Albrecht founded the Albertina University there. The duchy became the Kingdom of Prussia in 1701.
