Surrender of Metz: Bazaine's army laid down its arms, German troops took the fortress

29 October 1870

The capitulation protocol of the besieged Army of the Rhine was signed on the evening of 27 October; on the morning of 29 October Prussian flags were raised on the bastions and French troops left the fortress. Metz remained under military occupation until the Treaty of Frankfurt (10 May 1871).
