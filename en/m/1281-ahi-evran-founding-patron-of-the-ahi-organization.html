Ahi Evran — founding patron of the ahi organization

1281

Ahi Evran is regarded as one of the founders of the ahi organization in Anatolia and as the patron saint of the tanners (leatherworkers). His zawiya at Kırşehir continued into the early twentieth century as the spiritual center of the Ottoman tradesmen's/guild organization; the state officially confirmed this position in 1780, 1782, 1822-23 and 1842.
