Tribute of Dubrovnik (Ragusa)

7 March 1459

The merchant republic of Ragusa secured its autonomy and trade privileges in return for tribute; it prospered as the Ottomans' window on the Adriatic. The island of Mljet, territory of the republic, shared this status.
