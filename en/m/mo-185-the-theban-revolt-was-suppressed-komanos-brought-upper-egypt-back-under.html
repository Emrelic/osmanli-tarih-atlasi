The Theban revolt was suppressed: Komanos brought Upper Egypt back under Ptolemaic rule

185 BC

After some twenty years of separatist rule, the Ptolemaic commander Komanos retook Thebes from the native pharaoh Ankhwennefer, and Upper Egypt was again joined to the Ptolemaic state.
