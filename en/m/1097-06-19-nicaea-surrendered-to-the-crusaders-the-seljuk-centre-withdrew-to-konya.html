Nicaea surrendered to the Crusaders; the Seljuk centre withdrew to Konya, and Byzantium began to retake western Anatolia

19 June 1097

After the Crusaders' siege of Nicaea, the city surrendered to Byzantium on 19 June 1097; the Seljuk sultan Kilij Arslan I withdrew his centre to the interior, to Konya, while Byzantium took back places on the western Anatolian coast such as Ephesus.
