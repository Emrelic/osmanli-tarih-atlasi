Completion of the Bursa Green Mosque Complex

1 December 1419

The Green Mosque Complex, known for its tile and decoration program to which masters from Tabriz also contributed, was completed in December 1419. The complex consists of a mosque, a mausoleum (the Green Tomb), a madrasa, an imaret and a bath; it is one of the richest examples of early Ottoman architecture.
