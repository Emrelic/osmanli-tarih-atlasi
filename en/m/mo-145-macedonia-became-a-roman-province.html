Macedonia became a Roman province

145 BC

After the suppression of the revolt of Andriscus (148), the four regions of Macedonia were united as a single Roman province (146); to the south of the province, the supervision of Greece was also left to the governor of Macedonia. The Via Egnatia was built in the following years from the Adriatic to Thessalonica.
