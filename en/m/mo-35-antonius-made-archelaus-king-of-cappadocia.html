Antonius made Archelaus king of Cappadocia

35 BC

While reorganizing the East, Antonius gave the throne of Cappadocia to Archelaus; Archelaus ruled until he was summoned to Rome by Tiberius in AD 17. Cappadocia became a Roman province in AD 17 (this event is a separate item in the chronology).
