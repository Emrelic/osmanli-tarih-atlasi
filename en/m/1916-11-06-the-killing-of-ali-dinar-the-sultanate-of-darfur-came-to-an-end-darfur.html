The killing of Ali Dinar — the Sultanate of Darfur came to an end, Darfur was attached to British Sudan

6 November 1916

Ali Dinar, the Sultan of Darfur, who had drawn close to the Ottoman State in the First World War, had withdrawn in the direction of Jebel Marra after the fall of his capital El Fasher in May 1916; he was killed on 6 November 1916 by forces sent by the Sudan government. Some time later the lands of Darfur were attached to British Sudan as a province.
