Amir II was killed near Sana'a — end of the Tahirid dynasty

15 April 1517

Amir II, the last ruler of the Tahirids, in a war that began over relations soured by the Mamluk fleet, was first defeated near Zabid (November 1516), as the Yemeni tribes and the Zaydis supported the Mamluks, and then was killed in the battle near Sana'a. The Tahirid dynasty, which had ruled Yemen since 1454, thus came to an end; the dynasty's cities (including Taiz) were no longer attached to Tahirid rule.
