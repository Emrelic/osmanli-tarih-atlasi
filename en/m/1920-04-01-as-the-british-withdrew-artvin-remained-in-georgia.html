As the British withdrew, Artvin remained in Georgia

1 April 1920

According to the TDV article on Artvin, the British occupation lasted until the month of April 1920; as the British withdrew, they left the city to Georgia. Artvin remained in Georgian hands until the ultimatum of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey on 23 February 1921.
