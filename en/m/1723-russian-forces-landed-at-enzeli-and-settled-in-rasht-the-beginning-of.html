Russian forces landed at Enzeli and settled in Rasht — the beginning of the occupation of Gilan

1723

After the capture of Derbent, a Russian landing force of a thousand men coming from Astrakhan landed in the bay of Enzeli and settled in Rasht at the call of the governor of Rasht, who had asked for help against the Afghans. The rest of Gilan was taken by May 1723. The Ottoman army, for its part, entered the Caucasus by way of Tiflis the same spring; the two empires were advancing from two different ends into the collapsing Safavid land.
