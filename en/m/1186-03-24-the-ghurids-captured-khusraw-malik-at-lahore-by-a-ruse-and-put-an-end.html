The Ghurids captured Khusraw Malik at Lahore by a ruse and put an end to the Ghaznavid State

24 March 1186

Khusraw Malik, son of Khusraw Shah, who ruled in India after his father's death in 1160, was captured by the Ghurids by a ruse; the Ghaznavid State came to an end (582/1186).
