Eretna attached the districts of Tokat, Kayseri and Samsun

1339

Emir Eretna, who had gained power in Anatolia as an Ilkhanid governor, expanded his borders in 1339 after regaining the support of the Mamluk Sultan al-Nasir Muhammad, and attached the districts of Tokat, Kayseri and Samsun to himself. The Zamantı district east of Kayseri also came under Eretna's administration in this period. The beylik declared its independence in 1341 with the death of al-Nasir.
