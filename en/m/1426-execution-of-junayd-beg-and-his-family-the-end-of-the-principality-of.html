Execution of Junayd Beg and his family: the end of the Principality of Aydın

1426

Upon Junayd Beg, who had regained control of Aydın-ili, inciting the Anatolian beys and seeking contact with Venice, Murad II sent the Beylerbeyi of Anatolia Hamza Beg against him. When his son Kurd Hasan was defeated near Akhisar and captured, Junayd withdrew to İpsili opposite the island of Samos; when the help he expected from the Karamanid did not come and the Genoese, acting together with the Ottomans, blockaded him from the sea, he was forced to surrender and was eliminated together with his whole lineage. The Aydınid lands thus came entirely under Ottoman administration.
