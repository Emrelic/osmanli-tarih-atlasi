Masinissa attacked Carthaginian territory: Numidian-Carthaginian War

149 BC

After Masinissa's seizure of Carthaginian lands (151/150), Carthage went to war and was defeated by Masinissa; Rome regarded this as a violation of the treaty and declared war in 149. Masinissa died in 148 at the age of ninety.
