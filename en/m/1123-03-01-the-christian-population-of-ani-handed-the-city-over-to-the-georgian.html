The Christian population of Ani handed the city over to the Georgian King David; the city purchased by the Saltukid Emir Ali slipped from his grasp

1 March 1123

When the Shaddadid Shavur II tried to sell Ani to the Saltukid Emir Ali, who held Kars, the Christian inhabitants revolted, summoned the Georgian King David, who had recently taken Tiflis, and handed the city over to him; Shavur II was arrested with his family and sent to Georgia.
