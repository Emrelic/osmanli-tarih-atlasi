The Victory of the Plain of Oruç and the Suppression of the Canbolatoğlu Rebellion

24 October 1607

Canbolatoğlu Ali Pasha, who had established an independent principality in Aleppo and sought an alliance with Tuscany, was heavily defeated on the Plain of Oruç by the Grand Vizier Kuyucu Murad Pasha. With the re-establishment of central authority in Syria, the most dangerous branch of the Celali wave was broken. Kuyucu Murad Pasha's ruthless purge, lasting two years, secured order in Anatolia, if only temporarily.
