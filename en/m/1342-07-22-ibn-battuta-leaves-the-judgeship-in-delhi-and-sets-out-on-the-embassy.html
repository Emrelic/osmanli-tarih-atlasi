Ibn Battuta leaves the judgeship in Delhi and sets out on the embassy to China

22 July 1342

Ibn Battuta, who had served for years as qadi in the Tughluq Sultanate, leaves Delhi on 22 July 1342 for Sultan Muhammad b. Tughluq's plan to send an envoy to China; the embassy breaks up at Calicut.
