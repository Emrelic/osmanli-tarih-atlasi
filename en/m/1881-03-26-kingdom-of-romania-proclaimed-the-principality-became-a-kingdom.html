Kingdom of Romania proclaimed — the principality became a kingdom

26 March 1881

The Principality of Romania, whose independence was recognized at the Congress of Berlin after the Ottoman-Russian War of 1877-78, was turned into a kingdom on 26 March 1881 (14 March by the Julian calendar) with the crowning of King Carol I; the whole country, including Babadag, Bârlad, Buzău and the capital Bucharest, administratively became part of the new kingdom. With the proclamation of the kingdom the whole of Romania (Iași, Galați, Brăila, Constanța, Tulcea, Calafat and other cities) became the Kingdom of Romania.
