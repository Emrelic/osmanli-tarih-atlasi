The second half of Bregenz was sold to Archduke Ferdinand: Bregenz became entirely Habsburg territory

1523

Half of the county of Montfort-Bregenz had been sold to Duke Sigmund in 1451; in 1523 Hugo XVII von Montfort sold the other half to Archduke Ferdinand (Ferdinand I), and Bregenz became entirely Habsburg.
