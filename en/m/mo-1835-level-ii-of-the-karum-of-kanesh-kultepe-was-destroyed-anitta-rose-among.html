Level II of the karum of Kanesh (Kültepe) was destroyed; Anitta rose among the Anatolian city-states

1835 BC

The destruction of Kültepe Level II is the end of the first great period of the Assyrian merchant karums. Subsequently Anitta, king of Kussara, made Kanesh his capital and opened the way for the political foundation of the Hittites.
