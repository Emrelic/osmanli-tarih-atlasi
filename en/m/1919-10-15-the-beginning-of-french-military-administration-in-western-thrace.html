The beginning of French military administration in Western Thrace

15 October 1919

Western Thrace, held by Bulgaria, which had lost the First World War, was placed under the control of the Entente powers while the peace negotiations were continuing. According to the TDV article on Western Thrace, the region was occupied by French forces on 15 October 1919; the Western Thrace Society for the Defence of Rights moved its headquarters to Gümülcine during this period. French administration continued until the region was left to Greece in May 1920 by the San Remo decision.
