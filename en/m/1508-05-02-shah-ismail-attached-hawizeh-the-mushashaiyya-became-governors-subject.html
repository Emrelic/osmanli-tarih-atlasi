Shah Ismail attached Hawizeh — the Musha'sha'iyya became governors subject to the Safavids

2 May 1508

Shah Ismail had some members of the Musha'sha' dynasty, whom he accused of heresy, put to death and attached the Hawizeh region to his own administration. The Musha'sha' chiefs thereafter continued their existence as governors subject to the Safavids.
