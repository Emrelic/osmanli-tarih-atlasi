The brief liberation of Aydın and its reoccupation on 4 July

30 June 1919

National forces were organized around Ödemiş, Nazilli and Aydın against the Greek occupation. According to the TDV article on Aydın, the city, which had been occupied on 27 May 1919, was liberated for a short time on 30 June, but on 4 July it again came under Greek occupation. The city thereafter remained under occupation until 7 September 1922.
