Hieron II made an alliance with Rome: eastern Sicily became Rome's ally

262 BC

Hieron had at the beginning of the First Punic War allied with Carthage against Rome over the Messana question; in 263 he made peace and alliance with Rome and remained Rome's loyal ally for the rest of his life (until 215). Syracuse became the allied state of Roman Sicily that remained outside the province.
