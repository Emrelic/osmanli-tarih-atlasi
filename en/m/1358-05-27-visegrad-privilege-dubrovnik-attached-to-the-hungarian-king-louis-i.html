Višegrad Privilege — Dubrovnik attached to the Hungarian king Louis I

27 May 1358

When Venice renounced its rights over Dalmatia by the Peace of Zadar (February 1358), Dubrovnik negotiated separately with King Louis I; in May 1358 it was attached to the Hungarian crown by the Višegrad privilege (allegiance in return for protection, annual tribute and troops).
