The capture of Tepeaca — Cortés's first conquest after the Sad Night

4 September 1520

Cortés, who had withdrawn from Tenochtitlan on the night of 30 June 1520 (Noche Triste) with heavy losses, made his first attack after taking refuge in Tlaxcala against the chiefdom of Tepeaca, which was tied to the Triple Alliance. He took the town and founded a Spanish town under the name Segura de la Frontera; thus the road between the Veracruz coast and Tlaxcala was secured and the base for the second march on the capital was prepared.
