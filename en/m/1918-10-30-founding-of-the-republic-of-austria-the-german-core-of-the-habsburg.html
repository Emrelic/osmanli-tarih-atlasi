Founding of the Republic of Austria — the German core of the Habsburg legacy

30 October 1918

When it was announced on 29 October that Czechoslovakia had been founded in Prague and the State of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes in Zagreb, the German Austrians also founded the Republic of Austria on 30 October. On the same day the Ottoman Empire was signing the Armistice of Mudros; the ends of the two empires fell side by side on the calendar.
