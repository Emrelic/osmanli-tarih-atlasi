The Achaean League gave Acrocorinth to Antigonus Doson: Corinth returned to Macedonia

223 BC

The Achaean League, under pressure from Cleomenes III, asked Macedonia for help through Aratus and handed Acrocorinth and Corinth to Antigonus Doson; Doson founded the Hellenic League (Hellenike symmachia).
