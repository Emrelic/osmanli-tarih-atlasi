The Pechenegs brought the steppe between the Don and the Danube under their rule (282/895)

2 March 895

The Pechenegs, allied with the Bulgarian King Simeon, attacked the Magyars and forced them to migrate to Pannonia (282/895); they brought under their rule the steppe region stretching from the Don river to the Danube river. In the atlas, the Cambuyluk steppe, the Donets steppe and Yedişkul are recorded as the Pecheneg Confederation from this date.
