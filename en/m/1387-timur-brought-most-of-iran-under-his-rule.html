Timur brought most of Iran under his rule

1387

According to the Timur entry of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, Timur, who from the early 1380s eliminated the post-Ilkhanid local dynasties one by one, such as the Sarbadars, the Kartids and the Muzaffarids, effectively brought most of Iran under his rule during the 'three-year campaign' of 1386-1388; the final end of the Muzaffarid dynasty would come in 1393. After a century of fragmentation, the region was thus once again bound to a single central authority.
