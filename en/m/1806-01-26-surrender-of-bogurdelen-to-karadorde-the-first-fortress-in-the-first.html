Surrender of Böğürdelen to Karađorđe: the first fortress in the First Serbian Uprising

26 January 1806

During the first Serbian revolution, which began in 1804 as a reaction to the oppression of the dahis and yamaks in Belgrade and later took on a nationalist character, the Fortress of Böğürdelen was surrendered to Karađorđe on 26 January 1806. This fortress on the Sava was the first major Ottoman fortification seized by the rebels and foretold the fall of Belgrade. When the uprising was suppressed in 1813 and Belgrade retaken, the fortress also returned to Ottoman administration; however, by the garrison clause of the 1830 firman, Böğürdelen, together with Belgrade, Smederevo and Fethülislam, was defined as one of the four fortresses within autonomous Serbia where Ottoman troops remained, and it stayed in this status until the garrison was withdrawn in 1867.
