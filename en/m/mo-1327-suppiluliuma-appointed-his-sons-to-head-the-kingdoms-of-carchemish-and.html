Suppiluliuma appointed his sons to head the kingdoms of Carchemish and Aleppo

1327 BC

Suppiluliuma governed the Syria he had conquered through "kings" from the Hittite dynasty: Carchemish and Aleppo were given to his sons. The viceroyalty of Carchemish would continue to exist for about 500 years, even after the Hittite collapse.
