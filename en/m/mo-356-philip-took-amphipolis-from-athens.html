Philip took Amphipolis from Athens

356 BC

Philip besieged and took Athens's Amphipolis at the mouth of the Strymon, gained access to the city's gold and silver mines and timber resources, and opened himself to the Thracian coast. This conquest began the Athenian-Macedonian conflict.
