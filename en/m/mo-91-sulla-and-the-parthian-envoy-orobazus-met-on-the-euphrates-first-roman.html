Sulla and the Parthian envoy Orobazus met on the Euphrates; first Roman-Parthian diplomatic contact

91 BC

Sulla, the governor of Cappadocia, met Orobazus, the envoy of the Parthian king Mithridates II, on the bank of the Euphrates; Rome and Parthia established official contact for the first time. The Euphrates would later become the frontier between the two states.
