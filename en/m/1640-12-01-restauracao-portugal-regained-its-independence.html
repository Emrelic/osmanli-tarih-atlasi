Restauração: Portugal regained its independence

1 December 1640

While the Catalan revolt occupied Spain, a group of nobles in Lisbon raided the palace and overthrew Spanish rule, proclaiming the Duke of Braganza king under the name John IV. The sixty-year Iberian Union thus came to an end; however, Ceuta chose to remain with Spain, and this was recognized by the Treaty of Lisbon of 1668. This window, in which Portugal was bound up in the Habsburg wars, is also the frame of its losses in Asia: Hormuz was lost in 1622, Malacca in 1641, Colombo in 1656; Tangier and Bombay, on the other hand, were given to England as the price of independence. Source: Britannica, History of Portugal — Restoration.
