Selim the Grim's departure from Cairo and Hayır Bey's beylerbeyilik

10 September 1517

Selim the Grim, who had stayed in Egypt for about eight months and established the administrative, financial and military order, left Cairo; Hayır Bey, the former governor of Aleppo, who by withdrawing the left wing at Marj Dabiq had hastened the Mamluk rout, began the administration from the Citadel (Qal'at al-Jabal) as the first Ottoman beylerbeyi of Egypt. The choice of a governor of Mamluk origin was a deliberate policy of transition that ensured the new administration did not appear foreign to the local people. On his return journey the sultan took with him to Istanbul the last Abbasid caliph, al-Mutawakkil III, and the eminent artisans and scholars of Egypt.
