Founding of the Jalayirid State: post-Ilkhanid Azerbaijan

1340

According to the TDV's Jalayirids entry, Hasan-i Buzurg, grandson of the Ilkhanid emir Noyan, taking advantage of the struggles for the throne that broke out with the death of the Ilkhanid ruler Abu Sa'id Bahadur Khan, founded an independent state centered on Tabriz in 1340. The Jalayirids thus became the first great dynasty to fill the post-Ilkhanid vacuum in Azerbaijan and its surroundings; under his successor Uways (1356-1374) their lands would expand as far as Mosul and Diyarbekir.
