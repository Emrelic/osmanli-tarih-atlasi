The Red Army took Vilnius

13 July 1944

In the Vilnius Offensive of 5-13 July, the 3rd Belorussian Front encircled the German garrison and took the city (the Polish Home Army's «Ostra Brama» operation was simultaneous). Vilnius became the capital of the Lithuanian SSR.
