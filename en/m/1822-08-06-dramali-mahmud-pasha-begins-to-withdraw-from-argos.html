Dramali Mahmud Pasha begins to withdraw from Argos

6 August 1822

Forced to fall back before two weeks had passed since he occupied Argos, Dramali Mahmud Pasha began to pull the first division of his army toward Corinth; Argos again fell to the Greek insurgents.
