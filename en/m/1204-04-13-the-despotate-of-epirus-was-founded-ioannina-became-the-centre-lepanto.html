The Despotate of Epirus was founded — Ioannina became the centre, Lepanto passed to the despotate

13 April 1204

After the Fourth Crusade, Michael Angelos founded the Despotate of Epirus; Ioannina was made the centre of the despotate, and Lepanto also remained within the lands of the despotate.
