Pontius Pilate became prefect of Judaea (26-36)

26

Under the Roman emperor Tiberius, Pontius Pilate became prefect (governor) of Judaea, succeeding Valerius Gratus. Josephus writes that Pilate remained in Judaea for ten years. In Christian creeds the suffering of Jesus is referred to with the phrase "under Pontius Pilate".
