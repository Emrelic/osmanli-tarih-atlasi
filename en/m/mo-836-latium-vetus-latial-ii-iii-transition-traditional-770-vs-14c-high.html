Latium Vetus: Latial II -> III transition (traditional ~770 vs 14C high chronology 850-825): Latium: Latial II → III transition (high chronology)

836 BC

The transition from Latial II to III in the Early Iron Age of central Italy (Latium Vetus) is ~770 BCE in the traditional typo-chronology; the 14C of short-lived samples (Fidene seeds) pulls the transition to the third quarter of the 9th century BCE (850-825).
