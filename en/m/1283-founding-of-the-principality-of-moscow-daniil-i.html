Founding of the Principality of Moscow — Daniil I

1283

Daniil, the youngest son of Alexander Nevsky, began to rule Moscow as an independent principality. At that time Moscow was a small and insignificant center of the Vladimir-Suzdal lands; Russian historiography regards this date as the starting point of its later rise.
