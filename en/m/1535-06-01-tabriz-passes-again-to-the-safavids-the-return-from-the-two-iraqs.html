Tabriz passes again to the Safavids — the return from the Two Iraqs

1 June 1535

In the Campaign of the Two Iraqs the army had entered Tabriz, but because the real objective was Baghdad, no permanent garrison had been left in the city. As the army, having spent the winter in Baghdad, turned west, Shah Tahmasb re-entered the evacuated Azerbaijan and took Tabriz back. This was the repeated pattern of the Ottoman-Safavid wars: Tabriz was taken five times and could not be held in any of the five; the city would acquire a lasting Ottoman garrison only in 1585.
