Zhongdu was renamed Dadu (Great Capital)

4 March 1272

The Yuan court changed the name of Zhongdu to Dadu; in those same days Wang Shu (Marquis of Qi'an), the envoy of the king of Koryo, came to congratulate on the proclamation of the new state name.
