Mediolanum (Milan) fell to Rome: the Insubres were defeated, northern Italy opened to Rome

221 BC

Rome, which had defeated the Gauls at Telamon (225), took control of most of the Po plain in 222 BC with Marcellus's victory at Clastidium and the capture of Mediolanum. The region was organized as Cisalpine Gaul.
