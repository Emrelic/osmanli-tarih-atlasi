New Zealand's boundaries extended to include the Cook Islands and Niue

11 June 1901

New Zealand's boundaries were extended to include the Cook Islands (Rarotonga, Aitutaki and the southern islands, and the northern Cook Islands) and Niue; these islands became territory attached to New Zealand.
