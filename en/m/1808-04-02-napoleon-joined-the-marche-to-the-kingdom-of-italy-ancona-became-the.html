Napoleon joined the Marche to the Kingdom of Italy: Ancona became the center of the Metauro department

2 April 1808

By decree no. 158 issued from Saint-Cloud, the Marche, belonging to the Papacy, was added to the Kingdom of Italy; the decree of 20 April 1808 established the departments of Metauro (Ancona), Musone (Macerata) and Tronto (Fermo).
