The British occupation of Nablus: the collapse of the Palestine front

21 September 1918

In September 1918 Allenby's great offensive on the Palestine front shattered the Ottoman Seventh and Eighth Armies; Nablus passed into British hands and four centuries of Ottoman rule in the city came to an end. The city remained under British military administration until 1920.
