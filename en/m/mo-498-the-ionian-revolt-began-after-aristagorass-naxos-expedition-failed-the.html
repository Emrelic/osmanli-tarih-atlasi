The Ionian Revolt began: after Aristagoras's Naxos expedition failed, the Greek cities of Ionia rose against Persia

498 BC

When the Naxos expedition of Aristagoras, tyrant of Miletus, collapsed, the cities of Ionia overthrew their tyrants and asked Athens and Eretria for help. This revolt is the spark of the Greco-Persian Wars.
