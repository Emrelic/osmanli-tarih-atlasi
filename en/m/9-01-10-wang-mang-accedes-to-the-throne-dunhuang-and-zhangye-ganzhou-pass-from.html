Wang Mang accedes to the throne: Dunhuang and Zhangye (Ganzhou) pass from the Western Han to the ownerless interval

10 January 9

Wang Mang received the will in the golden casket at the Han's High Temple, ascended the throne and named the dynasty Xin; the Western Han ended. Because the source does not record Xin-period ownership for Dunhuang and Zhangye in the Hexi corridor, these points pass to the ownerless interval (declared gap). Chang'an passed directly to Xin (separate entry).
