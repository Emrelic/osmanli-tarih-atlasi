The capture of Gyumri — the Eastern Front advanced into the interior of Armenia

7 November 1920

The troops of Kâzım Karabekir Pasha, who continued the operation after the liberation of Kars, took Gyumri on 7 November 1920. Negotiations with the Armenians, who had agreed to an armistice, were conducted at Gyumri, and the war ended with the Treaty of Gyumri signed on 3 December 1920.
