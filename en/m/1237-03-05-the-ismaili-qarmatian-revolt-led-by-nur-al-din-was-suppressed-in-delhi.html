The Isma'ili (Qarmatian) revolt led by Nur al-Din was suppressed in Delhi

5 March 1237

In the first year of Radiyya Begum's reign, the Qarmatians under Nur al-Din (Nur Turk) staged a bloody revolt in Delhi (6 Rajab 634 / 5 March 1237); the revolt was put down with the help of the people. This was a continuation of the Isma'ili threat that had led to Iltutmish's death.
