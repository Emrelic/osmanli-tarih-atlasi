The Opening of the Mühendishâne-i Berrî-i Hümâyun

1 September 1795

A land engineering school was opened at Hasköy to train artillery, fortification and map-making officers for the Nizam-ı Cedid army; it was the land-army counterpart of the Naval Engineering School founded in 1773. In the programme algebra, geometry, mechanics, fortification and ballistics were taught, and mathematics and gunnery books were translated from European languages. This school, regarded as the root of modern engineering education in Turkey, is accepted as the beginning of today's Istanbul Technical University.
