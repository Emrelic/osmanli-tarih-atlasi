The Mongol commander Yasavur Noyan briefly seizes Malatya

1244

After the Köse Dağ victory (1243) the Mongols achieved no result from their first attempts against Malatya; Yasavur Noyan seized the city briefly in 1244, and in 1258 it was devastated by Baiju Noyan.
