Menua extended Urartu from Lake Van to the Araxes Valley and Lake Urmia

809 BC

Under Menua and his son Argishti I, Urartu was transformed from a centre on Lake Van into a kingdom embracing the whole highland country as far as the Araxes and the Lake Urmia region. Tushpa (Van) was enlarged with new settlement and irrigation works.
