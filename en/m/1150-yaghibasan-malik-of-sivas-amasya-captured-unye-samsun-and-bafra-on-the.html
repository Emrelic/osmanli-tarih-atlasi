Yaghibasan, Malik of Sivas-Amasya, captured Ünye, Samsun, and Bafra on the Black Sea coast

1150

The Danishmendid Yaghibasan, in the same year as Ayn al-Dawla's raids in the south, undertook conquests in the Black Sea region and took Ünye, Samsun, and Bafra; he later (1157) returned Bafra and Ünye to the Byzantine Emperor Manuel.
