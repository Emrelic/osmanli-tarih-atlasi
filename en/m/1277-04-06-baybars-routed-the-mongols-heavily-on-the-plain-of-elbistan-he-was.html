Baybars routed the Mongols heavily on the plain of Elbistan; he was received at Kayseri with great demonstrations of affection

6 April 1277

The Mamluk Sultan Baybars, who came to Anatolia at the invitation of Mu'in al-Din Parvana, routed the Mongols heavily on the plain of Elbistan (Dhu'l-Qa'da 675 / April 1277) and came to Kayseri; but because of Parvana's attitude the attempt to free Anatolia from Mongol domination came to nothing, and Abaqa Khan had 200,000 people massacred.
