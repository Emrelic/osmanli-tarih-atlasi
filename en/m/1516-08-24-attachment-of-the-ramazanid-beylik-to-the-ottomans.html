Attachment of the Ramazanid Beylik to the Ottomans

24 August 1516

During the appointments (tevcih) made after the victory of Marj Dabiq, the Ramazanid Beylik, centred on Adana, was given to Mahmud Bey and taken under Ottoman sovereignty. The beylik, which left Mamluk vassalage, was not abolished but left to the family with the status of 'ocaklık'; the Ramazanids continued the administration of Çukurova as Ottoman sancak beyis. This privileged arrangement continued until 1608, the year in which Pir Mansur gave up the beylik. Other places joined on the same date: Malatya, Tarsus, Mosul, Antep.
