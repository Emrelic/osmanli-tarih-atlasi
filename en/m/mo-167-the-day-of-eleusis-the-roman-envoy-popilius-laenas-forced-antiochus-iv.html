The 'Day of Eleusis': the Roman envoy Popilius Laenas forced Antiochus IV to withdraw from Egypt

167 BC

Immediately after the Battle of Pydna (22 June 168 BCE), the Roman envoy Gaius Popilius Laenas delivered to Antiochus, who was besieging Alexandria, the Senate's order to end the war; he drew a circle around the king so that he would consider his answer. Antiochus withdrew. Egypt kept its independence, but under Roman protection.
