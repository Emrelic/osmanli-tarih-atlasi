Surrender of the El Fasher garrison to the Mahdists

15 January 1884

Even after Slatin's surrender on 23 December 1883, the Egyptian garrison in the fortress of El Fasher continued to resist. The garrison, having lost its wells in a seven-day siege, surrendered on 15 January 1884; the arms were laid down and the fortress passed to the Mahdists.
