The Toplica region (Kuršumlija, Prokuplje) came under Ottoman rule

1433

It is considered probable that the Dubočica and Toplica region (around Leskovac, Kuršumlija and Prokuplje) passed to Ottoman rule during Murad II's betrothal to Mara Branković; the tahrir record of 1445-1447 shows that the region was given to Ottoman sipahis as timars. On the map Kuršumlija and Prokuplje are under Ottoman administration from this year.
