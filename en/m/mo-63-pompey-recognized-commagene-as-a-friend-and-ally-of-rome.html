Pompey recognized Commagene as a friend and ally of Rome

63 BC

In his eastern settlement Pompey confirmed the kingdom of Antiochus I and part of the Seleucia (Zeugma) region, making Commagene an allied buffer between Rome and Parthia. The kingdom was annexed to the Roman province in AD 17 and permanently in AD 72 (these events are separate items in the chronology).
