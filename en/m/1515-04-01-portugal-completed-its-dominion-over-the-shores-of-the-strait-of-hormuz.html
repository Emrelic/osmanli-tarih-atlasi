Portugal completed its dominion over the shores of the Strait of Hormuz

1 April 1515

The Persian Gulf campaign begun by Afonso de Albuquerque in 1507 was completed in 1515 so as to include the island of Hormuz and, on the opposite shore, Qishm and Buraimi; the Kingdom of Hormuz, dependent on Iran, effectively came under Portuguese protection, and Portugal took control of the Gulf trade.
