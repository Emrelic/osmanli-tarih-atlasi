Istanbul earthquake of 1894

10 July 1894

The earthquake that occurred off the Gulf of Izmit on 10 July 1894 struck Istanbul severely; many mosques and historic buildings, including the walls of Istanbul, were damaged. The TDV's entry on earthquakes counts this earthquake, together with those of 1509, 1719 and 1766, among the earthquakes that caused great loss of life and property in the Marmara region.
