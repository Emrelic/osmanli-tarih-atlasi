Thutmose III defeated the Canaanite coalition led by Kadesh at Megiddo; Canaan-Syria came under Egyptian rule

1456 BC

In year 23, on the 21st day of the 1st month of Shemu, Thutmose III defeated before Megiddo the Canaanite coalition headed by the ruler of Kadesh; the enemy fled into the city, and Megiddo surrendered after a long siege. This campaign, recorded in the Annals of Karnak, made Egypt's hold on Canaan and Southern Syria permanent and was the beginning of the empire's expansion.
