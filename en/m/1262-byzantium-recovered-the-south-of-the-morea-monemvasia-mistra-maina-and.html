Byzantium recovered the south of the Morea: Monemvasia, Mistra, Maina, and Geraki

1262

Villehardouin, captured at Pelagonia, ceded the fortresses of Monemvasia, Mistra, Maina (Mani), and Geraki to Byzantium in order to be freed; Byzantium regained its first permanent territory in the Morea, and Mistra would later become the center of the Despotate of the Morea.
