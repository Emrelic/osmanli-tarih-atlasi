First revelation: the first five verses of Surah al-ʿAlaq in the cave of Hira (year 610, last ten days of Ramadan)

610

According to Islamic belief, the first revelation came to Muhammad in the cave of Hira through Gabriel, and the first five verses of Surah al-ʿAlaq were revealed; this marks the beginning of prophethood (biʿthah). Historical record: the TDV places the event on a night within the last ten days of Ramadan of the year 610, according to some reports before dawn on a Monday.
