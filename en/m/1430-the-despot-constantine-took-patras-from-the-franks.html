The despot Constantine took Patras from the Franks

1430

Constantine, Despot of the Morea (the last Byzantine emperor in 1449), recovered Patras from the Latins; thus this important port in the northwest of the peninsula was also joined to the despotate. Patras came under Ottoman rule in 1458 in Mehmed II's Morea campaign.
