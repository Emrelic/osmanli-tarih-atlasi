The Founding of the Bulgarian Exarchate: The Separation of the Bulgarian Church from the Phanar

11 March 1870

By imperial firman, an independent Bulgarian church (exarchate) separate from the Greek Patriarchate was established, and which kazas would be attached to the exarchate was left to a popular vote. Although the Sublime Porte took this step to break Greek influence, the result worked in reverse: the church turned into the organised centre of Bulgarian national identity and of the Bulgarian claim over Macedonia. The institutional ground of the Bulgarian-Greek-Serbian rivalry in the Balkans and of the uprisings of 1876 was thus laid.
