Taking of İzmir from Aydınoğlu Cüneyd Bey

1 June 1415

İzmir, the centre of Aydınoğlu Cüneyd Bey, who throughout the Interregnum continually changed sides among the princes, was taken by Çelebi Mehmed after a siege of ten days; Cüneyd was pardoned and sent to the sancak beylik of Nicopolis. The city, which Timur had returned to the Aydınids in 1402, thus returned to the Ottomans after thirteen years. Cüneyd was to seize the city again in 1422, and the matter would be closed only by Murad II in 1424-1426.
