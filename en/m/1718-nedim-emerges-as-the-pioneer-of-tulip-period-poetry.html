Nedim emerges as the pioneer of Tulip Period poetry

1718

During the Tulip Period of 1718-1730, Nedim developed a new poetic style distinguished by perfection of expression, a pursuit of simplicity and a witty manner. This style, which developed around Nevşehirli Damad Ibrahim Pasha, is regarded as the greatest representative of the nativizing current in divan poetry.
