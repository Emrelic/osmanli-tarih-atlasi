Mithridates VI died at Panticapaeum; the Bosporus was left to Pharnaces

62 BC

Mithridates, who had fled from Pompey, committed suicide in the fortress of Panticapaeum (63) after the revolt of his son Pharnaces. Pharnaces handed the Bosporus over to Pompey and was recognized as a friendly king of Rome.
