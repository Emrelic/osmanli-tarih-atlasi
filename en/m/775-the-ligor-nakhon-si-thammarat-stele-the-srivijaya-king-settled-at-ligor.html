The Ligor (Nakhon Si Thammarat) stele: the Srivijaya king settled at Ligor on the Malay Peninsula and had Buddhist temples built (775)

775

The Wat Sema Muang stele shows that the Sumatran kingdom gained a foothold at Ligor on the Malay Peninsula in 775, and that the Srivijaya king (probably Dharmasetu) had a Buddhist temple complex built; the text on the second face refers to the Sailendra lineage.
