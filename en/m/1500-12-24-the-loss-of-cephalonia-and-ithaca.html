The loss of Cephalonia and Ithaca

24 December 1500

Shaken by the capture of Modon and Koron, Venice united with Spain and went on the counteroffensive, besieging Cephalonia, which had been in Ottoman hands since 1479. After forty-seven days of resistance the fortress fell, and the island, together with Ithaca, passed to Venice and did not return to Ottoman administration for three hundred years. This loss created a balance in which what was gained in the Morea was given back in the Ionian.
