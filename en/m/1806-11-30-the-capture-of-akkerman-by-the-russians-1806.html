The capture of Akkerman by the Russians (1806)

30 November 1806

One week after the Russian army crossed the Dniester without a declaration of war, Akkerman was taken by the forces of Prince Cantacuzino; the Tatars in the city abandoned it. Akkerman was left to Russia, together with Bessarabia, by the Treaty of Bucharest of 1812.
