The Bagirmi Sultanate came under French protection

1897

Faced with the threat of Rabih, Gawrang, the ruler of Bagirmi, concluded a compulsory protection treaty with France. When Rabih, reacting to this, moved to occupy Bagirmi, Gawrang burned the capital Massenya with his own hands.
