The Beginning of the Ottoman-Greek War of 1897: The Thessaly Front

17 April 1897

Upon Greece's landing of fleet and troops in Crete and the raids of bands on the Rumelian frontier, the Ottoman State declared war. The Ottoman army, trained by the German reform mission, advanced rapidly in Thessaly and within a month took Yenişehir, Çatalca and Dömeke; the Greek army was dispersed. With the intervention of the great powers the territories won were returned, but the war remained the only decisive military victory of the reign of Abdülhamid II and could not prevent the de facto loss of Crete.
