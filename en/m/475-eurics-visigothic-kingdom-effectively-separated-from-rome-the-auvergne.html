Euric's Visigothic kingdom effectively separated from Rome: the Auvergne was ceded to the Visigoths

475

Euric expanded his kingdom through conquests in Hispania and Gaul from 466; in 475 Julius Nepos ceded the Auvergne and thereby effectively recognized his independent sovereignty. The Visigothic kingdom controlled the Western Roman lands from the Loire to the Mediterranean.
