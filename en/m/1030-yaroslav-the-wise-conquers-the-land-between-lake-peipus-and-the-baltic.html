Yaroslav the Wise conquers the land between Lake Peipus and the Baltic and founds Yuryev (Tartu)

1030

In 1030 Yaroslav conquered the lands between Lake Peipus and the Baltic and founded there the city of Yuryev, named after his Christian name Yuri (George); the city is present-day Tartu in Estonia.
