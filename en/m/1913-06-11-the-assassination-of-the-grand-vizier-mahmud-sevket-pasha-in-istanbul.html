The Assassination of the Grand Vizier Mahmud Şevket Pasha in Istanbul

11 June 1913

Mahmud Şevket Pasha, who had been brought to the grand vizierate after the Raid on the Sublime Porte, was killed by shots fired at his carriage near Çarşıkapı while he was going to the Ministry of War. The Committee of Union and Progress, blaming the assassination on the opposition, began a wave of mass arrests and executions, and the Freedom and Accord Party was effectively liquidated. With this event the one-party dictatorship of the Unionists, based on the Enver-Talat-Cemal triumvirate, was firmly established.
