Carian War: Ptolemy II took Ionia, Caria, Lycia, Pamphylia and part of Cilicia from Antiochus I

279 BC

In a short and successful campaign Ptolemy II took the southern and western coast of Anatolia and reached Damascus; seeing that Antiochus's strength was not inferior to his father's, he made a truce. Antiochus accepted the loss, since he had to organize his father's conquests in the interior of Anatolia.
