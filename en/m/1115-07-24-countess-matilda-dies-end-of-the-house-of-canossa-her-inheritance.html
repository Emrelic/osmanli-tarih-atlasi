Countess Matilda dies; end of the House of Canossa (her inheritance became a matter of dispute between the papacy and the empire)

24 July 1115

The House of Canossa (Canossa/Matilda) came to an end (certainty: {"f": "yuzyil", "t": "gun"}). Also 'Matilde di Canossa' https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/matilde-di-canossa/ : Matilda's estate comprised Brescia, Bergamo, Mantua and the counties of Arezzo, Siena and Corneto.
