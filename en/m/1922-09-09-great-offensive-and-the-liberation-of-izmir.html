Great Offensive and the liberation of Izmir

9 September 1922

The offensive launched from Kocatepe on the morning of 26 August reached the annihilation of the main Greek forces on 30 August at Dumlupınar with the Battle of the Commander-in-Chief; in the pursuit that continued with the order 'Armies, your first objective is the Mediterranean, forward!', Izmir was liberated on 9 September. Eastern Thrace was recovered without war through the Armistice of Mudanya (11 October); the military victory determined the strength at the table of the new state to be established at Lausanne.
