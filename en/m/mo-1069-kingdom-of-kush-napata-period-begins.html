Kingdom of Kush — Napata period: begins

1069 BC

The Napata-centered period of the Kushite dynasty; the center shifts to Meroe in ~300 BCE. The 25th Dynasty (Kush), which conquered Egypt, falls in this period — this source does not include the dates of the 25th Dynasty.
