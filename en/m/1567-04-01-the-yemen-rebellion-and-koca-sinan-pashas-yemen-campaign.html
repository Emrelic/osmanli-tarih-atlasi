The Yemen Rebellion and Koca Sinan Pasha's Yemen Campaign

1 April 1567

The Zaydi imam al-Mutahhar rose in revolt on the grounds that taxes were being collected by force and tore Ottoman administration out of the greater part of Yemen. Koca Sinan Pasha, sent from Istanbul, re-established Ottoman dominion in the region through the operations he conducted between 1568 and 1570, taking Kawkaban after a long siege. The campaign was of vital importance for preserving control of the southern gate of the Red Sea and of the Indian Ocean trade route.
