Kingdom (culture) of Kerma (Kush): ends

1449 BC

A Nubian kingdom centered on Kerma below the Third Cataract; it passes through the Early Kerma (~2500), Middle and Classic Kerma phases and ends in ~1450 BCE. Honegger 2005: 'Kerma Classique (vers 1500 av. J.-C.)' a fortified settlement.
