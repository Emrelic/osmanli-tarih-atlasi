Surrender of the city of Tripoli to the Italians

9 October 1911

Tripoli surrendered on 9 October 1911 after three days of bombardment. Instead of defending the city, the Ottoman forces chose to withdraw to Aziziya and Gharyan and organize resistance in the interior; this resistance, in which Enver and Mustafa Kemal Beys took part, confined the Italians to the coastal strip until the end of the war. That is, although the city fell, the interior of the province remained under Ottoman control.
