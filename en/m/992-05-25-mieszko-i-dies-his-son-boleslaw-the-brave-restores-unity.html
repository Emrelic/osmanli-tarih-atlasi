Mieszko I dies; his son Bolesław the Brave restores unity

25 May 992

Mieszko, who died on 25 May 992, had divided his lands among his sons and relatives; his eldest son Bolesław the Brave (Chrobry) quickly eliminated his rivals and secured the unity of the state.
