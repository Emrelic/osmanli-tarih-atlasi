Pannonia was organized as a separate Roman province

9

After the suppression of the Pannonian and Dalmatian revolt that began in AD 6, the region was organized in AD 9 as a separate province garrisoned by three legions; under Trajan (about 106) it was divided into Upper and Lower Pannonia.
