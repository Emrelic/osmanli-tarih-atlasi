The death of Mustafa II

29 December 1703

Mustafa II, who left the throne to his brother Ahmed III through the Edirne Incident, died about five months after his deposition in Edirne, which he loved very much. To his illness was added grief over the latest developments. Some Western sources write that the belief that he had been poisoned was widespread among the people.
