Battle of Jabal al-Dayqa — the Shayqiya confederation is decisively defeated by the Egyptian army

4 December 1820

A month after the first clash at Kurti, the army of Ismail Pasha, son of Muhammad Ali Pasha, defeated the Shayqiya in the second and decisive battle near Jabal al-Dayqa. The Shayqiya chief Makk Subayr surrendered; Makk Shawish continued to resist. Egyptian rule over the Nile bend became permanent from then on.
