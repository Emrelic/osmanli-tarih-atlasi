The Opening of the Suez Canal: The Shortening of the Route to India via Egypt

17 November 1869

The construction, begun in 1859, lasted ten years, and the 163-kilometre canal linking the Mediterranean with the Red Sea entered service with a magnificent ceremony. When the route linking Europe with India was shortened by thousands of miles, Egypt suddenly became a key point of world trade. This geostrategic value would be the principal reason for England's occupation of Egypt thirteen years after the opening of the canal; the debt the canal placed on Egypt would drag the khedivate into bankruptcy.
