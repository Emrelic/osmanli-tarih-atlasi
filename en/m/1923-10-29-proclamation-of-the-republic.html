Proclamation of the Republic

29 October 1923

The Republic was proclaimed when the Grand National Assembly, through a constitutional amendment, adopted the provision that 'the form of government of the State of Turkey is a republic'; Mustafa Kemal Pasha was elected the first president. The 624-year Ottoman adventure, which began in 1299 as a frontier principality, was thus closed, and the new state, in which sovereignty belongs unconditionally to the nation, was founded upon the legacy of the empire.
