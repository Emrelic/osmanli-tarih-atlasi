The Siamese King Naresuan defeated the Burmese crown prince; Siamese independence became definitive

1 February 1593

Hall writes that Nanda Bayin's efforts to reestablish dominion over Siam were halted by a decisive defeat in 1593 and that from that moment Siamese independence was assured; Pamaree Surakiat also records that Naresuan won a great victory over the Burmese crown prince at Nong Sarai in 1593 and then took Tavoy and Tenasserim under his control.
