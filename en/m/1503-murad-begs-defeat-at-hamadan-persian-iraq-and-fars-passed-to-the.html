Murad Beg's defeat at Hamadan: Persian Iraq and Fars passed to the Safavids

1503

Sultan Murad, the second claimant to the Aqquyunlu throne, suffered a heavy defeat in a battle fought against Shah Ismail near Hamadan and fled to Baghdad. With this single battle the regions of Persian Iraq, Fars and Kerman came under Safavid administration; on the map these regions change hands at the same time. This is the single greatest step in the dissolution of the Aqquyunlu State — the loss of Tabriz had left the dynasty without a capital, and the defeat at Hamadan left it without land.
