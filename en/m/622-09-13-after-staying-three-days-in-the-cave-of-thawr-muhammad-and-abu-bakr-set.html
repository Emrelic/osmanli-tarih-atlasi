After staying three days in the cave of Thawr, Muhammad and Abu Bakr set out for Yathrib (1 Rabi' al-awwal / 13 September 622)

13 September 622

When the polytheists resolved to kill Muhammad, he left Mecca by night with Abu Bakr and hid in the cave on Mount Thawr; three days later they set out for Yathrib on camels brought by their guides. The Quraysh promised a reward of 100 camels to whoever captured them but achieved nothing.
