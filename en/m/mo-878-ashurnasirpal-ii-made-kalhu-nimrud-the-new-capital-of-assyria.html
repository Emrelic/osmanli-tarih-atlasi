Ashurnasirpal II made Kalhu (Nimrud) the new capital of Assyria

878 BC

Ashurnasirpal II transformed Kalhu, a small town, into a great capital with 47,000 workers; the north-west palace was built in a city six times the size of Aššur. Kalhu remained the capital of Assyria until 706.
