The Isma'ili leader Rukn al-Din Khurshah surrendered Alamut to Hulagu

19 November 1256

Rukn al-Din Khurshah, the leader of the Isma'ilis at Alamut, who could not hold out against the Mongols, surrendered to Hulagu on 19 November 1256; Hulagu razed the fortress and put its people to the sword, and the Isma'ili fortresses in Syria were taken by Otchigin Noyan.
