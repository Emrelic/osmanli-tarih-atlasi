Lithuania took over Vilnius — the price of the Soviet-Lithuanian mutual assistance pact

28 October 1939

Vilnius, which the Red Army had taken on 19 September 1939, was ceded to Lithuania by the mutual assistance pact of 10 October 1939; the Lithuanian army entered the city on 28 October. In return Soviet garrisons were stationed in Lithuania, and annexation followed in 1940.
