The Kedukan Bukit inscription: the king of Srivijaya set out on a siddhayatra expedition by water and land on 23 April 682; a month later he returned victorious to Srivijaya

23 April 682

According to the Kedukan Bukit inscription at the foot of the Seguntang hill near Palembang, a king set out on an expedition by boat on 23 April 682, left a river mouth with his army (by land and sea) on 19 May, and a month later brought victory, power and wealth to Srivijaya. The text is the earliest known dated document of Srivijaya.
