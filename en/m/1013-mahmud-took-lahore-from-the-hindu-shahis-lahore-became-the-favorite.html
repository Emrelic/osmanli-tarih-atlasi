Mahmud took Lahore from the Hindu Shahis; Lahore became the favorite city of the Ghaznavids

1013

Lahore, which had been in the hands of the Hindu Shahis since 991, passed to Mahmud of Ghazna in 1013–1014; Mahmud fortified Lahore Fort and established a garrison there of Turkish and Tajik soldiers brought from Khurasan. When the Ghaznavids lost their independence (after 1157), Lahore became the center of the portion that survived in the east.
