The Jewish revolt in Egypt, Cyrene and Cyprus: under Trajan, Rome suppressed the region with difficulty

115

While Trajan's Parthian campaign was under way, the Jews of Cyrene under Andreas massacred the Romans and Greeks; according to Dio, similar events took place in Egypt and Cyprus, and the number of dead was given in the hundreds of thousands. Lusius Quietus, whom Trajan sent, suppressed the revolt; Jews were forbidden to enter Cyprus.
