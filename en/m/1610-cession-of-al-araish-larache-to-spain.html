Cession of al-Araish (Larache) to Spain

1610

In the years of civil war in which the Saadian dynasty was fragmented, the port of al-Araish on the Atlantic coast was left to Spain. The TDV's Saadians entry records that in this period the country was divided into two as the emirates of Marrakesh (south) and Fez (north), and that the parties in the struggle for the throne sought outside help. The port would remain in Spanish hands for nearly eighty years, and Mulay Ismail would retake it in 1689.
