Oleg descends from Novgorod and takes Kiev; the Rurikid dynasty is established in Kiev

882

Oleg, the first successor of the dynasty founded by the Varangians (Rus) around Rurik, marched south from Novgorod, occupied Kiev and extended his rule over southern Russia; with this event the core of Kievan Rus' took shape.
