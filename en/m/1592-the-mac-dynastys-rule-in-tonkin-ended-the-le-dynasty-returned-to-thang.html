The Mạc dynasty's rule in Tonkin ended; the Lê dynasty returned to Thăng Long (Hanoi)

1592

Mạc Đăng Dung had usurped the throne in 1527, and in 1533 the Lê dynasty was re-established through the Nguyễn family (Nguyễn Kim drove the Mạc out of Nghệ An and Thanh Hóa) but left Tonkin in Mạc hands. According to Hall, the Mạc dynasty ruled Tonkin until 1592.
