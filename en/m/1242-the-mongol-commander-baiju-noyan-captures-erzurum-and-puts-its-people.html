The Mongol commander Baiju Noyan captures Erzurum and puts its people to the sword

1242

Baiju Noyan, the Mongol commander in Azerbaijan, taking advantage of the Seljuks' weakening by the Baba'i revolt, seized Erzurum at the end of 1242, put its people to the sword, and returned to the Mughan to attack Anatolia again in the spring.
