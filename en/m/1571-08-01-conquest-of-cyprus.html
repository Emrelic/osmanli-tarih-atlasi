Conquest of Cyprus

1 August 1571

A landing was made in 1570 on Cyprus, under Venetian rule; Nicosia fell quickly, while Famagusta surrendered only after an eleven-month siege in August 1571. The island, critical for the security of the Eastern Mediterranean trade and pilgrimage routes, was organized as a beylerbeylik and population from Anatolia was settled there; the conquest was also the pretext of the great Crusader alliance that would culminate at Lepanto.
