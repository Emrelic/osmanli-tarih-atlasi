Septimius Severus made Mesopotamia a province; Ctesiphon was sacked

198

In the Parthian War (195–198) Severus took Nisibis, sacked Ctesiphon, and organized Upper Mesopotamia as a Roman province. The province carried Rome's easternmost advance into the 3rd century.
