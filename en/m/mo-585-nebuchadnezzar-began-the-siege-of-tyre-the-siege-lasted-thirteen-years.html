Nebuchadnezzar began the siege of Tyre; the siege lasted thirteen years

585 BC

According to Josephus (Against Apion 1.156), Nebuchadnezzar besieged Tyre for thirteen years in the time of King Ithobaal. The siege finally ended in a peace or vassalage arrangement; Tyre came within the sphere of Babylonian influence while the independent government of the island city continued.
