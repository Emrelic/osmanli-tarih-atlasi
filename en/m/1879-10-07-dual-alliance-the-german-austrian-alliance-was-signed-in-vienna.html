Dual Alliance — the German-Austrian Alliance was signed in Vienna

7 October 1879

After the Congress of Berlin, Germany and Austria-Hungary formed a mutual defensive alliance against Russia. The secret treaty signed by Andrássy and Prince Reuss turned into the Triple Alliance with Italy's joining in 1882 and lasted until 1914.
