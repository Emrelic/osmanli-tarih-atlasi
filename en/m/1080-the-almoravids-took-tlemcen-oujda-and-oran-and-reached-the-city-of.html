The Almoravids took Tlemcen, Oujda and Oran and reached the city of Algiers

1080

Yusuf b. Tashfin turned toward the Central Maghrib, took Oujda, Tlemcen and Oran, and reached the city of Algiers (1080); in Algiers he had the Great Mosque built.
