The Nevşehir and Ürgüp region passed from the Karamanids to the Ottomans

1398

In Bayezid the Thunderbolt's campaigns against the Karamanids, the Nevşehir (Muşkara) and Ürgüp region passed to Ottoman administration in 800/1398; in 1402, after the Battle of Ankara, Timur gave the region back to the Karamanids.
