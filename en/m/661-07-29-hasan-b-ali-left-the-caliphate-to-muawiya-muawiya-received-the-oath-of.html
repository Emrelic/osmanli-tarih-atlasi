Hasan b. Ali left the caliphate to Mu'awiya; Mu'awiya received the oath of allegiance in Kufa, Kufa passed to Umayyad rule

29 July 661

Hasan b. Ali, who became caliph after Ali was martyred by Ibn Muljam in the Mosque of Kufa (January 661), left the caliphate to Mu'awiya; Mu'awiya came to Kufa and received the oath of allegiance from the people (25 Rabi' I 41 / 29 July 661). The Rashidun period closed and the city came under Umayyad administration (the Zubayrid interlude of 680-691 was not recorded separately).
