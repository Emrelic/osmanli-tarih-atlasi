The Volga Bulgars freed themselves from Khazar domination and became fully independent

965

When the Khazar Khaganate weakened as a result of Pecheneg and Cuman-Kipchak raids and the heavy blow it suffered from the Rus, the Volga Bulgars won their complete independence in 965.
