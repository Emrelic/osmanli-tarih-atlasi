Treaty of Ahmed Pasha — return of the greater part of Western Iran

8 January 1732

After Nadir Shah's victory at Malayer and successive Iranian offensives, the governor of Baghdad Ahmed Pasha signed a peace text without consulting the center. According to the treaty the Aras river was accepted as the border; Western Iranian cities such as Hamadan, Kermanshah, Ardalan, Ardabil and Tabriz were returned to Iran, while Ganja and Tbilisi remained Ottoman. This text, which was received with reaction in Istanbul, was found insufficient by Nadir Shah as well, and before the year 1732 was out the war flared up again. The atlas also regards Kotur, which follows Hoy's chain, as having passed from the Ottomans to the Safavids on this day (TDV hoy; no day specific to Kotur in the source — declared approximation).
