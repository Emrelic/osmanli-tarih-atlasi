The Uprising of Karayazıcı Abdülhalim

1599

Karayazıcı Abdülhalim, who gathered the sekbans and levends who had deserted the army in the Long War, took a wide region of Central Anatolia under his control, seized the Fortress of Urfa (November 1599), proclaimed his sultanate with the title 'Halim Shah' and sent out decrees bearing his own tughra. Regarded as the first leader of the great Celali wave, Karayazıcı died in 1602, and his movement was continued by his brother Deli Hasan. The uprising marks the beginning of the period of the 'great flight', when the Anatolian peasantry abandoned their land en masse.
