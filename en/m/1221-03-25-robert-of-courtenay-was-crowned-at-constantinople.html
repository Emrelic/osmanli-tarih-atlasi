Robert of Courtenay was crowned at Constantinople

25 March 1221

After the death of Empress Yolanda in 1219, the Latin throne passed to her second son Robert of Courtenay; Robert was crowned at Constantinople on 25 March 1221 by the new Latin Patriarch Matthew.
