Construction of the Kilitbahir fortress — control of the Dardanelles

1463

Mehmed the Conqueror had the Kilitbahir fortress built on the Rumelian shore in order to keep the sea passage of the Dardanelles under control; together with Kal'a-i Sultâniyye on the opposite (Anatolian) shore it formed a pair that held the narrowest point of the strait under cannon fire. There are two accounts as to when the fortress was built. Evliya Çelebi writes that the fortress was built before the conquest of Constantinople, in the year 856 (1452). The TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, however, accepts that the fortress was built after the conquest: Tursun Bey, the historian of the period, relates that the two fortresses were built after the conquest of Constantinople, and Kritovoulos reports that Yâkub Bey, the governor of Gallipoli and Bolayır, was charged with this task in 867 (1463) and that the construction was completed in 869 (1464-65). The fortress was repaired and enlarged with the addition of a tower in the reign of Süleyman the Lawgiver (inscription dated 948/1541).
