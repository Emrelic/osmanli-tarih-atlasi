Bocchus surrendered Jugurtha to Rome; western Numidia was added to Mauretania

104 BC

Bocchus I, king of Mauretania and Jugurtha's father-in-law, came to terms with Rome by handing Jugurtha over to Sulla; in return the western part of Numidia was left to him. Jugurtha died in prison in Rome in 104.
