The Peace of Samos: Kushite envoys obtained peace from Augustus; Rome lifted the tribute it had imposed on Kush, and the frontier was drawn in Lower Nubia (Hiera Sykaminos)

20 BC

The envoys of the Candace, guided by Petronius, went to Samos, where they found Augustus; Augustus granted all their requests and also lifted the tributes that Petronius had imposed on Kush. As a result of the peace, the Roman-Kushite frontier settled at the line of Hiera Sykaminos (Maharraqa), south of Philae, and for about three hundred years there was no serious conflict.
