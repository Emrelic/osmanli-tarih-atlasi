Treaty of Lausanne

24 July 1923

With the Treaty of Lausanne, signed at the end of eight months of hard negotiations, the borders, full independence and sovereignty of the new Turkish state were recognized internationally; the capitulations were abolished, the Ottoman Public Debt Administration was put into liquidation, and the exchange of populations was arranged. Lausanne, which effectively nullified Sèvres, is regarded as the diplomatic victory of the National Struggle and the founding foreign document of the Republic.
