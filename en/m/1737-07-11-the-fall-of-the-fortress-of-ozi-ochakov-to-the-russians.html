The Fall of the Fortress of Özi (Ochakov) to the Russians

11 July 1737

The Russian army under Field Marshal Münnich besieged the Fortress of Özi at the mouth of the Dnieper; the defence collapsed when the arsenal exploded, and the fortress fell with great loss of life. The loss of Özi, regarded as the lock of the northwestern shore of the Black Sea, opened the road to Crimea to the Russians and created great panic in Istanbul. The following year the fortress would be evacuated by the Russians because of epidemic and supply difficulties and would pass to the Ottomans again.
