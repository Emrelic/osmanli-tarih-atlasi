Nicaea was surrendered to the Byzantines

19 June 1097

The Turks, left helpless when Alexios blocked help by sea and the Crusaders received reinforcements, came to an agreement with the Byzantine commander Manuel Butumites and surrendered Nicaea to him (19 June 1097); the Seljuk dynasty chose Konya as its capital.
