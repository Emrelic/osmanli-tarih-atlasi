Muhammad ibn Saud began to rule as an independent emir in Diriyah

1726

Muhammad ibn Saud, a descendant of Mani' ibn Rabi'a and the emir who gave his name to today's Saudi dynasty, began to rule independently in Diriyah and its surroundings in 1726.
