Sökmen seizes Mardin after the death of his nephew Yaquti

1103

Mardin was under the rule of Sökmen's nephew Yaquti; taking advantage of the dispute that arose on his death, Sökmen seized the city in 1103 and bound to himself the greater part of the Diyarbakır region.
