Pope Gregory VII excommunicates Henry IV a second time and deposes him

7 March 1080

Under the pressure of the Saxons after the victory of Flarchheim (27 January 1080) and under the influence of false information, Gregory VII abandoned his policy of waiting; on 7 March 1080 he again declared Henry excommunicated and deposed, and released his subjects from their oaths of allegiance. The effect was much weaker than in 1076; the Synod of Brixen deposed Gregory on 25 June 1080.
