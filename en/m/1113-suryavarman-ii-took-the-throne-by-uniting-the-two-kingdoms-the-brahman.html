Suryavarman II took the throne by uniting the two kingdoms; the Brahman Divakara crowned him (the future builder of Angkor Wat)

1113

According to Coedès, Suryavarman II overthrew Dharanindravarman I after a one-day battle and united the 'double kingdom'; the coronation was performed by Divakara in 1113. The ruler undertook great campaigns against China, Dai Viet and Champa, and Angkor Wat was built in his reign.
