Mahmud of Ghazna took Sistan and captured Khalaf; the Saffarids came to an end

1003

Following Khalaf's killing of his son Tahir and further events, Mahmud marched on Sistan a second time and captured Khalaf, spared his life and sent him to Juzjan; the Saffarid dynasty came to an end in 393 (1003).
