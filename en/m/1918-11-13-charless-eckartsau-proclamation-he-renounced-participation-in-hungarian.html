Charles's Eckartsau proclamation: he renounced participation in Hungarian affairs of state and released officials from their oaths of allegiance

13 November 1918

The proclamation written at Eckartsau is dated 13 November 1918; it was published in Budapest on 14 November. Charles renounced only the exercise of his sovereign rights and did not use the words 'abdication of the throne'. On the map the Hungarian chains turn on 11 November, and the proclamation of the People's Republic in the künye on 16 November; 13 November is the legal link between these two days.
