Banja Luka is first mentioned in an instruction of King Vladislaus II

6 February 1494

The name Banja Luka first appears in an instruction of Vladislaus II, king of Hungary and Croatia, dated 6 February 1494; the region was attached to the Hungarian crown through the banate of Jajce. The Ottoman conquest was in the winter of 1527–28.
