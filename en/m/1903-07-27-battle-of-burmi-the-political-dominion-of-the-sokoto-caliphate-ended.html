Battle of Burmi — the political dominion of the Sokoto Caliphate ended

27 July 1903

Caliph Muhammad Tahir, who fled after the British occupation of Sokoto, was killed in a new battle with the British; the political dominion of the Sokoto Caliphate, which had lasted about a century, ended.
