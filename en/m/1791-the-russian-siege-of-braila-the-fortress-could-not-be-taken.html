The Russian siege of Brăila — the fortress could not be taken

1791

Although the fortress of Brăila was besieged by the Russians in June 1791, it could not be taken. After the rout that took place at Măcin in the same summer, the parties would sit down to an armistice, and Brăila would remain in Ottoman hands.
