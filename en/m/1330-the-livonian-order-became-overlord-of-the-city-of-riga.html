The Livonian Order became overlord of the city of Riga

1330

The archbishopric and city of Riga, which had fought against the Order with the support of the Lithuanian grand dukes Vytenis and Gediminas, were defeated in 1330; the Livonian Order became the city's overlord. With the 1452 Treaty of Salaspils, Riga came under the joint overlordship of the Order and the archbishop.
