The Greater Hamburg Act entered into force — end of Lübeck's status as an independent state

1 April 1937

The Greater Hamburg Act, enacted on 26 January 1937, entered into force on 1 April 1937; Lübeck lost its autonomy of more than 700 years and was attached to Prussia's province of Schleswig-Holstein, while Hamburg's borders expanded.
