Manuel Komnenos entered Antioch in ceremony

1159

With his Cilician and Syrian campaign of 1158-1159, Manuel Komnenos again demonstrated Byzantine superiority over the Crusader Principality of Antioch; his ceremonial entry into Antioch is regarded as the peak of his rule.
