Visit of Kaiser Wilhelm II to Istanbul and Jerusalem

18 October 1898

The German emperor's second visit cemented the Ottoman-German rapprochement; the way was opened to the Baghdad Railway concession, and in Damascus the speech about being the 'friend of three hundred million Muslims' was made.
