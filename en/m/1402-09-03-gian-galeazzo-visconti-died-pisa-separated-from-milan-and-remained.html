Gian Galeazzo Visconti died: Pisa separated from Milan and remained independent under Gabriele Maria Visconti

3 September 1402

With the death of Gian Galeazzo (September 1402), Pisa passed by will to his illegitimate son Gabriele Maria Visconti; Treccani EI and SNS describe this as Pisa's brief independence. Gabriele Maria sold his rights to Florence on 27 August 1405, and the city was conquered in October 1406.
