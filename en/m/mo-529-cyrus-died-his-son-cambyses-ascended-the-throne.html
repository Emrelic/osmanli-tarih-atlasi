Cyrus died; his son Cambyses ascended the throne

529 BC

According to Herodotus, Cyrus died in battle in the land of the Massagetae (according to Xenophon, he died in his palace); news of his death reached Babylon in December 530 and documents were dated to "the first year of Cambyses". Cambyses expanded the empire with the conquest of Egypt.
