The surrender of Erivan to Nadir Khan

3 October 1735

Nadir Khan, who after the rout at Arpaçay (Baghavard) had taken Ganja on 9 July and Tiflis on 12 August, captured Erivan as well. The nahiyes of Karpi (Üçkilise), Şerür and Maku also passed to Iran together with the provincial center.
