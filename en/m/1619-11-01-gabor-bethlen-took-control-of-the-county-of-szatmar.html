Gábor Bethlen took control of the county of Szatmár

1 November 1619

With Gábor Bethlen's Hungarian campaign in the autumn of 1619, the county of Szatmár came under the control of the prince of Transylvania; in November 1619 he was addressing the county by letter. The preliminary agreement of 23 January 1620 and the Peace of Nikolsburg left the seven counties, including Szatmár, to Bethlen for life. After Bethlen's death (15 November 1629) the return of the counties to the crown was completed on 30 March 1630.
