Katib Çelebi undertakes to rewrite the Cihannüma from scratch

1 December 1654

Katib Çelebi, who had left his first composition unfinished, undertook at the end of 1654 to rewrite the Cihannüma from scratch. This second version too could not be completed; the work was left unfinished with the author's death in 1657 when it had reached Van, and was printed in this form only in 1732 by Ibrahim Müteferrika.
