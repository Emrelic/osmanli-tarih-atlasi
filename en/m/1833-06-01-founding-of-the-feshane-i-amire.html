Founding of the Feshane-i Amire

1 June 1833

After the abolition of the Janissary Corps in 1826, to meet the fez needs of the army called the Asakir-i Mansure-i Muhammediyye, fez production began in 1833 at the Cündi Meydanı in Kadırga with a capital of 1.5 million kuruş. The facilities were moved in 1839 to the Hatice Sultan Palace at Eyüp; the Feshane formed the foundation of the Ottoman textile industry.
