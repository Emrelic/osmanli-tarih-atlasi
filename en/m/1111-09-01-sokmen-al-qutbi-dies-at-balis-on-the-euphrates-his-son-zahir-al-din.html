Sökmen al-Qutbi dies at Balis on the Euphrates; his son Zahir al-Din Ibrahim succeeds him

1 September 1111

Sökmen, who had joined the army of Mawdud, son of Altuntegin, sent by Muhammad Tapar against the Crusaders, left the army when his illness grew worse and died at Balis in September 1111. His son Ibrahim became head of the Shah-i Armans (Ahlatshahs) and remained until 1126/1127.
