Izapa (Chiapas): Middle Formative royal capital and first white clay surface: Izapa: Middle Formative settlement

749 BC

Izapa was founded as a royal capital in the second half of the Middle Formative (750–300 cal BC). In the Escalón phase (750–500 cal BC) a white clay surface was made beneath Mound 30a; in the Guillén phase (300–100 cal BC, Late Formative) construction and stela erection continue at Mound 62; the E-Group was founded at the end of the Middle Formative and expanded in Guillén.
