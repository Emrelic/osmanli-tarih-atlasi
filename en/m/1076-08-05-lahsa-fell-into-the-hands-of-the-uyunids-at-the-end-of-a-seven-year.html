Lahsa fell into the hands of the Uyunids at the end of a seven-year siege: the Qarmatians left Lahsa (469/1076)

5 August 1076

Abdallah b. Ali al-Uyuni, of the Banu Murra branch of the Abd al-Qays tribe, seized Lahsa at the end of a siege lasting seven years (469/1076); with this event the rule of the Qarmatian State of Bahrain in Lahsa came to an end. On the map Lahsa is shown as blank after this date (the Uyunids are not shown separately).
