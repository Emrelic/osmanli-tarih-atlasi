A Teutonic Order castle is founded for Ottokar II, king of Bohemia: Königsberg

1255

The city that grew around the castle built for the Teutonic Order (1255) during King Ottokar II's crusade in Prussia was named Königsberg in his honour. The city was destroyed in the Prussian uprising of 1263 and rebuilt in 1286.
