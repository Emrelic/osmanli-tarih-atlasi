Taking of the western Black Sea coast: Amasra

20 June 1459

Amasra, the fortified colony of Genoa on the western Black Sea, surrendered without resistance under the combined pressure of the fleet and land forces of Mehmed the Conqueror. This port on the Bartın-Zonguldak coast had stood as a Latin pocket between the Candarids and Ottoman territory; with its taking the coast was unified. The fall of Amasra was the first step of the great Black Sea campaign directed two years later (1461) at Sinop and Trabzon.
