The Republic of Dubrovnik purchased Pelješac together with Ston from the Serbian King Dušan

1333

In 1333 Dubrovnik purchased the Pelješac peninsula together with Ston from the Serbian king Stefan Dušan; with its saltworks (solana), Ston became the Republic's second city.
