Sullivan's army destroys Genesee (Chenesee) Town and its corn and bean crops

15 September 1779

Sullivan's army entered Genesee (Chenesee) Town, which had about seventy houses, on 14 September 1779; on 15 September the whole army took to the fields to destroy the crops, the corn and beans were burned, and the villages were left in flames as the march back began.
