Lipova (Lippa) surrendered to Caraffa — passed to Austria

22 June 1688

After Antonio Caraffa's three-day siege, Lipova surrendered between 12 and 22 June 1688 and passed into Austrian hands. The Ottomans later retook the city, Austria recaptured it in 1691; in the campaign of 1695 the Ottomans regained control. Other settlements that passed in the atlas from the Ottomans to the Habsburgs on the same date: Arad and Világos (Şiria). For Arad the source gives only the year (MKL, Arad: in Ottoman hands between 1551 and 1688); the day is taken from Lipova, and Világos follows Arad's chain (declared approximation). The fortresses of Lugos (Lugoj) and Karánsebes (Caransebeş) also passed to Austria at the end of June (Bánlaky 0016/1302 'Jenővel elveszett Lugos és Karánsebes'; 0017/1443 'június végén').
