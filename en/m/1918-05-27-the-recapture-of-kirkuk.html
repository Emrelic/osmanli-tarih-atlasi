The recapture of Kirkuk

27 May 1918

After a British occupation lasting twenty days, Kirkuk was retaken by Ottoman forces on 27 May 1918. The city passed again to British administration when, upon the British attacks that began on 28 October 1918, the Ottoman troops withdrew to Altun Kupri.
