After the Ustyurt-Mangyshlak campaign, Alp Arslan gave Khwarazm to his son Ayaz

1066

Alp Arslan set out on a campaign to ensure security in the Ustyurt between the Caspian and the Aral and to protect the Khwarazm-Itil trade caravans (458/1066); after the Mangyshlak campaign he gave Khwarazm to his son Ayaz. Khwarazm was probably governed in the reigns of Alp Arslan and Malikshah by governors chosen from among local chiefs.
