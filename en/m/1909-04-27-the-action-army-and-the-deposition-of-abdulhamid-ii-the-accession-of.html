The Action Army and the Deposition of Abdülhamid II: The Accession of Mehmed V Reşad

27 April 1909

The Action Army (Hareket Ordusu), which set out from Salonica upon the 31 March Incident, entered Istanbul in four columns on the night of 22-23 April, and on 24 April the Yıldız Palace surrendered. The Meclis-i Umûmî-i Millî decided on 27 April on the deposition of Abdülhamid II; the sultan was sent to Salonica, and his brother Mehmed V Reşad was placed on the throne. The personal rule of thirty-three years thus ended, but it was replaced by a new period in which the sultan became a symbol and the Committee of Union and Progress governed with the support of the army.
