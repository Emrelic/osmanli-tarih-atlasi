The taking of Asir under direct administration

1871

When the strategic value of the Red Sea rose with the opening of the Suez Canal, it was recognised that the Asir mountains, in the middle of the land route to Yemen, could not be left uncontrolled. The forces of Redif Pasha, setting out on the Yemen campaign, entered Abha and attached the region to direct Ottoman administration, and the sancak of Asir was founded. Because of the mountainous geography and Idrisid influence, this domination never became fully settled.
