Timur's 'Three-Year Campaign': conquest of Azerbaijan and northern Iran

1386

Timur, who had seen at close quarters the fragmented state of Iran during the Khorasan campaigns, marched west in 788 (1386). In the operations of 1386-1388, known as the 'Three-Year Campaign', he passed through Mazandaran, Luristan and Georgia into Azerbaijan and reached Karabakh; Tabriz, Ardabil, Urmia and Nakhchivan came under Timurid rule. His seizure of northern Iran and Azerbaijan was also the beginning of a long contest with the Golden Horde, as had once been the case between the ulus of Jochi and the Ilkhanids.
