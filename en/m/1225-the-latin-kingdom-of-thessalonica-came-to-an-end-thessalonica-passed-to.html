The Latin Kingdom of Thessalonica came to an end: Thessalonica passed to Epirus

1225

The Latin Kingdom of Thessalonica (1205-1225), founded by Boniface, was destroyed when Theodore Angelos, Despot of Epirus, took Thessalonica and assumed the title 'King and Emperor of the Romans'. Theodore founded the Empire of Thessalonica and entered into rivalry with Nicaea for Constantinople.
