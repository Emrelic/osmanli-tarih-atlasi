Nicaean troops entered Constantinople — the Byzantine Empire was re-established

25 July 1261

Nicaean troops entered Constantinople on 25 July 1261, ending Latin rule; the lands of the Laskaris (Nicaea) state — Bursa, İzmir, Çimpe, Adrianople, Thessalonica — became the Byzantine Empire again. At these points the atlas shows the period of the Empire of Nicaea before 1261 only for those that are named in the source.
