Aetolia made peace with Rome: she lost Ambracia and Oeniadae and recognized Roman sovereignty

188 BC

Aetolia, which had sided with Antiochus in the Antiochus War, made peace with Rome after Fulvius Nobilior's siege of Ambracia; she recognized the "majesty" of Rome, paid an indemnity and gave up cities such as Ambracia and Oeniadae.
