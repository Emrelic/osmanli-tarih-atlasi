The Ashgabat Committee (Transcaspian government) was formed in Ashgabat under Funtikov; Ashgabat slipped out of the hands of the Tashkent Soviet

14 July 1918

On 12 July 1918 at Kizil Arvat railwaymen killed Frolov, chairman of the Tashkent Cheka, and his guards; two days later a government was formed in Ashgabat under the leadership of the Socialist Revolutionary worker Funtikov, and uprisings followed in Krasnovodsk and Merv. The government called itself the Ashgabat Committee.
