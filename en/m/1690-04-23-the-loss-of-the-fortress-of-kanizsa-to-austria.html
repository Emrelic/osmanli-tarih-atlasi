The Loss of the Fortress of Kanizsa to Austria

23 April 1690

Kanizsa, placed under the tight blockade of a Habsburg army of 60,000 men from January 1690, held out for months without receiving help and then was lost through a surrender agreement signed by its last commander, Mustafa Pasha. Thus ninety years of Ottoman dominion ended in the fortress remembered for the epic defence of Tiryaki Hasan Pasha. The loss of Kanizsa was the collapse of the Ottomans' last great stronghold in western Hungary.
