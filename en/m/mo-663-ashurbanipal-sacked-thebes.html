Ashurbanipal sacked Thebes

663 BC

After the death of Esarhaddon, when the Kushite revolt flared up again in Egypt under Tanutamun, Ashurbanipal entered Upper Egypt and sacked Thebes. Assyria found it difficult to hold Egypt in practice and withdrew shortly afterwards.
