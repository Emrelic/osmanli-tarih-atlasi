Founding of the fortress of Mazagan: the Portuguese position that would last longest in Morocco

1514

In the year after the taking of Azemmour, in the summer of 1514, the stronghold of Mazagan was built on the coast. After the general collapse of 1541, when Portugal abandoned all its positions in Morocco, it did not abandon Mazagan; on the contrary, it had the fortress rebuilt from the ground up with Italian-style bastions, and the city was held for two hundred and fifty-five years. The great siege of 1562 was also repelled. Mazagan would be evacuated in 1769 as Portugal's last position in Morocco.
