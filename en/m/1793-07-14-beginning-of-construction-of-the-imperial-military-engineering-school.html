Beginning of construction of the Imperial Military Engineering School (Mühendishane-i Berri-i Hümayun)

14 July 1793

Construction of the Mühendishane-i Berri-i Hümayun, one of the most important institutions of Selim III's Nizam-i Cedid reforms, began on 14 July 1793. The building was completed in September 1795 and opened for instruction; the institution was the Ottomans' second military engineering school, modeled on the military academies of Europe.
