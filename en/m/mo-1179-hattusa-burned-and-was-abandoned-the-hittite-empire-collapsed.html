Hattusa burned and was abandoned; the Hittite Empire collapsed

1179 BC

The city of Hattusa was burned and abandoned in the time of the last Hittite king, Suppiluliuma II; it is part of the Late Bronze Age collapse, experienced in the context of famine, internal conflict and the Sea Peoples. The Hittite state gave way to the Neo-Hittite city-states such as Carchemish and Malatya.
