Emir Ghazi dies; his eldest son Malik Muhammad is proclaimed ruler at Malatya

1134

Emir Ghazi (Malik Ghazi), the most influential ruler in Anatolia, died in 528/1134. The diploma of investiture (manshur), golden staff and gifts sent by the Abbasid caliph al-Mustarshid-Billah and Sultan Sanjar were given to Malik Muhammad, and he was proclaimed ruler at Malatya. His brothers Ayn al-Dawla and Yaghan rebelled.
