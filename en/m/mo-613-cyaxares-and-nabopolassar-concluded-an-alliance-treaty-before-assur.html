Cyaxares and Nabopolassar concluded an alliance treaty before Aššur

613 BC

When the Medes took Aššur, the Babylonian king Nabopolassar, who arrived late, met the Median king near the city and formed an alliance. According to Berossus, the bond was strengthened by Nebuchadnezzar's marriage to Amytis, the daughter of Cyaxares.
