Hammurabi took Larsa and defeated Rim-Sin I; southern Mesopotamia was bound to Babylon

1762 BC

In the same year Hammurabi fought Elam and Eshnunna, turned on Larsa and defeated Rim-Sin I. Southern Mesopotamia (Sumer) was added to the kingdom of Babylon; from that day this region is called the "land of Akkad" (Babylonia).
