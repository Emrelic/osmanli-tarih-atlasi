The Cretan Campaign was decided in the divan and the fleet set out

1 April 1645

The opening of a large-scale campaign against the island of Crete, belonging to Venice, was decided in the Imperial Council, and the Ottoman fleet under the command of the kapudan pasha moved from Istanbul toward Crete. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on 'Crete', this decision formed the political beginning of a long period of siege and war lasting about twenty-five years. The opening of the campaign had led to wide debates among the statesmen as the greatest foreign-policy venture of Ibrahim's reign.
