Proclamation of the Kingdom of Serbia — the former Ottoman vassal principality became a kingdom

6 March 1882

The Principality of Serbia, which gained independence in 1878 and in the same year also took Niš from the Ottomans, was turned into a kingdom in the time of Milan Obrenović. By the first article of the law issued in Belgrade the principality was proclaimed the Kingdom of Serbia and Prince Milan became king under the name Milan I; the law was published the same day in the official gazette Srpske novine. Thus Serbia, a principality dependent on the Ottomans until 1878, took its place in the Balkans with the title of kingdom. With this proclamation the whole of the Principality of Serbia (Belgrade, Niš, Jagodina, Vranje, Leskovac, Prokuplje, Pirot and others) became the Kingdom of Serbia.
