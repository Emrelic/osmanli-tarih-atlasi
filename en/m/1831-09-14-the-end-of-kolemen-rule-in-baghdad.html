The End of Kölemen Rule in Baghdad

14 September 1831

Mahmud II, who pursued a policy of centralisation, declared the governor of Baghdad, Dâvud Pasha, who did not send the money demanded and had the official sent from the centre killed, a rebel. The force under Ali Rızâ Pasha captured the city after a siege lasting ninety days, and nearly all the kölemens were purged. Thus the semi-independent Kölemen administration that had lasted since 1704 came to an end and Iraq was again attached directly to the centre.
