Evliya made the pilgrimage in Mecca and Medina, went to Egypt

1672

He completed the pilgrimage in Mecca and Medina, then headed for Egypt, Sudan and Abyssinia. The 10th volume of the Seyahatname is devoted to Egypt-Sudan-Abyssinia, and the work was left unfinished.
