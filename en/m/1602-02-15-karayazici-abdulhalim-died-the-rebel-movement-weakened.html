Karayazıcı Abdülhalim died, the rebel movement weakened

15 February 1602

Karayazıcı Abdülhalim, who withdrew wounded after the heavy defeat he suffered on 12 August 1601 at Sepetli near Elbistan, died in the year 1010 (1602). According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, the news of his death reached Istanbul in Ramadan of the year 1010 (February-March 1602); the sources note that it is disputed whether his death resulted from the wound or was carried out by his own men.
