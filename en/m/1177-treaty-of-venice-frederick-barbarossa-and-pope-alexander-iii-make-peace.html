Treaty of Venice: Frederick Barbarossa and Pope Alexander III make peace; six-year truce with the Lombard League

1177

After Legnano, Frederick recognised Alexander III as the true pope by the Treaty of Venice of August 1177 and was released from excommunication (in July 1177 he kissed the pope's foot); a six-year truce was made with the Lombard League. The Matildine inheritance was left to arbitration.
