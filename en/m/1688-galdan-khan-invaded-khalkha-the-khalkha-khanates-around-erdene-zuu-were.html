Galdan Khan invaded Khalkha; the Khalkha khanates around Erdene Zuu were dispersed, the Khalkha lords withdrew to the Qing frontier

1688

The Dzungar ruler Galdan attacked Khalkha; the Zasagt Khan's country collapsed in Khangai, the Tüsheet Khan and the Jebtsundamba fled from Erdene Zuu, and the Khalkha Mongols migrated inside the Qing frontier.
