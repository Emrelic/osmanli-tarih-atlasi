Marco Sanudo founded the Duchy of Naxos (Duchy of the Archipelago)

1207

Marco Sanudo, nephew of the Venetian Doge Dandolo, landed in the Aegean islands with an adventurous band; seventeen islands surrendered easily, only Naxos resisted, and the duchy founded there in 1207 lasted more than three centuries. Sanudo himself offered his allegiance not to Venice but to the Latin Emperor Henry.
