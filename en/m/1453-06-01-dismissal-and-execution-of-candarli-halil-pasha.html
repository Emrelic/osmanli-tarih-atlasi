Dismissal and execution of Çandarlı Halil Pasha

1 June 1453

After the conquest, the grand vizier from the long-established Çandarlı family, who had kept his distance from the siege, was executed; for the first time a grand vizier was being put to death by the sultan's order, and the age of viziers of devşirme origin was opening.
