The Normans choose Melfi as their capital; William Iron Arm is elected count of Apulia

1042

Norman mercenaries fighting against Byzantium in southern Italy chose Melfi as their capital in 1042; in September of the same year William (Iron Arm), son of Tancred de Hauteville, was elected count. This is the de facto birth of the Norman County of Apulia.
