Conquest of Prizren — Ottoman sancak seat in Kosovo

20 June 1455

On 4 Rajab 859 (20 June 1455) the Ottoman army under Mehmed the Conqueror took Prizren after the war with the Serbian despot Đurađ Branković — Vılkoğlu in Ottoman sources. The city had passed from Byzantium to the Serbian Kingdom in the middle of the thirteenth century and had been a royal residence in the times of King Milutin, Tsar Dušan and King Uroš; its importance had declined after Dušan's death. After the conquest it was made the seat of a sancak and kept this status for four and a half centuries, until 1912. Before the breakup of Yugoslavia, Prizren was the only city in the Balkans where three languages — Albanian, Turkish and Serbian — were spoken together.
