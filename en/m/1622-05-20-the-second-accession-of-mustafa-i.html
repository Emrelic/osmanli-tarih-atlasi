The second accession of Mustafa I

20 May 1622

After the killing of Osman the Young by the Janissaries, since there was no other adult male member in the dynasty, Mustafa I was placed on the throne for the second time. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on 'Mustafa I', this second reign, no differently from the first, passed without his being able actually to conduct the affairs of state, and the administration of the state was left to the viziers and the valide sultan. This period, lasting fourteen months, ended in 1623 with the accession of Murad IV.
