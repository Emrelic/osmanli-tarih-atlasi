Tilsit — Russian rights over the Republic of the Seven Islands transferred to France

7 July 1807

By the agreement reached at Tilsit between Russia and France, Russia ceded its rights over the Seven Islands to France; French troops settled on the islands in the summer of 1807 and the Republic of the Seven Islands ceased to exist.
