Ottoman-Two Sicilies (Naples) Defensive Alliance — common cause against France

21 January 1799

In the face of the French invasion of Italy, Malta and Egypt, the Ottoman State and the Kingdom of the Two Sicilies concluded a defensive alliance of nine articles. The parties undertook to wage war jointly, not to make a separate peace and to give each other land and naval aid.
