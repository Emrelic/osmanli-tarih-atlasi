Rim-Sin I, king of Larsa, conquered Isin; the Dynasty of Isin came to an end

1793 BC

Rim-Sin I, king of Larsa, conquered and took Isin, his long-standing rival, and made Larsa the sole great power of southern Mesopotamia. Thus the Dynasty of Isin ended after 223 years of rule.
