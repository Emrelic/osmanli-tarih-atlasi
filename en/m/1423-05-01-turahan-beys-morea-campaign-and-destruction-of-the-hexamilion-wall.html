Turahan Bey's Morea campaign and destruction of the Hexamilion Wall

1 May 1423

When Byzantium supported the rival claimant to the throne Mustafa Çelebi, Murad II sent Turahan Bey against the Morea. Turahan Bey destroyed the Hexamilion wall that closed the Isthmus of Corinth from end to end, entered the peninsula and caused great devastation. Peace was secured on the conditions that the wall would not be rebuilt and that an annual tax would be paid; the Despotate of the Morea was thus left militarily defenceless, and the ground for the final conquest of 1460 was prepared thirty-seven years in advance.
