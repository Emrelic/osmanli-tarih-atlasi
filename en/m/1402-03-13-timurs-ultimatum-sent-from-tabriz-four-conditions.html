Timur's ultimatum sent from Tabriz — four conditions

13 March 1402

Timur communicated four conditions through an envoy sent from Tabriz: the return of Kemah to Mutahharten, the restoration to their owners of the lands taken from the Anatolian beys, the sending of an Ottoman prince as hostage and the acceptance of the cap (külah) and belt as marks of vassalage, and the surrender of Qara Yusuf. Bayezid accepted none of these; despite the insistence of statesmen and the mediation of Mutahharten, the negotiations collapsed. Timur was determined to close the Anatolian question definitively before setting out on the China campaign.
