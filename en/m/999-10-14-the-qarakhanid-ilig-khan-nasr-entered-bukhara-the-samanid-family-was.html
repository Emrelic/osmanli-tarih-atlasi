The Qarakhanid Ilig Khan Nasr entered Bukhara; the Samanid family was exiled to Uzgend, and the state effectively ended

14 October 999

In Dhu al-Qa'da 389 (October 999) the Qarakhanids occupied Bukhara; Abd al-Malik was taken captive, and the Samanid family was taken to Uzgend and imprisoned. Ilig Khan Nasr appointed his own governors to the region.
