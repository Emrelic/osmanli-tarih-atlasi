Maccabean Revolt: uprising in Judaea against the religious prohibitions of Antiochus IV

166 BC

When Antiochus IV Epiphanes dedicated the Jerusalem Temple to Zeus Olympios and banned Jewish religious practices, Mattathias and his sons rose in revolt at Modein. Judas Maccabeus recovered and purified the Temple in 164 (Hanukkah); the Hasmonean state was born.
