The Holding of the Southern Banat Palankas by Forces Sent from Temesvár — Versec, Haram, Keve

27 July 1552

After Temesvár's surrender to the Ottoman army, units detached from the besieging army turned to the small palankas of the Banat; according to Bánlaky's account, the palankas of Versec, Facset, Mehádia, Csákóvár, Haramvár and Keve were held by these forces. Thus Haram on the Danube bank also fell into Ottoman hands.
