The Russian capture of Baku

6 August 1723

In the second year of Peter I's Caspian campaign, General Matyushkin's force came by sea and captured Baku. The city remained under a Russian garrison until it was returned to Iran in 1735 by the Treaty of Ganja. The TDV entry on Baku places the return in 1734; Russian and Iranian sources give the Treaty of Ganja of 1735.
