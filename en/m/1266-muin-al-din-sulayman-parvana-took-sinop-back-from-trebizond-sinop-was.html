Mu'in al-Din Sulayman Parvana took Sinop back from Trebizond; Sinop was given by Kilij Arslan IV to Parvana as a property

1266

Mu'in al-Din Sulayman Parvana, who obtained permission from Abaqa Khan to seize the lands that had been lost, took Sinop back (664/1266); Kilij Arslan granted it to him as property by a diploma, and the foundation of the Parvanid principality was laid. In the same year Parvana had Kilij Arslan killed by the Mongols and put the child Kaykhusraw III in his place.
