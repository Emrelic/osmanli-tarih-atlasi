Naram-Sin suppressed the "Great Revolt": the coalition of city-states led by Kish and Uruk was defeated

2249 BC

At the beginning of Naram-Sin's reign the cities of the south rose against Akkad under the leadership of Iphur-Kish, king of Kish. Naram-Sin defeated the rebels in nine battles and re-established Akkadian sovereignty; he recounted his victory in an inscription dedicated to the temples of the cities.
