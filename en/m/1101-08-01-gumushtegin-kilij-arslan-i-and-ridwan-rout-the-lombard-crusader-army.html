Gümüshtegin, Kilij Arslan I and Ridwan rout the Lombard crusader army near Merzifon

1 August 1101

The Lombard Crusaders, who had taken Ankara to free Bohemond from captivity and advanced towards Çankırı, were routed in August 1101 near Merzifon by the joint Turkish army of 20,000 men of Gümüshtegin, Kilij Arslan I, Ridwan, ruler of Aleppo, and Qaraja, emir of Harran.
