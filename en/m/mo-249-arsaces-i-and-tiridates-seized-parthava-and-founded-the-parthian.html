Arsaces I and Tiridates seized Parthava and founded the Parthian (Arsacid) state

249 BC

Arsaces, leader of the nomadic Parni tribe, overthrew the Seleucid satrap Andragoras, seized Parthava and became the first king of the Parthian kingdom; his capital was Hecatompylos. The Parthian state would dominate Mesopotamia and Iran for the following three centuries.
