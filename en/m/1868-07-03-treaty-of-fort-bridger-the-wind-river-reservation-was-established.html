Treaty of Fort Bridger — the Wind River Reservation was established

3 July 1868

The treaty that the Eastern Shoshone and Bannock signed with the United States granted the Shoshone under Washakie a reservation in the Wind River basin. The Shoshone, unlike the other peoples who fought the United States on the plains, had chosen the path of alliance and had in this way kept part of their homeland. The treaty determined the legal status of the Shoshone lands in the northern part of the Great Basin.
