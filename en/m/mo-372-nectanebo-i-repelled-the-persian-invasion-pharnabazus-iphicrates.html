Nectanebo I repelled the Persian invasion (Pharnabazus-Iphicrates)

372 BC

Nectanebo I, the founder of the Thirtieth Dynasty, turned back the Persian army under Pharnabazus and the landing of the Athenian Iphicrates in the Delta thanks to the Nile flood and disagreement between the commanders. This success prolonged Egypt's independence by another 30 years.
