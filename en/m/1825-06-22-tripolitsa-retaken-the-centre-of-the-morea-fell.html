Tripolitsa retaken — the centre of the Morea fell

22 June 1825

With the fall of Tripolitsa, the administrative centre of the revolt, the greater part of the Morea came again under control. On the map the peninsula appears in the tributary tone because it is in the hands of the Egyptian forces.
