French troops entered Mulhouse: the return of Alsace began

17 November 1918

Six days after the Armistice (11 November 1918), French troops entered Mulhouse. Article 51 of Versailles treats the return of Alsace-Lorraine to French sovereignty as retroactive to 11 November 1918; signature 28 June 1919, entry into force 10 January 1920.
