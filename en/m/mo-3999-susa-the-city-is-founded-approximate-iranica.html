Susa: the city is founded (approximate, Iranica)

3999 BC

The center of Susiana; its foundation is given in Iranica as ca. 4000 BCE. Late Susa II (c. 3500-3100 BCE) shares the same material culture as Uruk; it is followed by the Proto-Elamite period, the Akkadian conquest (c. 2300 BCE), the incorporation into Ur III (Susa VB) and the Elamite kingdoms.
