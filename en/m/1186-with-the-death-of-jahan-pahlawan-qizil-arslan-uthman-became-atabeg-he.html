With the death of Jahan Pahlawan, Qizil Arslan Uthman became atabeg; he imprisoned Tughrul II

1186

Upon the death of the Atabeg Jahan Pahlawan, his brother Qizil Arslan Uthman was installed in the office of atabeg (581/1186); Tughrul II, who had grown weary of the domination of the atabegs, entered the struggle but could not succeed and was imprisoned in a fortress.
