Ala al-Din Kayqubad I died; the age of ascent came to an end and his son Kaykhusraw II ascended the throne

1237

In the time of Kayqubad I the borders of the state had reached beyond Erzurum in the east and the basin of Lake Van; with his death (634/1237) the age of ascent came to an end, and the age of decline began with his son Kaykhusraw II, who succeeded him.
