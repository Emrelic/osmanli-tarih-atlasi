The Ganzhou Uyghur army captured Dunhuang

911

Dunhuang, the center of the autonomous kingdom founded by a Chinese commander in 905, was taken in 911 by the Uyghur army under Tegin; the victory increased the prestige of the Ganzhou Uyghurs in the eyes of the Chinese.
