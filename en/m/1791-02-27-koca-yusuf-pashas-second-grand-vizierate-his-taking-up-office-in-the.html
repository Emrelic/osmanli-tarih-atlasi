Koca Yûsuf Pasha's second grand vizierate — his taking up office in the camp at Shumen

27 February 1791

After the short grand vizierate of Şerif Hasan Pasha, which ended in execution, the seal was given again to Koca Yûsuf Pasha; the pasha went to the camp at Shumen on 27 February 1791 and took up his duty. The armistice and peace negotiations that would end the war would be conducted during his second grand vizierate.
