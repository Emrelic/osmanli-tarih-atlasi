Burebista fell victim to assassination: the Dacian kingdom was fragmented

43 BC

Strabo writes that Burebista was killed in an internal coup before Caesar's death, and that the kingdom was divided into five, then four, parts. The Dacian union could not be re-established until Decebalus (AD 87).
