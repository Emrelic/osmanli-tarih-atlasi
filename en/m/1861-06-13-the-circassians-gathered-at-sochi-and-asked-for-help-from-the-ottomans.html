The Circassians gathered at Sochi and asked for help from the Ottomans, Britain and France

13 June 1861

The Circassians, who continued to fight the Russians even after the surrender of Sheikh Shamil (1859), gathered at Sochi on 13 June 1861 and asked for help from the Ottoman State and from Britain and France. No help came; in the same year, with the tsar's approval, the deportation of the Circassians was formally decided, and in 1861-1864 the lands of the Abzakh, Shapsug and Ubykh were occupied. The people were told that within a month they must settle in the places that would be shown to them along the Kuban or emigrate to Ottoman lands, otherwise they would be deported to the interior of Russia as prisoners of war.
