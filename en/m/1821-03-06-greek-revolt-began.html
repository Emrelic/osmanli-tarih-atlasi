Greek Revolt began

6 March 1821

The uprising prepared by the Filiki Eteria organization broke out first in Wallachia and then, with its main force, in the Morea; the state, busy with the Ali Pasha of Tepelena affair, was late in its first response. Thanks to the intense Greek sympathy (philhellenism) in European public opinion, the revolt quickly turned into an international cause; thus was lit the fuse of the first successful independence movement of the age of nationalism on Ottoman land. Other settlements lost on the same date: Athens, Patras (Balyabadra), Thebes, Livadeia, Eleusis (Attica), Missolonghi.
