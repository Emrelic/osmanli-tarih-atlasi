Zadar came under Venetian rule — Santa Intrada

31 July 1409

After King Ladislas of Naples sold Dalmatia to Venice, the people of Zadar drove the Neapolitan soldiers out of the city; on 31 July 1409 the Venetian provveditori entered the city and established the Venetian rule that would last until 1797.
