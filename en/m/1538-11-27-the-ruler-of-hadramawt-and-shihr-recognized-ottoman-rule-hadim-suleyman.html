The ruler of Hadramawt and Shihr recognized Ottoman rule — Hadım Süleyman Pasha's return from the India campaign

27 November 1538

Hadım Süleyman Pasha, returning from the siege of Diu, called at the port of Shihr on 27 November 1538; when the ruler of Hadramawt and Shihr accepted Ottoman rule, he moved on to Aden. Hadramawt was organized as a sancak attached to Yemen, but the chiefs of the Kathiri tribe continued to hold the administration in the region. This subordination lasted until the Ottoman withdrawal from Yemen in 1635.
