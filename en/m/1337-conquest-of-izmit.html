Conquest of Izmit

1337

With the fall of Izmit (Nicomedia), the last important Byzantine fortress on the Anatolian side also came under Ottoman rule; the whole Kocaeli peninsula, as far as the opposite shore of Constantinople, became Ottoman territory. The principality thus came to dominate the entire southern and eastern shores of the Marmara and prepared the groundwork for the crossing into Rumelia.
