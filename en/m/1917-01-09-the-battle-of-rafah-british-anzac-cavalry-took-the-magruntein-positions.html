The Battle of Rafah — British-ANZAC cavalry took the Magruntein positions and Rafah

9 January 1917

The Anzac Mounted Division, the Imperial Camel Corps Brigade and a British yeomanry brigade of the Egyptian Expeditionary Force, having crossed the Sinai, attacked the Ottoman position held with redoubts at Magruntein, about one kilometre south of Rafah. After hard fighting, the breakthrough by the New Zealanders and the camel units collapsed the Ottoman defence; the position and nearby Rafah were captured, and the British forces reached the Palestinian frontier.
