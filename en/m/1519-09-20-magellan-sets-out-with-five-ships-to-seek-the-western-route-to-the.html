Magellan sets out with five ships to seek the western route to the Moluccas

20 September 1519

The Portuguese Magellan, in Castilian service, set out westward from Sanlúcar with the Trinidad, San Antonio, Concepción, Victoria and Santiago; the goal was to reach the Spice Islands on the Spanish side of the Tordesillas line.
