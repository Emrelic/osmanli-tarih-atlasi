The liberation of Antep — the French evacuation of the city

25 December 1921

After the Treaty of Ankara, French forces evacuated Antep. The city had been occupied by the British on 17 December 1918 and left to the French on 5 November 1919; the people had put up a long defense against French forces between 1 April 1920 and 7 February 1921.
