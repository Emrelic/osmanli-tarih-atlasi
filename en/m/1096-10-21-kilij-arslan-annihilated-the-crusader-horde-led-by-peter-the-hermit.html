Kilij Arslan annihilated the Crusader horde led by Peter the Hermit

21 October 1096

Kilij Arslan annihilated almost the entire body of Crusaders, composed of peasants, adventurers and brigands led by the monk Peter the Hermit (1 Dhu'l-Qa'da 489 / 21 October 1096), and, encouraged by this success, set out on a campaign against Malatya.
