The conquest of Taiz

1 February 1547

In Yemen, where nine years earlier the Ottomans had contented themselves with the coastal strip, the Zaydi imams were growing steadily stronger in the mountainous interior; in response, the forces of Özdemir Pasha climbed onto the plateau and seized Taiz.
