Nanzhao moved its capital to the city of Taihe (739)

739

Piluoge (Guiyi), to whom the Tang had given the title Prince of Yunnan, moved his residence to the city of Taihe (Dahe) in the 27th year of Kaiyuan; Taihe lies on the shore of Erhai, near Dali.
