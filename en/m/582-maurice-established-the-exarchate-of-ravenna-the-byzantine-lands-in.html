Maurice established the Exarchate of Ravenna: the Byzantine lands in Italy were placed under a military governor

582

Emperor Maurice gave the Byzantine administration in Italy to an 'exarchos' regarded as the emperor's deputy against the Lombards; the centre was Ravenna. This arrangement is considered the first step of the later 'theme' system.
