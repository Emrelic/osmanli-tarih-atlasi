Al-Muwaffaq granted Kirman, Fars and Khurasan to Amr ibn Layth as an iqta'

21 June 888

Al-Muwaffaq, who made peace with Amr on 7 Safar 275 (21 June 888) because of Khumarawayh's revolt, granted Kirman, Fars and Khurasan to Amr as an iqta' in return for 10 million dirhams.
