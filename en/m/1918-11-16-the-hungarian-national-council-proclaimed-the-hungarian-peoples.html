The Hungarian National Council proclaimed the Hungarian People's Republic — the monarchy officially abolished under Károlyi

16 November 1918

Károlyi had been appointed prime minister by Charles on 31 October 1918 (Aster Revolution). On 16 November 1918 the National Council and government proclaimed the People's Republic; on 11 January 1919 Károlyi became provisional president. The atlas's state entries for the Hungarian Regency and the Hungarian People's Republic begin from this day. With this event the cities of the Kingdom of Hungary outside Croatia, including Aquincum-Óbuda-Pest, Esztergom, Eger, Szeged, Debrecen, Oradea and Upper Hungary, passed to the Hungarian People's Republic.
