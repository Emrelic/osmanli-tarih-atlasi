Muhammad Bakhtiyar Khalji took the Sena capital and laid the foundation of the Khalji dynasty, which also included Nadia

1202

Muhammad Bakhtiyar Khalji, under the command of the Ghurid commander Aybak, captured Lakhnawti, the capital of the Hindu Sena dynasty; making it his center, he laid the foundations of the Khalji dynasty, which included Devkot, Nadia and Bengal (1202). Bihar had earlier fallen into the hands of the Khaljis in 1193.
