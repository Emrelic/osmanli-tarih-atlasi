The occupation of Bijâya (Bougie)

29 September 1833

The port of Bijâya, where the Kabylia mountains open to the sea, was the last independent link of the coastal line between Algiers and Annaba. It was taken with the support of naval artillery; however, the mountainous interior behind it would not come under French control for thirty more years. These coastal occupations make up the year-by-year melting away of Ottoman Algiers on the map.
