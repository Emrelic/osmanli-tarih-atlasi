By an agreement made through Sebuk Tegin, the Syr Darya region as far as Qatwan remained with the Karakhanids; Fa'iq became governor of Samarkand

25 January 996

Ilig Khan Nasr marched into Samanid territory in 386 (996) at Fa'iq's urging; by a treaty made through the mediation of Sebuk Tegin, the governor of Ghazna, the Syr Darya region as far as Qatwan was left to the Karakhanids, and Fa'iq became governor of Samarkand.
