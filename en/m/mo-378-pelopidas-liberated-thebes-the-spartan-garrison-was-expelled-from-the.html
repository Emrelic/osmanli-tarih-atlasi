Pelopidas liberated Thebes; the Spartan garrison was expelled from the Cadmea

378 BC

The Theban Cadmea, which Sparta had seized and garrisoned in 382 BC, was recovered by a night raid of the exiles; Thebes again became the centre of the Boeotian League. The event is the beginning of the dissolution of Spartan hegemony.
