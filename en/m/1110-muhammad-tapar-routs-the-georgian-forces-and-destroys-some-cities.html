Muhammad Tapar routs the Georgian forces and destroys some cities

1110

When King David launched a new invasion with a great army obtained from the Kipchaks and came as far as Ganja, the Seljuk Sultan Muhammad Tapar marched on Georgia, routed the Georgian forces and destroyed some cities (503/1110).
