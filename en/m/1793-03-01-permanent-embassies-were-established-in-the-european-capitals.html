Permanent embassies were established in the European capitals

1 March 1793

Selim III, for the first time in Ottoman history, sent permanent ambassadors to serve continuously in the major European centres. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Elçi, this step gave a lasting organisation to Ottoman diplomacy, which until then had been conducted only with temporary, one-off missions. The main duty of the new embassies was to report regularly to Istanbul the political and military developments in Europe.
