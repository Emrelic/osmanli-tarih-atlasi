Ibn Jubayr returned to al-Andalus by way of Acre-Sicily

25 April 1185

He left Acre on 17 October 1184 on a Genoese ship, which sank off Messina; setting out from Trapani on 26 March 1185, he landed at Cartagena and reached home on 25 April 1185.
