The withdrawal of the Knights: the handing over of Bodrum, Kos and the Dodecanese

5 January 1523

Under the terms of the surrender of Rhodes, the Knights of Saint John evacuated not only the island's centre but all the positions they had held for two hundred years. The fortress of Bodrum (Saint Peter) on the Anatolian coast, together with the islands of Kos, Kalymnos and Leros, thus passed to Ottoman administration without fighting. With this transfer, completed two weeks after the fall of Rhodes, the Latin presence in the southeastern corner of the Aegean came to a complete end.
