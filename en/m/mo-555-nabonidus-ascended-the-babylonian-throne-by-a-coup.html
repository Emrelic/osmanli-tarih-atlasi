Nabonidus ascended the Babylonian throne by a coup

555 BC

After the death of Nebuchadnezzar (562) there had come Amel-Marduk, Neriglissar (559-556) and the short-lived Labaši-Marduk; Nabonidus, although not of royal descent, carried out a coup in 556. His special devotion to the moon god Sin offended the priests of Marduk.
