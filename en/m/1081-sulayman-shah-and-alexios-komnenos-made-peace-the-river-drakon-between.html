Sulayman Shah and Alexios Komnenos made peace: the river Drakon between Izmit and Constantinople became the frontier

1081

According to the peace signed in 1081 between Sulayman Shah and the Byzantine Emperor Alexios Komnenos, the river Drakon between Izmit and Constantinople was to be accepted as the frontier between the two states.
