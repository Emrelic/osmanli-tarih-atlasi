Qutb al-Din Aybak defeated Yildiz at Lahore and entered Ghazna; Yildiz recaptured Ghazna

16 July 1208

Aybak's most dangerous rivals were the Ghurid emirs Taj al-Din Yildiz and Nasir al-Din Qabacha. When Yildiz broke the agreement, Aybak marched on Lahore, defeated him and entered Ghazna; however, Yildiz later retook Ghazna (1208-1209).
