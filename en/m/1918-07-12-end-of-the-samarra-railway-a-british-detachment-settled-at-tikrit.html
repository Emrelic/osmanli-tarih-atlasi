End of the Samarra railway: a British detachment settled at Tikrit

12 July 1918

After the battle of Tikrit on 5 November 1917, the British forces had withdrawn to Samarra on 10 November. On 12 July 1918 a detachment from I Corps occupied Tikrit to protect the extension of the Samarra railway.
