Untash-Napirisha founded the city of Dur-Untash (Chogha Zanbil) and its ziggurat

1274 BC

Untash-Napirisha, king of Middle Elam, founded a ziggurat-bearing sacred city near Susa for the god Inshushinak; although the city was left unfinished, it was used for more than two centuries. Chogha Zanbil is the best-preserved ziggurat in Iran.
