Russia marched on Azov and the Crimea — the Ottoman-Russian War in effect began

24 March 1736

Russia, which regarded the Crimean forces' passage through Kabarda on the way to the Iranian front as a violation of its territory, used this as a pretext and, without declaring war, marched on Azov and the Crimea in two columns. Münnich crossed the Don and took the fortresses before Azov. The Ottoman divan took the decision for war only on 2 May 1736.
