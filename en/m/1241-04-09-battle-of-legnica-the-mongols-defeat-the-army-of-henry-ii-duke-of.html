Battle of Legnica: the Mongols defeat the army of Henry II, duke of Silesia

9 April 1241

On 9 April 1241 at Legnica (Liegnitz) the Mongols defeated the army of the united Silesian princes, burned the Silesian cities and turned towards Hungary. The Mongol raid coming from the east of the country brought the state in Poland to the point of dissolution.
