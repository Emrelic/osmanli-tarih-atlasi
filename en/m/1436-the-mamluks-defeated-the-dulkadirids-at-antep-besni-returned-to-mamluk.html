The Mamluks defeated the Dulkadirids at Antep; Besni returned to Mamluk administration (approximate)

1436

The Mamluk army defeated the Dulkadirids at Antep in 1436, and Mehmed Bey withdrew to Akdağ. The year of Besni's return to the Mamluks is not written in the source; Besni had been joined to Dulkadir in 1418, and had returned to the Mamluks before Shahsuvar Bey took it from them in 1465. On the map, Besni returns to the Mamluks together with this defeat at the neighbouring center of Antep (approximate information).
