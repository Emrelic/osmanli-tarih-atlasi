Kuk Swamp (early agricultural field): begins

8114 BC

Plant use and some cultivation at the swamp edge in the New Guinea highlands (Phase 1: 10220-9910 cal BP = approximately 8270-7960 BCE). Independent agriculture at the latest 6950-6440 cal BP (approximately 5000-4490 BCE, Phase 2 mound), ditched agriculture 4350-3980 cal BP (approximately 2400-2030 BCE, Phase 3).
