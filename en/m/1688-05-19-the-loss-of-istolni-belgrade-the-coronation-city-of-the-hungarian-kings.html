The loss of Istolni Belgrade — the coronation city of the Hungarian kings

19 May 1688

The city in which the Hungarian kings were crowned and buried for centuries had been taken in 1543 by Süleyman the Lawgiver and had become one of the most important sancak centres of the province of Buda. About two years after the fall of Buda, the fortress, its surroundings entirely cut off, surrendered. With this loss the Ottoman presence west of the Danube was squeezed into the Srem and Osijek region.
