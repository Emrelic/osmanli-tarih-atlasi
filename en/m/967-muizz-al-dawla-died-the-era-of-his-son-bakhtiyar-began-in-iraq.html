Mu'izz al-Dawla died; the era of his son Bakhtiyar began in Iraq

967

Mu'izz al-Dawla, one of the elders of the dynasty, died in 967; his son Bakhtiyar took his place in Iraq. After the death of Rukn al-Dawla (976), Adud al-Dawla became head of the dynasty.
