Regulation of the muhtar and council of elders system by the Provincial Regulation of 1864

1 June 1864

With the Provincial Regulation of 1864, village administration was reorganized; in villages where Muslims and non-Muslims lived together, it was accepted that each community would elect its own muhtar and council of elders. The council of elders thus acquired for the first time an official place in village/neighborhood administration.
