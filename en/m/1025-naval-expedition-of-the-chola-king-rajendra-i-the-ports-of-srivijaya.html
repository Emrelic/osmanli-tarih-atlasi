Naval expedition of the Chola king Rajendra I: the ports of Srivijaya (Palembang), Pannai and Kadaram (Kedah) were plundered, and the king Sangramavijayottungavarman was taken prisoner

1025

According to the Rajendra inscription at Tanjore (1030-31), the Chola fleet captured the king of Kadaram and conquered/plundered Srivijaya (Palembang), Pannai and other centers. According to Coedès, the raid temporarily weakened the Sumatran kingdom; this weakening also affected events in East Java.
