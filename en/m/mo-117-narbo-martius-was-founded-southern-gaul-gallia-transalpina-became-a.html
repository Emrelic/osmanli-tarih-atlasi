Narbo Martius was founded: Southern Gaul (Gallia Transalpina) became a Roman province

117 BC

Rome, which had entered Gaul at the call of Massalia (125), took the Rhône valley with the victories of Aquae Sextiae (122) and over the Allobroges; in 118 BC Narbo Martius was founded in Gaul as the first Roman colony. The region became the province of Gallia Transalpina.
