Hulagu's vanguard units moved against the Isma'ilis and received the surrender of many fortresses

1 May 1253

By the quriltai decision of the Great Khan Mongke, Hulagu had been assigned the tasks of eliminating the Isma'ilis, subjugating the Abbasid caliphate, and seizing Egypt and Syria. The vanguard units set out in May 1253 and received the surrender of many Isma'ili fortresses before Hulagu arrived; Alamut fell on 19 November 1256.
