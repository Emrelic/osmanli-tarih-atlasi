Rome: Roman Empire (Principate-Dominate, combined) → ownerless interval

395

At Rome, ownership passes from the Roman Empire (Principate-Dominate, combined) to the ownerless interval. Transition source information: Marcellinus Comes, Chronicon s.a. 476 (consulship of Basiliscus and Armatus; Mommsen CM II, Latin Library marcellinus1.html): Odoacer took Rome, killed Orestes, exiled Augustulus to Lucullanum, and the Western empire perished with Augustulus.
