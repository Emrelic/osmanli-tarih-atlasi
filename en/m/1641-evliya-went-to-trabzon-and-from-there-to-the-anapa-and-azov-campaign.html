Evliya went to Trabzon, and from there to the Anapa and Azov campaign

1641

With Ketenci Ömer Pasha he went by sea to Trabzon, and from there to Anapa. He joined the campaign to retake Azov under the command of the serdar Hüseyin Pasha; the first attempt failed.
