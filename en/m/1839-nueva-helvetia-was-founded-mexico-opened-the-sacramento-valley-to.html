Nueva Helvetia was founded — Mexico opened the Sacramento valley to settlement

1839

The Swiss immigrant John Sutter founded Nueva Helvetia at the confluence of the Sacramento and American rivers, with permission and a land grant obtained from the Mexican authorities. The fortified ranch became Mexico's most advanced outpost in the interior of Alta California; with the discovery of gold in 1848 it would turn into the centre of the California migration.
