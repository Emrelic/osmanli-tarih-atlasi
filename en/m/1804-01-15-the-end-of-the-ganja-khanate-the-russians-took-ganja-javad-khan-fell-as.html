The end of the Ganja Khanate: the Russians took Ganja, Javad Khan fell as a martyr

15 January 1804

After General Sisianov's siege, which began in November 1803, the Khan of Ganja, Javad Khan, rejected the offer of surrender and fell as a martyr; Ganja passed into Russian hands.
