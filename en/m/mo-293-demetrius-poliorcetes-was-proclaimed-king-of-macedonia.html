Demetrius Poliorcetes was proclaimed king of Macedonia

293 BC

Demetrius, who took advantage of the struggle over the throne in the dynasty of Cassander, was proclaimed king of Macedonia by the army in the autumn of 294. It was the first sovereignty of the line of Antigonus in Macedonia; it collapsed in 288 and Gonatas made it permanent in 276.
