Establishment of a British protectorate on the northern Somali coast — the beginning of Somaliland

14 July 1884

According to the TDV article on Somalia, Zeila, Berbera and Tajura were colonized by the British in 1884 under the name Somaliland; according to the standard academic source, this process began with a treaty signed at Berbera with the Habar Awal tribe on 21 Ramadan 1301 (14 July 1884), and expanded between 1884 and 1886 as similar treaties were signed with the Gadabursi, Habar Toljaala, Habar Garhajis, Issa and Warsangeli tribes; in return, Britain promised military protection. This chain of treaties laid the groundwork for the proclamation of the official British Somaliland Protectorate in 1887.
