Üsküp, the center of the vilayet of Kosovo, was left without resistance

26 October 1912

After the Kumanovo rout (23-24 October), Üsküp, the center of the vilayet of Kosovo, was left defenseless. As the populace did not want fighting around the city, the Serbian forces under the command of Prince Aleksandar, invited through the consuls, entered the city without resistance on the evening of 26 October.
