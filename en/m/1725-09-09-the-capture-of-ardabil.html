The capture of Ardabil

9 September 1725

After Tabriz, the forces of Köprülüzâde Abdullah Pasha entered Ardabil, the spiritual center of the Safavids. In 1728 the city was registered as the Ottoman liwa of Ardabil, together with the nahiyes of Serab and Miyane; the neighboring Halkhal was also entered in the register as a separate liwa. Nadir retook Ardabil in 1730.
