Balchik and the Dobruja coast came under Ottoman administration

1 July 1389

After the victory at Kosovo, Balchik on the Dobruja coast came under Ottoman administration. The Wallachian voivode Mircea would occupy the region immediately afterwards, and Ottoman rule would be reestablished in 1393.
