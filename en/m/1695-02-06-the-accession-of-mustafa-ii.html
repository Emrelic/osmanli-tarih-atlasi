The accession of Mustafa II

6 February 1695

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Mustafa II, Mustafa II, who ascended the throne at the age of thirty-one, proclaimed his sultanate without waiting for the approval of the grand vizier and the şeyhülislam; the sword-girding ceremony was held a few days later at the Eski Cami. The sultan, who came to the throne at a time when multi-front wars were continuing after the Vienna debacle, became the last Ottoman sultan to go on campaign in person.
