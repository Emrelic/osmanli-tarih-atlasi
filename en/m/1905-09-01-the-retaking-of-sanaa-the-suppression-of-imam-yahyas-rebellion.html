The Retaking of Sana'a: The Suppression of Imam Yahya's Rebellion

1 September 1905

The uprising that began in 1904 under the leadership of the Zaydi Imam Yahya ended in April 1905 with the fall of Sana'a, and hunger and epidemic broke out in the city. The reinforcement armies under Ali Rızâ Pasha and Ahmed Feyzi Pasha retook the city at the price of heavy losses, in an operation carried out between 15 July and 1 September 1905 and supported also by the forces at Taiz, and the rebellion was suppressed. The Yemen operation cost the Ottoman army tens of thousands of casualties and brought an enormous financial burden; among the officers in Rumelia, the fear of 'exile to Yemen' was foremost among the grievances feeding Unionist organisation.
