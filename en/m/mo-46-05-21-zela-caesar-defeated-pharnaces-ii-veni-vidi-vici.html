Zela: Caesar defeated Pharnaces II ("veni vidi vici")

21 May 46 BC

Pharnaces II, son of Mithridates, tried to recover Pontus by taking advantage of the civil war; Caesar defeated him at Zela in 47 and reorganized Pontus under Roman control. Pharnaces fled to the Bosporus and died there.
