Kaminaljuyu (Guatemala Valley): Providencia phase / Late Preclassic rise: Kaminaljuyú: the Providencia phase

349 BC

At Kaminaljuyu, the end of the Las Charcas phase and the beginning of the Providencia phase (~350 BC) coincide with the Middle–Late Preclassic boundary; Providencia ~350–100/75 BC, followed by a population and construction boom in the Verbena-Arenal (Terminal Preclassic) phases. The chronology of Inomata et al. 2014 and 2020 shifted the traditional dates of Shook & Popenoe de Hatch (1999) 300–400 years later.
