Aurelian evacuated Dacia: the area north of the Danube was abandoned

271

Under Gothic pressure Aurelian evacuated the province of Dacia and withdrew the legions and the civilian population to the south of the Danube (Dacia Ripensis). After 165 years Rome gave up its first major territory north of the Danube.
