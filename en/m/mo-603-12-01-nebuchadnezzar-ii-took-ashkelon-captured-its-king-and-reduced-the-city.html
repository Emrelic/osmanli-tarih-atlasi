Nebuchadnezzar II took Ashkelon, captured its king and reduced the city to ruins

1 December 603 BC

In Nebuchadnezzar's first year "all the kings of the land of Hatti" brought him tribute; only Ashkelon resisted. The city was taken in the month of Kislîmu, the king was captured and the city was turned into a ruin mound.
