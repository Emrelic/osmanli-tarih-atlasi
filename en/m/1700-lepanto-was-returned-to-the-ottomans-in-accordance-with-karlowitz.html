Lepanto was returned to the Ottomans in accordance with Karlowitz

1700

Lepanto, which, after resisting for a long time during the wars of 1683-1699, fell to Venice together with the neighbouring fortresses in July 1687, remained in Venetian hands until a year after the Treaty of Karlowitz and then returned to the Ottomans. The fortress at the mouth of the Gulf of Corinth thus came back under Ottoman administration fifteen years before the Morea.
