Acre fell in two hours

3 November 1840

Under the bombardment of the allied fleet, the fortress's magazine exploded and Acre surrendered within a few hours. The fortress that Ibrahim Pasha had been able to take in six months eight years earlier showed the superiority of naval artillery.
