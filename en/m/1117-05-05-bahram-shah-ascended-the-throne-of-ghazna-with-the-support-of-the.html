Bahram Shah ascended the throne of Ghazna with the support of the Seljuk Sanjar

5 May 1117

Bahram Shah, who confronted Arslan-Shah, the successor of Shirzad on the throne, gained the Ghaznavid throne with the help of Sanjar, malik of Khurasan (and of Muhammad Tapar) (511/1117); Arslan-Shah withdrew to India and died in 512/1118. The Ghaznavids became subject to the Seljuks.
