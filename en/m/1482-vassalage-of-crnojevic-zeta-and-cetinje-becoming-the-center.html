Vassalage of Crnojević Zeta and Cetinje becoming the center

1482

With Shkodër remaining Ottoman in 1479, the mountainous Zeta region in its backyard also fell within the empire's borders. The Crnojević family, which held the region, recognized the sovereignty of Bayezid II, who ascended the throne after the Conqueror's death, and, bound to tribute, remained free in its internal affairs; Ivan Crnojević moved his center from the plain to Cetinje at the foot of Mount Lovćen and founded the city. The monastery he had built there two years later became the religious center of the region. Thus Montenegro appears on the map not as directly Ottoman land but as a vassal region in a light tone. The TDV entry on Karadağ (Montenegro) mentions the dynasty and the bishopric of Cetinje and writes that the rule was turned into a separate sancak in 1514 by Iskender Beg (of Crnojević descent, raised at the Ottoman court); the year 1482 does not appear in the TDV, it is the date given by Montenegrin historiography. Because of the harshness of the geography, the administration here never functioned as tightly as on the plains.
