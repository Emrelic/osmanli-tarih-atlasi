The Genoese fleet loses a naval battle off the island of Giglio

1241

Genoa, allied with the pope and Venice in the time of Frederick II, lost a naval battle off the island of Giglio in 1241; however, it recovered quickly and in 1245 at the Council of Lyon (convened by the Genoese Pope Innocent IV) made its will accepted.
