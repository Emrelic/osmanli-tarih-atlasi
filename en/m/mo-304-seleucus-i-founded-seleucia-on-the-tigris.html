Seleucus I founded Seleucia on the Tigris

304 BC

Seleucus founded Seleucia on the bank of the Tigris near Babylon and began transferring the population of Babylon there. With a population of about 300,000, Seleucia became the largest Hellenistic city of the East and later the neighbour of the Parthian Empire's capital.
