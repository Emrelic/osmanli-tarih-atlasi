John of Brienne was elected Latin Emperor

1229

With Robert's death in the Morea (1228) and Baldwin II being a child, John of Brienne, the former King of Jerusalem, was elected to the empire the following year. John heroically defended Constantinople against the Bulgarian-Nicaean siege.
