Moscow's establishment of a forward garrison at Voronezh against the steppe

1585

At the end of the sixteenth century, what is today Sloboda Ukraine and the upper basin of the Don were an uninhabited wild steppe through which the Tatars passed on raids against Moscow. The Muscovite government established a series of forward garrisons in this steppe; Voronezh was founded in 1585 as one of them. In the following decades the fortress, together with Belgorod and Kursk, became one of the first links of Moscow's southern defensive line.
