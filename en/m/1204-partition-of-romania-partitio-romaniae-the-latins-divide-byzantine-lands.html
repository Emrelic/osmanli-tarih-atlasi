Partition of Romania (Partitio Romaniae): the Latins divide Byzantine lands

1204

After the Fourth Crusade took Constantinople (1204), the Venetians and the crusaders concluded the Partitio Romaniae to divide the spoils and the Byzantine lands. Treccani DBI stresses that these arrangements regulated the areas belonging not to the Commune of Venice but to the doge and his men, and that the commune was systematically excluded from the partition projects.
