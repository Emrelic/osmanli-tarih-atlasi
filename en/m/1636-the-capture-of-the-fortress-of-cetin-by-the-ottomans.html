The capture of the fortress of Cetin by the Ottomans

1636

Cetin, which had been the key fortress in the defence of the Slunj area against the Ottomans since the middle of the sixteenth century, passed to Ottoman administration in 1636 after repeated sieges. This first Ottoman period lasted two years.
