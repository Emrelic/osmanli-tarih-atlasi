Nebuchadnezzar I (Isin II) marched on Elam and recovered the statue of Marduk

1109 BC

Nebuchadnezzar I of the Second Dynasty of Isin marched on Elam and brought back to Babylon the statue of Marduk that Kutir-Nahhunte had carried off. The victory is remembered in Babylonian history as "the return of Marduk" and made the cult of Marduk central.
