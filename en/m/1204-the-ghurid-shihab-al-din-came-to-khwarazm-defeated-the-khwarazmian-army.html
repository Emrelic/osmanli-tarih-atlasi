The Ghurid Shihab al-Din came to Khwarazm, defeated the Khwarazmian army at Karasu and besieged Gurganj; he withdrew when the Qara Khitai army arrived

1204

Shihab al-Din came to Khwarazm with his great army, defeated the Khwarazmian army at Karasu and besieged the capital Gurganj; he was forced to withdraw when the Qara Khitai army, for which Ala al-Din had asked help, arrived.
