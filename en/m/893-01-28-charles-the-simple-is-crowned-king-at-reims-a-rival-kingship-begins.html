Charles the Simple is crowned king at Reims; a rival kingship begins against Odo

28 January 893

Charles III 'the Simple' had been excluded on account of his youth at the deposition of Charles the Fat in 887; during Odo's kingship he was crowned on 28 January 893 at Reims with the recognition of some of the notables, and with the emperor's alliance forced Odo to give up part of Neustria. With Odo's death in 898 he held the whole kingdom.
