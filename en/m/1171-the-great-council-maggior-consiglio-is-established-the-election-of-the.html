The Great Council (Maggior Consiglio) is established; the election of the doge passes to the aristocracy

1171

After the Byzantine disaster of 1171 a constitutional reform was carried out and the Great Council was established. From 1172, when Ziani was elected, the new doge was presented to the people only for acclamation.
