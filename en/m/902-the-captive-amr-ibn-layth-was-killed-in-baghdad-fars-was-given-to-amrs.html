The captive Amr ibn Layth was killed in Baghdad; Fars was given to Amr's grandson Tahir

902

Amr ibn Layth, who had been sent to Baghdad because his grandsons did not pay the ransom the Samanids demanded, was killed there (289/902); in the same year the caliph al-Muktafi gave the Fars region to Tahir ibn Muhammad.
