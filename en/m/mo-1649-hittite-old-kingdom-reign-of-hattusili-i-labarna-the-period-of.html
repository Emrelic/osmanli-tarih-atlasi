Hittite Old Kingdom — reign of Hattusili I (Labarna): the period of Hattusili I (hMC traditional value)

1649 BC

The traditional reign of Hattusili I is c. 1650-1620 in the hMC; the beginning is found by counting back two 25-year generations from the Babylon 1595 datum of Mursili. The Hittite kingdom unified Central Anatolia at the end of the Middle Bronze Age.
