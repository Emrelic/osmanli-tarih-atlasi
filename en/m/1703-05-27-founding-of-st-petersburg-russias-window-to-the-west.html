Founding of St. Petersburg — Russia's window to the West

27 May 1703

On Hare Island in the Neva delta, which had just been recaptured from Sweden during the Great Northern War, Peter the Great laid the foundation of the Peter and Paul Fortress (Petropavlovsk Fortress) on 16/27 May 1703; this day is regarded as the founding date of the city of St. Petersburg. Peter conceived of the marshy delta as a 'window' opening to the West, and the city became Russia's new capital in 1712 — the region would be formally attached to Russia by the 1721 Treaty of Nystad.
