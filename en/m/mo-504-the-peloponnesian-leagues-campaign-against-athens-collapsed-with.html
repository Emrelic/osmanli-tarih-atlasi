The Peloponnesian League's campaign against Athens collapsed with Corinth's withdrawal

504 BC

Sparta led the army of the Peloponnesian League into Attica against the democratic Athens of Cleisthenes; the withdrawal of the Corinthian army at Eleusis and the disagreement of the two Spartan kings brought the campaign down. The episode is regarded as the first known instance of the allies' right to vote in the League assembly coming into operation.
