Kilij Arslan II took Malatya: the Malatya branch of the Danishmendids came to an end

25 October 1178

After a siege of four months, Kilij Arslan II captured Malatya on 11 Jumada al-ula 574 (25 October 1178) and put an end to this branch of the Danishmendid dynasty.
