Epirus plundered by Rome: seventy towns destroyed

166 BC

After the Third Macedonian War the Molossian towns of Epirus that had remained close to Perseus were plundered on the order of Aemilius Paullus, and about 150,000 people were enslaved. Epirus remained poor under Roman rule in the following century.
