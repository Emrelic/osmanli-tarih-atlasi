Perdiccas's Egyptian campaign failed at the crossing of the Nile; Perdiccas was killed by his own army

320 BC

In the war of the Diadochi, Perdiccas, regent of Alexander, entered Egypt against Ptolemy, who had hijacked Alexander's funeral cortege, but could not cross the Nile, lost many men, and was killed by his officers. Ptolemy kept Egypt.
