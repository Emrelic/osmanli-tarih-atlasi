Battle of Edington (Ethandun) and the Peace of Wedmore: Alfred defeated the Danes (Guthrum)

878

After the Danes' sudden raid on Chippenham in January 878, Alfred hid with a small band of men; the armies met at Edington in Wiltshire. After the Peace of Wedmore (878) the Danes withdrew from Wessex; the northeastern half, including London, remained with the Danes, but "the tide had turned".
