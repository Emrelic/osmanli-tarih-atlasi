Battle of Otlukbeli

11 August 1473

Uzun Hasan, the Aqquyunlu ruler who had become a great power in the East and allied with Venice, suffered a heavy defeat at Otlukbeli before the Conqueror's army equipped with firearms. The victory made Ottoman supremacy in Anatolia definitive; it was also an early proof of the superiority of a central army using cannon and muskets over a classical army. According to the TDV entry on Gümüşhane, the city had been under Aqquyunlu rule since 1467; this rule ended with the defeat at Otlukbeli and Gümüşhane definitively passed to Ottoman rule.
