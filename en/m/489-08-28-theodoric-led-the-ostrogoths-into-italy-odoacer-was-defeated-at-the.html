Theodoric led the Ostrogoths into Italy: Odoacer was defeated at the Isonzo and Verona

28 August 489

With the approval of the Eastern Roman emperor Zeno, Theodoric entered Italy in 489 and defeated Odoacer at the Isonzo and Verona; Odoacer shut himself up in Ravenna. The three-year siege ended in 493 with the surrender of Ravenna.
