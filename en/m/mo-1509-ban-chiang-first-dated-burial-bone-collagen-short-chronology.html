Ban Chiang: first dated burial (bone collagen, short chronology)

1509 BC

A Neolithic rice-farmer settlement/cemetery in northeastern Thailand; according to Higham's short chronology, rice farmers in the 16th century BCE, the Bronze Age transition in ~1000 BCE, the Iron Age in the second half of the 1st millennium BCE.
