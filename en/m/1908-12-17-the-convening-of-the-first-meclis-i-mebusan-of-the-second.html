The Convening of the First Meclis-i Meb'ûsan of the Second Constitutional Era

17 December 1908

The assembly, formed through the two-stage elections held after an interval of thirty years, was opened on 17 December 1908 with the sultan's speech, and Ahmed Rızâ Bey, the leader of the Young Turk movement, was elected its president. Composed of Turkish, Arab, Albanian, Greek, Armenian and Jewish deputies, the assembly was the last great trial of the ideal of Ottomanism. However, Bulgaria's independence, the annexation of Bosnia and Crete's declaration of union with Greece had overshadowed this optimism even before the assembly opened.
