Treaty of Apamea: the Seleucids withdrew from the west of the Taurus Mountains and paid an indemnity

187 BC

The treaty between Rome and Antiochus III gave Anatolia west of the Taurus to Rome's allies (Pergamum, Rhodes); the Seleucid fleet and the number of elephants were limited, and an indemnity of 15,000 talents was paid. The Seleucids now became a Syrian-Mesopotamian state.
