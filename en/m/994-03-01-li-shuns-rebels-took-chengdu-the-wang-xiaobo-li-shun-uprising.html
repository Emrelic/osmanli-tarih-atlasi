Li Shun's rebels took Chengdu (the Wang Xiaobo-Li Shun uprising)

1 March 994

Li Shun, who took charge of the uprising after Wang Xiaobo was killed at Jiangyuan in the 12th month of the 4th year of Chunhua, took Hanzhou and Pengzhou in the 1st month of the following year and captured Chengdu on the 16th day of that month (己巳); the governor Guo Zai fled to Zizhou, Li Shun settled in the city, and the rebels attacked the surrounding prefectures.
