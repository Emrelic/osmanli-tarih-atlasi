The death of Bayezid II on the road to Dimetoka

10 June 1512

The former sultan, who after abdicating left Istanbul to go to Dimetoka, the city of his birth, fell ill at the village of Abalar near Çorlu while advancing in his litter, which could cover only 5-6 km a day, and died on 25 Rabi al-Awwal 918 (10 June 1512). The TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam finds the cause of death very suspicious; according to some native and foreign sources, the possibility that he was poisoned is under consideration. After a reign of thirty-one years, the eighth sultan of the line of Osman Gazi thus lost his life.
