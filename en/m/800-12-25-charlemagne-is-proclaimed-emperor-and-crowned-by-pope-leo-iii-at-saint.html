Charlemagne is proclaimed emperor and crowned by Pope Leo III at Saint Peter's in Rome

25 December 800

Charlemagne went to Rome in 800 with the aim of restoration; on Christmas Day, as he rose from prayer in the Church of Saint Peter, he was crowned by Pope Leo III and proclaimed emperor and augustus.
