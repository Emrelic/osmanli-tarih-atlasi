The Avars destroyed the Gepid kingdom; the Avar Khaganate began on the Hungarian plain

567

The Avars, allied with the Lombards, crushed the Gepids (567-568), and the Lombards withdrew to Italy; Avar dominion spread from the area between the Danube and the Tisza across the Carpathian Basin.
