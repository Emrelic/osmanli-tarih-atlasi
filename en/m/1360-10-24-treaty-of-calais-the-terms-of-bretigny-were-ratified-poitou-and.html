Treaty of Calais: the terms of Brétigny were ratified, Poitou and Aquitaine were ceded to England: Poitiers

24 October 1360

The preliminary agreement of Brétigny of 8 May 1360 was ratified at Calais on 24 October 1360: Poitou, Saintonge, Aunis, Agenais, Périgord, Limousin, Quercy, Angoumois and Rouergue were left to England. Limoges, La Rochelle and Poitiers pass to England in the atlas on this day.
