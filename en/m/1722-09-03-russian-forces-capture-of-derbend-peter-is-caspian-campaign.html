Russian forces' capture of Derbend: Peter I's Caspian campaign

3 September 1722

While the Safavid State was being dragged into internal disorder by the Afghan invasion, the Russian Tsar Peter I set out on a campaign to seize the Iranian lands on the Caspian shore; the coastal city of Derbend surrendered to the Russian forces without any notable resistance. According to the Derbend entry of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, this occupation was the beginning of a temporary Russian rule that would last thirteen years; the Ottoman State, too, taking advantage of the same disorder, turned toward the western part of the Caucasus. The army had gone ashore from Astrakhan by sea in the Agrakhan gulf, and from there had marched overland along the coast via Tarku; the shamkhal of Tarku hosted Peter and entered Russian allegiance.
