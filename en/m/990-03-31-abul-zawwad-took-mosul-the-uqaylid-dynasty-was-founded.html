Abū'l-Zawwād took Mosul; the Uqaylid dynasty was founded

31 March 990

The Uqaylid Abū'l-Zawwād Muhammad b. al-Musayyab seized Mosul (380 / 990); the first day of the intersection of Hijri 380 ∩ 990, 31 March 990, was chosen (Hijri-Gregorian table, ±1-2 days); the day is not in the source.
