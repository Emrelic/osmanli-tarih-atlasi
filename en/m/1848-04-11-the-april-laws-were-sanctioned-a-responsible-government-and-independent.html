The April Laws were sanctioned — a responsible government and independent ministries for Hungary

11 April 1848

The laws adopted by the Diet of Pozsony in March were sanctioned by King Ferdinand V: a Hungarian council of responsible ministers, the abolition of serfdom obligations, and an extended franchise. These laws were revived as the constitutional basis leading to the Ausgleich (1867).
