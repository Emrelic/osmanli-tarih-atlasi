End of the Eretna Beylik and founding of the State of Kadı Burhaneddin

1 October 1381

The Eretnids, who arose in 1335 from the Ilkhanid governorship and governed Central Anatolia, centred on Sivas and Kayseri, for forty-six years, dissolved through palace intrigues. Kadı Burhaneddin, who became vizier in May 1378, after eliminating his rivals Kılıcarslan and Hacı Şadgeldi, proclaimed his sultanate in the autumn of 1381, ended the beylik and founded the state known by his name. The eastern neighbour of the Ottomans thus changed hands; the Sivas-Kayseri line was to remain in the hands of Kadı Burhaneddin for seventeen years thereafter.
