Synod of Worms: Henry IV declares that he has deposed Gregory VII; Gregory excommunicates him

1076

The synod of German bishops that met at Worms in January 1076 declared Gregory deposed; Henry wrote to the pope as 'not pope, but false monk'. Henry was at once excommunicated; his supporters dwindled and the Saxons took up arms.
