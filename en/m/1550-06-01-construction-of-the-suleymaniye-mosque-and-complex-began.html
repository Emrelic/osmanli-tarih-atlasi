Construction of the Süleymaniye Mosque and complex began

1 June 1550

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on the Süleymaniye Mosque and Complex, the complex, whose foundation was laid in 1550 under the chief architect Mimar Sinan, was designed as a great social complex made up, besides the mosque, of madrasas, a hospital, a soup kitchen and a library. The construction was one of the greatest building programmes symbolising the power of Süleyman the Lawgiver's reign and the development of Istanbul.
