Dulkadiroğlu Shahsuvar Bey took Besni from the Mamluks (approximate)

1467

Shahsuvar Bey (1465-1472), appointed Dulkadir bey by Mehmed the Conqueror, took Besni, Gerger, Birecik and Rumkale, which were in Mamluk hands; the Mamluks were defeated at Turnadağı in the autumn of 1467 and near Antep in the spring of 1468.
