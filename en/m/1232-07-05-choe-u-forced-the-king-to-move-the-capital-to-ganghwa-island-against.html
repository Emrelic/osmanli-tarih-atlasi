Choe U forced the king to move the capital to Ganghwa Island (against the Mongol invasions)

5 July 1232

After the first Mongol invasion (1231), under Mongol pressure Choe U forced King Gojong to move the court to Ganghwa; the next day construction of a palace began at Ganghwa. The move marks the beginning of Goryeo's thirty-year strategy of resistance to the Mongols.
