Badr al-Dīn Luʾluʾ sultan of Mosul

1233

With the death of the last Zengid atabeg, Badr al-Dīn Luʾluʾ was proclaimed sultan by the caliph. (Rabīʿ al-awwal 631 / December 1233; in conformity with the register entry (künye), the atlas writes it at year precision.)
