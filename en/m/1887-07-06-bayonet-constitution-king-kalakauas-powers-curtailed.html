Bayonet Constitution: King Kalākaua's powers curtailed

6 July 1887

An armed militia group threatened King Kalākaua and forced him to sign a new constitution that largely curtailed the powers of the kingdom; the document is known as the 'Bayonet Constitution' and placed the throne effectively under the control of the white settler elite.
