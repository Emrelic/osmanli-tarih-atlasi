The Banat was divided, Timișoara (Temeşvar) was left to Romania (Trianon arts. 27/3 and 45)

4 June 1920

The Treaty of Trianon drew the Hungarian-Romanian border with art. 27/3, and with art. 45 Hungary renounced in favour of Romania all its rights and titles in the former monarchy's territories beyond this border; thus the union of Transylvania, the Banat, Crișana and Maramureș with Romania was recognised in international law. The Banat region was divided between Romania and Serbia; the city of Timișoara was joined to the Kingdom of Romania. Romanian administration had been in effective operation in Timișoara since 3 August 1919; the treaty put this situation into law.
