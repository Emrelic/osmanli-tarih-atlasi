The retaking of Afyon — the battles of Aslıhanlar

7 April 1921

After the Greek offensive that was halted on 1 April at the Second İnönü, Afyon was retaken on 7 April on the southern front. The TDV article on the National Struggle summarizes this as 'after a heavy defeat at Aslıhanlar, Afyon was retaken on 7 April'; however, military-history studies place the Aslıhanlar–Dumlupınar fighting after the evacuation of Afyon, between 8 and 15 April, and write that tactical superiority remained on the Greek side — the two accounts do not agree, and both are given. The victory of İnönü was celebrated with rallies throughout the country, first of all in Istanbul.
