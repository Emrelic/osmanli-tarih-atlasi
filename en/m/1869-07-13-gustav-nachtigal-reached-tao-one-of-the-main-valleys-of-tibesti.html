Gustav Nachtigal reached Täo, one of the main valleys of Tibesti

13 July 1869

Gustav Nachtigal, with his caravan from Murzuk, advanced along the south-western foot of the Tibesti highlands; in his book Sahara und Sudan he describes his arrival on 13 July in the Täo valley, which he presents as one of Tibesti's main valleys and the true home of the noble families (the chapter «Ankunft in Täo»). He writes that on the morning of the same day they entered the plain of the Täo riverbeds and then met Galma, a relative of Bü Zeid.
