Second Albanian campaign — the Conqueror's personal march against Skanderbeg

1 June 1466

To break Skanderbeg's resistance, which had lasted more than a quarter century, Mehmed the Conqueror mounted great campaigns in 1466-1467, commanding them in person. The Ottoman army besieged Akçahisar (Kruja) and built the fortress of Ilbasan (Elbasan) in order to bring the region under control. The campaign, while breaking the back of Albanian resistance, did not succeed in bringing down the fortress; the full subjugation of the region was to take ten years after Skanderbeg's death.
