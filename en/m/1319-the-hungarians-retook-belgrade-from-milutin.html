The Hungarians retook Belgrade from Milutin

1319

After Dragutin's death in 1316, Serbia wanted to keep Belgrade while Hungary demanded its return; in 1319 the Hungarians attacked, and after months of fighting the Serbian army withdrew and the city, in ruins, was joined to the Hungarian banate of Mačva. Belgrade remained under Hungarian rule until it was left to Stefan Lazarević in 1403-04.
