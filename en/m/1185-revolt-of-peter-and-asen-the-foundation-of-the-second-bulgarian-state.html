Revolt of Peter and Asen: the foundation of the Second Bulgarian State around Tarnovo

1185

The boyar brothers Peter and Asen rose against Byzantine taxes around Tarnovo; although the emperor retook the lands in 1186, the brothers recovered northern Bulgaria with an army they obtained from the Cumans beyond the Danube, and in 1187 Byzantium was forced to make peace.
