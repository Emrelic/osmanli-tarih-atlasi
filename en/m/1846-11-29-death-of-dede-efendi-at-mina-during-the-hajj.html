Death of Dede Efendi at Mina during the hajj

29 November 1846

Hammamizade Ismail Dede Efendi, who caught cholera at Mina, where he had gone to perform the obligation of the hajj, died on 29 November 1846. With the death of the artist, who between 1824 and 1839 had composed seven separate Mevlevi ayins, an era of classical Ottoman music closed.
