Tashkent passed to the administration of Yunus Khan, khan of Moghulistan

1485

During the struggle for the throne among the Timurid princes, Tashkent and its surroundings fell into the hands of Yunus Khan, khan of Eastern Chagatai (Moghulistan). When Yunus Khan died two years later, his son Mahmud Khan succeeded him; the city remained with the Moghul khans until Shaybani Khan's Uzbeks took it in 1503.
