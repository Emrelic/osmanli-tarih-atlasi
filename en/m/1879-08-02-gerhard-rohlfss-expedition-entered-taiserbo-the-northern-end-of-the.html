Gerhard Rohlfs's expedition entered Taiserbo, the northern end of the Kufra oasis archipelago

2 August 1879

Gerhard Rohlfs's expedition for the German African Society, which had left Tripoli, marched south after the agreement made with Suya in July 1879 and entered the Taiserbo oasis, the northernmost island of Kufra, at 2 a.m. Rohlfs describes this as finally reaching the «mysterious oasis of Kufra»; the Djrängedi well was reached only at 11 the next morning.
