Murad IV's ban on tobacco and coffeehouses

1 October 1633

After the Cibali Fire, out of concern for fire and public order, Murad IV banned tobacco smoking and coffeehouses; heavy penalties, including the death penalty, were applied to those who did not comply. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on 'Murad IV', the sultan followed so strict a policy of internal discipline that he roamed the streets of the city in disguise himself to enforce the ban. These harsh measures are regarded as part of Murad IV's effort to re-establish central authority.
