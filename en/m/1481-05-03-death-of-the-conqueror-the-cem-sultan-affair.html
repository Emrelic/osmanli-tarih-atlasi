Death of the Conqueror — the Cem Sultan affair

3 May 1481

Upon the sudden death of the Conqueror on the road of campaign, Bayezid II came to the throne; his brother Prince Cem, claiming the sultanate, had the sermon read and coins struck in his name at Bursa. Defeated, he took refuge first with the Mamluks, then with the Knights of Rhodes; Cem, who became a bargaining chip of European diplomacy in the hands of the knights and the papacy, died in Naples in 1495. This affair forced Ottoman foreign policy to behave cautiously for fifteen years.
