Landgrave Philip 'the Magnanimous' died in Kassel — Hesse was divided among his four sons

31 March 1567

Philip I, Landgrave of Hesse, one of the political leaders of the Reformation, died in Kassel on 31 March 1567. In accordance with his will of 1562, the country was shared among his four sons as Hesse-Kassel (Wilhelm IV), Hesse-Marburg (Ludwig IV), Hesse-Rheinfels (Philipp II) and Hesse-Darmstadt (Georg); the united Landgraviate of Hesse thus came to an end.
