Death of Murad II and the second accession of Mehmed II

18 February 1451

Murad II, who fell ill after his son's wedding, died at Edirne on 1 Muharram 855 (3 February 1451). The nineteen-year-old Mehmed II acceded to the Ottoman throne for the second time on 16 Muharram 855 (18 February 1451) — this time permanently. The return of the young ruler who had been deposed at Buçuktepe five years earlier is the beginning of the reign that, two years later, would be crowned with the conquest of Constantinople and transform the Ottoman Beylik into a world empire.
