Dursun Mehmed Pasha entered Vidin — the Danube bank is Ottoman again

29 August 1690

In Fâzıl Mustafa Pasha's counteroffensive, the land army of Dursun Mehmed Pasha, the mutasarrıf of Karaman, and the Danube fleet of Mezemorta Hüseyin Pasha retook Vidin from Austria.
