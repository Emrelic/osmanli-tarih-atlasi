Avidius Cassius proclaimed himself emperor in Syria and Egypt; he was killed three months later

175

Cassius, commander of the eastern provinces, proclaimed himself emperor on the news of Marcus Aurelius's death; the revolt spread to Syria and Egypt (Dio records that the prefect of Egypt, Flavius Calvisius, because of his connection with the revolt, was not put to death afterward but exiled to an island). According to Dio, after a 'dream of empire' lasting three months and six days, Cassius was killed by one of his own officers; Marcus Aurelius visited Egypt and Syria and punished no one.
