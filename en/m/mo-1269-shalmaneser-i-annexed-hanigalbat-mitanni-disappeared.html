Shalmaneser I annexed Hanigalbat; Mitanni disappeared

1269 BC

Shalmaneser I, who suppressed the revolt of Shattuara II, king of Hanigalbat, annexed the country directly to Assyria and deported its people. The Mitanni state was erased from the stage of history by the successive blows of the Hittites and the Assyrians.
