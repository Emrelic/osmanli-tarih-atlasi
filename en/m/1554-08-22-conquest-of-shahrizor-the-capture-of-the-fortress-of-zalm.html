Conquest of Shahrizor: the capture of the fortress of Zalm

22 August 1554

During Süleyman the Lawgiver's Nakhchivan campaign, the Beylerbeyi of Baghdad Osman Pasha had been charged with seizing the Shahrizor region; Baltacı Mehmed Pasha, who became beylerbeyi of Baghdad upon his death, besieged Zalm, the central fortress of the region, and took it on 22 August 1554 (23 Ramadan 961). Thus the Shahrizor basin on the western slope of the Zagros came under Ottoman administration and the connection between Baghdad and Kurdistan was secured; the region was first made a sancak and given to Murad Beg. In 1563 Beylerbeyi Muzaffer Pasha built the fortress of Gülanber as the new provincial center; the fortress would be demolished in 1623 on Shah Abbas's order and rebuilt in 1638 in the time of Hüsrev Pasha.
