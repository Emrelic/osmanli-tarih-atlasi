The Beginning of the Ottoman-Austrian 'Long War'

1 July 1593

Upon the defeat and martyrdom of the Bosnian Beylerbeyi Telli Hasan Pasha before Kulpa (Sisak), the Ottoman State declared war on Austria and the Long War, which would last thirteen years, began. This war, in which infantry with firearms was decisive, accelerated the employment of sekban and musket-armed troops in the Ottoman army order and deepened the decline of the timar-holding sipahi. The war would end in 1606 with the Treaty of Zsitvatorok.
