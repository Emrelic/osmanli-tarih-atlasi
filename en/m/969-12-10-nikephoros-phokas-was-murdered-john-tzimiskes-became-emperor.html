Nikephoros Phokas was murdered; John Tzimiskes became emperor

10 December 969

Six weeks after the conquest of Antioch, in the night of 10-11 December 969, Nikephoros II Phokas was killed in a palace assassination; John Tzimiskes (969-976) succeeded him.
