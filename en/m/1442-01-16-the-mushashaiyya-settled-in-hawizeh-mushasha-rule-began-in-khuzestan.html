The Musha'sha'iyya settled in Hawizeh — Musha'sha' rule began in Khuzestan

16 January 1442

Muhammad b. Falah, who had appeared claiming to be the Mahdi, after establishing himself among the Ma'adi tribes, succeeded the following year in settling in Hawizeh, which was under Timurid control. The city became the centre of the Musha'sha'i dynasty that would rule in Khuzestan.
