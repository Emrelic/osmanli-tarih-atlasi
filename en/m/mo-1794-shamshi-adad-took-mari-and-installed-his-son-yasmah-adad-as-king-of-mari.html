Shamshi-Adad took Mari and installed his son Yasmah-Adad as king of Mari

1794 BC

Shamshi-Adad, king of Aššur and Ekallatum, seized the kingdom of Mari on the Euphrates, eliminated the dynasty of Yahdun-Lim and installed his younger son Yasmah-Adad at Mari. The "Kingdom of Upper Mesopotamia" thus became an Amorite empire stretching from the Zagros to the Euphrates.
