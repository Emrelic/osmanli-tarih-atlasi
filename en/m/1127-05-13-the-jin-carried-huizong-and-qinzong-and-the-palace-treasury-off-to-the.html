The Jin carried Huizong and Qinzong and the palace treasury off to the north

13 May 1127

At the end of the 3rd month (丁巳) the Jin forced the Retired Emperor Huizong to march north; on the first day of the 4th month the emperor Qinzong, the empress and the crown prince were also taken away; the palace carriages, temple objects, maps and books, craftsmen and the treasury were removed from Kaifeng.
