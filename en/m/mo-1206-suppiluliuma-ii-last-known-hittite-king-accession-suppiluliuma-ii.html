Suppiluliuma II (last known Hittite king) — accession: Suppiluliuma II accedes (last Hittite king)

1206 BC

The reign of the last known Hittite king begins in ~1207 BCE; the Hittite empire and its central administration collapse 'around or shortly after 1200'; terminus ante quem Ramesses III year 8 (Medinet Habu).
