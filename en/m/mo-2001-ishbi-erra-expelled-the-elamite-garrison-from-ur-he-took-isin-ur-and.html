Ishbi-Erra expelled the Elamite garrison from Ur; he took Isin, Ur and Nippur under his dominion

2001 BC

After the capture of Ur by Elam (c. 2004), Ishbi-Erra expelled the Elamite garrison from Ur and bound Ur and Nippur to Isin. As the political heir of Ur, Isin bore the title of king of Sumer and Akkad for centuries.
