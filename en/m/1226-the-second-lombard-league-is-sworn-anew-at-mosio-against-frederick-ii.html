The Second Lombard League is sworn anew at Mosio against Frederick II

1226

When Frederick II demanded oaths of allegiance from the Lombard communes, the communes renewed the League in 1226; Milan, Bologna, Brescia, Bergamo, Turin, Vicenza, Padua, Treviso and later Piacenza, Verona, Faenza and others joined. The emperor annulled the privileges of Constance.
