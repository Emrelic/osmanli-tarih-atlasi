The landing on Cyprus: the capture of Limassol

2 July 1570

The Ottoman army that landed on the island seized Limassol without encountering much resistance and the next day turned toward Larnaca. Because the local population was dissatisfied with Venetian rule and helped the Ottoman forces, no serious resistance was met at Larnaca either; the landing of Tuzla was made a base for landing troops and equipment. Venice had concentrated its defence at Nicosia and Famagusta; the governor of Paphos declared his obedience to the commander-in-chief only after Nicosia had fallen.
