The Empire of Trebizond was founded — Giresun and Sinop also within its borders

1204

Alexios Komnenos, who fled to Trebizond upon the capture of Constantinople by the Latins, proclaimed himself emperor; the city became the centre of the new state. The state founded by the Komnenian grandsons Alexios and David extended as far as Sinop; Giresun also remained within the borders of the Empire of Trebizond.
