Leo, in the Cathedral of Tarsus, puts on the crown sent by the Holy Roman Emperor Henry VI and becomes the first king of the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia

6 January 1199

Leo, the Rubenid prince, put on the crown sent by the Holy Roman Emperor Henry VI in the Cathedral of Tarsus and was anointed by the papal legate; the Rubenid principality became the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia.
