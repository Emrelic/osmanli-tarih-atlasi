The loss of Modon to Venice

10 July 1686

Modon, at the southwestern tip of the Morea peninsula, was the port that Venice had lost three centuries earlier and called 'one of the two eyes of the state'. The fortress, besieged after the fall of Koroni, was taken by being battered simultaneously from land and sea. Morosini's Morea operation thus captured the entire western coast of the peninsula, and the road to the interior was opened.
