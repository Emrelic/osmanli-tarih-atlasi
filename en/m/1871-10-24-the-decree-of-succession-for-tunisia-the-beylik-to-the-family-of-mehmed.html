The decree of succession for Tunisia — the beylik to the family of Mehmed Sadık Pasha

24 October 1871

By a firman issued through the efforts of Hayreddin Pasha, whom the bey of Tunis, Mehmed Sadık Pasha, sent to Istanbul taking advantage of France's defeat by Prussia, Tunisia was given to Mehmed Sadık Pasha and his progeny by hereditary succession, remaining directly attached to Istanbul. War and peace negotiations would not be conducted without the sultan's permission, no territory of the country would be given to anyone else, and the Ottoman demand for troops would be met. France did not recognise the firman; ten years later it occupied Tunisia.
