The fortress of Faş (Poti) passed into Russian hands a second time — it remained with Russia by the Treaty of Edirne

26 June 1828

At the beginning of the Ottoman–Russian war of 1828–1829, the fortress of Faş was surrendered to the Russians on 26 June 1828. By the Treaty of Edirne of 14 September 1829, Poti was left to Russia together with Akhaltsikhe, Akhalkalaki and Anapa.
