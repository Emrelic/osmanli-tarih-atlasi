The Second Deposition of Mustafa I and the Accession of Murad IV

10 September 1623

Mustafa I, who had been placed on the throne for a second time after the killing of Osman II, was deposed again after one year and three months, and the eleven-year-old Murad IV was proclaimed sultan. For the first nine years the state was in effect governed by his mother Kösem Sultan and the notables of the palace. Murad IV would take over the administration in person from 1632 and begin a period of harsh centralisation.
