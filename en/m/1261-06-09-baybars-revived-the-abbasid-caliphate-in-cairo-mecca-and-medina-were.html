Baybars revived the Abbasid caliphate in Cairo — Mecca and Medina were attached to the Mamluks

9 June 1261

Baybars proclaimed in Cairo as caliph the Abbasid Ahmad, who had survived the destruction of Baghdad, and swore allegiance to him; as patron of the caliphate he also bound the Haramayn to Mamluk suzerainty. The day is that of the revival of the caliphate; the day of the attachment of Mecca and Medina is not in the source (lower bound).
