The loss of Preveza and Vonitsa to Venice — the opening of the Epirote coast

29 September 1684

Venice, which had joined the Holy League, before beginning the Morea campaign took the two fortresses holding the mouth of the Gulf of Arta: Preveza and Vonitsa on the opposite shore. In the same summer Santa Maura (Lefkada) had also fallen. These three positions allowed the fleet to be brought safely from the Ionian Sea into the interior of Greece and became the base of the Morea operation that would begin the following year. Preveza and Vonitsa were also left with Venice at Passarowitz; they would return to Ottoman administration only in 1798.
