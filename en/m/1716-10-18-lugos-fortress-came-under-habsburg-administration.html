Lugos fortress came under Habsburg administration

18 October 1716

Lugoj, a sanjak fortress dependent on the Eyalet of Temesvár, had been in Ottoman hands since 1695; with the surrender of the provincial center at the end of the 1716 siege, it was left to the Habsburg Monarchy. The old record placed the transfer at 1 January 1716, the year floor.
