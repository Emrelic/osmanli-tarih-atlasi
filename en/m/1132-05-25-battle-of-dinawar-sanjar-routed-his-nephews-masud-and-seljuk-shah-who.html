Battle of Dinawar: Sanjar routed his nephews Mas'ud and Seljuk Shah, who were heading for Azerbaijan

25 May 1132

When Sanjar turned toward Rayy, Mahmud's brothers Mas'ud and Seljuk Shah had formed an alliance with the caliph; Sanjar routed his nephews, who were on their way to Azerbaijan, near Dinawar (8 Rajab 526 / 25 May 1132), gave Mas'ud Arran, whose centre was Ganja, and placed Tughrul on the Iraq Seljuk throne.
