The decree for the founding of the Darülfünun was issued

8 April 1869

The Darülfünun was officially founded by an imperial decree (irade) prepared by Grand Vizier Ali Pasha and Minister of Education Safvet Pasha; the first lectures, however, had begun earlier, in 1863. According to the TDV article on 'Darülfünun', the institution actually began teaching in February 1870 under the directorship of Hoca Tahsin Efendi, with departments of philosophy and literature, science and law.
