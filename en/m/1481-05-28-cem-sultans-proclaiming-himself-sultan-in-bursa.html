Cem Sultan's proclaiming himself sultan in Bursa

28 May 1481

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Cem Sultan, Cem, who reached Bursa by way of Karaman, seized the city, proclaimed himself sultan and had the khutba read. This short-lived sultanate ended when the forces sent by his elder brother Bayezid II defeated Cem at Yenişehir. After the defeat, Cem was forced to flee toward Egypt.
