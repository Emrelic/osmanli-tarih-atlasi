Battle of Tit — the Ahaggar Tuareg defeated by French forces

7 May 1902

In the battle fought near the Tit oasis on 7 May 1902 during the Cottenest expedition, French forces decisively defeated the Kel Ahaggar Tuareg; this interior region of the Sahara, including Abalessa, the center of the Ahaggar, and Idelès, came under the control of French Algeria.
