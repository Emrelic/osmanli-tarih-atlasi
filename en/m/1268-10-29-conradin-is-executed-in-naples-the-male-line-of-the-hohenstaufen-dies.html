Conradin is executed in Naples: the male line of the Hohenstaufen dies out

29 October 1268

Conradin, the last representative of the Hohenstaufen dynasty, was defeated by Charles, duke of Anjou, at Tagliacozzo (23 August 1268); he was then tried as a traitor in Naples and executed on 29 October together with his companion Frederick of Baden (titular duke of Austria). The male line of the Hohenstaufen died out.
