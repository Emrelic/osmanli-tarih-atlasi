Cleisthenes' reform: the institutional foundation of Athenian democracy was laid

507 BC

After the fall of Hippias, Cleisthenes organized the citizens through the demes, which were residential units, and ten new tribes; the Council of Five Hundred became the center of this order. It is the arrangement in which the concepts of isonomia/demokratia were born.
