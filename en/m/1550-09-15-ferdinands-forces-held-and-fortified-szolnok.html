Ferdinand's forces held and fortified Szolnok

15 September 1550

As the Ottomans were preparing to build a fortress at Szolnok on the Tisza, the forces of Ferdinand I got ahead of them: in mid-September 1550 they occupied Szolnok, and the fortress was made defensible by mid-October. Thus Szolnok, which belonged to Isabella's country, fell into Habsburg hands a year before Transylvania passed to Ferdinand; the Ottomans took the fortress in 1552.
