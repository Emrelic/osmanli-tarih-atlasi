Decree of Cyrus: the Jews exiled in Babylon were able to return to Jerusalem, and the rebuilding of the Temple was permitted

537 BC

Cyrus, who had taken Babylon, sent back the divine images of the exiled peoples and allowed them to return to their homelands (the Cyrus Cylinder). The Torah (Ezra 1:1-4) relates this decree separately for the Jews: the exiles could return to Jerusalem and rebuild the Temple. The region became the Persian satrapy of "Yehud".
