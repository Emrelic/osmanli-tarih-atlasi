Sultan Mas'ud died; Caliph al-Muqtafi became sole ruler in Iraq and took Hilla, Kufa and Wasit

8 April 1152

With the death of Sultan Mas'ud, Caliph al-Muqtafi li-Amr Allah established rule in Baghdad and Iraq independent of Seljuk influence; the caliph's vizier seized Hilla, and Kufa and Wasit were also taken.
