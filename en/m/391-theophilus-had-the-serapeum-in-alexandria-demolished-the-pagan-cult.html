Theophilus had the Serapeum in Alexandria demolished: the pagan cult centre in Egypt was closed

391

Following the edicts of Theodosius I banning pagan cults, Theophilus, bishop of Alexandria, had the temple of Serapis (the Serapeum) demolished; the tension between Christians and pagans in the city reached its climax in this episode. The fate of the sacred texts and book collections is disputed.
