Lysimachus placed his treasury at Pergamum; Philetaerus became the guardian of the fortress

300 BC

After Ipsus, Lysimachus had his treasury kept in the hilltop fortress of Pergamum and gave its guardianship to the eunuch Philetaerus. In 281 Philetaerus went over to Seleucus and laid the foundation of the dynasty.
