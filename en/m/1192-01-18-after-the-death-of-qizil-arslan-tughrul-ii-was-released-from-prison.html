After the death of Qizil Arslan, Tughrul II was released from prison

18 January 1192

Tughrul II, who was released from prison when Qizil Arslan was killed (588/1192), this time had to fight the Khwarazm-Shah Ala al-Din Tekish, who had designs on Iraq Seljuk territory; the Abbasid Caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah was also encouraging him against Tekish.
