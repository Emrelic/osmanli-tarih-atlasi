Philip II was assassinated at Aegae; Alexander ascended the throne

335 BC

In place of Philip, who was killed in the theatre by his bodyguard Pausanias at the wedding festival of his daughter Cleopatra, his son Alexander was proclaimed king by the army. The Persian campaign and the control of Greece were left to Alexander.
