Passage of Baghdad to the Safavids: Arab Iraq changed hands

1508

Following the Aqquyunlu Sultan Murad, who had taken refuge in Baghdad after the defeat at Hamadan, Shah Ismail entered Arab Iraq in 1508 and took Baghdad. The passing of the shrines at Karbala and Najaf to Safavid administration was as important as the taking of Tabriz with regard to the movement's sectarian claim. On the map Arab Iraq changes hands on the same day; this is the last and widest geographical step of the dissolution. Baghdad remained in Safavid hands until Süleyman the Lawgiver's Campaign of the Two Iraqs in 1534.
