Conquest of Belgrade

29 August 1521

Belgrade, which the Conqueror had been unable to take in 1456 and which was regarded as the 'key to Central Europe', fell in the Lawgiver's first campaign through a siege at the confluence of the Danube and the Sava. Hungary's southern line of defence had collapsed; this conquest opened the door to the Central European campaign that extended to Mohács five years later and to the annexation of Buda twenty years later.
