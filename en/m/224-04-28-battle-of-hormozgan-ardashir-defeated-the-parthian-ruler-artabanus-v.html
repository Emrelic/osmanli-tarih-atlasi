Battle of Hormozgan: Ardashir defeated the Parthian ruler Artabanus V — Isfahan entered the Sasanian core

28 April 224

In Fars, the local ruler Ardashir defeated the Parthian ruler Artabanus V at Hormozgan; the Parthian (Arsacid) dynasty collapsed and the Sasanian dynasty rose. The atlas regards Isfahan as Sasanian land from this day.
