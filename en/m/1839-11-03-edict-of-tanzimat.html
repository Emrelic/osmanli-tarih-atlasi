Edict of Tanzimat

3 November 1839

With the Imperial Rescript (Hatt-ı Hümâyun) read at Gülhane, the security of life, property and honour of all subjects without distinction of religion was placed under state guarantee; the principles of justice in taxation, order in military service, and no punishment without trial were proclaimed. The edict, the work of Mustafa Reşid Pasha, is regarded as the first great document of Ottoman modernization of a constitutional character, and gave its name to the 'Tanzimat Era' (1839–1876), the age of reform; a radical restructuring in administration, law, education and finance was carried out within this framework.
