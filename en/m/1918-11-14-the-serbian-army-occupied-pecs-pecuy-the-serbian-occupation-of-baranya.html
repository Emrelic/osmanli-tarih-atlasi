The Serbian army occupied Pécs (Peçuy) — the Serbian occupation of Baranya began

14 November 1918

Pécs, of strategic importance because of its coal mines, came under Serbian occupation on 14 November 1918. The Serbian occupation of Pécs and the province of Baranya lasted until August 1921.
