The First Wahhabi Capture of Mecca

30 April 1803

Two and a half months after the fall of Taif, the Wahhabi forces entered Mecca, destroyed the tombs and established an order according to their own understanding; Sharif Ghalib was forced to withdraw to Jeddah. The closing of the pilgrimage routes echoed throughout the Islamic world and obliged Istanbul to ask the governor of Egypt, Mehmed Ali, for military aid. This first dominion would last only three months.
