Suleyman Shah II dies; his young son Kilij Arslan III takes the throne

1204

Suleyman Shah II, who had achieved no result from the Georgian campaign of 1202, died on the road in 1204 as he marched on a second Georgian campaign (600/1204). He was succeeded by his young son Kilij Arslan III; during his reign of seven or eight months the Isparta region was conquered.
