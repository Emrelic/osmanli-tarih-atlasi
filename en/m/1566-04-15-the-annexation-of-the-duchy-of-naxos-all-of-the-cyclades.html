The annexation of the Duchy of Naxos — all of the Cyclades

15 April 1566

The Latin Duchy of Naxos, which had lasted since 1207 and had paid tribute to the Ottomans since 1537, was abolished on the pretext of the complaints that came in about its last duke, Giacomo Crispo, oppressing his people. Naxos, Paros, Milos, Andros, Syros, Mykonos, Ios, Amorgos and all the Cyclades islands around them were brought under direct Ottoman administration by a single decision. Süleyman the Lawgiver gave the administration of the islands for a time to Joseph Nasi; three and a half centuries of Latin rule thus ended in the middle of the Aegean.
