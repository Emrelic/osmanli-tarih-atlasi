Loss of Gallipoli (Savoyard Crusade)

1 August 1366

The fleet of Amadeus of Savoy, cousin of the Byzantine emperor, took Gallipoli and handed it over to Byzantium; the Ottoman bridgehead in Rumelia was for ten years governed from across the strait. Other settlements lost on the same date: Çimpe.
