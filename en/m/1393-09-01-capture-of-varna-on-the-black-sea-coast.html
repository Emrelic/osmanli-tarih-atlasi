Capture of Varna on the Black Sea coast

1 September 1393

While the Bulgarian Tsardom's lands between the Danube and the Black Sea were being squeezed, Varna, the largest port on the coast, came under Ottoman administration. The capture of the port opened the way for the advance toward Dobruja and gave the Ottomans their first major landing place on the western Black Sea coast. Fifty-one years later Varna would also be the scene of the victory of 1444, in which a Crusader army was destroyed.
