The Kingdom of Egypt was proclaimed — Sultan Ahmed Fuad took the title of king

15 March 1922

On 18 December 1914 Britain had unilaterally abolished the Ottoman State's rights of sovereignty over Egypt and taken the country under its protection. When the negotiations between Britain and Egypt after the war produced no result, Britain, again by a unilateral declaration on 28 February 1922, proclaimed Egypt an independent state. Sultan Ahmed Fuad took the title of king (malik) on 15 March 1922, and a monarchy was proclaimed in Egypt. The TDV regards this independence as nominal; the constitution of the kingdom's order would come into force on 19 April 1923.
