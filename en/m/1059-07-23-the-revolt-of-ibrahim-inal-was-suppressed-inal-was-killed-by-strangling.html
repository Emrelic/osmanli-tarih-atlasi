The revolt of Ibrahim Inal was suppressed; Inal was killed by strangling with a bowstring

23 July 1059

Ibrahim Inal, Toghril Beg's maternal half-brother, rebelled against the sultan in 449 (1058); the revolt, which lasted more than eight months, was suppressed with the help of Alp Arslan and Qavurd Beg, and Inal was killed on 9 Jumada al-Akhira 451 (23 July 1059).
