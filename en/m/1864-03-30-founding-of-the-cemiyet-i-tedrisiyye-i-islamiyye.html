Founding of the Cemiyet-i Tedrisiyye-i Islamiyye

30 March 1864

The Cemiyet-i Tedrisiyye-i Islamiyye was founded on 30 March 1864 in order to undertake the education of poor Muslim apprentices; this civil association initiative is a pioneering example of the charitable tradition being transformed into a modern school project. The association would open the Darüşşafaka nine years later.
