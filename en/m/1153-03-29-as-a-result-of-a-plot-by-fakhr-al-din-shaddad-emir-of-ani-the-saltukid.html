As a result of a plot by Fakhr al-Din Shaddad, emir of Ani, the Saltukid army is defeated by the Georgian King Demetrius; Izz al-Din Saltuk is taken prisoner

29 March 1153

Shaddad, emir of Ani, who had sought Saltuk's daughter and been refused, announced that he wished to sell the city to Saltuk and enter his service; when Saltuk came with his army, he informed the Georgian king. The Saltukid army was heavily defeated by King Demetrius, and Saltuk was taken prisoner and freed for a ransom of 100,000 dinars.
