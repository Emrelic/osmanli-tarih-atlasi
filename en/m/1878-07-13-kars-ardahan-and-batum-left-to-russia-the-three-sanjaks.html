Kars, Ardahan and Batum left to Russia — the Three Sanjaks

13 July 1878

By the Treaty of Berlin the Ottoman State left Kars, Ardahan and Batum to Russia against part of the war indemnity; the Doğubayazıt and Eleşkirt valley given to Russia at San Stefano remained Ottoman. Although Batum was given as a free port, it could preserve this status only until 1886. The Russians made Kars a province attached to the Transcaucasian governorate-general centered at Tbilisi; a population of up to 100,000 from the Three Sanjaks had to migrate toward Erzurum. By the same treaty Southern Bessarabia (Kiliya, Cahul, Bolgrad) was given back from Romania to Russia.
