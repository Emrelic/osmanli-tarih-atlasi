Bogislaw XIV, the last Duke of Pomerania, died in Stettin — the Griffin dynasty ended

10 March 1637

Bogislaw XIV, the last duke of Pomerania, died in Stettin on 10 March 1637, and with him the native Pomeranian dynasty became extinct. The actual partition of the duchy (Brandenburg-Sweden) was registered by the Peace of Westphalia in 1648.
