The execution of Prince Ahmed on the order of Selim the Grim

24 April 1513

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Selim I, Prince Ahmed, the favourite of his father Bayezid II and the real rival for the throne, was routed on the plain of Yenişehir in the struggle he undertook after his brother Selim's accession, and was caught and killed while fleeing from the battlefield. With this event Selim largely ended the rivalry for the throne among his brothers and nephews.
