Second Treaty of Thorn — Danzig (Gdańsk) and West Prussia attached to Poland

19 October 1466

The Second Treaty of Thorn (Toruń), which ended the Thirteen Years' War that had begun with the 1454 uprising of the Prussian Confederation against the rule of the Teutonic Order, was signed on 19 October 1466; the Order ceded to the Kingdom of Poland Eastern Pomerania, including Danzig (Gdansk), the Vistula mouth region including Elbing (Elbląg), and the Kulm land — these territories took the name 'Royal Prussia'. East Prussia, however, would remain with the Order until 1525.
