Two Roman provinces were founded in Hispania: Citerior and Ulterior

196 BC

The lands of Hispania taken from Carthage were in 197 divided for the first time into two separate provinces, each governed by a praetor. After this date the Celtiberian and Lusitanian wars on the peninsula were fought to extend the Roman provincial order.
