Egyptian forces evacuated the Morea

5 October 1828

The last units were embarked on ships; the peninsula was lost in practice and, with the London Protocol of 1830, became the nucleus of independent Greece. Mehmed Ali, for his part, had not received the promised governorship of the Morea and had lost a great fleet and army in return. Other settlements lost on the same date: Modon, Coron.
