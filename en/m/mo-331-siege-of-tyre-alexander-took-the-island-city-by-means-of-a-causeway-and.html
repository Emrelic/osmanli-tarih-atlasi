Siege of Tyre: Alexander took the island city by means of a causeway and destroyed it

331 BC

Alexander took the island of Tyre, the base of the Persian fleet, in seven months by building an artificial causeway; the city was plundered and most of its people were killed or enslaved. Persian sea power thus collapsed and the road to Egypt was opened.
