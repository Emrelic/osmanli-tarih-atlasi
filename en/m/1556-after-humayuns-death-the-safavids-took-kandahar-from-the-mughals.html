After Humayun's death the Safavids took Kandahar from the Mughals

1556

After Humayun's death in Delhi in 1556, Shah Tahmasb took advantage of the dispute among the Mughal governors of Kandahar and Zamindawar; a Safavid detachment, which came at the invitation of the governor of Zamindawar, entered Kandahar, and Tahmasb appointed his cousin Sultan Husayn Mirza as governor of the city. The city remained in Safavid hands until 1595.
