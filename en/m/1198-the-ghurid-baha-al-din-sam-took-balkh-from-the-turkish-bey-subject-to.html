The Ghurid Baha al-Din Sam took Balkh from the Turkish bey subject to the Qara Khitai

1198

In 1198 the Ghurid Baha al-Din Sam b. Muhammad took the city of Balkh, which was governed by a Turkish bey subject to the Qara Khitai; thereupon a Qara Khitai army under Tayangu crossed the Oxus and plundered Juzjan, but was defeated in battle by the Ghurid army and lost 12,000 men.
