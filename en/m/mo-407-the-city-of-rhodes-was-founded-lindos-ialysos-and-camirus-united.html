The city of Rhodes was founded: Lindos, Ialysos and Camirus united

407 BC

In 408 BC the three cities of the island of Rhodes formed a common state and united in the new city of Rhodes; the city, built to the plan of Hippodamus, became one of the most important naval bases and trade centres of the Hellenistic world.
