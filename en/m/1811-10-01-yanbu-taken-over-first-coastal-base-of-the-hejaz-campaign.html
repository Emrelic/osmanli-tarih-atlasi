Yanbu taken over — first coastal base of the Hejaz campaign

1 October 1811

Some two thousand Albanian infantry, who set out from Suez in newly built ships, landed near Yanbu in October 1811; the town surrendered after two days of weak resistance. The cavalry under Tosun's command followed the pilgrimage road by land and reached Yanbu only two weeks later by way of Aqaba and al-Wajh. Yanbu became the first base in the Hejaz of the Egyptian army that was to march on Medina and Mecca.
