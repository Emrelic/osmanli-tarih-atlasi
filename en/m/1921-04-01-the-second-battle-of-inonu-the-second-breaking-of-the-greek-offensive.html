The Second Battle of İnönü: The Second Breaking of the Greek Offensive

1 April 1921

The Greek army, which had achieved no result from the London Conference, launched a new offensive from the directions of Bursa and Uşak, but was again stopped on the İnönü ridges and obliged to withdraw. The telegram that Mustafa Kemal Pasha sent to İsmet Pasha, 'There you defeated not only the enemy but the nation's ill fortune', was written after this victory. The battle raised the international standing of the National Struggle and accelerated the process of Italy's withdrawal from Anatolia.
