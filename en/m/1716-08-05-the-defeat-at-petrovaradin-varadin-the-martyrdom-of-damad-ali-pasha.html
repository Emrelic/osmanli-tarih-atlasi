The Defeat at Petrovaradin (Varadin) — The Martyrdom of Damad Ali Pasha

5 August 1716

The Ottoman army, which crossed the Danube by way of Belgrade, met the Austrian forces under Prince Eugene before Petrovaradin; although in the first onset the Janissary charge forced the enemy trenches, the ranks broke up with the collapse of the cavalry wing. The Grand Vizier Silâhdar Damad Ali Pasha was shot in the forehead and martyred, and the camp and the artillery train fell entirely into enemy hands. This defeat, which the Ottoman chronicles recall as 'the year of Varadin', was the first link of the chain leading to the loss of Temesvár and, the following year, of Belgrade.
