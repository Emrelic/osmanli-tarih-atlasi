The fall of Dubica to Austria

26 August 1788

The first Austrian attacks in the spring of 1788 had been repulsed. In August Marshal Laudon re-established the siege; the fortress, whose food and ammunition had run out, surrendered on 26 August 1788. Dubica, together with Novi, which fell in October, was Austria's only military success that year. The fortress was returned to the Ottomans by the Treaty of Sistova of 1791.
