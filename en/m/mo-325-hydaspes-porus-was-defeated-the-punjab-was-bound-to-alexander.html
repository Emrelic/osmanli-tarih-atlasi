Hydaspes: Porus was defeated; the Punjab was bound to Alexander

325 BC

In May 326, with the support of Ambhi, the Raja of Taxila, Alexander crossed the Jhelum and defeated Porus; Porus was left in possession of his kingdom as Alexander's vassal. From this point Alexander advanced eastward but was halted at the Hyphasis by the objection of the army.
