Intensive export of Ankara mohair to Europe

1 June 1599

The mohair (sof) and shawl cloth (shali) woven in Ankara were exported not only to domestic markets such as Istanbul, Bursa, Damascus and Aleppo but also to Venice, Poland, England and France. In an example given by the TDV, in 1599 eighteen merchants bought 162 loads of mohair worth 5,700,000 akçe and shipped them to Aleppo and Venice.
