The Qara Khitai chamberlain Barak became governor of Isfahan for the Khwarazmshah Ghiyath al-Din Pirshah; the next year he was to take control of Kirman

1221

Barak had been the chamberlain of the Qara Khitai Gurkhan; after the fall of the Qara Khitai he entered the service of the Khwarazmshah Ala al-Din Muhammad. When his son Ghiyath al-Din Pirshah settled in Iraq-i Ajam, Barak joined the ranks of his emirs and was appointed governor of Isfahan (1221); with the title Qutlugh Khan he took control of Kirman the following year and founded the Qutlughkhanids.
