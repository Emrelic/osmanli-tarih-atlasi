Ferdinand I proclaimed king of Hungary — western and northern Hungary under the Habsburg crown

17 December 1526

The Habsburg Archduke Ferdinand, basing himself on his sister's marriage to Louis II, claimed the Hungarian throne and was proclaimed king by a portion of the nobles. Hungary was thus divided between two kings; its western and northern parts recognized Ferdinand. The Croatian nobles elected him separately in early 1527.
