Fall of Agadir: the Saadians begin to uproot Portugal from Morocco

12 March 1541

The Saadian sultan Muhammad al-Shaykh besieged the fortress of Santa Cruz on 16 February 1541 and captured it on 12 March; about six hundred Portuguese, including the governor Guterre de Monroy, were taken prisoner.
