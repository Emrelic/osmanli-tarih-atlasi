Definitive conquest of Silifke — breaking of the last resistance of the Karamanids

1473

Silifke Fortress had surrendered once to Gedik Ahmed Pasha in 1472, but in the same year Kasım Bey of Karaman, with the help of the Crusader fleet in the Mediterranean, had retaken the fortress. After the victory of Otlukbeli, the Conqueror assigned his son and Gedik Ahmed Pasha to seize the region definitively; with the help of the former Ottoman artillerymen the powder magazine was set alight and the fortress was swiftly brought down. Thus the last resistance of the Karamanids in the Taşeli-İçel mountains was broken; the liquidation of the principality would be completed in 1474.
