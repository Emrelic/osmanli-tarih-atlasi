The Egyptian garrison withdrew from Sulaymiyah in al-Kharj and reached Manfuha — southern Najd left without a head

13 August 1819

The Egyptian post that Ibrahim Pasha had placed at Sulaymiyah in al-Kharj after the destruction of Diriyah, before setting out on the Pasha's order of withdrawal, had four sheikhs of Dilam killed at a banquet and was besieged by the Bedouins. The rescued garrison reached Manfuha on 13 August 1819 and the march to Qasim began. In southern Najd no central authority remained; the Saud family's initiatives yielded no result until 1824.
