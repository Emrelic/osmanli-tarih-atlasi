Benghazi was definitively incorporated into Ottoman administration

1578

After Barqa came under Ottoman rule in 1551, Benghazi was definitively incorporated into Ottoman administration in 1578; it then remained, as a sanjak of the Tripoli province, under the rule of the deys in the 17th century and of the Karamanli family in the 18th century.
