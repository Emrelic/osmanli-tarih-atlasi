The Death of Selim II

13 December 1574

Selim II, unable to attend the divan for about two months after a fall, died. His death was kept secret in the palace for twelve days until his eldest son Murad came to Istanbul from Manisa. The sources differ on the day of death: Selânikî says 15 December; Gerlach, Mehmed b. Mehmed and Cenâbî say 13 December.
