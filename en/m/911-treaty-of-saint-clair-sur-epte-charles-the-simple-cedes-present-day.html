Treaty of Saint-Clair-sur-Epte: Charles the Simple cedes present-day Normandy to the Normans

911

Charles III made a treaty with the Normans at Saint-Clair-sur-Epte in 911; some of the Normans were baptized and the land that would later be called the Duchy of Normandy was ceded to them. The story of the marriage of Rollo with the king's sister is Dudo's narrative and is highly doubtful.
