Pyrrhus crossed to Italy to help Tarentum; he defeated the Romans at Heraclea

279 BC

Pyrrhus, who landed in Italy in 280 at Tarentum's call against Rome, defeated the army of Valerius Laevinus at Heraclea with elephants and cavalry but suffered heavy losses. He won again at Asculum in 279 and crossed to Sicily in 278-276.
