Selim III begins abolishing the monopoly of some tradesmen's guilds

1 June 1794

According to the TDV's Esnaf entry, in the Ottoman guild order, intertwined from its founding with the ahi organization, officials such as the "ahi baba" and the "sheikh" served as guild leaders until the beginning of the nineteenth century; when in the eighteenth century the guilds' raising of prices above the narh through monopoly created economic pressure, Selim III from 1794 began abolishing the monopoly of the basic-foodstuff guilds.
