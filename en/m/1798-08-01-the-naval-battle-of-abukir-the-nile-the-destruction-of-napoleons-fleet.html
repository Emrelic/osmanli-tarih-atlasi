The naval battle of Abukir (the Nile) — the destruction of Napoleon's fleet

1 August 1798

The French fleet that had carried Napoleon's army to Egypt was caught at anchor in Abukir Bay by the fleet of the British Admiral Nelson and was destroyed in a battle that lasted a night; the flagship L'Orient blew up. Nelson thus deprived France's land army in Egypt of naval support: the army could neither receive reinforcements nor return. Five weeks later the Ottoman State also declared war on France.
