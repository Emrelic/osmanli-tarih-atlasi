Ibn Saud took al-Ahsa — the end of Ottoman rule on the Gulf coast

8 July 1913

The emir of Najd, Abd al-Aziz b. Saud, expelled the Ottoman garrison and took the al-Ahsa region, and established his headquarters at Hofuf. Together with the ports of Qatif and Uqair, the western coast of the Gulf passed to Saudi rule; the Ottoman State's fifty-year administration of the region came to an end.
