The Death of Abdülhamid II at the Beylerbeyi Palace

10 February 1918

Abdülhamid II, who had been deposed in 1909 and brought first to Salonica and then, when the city was lost in the Balkan War, to Istanbul and kept under house arrest in the Beylerbeyi Palace, died. Throughout his reign of thirty-three years he had tried to gain time by keeping the state away from wars, but he was also remembered for his despotic rule. He was buried with a great crowd in the Tomb of Mahmud I on Divanyolu; his death coincided with a year of war in which the order he had established had long since been liquidated.
