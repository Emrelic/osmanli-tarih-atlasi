Treaty between Augustus and Phraates IV: the Roman standards of Crassus were returned

19 BC

The lost Roman standards of Crassus and Antony were returned to Augustus by Phraates IV as a diplomatic gesture. The treaty consolidated the Euphrates as the Roman-Parthian frontier and left Armenia as a buffer.
