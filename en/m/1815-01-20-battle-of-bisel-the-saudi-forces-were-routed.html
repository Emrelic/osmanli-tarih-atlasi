Battle of Bisel: the Saudi forces were routed

20 January 1815

The Saudis, who suffered a heavy defeat in the pitched battle, withdrew to the interior. The truce that was made did not last; two years later the operation would be carried into the heart of Najd. After this victory Turaba and Hurma east of Ta'if are shown on the map as having left Saudi hands (event source Lorimer, Gazetteer; the settlement-specific attestation and the 13 January day are approximate information).
