The occupation of Yanova (Ineu) by Transylvanian forces

22 October 1595

In the Long War, Transylvanian forces, allies of the Habsburgs, under György Borbély occupied the town of Yanova and the palisaded fort of Erdöç; the fortress passed out of Ottoman hands.
