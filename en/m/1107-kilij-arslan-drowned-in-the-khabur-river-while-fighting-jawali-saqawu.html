Kilij Arslan drowned in the Khabur river while fighting Jawali Saqawu, governor of Mosul

1107

Kilij Arslan, who crossed to Mosul at Zengi's invitation, went to war with Jawali Saqawu, governor of Mosul for the Great Seljuk Muhammad Tapar; when the south-eastern Anatolian beys with him deserted, he drove his horse into the Khabur river to avoid capture and drowned (500/1107); his youngest son Tughril Arslan was placed on the throne in Malatya.
