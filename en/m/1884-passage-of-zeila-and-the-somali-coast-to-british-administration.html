Passage of Zeila and the Somali coast to British administration

1884

When the Mahdist uprising collapsed Egypt's garrisons in the Sudan and on the Red Sea, Cairo was forced to evacuate its remote posts on the Abyssinian coast. The British administration in Aden landed troops at Zeila and Berbera in 1884; the following year protectorate treaties were signed with the Somali tribal chiefs and British Somaliland was established. Zeila, which had been attached to the province of Habesh since 1559, was thus lost.
