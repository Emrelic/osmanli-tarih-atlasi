Khan Krum died: preparations for the siege of Constantinople were left unfinished

14 April 814

While Krum was preparing a great army equipped with numerous siege engines against Constantinople, he died on 14 April 814 of a sudden rupture of a blood vessel. His son Omurtag made a thirty-year peace with Byzantium.
