Amenhotep IV (Akhenaten) moved the capital of Egypt to Akhetaten (Amarna)

1347 BC

Akhenaten made the worship of the Aten the state religion and declared a new city (Akhetaten), opened in the desert in Middle Egypt, the capital; the 'Boundary Stelae' defining the city's limits are dated to years 4-5. The Amarna letters show that in this age the Syrian vassals asked for help but Egypt did not respond.
