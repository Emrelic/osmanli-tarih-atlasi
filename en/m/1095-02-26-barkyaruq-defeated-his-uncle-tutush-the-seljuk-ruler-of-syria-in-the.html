Barkyaruq defeated his uncle Tutush, the Seljuk ruler of Syria, in the battle near Rayy; Tutush died on the battlefield

26 February 1095

Tutush was defeated in the battle fought near Rayy because some of his soldiers went over to the other side, and died on the battlefield (17 Safar 488 / 26 February 1095; Dāshilū, near Rayy); this time Barkyaruq was faced by his brother Muhammad Tapar, the ruler of Arran.
