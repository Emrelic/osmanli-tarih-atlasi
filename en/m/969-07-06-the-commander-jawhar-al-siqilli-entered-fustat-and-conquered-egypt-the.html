The commander Jawhar al-Ṣiqillī entered Fustat and conquered Egypt; the Ikhshidids came to an end

6 July 969

In the name of the Fatimid caliph al-Muʿizz li-Dīn Allāh, Jawhar al-Ṣiqillī entered Fustat (17 Shaʿbān 358 / 6 July 969); Egypt passed from the Ikhshidids to the Fatimids, and the region where Cairo was to be founded came under Fatimid rule.
