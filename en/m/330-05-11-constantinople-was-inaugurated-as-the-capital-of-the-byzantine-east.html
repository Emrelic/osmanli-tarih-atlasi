Constantinople was inaugurated as the capital of the Byzantine (East Roman) Empire

11 May 330

Byzantion, refounded by Constantine, was inaugurated as the new capital of the empire with ceremonies lasting days. The atlas's Byzantine (East Roman) record at Constantinople begins with this day.
