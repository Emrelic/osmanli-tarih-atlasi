Second Siege of Vienna began — the army before Vienna

14 July 1683

The Ottoman army under the command of the grand vizier Kara Mustafa Pasha arrived before Vienna on 14 July 1683. Emperor Leopold I left the defence of the city to Starhemberg and withdrew into the interior of Austria. This second siege, prepared with Vienna directly as the target, unlike the first, was to last until 12 September.
