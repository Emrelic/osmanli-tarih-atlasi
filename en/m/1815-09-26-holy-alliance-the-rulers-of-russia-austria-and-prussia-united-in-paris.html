Holy Alliance — the rulers of Russia, Austria and Prussia united in Paris

26 September 1815

After Napoleon's defeat, the Russian Tsar Alexander I, the Austrian Emperor Francis I and the Prussian King Frederick William III signed a declaration pledging to rule according to Christian principles and to help one another. The alliance became the conservative support of the Vienna Congress order.
