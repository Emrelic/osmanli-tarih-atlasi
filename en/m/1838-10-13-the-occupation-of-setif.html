The occupation of Sétif

13 October 1838

After the fall of Constantine a year earlier, French forces climbed onto the plateau and, taking the old Roman city of Sétif, established a permanent garrison there. Sétif was the point where the caravan routes between the coast and the Sahara intersected; its occupation showed that France had begun to control not only the ports but also the inland trade network.
