Death of John Szapolyai — the Buda question and the door to the partition of Hungary

22 July 1540

Szapolyai, the Ottoman vassal king of Eastern Hungary, died leaving as his heir the son of whose birth he had learned shortly before. Suleiman the Lawgiver's entry into Buda in 1541 and the division of Hungary into three (Royal Hungary, the Eyalet of Buda, Transylvania) were the result of this death.
