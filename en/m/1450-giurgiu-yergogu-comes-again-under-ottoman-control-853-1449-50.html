Giurgiu (Yergöğü) comes again under Ottoman control (853/1449-50)

1450

The fortress of Giurgiu (Yergöğü), which the Wallachians had retaken in 1427, came again under Ottoman control in the year 853. Since the Hijri year extends from February 1449 to February 1450, the date was entered as the beginning of 1450, which falls within the range. The fortress thereafter became an Ottoman frontier post guarding the Danube crossing.
