Mahmud II acceded to the throne

28 July 1808

After the killing of Selim III, Mahmud, who remained the only male member of the dynasty, came to the throne with the support of Alemdar Mustafa Pasha. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Mahmud II, the new sultan spent the first months of his reign under the tutelage of Alemdar, who had put him on the throne, and in an effort to re-establish the balance between the ayans and the centre. This period was the beginning of the radical reform process of the following twenty years.
