The Taking of the Name Romania by the United Principalities

21 February 1862

Wallachia and Moldavia, which had effectively united through the double election of Cuza in 1859, gathered their assemblies and governments under a single roof in Bucharest in 1862 and officially took the name Romania. Although the Ottoman State legally retained the status of suzerain and the annual tribute, it effectively lost its control over the principality. The end of four centuries of Ottoman influence north of the Danube is symbolised by this date.
