Mehmed Ali declared his intention of independence

25 May 1838

The governor announced to the consuls of the great powers that he was considering proclaiming the independence of Egypt and Syria. Britain opposed this categorically: the emergence of a strong state in the Mediterranean close to France would threaten the road to India.
