Ashur: Old Assyrian State (city-state of Assur) → Kingdom of Upper Mesopotamia (Shamshi-Adad I)

1807 BC

At Ashur, ownership passes from the Old Assyrian State (city-state of Assur) to the Kingdom of Upper Mesopotamia (Shamshi-Adad I).
