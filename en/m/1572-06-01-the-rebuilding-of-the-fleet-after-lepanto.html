The Rebuilding of the Fleet after Lepanto

1 June 1572

The first task of Kılıç Ali Pasha, who was appointed to the grand admiralty after the disaster of Lepanto, was to rebuild the fleet that had suffered heavy losses; the shipyards worked without interruption throughout the winter of 1571-1572. In June 1572 a new fleet consisting of about 250 galleys and 300 volunteer reis galleys put to sea in the Mediterranean. This rapid recovery showed that Lepanto had not permanently broken Ottoman naval power.
