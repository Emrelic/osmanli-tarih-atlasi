Surrender of Buda after Mohács — two kings for the Hungarian throne

1 September 1526

The keys of the city were presented to the sultan on 11 September 1526 by the Ottoman army marching on Buda after the victory of Mohács; however, the Lawgiver did not hold Buda and withdrew his army. In Hungary, left without a king, the nobles elected two separate kings: János Szapolyai on 10 November 1526, and the Habsburg Ferdinand I on 17 December 1526. Ferdinand took Buda in August 1527. Szapolyai's settling in Buda as a king under Ottoman protection would come with the campaign of 1529 (see the 1529 entry), and direct Ottoman administration would be established in 1541.
