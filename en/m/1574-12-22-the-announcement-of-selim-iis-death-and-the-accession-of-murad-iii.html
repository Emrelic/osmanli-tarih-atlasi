The Announcement of Selim II's Death and the Accession of Murad III

22 December 1574

Selim II's death and the accession of Murad III, who was brought from Manisa, were officially announced on 22 December 1574. The new sultan would remain on the throne for twenty-one years, and his reign would be remembered for the long Iranian wars in the East, the eve of the Long War in the West, and the debasement of the akçe at home. Until the death of Sokollu Mehmed Pasha in 1579, the old order continued in the administration of the state.
