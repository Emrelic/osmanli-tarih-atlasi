The Karakhanids split into Eastern and Western branches: Ayn al-Dawla Muhammad and Bori Tegin Ibrahim founded an independent state in Transoxiana

31 August 1041

After their victories over the sons of Ali Tegin, Ayn al-Dawla Muhammad and his brother Bori Tegin Ibrahim became masters of the whole of Transoxiana and founded an independent state there (433/1041-42); Ayn al-Dawla took the title Arslan Khaqan, and Ibrahim the title Tamghach Bughra Qara Khaqan. The line of Yusuf Qadir Khan continued in the east (Balasagun-Kashgar) as the Eastern Karakhanids.
