The Execution of Prince Bayezid at Qazvin

23 July 1562

Prince Bayezid, who had taken refuge in Iran, was, after long bargaining, handed over by Shah Tahmasb to an Ottoman delegation and was strangled together with his four sons. In return for the handover the Safavids obtained significant material concessions and a guarantee of the frontier. With this execution the last obstacle to Selim II's accession to the throne was removed, and the peace order of the Treaty of Amasya was preserved.
