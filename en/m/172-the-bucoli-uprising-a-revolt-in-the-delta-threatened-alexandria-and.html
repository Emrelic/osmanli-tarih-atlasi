The Bucoli uprising: a revolt in the Delta threatened Alexandria, and Avidius Cassius suppressed it

172

The people of the Delta known as the Bucoli (herdsmen) rose under a priest named Isidorus; according to Dio, the rebels defeated a Roman unit and came close to seizing Alexandria. Cassius, sent from Syria, broke up the rebels' unity and suppressed the uprising.
