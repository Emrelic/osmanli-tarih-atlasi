Marseille, Massalia: Massalia (Marseille) → Roman Republic

48 BC

At the points Marseille and Massalia, ownership passes from Massalia (Marseille) to the Roman Republic. Transition source information: Caesar BC 2.22: after the city was defeated, 'conservans' and a garrison were left - the end of free allied status, Roman control.
