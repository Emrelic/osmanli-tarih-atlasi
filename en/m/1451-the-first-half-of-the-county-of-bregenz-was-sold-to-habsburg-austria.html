The first half of the County of Bregenz was sold to Habsburg Austria

1451

Elisabeth of Hochberg, heir of Montfort-Bregenz, sold half of the County of Bregenz to the Habsburgs in 1451; the remaining half was acquired in 1523. Bregenz thus became part of Habsburg Vorarlberg.
