Death of John Szapolyai — the Buda question and the gateway to the partition of Hungary

22 July 1540

Szapolyai, the Ottoman vassal king of eastern Hungary, died leaving as heir his son, born only shortly before. Süleyman the Lawgiver's entry into Buda in 1541 and the division of Hungary into three (Royal Hungary, the Eyalet of Buda, Transylvania) are the consequence of this death.
