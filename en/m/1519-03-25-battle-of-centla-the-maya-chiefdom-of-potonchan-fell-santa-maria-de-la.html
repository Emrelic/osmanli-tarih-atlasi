Battle of Centla — the Maya chiefdom of Potonchán fell, Santa María de la Victoria was founded

25 March 1519

Cortés's fleet, which had set out from Cuba, put in at Potonchán, a Chontal Maya town at the mouth of the Grijalva River. The Spaniards, who won the battle on the plain of Centla through their superiority in cavalry and firearms, founded the town of Santa María de la Victoria in place of the town. This was the first permanent Spanish settlement on the Mexican mainland; Malintzin (doña Marina), who would act as Cortés's interpreter, was among the gifts presented here.
