Samos entered the possession of the Maona of Chios

15 June 1346

With the taking of Chios by the Genoese Simone Vignoso and the leaving of the administration to the Maona company, Samos also entered the possession of the Genoese of Chios (the Maona) (TDV: 1346-1475).
