The surrender of the fortress of Izmail to General Zass

26 September 1809

During the Ottoman-Russian war of 1806-1812 the fortress of Izmail on the Danube surrendered to General Zass. The fortress was ceded to Russia, together with Bessarabia, by the Treaty of Bucharest of 1812.
