Sargon II defeated Hamath; he killed Yau-bidi at Qarqar and made the kingdom a province

719 BC

Yau-bidi, king of Hamath, was at the head of the Syro-Palestinian coalition formed against Sargon; Sargon defeated him at Qarqar and destroyed the city, and Hamath was turned into an Assyrian province.
