The withdrawal of Italian troops from Marmaris and Kuşadası

29 April 1922

The Italians, who had been on the southwestern Anatolian coasts since May 1919, evacuated their occupation zone step by step after the rapprochement with the Ankara Government. Antalya and Bodrum had been evacuated in the summer of 1921; the last Italian troops left Kuşadası and Marmaris as well at the end of April 1922.
