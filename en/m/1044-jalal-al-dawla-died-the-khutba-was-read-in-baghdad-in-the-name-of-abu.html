Jalal al-Dawla died; the khutba was read in Baghdad in the name of Abu Kalijar, and the Buyid branches were united

1044

With Jalal al-Dawla's death in 1044, the khutba in Baghdad was read in the name of Abu Kalijar, who came from Kirman; thus Abu Kalijar gathered the Buyid branches of Iraq, Fars and Kirman under a single administration.
