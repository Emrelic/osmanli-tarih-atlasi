Ilipa and Gades: Hispania passed from Carthage to Rome

205 BC

In 206 at Ilipa Scipio defeated the army of Hasdrubal Gisgo and Mago and ended Carthaginian control in Hispania; Gades also passed to Rome shortly afterwards. Carthage lost the silver mines of Hispania and its reservoir of soldiers.
