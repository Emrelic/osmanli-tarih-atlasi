Alexander consulted the Oracle of Ammon at the Siwa Oasis

330 BC

Alexander crossed from the coast into the desert and went to the Siwa Oasis, where the Temple of Ammon stands, and consulted the oracle; Arrian writes that he received the answer that would please him. This visit fed Alexander's legitimacy in Egypt and the later accounts of his being regarded as the son of the god.
