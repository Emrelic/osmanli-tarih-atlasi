Expulsion of the Hyksos from Avaris (Ahmose's conquest of Avaris): Avaris passes to Ahmose (end of the Hyksos)

1531 BC

Ahmose's capture of Avaris marks the end of the Hyksos 15th Dynasty; at Tell el-Dab'a this is seen in the 'large-scale abandonment of the city' (Bietak 2023: no destruction/fires, probable surrender).
