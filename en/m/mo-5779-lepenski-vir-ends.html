Lepenski Vir: ends

5779 BC

A Mesolithic hunter-gatherer settlement in the Iron Gates gorge of the Danube; the latest phase is Early Neolithic (Starčevo pottery, the first domestic animals). The beginning is not a 'single continuous' settlement: the Proto-LV phase may be discontinuous.
