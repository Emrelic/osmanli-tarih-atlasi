The end of the Italian occupation of Burdur

1921

Burdur, which was occupied by the Italians on 28 June 1919, experienced, according to the TDV article on Burdur, a short-lived Italian occupation between the years 1919 and 1921. The source does not give the day of the evacuation; on the map the occupation ends at the beginning of the year 1921. Italy withdrew completely from its occupation zone in Anatolia in the summer of 1921.
