George Maniakes landed at Taranto as catepan of Italy

1042

George Maniakes, whom Michael V had released from prison and made 'magistros and catepan', arrived at Taranto at the end of April 1042, during the joint rule of Zoe and Theodora; his campaigns against the Norman and Apulian rebels were left unfinished when he was dismissed in September 1042.
