The Norman duke William is crowned king of England at Westminster

25 December 1066

William, who landed at Pevensey on 28 September 1066, crushed Harold at the Battle of Senlac (Hastings) on 14 October; he was crowned at Westminster on Christmas Day. His subjugation of the south, west and north of England took five more years.
