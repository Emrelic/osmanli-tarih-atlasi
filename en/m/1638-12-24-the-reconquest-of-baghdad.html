The Reconquest of Baghdad

24 December 1638

Murad IV, who left Istanbul on 8 May 1638, retook Baghdad, which had been in Safavid hands for fifteen years, after a fierce siege lasting forty days; the Vizier Tayyar Mehmed Pasha was martyred in the siege. With the conquest of the city Ottoman administration was re-established in Iraq, and the sultan took the title 'Conqueror of Baghdad'. The conquest was made permanent by the Treaty of Qasr-i Shirin the following year and laid the foundation of the present Turkey-Iran frontier.
