Duke Conrad of Masovia calls the Teutonic Order to the Prussian frontier; he donates the Chełmno land

1228

Conrad, who could not cope with the Prussians' raids, called the Teutonic Order to his aid and gave it Chełmno and some frontier towns together with the lands it would conquer (1228); the whole of the Chełmno land was left to the order in 1230. In 1229 the conquest of Prussia began (a castle at Thorn).
