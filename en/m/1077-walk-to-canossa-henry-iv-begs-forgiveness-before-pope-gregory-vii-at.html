Walk to Canossa: Henry IV begs forgiveness before Pope Gregory VII at Matilda's castle

1077

The culmination of the Investiture Controversy between Pope Gregory VII and Emperor Henry IV took place in 1077 at Canossa Castle, which belonged to Matilda; the emperor was humiliated before the pope.
