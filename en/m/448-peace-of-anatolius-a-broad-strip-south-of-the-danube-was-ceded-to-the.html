Peace of Anatolius: a broad strip south of the Danube was ceded to the Huns

448

After the Eastern Roman defeat at the Utus, Anatolius made peace with Attila in 447: Rome ceded to the Huns a strip five days' journey wide south of the Danube, extending from Singidunum to Novae, and the annual tribute was raised to 2,100 pounds of gold.
