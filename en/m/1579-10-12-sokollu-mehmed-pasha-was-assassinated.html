Sokollu Mehmed Pasha was assassinated

12 October 1579

Sokollu Mehmed Pasha, who had served fourteen years as grand vizier under three sultans, was stabbed to death on 20 Sha'ban 987 (12 October 1579) by a dervish who approached as if to hand a petition to the afternoon divan in his mansion. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, it is debated whether the assassination may have been connected with vengeance for the executed Hamzawi sheikh Hamza Bali.
