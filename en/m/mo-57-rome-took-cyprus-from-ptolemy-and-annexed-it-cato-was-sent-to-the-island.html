Rome took Cyprus from Ptolemy and annexed it; Cato was sent to the island

57 BC

The king of Cyprus, a brother of Ptolemy XII, did not resist Rome's taking of the island on Clodius's proposal; Cato was sent to take over the island. The Ptolemaic state lost Cyprus and lost its last naval base on the coast. Plutarch mentions Cato's 'being sent to Cyprus' in the life of Pompey.
