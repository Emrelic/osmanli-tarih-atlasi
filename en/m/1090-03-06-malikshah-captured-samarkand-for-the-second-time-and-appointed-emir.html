Malikshah captured Samarkand for the second time and appointed Emir Atsiz as governor

6 March 1090

Upon the revolt of Ayn al-Dawla, the commander of the Chigils, and Yaqub Tegin's crossing to Samarkand, Malikshah captured Samarkand for a second time at the end of the year 483 (1090) and appointed Emir Atsiz governor there; he then placed Yaqub Tegin at the head of the Eastern Qarakhanids and charged him with fighting Tughril b. Yinal.
