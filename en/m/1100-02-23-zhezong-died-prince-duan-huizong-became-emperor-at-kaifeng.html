Zhezong died; Prince Duan (Huizong) became emperor at Kaifeng

23 February 1100

Zhezong died without a son; the Empress Dowager acted as regent from behind the curtain and, in answer to Zhang Dun's view that 'Prince Jian, a full brother, should be chosen', said that Prince Shen's eyes were diseased and asked that Prince Duan be chosen; Prince Duan was summoned to the palace and proclaimed emperor, and a general amnesty was issued the next day.
