Philetaerus went over to Seleucus after Corupedium; Pergamum effectively founded an independent dynasty

280 BC

After the fall of Lysimachus, Philetaerus attached himself, together with the treasury, to Seleucus and protected and enriched Pergamum; with Seleucus's death (281) he became effectively independent. Livy gives the Attalid dynasty as ruling Pergamum from 283 to 129.
