Evacuation of Otranto

10 September 1481

The garrison of Otranto, with no hope of fresh reinforcement upon the death of Mehmed the Conqueror (3 May 1481), evacuated the fortress by agreement in the face of the siege of Alfonso, heir of Naples; the only Ottoman bridgehead in Italy was thus lost after one year.
