Sharaf al-Dawla died; Baha al-Dawla took the Buyid throne in Baghdad

989

With the death of Sharaf al-Dawla in 989, Baha al-Dawla, still young, took the throne in Baghdad; Fakhr al-Dawla, summoned by the vizier al-Sahib ibn Abbad, established his rule in Isfahan, Hamadan and Rayy and emerged as a rival.
