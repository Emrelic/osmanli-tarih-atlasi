The Northern and Western Defence Belt of Buda: Vác, Hatvan and Simontornya

1 September 1544

After Esztergom and Székesfehérvár were taken, the beylerbeyi of Buda, without a campaign of the sultan, set out to complete the ring of fortresses surrounding the capital. Vác at the Danube bend, Hatvan, the gate of the Alföld, and Simontornya on the Sió line were taken over one by one. The gains of this quiet year turned Buda from a frontier city into a provincial centre that would endure for a hundred and forty years.
