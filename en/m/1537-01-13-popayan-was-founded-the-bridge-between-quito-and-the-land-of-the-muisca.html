Popayán was founded — the bridge between Quito and the land of the Muisca

13 January 1537

Benalcázar, marching north from Quito, founded Popayán in the Cauca valley. The city became the northern extremity of the Spanish advance from Peru and opened the road that led, the following year, to his meeting with Quesada in the land of the Muisca.
