First Battle of Kosovo — martyrdom of Murad I

15 June 1389

The Balkan alliance under Prince Lazar was decisively defeated on the Plain of Kosovo; Serbia came under Ottoman suzerainty. Murad I, who was touring the battlefield, was stabbed with a dagger by the Serbian nobleman Miloš Obilić and martyred — he is the only Ottoman sultan to die on the battlefield. Bayezid the Thunderbolt (Yıldırım) was enthroned in the camp in his place; his brother Yakub Çelebi was strangled, and this was one of the first known examples of the practice of fratricide.
