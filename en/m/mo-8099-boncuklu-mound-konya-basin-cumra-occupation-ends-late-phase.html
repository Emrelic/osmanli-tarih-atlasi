Boncuklu Mound (Konya basin, Çumra): occupation ends (late phase)

8099 BC

At Boncuklu, cultivation was adopted by local hunter-gatherers in the second half of the 9th millennium; the community is interpreted as the ancestor of Çatalhöyük East (Çatalhöyük East from 7100 cal BC).
