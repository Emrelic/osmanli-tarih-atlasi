Building of the Piyale Pasha Complex

1573

Piyale Pasha, Kaptan-ı Deryâ and one of Selim II's sons-in-law, purchased the site of the complex in 1573 and had Mimar Sinan build it. The complex was designed as a broad compound consisting of a mosque, madrasa, tekke, mausoleum, cemetery, primary school, fountain, market and bath.
