Valdemar II mounts a crusade to Estonia and founds Tallinn (Lyndanisse)

1219

In 1219 Valdemar II occupied northern Estonia as part of the crusade directed at the eastern Baltic and had a castle built at Tallinn; the city became an episcopal seat. According to the traditional account, the Dannebrog fell from the sky in this battle (15 June 1219, Lyndanisse).
