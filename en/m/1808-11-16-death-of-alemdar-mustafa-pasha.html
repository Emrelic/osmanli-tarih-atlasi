Death of Alemdar Mustafa Pasha

16 November 1808

In the Janissary uprising that began on the night of 15 November 1808, the reformist grand vizier, besieged at the Sublime Porte, when the help he expected did not come, set the arsenal alight the next day and died together with hundreds of attackers; the order of the Deed of Alliance and the first attempt at reform were buried with him.
