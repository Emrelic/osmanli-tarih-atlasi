Ptolemy added Cyrene (Cyrenaica) to Egypt

321 BC

Intervening in the strife at Cyrene, Ptolemy attached the Libyan region of Cyrenaica to Egypt under Ophellas. Thus Ptolemaic Egypt also expanded to the west; Cyrenaica remained in the hands of a branch of the Ptolemaic dynasty in the following centuries.
