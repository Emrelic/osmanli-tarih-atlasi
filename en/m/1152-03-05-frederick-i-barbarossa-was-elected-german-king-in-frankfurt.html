Frederick I Barbarossa was elected German king in Frankfurt

5 March 1152

The princes raised Frederick to the kingship on 5 March 1152 in Frankfurt; he was crowned in Aachen on 9 March. The Barbarossa period of the Hohenstaufen dynasty thus began.
