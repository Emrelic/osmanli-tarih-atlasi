Askia Muhammad took Zagha (Dia)

1494

Askia Muhammad, who had ascended the Songhay throne, captured the city of Zagha (Dia) in the west of the inland Niger delta at the end of the year 899 AH, through his brother Kurmina-Fari Amar Komzagho.
