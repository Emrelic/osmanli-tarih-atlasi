Toghril Beg brought the caliph back to Baghdad; Basasiri, who had the Fatimid khutba read, was captured and killed

8 January 1060

Toghril Beg moved against Arslan al-Basasiri, who had rebelled against the caliph, occupied Baghdad with his ally Quraysh ibn Badran, and had the khutba read in the name of the Fatimid caliph, and ensured that the caliph was brought back to Baghdad; Basasiri was captured and killed in Dhu'l-Hijja 451 (January 1060).
