The Danishmendids take control of Çankırı, Kastamonu and the Black Sea coasts

1129

The Danishmendids, the most powerful state in Anatolia in the time of Emir Ghazi, took control in 1129 of Ankara, Çankırı, Kastamonu and the Black Sea coasts; the greater part of the lands extending from Malatya to the Sakarya came under Danishmendid rule.
