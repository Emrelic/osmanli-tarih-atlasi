Queen Rusudan agrees to pay tribute to the Mongols and formally recognises the Great Khan as suzerain

1243

Realising that she could not cope with the Mongols, Queen Rusudan agreed to become subject to them by paying tribute and in 1243 formally recognised the Great Khan as suzerain. After Rusudan's death the Mongols divided the Caucasus into eight provinces.
