Treaty of Paris (Meaux-Paris): Raymond VII cedes most of his fiefs to the French crown

1229

By the Treaty of Paris of 1229 the king of France deprived the house of Toulouse of most of its fiefs; the county was to pass to the French crown in 1271 through Raymond's daughter Jeanne and her husband Alphonse (brother of Louis IX).
