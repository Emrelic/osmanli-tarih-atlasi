Bayburt passed to the Anatolian Seljuk State when Saltukid rule ended

25 June 1202

When the Seljuks put an end to the Saltukid State in 1202 they took Bayburt as well; the city flourished in the time of Suleyman Shah's brother, the Malik of Erzurum Mughith al-Din Tughril Shah, and his son Jahan Shah (1202–1230).
