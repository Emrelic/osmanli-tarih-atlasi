The Convention of Balta Liman: A Joint Ottoman-Russian Protectorate in Wallachia and Moldavia

1 May 1849

When the revolutions of 1848 spread to Wallachia and Moldavia, Ottoman and Russian forces had jointly occupied the principalities. By the convention signed at Balta Liman it was decided that the voivodes would be appointed by the sultan for seven years rather than elected, and that the two states would establish joint control in the region. While preserving Ottoman sovereignty on paper, the agreement granted Russia a de facto right of partnership and hastened the severing of the Danubian principalities from the Ottomans.
