The transfer of the county of Montfort-Feldkirch to the Habsburgs was completed: Feldkirch in the hands of Duke Leopold III (sale May 1375)

1379

Count Rudolf von Montfort-Feldkirch sold the County of Feldkirch and his possessions to Duke Leopold III in May 1375; after payment the transfer was made in 1379, the count retaining the right of usufruct for life (Vogt); the Montfort-Feldkirch line died out in 1390.
