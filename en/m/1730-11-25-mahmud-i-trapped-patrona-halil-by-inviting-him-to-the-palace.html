Mahmud I trapped Patrona Halil by inviting him to the palace

25 November 1730

Seven weeks after his accession, Mahmud I broke Patrona Halil's influence by winning over the military notables through men he trusted — Hacı Beşir Agha in the palace, and Kaplan Giray I, Khan of the Crimea, outside it. On 25 November 1730, Patrona Halil, summoned to a meeting in the Circumcision Chamber in the Topkapı Palace, was admitted along with Muslu Beşe and Kel Mehmed Agha; at a sign from Grand Vizier Silâhdar Mehmed Pasha, thirty-two armed men who had been concealed beforehand emerged and killed all three. Mahmud I thus took complete control of the rebellion.
