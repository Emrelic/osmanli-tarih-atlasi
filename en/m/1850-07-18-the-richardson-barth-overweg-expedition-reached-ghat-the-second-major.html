The Richardson–Barth–Overweg expedition reached Ghat, the second major stop on the Saharan route after Murzuk

18 July 1850

In his diary entry of 18 July 1850, written while crossing the Sahara with the British government expedition led by Richardson, Heinrich Barth describes approaching and arriving at Ghat, which he counts as the expedition's second major stop; some of the inhabitants, who knew Richardson from an earlier visit to the place, came out to greet them. This was not the first European visit to the region.
