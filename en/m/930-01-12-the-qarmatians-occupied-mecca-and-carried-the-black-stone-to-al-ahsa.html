The Qarmatians occupied Mecca and carried the Black Stone to al-Ahsa (Lahsa); the Qarmatian State of Bahrain as the owner of Lahsa, at the first dated attestation

12 January 930

The Isma'ili Qarmatians (the Qarmatian State of Bahrain) occupied Mecca in 317 AH / 929 and carried the Black Stone to al-Ahsa (Lahsa); but it was restored to its place twenty years later. In the atlas, Lahsa, which was a region of the Abbasid Caliphate, is recorded as Qarmatian from this first dated attestation.
