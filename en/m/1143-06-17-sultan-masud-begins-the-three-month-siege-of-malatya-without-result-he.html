Sultan Mas'ud begins the three-month siege of Malatya; without result, he lifts it on 14 September 1143

17 June 1143

Sultan Mas'ud, who had taken Sivas from the dynasty that had divided into three on the death of the Danishmendid Malik Muhammad (537/1142), besieged Malatya on 1 Dhu al-Hijja 537 / 17 June 1143; after three months without result, on 2 Rabi' al-awwal 538 / 14 September 1143 he lifted the siege and returned to Konya.
