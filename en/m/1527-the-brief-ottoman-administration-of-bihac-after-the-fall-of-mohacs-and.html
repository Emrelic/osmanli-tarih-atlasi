The brief Ottoman administration of Bihać — after the fall of Mohács and Jajce

1527

After the victory at Mohács the Ottomans took Jajce, the centre of the Hungarian buffer zone in Bosnia, and Banja Luka. According to the TDV article on Bihać, after the taking of the fortress of Jajce in the year following Mohács, Bihać passed again under Ottoman administration for a short time. The source does not say when and how the fortress was lost; the TDV states only that in the 1530s, when organizing the Croatian frontier region, the Habsburgs made Bihać, then in Christian hands, their main centre. The permanent conquest of Bihać would take place in 1592 through the campaign of Hasan Pasha, Beylerbeyi of Bosnia.
