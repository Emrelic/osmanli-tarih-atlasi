Tasvir-i Efkar was published

27 June 1862

Tasvir-i Efkâr, issued by Şinasi, began publication as the fourth Turkish newspaper after Takvîm-i Vekāyi', Cerîde-i Havâdis and Tercüman-ı Ahvâl. According to the TDV article on 'Tasvîr-i Efkâr', the newspaper became the mouthpiece of the Young Ottomans movement in the period of Namık Kemal, who later took over its editorship.
