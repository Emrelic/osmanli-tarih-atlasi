The Conquest of Kamaniçe and the Establishment of the Province of Podolia

27 August 1672

After a siege of nine days Kamaniçe, regarded as Poland's strongest fortress in the east, was taken; on 2 September Mehmed IV performed the first Friday prayer in the mosque converted from the cathedral. With the fortress made the centre, the Province of Podolia was established, and the Ottoman flag reached its northernmost point on the territory of present-day Ukraine. Kamaniçe would remain the northwestern extremity of the empire until the 1699 Treaty of Karlowitz.
