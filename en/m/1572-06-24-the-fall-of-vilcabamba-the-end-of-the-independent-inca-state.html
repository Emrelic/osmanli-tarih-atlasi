The fall of Vilcabamba — the end of the independent Inca state

24 June 1572

After the fall of Cuzco in 1533, Manco Inca and his successors maintained an independent Inca state in the Vilcabamba mountains for forty years. The expedition organized by the viceroy Francisco de Toledo captured the abandoned capital; the last ruler, Túpac Amaru, who had fled, was seized and brought to Cuzco and was executed there. The political existence of the four-century Inca dynasty in the Andean world thus came to an end.
