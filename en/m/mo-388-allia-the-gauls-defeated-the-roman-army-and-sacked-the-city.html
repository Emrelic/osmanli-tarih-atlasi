Allia: the Gauls defeated the Roman army and sacked the city

388 BC

On the bank of the Allia, a tributary of the Tiber, the Senonian Gauls routed the Roman army; Rome was occupied and plundered except for the Capitol. Livy writes that the "day of the Allia" (18 July) was regarded by the Romans as ill-omened.
