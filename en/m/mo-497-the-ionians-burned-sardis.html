The Ionians burned Sardis

497 BC

Ionian forces together with Athenian and Eretrian contingents marched on Sardis, the seat of the Persian satrapy, and burned the city. Darius thereafter resolved to punish Athens; the event is the pretext for the Marathon expedition.
