Uprising in Seville, Valladolid and Burgos: the cities fell to the Nationalists

18 July 1936

The military uprising that began the Spanish Civil War broke out on 18 July 1936 in the Canary Islands, Seville, Valladolid and Burgos. The Gran Enciclopèdia Catalana mentions Seville as 'the base of the rebellion in Andalucía'. These cities passed through the uprising to Nationalist rule (against the Spanish Republic).
