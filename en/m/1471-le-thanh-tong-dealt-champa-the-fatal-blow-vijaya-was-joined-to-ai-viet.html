Lê Thánh Tông dealt Champa the fatal blow; Vijaya was joined to Đại Việt

1471

According to Hall, in 1446 the Annamese, exploiting a civil war, had briefly occupied Vijaya and the Chams had retaken it; Lê Thánh Tông (1460-97), the greatest of the Lê dynasty, dealt the death blow to the Cham kingdom in 1471 and made Champa a link in his sphere of sovereignty.
