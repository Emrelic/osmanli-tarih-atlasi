Truce of Yam-Zapolsky — Livonia and Dorpat passed to Poland

15 January 1582

The Truce of Yam-Zapolsky was signed between Poland-Lithuania and Russia; Moscow renounced its claims to Livonia and returned the Polotsk lands. Dorpat (Tartu) remained in Polish hands from 1582.
