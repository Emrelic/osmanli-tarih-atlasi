Relocation of the Imperial Arsenal (Tersane-i Amire) to Istanbul (Kasımpaşa/Golden Horn)

1515

The new arsenal, whose construction was begun in the time of Selim the Grim in the bay west of the Galata walls, was completed in 1515 with 160 bays; thus the arsenal activities centered at Gallipoli were moved to Istanbul. The institution later formally took the name "Tersane-i Amire" in the time of Barbaros Hayreddin Pasha.
