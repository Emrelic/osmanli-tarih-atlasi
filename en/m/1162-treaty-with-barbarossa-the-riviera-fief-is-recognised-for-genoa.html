Treaty with Barbarossa: the Riviera fief is recognised for Genoa

1162

The treaty made in 1162 with Emperor Frederick Barbarossa secured for Genoa the infeudation of the Riviera; new walls were built the same year. Until this date Genoese policy had rested on an alliance with Norman Sicily.
