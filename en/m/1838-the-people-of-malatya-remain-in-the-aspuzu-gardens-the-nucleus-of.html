The people of Malatya remain in the Aspuzu gardens — the nucleus of modern Malatya

1838

In the summer of 1838 the Ottoman general headquarters against İbrahim Pasha's Egyptian army moved from Mezraa to Malatya. The people of Malatya, who lived in their summer houses at Aspuzu, did not return after the army's departure to their winter houses in the old city, which had been left in ruins; Aspuzu became the nucleus of modern Malatya. Old Malatya (Battalgazi) continued to live on, reduced in size.
