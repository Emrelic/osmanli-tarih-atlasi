Rome settled the Visigoths in Aquitania Secunda: the Visigothic kingdom centered on Toulouse

418

After using Wallia's Visigoths against the Vandals and Alans in Hispania, Rome under Constantius settled them in 418 as foederati in Aquitania Secunda in the Garonne valley. The Visigothic kingdom centered on Toulouse is regarded as the first Germanic kingdom on Roman soil.
