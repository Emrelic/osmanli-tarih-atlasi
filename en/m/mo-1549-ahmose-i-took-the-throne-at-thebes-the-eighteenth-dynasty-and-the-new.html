Ahmose I took the throne at Thebes; the Eighteenth Dynasty and the New Kingdom began

1549 BC

Ahmose, brother of Kamose, the last king of the Seventeenth Dynasty at Thebes, took the throne as a child; during his reign he was to drive the Hyksos from Egypt and re-establish rule over Nubia and Canaan, beginning the Eighteenth Dynasty and the New Kingdom. His accession is not the end of the Hyksos but the beginning of the last phase of the struggle.
