Michael VIII was crowned in Hagia Sophia

15 August 1261

Michael VIII entered the city through the Golden Gate before the icon of the Hodegetria, marched in procession, and was crowned in Hagia Sophia on 15 August 1261. The Palaiologan dynasty remained in power until the end of the empire.
