Belgrade retaken

8 October 1690

The grand vizier Köprülüzade Fazıl Mustafa Pasha retook Belgrade from Austria on 8 October 1690; the city lost in 1688 thus returned to the Ottomans (TDV II. Süleyman). Another settlement returning from the Habsburgs to the Ottomans on the same day: Hisarcık (Grocka) — the date of Belgrade was taken as the basis (TDV II. Süleyman; approximate information).
