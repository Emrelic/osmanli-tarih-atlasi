The Baba Ishaq rebellion was bloodily suppressed on the Malya plain of Kırşehir

1240

The Turkmens of the Malatya region rose in revolt in 1240 at the signal of Baba Ishaq of Khurasan and defeated the state forces, and the sultan fled to the Kubadabad palace; the rebellion was finally suppressed bloodily on the Malya plain of Kırşehir and showed that the moral strength of the Seljuk army was weak.
