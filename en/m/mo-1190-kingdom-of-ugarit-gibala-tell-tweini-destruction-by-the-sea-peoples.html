Kingdom of Ugarit (Gibala/Tell Tweini) - destruction by the Sea Peoples: Ugarit/Tell Tweini is destroyed (age of the Sea Peoples)

1190 BC

Late Bronze Age destructions in the northern Levant (Gibala, Ugarit, Ras Ibn Hani, Tell Kazel); 1192-1190 cal BC within the framework of the 14C weighted mean (2962±14 BP) + the solar eclipse of KTU 1.78 (21 January 1192).
