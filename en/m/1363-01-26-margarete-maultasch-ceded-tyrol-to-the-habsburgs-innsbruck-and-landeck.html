Margarete Maultasch ceded Tyrol to the Habsburgs: Innsbruck and Landeck became Habsburg territory

26 January 1363

On 26 January 1363, at Bozen, Margarete 'Maultasch', countess of Tyrol, ceded her rights over the land to the Habsburg duke Rudolf IV and his brothers; the County of Tyrol came to an end, and Innsbruck and Landeck were attached to the Habsburg duchies.
