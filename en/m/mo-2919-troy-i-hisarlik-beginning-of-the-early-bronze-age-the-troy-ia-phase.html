Troy I (Hisarlik, beginning of the Early Bronze Age): the Troy Ia phase begins (Weninger 1995)

2919 BC

A date derived from dendro-calibrated 14C for the beginning of the Troy Ia phase (apsidal stone foundation); Troy I comprises 9 architectural phases in total (Ia-Ii), assuming an average phase length of 50 years.
