At Carchemish Kuzi-Teshub continued the Hittite legacy with the title "Great King"

1174 BC

After the destruction of Hattusa, the kingdom of Carchemish survived in the hands of a branch of the Hittite dynasty; Kuzi-Teshub proclaimed himself "Great King". Luwian-Aramaean city-states such as Carchemish, Malatya (Melid) and Hama formed the "Neo-Hittite" world.
