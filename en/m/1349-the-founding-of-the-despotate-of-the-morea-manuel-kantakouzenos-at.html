The founding of the Despotate of the Morea — Manuel Kantakouzenos at Mistra

1349

The Byzantine emperor John Kantakouzenos gave his son Manuel a separate administrative region in the Morea; thus the Despotate of the Morea, centred on Mistra, came into being. Manuel ruled until 1380; during this period Byzantine culture and art flourished especially at Mistra. The despotate lasted until Mehmed II's conquest in 1460.
