Shang dynasty/period boundaries (Erligang-Yinxu framework): the Shang period begins (14C model)

1499 BC

Two values for the beginning of the Shang: ~1500 (Sci Rep 2018, Erligang Zhengzhou) and ~1600 (Periodization Project; earlier wave, Guangming Daily/Lee 2007). End: Zhou conquest 1046 (Li 2002) versus 14C ~1041.
