Chubuk Beg conquers Harput and founds the Chubukid Principality, embracing the Palu and Çemişgezek areas

1085

During the Turkish raids directed at Anatolia after 1071, Chubuk Beg conquered Harput and founded the principality, which included the Palu and Çemişgezek areas. The principality's life was short; in the 1110s the Artuqid Belek took the city.
