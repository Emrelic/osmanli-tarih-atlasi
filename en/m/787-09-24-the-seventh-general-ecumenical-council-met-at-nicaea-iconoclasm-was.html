The Seventh General (Ecumenical) Council met at Nicaea: iconoclasm was condemned

24 September 787

At the call of Empress Irene, about 350 bishops assembled in the Church of Hagia Sophia (Holy Wisdom) at Nicaea and condemned iconoclasm. This is the last council that the Orthodox Church counts as ecumenical.
