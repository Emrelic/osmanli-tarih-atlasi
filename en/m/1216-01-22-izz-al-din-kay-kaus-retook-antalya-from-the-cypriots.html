Izz al-Din Kay Ka'us retook Antalya from the Cypriots

22 January 1216

Kay Ka'us I Izz al-Din reconquered Antalya, which had been lost in 1212 as a result of a revolt carried out with the support of the king of Cyprus, and again gave its governorship to Mubariz al-Din Ertokush.
