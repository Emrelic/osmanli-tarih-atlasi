The rout at Galați

1 May 1789

The Ottoman forces were defeated at Galați on 1 May 1789. This rout was the first link in the series of defeats that would be repeated through the summer at Focșani and Rymnik, and it shook the position of the grand vizier-commander Koca Yûsuf Pasha.
