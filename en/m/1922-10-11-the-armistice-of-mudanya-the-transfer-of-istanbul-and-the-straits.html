The Armistice of Mudanya: The Transfer of Istanbul and the Straits without War

11 October 1922

After the Great Offensive, when the Turkish army came face to face with British forces at Çanakkale and İzmit, the conference convened at Mudanya ended, after eleven days of hard bargaining, in an armistice. It was accepted that Eastern Thrace as far as the Maritsa would be evacuated by Greece within fifteen days and transferred to Turkish administration, and that Istanbul and the Straits would also be handed over with the peace treaty. Thus the armed phase of the National Struggle closed with a diplomatic victory won without a single shot being fired, and the door to Lausanne was opened.
