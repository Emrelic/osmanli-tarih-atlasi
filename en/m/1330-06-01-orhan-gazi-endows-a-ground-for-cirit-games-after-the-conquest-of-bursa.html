Orhan Gazi endows a ground for cirit games after the conquest of Bursa

1 June 1330

According to the TDV's Cirit entry, Orhan Gazi, after the conquest of Bursa, endowed a field in the vicinity for horse races and cirit games — it is one of the first recorded examples of the institutionalization of the cirit game among the Ottomans.
