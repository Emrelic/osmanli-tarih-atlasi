Anitta, king of Kussara, made Kanesh his capital and destroyed Hattusa

1749 BC

According to the Anitta Text, Anitta, king of Kussara, united many city-states in Anatolia, made Neša (Kanesh) his capital, and destroyed Hattusa and pronounced a curse on it. It is remembered as the first Hittite attempt at political unification.
