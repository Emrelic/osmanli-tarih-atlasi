The appointment of Melek Mehmed Pasha to the grand vizierate — the formation of the reform cadre

4 May 1792

Melek Mehmed Pasha, who had been removed from the vizierate in 1788 for being pro-peace, was brought to the grand vizierate on 4 May 1792. With his arrival in the capital on 7 June, the collection of memoranda concerning the order of the state (nizâm-ı devlet) from the dignitaries of the state began; the preparation of the Nizâm-ı Cedîd program proceeded with this cadre.
