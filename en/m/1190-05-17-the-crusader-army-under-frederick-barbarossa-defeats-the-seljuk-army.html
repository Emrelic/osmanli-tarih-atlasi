The crusader army under Frederick Barbarossa defeats the Seljuk army and enters Konya

17 May 1190

Barbarossa, the German emperor, who crossed into Anatolia from Gallipoli in March 1190, entered Seljuk territory near Uluborlu and on 17 May 1190 defeated the army of Qutb al-Din Malik-Shah and the other Seljuk maliks, and entered Konya, the capital, which the sultan had evacuated.
