Accession of Indraditya in the Tai principality of Sukhothai (c. 1220): break from Khmer rule and beginning of the Tai kingdom

1220

Coedès notes that there is no firm date for Sukhothai's independence or for Indraditya's kingship; reckoning backward from the reign of Rama Khamhaeng (Indraditya's third son and second successor) in the last two decades of the 13th century, Indraditya's coronation falls at about 1220.
