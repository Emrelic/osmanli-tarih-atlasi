The city of Pagan (Arimaddanapura) was founded: at the confluence of the Irrawaddy and Chindwin, a new center born of the Pyu collapse

849

According to Coedès, the population loss of Prome benefited Pagan; Pagan (Arimaddanapura), formed by the merging of several villages, was founded in 849 at the junction of the Irrawaddy and the Chindwin, where the routes from Assam, Yunnan and the Shan country intersect.
