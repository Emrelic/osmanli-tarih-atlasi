The anti-Habsburg Kuruc uprising began under Ferenc II Rákóczi

1703

Rákóczi's Brezán proclamation (cited as 6 May 1703) and Tamás Esze's meeting with the Kuruc troops in June 1703 began the uprising; the war ended with the Peace of Szatmár in 1711. Only the year is known: the day of the proclamation/beginning differs in secondary sources as 6 May, 21 May, 15 June; no academic source for the day was found.
