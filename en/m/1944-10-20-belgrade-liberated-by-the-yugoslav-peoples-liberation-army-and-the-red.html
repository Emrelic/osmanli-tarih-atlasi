Belgrade liberated by the Yugoslav People's Liberation Army and the Red Army

20 October 1944

Units of the Yugoslav People's Liberation Army, with the support of the Red Army, liberated Belgrade from German occupation on 20 October 1944. The city passed to Partisan rule; Nedić's Serbia had effectively dissolved two weeks earlier.
