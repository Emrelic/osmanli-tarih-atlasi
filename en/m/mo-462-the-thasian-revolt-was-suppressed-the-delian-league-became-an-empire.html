The Thasian revolt was suppressed; the Delian League became an empire whose members could not leave

462 BC

Thasos clashed with Athens over mines and tried to leave the league; after a two-year siege (463 BCE) it had its walls demolished, surrendered its fleet, and lost the mining region on the Thracian coast. The episode is the first precedent showing that membership in the League could not be abandoned.
