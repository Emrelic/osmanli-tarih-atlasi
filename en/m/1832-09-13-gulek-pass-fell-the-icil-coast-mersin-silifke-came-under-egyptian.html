Gülek Pass fell — the İçil coast (Mersin, Silifke) came under Egyptian control

13 September 1832

The Gülek Pass, the Çukurova gate of the Taurus, fell into the hands of the Egyptian army. Ibrahim Pasha appointed his own mütesellim to the Sancak of İçil, and all the landing places on the coast came under Egyptian control. On the map Mersin and Silifke are shown as attached to Egypt from this day (the day of the appointment is not in the source; this date is a lower bound); since in the Kütahya decision only the muhassıllık of Adana was transferred, this control ends with the evacuation of Anatolia.
