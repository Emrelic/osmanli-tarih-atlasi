Burebista united the Dacian and Getic tribes

81 BC

According to Strabo, Burebista gathered the Getae and Dacians into a single kingdom and campaigned south of the Danube as far as the frontiers of Thrace and Illyria. The kingdom began to pose a threat to Rome along the Danube.
