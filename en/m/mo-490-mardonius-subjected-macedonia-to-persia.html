Mardonius subjected Macedonia to Persia

490 BC

Mardonius, son-in-law of Darius, crossed Thrace and brought Macedonia under Persian rule; the Macedonian king gave earth and water to Persia. Macedonia threw off this dependence after Plataea (479).
