Berat passed from Byzantium to the Serbs — Stefan Dušan's conquest of Albania

1343

The fortress of Berat, which had repulsed the Angevin siege in 1281 and had been since then the centre of Byzantium's last province on the Adriatic, fell into Serbian hands during Stefan Dušan's advance into Albania while the Byzantine civil war was under way. After the surrender of Kruja in May or June 1343, Berat, Kanina and Valona belonged to the Serbs within two years; Dušan's brother-in-law John Komnenos Asen was placed at the head of the province.
