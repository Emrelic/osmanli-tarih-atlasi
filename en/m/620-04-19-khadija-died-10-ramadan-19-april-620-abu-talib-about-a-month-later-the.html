Khadija died (10 Ramadan / 19 April 620); Abu Talib about a month later: the "Year of Sorrow"

19 April 620

Khadija, Muhammad's wife and first supporter, died in the 10th year of prophethood; when he also lost his uncle Abu Talib about a month later, the Muslims called this year the "Year of Sorrow" (sanat al-huzn).
