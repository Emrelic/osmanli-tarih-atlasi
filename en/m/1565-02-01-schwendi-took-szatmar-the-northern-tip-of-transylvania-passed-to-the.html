Schwendi took Szatmár — the northern tip of Transylvania passed to the Habsburgs

1 February 1565

Szatmár, which had passed to the Habsburgs in 1561 when Menyhért Balassa changed sides, had been retaken by a surprise attack by István Báthory in the autumn of 1564 and joined to Transylvania. The Habsburg commander Lazarus von Schwendi launched a counteroffensive in February 1565: Tokaj fell on 9 February, Szerencs four days later, and Szatmár came into the hands of the imperial forces almost without resistance.
