Union of Lublin — Ukrainian lands passed from Lithuania to Poland

1 July 1569

The Union of Lublin, signed on 1 July 1569, united the Grand Duchy of Lithuania and the Kingdom of Poland in a single state (the Rzeczpospolita). In accordance with the terms of the agreement, Podolia, Volhynia and the Kiev region (including Bar, Berdychiv, Białystok and Bratslav) were administratively transferred directly from Lithuania to the Kingdom of Poland.
