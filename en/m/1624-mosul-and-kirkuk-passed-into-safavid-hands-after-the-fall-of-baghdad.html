Mosul and Kirkuk passed into Safavid hands — after the fall of Baghdad

1624

Shah Abbas, who captured Baghdad at the end of the events that began with the revolt of Bekir Subaşı, sent his commander Qarchaqay Khan with his forces against Mosul and Kirkuk. Çerkez Ahmed Pasha, governor of Mosul, was able to defend the city for a few days; Mosul, like Kirkuk, passed under Iranian rule, and Qasim Khan was installed as governor. When Sipahi Küçük Ahmed, who commanded the vanguard of Hafız Ahmed Pasha, appeared before Mosul, Qasim Khan abandoned the city and withdrew to Baghdad, but through Shah Abbas's counter-operation Mosul again passed into Safavid hands in 1624.
