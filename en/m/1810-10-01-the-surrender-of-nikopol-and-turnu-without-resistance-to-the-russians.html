The surrender of Nikopol and Turnu without resistance to the Russians

1 October 1810

After the surrender of Ruse and Giurgiu, the commander-in-chief Kamensky marched west along the river to take the Danube fortresses as far as the Serbian border. Nikopol and Turnu on the opposite bank surrendered without resistance; Russian detachments took Pleven, Lovech and Sevlievo and destroyed their fortifications. The Russians held Nikopol for six months and left it in April 1811 after blowing up the fortress.
