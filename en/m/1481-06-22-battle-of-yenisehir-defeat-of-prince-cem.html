Battle of Yenişehir — defeat of Prince Cem

22 June 1481

The two brothers, who moved for the throne after the Conqueror's death, faced each other on the plain of Yenişehir, and Prince Cem suffered a decisive defeat. The prince, who ruled in Bursa for eighteen days and had the khutba read in his name, was forced after the defeat to take refuge first in Konya and then in Mamluk territory. Yenişehir began the longest-lasting dynastic crisis of Ottoman history: for fourteen years the Cem question was to paralyse Ottoman foreign policy vis-à-vis Europe.
