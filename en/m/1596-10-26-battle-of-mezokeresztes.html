Battle of Mezőkeresztes

26 October 1596

In the greatest pitched battle of the Long War (1593–1606), the army, at whose head Mehmed III himself stood, rallied at the moment it was about to disperse through the exhortation of Hoca Sâdeddin Efendi and routed the Habsburg army. It is regarded as the last great classical pitched battle won by the Ottomans; however, the fact that it did not change the overall course of the war was also taken as a sign of the wear in the military system.
