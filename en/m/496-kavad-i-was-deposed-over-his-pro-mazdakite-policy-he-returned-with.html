Kavad I was deposed over his pro-Mazdakite policy; he returned with Hephthalite help in 498-499

496

Kavad I (king since 488) was overthrown by the reaction of the nobility and clergy, took refuge with the Hephthalites, and with their military support returned to Ctesiphon in 498-499 and regained the throne. The Hephthalite support gave rise to a tribute he had to pay; the war with Rome in 502 arose from this need.
