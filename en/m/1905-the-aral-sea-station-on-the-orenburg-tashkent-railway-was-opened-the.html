The Aral Sea station on the Orenburg–Tashkent railway was opened — the settlement of Aralsk was founded

1905

As the Russian Empire's railway connecting Orenburg with Tashkent passed the northeastern tip of the Aral Sea, the station 'Aralskoye More' was opened on the shore; a fishing settlement grew up beside the station. The settlement received town status in 1938 and took the name Aralsk.
