The Baglioni lordship came to an end: Perugia came under the direct papal rule of Paul III

1540

At the end of the Salt War the Baglioni lordship was abolished and Paul III began the construction of the Rocca Paolina; the de facto independence of the Commune of Perugia came to an end (year precision; day not read).
