Karamanoğlu Mehmed Bey appointed subaşı of Ermenek and took İç İl under his administration

15 June 1276

Karamanoğlu Mehmed Bey was appointed subaşı of Ermenek in 675 (1276) and brought İç İl under his administration; Ermenek became the first center of the Karamanids, who would march on Konya the following year.
