Mulay Ismail's recovery of al-Araish from the Spaniards

1689

The TDV's Mulay Ismail entry is explicit: "In 1100 (1689) they retook Araish from the Spaniards, capturing 200 cannon, and two years later retook Asilah." This is one of the links in the Filali sultan's chain of cleansing the coast — Tangier was retaken from the English in 1678, Mamora in 1681, al-Araish in 1689, Asilah in 1691. The port, abandoned eighty years earlier in the Saadian civil war, thus returned to Morocco.
