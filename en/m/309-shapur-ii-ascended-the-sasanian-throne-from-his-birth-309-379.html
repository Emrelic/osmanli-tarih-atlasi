Shapur II ascended the Sasanian throne from his birth (309-379)

309

Shapur II, born to the queen left pregnant in the struggle over the throne after the death of Hormizd II, was crowned before birth and reigned for 70 years. His reign is the period of renewed struggle with Rome and of the Kushans' coming under Sasanian rule.
