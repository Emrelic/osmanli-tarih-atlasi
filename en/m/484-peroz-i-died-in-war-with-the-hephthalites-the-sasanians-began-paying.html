Peroz I died in war with the Hephthalites; the Sasanians began paying tribute to the Huns

484

Peroz perished together with his army before the Hephthalites (White Huns) led by Akhshunwar. The Sasanian state submitted to a large tribute and to the holding of his son Kavad as hostage at the Hephthalite court; the eastern frontier was permanently weakened.
