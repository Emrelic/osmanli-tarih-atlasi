Union of South Africa founded

31 May 1910

In accordance with the South Africa Act of 1909, the colonies of the Cape, Natal, Transvaal and Orange River united on 31 May 1910 under the name Union of South Africa, attached to the British Empire; Pretoria became the executive seat and Cape Town the legislative seat. With this union the cities of the colonies of the Cape, Natal, Transvaal and Orange (Kimberley, Durban, Pietermaritzburg, Potchefstroom, Port Elizabeth and others) passed to the Union of South Africa.
