Baybars took Jaffa from the Crusaders: the Kingdom of Jerusalem's rule at Jaffa ended

8 March 1268

The Mamluk sultan Baybars I ended Crusader rule at Jaffa and had the city demolished; he sent the wood and marble of the citadel to Cairo for the construction of his mosque.
