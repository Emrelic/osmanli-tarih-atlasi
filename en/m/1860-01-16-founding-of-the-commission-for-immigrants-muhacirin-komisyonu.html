Founding of the Commission for Immigrants (Muhacirin Komisyonu)

16 January 1860

In order to manage the growing wave of migration from the Caucasus, the Ottoman government founded the Commission for Immigrants on 16 January 1860. The commission coordinated the resettlement work in Anatolia, the Balkans and Syria and was active until 1875.
