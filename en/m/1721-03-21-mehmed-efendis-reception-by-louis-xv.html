Mehmed Efendi's reception by Louis XV

21 March 1721

According to the TDV article on 'Yirmisekiz Çelebi Mehmed Efendi', the ambassador was officially received at the Tuileries Palace by Louis XV, King of France, and presented the gifts and the letter of Ahmed III. During his visit he toured the palaces of Versailles, Trianon and Marly, hospitals, observatories and workshops, and recorded what he saw with drawings.
