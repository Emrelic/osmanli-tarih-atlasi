Gümülcine, the center of Western Thrace, opened to the Bulgarians

21 November 1912

With the withdrawal of Yaver Pasha's detachment, retreating from Kırcaali, toward Fere, Gümülcine surrendered to the Bulgarian forces. The center of Western Thrace was among the foremost of the lands that would be left to Bulgaria at the end of the war.
