The SHS army entered Prekmurje — Murska Sobota and Lendava left Hungarian administration

12 August 1919

Troops of the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes entered Prekmurje, beyond the Mura river, on 12 August 1919; Hungarian administration in the region ended on that day. The legal transfer took place with the Treaty of Trianon in 1920.
