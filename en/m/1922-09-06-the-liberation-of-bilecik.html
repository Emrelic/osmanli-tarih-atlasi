The liberation of Bilecik

6 September 1922

According to the TDV article on Bilecik, the city came under Greek occupation three times during the National Struggle; the third occupation, which began on 22 July 1921, ended on 6 September 1922. In the fire set by the Greek forces as they evacuated the city, most of the houses, shops and the government house were burned.
