The Mongols seized Gurganj (Urgench), the capital of Khwarazm

1221

After the long siege by Chagatai, Jochi and Ogedei, the moats were filled in and the city was set on fire with naphtha; the population, except for the craftsmen, was massacred, and Shaykh Najm al-Din Kubra was also killed. Genghis Khan gave Khwarazm and Western Siberia to Jochi.
