Kaloyan attacked Didymoteicho twice; the walls were demolished

1206

The Bulgarian army under Tsar Kaloyan invaded Thrace at the beginning of 1206 and attacked Didymoteicho twice; in the second assault the walls of the besieged city were demolished. The defence had been entrusted to the Greek units of Theodore Branas.
