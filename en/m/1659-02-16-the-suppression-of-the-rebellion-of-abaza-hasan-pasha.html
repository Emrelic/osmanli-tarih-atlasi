The Suppression of the Rebellion of Abaza Hasan Pasha

16 February 1659

Abaza Hasan Pasha, who had won over the Anatolian beylerbeyis and sekban agas against Köprülü Mehmed Pasha's harsh purges, began a great uprising by demanding the dismissal of the grand vizier. The rebellion was suppressed at Aleppo and its ringleaders were eliminated; this was the last collective resistance of the provincial pashas to the centre. Köprülü's authority thus became unquestioned, and the office of the grand vizierate rose again to the position of the true executive centre of the state.
