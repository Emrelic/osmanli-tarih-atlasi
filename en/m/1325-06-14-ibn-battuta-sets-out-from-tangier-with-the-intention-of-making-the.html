Ibn Battuta sets out from Tangier with the intention of making the pilgrimage

14 June 1325

The Moroccan jurist Ibn Battuta, at 21, sets out alone on the pilgrimage journey. Along the North African coast he goes to Tunis and from there with the pilgrim caravan to Alexandria.
