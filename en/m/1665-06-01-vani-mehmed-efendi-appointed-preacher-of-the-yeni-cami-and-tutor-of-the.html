Vânî Mehmed Efendi appointed preacher of the Yeni Cami and tutor of the sultan

1 June 1665

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Mehmed Efendi, Vânî, Vânî Mehmed Efendi, who was summoned to Istanbul when Köprülüzade Fazıl Ahmed Pasha became grand vizier and presented to Mehmed IV, quickly won the sultan's close interest. In 1665 he was appointed preacher of the Yeni Cami and tutor of the sultan and became one of the most influential men of religion in the palace.
