Kalender Shah rebellion

1526

Owing to financial hardships in Anatolia, discontent with new administrative arrangements, and the influence of Safavid propaganda, large Turkmen tribes such as the Çiçekli, Akça Koyunlu, Masadlı and Bozoklu, together with groups left over from the earlier Baba Zunnun rebellion, gathered around Kalender Shah and reached a force of about thirty thousand. The rebels first defeated the Beylerbeyi of Rum; when Grand Vizier Makbul Ibrahim Pasha raised troops and promised them their rights, Kalender's support melted away and the rebellion was suppressed around Elbistan; Kalender Shah and Veli Dündar were killed in June 1527.
