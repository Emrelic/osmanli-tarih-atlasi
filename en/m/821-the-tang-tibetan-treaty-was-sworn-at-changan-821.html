The Tang-Tibetan treaty was sworn at Chang'an (821)

821

When Tibet sued for peace in the 9th month, the Tang court, finding it unnecessary to proclaim the treaty in the ancestral temple, appointed Liu Yuanding 'Oath Envoy to the Western Barbarians'; on the 10th day of the 10th month the oath was sworn at Chang'an with the Tibetan envoys, and the two states were to respect the declared border and not invade one another. Liu Yuanding later went to the Tibetan camp and completed the oath there on the 6th day of the 5th month of the following year (the report arrived in the 6th month).
