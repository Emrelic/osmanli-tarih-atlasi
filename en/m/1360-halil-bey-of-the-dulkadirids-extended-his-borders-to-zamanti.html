Halil Bey of the Dulkadirids extended his borders to Zamantı

1360

With the accession of the child Mehmed Bey at the head of the Eretna Beylik, Turkmen raids from the south increased against the weakened Eretna lands. In 1360, while the Turkmen chief Ömer Bey took Malatya, Halil Bey of the Dulkadirids extended the borders of his beylik to Zamantı, east of Kayseri. Thus the Zamantı district between Elbistan and Kayseri passed into the hands of the Dulkadirids, and the beylik held this region until the Ottoman annexation of 1515.
