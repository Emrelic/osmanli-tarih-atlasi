Death of Murad IV and accession of Sultan Ibrahim

9 February 1640

After the death of Murad IV at the age of twenty-seven (twenty-eight by the Hijri count), his brother Ibrahim, the only surviving male member of the dynasty, ascended the throne. In the reign of the new sultan, who had lived for years in the cage, palace expenditures and struggles for influence within the palace shook the order of the state. Eight years later, with his deposition and murder, the second sultan-killing in Ottoman history would occur.
