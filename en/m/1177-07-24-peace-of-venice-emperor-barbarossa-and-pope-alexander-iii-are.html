Peace of Venice: Emperor Barbarossa and Pope Alexander III are reconciled at San Marco

24 July 1177

Negotiations between the Lombard League and Barbarossa were conducted in Venice; on Sunday, 24 July 1177, the emperor and the pope were reconciled with ceremony in the Basilica of San Marco. The peace was regarded as a triumph of Venetian diplomacy.
