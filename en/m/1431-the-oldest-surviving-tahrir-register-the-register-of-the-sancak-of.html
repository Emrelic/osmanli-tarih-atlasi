The oldest surviving tahrir register — the Register of the Sancak of Arvanid

1431

The oldest surviving example of the tahrir censuses, which recorded taxpayers, their households, their lands and their produce, is the Register of the Sancak of Arvanid dated 835 (1431). The survey commissions compared the old records with the situation on the spot, added those who had newly come of age to the register and struck off the dead. It is understood that the register procedure already existed in the fourteenth century; the system was renewed regularly in most provinces throughout the sixteenth century.
