The count of Flanders ceded Walloon Flanders (Lille, Douai, Béthune) to King Philip IV of France: Lille

1305

The 'French' (Walloon) part of the County of Flanders — the castellanies of Douai, Béthune and Lille — was given to the king of France in 1305 (definitive transfer 1320); in 1369 the dukes of Burgundy took it by marriage.
