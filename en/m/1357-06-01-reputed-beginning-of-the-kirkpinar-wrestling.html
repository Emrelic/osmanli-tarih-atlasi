Reputed beginning of the Kırkpınar wrestling

1 June 1357

According to tradition, the Kırkpınar wrestling tradition began with the wrestling contests among the forty ghazis who crossed with Süleyman Pasha, the conqueror of Rumelia. The TDV's Güreş and Kırkpınar entries relate this tradition but do not give a definite founding date.
