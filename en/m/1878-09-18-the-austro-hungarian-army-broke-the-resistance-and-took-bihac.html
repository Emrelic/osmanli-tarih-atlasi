The Austro-Hungarian army broke the resistance and took Bihać

18 September 1878

Austria-Hungary, which obtained the right to occupy Bosnia-Herzegovina by the Treaty of Berlin, met local Muslim resistance at Bihać. Forces under General Wilhelm von Reinländer took the city on 18 September 1878; the garrison of Ostrovica in the Una valley also passed out of Ottoman hands in the same operation.
