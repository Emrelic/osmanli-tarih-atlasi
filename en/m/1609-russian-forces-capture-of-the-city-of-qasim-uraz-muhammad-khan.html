Russian forces' capture of the city of Qasim, Uraz Muhammad Khan

1609

As a reflection of the Time of Troubles (Smuta) in Russia, Russian forces besieged and captured the city of Qasim and put most of its people to the sword. Uraz Muhammad, at the head of the khanate, had been targeted because he supported the Second False Dmitry; the following year (1610) he himself was killed in a plot.
