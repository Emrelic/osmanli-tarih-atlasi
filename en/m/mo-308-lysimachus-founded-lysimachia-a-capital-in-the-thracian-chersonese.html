Lysimachus founded Lysimachia: a capital in the Thracian Chersonese

308 BC

Lysimachus made Lysimachia, which he founded at the neck of the Gallipoli Peninsula, the capital of his Thracian kingdom; the crossing of the Straits and the Dardanelles was gathered in his hands. The city was devastated by an earthquake in the third century BC.
