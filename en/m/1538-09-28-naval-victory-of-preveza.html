Naval Victory of Preveza

28 September 1538

The Crusader fleet formed under the leadership of the Pope, although numerically superior, was scattered off Preveza by Barbarossa's tactics, which made skilful use of the wind and the coast; Andrea Doria abandoned the field of battle. The victory established an undisputed Ottoman naval supremacy in the Mediterranean that would last about 33 years; it is the symbolic date of Navy Day in the Turkish maritime tradition. Other places annexed on the same date: Aden, Bender.
