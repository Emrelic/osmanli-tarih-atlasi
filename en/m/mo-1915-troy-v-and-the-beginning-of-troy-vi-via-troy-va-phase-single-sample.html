Troy V and the beginning of Troy VI (VIa): Troy Va phase (single sample)

1915 BC

The beginning of Troy V lies within the range 1916-1877 cal BC; for the end of Troy V / beginning of Troy VI the literature has a consensus of c. 1750 cal BC (with reference to Pavúk, Blum, Pernicka).
