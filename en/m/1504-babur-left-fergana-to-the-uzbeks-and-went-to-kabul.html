Babur left Fergana to the Uzbeks and went to Kabul

1504

The Timurid Babur, son of Umar Shaykh Mirza, left Fergana, over which he had ruled since 1494, to the Uzbeks after his wars with Muhammad Shaybani Khan, and crossing the Hindu Kush settled in Kabul.
