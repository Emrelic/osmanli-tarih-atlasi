Battle of Soissons: King Robert I dies, Raoul, Duke of Burgundy, is elected king

15 June 923

Robert, elected to the kingship against Charles the Simple in 922, died in the Battle of Soissons; Robert's partisans, who won the victory, elected Raoul (Rudolph), Duke of Burgundy, king. Charles, trusting Herbert, Count of Vermandois, was captured and died at Péronne in 929.
