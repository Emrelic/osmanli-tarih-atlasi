Battle of Çamurlu — restoration of unity

5 July 1413

Çelebi Mehmed, having secured the support of Byzantium, the Serbian despotate and the Rumelian frontier lords, decisively defeated his brother Musa at Çamurlu; Musa was killed while fleeing. Rumelia, which Musa had held, passed to Mehmed together with Edirne, and Mehmed Çelebi proclaimed himself in Edirne the sole ruler of the Ottoman State. The eleven-year Interregnum came to an end, and the princely shares on the map were merged into a single body.
