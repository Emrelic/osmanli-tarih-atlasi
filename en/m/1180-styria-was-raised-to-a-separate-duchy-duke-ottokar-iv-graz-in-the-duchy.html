Styria was raised to a separate duchy (Duke Ottokar IV): Graz in the Duchy of Styria

1180

The March of Styria, under the overlordship of the duke of Bavaria, became an independent duchy in 1180; Graz was the center of this duchy. The day is not in the source (the Diet of Gelnhausen is mentioned).
