Withdrawal from Tabriz — the end of a week-long occupation and the return via Kars and Bayburt

15 September 1514

Yavuz Sultan Selim, who entered Tabriz on 6 September after the victory of Chaldiran, stayed in the city for about a week; he had Shah Ismāʿīl's treasury and some of the city's artisans and scholars, together with their families, transferred to Istanbul. Although he wished to spend the winter in Karabakh and continue the campaign the following year, he gave up this plan in the face of the Janissaries' opposition, and the army turned back via Kars and Bayburt. Thus Tabriz did not remain in Ottoman hands: Chaldiran was a victory in the field, while a lasting territorial gain came the following year with the campaign of Kemah, Diyarbekir and Eastern Anatolia.
