The siege of Rhodes by Demetrius Poliorcetes ended in failure

303 BC

Demetrius besieged Rhodes, which had stayed on Ptolemy's side against Antigonus, in 305 with gigantic siege engines; the Rhodians resisted for a year and peace was made in the summer of 304. The Colossus of the sun god Helios was erected in the city with the money from this victory.
