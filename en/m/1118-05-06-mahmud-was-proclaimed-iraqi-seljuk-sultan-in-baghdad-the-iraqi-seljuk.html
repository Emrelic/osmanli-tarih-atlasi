Mahmud was proclaimed Iraqi Seljuk sultan in Baghdad — the Iraqi Seljuk State was founded

6 May 1118

After Muhammad Tapar's death, his son Mahmud was proclaimed sultan by the caliph; the Iraqi Seljuk State was founded. Baghdad, Mosul and Tikrit remained attached to the Iraqi Seljuk sultanate, and Azerbaijan (Maragha, Nakhchivan) also fell to the Iraqi Seljuk share. In the source, Tikrit appears this year as the iqta of the shihna of Baghdad.
