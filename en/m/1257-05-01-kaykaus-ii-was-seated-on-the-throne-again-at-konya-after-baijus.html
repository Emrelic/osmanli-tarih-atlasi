Kaykaus II was seated on the throne again at Konya after Baiju's departure

1 May 1257

When Baiju Noyan left Anatolia, Kaykaus II was seated on the throne again at Konya (14 Rabi II 655 / 1 May 1257); by the yarligh of Möngke Khan the country was divided in two in 657 (1259) between Izz al-Din Kaykaus and Kilij Arslan.
