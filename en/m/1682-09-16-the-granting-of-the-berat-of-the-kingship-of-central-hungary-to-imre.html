The Granting of the Berat of the Kingship of Central Hungary to Imre Thököly

16 September 1682

Imre Thököly, the Protestant Hungarian nobleman who rose against Habsburg pressure, began to be regarded from April 1682 as the 'elected king of Central Hungary', and on 16 September the Beylerbeyi of Buda, Ibrahim Pasha, presented him with the insignia of princeship. Thus a new vassal Hungarian state was established under Ottoman protection. This intervention made war with Austria unavoidable and set in motion the chain leading to the Second Siege of Vienna the following year.
