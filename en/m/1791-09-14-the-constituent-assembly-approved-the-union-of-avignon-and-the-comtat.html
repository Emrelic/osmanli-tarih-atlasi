The Constituent Assembly approved the union of Avignon and the Comtat Venaissin with France

14 September 1791

After the expulsion of the papal vice-legate in Avignon (12 June 1790), the Constituent Assembly examined the request for union; the union was approved on 14 September 1791. Pius VI protested on 5 November 1791; by Article 6 of the Treaty of Tolentino (19 February 1797) the papacy renounced Avignon and the Comtat.
