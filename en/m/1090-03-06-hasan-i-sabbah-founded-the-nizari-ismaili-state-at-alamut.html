Hasan-i Sabbah founded the Nizari Isma'ili state at Alamut

6 March 1090

In the period when Malikshah made the struggle against the Batinis a matter of state policy, Hasan-i Sabbah founded the Nizari Isma'ili state at Alamut (483/1090); Malikshah made the struggle against the Batinis a policy of state but could not put an end to Batini activities in the country; the vizier Nizam al-Mulk was killed by the Batinis in 485 (1092).
