The capture of the Yanova (Ineu) fortress — Szigetvár campaign

25 July 1566

During the Szigetvár campaign of Süleyman the Lawgiver, the second vizier Pertev Pasha captured the Yanova fortress. The Ottomans gave the place the name Yanova; the fortress became the center of the sancak of Yanova-Pankota and was later attached to the beylerbeylik of Temesvár.
