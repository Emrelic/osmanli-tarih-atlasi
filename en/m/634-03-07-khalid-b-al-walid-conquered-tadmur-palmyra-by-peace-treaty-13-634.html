Khalid b. al-Walid conquered Tadmur (Palmyra) by peace treaty (13/634)

7 March 634

Tadmur (Palmyra) was conquered by Khalid b. al-Walid by peace treaty; it passed from the Byzantine (Eastern Roman) Empire to the administration of the Rashidun (Medina Caliphate). The TDV also mentions that after the people broke the agreement the city was retaken by force by Dihya b. Khalifa al-Kalbi (undated).
