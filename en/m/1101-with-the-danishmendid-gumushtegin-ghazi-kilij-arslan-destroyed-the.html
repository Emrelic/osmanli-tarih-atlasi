With the Danishmendid Gumushtegin Ghazi, Kilij Arslan destroyed the Lombard Crusaders marching on Niksar; the other Crusader armies were routed near Eregli

1101

In 1101 Kilij Arslan, in alliance with the Danishmendid Gumushtegin Ghazi, won a brilliant victory over the Crusaders made up of Lombards who were marching towards Niksar to rescue Bohemond, Prince of Antioch; the other Crusader forces were also destroyed near Eregli (Konya).
