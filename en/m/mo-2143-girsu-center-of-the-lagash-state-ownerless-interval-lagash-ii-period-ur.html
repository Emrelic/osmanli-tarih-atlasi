Girsu (center of the Lagash state): ownerless interval → Lagash II Period (Ur-Baba, Gudea, Ur-Ningirsu)

2143 BC

At Girsu (center of the Lagash state), ownership passes from the ownerless interval to the Lagash II Period (Ur-Baba, Gudea, Ur-Ningirsu). Transition source information: independence of Girsu/Lagash from Akkad: Puzurmama is the first independent ruler; "period of confusion" (after Sharkalisharri, MC 2181-2177) | Source: Sallaberger, W. & Schrakamp, I.
