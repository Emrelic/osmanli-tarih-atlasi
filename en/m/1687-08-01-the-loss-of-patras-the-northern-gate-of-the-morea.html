The loss of Patras — the northern gate of the Morea

1 August 1687

Patras, at the mouth of the Gulf of Corinth, was the port controlling the passage between the Morea and Rumelia. After the Venetian army took the city, the fortresses on both sides of the gulf also fell one after another, and the peninsula was entirely cut off by land. At the end of this operation the whole of the Morea passed to Venice; the region would be administered until 1715 under the name 'Regno di Morea'.
