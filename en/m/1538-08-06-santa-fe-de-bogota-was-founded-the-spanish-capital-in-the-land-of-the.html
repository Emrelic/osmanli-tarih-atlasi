Santa Fe de Bogotá was founded — the Spanish capital in the land of the Muisca

6 August 1538

Quesada founded Santa Fe de Bogotá beside Bacatá, the seat of the zipa. Immediately after the foundation, the conquest columns that had come from three different directions — Quesada, Benalcázar and the German commander Federmann — met on the same plateau and agreed to settle their claims in Spain. The city became the capital of the Kingdom of New Granada.
