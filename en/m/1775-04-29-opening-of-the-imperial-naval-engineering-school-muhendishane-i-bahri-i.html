Opening of the Imperial Naval Engineering School (Mühendishane-i Bahri-i Hümayun)

29 April 1775

The Ottomans' first modern naval engineering school, the Mühendishane-i Bahri-i Hümayun, was opened, according to French archive documents, on 29 April 1775, in the reign of Abdulhamid I, during the grand vizierate of Dervish Mehmed Pasha.
