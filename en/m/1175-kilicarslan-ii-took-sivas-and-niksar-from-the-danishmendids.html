Kılıcarslan II took Sivas and Niksar from the Danishmendids

1175

After the death of Nūr al-Dīn Zengī, Kılıcarslan II seized Sivas, Niksar, Tokat and the other Danishmendid lands in the summer of 1175; the Sivas branch came to an end.
