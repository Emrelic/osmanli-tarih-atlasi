Opening of the Nuruosmaniye Mosque and Complex

5 December 1755

The opening of the complex, begun by Mahmud I but not seen to completion by him, took place on 5 December 1755 in the reign of his brother Osman III. The mosque took its name, in reference to Osman III who performed the opening, in the sense of "Osman's Light."
