Hannibal took Saguntum: the fuse of the Second Punic War was lit

218 BC

Hannibal took Saguntum, Rome's ally, after an eight-month siege (219 BC); Rome sent envoys to demand Hannibal's surrender and, when this was refused, declared war. The war lasted seventeen years in Italy, Hispania and Africa.
