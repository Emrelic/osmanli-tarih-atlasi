Passage of Daju rule in Darfur to the Tunjur

1400

According to the Darfur entry of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, it is the Daju tribe who first settled the region and founded the first state; the Daju, who dominated here throughout the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries, lost commercial dominance to the Tunjur, and in the fifteenth century the situation, especially in the central region, turned entirely in the Tunjur's favor.
