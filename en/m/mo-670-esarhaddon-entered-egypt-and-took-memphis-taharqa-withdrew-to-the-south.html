Esarhaddon entered Egypt and took Memphis; Taharqa withdrew to the south

670 BC

The Assyrian king Esarhaddon marched on Egypt in his 10th year; the Assyrian army carried out a great slaughter in Egypt and the Kushite king Taharqa fled south. The principalities of the Delta became Assyrian vassals, and Memphis and Northern Egypt temporarily fell into Assyrian hands. Esarhaddon died on the road two years later as he set out on a new campaign.
