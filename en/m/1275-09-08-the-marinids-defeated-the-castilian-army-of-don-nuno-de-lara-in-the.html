The Marinids defeated the Castilian army of Don Nuño de Lara in the battle of Écija

8 September 1275

Abu Yusuf Ya'qub crossed to al-Andalus and defeated the Castilian army at Écija near Cordoba (15 Rabi I 674 / 8 September 1275); he took many prisoners and much booty.
