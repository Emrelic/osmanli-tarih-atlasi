The Death of Osman III and the Accession of Mustafa III

30 October 1757

With the death of Osman III after a reign of three years, Mustafa III, who was open to ideas of reform and showed close interest in the affairs of state, came to the throne. The new sultan, relying on the experience of the Grand Vizier Koca Râgıb Pasha, tried to set the finances right and renew the army; Baron de Tott's rapid-fire artillerymen were established in this period. However, the Russian war entered in 1768 would overshadow all the achievements of the seventeen-year reign.
