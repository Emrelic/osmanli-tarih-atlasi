The Loss of Esztergom to Austria

2 September 1595

The garrison of Esztergom, which did not receive sufficient aid during the siege led by Karl von Mansfeld, surrendered the fortress to Archduke Matthias. With the end of fifty-two years of Ottoman dominion, the defensive chain on the Danube line was broken and Buda came under direct threat. The fortress could be retaken only ten years later, in 1605.
