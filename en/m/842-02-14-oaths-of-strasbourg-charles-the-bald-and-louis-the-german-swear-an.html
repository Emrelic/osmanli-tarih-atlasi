Oaths of Strasbourg: Charles the Bald and Louis the German swear an alliance at Strasbourg

14 February 842

In the year after the victory of Fontenoy, Charles the Bald and Louis the German strengthened their alliance with the Oaths of Strasbourg; Charles swore in the Germanic language of Louis's people, and Louis in the Romance language of Charles's subjects.
