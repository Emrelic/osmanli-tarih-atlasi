Hamid-ili joined the Ottomans: Isparta, Burdur, Uluborlu, Eğirdir

1391

Bayezid the Thunderbolt, who set out on campaign after the attack of Karamanoğlu Alaeddin Bey on Hamid-ili and the complaint of the people, seized all the remaining lands of the Hamidids and gave their administration to his son Isa Çelebi.
