The Granting of the Haydarpaşa-Ankara Railway Concession to the Germans

24 September 1888

By the sultan's irade, the concession for the construction and operation of the Haydarpaşa-Ankara line was given to Alfred von Kaulla, the banker representing the Deutsche Bank, and the Anatolian Railway Company was established. This concession was the turning point that broke the Anglo-French monopoly in Ottoman transport and foreign policy and established German influence. Together with the Baghdad line concession to be given to the same company eleven years later, the dream of 'Berlin-Baghdad' would be born, and this would become one of the main axes of great-power rivalry before the First World War.
