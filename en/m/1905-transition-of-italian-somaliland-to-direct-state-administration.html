Transition of Italian Somaliland to direct state administration — liquidation of the Benadir Company

1905

According to the TDV article on Somalia, Italy had established its dominion over the southern Somali coast (Benadir) in 1889-1891 through agreements with the Sultanate of Zanzibar and Britain, and had initially left its administration to a chartered trading company (the Benadir Company); according to the standard academic source, this company's concession ended on 24 January 1905, and the Italian state purchased direct sovereignty over the Benadir coast (including Mogadishu, Merca and Brava) and established an official colonial administration. The colony's actual expansion toward the interior (Bardera, Baidoa) would be completed with the 1908 founding decree and in the following years.
