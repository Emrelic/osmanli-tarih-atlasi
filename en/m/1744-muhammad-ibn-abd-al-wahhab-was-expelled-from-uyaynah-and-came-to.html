Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab was expelled from Uyaynah and came to Muhammad ibn Saud in Diriyah: the basis of the Wahhabi-Saudi alliance

1744

In 1744, when Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab, who was under the protection of Uthman ibn Ahmad Al Mu'ammar, chief of the Banu Tamim in Uyaynah, was expelled from the town, he came to Diriyah to Muhammad ibn Saud; the two formed a religious-political force.
