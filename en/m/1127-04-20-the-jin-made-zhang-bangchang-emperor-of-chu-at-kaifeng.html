The Jin made Zhang Bangchang emperor of 'Chu' at Kaifeng

20 April 1127

After taking Kaifeng, while the emperor Qinzong was being held in the Jin camp at Qingcheng, the Jin on the 7th day of the 3rd month proclaimed Zhang Bangchang, one of the Song chancellors, puppet emperor of 'Chu'.
