The liberation of Kars — the Eastern Front entered Kars

30 October 1920

According to the TDV article on Kars, Kâzım Karabekir Pasha entered Kars on 30 October 1920, and the city was freed from the Armenian administration to which the British had left control in 1919. The article on Karabekir stresses that this was the second liberation of Kars: the city had first been taken from the Armenians in April 1918. The Eastern Front advanced and took Gyumri on 7 November; the war ended with the Treaty of Gyumri on 3 December 1920.
