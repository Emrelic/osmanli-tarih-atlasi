Alfonso XI of Castile took Algeciras: Marinid rule ended after a twenty-month siege

26 March 1344

After the siege of Alfonso XI of Castile, lasting about twenty months, at the end of negotiations that began on 21 March 1344 the Marinid ruler Abu'l-Hasan notified the Muslims to leave the city; on 26 March Villa Nueva and the next day Villa Vieja were evacuated and the Castilians took over the city. Alfonso XI entered the city in ceremony on 28 March 1344 (Palm Sunday). Algeciras remained in Castile until 1369.
