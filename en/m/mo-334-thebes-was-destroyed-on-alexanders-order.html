Thebes was destroyed on Alexander's order

334 BC

Alexander took Thebes, which had risen on Philip's death, in the autumn; he placed a Macedonian garrison on the Cadmea, had the city destroyed and its people enslaved (except Pindar's house). The Boeotian League effectively came to an end with this destruction (Cassander refounded the city in 316).
