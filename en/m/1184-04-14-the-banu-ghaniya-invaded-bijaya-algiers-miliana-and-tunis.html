The Banu Ghaniya invaded Bijaya, Algiers, Miliana, and Tunis

14 April 1184

The Banu Ghaniya (the continuation of the Almoravids), who had crossed to the Maghrib after submitting to the Abbasids, took advantage of the death of the Almohad ruler and invaded Bijaya, Algiers, Miliana, and Tunis (580/1184).
