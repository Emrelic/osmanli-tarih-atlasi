Murad IV's Conquest of Revan

8 August 1635

Murad IV, who set out from Istanbul on 28 March 1635, besieged Revan on 28-29 July and received the surrender of the fortress on 8 August; he then entered the evacuated Tabriz and had the city destroyed. In memory of the victory the sultan had the Revan Pavilion built in Topkapı Palace. This campaign was the first great eastern operation after an interval of thirty years in which the sultan commanded the army in person.
