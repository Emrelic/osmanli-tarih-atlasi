Battle of Edessa: Shapur I captured the Roman emperor Valerian

260

Shapur I routed the army of Emperor Valerian near Edessa and took the emperor himself captive. The reliefs of Bishapur and Naqsh-e Rustam depict this victory; Valerian's death in Sasanian captivity is the greatest humiliation in Roman history.
