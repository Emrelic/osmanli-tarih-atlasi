Eric IX organises the church; the crusade to Finland begins Sweden's overseas domination

1150

King Eric IX (1150-1160) organised the Swedish church on the Western model and, by conducting a crusade against the pagan Finns, began Sweden's overseas rule. (The year of the expedition is not given in the source.)
