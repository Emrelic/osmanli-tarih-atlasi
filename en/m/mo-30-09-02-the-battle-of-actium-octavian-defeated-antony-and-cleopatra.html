The Battle of Actium: Octavian defeated Antony and Cleopatra

2 September 30 BC

Octavian's fleet defeated the fleet of Antony and Cleopatra at Actium; the pair withdrew to Egypt. Dio, an author who avoids giving dates, specifically notes this day because from then on Octavian held the empire alone and the years of his power were counted from this day.
