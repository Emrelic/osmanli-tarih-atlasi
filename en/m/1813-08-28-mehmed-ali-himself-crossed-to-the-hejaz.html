Mehmed Ali himself crossed to the Hejaz

28 August 1813

Seeing that the campaign was being conducted slowly, the governor took the administration directly into his own hands; by bargaining with the tribal chiefs he tried to dissolve the Saudi front from within. Egypt's share in the Red Sea trade also grew through this campaign.
