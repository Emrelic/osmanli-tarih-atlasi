The constitution of the Hungarian People's Republic came into force — a Soviet-type one-party regime

20 August 1949

The constitution dated 20 August 1949 made Hungary a People's Republic. The macaristan-halk künye (1946-1989) holds these two regimes as a single polity; the map color does not change.
