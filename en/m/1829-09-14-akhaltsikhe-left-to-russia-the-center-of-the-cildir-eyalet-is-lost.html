Akhaltsikhe left to Russia — the center of the Çıldır eyalet is lost

14 September 1829

Akhaltsikhe, which had been under Ottoman administration since the Battle of Çıldır in 1578 and was the center of the Çıldır eyalet, was left to Russia by the Treaty of Edirne signed at the end of the 1828-1829 war. The loss of the city created deep sorrow among the people and became the subject of many laments. With this event Akhalkalaki, Zazalo and Ts'q'altbila (Akhaltsikhe–Akhalkalaki region) passed to Russia.
