End of the Shirvanshahs: Elqas Mirza took Shahrukh captive, Shirvan became a Safavid province

1 October 1538

Elqas Mirza, sent by Shah Tahmasb, plundered Shirvan, took the fortress of Baykurd, and carried off the fifteen-year-old Shirvanshah Shahrukh to Tabriz as a captive. The rule of the Shirvanshahs, who had long maintained their sovereignty in the region, thus came to an end; Shirvan became a province of the Safavid State.
