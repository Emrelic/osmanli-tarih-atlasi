Return of the Morava valley to Ottoman administration — Jagodina, Kragujevac and Čačak

1 August 1738

With the recapture of Semendire in August 1738, the Morava valley lying between Semendire and the main body of the Ottoman Empire also returned to Ottoman administration: Jagodina, Kragujevac and Čačak are counted as Ottoman from this date. The sources do not date and mention the return of these three places separately; the map works with Semendire's date. The Treaty of Belgrade (1739-09-18) confirmed this situation.
