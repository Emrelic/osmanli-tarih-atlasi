Wang Geon (Taejo) ascended the throne in the Bojeong Hall at Gaegyeong and named the country Goryeo

25 July 918

Gungye was overthrown by the uprising of the Taebong generals Hong Yu, Bae Hyeongyeong, Sin Sung-gyeom and Bok Ji-gyeom; Wang Geon ascended the throne in the summer of 918, proclaimed the country under the name Goryeo, and gave his reign the name Cheonsu ('Gift of Heaven'). The capital was Gaegyeong (Kaesong).
