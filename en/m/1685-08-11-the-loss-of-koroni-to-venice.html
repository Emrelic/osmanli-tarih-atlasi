The Loss of Koroni to Venice

11 August 1685

The Venetian forces under Morosini captured the fortress of Koroni at the southwestern tip of the Morea peninsula; the city would remain under Venetian administration for thirty years thereafter. The fall of Koroni was the first great gain of the Venetian operation aimed at the conquest of the Morea. The fortress could be retaken only in 1715 by the Ottoman army under Silâhdar Ali Pasha.
