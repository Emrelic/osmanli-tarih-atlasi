Tabinshwehti entered Pegu (Bago) without a fight; the Mon capital fell into the hands of the king of Toungoo

1539

The execution of two commanders of the Talaing (Mon) kingdom as traitors cost the army its morale and its commanders; the Mon king fled to Prome, and Tabinshwehti entered Pegu without a fight in 1539 and set up his palace there.
