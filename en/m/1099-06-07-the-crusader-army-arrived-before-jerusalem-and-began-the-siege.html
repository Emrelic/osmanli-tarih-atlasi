The Crusader army arrived before Jerusalem and began the siege

7 June 1099

After a three-year march, on the morning of Tuesday, 7 June 1099, the Crusaders reached the highest point facing Jerusalem, then in Fatimid hands, and laid siege to the city; the five-week siege ended with the fall of the city on 15 July.
