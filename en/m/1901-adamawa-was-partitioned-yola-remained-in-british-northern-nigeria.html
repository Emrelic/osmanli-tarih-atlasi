Adamawa was partitioned — Yola remained in British Northern Nigeria

1901

Adamawa was partitioned between British-ruled Northern Nigeria and German-ruled Cameroon; Bobbo Ahmadu, the fourth son of Adama, became emir of Yola in the British zone.
