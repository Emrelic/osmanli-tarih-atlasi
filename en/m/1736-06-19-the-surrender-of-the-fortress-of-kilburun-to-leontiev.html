The surrender of the fortress of Kılburun to Leontiev

19 June 1736

As Münnich advanced in the Crimea, the Leontiev column he had detached moved on Kılburun at the mouth of the Dnieper, and the fortress surrendered. Since the source does not give the day on which the Russian forces withdrew from the fortress, the occupation window is not shown on the map.
