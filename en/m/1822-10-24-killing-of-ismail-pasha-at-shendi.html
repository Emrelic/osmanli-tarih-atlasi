Killing of Ismail Pasha at Shendi

24 October 1822

The local emir Nimr, enraged by the demands for heavy taxes and soldiers, set fire to the house in which Ismail Pasha was lodged and burned him and his retinue. The punitive expedition turned the region into a lake of blood; the administration of Sudan was thereafter founded on harshness.
