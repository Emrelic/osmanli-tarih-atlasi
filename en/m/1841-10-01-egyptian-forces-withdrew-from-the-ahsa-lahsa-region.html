Egyptian forces withdrew from the Ahsa (Lahsa) region

1 October 1841

The Egyptian forces of Mehmed Ali Pasha had occupied Najd and eastern Arabia (including Ahsa, Qatif, Uqair and Jubail) in 1836; when the 1840 London Straits Convention made Egypt renounce its conquests in the Arabian peninsula, the forces withdrew from the region in 1841, and the administration returned to local powers (Saudi/Al Khalifa influence).
