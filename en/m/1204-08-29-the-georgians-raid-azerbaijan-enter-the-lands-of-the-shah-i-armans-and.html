The Georgians raid Azerbaijan, enter the lands of the Shah-i Armans and plunder the Malazgirt and Erciş region

29 August 1204

After a successful raid on Azerbaijan in 601/1204-05, the Georgians entered the lands of the Shah-i Armans, advanced as far as Manzikert and then the Erciş area, burning and destroying everywhere; no troops came out against them.
