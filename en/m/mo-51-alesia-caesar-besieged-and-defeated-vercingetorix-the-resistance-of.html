Alesia: Caesar besieged and defeated Vercingetorix; the resistance of Gaul was broken

51 BC

In 52 BC the Gallic uprising led by the Arvernian Vercingetorix was suppressed by Caesar's siege of Alesia and the defence of his siege lines; Vercingetorix surrendered. The following year Gaul came entirely under Roman control.
