Corsica attached to the British crown: the Anglo-Corsican Kingdom

15 June 1794

Paoli, who rose against Revolutionary France, cleared the island of French garrisons with the support of the British fleet; the consulta assembled at Corte adopted a constitution attaching Corsica to the British king. For two years the island remained the Anglo-Corsican Kingdom under the administration of the British viceroy Gilbert Elliot.
