The passing of the sancak of Bayezid to Safavid domination (Van and Erciş were already Safavid in 1535)

1547

The sancak of Bayezid, in the middle of the Ottoman-Safavid frontier, passed again into Iranian hands (Kaya 2017 mentions this 'together with Van and Erciş'; however, according to Karataş 2025 and the TDV, Van and Erciş had returned to the Safavids on 22 September 1535); in this change of hands some places in the sancak were ruined. The sancak changed hands between the two states many times until the last quarter of the 16th century.
