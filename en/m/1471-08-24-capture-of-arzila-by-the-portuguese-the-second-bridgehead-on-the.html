Capture of Arzila by the Portuguese: the second bridgehead on the Moroccan coast

24 August 1471

Afonso V landed in person on the Moroccan coast with a force of 30,000 men and a fleet of 400 vessels and took Arzila, under Wattasid administration, on 24 August 1471. The fall of the city broke Tangier's resistance; Tangier surrendered without a fight four days later, on 28 August. Portugal thus established, after Ceuta (1415) and Ksar es-Seghir (1458), its third and fourth positions on Morocco's Atlantic coast; after this campaign the king had the phrase "lord of Africa" added to his title.
