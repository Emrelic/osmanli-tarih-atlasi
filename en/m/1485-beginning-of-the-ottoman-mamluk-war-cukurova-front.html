Beginning of the Ottoman-Mamluk War (Çukurova front)

1485

The contest for influence over the Dulkadirids and the enmity left by Prince Cem's taking refuge in Mamluk lands began in 1485 a war between the two great Sunni powers that would last six years. In the clashes in Çukurova around Adana and Tarsus the parties could not gain the upper hand over one another; the Ottoman armies took several heavy blows. This war is the first phase of the break in Ottoman-Mamluk relations and the opening of the process that was to conclude thirty years later with Selim the Grim's campaigns of Marj Dabiq and Ridaniya.
