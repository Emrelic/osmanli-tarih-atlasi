Battle of the Halys (Kızılırmak): after a solar eclipse Alyattes and Cyaxares made peace; the Halys became the Lydian-Median frontier

584 BC

The five-year war between Alyattes and the Median king Cyaxares ended in peace after a solar eclipse that occurred in daytime; Syennesis, king of Cilicia, and the king of Babylon, "Labynetus", mediated. The Halys river became the frontier between the two kingdoms, and Media was regarded as extending into the interior of Anatolia.
