The conquest of Shirvan: the taking of Shamakhi

1 November 1578

The army, which had opened onto the Caucasus after the Victory of Çıldır, entered Shirvan, the richest province of the Safavids on the Caspian shore, and took its central city, Shamakhi. With Özdemiroğlu Osman Pasha appointed beylerbeyi there, the northern branch of the Silk Road was brought under Ottoman control. Shirvan would become the front that changed hands most often and cost the most dearly in the twelve-year eastern war.
