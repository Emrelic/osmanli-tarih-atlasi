The taking over of Eastern Thrace — Kırklareli and Tekirdağ

10 November 1922

The Armistice of Mudanya provided for the evacuation of Eastern Thrace by the Greek army and its handing over to Turkey; Refet Pasha, charged with taking over Thrace, came to Istanbul on 19 October. According to the TDV, Kırklareli, which had been under occupation since 26 July 1920, was retaken on 10 November 1922, and Tekirdağ was also returned to the Turks on 13 November in accordance with the armistice. Thus Eastern Thrace was joined to Turkish territory without fighting.
