Babylon, Uruk: Achaemenid (Persian) Empire → Empire of Alexander the Great

22 October 330 BC

At the points Babylon and Uruk, ownership passes from the Achaemenid (Persian) Empire to the Empire of Alexander the Great. Source transition information: Alexander takes Babylon (331); source Livius summary, Arrian 3.16 not read | Source: Lendering, J., Livius.org: Babylon | https://www.livius.org/articles/place/babylon/ | «In 331, the Macedonian conqueror Alexander the Great, who was fighting a war against the Persians, captured Babylon»
