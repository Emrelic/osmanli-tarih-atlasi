The entry of Greek forces into Manisa and Aydın

26 May 1919

The Greek army, which landed at Izmir on 15 May, soon spread to the interior. According to the TDV article on Manisa, Greek forces entered the city on 26 May 1919; according to the article on Aydın, Aydın was occupied the next day, 27 May. The occupation of Manisa would last until September 1922.
