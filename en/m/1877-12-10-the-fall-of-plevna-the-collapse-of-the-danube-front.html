The Fall of Plevna: The Collapse of the Danube Front

10 December 1877

Osman Pasha, whose provisions ran out in the siege of five months, attempted a sortie across the Vid river; when he was wounded and taken prisoner, the city surrendered. The Russian commanders saluted the captured pasha by returning his sword, and Osman Pasha was given the title 'Gazi'. With the fall of Plevna the road lay open to the Russian army; within two months Sofia, Philippopolis and Edirne fell, and the Russian vanguards reached Yeşilköy.
