Rome took Sardinia and Corsica from Carthage

237 BC

When Carthage's rebel mercenaries in Sardinia asked Rome for help, Rome occupied the island; under the threat of war Carthage was forced to give up the islands and pay an additional 1,200 talents. Rome created its second province (Sardinia et Corsica).
