Orsova and Fethülislâm taken into an Austrian garrison

25 October 1689

After the bloodless taking of Vidin (October 1689, TDV Vidin), Baden repaired and garrisoned Orsova and Fethülislâm on 25 October 1689; Orsova remained Austrian until the autumn of 1690. The day rests on Bánlaky/Röder and has not been re-verified.
