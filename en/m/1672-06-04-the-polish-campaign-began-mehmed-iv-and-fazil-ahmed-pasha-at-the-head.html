The Polish Campaign Began — Mehmed IV and Fâzıl Ahmed Pasha at the Head of the Army

4 June 1672

When Polish attacks continued against the Ukrainian hetman Petro Doroshenko, who had come under Ottoman protection, Istanbul asked the Polish king to desist from the attacks; when no favourable answer came, the decision for a campaign against Poland was taken. The Grand Vizier Fâzıl Ahmed Pasha set out for Poland on 4 June 1672 with the army, in which the sultan was also present. The campaign would end in the conquest of Kamaniçe and the Treaty of Buczacz.
