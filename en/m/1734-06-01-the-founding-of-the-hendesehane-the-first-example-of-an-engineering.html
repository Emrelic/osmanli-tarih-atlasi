The founding of the Hendesehane (the first example of an engineering school)

1 June 1734

According to the TDV article on 'Mahmud I', Humbaracı Ahmed Pasha, by founding at Üsküdar the Hendesehane, also called the Humbarahane, gave military education based on mathematics and geometry to students he selected from among palace servants and bostancıs. This institution is regarded as the first example of the engineering schools that would develop in the following period.
