Ottoman-Russian Defensive Alliance — joint defense against France

3 January 1799

Following Napoleon's invasion of Egypt, Selim III and the Russian Tsar Paul I formed an eight-year defensive alliance of fourteen articles. The parties undertook to have common friends and enemies, to give mutual aid and not to make a separate peace.
