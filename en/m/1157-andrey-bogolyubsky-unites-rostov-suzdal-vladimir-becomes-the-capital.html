Andrey Bogolyubsky unites Rostov-Suzdal; Vladimir becomes the capital

1157

Andrey, who had left Vyshhorod in 1155 and settled in Vladimir, became prince of Rostov and Suzdal in 1157 on the death of his father Yuri Dolgorukiy; he united north-eastern Rus' in one hand.
