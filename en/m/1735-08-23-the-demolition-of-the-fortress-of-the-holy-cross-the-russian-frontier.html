The demolition of the fortress of the Holy Cross — the Russian frontier was drawn back to the Terek, the fortress of Kizlyar was founded

23 August 1735

After the treaties of Rasht and Ganja, Russia's new frontier became the Terek river; it was decided to demolish the fortress of the Holy Cross on the Sulak, and Levashov reported the demolition in August 1735. In the same year Levashov chose the site of the fortress of Kizlyar on the Terek; the Russian presence on the Dagestan coast, which had continued since 1722, was thus drawn back to the north of the Terek.
