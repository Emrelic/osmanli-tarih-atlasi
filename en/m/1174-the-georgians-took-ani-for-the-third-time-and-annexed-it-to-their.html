The Georgians took Ani for the third time and annexed it to their country

1174

The Georgians took the city of Ani in 1174 and annexed it to their country; in response the Shah of Ahlat, Sökmen, and the Seljuk army assembled at Nakhchivan in summer 1175 and conducted a campaign against Georgian lands.
