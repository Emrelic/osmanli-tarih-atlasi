Loss of Léva

14 June 1664

After Nitra, De Souches besieged Léva on 9 June 1664; the capitulation was signed on 13 June and the fortress was in the hands of the imperial forces on 14 June. The battle of Léva (Garamszentbenedek) on 19 July 1664 did not change the ownership; it ended with the Habsburgs retaining Léva and Nitra.
