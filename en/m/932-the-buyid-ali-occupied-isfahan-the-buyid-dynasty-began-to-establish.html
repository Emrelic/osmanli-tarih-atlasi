The Buyid Ali occupied Isfahan; the Buyid dynasty began to establish rule in western Iran

932

Ali, son of Buwayh the Daylamite, went south to seek the caliph's support against the Ziyarid Mardawij and occupied Isfahan (932); on encountering Mardawij's allies, he left the city and turned towards Fars.
