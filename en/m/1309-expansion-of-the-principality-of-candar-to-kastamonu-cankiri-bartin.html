Expansion of the Principality of Candar to Kastamonu-Çankırı-Bartın-Safranbolu

1309

According to the TDV's Candaroğulları entry, the principality was founded in the early fourteenth century around Kastamonu-Sinop by Shams al-Din Yaman Candar, to whom the Ilkhanid ruler Gaykhatu had granted Eflani and its surroundings as an iqta; because of the inadequacy of the sources, the exact date of the founding is not known. Candar's successor Süleyman Beg is mentioned as a ruler who took the place of the Principality of the Chobanids — that is, the Kastamonu and Çankırı area passed to the Candarids from the Chobanids, and the Bartın-Safranbolu-Eflani line from direct Ilkhanid administration.
