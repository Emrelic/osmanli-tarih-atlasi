Annexation of the Kingdom of Imereti — King Solomon II deposed

4 March 1810

Solomon II, the last king of the West Georgian Kingdom of Imereti, which had been compelled in 1804 to accept Russian vassalage by the Treaty of Elaznauri, continued to resist increasing Russian pressure; Russian troops seized the capital Kutaisi on 20 February 1810, deposed Solomon, and forced the local nobles to swear allegiance to Tsar Alexander I; the Kingdom of Imereti thus effectively came to an end and was attached to direct Russian administration. Solomon fled to Ottoman lands and died in exile (1815).
