Treaty of Hippo: Rome recognized the Vandals as foederati in Numidia and part of Mauretania

435

After the Vandal king Gaiseric crossed to Africa in 429, he concluded the treaty of Hippo with Rome in 435; the Vandals held Numidia and a part of Mauretania as foederati. The treaty was broken in 439 with the taking of Carthage.
