Surrender of the fortress of Munkács (Mukacheve) to Austria after a three-year siege

17 January 1688

The fortress of Munkács, besieged by imperial forces at the end of 1685, resisted for three years under the command of Ilona Zrínyi, wife of Imre Thököly; according to many published reference works, the fortress was surrendered to General Antonio Caraffa on 17 January 1688. This was the fall of one of the last great fortresses of the pro-Ottoman resistance in Hungary on the Ottoman-Habsburg front.
