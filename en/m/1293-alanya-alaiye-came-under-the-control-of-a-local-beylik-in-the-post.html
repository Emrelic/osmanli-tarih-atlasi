Alanya (Alaiye) came under the control of a local beylik in the post-Seljuk period

1293

With the effective disintegration of the Anatolian Seljuk State in the 1290s, the Mediterranean port of Alaiye (Alanya) became independent of the central authority and came under the control of the beyliks of the region (in this period within the sphere of influence of the Karamanids); the exact date and which beylik directly ruled it are not clear in the sources.
