Queen Joanna of Naples sold Avignon to Pope Clement VI

9 June 1348

As countess of Provence, Queen Joanna of Naples sold the city of Avignon to Pope Clement VI for 80,000 florins on 9 June 1348; the city remained a papal exclave until 1791. The settlement record carried this border; the entry was missing.
