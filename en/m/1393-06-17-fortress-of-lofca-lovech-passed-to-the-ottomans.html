Fortress of Lofça (Lovech) passed to the Ottomans

17 June 1393

Lofça, one of the fortresses of the Bulgarian tsar Ivan Shishman, was annexed to Ottoman lands together with Shishman's other provinces in the campaign of 1393 in which Tırnova fell. The source does not speak definitively: it is 'thought' that the conquest was in this year.
