The atabegate of Akhaltsikhe came under Ottoman administration — Altunkale, Hırtıs and Akhalkalaki

9 August 1578

Immediately after Lala Mustafa Pasha defeated the Safavid vanguard at Çıldır, the lands held by the Georgian atabegs also passed to Ottoman administration. Dedis Imedi, who held Altunkale and its surroundings, and his sons declared their obedience; the sancak bey of Ardahan took Çıldır and Tümük, and other Ottoman units took Hırtıs and Akhalkalaki. A sancak each was left to Manuchihr and his brother, and the rest of the atabeg country was placed under direct administration. Akhaltsikhe would thereafter be the centre of the newly founded province of Çıldır; Manuchihr, who came to Istanbul, became a Muslim and took the name Mustafa, would be placed at the head of this province on 1 July 1579.
