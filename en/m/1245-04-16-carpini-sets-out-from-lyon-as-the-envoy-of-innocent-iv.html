Carpini sets out from Lyon as the envoy of Innocent IV

16 April 1245

The Franciscan friar Giovanni da Pian del Carpine, sent by the Pope to the Mongols, leaves Lyon and, passing through Bohemia, Poland and the Rus lands, heads for Mongol territory.
