Davud Agha becomes chief imperial architect

1592

After a short interval following Mimar Sinan's death in 1588, Davud Agha began to be referred to in official documents as chief imperial architect from 1592. Davud Agha, who undertook some of the supplementary elements of the Selimiye Complex and the first construction of the New Mosque, is one of the most important architects of the period.
