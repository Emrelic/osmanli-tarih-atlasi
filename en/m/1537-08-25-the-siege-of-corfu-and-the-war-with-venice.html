The Siege of Corfu and the War with Venice

25 August 1537

After the Apulia (Pulya) campaign the Ottoman army landed troops on Corfu and besieged the island's strong Venetian fortress. Because of the fortress's steep structure, heavy losses and the conditions of the season, the blockade was lifted and the army withdrew on 6 September. Although the siege was inconclusive, the war opened with Venice ended with the annexation of many islands in the Aegean and the Adriatic to the Ottomans and with the victory of Preveza the following year.
