Shah-i Arman Sökmen, Izz al-Din Saltuk and Dawlatshah besiege Ani; the Muslims are defeated when Saltuk leaves the camp

1 August 1161

After the Georgian King Giorgi captured Ani, Sökmen II, Shah of Ahlat, Izz al-Din Saltuk II, Fakhr al-Dawla Dawlatshah, lord of Erzen and Bitlis, and other Turkish emirs united and besieged the city. On King Giorgi's arrival, Saltuk left the camp without informing the other lords; the Muslims were defeated, very many were killed, and 9,000 were taken prisoner.
