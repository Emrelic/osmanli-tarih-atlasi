Ashur (beginning of documents of the Old Assyrian period and the fall of 614 BCE): Ashur: the Old Assyrian eponym list begins (beginning of documentation, not foundation)

1971 BC

Ashur, a trading center in the period covered by the Old Assyrian eponym list (MC 1972-1718); in 614 BCE (Nabopolassar year 12, month of Abu) the Medes took and destroyed Ashur.
