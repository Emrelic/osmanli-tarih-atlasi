In the Amarna age Egypt's rule in Northern Syria dissolved; Amurru and Kadesh slipped into the Hittite sphere of influence

1339 BC

The Amarna letters (EA) show that the vassals of Northern Syria (Aziru of Amurru, the rulers of Kadesh) played Egypt, Mitanni and Hatti against one another, and that Egypt could not intervene in the region. As a result of the campaigns of the Hittite king Suppiluliuma I that wore down Mitanni, Amurru and Kadesh came under Hittite influence; thus Egypt's northern frontier in Syria fell back to Canaan.
