Hammurabi took Mari; two years later he had its walls demolished

1760 BC

Hammurabi, who captured the Mari of his former ally Zimri-Lim, also had the city walls demolished. The destruction of Mari marks the end of the Amorite kingdom in the Euphrates valley; Babylon became the dominant power as far as Aššur and Eshnunna in the north.
