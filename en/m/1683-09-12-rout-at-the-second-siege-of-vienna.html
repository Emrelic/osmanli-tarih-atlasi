Rout at the Second Siege of Vienna

12 September 1683

The two-month siege of Vienna by Merzifonlu Kara Mustafa Pasha turned into a rout through the sudden blow dealt by the Polish king Jan Sobieski from the Kahlenberg heights; the army withdrew leaving its baggage, and Merzifonlu was executed in Belgrade. The war that followed with the Holy League lasted 16 years; this rout is pointed to as the starting moment of the great decline in Ottoman history.
