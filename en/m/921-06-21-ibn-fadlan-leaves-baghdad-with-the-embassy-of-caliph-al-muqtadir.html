Ibn Fadlan leaves Baghdad with the embassy of Caliph al-Muqtadir

21 June 921

At the request of Almış, khan of the Volga Bulgars, the delegation sent by the Caliph sets out under the leadership of Sawsan al-Rassi; Ibn Fadlan is the delegation's religious adviser.
