The Death of Mahmud I and the Accession of Osman III

13 December 1754

Mahmud I, who in his reign of twenty-four years had recovered the lands lost by the Treaty of Belgrade and supported the reform of the Humbaracı Corps, died while returning from the Friday selamlık. He was succeeded by his brother Osman III, who had lived the life of the cage until the age of fifty-eight. A short and uneasy reign began; in the period of Osman III, who changed six grand viziers in three years, a marked stagnation prevailed in the affairs of state.
