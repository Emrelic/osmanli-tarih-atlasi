Isaac II Angelos ascended the throne

12 September 1185

On learning that Thessalonica had been taken by the Normans, the people of Constantinople rose against Andronikos Komnenos and placed Isaac Angelos on the throne on 12 September 1185. The period of the Angelos dynasty (1185-1204) began.
