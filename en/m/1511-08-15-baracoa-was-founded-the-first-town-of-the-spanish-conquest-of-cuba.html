Baracoa was founded — the first town of the Spanish conquest of Cuba

15 August 1511

Diego Velázquez de Cuéllar began the conquest of Cuba in 1511 with a force he brought over from Hispaniola and founded Nuestra Señora de la Asunción de Baracoa, the first Spanish town on the island. In the following twenty years Baracoa was the base region for the expeditions directed at Mexico (Hernández de Córdoba, Grijalva, Cortés).
