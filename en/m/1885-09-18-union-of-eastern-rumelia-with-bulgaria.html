Union of Eastern Rumelia with Bulgaria

18 September 1885

The autonomous province of Eastern Rumelia was joined to Bulgaria by a coup; the Sublime Porte accepted the fait accompli. With this union the whole of Eastern Rumelia (Stara Zagora, Pazardzhik, Sliven, Haskovo, Burgas, Aytos, Elhovo) passed to the Principality of Bulgaria.
