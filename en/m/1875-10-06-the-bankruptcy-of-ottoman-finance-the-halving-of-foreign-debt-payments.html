The Bankruptcy of Ottoman Finance: The Halving of Foreign Debt Payments

6 October 1875

The loans taken one after another since 1854, the expenses of the Crimean War, the palace constructions and the Anatolian famine of 1873-74 turned the budget deficit into an abyss exceeding five million lira. The government of Mahmud Nedim Pasha effectively declared a moratorium by announcing that debt interest and principal payments would be halved for five years. This decision, which aroused great reaction on the European exchanges, began the process that would end six years later with the Decree of Muharrem and the founding of the Ottoman Public Debt Administration (Düyûn-ı Umûmiyye İdaresi).
