Suppiluliuma I ascended the Hittite throne; the New Kingdom period began

1349 BC

Suppiluliuma I is the founder of the Hittite New Kingdom and the creator of the professional chariot army. His thirty-five-year reign transformed the Hittites into an empire stretching from Anatolia to Syria.
