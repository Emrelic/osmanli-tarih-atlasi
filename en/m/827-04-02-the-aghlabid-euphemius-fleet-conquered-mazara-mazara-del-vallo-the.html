The Aghlabid–Euphemius fleet conquered Mazara (Mazara del Vallo): the conquest of Sicily began

2 April 827

After the Byzantine naval commander Euphemius rebelled against the governor Constantine Soudas and asked for aid from the Aghlabid emir Ziyadat Allah, a Muslim fleet of 10,000 men and more than seventy ships under Asad b. al-Furat came and joined Euphemius and conquered Mazara in 212 (827). The conquest of the island would last 75 years and be completed in 902.
