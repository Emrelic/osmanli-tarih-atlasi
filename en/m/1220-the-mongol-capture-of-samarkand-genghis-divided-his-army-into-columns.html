The Mongol capture of Samarkand; Genghis divided his army into columns and sent them to seize the subject provinces

1220

Samarkand, the strongest defensive centre of Transoxiana, passed into the hands of the Mongols (1220); Bukhara fell at the same time. Genghis divided his army into columns and charged them with seizing the provinces of the Khwarazm-Shah.
