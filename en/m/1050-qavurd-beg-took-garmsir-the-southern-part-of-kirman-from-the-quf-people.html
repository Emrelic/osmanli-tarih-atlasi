Qavurd Beg took Garmsir, the southern part of Kirman, from the Quf people and conquered all of Kirman; the emir of Hormuz became a vassal

1050

The south of Kirman, called Garmsir, was in the hands of the brigand Quf people; Qavurd Beg suppressed them in the eastern mountains, destroyed most of them, and took southern Kirman as well (442/1050); Badr Isa, emir of the island of Hormuz, became a vassal of the Seljuks, and Qavurd also conquered Oman.
