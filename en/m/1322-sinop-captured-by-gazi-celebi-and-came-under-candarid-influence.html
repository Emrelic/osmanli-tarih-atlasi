Sinop captured by Gazi Çelebi and came under Candarid influence

1322

With the weakening of Ilkhanid rule, Gazi Çelebi, of the founding family of the Candaroğlu (İsfendiyaroğlu) beylik, which had grown strong in the Kastamonu and Sinop region, captured Sinop around 1322 and made the city a port center of the beylik.
