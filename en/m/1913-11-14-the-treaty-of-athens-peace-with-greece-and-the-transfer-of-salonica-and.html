The Treaty of Athens: Peace with Greece and the Transfer of Salonica and Ioannina

14 November 1913

The treaty signed between the Ottoman State and Greece officially recognised the passing to Greece of Salonica, Ioannina and Southern Macedonia, lost in the Balkan War, and gave up the Ottoman rights over Crete. The religious and property rights of the Muslims remaining in Greece, the status of the pious foundations and the appointment of muftis were regulated in detail. The status of the Aegean islands, however, was left unresolved and remained a source of constant tension between the two states.
