The liberation of Ardahan and Artvin

23 February 1921

After the Treaty of Gyumri, the Eastern Front turned to the part of the Three Provinces (elviye-i selâse) that was in Georgian hands. According to the TDV article on Ardahan, at the end of the struggle against Georgian and Armenian bands, the sancak of Ardahan was liberated together with Artvin on 23 February 1921. The article on Kâzım Karabekir writes that with this move the lands of the Three Provinces, other than the districts of Çürüksu, Acara and Batum, were recovered.
