Maslama began the siege of Constantinople (15 August 717)

15 August 717

The Umayyad commander Maslama crossed the strait from Abydos and besieged Constantinople on 15 August 717 with an army of about 180,000 men and a great fleet. The Bulgar Khan Tervel helped Byzantium by attacking Arab forces in Thrace.
