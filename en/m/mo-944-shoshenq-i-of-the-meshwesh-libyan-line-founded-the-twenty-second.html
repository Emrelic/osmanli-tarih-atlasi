Shoshenq I (of the Meshwesh/Libyan line) founded the Twenty-second Dynasty; the capital was Bubastis

944 BC

The generals of the Libyan Meshwesh, who had settled in the Delta after the New Kingdom, included Shoshenq I, who reunited Egypt after the Twenty-first Dynasty at Tanis. By making his son High Priest of Amun he also tied Thebes to the dynasty. This age brought a brief recovery that turned Egypt again toward foreign policy (the Canaanite campaign).
