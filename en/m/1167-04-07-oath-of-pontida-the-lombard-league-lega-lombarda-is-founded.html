Oath of Pontida: the Lombard League (Lega Lombarda) is founded

7 April 1167

On 7 April 1167, at the monastery of Pontida, the Lombard communes swore an oath of league against Barbarossa; cities such as Cremona, Bergamo, Brescia, Mantua and Milan took part.
