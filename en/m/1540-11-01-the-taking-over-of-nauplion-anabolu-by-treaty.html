The Taking Over of Nauplion (Anabolu) by Treaty

1 November 1540

The Venetian war, which lasted three years after Preveza, ended with the republic's consenting to give up its last two fortresses in the Morea. Nauplion (Anabolu) and Monemvasia (Benefşe), which could not be taken by siege, were handed over to the Ottomans at the table, in addition to a war indemnity of three hundred thousand ducats. The Morea peninsula thus became unified, with not a single Venetian footprint remaining.
