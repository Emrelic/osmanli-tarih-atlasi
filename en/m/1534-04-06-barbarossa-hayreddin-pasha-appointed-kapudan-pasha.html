Barbarossa Hayreddin Pasha appointed kapudan pasha

6 April 1534

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Barbaros Hayreddin Pasha, Hayreddin Pasha, known for his successes in Algiers, was summoned to Istanbul on 6 April 1534 and appointed kapudan pasha and beylerbey of Cezâyir-i Bahr-i Sefîd. This appointment is regarded as the beginning of the restructuring of the Ottoman navy and the institutionalisation of naval power in the Mediterranean.
