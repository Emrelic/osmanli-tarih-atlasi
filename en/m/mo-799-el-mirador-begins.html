El Mirador: begins

799 BC

In the Mirador Basin, primary midden deposition of the Middle Preclassic in the Trogón Group (800–400 BCE); in addition, the Danta pyramid complex (72 m) was built in the Late and Terminal Preclassic (350 BCE–250 CE).
