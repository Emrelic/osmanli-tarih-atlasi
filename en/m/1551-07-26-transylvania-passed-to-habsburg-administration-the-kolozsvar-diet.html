Transylvania passed to Habsburg administration: the Kolozsvár diet recognized Isabella's abdication

26 July 1551

Following Martinuzzi's agreement with Ferdinand, Queen Isabella, on behalf of the infant king John Sigismund, signed on 19 June 1551 at the fortress of Szászsebes the act of renunciation imposed upon her. The Transylvanian diet, meeting at Kolozsvár on 26 July, recognized this renunciation and the sovereignty of King Ferdinand; the Habsburg army of General Castaldo entered Weissenburg (Gyulafehérvár). Thus Transylvania, dependent on the Ottomans, passed to a Habsburg administration that would last five years.
