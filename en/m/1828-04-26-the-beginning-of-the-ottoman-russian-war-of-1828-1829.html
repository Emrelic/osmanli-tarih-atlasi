The Beginning of the Ottoman-Russian War of 1828-1829

26 April 1828

Upon the burning of the fleet at Navarino and the Sublime Porte's declaration that it did not recognise the Akkerman Convention, Russia declared war on two fronts at once. Since the Janissary Corps had only just been abolished and the new army was still in the process of being formed, the Ottoman defence was weak from the start; as the Russians crossed the Danube, they took Kars and Akhaltsikhe in the east. The Russian army, which within a year advanced as far as Edirne, would impose the heavy terms of the 1829 Treaty of Edirne and the independence of Greece.
