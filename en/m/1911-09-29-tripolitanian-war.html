Tripolitanian War

29 September 1911

Italy, in search of colonies, declared war on Tripolitania, the Ottomans' last land in North Africa, and occupied the coasts; since the state, lacking a navy, could not send regular troops, young officers such as Mustafa Kemal and Enver Bey crossed secretly into the region and organized an effective resistance with the Senussi tribes. With the Treaty of Ouchy signed upon the outbreak of the Balkan War (18 October 1912), Libya was ceded to Italy; Italy also occupied Rhodes and the Dodecanese.
