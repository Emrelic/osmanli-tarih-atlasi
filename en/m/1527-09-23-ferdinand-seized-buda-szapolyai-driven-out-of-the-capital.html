Ferdinand seized Buda: Szapolyai driven out of the capital

23 September 1527

After Mohács two kings had emerged on the Hungarian throne: some of the nobles had elected János Szapolyai, others the Habsburg Ferdinand. In 1527 Ferdinand seized Buda and drove Szapolyai out of the capital; Szapolyai asked Sultan Süleyman for help. This is why the first target of the 1529 Vienna campaign was Buda.
