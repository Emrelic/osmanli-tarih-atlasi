The surrender of Koroni — the completion of the Morea campaign

16 August 1715

With the surrender of the fortress of Koroni, which holds the mouth of the Gulf of Messenia, Venetian administration on the Morean peninsula came completely to an end. The peninsula, lost at Karlowitz, was thus wholly recovered only three months after the opening of the campaign. In the continuation of the same campaign, in September 1715, the island of Santa Maura (Lefkada) in the Ionian Sea was also taken from Venice. This speed forced Venice to bring Austria into the war and opened the road, three years later, to Passarowitz.
