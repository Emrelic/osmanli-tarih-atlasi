Dionysius I became tyrant of Syracuse

404 BC

In the years when Carthage took Acragas and Gela, Dionysius became strategos and then tyrant of Syracuse in 405; he made peace with Carthage (405) and bound the Greek cities of Sicily to himself. Dionysius's rule, which would last 38 years, made Sicily one of the great powers of the Mediterranean.
