The Emirate of Ilorin captured by the forces of the Royal Niger Company

16 February 1897

The Emirate of Ilorin, dependent on the Sokoto Caliphate, was captured in the February 1897 campaign of the forces of the Royal Niger Company under Sir George Goldie; the military power of the emirate was broken and the region effectively came under British influence (formal annexation was completed with the establishment of the Northern Nigeria Protectorate in 1900).
