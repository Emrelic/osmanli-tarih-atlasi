The disintegration of the Ilkhanate: the death of Abu Sa'id Bahadur Khan

1 December 1335

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on the İlhanlılar, Abu Sa'id's death without leaving an heir led to the fragmentation of the Ilkhanate, which had ruled Iran for a century; the emirs, taking advantage of the struggles for the throne, founded local dynasties such as the Jalayirids, the Qaraquyunlu, the Muzaffarids, the Sarbadars of Khorasan and the Eretnids. This disintegration divided the region into a mosaic of principalities that would last a century; it would ultimately be reunited by Timur's campaigns.
