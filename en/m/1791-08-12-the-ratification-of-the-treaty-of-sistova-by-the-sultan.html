The ratification of the Treaty of Sistova by the sultan

12 August 1791

The Treaty of Sistova, signed on 4 August 1791, was ratified on 12 August. The ratification came the day after the armistice with Russia was signed: the Ottoman State had legally ended the fighting on both fronts within the same week.
