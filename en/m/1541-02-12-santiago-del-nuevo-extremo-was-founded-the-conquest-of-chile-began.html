Santiago del Nuevo Extremo was founded — the conquest of Chile began

12 February 1541

Pedro de Valdivia, marching south from Peru, founded Santiago del Nuevo Extremo in the Mapocho valley. In September of the same year the town was burned in a native attack led by Michimalonco and was refounded; nevertheless, the Spanish conquest of Chile was directed from this point.
