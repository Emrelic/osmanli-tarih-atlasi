King William I dies in Palermo

7 May 1166

William I, nicknamed 'the Bad', died in Palermo on the afternoon of 7 May 1166 at the age of 46; his twelve-year-old son William II came to the throne under the regency of his mother Margaret. Roger's African conquests had been lost in this period.
