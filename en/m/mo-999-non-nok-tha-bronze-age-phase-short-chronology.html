Non Nok Tha: Bronze Age phase (short chronology)

999 BC

A Bronze Age cemetery in northeastern Thailand excavated by Bayard in the 1960s; in the redating by Higham et al. 2015, the bronze burials extend from the 9th century BCE (MP 3: 840-800 BCE) to the 8th-6th centuries BCE.
