The second fall of Ibrail — Tsar Nicholas before the fortress

23 June 1828

Ibrail, which had been returned to the Ottomans in 1812 and re-fortified in 1817-1818, was besieged in April with 4000 guards and 278 cannon inside. Its commander Ebubekir Pasha set up a new defence line; Tsar Nicholas I himself came before the fortress at the end of May. The city came under Russian occupation, but would be returned with the Treaty of Edirne and left to the principality of Wallachia.
