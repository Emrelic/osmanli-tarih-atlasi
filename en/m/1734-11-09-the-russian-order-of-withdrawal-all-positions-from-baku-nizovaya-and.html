The Russian order of withdrawal — all positions from Baku, Nizovaya and the Kura to Derbent were to be evacuated

9 November 1734

The order of Empress Anna directed the Russian forces to withdraw first from Baku and the fortress of Nizovaya to Derbent, and Derbent and the remaining provinces were also to be evacuated when the season permitted. Nadir's pressure had borne fruit before the treaty.
