Death of István Bocskai — the county of Szatmár returned to the crown

29 December 1606

The prince of Transylvania, István Bocskai, died at Kassa on 29 December 1606. The Peace of Vienna (23 June 1606) had left him the counties, including Szatmár, for life, to return to the crown if there was no male heir; by this provision the county of Szatmár passed back to the Habsburg Hungarian kingdom. Transylvania entered a search for a new prince.
