The accession of Bayezid II in Istanbul

22 May 1481

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Bayezid II, Prince Bayezid, the sancak bey of Amasya, upon the news of his father's death came quickly to Istanbul and ascended the throne; with the support of the Janissaries he got ahead of his brother Cem, who had set out from Konya. At the accession, the accession gratuity customary for the Janissaries was distributed. This change of throne also lit the fuse of the struggle between the two brothers.
