Aqquyunlu vanguard forces took Bingöl fortress from the Qaraqoyunlu

1 October 1467

In the last weeks of Cihan Shah's campaign against Uzun Hasan, Aqquyunlu vanguard forces seized Bingöl fortress; with Cihan Shah's killing on 10 November 1467, Qaraqoyunlu rule in the region came to an end.
