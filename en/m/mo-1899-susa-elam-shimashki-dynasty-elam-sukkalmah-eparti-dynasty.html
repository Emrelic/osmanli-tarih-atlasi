Susa: Elam — Shimashki Dynasty → Elam — Sukkalmah (Eparti) Dynasty

1899 BC

At Susa, ownership passes from the Elam — Shimashki Dynasty to the Elam — Sukkalmah (Eparti) Dynasty.
