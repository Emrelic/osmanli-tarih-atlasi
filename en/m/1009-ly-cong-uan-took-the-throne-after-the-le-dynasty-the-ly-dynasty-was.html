Lý Công Uẩn took the throne after the Lê dynasty; the Lý dynasty was founded (1009-1225)

1009

Within four years of Le Hoan's death, Lý Công Uẩn, who had been raised by monks and had made his career at the Hoa Lư court, became king and moved the court from Hoa Lư to Đại La. Taylor gives Lý Công Uẩn's reign as 1009-1028; the Song recognized him as a vassal in 1010.
