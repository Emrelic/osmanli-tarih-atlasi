The 1723 Iranian Campaign: entry into Shirvan and Georgia

1 August 1723

When the Safavid State collapsed under the Afghan invasion, a campaign was launched to the east on the grounds of preventing Russia from descending onto the Caspian shores. The Erzurum army entered Tbilisi by way of Georgia, and the Shirvan column entered Shamakhi, retaking territories lost a hundred and thirty years earlier. This campaign was the Ottomans' last great advance in the Caucasus.
