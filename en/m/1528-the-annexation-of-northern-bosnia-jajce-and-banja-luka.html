The annexation of northern Bosnia: Jajce and Banja Luka

1528

When the Hungarian defensive line collapsed after Mohács, Gazi Husrev Bey, the sancak bey of Bosnia, marched into the Vrbas valley and took the last Bosnian fortresses remaining in the hands of the Hungarian kingdom. With the fall of Jajce and Banja Luka, the province of Bosnia reached the Sava River in the north, and the Croatian front was born. Banja Luka soon became the province's second centre.
