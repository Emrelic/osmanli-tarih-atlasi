The defeat at Stavuchany and the surrender of Khotin to the Russians (Ottoman–Russian War of 1736–1739)

28 August 1739

In the campaign of 1739 the main Russian army under Field Marshal Münnich passed through Polish territory and entered Moldavia, and at Stavuchany near Khotin inflicted a heavy defeat on the army of the serasker Veli Pasha. Immediately after this battle the fortress of Khotin surrendered to the Russians, and Russian forces entered Iași at the beginning of September. However, when Austria made a separate peace, the war ended with the Treaty of Belgrade signed in the same month.
