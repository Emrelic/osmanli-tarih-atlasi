The Aynalıkavak Tenkihnâme — A Provisional Settlement over Crimea

21 March 1779

The two states, brought to the brink of war over who would take the head of the Crimean khanate, signed an explanatory text at the Aynalıkavak Pavilion through the mediation of France. Russia agreed to withdraw its troops from Crimea, and the Ottoman State to recognise the khanate of the pro-Russian Şahin Giray; the sultan's religious right of caliphate over Crimea was confirmed. This fragile balance, which would last four years, would collapse completely with Russia's direct annexation of the peninsula in 1783.
