Renewed annexation of the western Anatolian principalities: Menteşe and Aydın

1 June 1425

The two Aegean principalities re-established during the Interregnum were definitively annexed to Ottoman lands in the time of Murad II. According to the TDV entry on the Menteşeids, Murad II took the Menteşe province in 827 (1424) and had the two brothers at the head of the principality imprisoned. According to the entry on the Aydınids, Hamza Bey, the Beylerbeyi of Anatolia, had Cüneyd Bey, who had again withdrawn his obedience, seized and executed together with his family (829/1425-26), and the Aydın province passed to the Ottomans. The Knights of St. John, who had held the harbour fortress of Izmir since 1344, had lost it to Timur in 1402, and the region had returned to Aydınid control; with this annexation it too was joined to the Ottomans. The Teke province (Antalya) is not the subject of this entry: the Tekeids had been liquidated separately in 1423. Germiyan, on the other hand, would be annexed by testament in 1429.
