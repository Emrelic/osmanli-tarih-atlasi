Antigonus Gonatas became king of Macedonia: the Antigonid dynasty was established

275 BC

Gonatas gained fame by defeating the Galatians at Lysimachia in 277 and settled on the Macedonian throne in 276. The dynasty ruled Macedonia until 168; in Greece it held the fortresses of Corinth, Chalcis and the Piraeus.
