Grand Principality of Tver annexed to Moscow — Ivan III took the city

12 September 1485

Following the siege by the Muscovite army, the last Grand Prince of Tver, Mikhail Borisovich, fled to Lithuania and Tver surrendered to Ivan III; Moscow's strongest rival was thus eliminated.
