Kim Bang-gyeong and the Mongol commanders crushed the Sambyeolcho base at Jindo; the false king Seung-hwa-hu On was killed

23 June 1271

The center of the Sambyeolcho (elite guard unit) rebellion at Jindo was taken in 1271 by an attack in three columns under Kim Bang-gyeong, Hindu (Xindu) and Hong Da-gu; the false king Seung-hwa-hu On was killed, and the survivors fled to Jeju (Tamna).
