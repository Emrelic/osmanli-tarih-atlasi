The Serbian occupation of Pécs (Peçuy) ended — the Hungarian National Army entered the city

22 August 1921

Serbian troops began to evacuate the region on 18 August 1921; on 20-21 August the administration of the city and province was handed over to representatives of the Hungarian government, and on 22 August units of the Hungarian National Army entered the city.
