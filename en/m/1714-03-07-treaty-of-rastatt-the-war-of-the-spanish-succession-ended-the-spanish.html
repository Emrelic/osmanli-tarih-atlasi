Treaty of Rastatt — the War of the Spanish Succession ended; the Spanish Netherlands and Milan passed to Austria

7 March 1714

The Treaty of Rastatt, which ended the War of the Spanish Succession between France and Austria, was signed on 7 March 1714 (Utrecht in 1713 had settled matters between Britain and France); by the treaty the Austrian Habsburgs took over the Spanish Netherlands (present-day Belgium) and Naples, Milan and Sardinia in Italy. This was the greatest territorial expansion in Austria's history, and it added to the weight of the Habsburg dynasty in Central Europe a new front in Western and Southern Europe.
