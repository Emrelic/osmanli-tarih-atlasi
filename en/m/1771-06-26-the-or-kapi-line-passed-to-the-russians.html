The Or Kapı line passed to the Russians

26 June 1771

The Russian army under Dolgorukov, in a night attack, scaled the left flank of the Or Kapı line with ladders. At the same time Prozorovsky's cavalry crossed the shallows of the Sivash and came out behind the line and dispersed the Crimean-Ottoman cavalry. The next day the fortress garrison surrendered; two days later the Russian army marched into the interior of the peninsula.
