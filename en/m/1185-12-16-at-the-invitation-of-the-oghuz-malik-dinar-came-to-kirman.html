At the invitation of the Oghuz, Malik Dinar came to Kirman

16 December 1185

Malik Dinar, one of the well-known Oghuz begs of Khurasan, came to Kirman at the invitation of the Oghuz on 21 Ramadan 581 (16 December 1185), took command of the Oghuz and brought some cities under his rule; Muhammad Shah went to Iraq to obtain help and never returned.
