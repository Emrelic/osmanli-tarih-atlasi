Kanûn-ı Esâsî — First Constitutional Era

23 December 1876

On the day the international conference that had gathered in Istanbul to discuss the Balkan crisis opened, the first Ottoman constitution was proclaimed with cannon salutes; with the Kanûn-ı Esâsî, the work of the 'Young Ottomans' movement led by Midhat Pasha, a two-chamber parliament was established. The Meclis-i Meb'ûsan opened in March 1877; however, Abdülhamid II, citing the War of '93, adjourned the parliament indefinitely in February 1878, and the constitution remained suspended for thirty years.
