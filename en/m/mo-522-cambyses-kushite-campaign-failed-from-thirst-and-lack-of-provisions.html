Cambyses' Kushite campaign failed from thirst and lack of provisions; Egypt's southern border remained at Elephantine

522 BC

After Egypt, Cambyses planned a campaign against the 'Long-lived Ethiopians' (Kush); according to Herodotus, the king of Kush sent his bow to the Persian envoys and told them to attack 'when they could draw the bow with ease'. The army ran out of provisions in the desert and turned back. Strabo also mentions the place where Cambyses' army perished in a storm in the desert. Kush remained independent; the Persians' southern border stopped at Elephantine.
