Yergöğü passes to Wallachia

1427

The fortress of Yergöğü (Giurgiu) on the Danube bank was retaken by the Wallachians in 1427. The fortress, one of the Ottomans' first footholds on the northern bank of the Danube, remained in Wallachia for more than twenty years.
