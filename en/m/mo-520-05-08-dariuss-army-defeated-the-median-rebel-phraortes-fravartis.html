Darius's army defeated the Median rebel Phraortes (Fravartiš)

8 May 520 BC

The army of Fravartiš, who had proclaimed himself king in Media as a descendant of Cyaxares, was defeated; while fleeing, Fravartiš was captured at Rhagae (near Tehran) and put to death under torture. Media became a Persian province again.
