Veii fell to Rome: the first great city of Etruria in the south was lost

395 BC

After a ten-year siege Camillus took Veii (tradition: 396 BC); its people were enslaved and its lands were added to Rome, almost doubling Rome's ager Romanus. The power of the Etruscan cities south of the Tiber was broken.
