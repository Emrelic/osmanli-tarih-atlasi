Ebussuud Efendi became şeyhülislam

1 October 1545

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Ebüssuûd Efendi, Ebussuud Efendi, who had served for eight years as kazasker of Rumelia, was appointed to the şeyhülislamate in Sha'ban 952 (October 1545) and remained in this office for twenty-eight years and eleven months. Ebussuud Efendi, known for his fatwas that brought the kanunnames of Süleyman the Lawgiver into harmony with the sharia, played a decisive role in the shaping of the legal and educational institutions of his time.
