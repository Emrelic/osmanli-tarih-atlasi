Mithridates I ascended the Parthian throne; the era of Parthian imperial expansion began

170 BC

Mithridates I transformed Parthia into an empire: he took Media (147) and Babylonia and Seleucia (141), and established his sovereignty over Elymais and Persis. Ctesiphon would later become the winter capital.
