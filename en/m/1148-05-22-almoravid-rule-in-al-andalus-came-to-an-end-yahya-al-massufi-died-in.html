Almoravid rule in al-Andalus came to an end (Yahya al-Massufi died in Granada)

22 May 1148

With the death in Granada (543/1148) of Yahya b. Ali al-Massufi, the governor of al-Andalus from the Banu Ghaniya, who was trying to re-establish Almoravid rule, Almoravid rule in al-Andalus came to an end.
