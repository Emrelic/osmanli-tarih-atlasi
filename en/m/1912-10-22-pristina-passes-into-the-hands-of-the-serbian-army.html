Pristina passes into the hands of the Serbian army

22 October 1912

The Serbian army, which entered Kosovo in the First Balkan War, took Pristina, and a policy of re-Serbianization began in the city. The fall of Pristina is one of the first links in the disintegration of Ottoman resistance in the vilayet of Kosovo.
