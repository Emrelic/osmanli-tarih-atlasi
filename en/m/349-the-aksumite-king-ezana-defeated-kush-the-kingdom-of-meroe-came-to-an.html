The Aksumite king Ezana defeated Kush; the Kingdom of Meroe came to an end (c. AD 350)

349

The Aksumite king Ezana attacked the kingdom of Kush, which had been weakened by pressure from the Noba and the Blemmyes; Ezana's inscriptions describe his campaign against the Noba and Kasu (Kush) peoples, and two fragmentary Greek inscriptions commemorating the Aksumite victory have been found at Meroe. The last pyramids at Meroe are dated to the middle of the fourth century; the Kushite state disintegrated and split into the kingdoms of Nobatia, Makuria and Alodia.
