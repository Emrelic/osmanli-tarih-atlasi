The Ankara Agreement (İtilâfnâme): Peace with France and the Closing of the Southern Front

20 October 1921

With the agreement signed in Ankara with France after the victory of Sakarya, the war on the southern front ended; the French forces evacuated Çukurova and the Southeast, including Adana, Antep, Maraş and Urfa, and withdrew to the Syrian frontier. One of the Entente states thus effectively recognised the Ankara government, and the first crack opened in the Sèvres front. The arms taken over from the evacuated regions and the units freed played a critical role in the preparation of the Great Offensive.
