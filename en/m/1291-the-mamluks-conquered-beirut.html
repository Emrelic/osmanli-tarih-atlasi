The Mamluks conquered Beirut

1291

Beirut, which had remained in the hands of the Crusaders until 1291, was conquered on this date in the name of the Mamluk Sultan al-Malik al-Ashraf Khalil by the Governor of Damascus, Emir Sanjar Abu Shuja'i.
