The surrender of Qurna to the British

9 December 1914

The forces of Subhi Bey, the governor of Basra, who had withdrawn to Qurna, where the Tigris and the Euphrates meet, after the fall of Basra, surrendered on the morning of 9 December 1914, after the clashes of 4-8 December.
