The loss of Sinj Fortress to Venice: the interior of Dalmatia

30 September 1686

During the Morean War, Venetian forces took the fortress of Sinj in the interior of Dalmatia from the Ottomans in 1686; the fortress also withstood the Ottoman siege of 1715 and remained with Venice.
