Puzur-Inshushinak, the last Awan king in Elam (Susa), freed himself from Akkadian overlordship

2099 BC

Puzur-Inshushinak, the last king of the Awan dynasty, united Susa and the Elamite highland during the collapse of the Akkadian state, broke free of Akkadian overlordship and left inscriptions in "Linear Elamite" script as well as cuneiform. With the rise of Ur-Nammu, Elam came under Mesopotamian control again.
