Habsburg administration ended in Transylvania: Transylvania again became a voivodeship dependent on the Ottomans

12 March 1556

Because János Zsigmond was a minor, the Habsburgs tried in 1551 to take control of Transylvania by the hand of the Croatian-born priest Martinuzzi (Fráter György), appointed his guardian; Gyulafehérvár, Kolozsvár, Brassó and Segesvár remained under Habsburg administration for five years. The Ottomans prevented this situation, and Transylvania again became a tributary voivodeship dependent on them. Thereafter, in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries, Transylvania for about 150 years maintained a status based on broad autonomy in internal affairs and dependence on Istanbul in external affairs.
