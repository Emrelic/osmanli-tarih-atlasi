Fatimid rule in Egypt ended, the Ayyubids came to power — Alexandria, the Fayyum and Mecca (Hejaz)

13 September 1171

With Saladin's vizierate and the death of the Fatimid caliph al-ʿĀḍid (567/1171), Egypt passed to the Ayyubids; Alexandria and the Fayyum came under Ayyubid administration. The day is not in the source — it was taken over from the end date of the atlas's Fatimid entry (that date is not itself a source). The Hejaz also came under Ayyubid administration; the suzerainty of Mecca passed from the Fatimids to the Ayyubids.
