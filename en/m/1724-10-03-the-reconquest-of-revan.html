The reconquest of Revan

3 October 1724

After a hard siege lasting ninety days, Revan, the key fortress of the Aras valley, again came under Ottoman administration. The fortress, built in 1583 and lost in 1604, was this time fortified with a stronger garrison. The length of the siege was an indication that the Ottoman army no longer advanced easily. The nahiyes of Karpi (Üçkilise), Şerür, Maku and Şüregel were also attached to the province of Revan.
