The Umayyad state came to an end: Mecca passed to Abbasid rule

5 August 750

The last Umayyad caliph, Marwān II, was killed on 26 Dhū'l-Ḥijja 132 / 5 August 750; Mecca entered the Abbasid governorship of the Haramayn (no day specific to Mecca — the day of the end of the state, declared approximation).
