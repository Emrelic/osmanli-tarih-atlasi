The Archbishopric of Nidaros is established; Greenland is also joined to the Norwegian ecclesiastical province (1152/53)

1152

The foundation of the Archbishopric of Nidaros was laid during the stay of the English cardinal Nicholas Breakspear in Trondheim in 1152/53; in 1153 the bishopric of Greenland was also incorporated into the Norwegian ecclesiastical province.
