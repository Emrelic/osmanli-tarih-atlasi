The dismissal and exile of Midhat Pasha from the grand vizierate

5 February 1877

Grand Vizier Midhat Pasha, the architect of the Kanun-i Esasi, six weeks after its proclamation was dismissed by Abdülhamid II and exiled abroad within one night. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Midhat Pasha, the dismissal is linked to the sultan's discomfort with the provisions in the constitution restricting his own powers. The pasha went first to Naples, then to various cities of Europe.
