Birth of Hammamizade Ismail Dede Efendi

9 January 1778

Hammamizade Ismail Dede Efendi, one of the most prolific composers of Turkish music, was born on 9 January 1778 in the Şehzadebaşı quarter of Istanbul. In his lifetime he composed more than 500 works; about 294 of these have survived to the present day.
