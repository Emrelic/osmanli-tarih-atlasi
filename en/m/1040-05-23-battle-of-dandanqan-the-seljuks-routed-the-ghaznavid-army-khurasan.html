Battle of Dandanqan: the Seljuks routed the Ghaznavid army; Khurasan slipped out of Ghaznavid hands

23 May 1040

Sultan Mas'ud with his army of 50,000 men met the Seljuks before the fortress of Dandanqan near Merv; with the defection of a group from the Ghazna Turkish household army to the Seljuks, the Ghaznavid army was routed after three days of fighting (Friday, 8 Ramadan 431 / 23 May 1040). Mas'ud fled with an entourage of 100; Khurasan passed to the Seljuks.
