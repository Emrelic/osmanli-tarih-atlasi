Süleyman b. Kutalmış was defeated and killed at Aynüseylem; Antioch left the Anatolian Seljuks

4 June 1086

Süleyman b. Kutalmış, who had taken Antioch from Byzantium in 1084, was killed in the battle of Aynüseylem that he fought with Tutuş, the ruler of the Syrian Seljuks; the city passed to the control of the Great Seljuk Sultan Malik-Shah, and Yağısıyan was appointed governor.
