Conquest of Bilecik and Yarhisar by night raid

1299

TDV, Bilecik entry: 'Although the other tekfurs of the region, among them the tekfur of Bilecik, prepared an assassination of Osman Gazi, when this plot was learned of the Ottoman forces conquered Yarhisar and Bilecik by a sudden raid (1299).' The two fortresses were taken in a single operation, in the same night raid. This date is the turning point accepted as the year of the founding of the Ottoman State. Other places joined on the same date: İnegöl.
