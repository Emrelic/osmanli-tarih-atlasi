The Sarıkamış Operation: Catastrophe on the Caucasus Front

22 December 1914

Enver Pasha, in order to retake Kars-Ardahan-Batum, lost in 1878, and to rouse the Turks in the Caucasus, drove the Third Army in winter over the Allahuekber mountains in a manoeuvre to encircle the Russian forces. Because of inadequate winter equipment, exhausted supplies and blizzards reaching minus thirty degrees, the army melted away before even entering battle; tens of thousands of soldiers froze to death. The operation became one of the heaviest catastrophes of the Ottomans in the war and left the eastern front defenceless for two years.
