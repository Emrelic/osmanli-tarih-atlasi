Conquest of Baghdad — the Campaign of the Two Iraqs

4 December 1534

In the campaign organized against the 'Two Iraqs' (Arab Iraq and Persian Iraq), Tabriz was entered first, then Baghdad without fighting; the capture of the old capital of the Abbasids earned the Ottoman ruler great prestige in the Islamic world. The tomb of Imam-ı Azam Abu Hanifa was restored; with the annexation of Baghdad and Basra, Ottoman lands reached the Persian Gulf. Other places annexed on the same date: Kirkuk, Karbala.
