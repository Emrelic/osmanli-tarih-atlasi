Grand Prince Vladimir dies at Vyshhorod

15 July 1015

Vladimir died on 15 July 1015 at Vyshhorod near Kiev while preparing a campaign against his son Yaroslav; Sviatopolk I briefly ascended the throne of Kiev, and a war of succession with Yaroslav began (Liubech 1015).
