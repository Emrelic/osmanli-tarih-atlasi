The Loss of Klis to Venice

31 March 1648

On the Dalmatian front of the Cretan (Candia) War, the counter-offensive of the Venetian commander Leonardo Foscolo also encompassed Klis, which had been a sancak centre since 1537; the fortress passed to Venice. The Ottomans' concentrating all their strength on Crete made it impossible to retake this loss on the Dalmatian frontier — Klis would never return to Ottoman administration; the sancak centre was moved to Livno.
