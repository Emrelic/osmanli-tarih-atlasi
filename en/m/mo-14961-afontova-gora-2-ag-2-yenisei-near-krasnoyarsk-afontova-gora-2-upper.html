Afontova Gora-2 (AG-2), Yenisei, near Krasnoyarsk: Afontova Gora-2: Upper Paleolithic Siberian settlement (off the horizon)

14961 BC

Individual AG-2 of the Upper Paleolithic Afontova Gora complex on the Yenisei; a genetic signature similar to MA-1 — shows that Siberia was inhabited during the Last Glacial Maximum.
