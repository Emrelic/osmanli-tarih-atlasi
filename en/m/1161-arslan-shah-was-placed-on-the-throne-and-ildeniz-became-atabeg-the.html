Arslan Shah was placed on the throne and Ildeniz became atabeg; the sultanate remained in practice in the shadow of the atabegs

1161

When Sulayman Shah too was found incompetent, Arslan Shah, son of Sultan Tughrul, who ruled Arran and part of Azerbaijan and was at Ildeniz's side, was placed on the throne (556/1161) and Ildeniz became atabeg; the age of the atabegs began among the Iraq Seljuks.
