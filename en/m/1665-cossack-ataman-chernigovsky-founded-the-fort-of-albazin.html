Cossack ataman Chernigovsky founded the fort of Albazin

1665

The fort of Albazin was founded in 1665 by the Cossack ataman N. R. Chernigovsky on the site of the fortified winter quarters destroyed in 1651 by Khabarov's detachment; the winter quarters had stood on the land of the settlement of Yaksa, in the domain of the Daur prince Albazi.
