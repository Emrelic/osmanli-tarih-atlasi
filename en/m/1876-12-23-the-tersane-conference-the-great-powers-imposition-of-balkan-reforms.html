The Tersane Conference: The Great Powers' Imposition of Balkan Reforms

23 December 1876

The conference that the great powers convened in Istanbul to resolve the Balkan crisis demanded the division of Bulgaria into two autonomous vilayets and the application of reforms under international supervision in Bosnia-Herzegovina. On the day the conference opened, the Sublime Porte announced the proclamation of the Kanûn-ı Esâsî with cannon salvos, trying to meet the impositions with a constitutional reform. The delegates regarded this as stalling and dispersed on 20 January 1877; four months later Russia declared war.
