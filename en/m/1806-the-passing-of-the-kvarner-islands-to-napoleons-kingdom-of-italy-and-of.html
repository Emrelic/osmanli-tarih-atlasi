The passing of the Kvarner islands to Napoleon's Kingdom of Italy and of the Bay of Kotor to Russian administration

1806

After the Treaty of Pressburg, the Kvarner islands — unlike Dalmatia — were attached not directly to France but to Napoleon's Kingdom of Italy; they entered the Illyrian Provinces only in 1809. In the Bay of Kotor, Austrian administration ended and Russian administration was established between 1806 and 1807; the Bay passed to France only in 1807.
