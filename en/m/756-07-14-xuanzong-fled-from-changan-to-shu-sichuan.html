Xuanzong fled from Chang'an to Shu (Sichuan)

14 July 756

Three days after Tongguan fell (甲午), Xuanzong issued an edict saying 'I am going on campaign in person'; the next day (乙未), before dawn, he left by the Yanqiu Gate. A light rain was falling; with him were only the chancellors Yang Guozhong and Wei Jiansu, the eunuch Gao Lishi, the crown prince and some of the princes; the rest could not keep up.
