Jalal al-Din Khwarazmshah reconquered Kars and its surroundings

2 January 1226

Jalal al-Din Khwarazmshah's reconquest of Kars and its surroundings (623/1226), when the region was in the hands of the Georgians, aroused rejoicing in the Islamic world; after his death the city again passed into the hands of the Georgians.
