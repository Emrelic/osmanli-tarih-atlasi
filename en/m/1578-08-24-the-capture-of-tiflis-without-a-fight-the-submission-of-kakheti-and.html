The capture of Tiflis without a fight — the submission of Kakheti and Imereti

24 August 1578

The Ottoman army under Lala Mustafa Pasha, advancing after the victory of Çıldır, reached Tiflis on 24 August and took the evacuated fortress without a fight. After the conquest of Tiflis the rulers of Imereti and Kakheti announced their submission to the Ottomans.
