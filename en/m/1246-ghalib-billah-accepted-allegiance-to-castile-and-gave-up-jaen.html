Ghalib-Billah accepted allegiance to Castile and gave up Jaén

1246

Muhammad I, the founder of the Nasrids, being helpless in the defence of Jaén, signed a treaty on condition of recognizing the sovereignty of the King of Castile, giving up Jaén and some fortresses, and paying an annual tribute (643/1246).
