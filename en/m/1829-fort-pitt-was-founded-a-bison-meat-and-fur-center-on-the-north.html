Fort Pitt was founded — a bison meat and fur center on the North Saskatchewan

1829

The Hudson's Bay Company founded Fort Pitt on the bank of the North Saskatchewan in order to close the gap between Fort Carlton and Fort Edmonton. The post became one of the main centers of pemmican supply and a nodal point of trade between the Plains Cree and the company.
