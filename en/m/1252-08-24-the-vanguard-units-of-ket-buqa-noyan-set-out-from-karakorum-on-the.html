The vanguard units of Ket-Buqa Noyan set out from Karakorum on the western campaign

24 August 1252

By decision of the quriltai of Mongke Khan, Hulagu was assigned the tasks of eliminating the Isma'ilis, subjugating the Abbasid caliphate, and seizing Egypt and Syria; the vanguard units of Ket-Buqa Noyan set out on 24 August 1252.
