The second Greek offensive — the occupation of Bilecik, Afyon and Adapazarı

23 March 1921

The Greek army, which landed new forces at Izmir after the defeat at the First İnönü, went on the offensive again before the period granted by the London Conference had expired. According to the TDV article on the National Struggle, the Greeks occupied Bilecik and Afyon on 23 March. In the north Adapazarı too came under Greek occupation on 25 March.
