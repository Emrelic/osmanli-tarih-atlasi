Süleyman Çelebi – Byzantine treaty: return of Thessaloniki

1 June 1403

Süleyman Çelebi, who ruled Rumelia during the Interregnum, by agreement with Byzantium and the Latin states ceded Thessaloniki and the Black Sea coasts as far as Mesembria to Byzantium (TDV fetret-devri: Treaty of Gallipoli, February 1403) — on the map İğneada, Rezve and Ahtapolu pass to Byzantium; on the map Thessaloniki leaves the Ottomans at this date.
