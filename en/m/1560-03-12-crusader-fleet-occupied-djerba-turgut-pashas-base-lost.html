Crusader fleet occupied Djerba: Turgut Pasha's base lost

12 March 1560

The Crusader fleet, made up of Spanish, Papal, Maltese, Genoese and Florentine ships, targeted Djerba, which Turgut Reis had used as a raiding base since 1551. Because of storms and an epidemic it could reach the island only in early 1560; on 12 March 1560 it occupied the island and built a fortress. On receiving the news, Piyale Pasha set out from Istanbul on 28 March with his fleet of 120 galleys. The occupation did not last long: the Ottoman fleet defeated the allies before Djerba in May, and the fortress was retaken at the end of July. This expedition is the beginning of the second great naval operation, after Preveza, that consolidated Ottoman supremacy in the Mediterranean.
