Ibn Battuta goes to Constantinople with the caravan of a Golden Horde princess

5 July 1332

Ibn Battuta, who toured the Anatolian beyliks and crossed from Sinop to Crimea, joins the caravan of Bayalun, wife of Özbek Khan, and arrives at the Byzantine capital Constantinople; he meets Andronikos III.
