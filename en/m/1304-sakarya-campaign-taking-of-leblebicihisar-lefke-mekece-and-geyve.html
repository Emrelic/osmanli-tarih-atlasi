Sakarya campaign: taking of Leblebicihisar, Lefke, Mekece and Geyve

1304

TDV, Osman I entry gives the order of the Sakarya campaign of 704 (1304): 'When he came to Leblebüci Castle, the tekfur submitted... He arrived at Lefke... He reached Mekece... He went to Geyve and took the castle, which he found empty.' Lefke is the gate of the Sakarya valley opening toward Bilecik. This single campaign, by bringing the chain of fortresses extending along the valley under Ottoman control, closed the eastern flank of the Nicaea basin.
