The taking of Kermanshah — the gate of the Zagros

1 October 1723

The Baghdad army crossed the passes of the Zagros and seized Kermanshah, regarded as the western gate of Iran. The city lay on the caravan and army road from Baghdad to Isfahan, and its capture secured the eastern safety of the province of Iraq. This advance began a race for partition between the Ottomans and Russia in the dissolving Safavid land.
