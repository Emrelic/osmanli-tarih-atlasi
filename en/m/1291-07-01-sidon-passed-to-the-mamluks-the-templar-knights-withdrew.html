Sidon passed to the Mamluks (the Templar knights withdrew)

1 July 1291

On the conquest of Acre (690/1291), the Templar knights who left it took refuge in Sidon; the city fell into the hands of the Mamluks in Rajab 690 / July 1291, and its walls were demolished.
