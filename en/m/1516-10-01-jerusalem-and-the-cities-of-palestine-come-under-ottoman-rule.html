Jerusalem and the cities of Palestine come under Ottoman rule

1 October 1516

After the victory of Marj Dabiq, with Selim the Grim's arrival at Damascus on 27 September, the cities of Syria and Palestine passed one after another under Ottoman rule. TDV states that Nablus came under Ottoman rule in the autumn of 1516 and Jerusalem probably in October 1516, before the sultan's arrival. The sultan came to Jerusalem only after the battle of Gaza, on 29 December 1516.
