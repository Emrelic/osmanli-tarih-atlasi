Treaty of Orvieto: Anjou, Venice and the Latin Emperor Philip formed an alliance against Byzantium

3 July 1281

Under the protection of Pope Martin IV, Charles of Anjou concluded on 3 July 1281 at Orvieto an alliance treaty against Byzantium with Philip (son of Baldwin II), who bore the title of Latin Emperor, and the Republic of Venice. The Sicilian Vespers (1282) left this plan unfinished.
