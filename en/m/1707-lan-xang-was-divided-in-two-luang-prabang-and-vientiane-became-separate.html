Lan Xang was divided in two: Luang Prabang and Vientiane became separate kingdoms

1707

In 1700 Sai-Ong-Hué took Vientiane with Vietnamese support and became king; the grandsons of Souligna-Vongsa, King-Kitsarat and Int'a-Som, with a force of 6,000 assembled at Sip-Song-Panas, drove Sai-Ong-Hué's brother T'ao-Nong out of Luang Prabang in 1707; Kitsarat was proclaimed king and announced that the north of Chieng Khane was a separate independent kingdom. Hall: from 1707 Luang Prabang and Vientiane are the capitals of two separate and hostile states.
