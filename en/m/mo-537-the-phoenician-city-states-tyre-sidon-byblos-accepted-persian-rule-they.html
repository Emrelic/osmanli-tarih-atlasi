The Phoenician city-states (Tyre, Sidon, Byblos) accepted Persian rule; they became the core of the Persian fleet

537 BC

With the fall of Babylon the Phoenician coast came under Persian rule within the satrapy of "Beyond the River" (Ebir-Nari). The Phoenician fleets became the backbone of Persian sea power; Sidon and Tyre retained the privilege of internal self-government.
