Baiju Noyan defeated Kaykaus II in the battle of Sultanhanı

14 October 1256

Sultan Kaykaus, who was in Antalya, set out when he learned that Baiju Noyan had entered Seljuk territory, but was defeated in the battle fought at Sultanhanı near Aksaray (654/1256); Kilij Arslan IV was placed on the throne at Konya (655/1257).
