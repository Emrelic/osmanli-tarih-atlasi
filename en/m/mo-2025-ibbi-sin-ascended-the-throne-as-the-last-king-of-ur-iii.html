Ibbi-Sin ascended the throne as the last king of Ur III

2025 BC

Ibbi-Sin (c. 2028-2004), as the successor of Shu-Sin, presided over the process of the state's disintegration: Amorite pressure, famine, the loss of Susa and the eastern provinces, and the independence of Ishbi-Erra at Isin. His reign ended with the sack of Ur by an Elamite/Simashki attack.
