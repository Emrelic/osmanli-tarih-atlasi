Mahmud made his second campaign into Ghur and brought the Afghans under obedience

27 April 1020

The second campaign into Ghur was made in 411 (1020); the Afghans, who lived in the mountains between the Indus and Ghazna and raided caravans, were also brought under obedience by a campaign in the same year, and teachers were appointed to the region.
