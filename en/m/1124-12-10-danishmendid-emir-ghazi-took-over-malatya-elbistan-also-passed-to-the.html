Danishmendid Emir Ghazi took over Malatya; Elbistan also passed to the Danishmendids

10 December 1124

After the death of Belek, Emir Ghazi, after a long siege, took Malatya from the Seljuk prince Tughrul Arslan; with the capture of the Malatya region, Elbistan also came under Danishmendid rule.
