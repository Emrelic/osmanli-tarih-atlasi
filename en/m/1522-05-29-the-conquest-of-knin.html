The conquest of Knin

29 May 1522

Through the raids in the years 927-928 (1521-1522) of Gazi Husrev Bey, the sancak bey of Bosnia, a series of Venetian fortresses, foremost among them Knin, Skradin and Ostrovica, passed to Ottoman administration; the frontier was pushed beyond the second mountain range. This chain of conquests laid the foundation of the sancak of Dalmatia, which would be completed fifteen years later with the capture of Klis.
