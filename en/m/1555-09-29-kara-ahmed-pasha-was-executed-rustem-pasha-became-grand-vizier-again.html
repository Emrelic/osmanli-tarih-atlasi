Kara Ahmed Pasha was executed, Rüstem Pasha became grand vizier again

29 September 1555

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam articles on Rüstem Pasha and Kara Ahmed Pasha, in place of Kara Ahmed Pasha, who was suddenly executed during a divan meeting on 13 Dhu al-Qa'da 962 (29 September 1555), Rüstem Pasha was made grand vizier for the second time the same day, with the support of the sultanas Hürrem and Mihrimah. The background of the incident is left open in the sources and has been assessed as the result of palace intrigues.
