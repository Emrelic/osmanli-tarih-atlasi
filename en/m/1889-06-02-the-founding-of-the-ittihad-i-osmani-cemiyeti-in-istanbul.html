The Founding of the İttihâd-ı Osmânî Cemiyeti in Istanbul

2 June 1889

Four students of the Military Medical School founded the İttihâd-ı Osmânî Cemiyeti as a secret organisation against the despotic regime; this body soon turned into the Committee of Union and Progress, which would gather all the opposing elements of the Young Turk movement under its roof. The cell-based organisation united with the exiles in Europe and the officers in Salonica. The cadre that would have the Second Constitutional Era proclaimed in 1908 and would rule the state alone after 1913 came out of this small student cell.
