Aššur-uballit I freed himself from Mitanni overlordship; Assyria became independent and a "great king"

1362 BC

Aššur, a vassal of Mitanni, gained its independence in the time of Aššur-uballit I by taking advantage of the blow the Hittites dealt Mitanni. The king wrote to the pharaoh of Egypt with the title "great king"; this begins the Middle Assyrian period.
