Definitive annexation of Kars to Ottoman lands — the linchpin of the eastern frontier

1537

After the destruction of the Aqquyunlu State by the Safavids, Kars had for a time remained in the hands of Sevündük Khan Qurchibashi of the Afshar Turkmens. When the fortresses of Pasin, Şüregel and Oltu were taken during Süleyman the Lawgiver's Campaign of the Two Iraqs (940/1534), it is highly probable that Kars Fortress was also attached to the Ottomans; the definitive annexation of the city, however, came three years later. The TDV's record is as follows: "Kars was definitively incorporated into Ottoman lands in 944 (1537) by Dulkadirli Mehmed Khan, who had entered Ottoman service." Because the city and fortress had been largely ruined by long struggles, a rapid repair was undertaken; while the fortress was being repaired in 955 (1548) the Safavids attacked the city and destroyed the fortress once more. Kars would thereafter for three hundred and forty years remain the lock of the Ottoman northeastern frontier, to be ceded to Russia after the war of 1877-78.
