Umur Bey took the harbour fortress of Izmir from the Genoese

1329

Umur Bey of the Aydınids besieged for two and a half years the harbour fortress of Izmir, which remained in Latin hands. The Genoese administrator of the fortress, Martino Zaccaria, surrendered the fortress and went to Chios.
