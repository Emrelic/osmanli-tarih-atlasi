The taking of Chios from the Zaccaria and its attachment to Byzantium

1329

Emperor Andronikos III removed the Zaccaria from the island and attached the island to Byzantium under the governorship of Benedetto III. The island will be occupied in 1346 by the Genoese Simone Vignoso.
