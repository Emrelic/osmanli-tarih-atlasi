Darius retook Babylon; Nidintu-Bêl (Nebuchadnezzar III) was captured and killed

18 December 521 BC

On Gaumata's death, Nidintu-Bêl proclaimed himself the son of Nabonidus and Nebuchadnezzar III and tore Babylon away from Persia. Darius's army crossed the Tigris on inflated skins on 13 December and routed the rebel army; Babylon was retaken on 18 December and Nidintu-Bêl was killed. The Elamite uprising at Susa was suppressed at this time.
