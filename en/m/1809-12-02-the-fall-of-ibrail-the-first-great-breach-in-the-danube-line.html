The fall of Ibrail — the first great breach in the Danube line

2 December 1809

In the Russian war that began in 1806, the fortress of Ibrail on the northern bank of the Danube was besieged and bombarded by Russian forces under Count Kamensky. Inside were 4100 infantry, 1100 cavalry and 260 cannon; the fortress surrendered to General Essen because of a shortage of food. The northern end of the Danube defence line was thus opened.
