The liberation of Uşak

1 September 1922

After the Battle of the Commander-in-Chief, the remnants of the Greek army withdrew to the west, in the direction of Izmir. According to the TDV article on Uşak, Uşak, which had been under occupation since 29 August 1920, was liberated on 1 September 1922 with the entry of the national forces into the city. About three-quarters of the city was burned in the fire set by the retreating occupation forces.
