Battle of İnceğiz — Çelebi Mehmed's first defeat in Rumelia

1 October 1412

Çelebi Mehmed, who crossed to Rumelia with Byzantine ships, engaged Musa Çelebi in battle at İnceğiz; Musa, with seven thousand Janissaries under his command, defeated Mehmed, and Mehmed was forced to return to Anatolia. This defeat turned Mehmed away from direct fighting toward a strategy of drawing the Rumelian frontier lords and the Serbian despotate to his own side. The ground for the victory of Çamurlu nine months later was prepared by this diplomacy.
