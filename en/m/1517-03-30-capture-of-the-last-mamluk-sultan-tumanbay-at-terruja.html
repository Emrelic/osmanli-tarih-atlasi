Capture of the last Mamluk sultan Tumanbay at Terrūja

30 March 1517

Tumanbay, who hid in the Nile delta after leaving Cairo, was denounced by the Bedouin who had given him refuge and was seized in the Terrūja region. His capture eliminated the organized centre of Mamluk resistance. The last sultan would be held thereafter for fourteen days in Ottoman hands.
