Murad I ascended the throne

March 1362

Murad I (Hüdavendigâr), who came to the throne upon the death of Orhan Gazi, carried the state from a frontier principality to the skeleton of an empire during his 27-year reign. With the pençik system the foundation of the Janissary Corps was laid, the timar system was extended, the Beylerbeylik of Rumelia was established and the institution of the vizierate was strengthened. The Balkan conquests became a systematic state policy during his reign.
