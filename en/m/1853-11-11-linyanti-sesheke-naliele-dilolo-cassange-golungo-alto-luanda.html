Linyanti – Sesheke – Naliele – Dilolo – Cassange – Golungo Alto – Luanda (Livingstone, 1853-54)

11 November 1853

Route: Linyanti → Sesheke → Naliele → Leeba–Zambezi confluence → Lake Dilolo → village of Ionga Panza → Quango (Kwango) crossing → Cassange (Kasanje) → Ambaca → Golungo Alto → Luanda. The whole journey is shown on the map as a travel line.
