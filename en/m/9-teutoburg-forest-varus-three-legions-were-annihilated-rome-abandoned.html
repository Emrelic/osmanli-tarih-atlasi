Teutoburg Forest: Varus' three legions were annihilated; Rome abandoned the Elbe line

9

In 9 CE a Germanic coalition led by Arminius annihilated the XVII, XVIII, and XIX legions of P. Quinctilius Varus in the Teutoburg Forest. Augustus gave up conquests beyond the Rhine, and the Rhine frontier became permanent.
