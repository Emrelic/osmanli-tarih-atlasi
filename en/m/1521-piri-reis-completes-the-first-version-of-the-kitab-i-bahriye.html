Piri Reis completes the first version of the Kitab-i Bahriye

1521

Piri Reis completed in 1521 the first (prose) version of the Kitab-i Bahriye, which compiles information on the Mediterranean coasts, harbors and navigation. This first version had not yet been presented to the sultan and was in the nature of a draft to be expanded in the following years.
