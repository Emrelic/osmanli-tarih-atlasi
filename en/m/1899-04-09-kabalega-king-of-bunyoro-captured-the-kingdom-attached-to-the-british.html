Kabalega, King of Bunyoro, captured; the kingdom attached to the British Uganda Protectorate

9 April 1899

Kabalega, King of Bunyoro, who had resisted British forces since 1894, was captured in the Lango region (near Dokolo) on 9 April 1899 and sent into exile in the Seychelles; the kingdom's territory, including the capital Hoima and Masindi, was added to the British Uganda Protectorate.
