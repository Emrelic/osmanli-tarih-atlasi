Fort George (Chisasibi) was founded — the east coast of James Bay

1803

The Hudson's Bay Company founded Fort George on the east coast of James Bay at the mouth of the La Grande River. The post enabled the Cree groups of the interior to trade without descending to the coast and completed the company's eastern coastal line.
