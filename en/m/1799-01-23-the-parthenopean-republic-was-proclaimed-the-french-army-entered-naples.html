The Parthenopean Republic was proclaimed: the French army entered Naples, a sister republic replaced the Bourbon kingdom

23 January 1799

Championnet's army took Naples; the Bourbon Ferdinand IV had fled to Palermo. The actual control of the republic is largely the city of Naples and its surroundings (the provinces remained pro-Bourbon).
