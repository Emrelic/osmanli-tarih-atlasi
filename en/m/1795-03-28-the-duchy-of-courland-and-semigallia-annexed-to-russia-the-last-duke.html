The Duchy of Courland and Semigallia annexed to Russia — the last duke, Peter von Biron, renounced

28 March 1795

In the course of the Third Partition of Poland, the Courland nobles offered allegiance to Catherine, Duke Peter von Biron abdicated, and Courland became a guberniya of Russia (24 October 1795 also appears in the sources).
