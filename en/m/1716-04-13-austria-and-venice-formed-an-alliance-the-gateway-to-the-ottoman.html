Austria and Venice Formed an Alliance — The Gateway to the Ottoman-Austrian War of 1716-1718

13 April 1716

After the Ottomans retook the Morea from Venice, Austria, claiming that the Treaty of Karlowitz had been violated, signed an alliance treaty with Venice; Prince Eugene, in a letter to the grand vizier, demanded the return of the places taken from Venice. In the consultation at the camp of Davutpaşa, war was declared on Austria under the pressure of the grand vizier Ali Pasha, and a fatwa was obtained from the şeyhülislam for this.
