Free City of Kraków abolished, annexed to Austria

11 November 1846

Using the Kraków uprising of February 1846 as a pretext, Russia, Austria and Prussia decided at a conference in Vienna to abolish the free city and annex it to Austrian territory; the objections of Britain and France had no effect.
