Nebuchadnezzar attacked the Egyptian frontier; in an open battle both sides suffered heavy losses and Babylon withdrew

600 BC

The Babylonian king Nebuchadnezzar marched on Egypt in his 4th year; the Egyptian king gathered his army and met him, and in the open battle the two sides wore each other down heavily. Nebuchadnezzar returned to Babylon and withdrew to refit his army. Egypt defended its frontier, but Syria-Palestine had been left to Babylon.
