The appointment of Péter Petrovics as sancakbeyi of Lugos and Karánsebes

7 April 1554

At the insistent request of Isabella and Petrovics, Süleyman the Lawgiver decided to send the young John Sigismund back to Transylvania. In a letter written from Aleppo on 7 April 1554 he ordered the people of Transylvania to take the child back to his country; he also commanded the voivodes of Wallachia and Moldavia to assist Petrovics, whom he appointed at the same time as sancakbeyi of Lugos and Karánsebes. Lugos, conquered in 1552, was thus given to Petrovics as an Ottoman sancak.
