Alfonso VI entered Toledo: the city passed to Castilian rule

25 May 1085

Treccani: Alfonso VI entered Toledo on 25 May 1085; two years later the city became the capital of Castile (TDV Tuleytula gives only the year: 478/1085; the surrender day of 8 May 1085 is recorded separately in the tail chronology).
