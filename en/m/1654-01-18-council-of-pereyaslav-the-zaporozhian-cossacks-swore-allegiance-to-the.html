Council of Pereyaslav — the Zaporozhian Cossacks swore allegiance to the Russian tsar

18 January 1654

Bohdan Khmelnytsky, hetman of the Zaporozhian Cossacks, who had been in revolt against Poland-Lithuania since 1648, swore allegiance to Tsar Alexis I Mikhailovich at the Council of Pereyaslav on 18 January 1654 (8 January Julian), attended by a delegation from Moscow headed by Vasily Buturlin; this is regarded as the beginning of the Ukrainian Cossack Hetmanate's entry under Russian protection, and it shifted actual control of the Chernigov-Seversk region from Poland to Russia — the official border would be drawn by the 1667 Truce of Andrusovo.
