Victory at Lugoj — Mustafa II's first Austrian campaign

22 September 1695

On the first campaign in which the sultan personally took part, Lipova was retaken and Austrian forces were defeated at Lugoj; the first break occurred in the chain of defeats that had lasted for years. The atlas also regards Caransebeș as having returned from the Habsburgs to the Ottomans in this campaign; the source gives only September 1695 (TDV mustafa-ii), the atlas day was taken from the Lugoj record (declared approximation).
