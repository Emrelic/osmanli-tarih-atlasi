Alexios V Murtzouphlos ascended the throne

8 February 1204

With a palace coup Alexios IV and his father Isaac II were deposed, and Murtzouphlos, the son-in-law of Alexios III, became emperor with the title Alexios V. The Crusaders regarded this as a challenge and decided to take the city.
