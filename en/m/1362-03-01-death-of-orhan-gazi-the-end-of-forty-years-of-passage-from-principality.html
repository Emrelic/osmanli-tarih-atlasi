Death of Orhan Gazi: the end of forty years of passage from principality to state

1 March 1362

Orhan Gazi, who took charge of the principality with Osman Gazi's death in 1324, in his thirty-eight years of rule transformed the Ottomans from a frontier principality into a state seated on both shores of the Marmara: Bursa (1326), Iznik (1331) and Izmit (1337) were taken, the Principality of Karesi was annexed, the first akçe was minted (1327), the first madrasa was founded at Iznik (1331), and the crossing into Rumelia became permanent via Çimpe (1352) and Gallipoli (1354). He lost his eldest son Süleyman Pasha, who led the conquest of Rumelia, in a hunting accident in 1357; he himself died in Bursa in March 1362 and was succeeded by his son Murad I.
