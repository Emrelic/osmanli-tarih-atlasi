Battle of Yenice: Yenice-i Vardar passes to the Greeks

2 November 1912

Hasan Tahsin Pasha's 8th Composite Corps was defeated by the Greek army at Yenice, west of Salonica, and withdrew to the Salonica area. During the battle a large part of the town burned; the road to Salonica was opened to the Greek army.
