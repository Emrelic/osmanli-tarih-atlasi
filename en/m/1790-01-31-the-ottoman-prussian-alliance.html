The Ottoman-Prussian Alliance

31 January 1790

Selim III, who sought allies in Europe as soon as he came to the throne, signed with Prussia an alliance treaty envisaging mutual assistance; Prussia undertook to declare war on Russia and Austria, and the Ottoman State to make no peace until Crimea was recovered. This was the first real text of mutual alliance signed with a European state in Ottoman history and showed that the classical notion of 'no alliance with the infidels' had been abandoned. Prussia did not enter the war, but by forcing Austria to peace it paved the way for the Treaty of Sistova.
