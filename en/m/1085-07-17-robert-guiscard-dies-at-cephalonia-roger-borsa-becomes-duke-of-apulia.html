Robert Guiscard dies at Cephalonia; Roger Borsa becomes duke of Apulia

17 July 1085

Robert Guiscard dies at Cephalonia; Roger Borsa, whom he had designated as heir, becomes duke of Apulia. Treccani also confirms the date of death: 'quando la morte lo colpiva il 17 luglio 1085' (Enciclopedia Italiana, 'Roberto il Guiscardo').
