The Recapture of Esztergom

3 October 1605

The grand vizier Lala Mehmed Pasha besieged Esztergom, lost ten years earlier, and brought it back under Ottoman administration. In the same campaign Pest and Vác were also liberated, and the Ottomans had consolidated their line in Hungary. This success made it possible to sit at the table from a relatively strong position at Zsitvatorok the following year; Esztergom would remain in Ottoman hands until 1683.
