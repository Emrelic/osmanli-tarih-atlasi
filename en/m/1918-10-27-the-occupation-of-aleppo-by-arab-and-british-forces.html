The occupation of Aleppo by Arab and British forces

27 October 1918

Following the collapse of the Palestine front, the forces advancing north entered Aleppo; the city passed first into the hands of Arab and then of British troops. This occupation, which took place three days before the Armistice of Mudros, effectively ended the Ottoman State's four-century rule in Syria.
