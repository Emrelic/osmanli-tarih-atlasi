The iltizam system matures as it falls under the monopoly of the military class

1650

The iltizam method (the farming out of tax collection to private persons for a fee) had been applied since the second half of the fifteenth century but from the beginning of the sixteenth century was spreading rapidly. According to the TDV, the process of the sector falling entirely under the monopoly of members of the military class was completed in 1650; iltizam thus became the main fiscal method replacing the timar system.
