Yekaterinburg ironworks-fortress founded — center of the Urals mining region

18 November 1723

At the site chosen by Tatishchev on the Iset River, the hammer mills of the ironworks completed by de Gennin were set in operation; Yekaterinburg counts its founding date from this day. The settlement, which would become the center of the Urals mining and iron region, became the gateway of the Russian Tsardom on the road to Siberia.
