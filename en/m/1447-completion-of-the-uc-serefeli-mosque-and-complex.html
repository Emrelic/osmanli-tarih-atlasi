Completion of the Üç Şerefeli Mosque and Complex

1447

The Üç Şerefeli Mosque, built over a period of ten years (1437-1447) by the masters Muslihuddin and Shihabeddin at the order of Murad II, is regarded as the first example of centrally planned mosques in Ottoman architecture and as one of the turning points of the transitional architecture before the classical period.
