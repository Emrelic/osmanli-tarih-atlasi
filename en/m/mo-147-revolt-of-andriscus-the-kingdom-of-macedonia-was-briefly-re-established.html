Revolt of Andriscus: the kingdom of Macedonia was briefly re-established and suppressed by Metellus

147 BC

Andriscus, who claimed to be the son of Perseus, reunited Macedonia in 150/149 and defeated the first Roman forces; in 148 Metellus recovered Macedonia. The uprising became the reason for Macedonia's being bound to direct Roman rule.
