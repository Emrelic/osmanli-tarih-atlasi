David ended Seljuk rule in Tiflis and took the city; Tiflis became the capital of Georgia

1121

When the Georgian King David, with Kipchak help, ended Seljuk rule in Tiflis, the family of the Banu Ja'far was expelled from the city; after the Battle of Didgori (12 August 1121) Tiflis and Ani were occupied and Tiflis was made the capital of Georgia.
