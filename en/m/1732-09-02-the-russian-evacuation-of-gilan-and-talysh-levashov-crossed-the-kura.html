The Russian evacuation of Gilan and Talysh — Levashov crossed the Kura

2 September 1732

As the deadline for handing over under the Treaty of Rasht expired, the Russian Gilan corps withdrew from Gilan and Talysh and crossed to the north of the Kura river; in Gilan alone more than a thousand soldiers had died of disease between January and March 1732. The Russian presence on the coast now consisted of the area north of the Kura, Baku and Derbent; these, too, were returned by the Treaty of Ganja of 1735.
