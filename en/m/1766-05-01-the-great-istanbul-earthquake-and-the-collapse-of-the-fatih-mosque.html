The great Istanbul earthquake and the collapse of the Fatih Mosque

1 May 1766

According to the TDV article on the 'Fâtih Mosque and Complex', in the great earthquake of 1766 the main dome of the mosque collapsed completely and its walls were damaged beyond repair. The reconstruction, begun in 1767 by order of Mustafa III under the architects Sarım Ibrahim Efendi and İzzet Mehmed Bey, was completed in 1771 with a new design different from the old plan.
