Battle of Stiklestad: King Olav II Haraldsson falls in Trøndelag

29 July 1030

On 29 July (most probably 1030), at Stiklestad in Trøndelag (present-day Verdal), Olav, returning from exile and seeking to recover Norway, met the magnates and farmers of the region and was killed. Olav later became the national saint of Norway; Danish rule lasted a few years.
