The British occupied Nasiriyah — the Euphrates front

25 July 1915

The British forces advancing along the Euphrates from Basra defeated the Ottoman units on 24 July 1915 and occupied Nasiriyah the next day. Basra had fallen in November 1914; Nasiriyah had remained in Ottoman hands for about eight months longer.
