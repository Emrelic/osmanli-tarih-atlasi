Austria's recovery of the Illyrian Provinces and Dalmatia — end of Napoleonic rule

1813

At the end of 1813 Habsburg troops retook the region south of the Sava and the old frontier organization was re-established; the Austrian army entered Istria in October 1813. After that date Karlovac became part of the Austrian Kingdom of Illyria. The period of French administration in Dalmatia also closed in 1813. The region's belonging to Austria was officially confirmed by the decisions of the 1814 Peace of Paris and the 1815 Congress of Vienna. With this operation Villach, Tarvis, Gorizia, Gradisca and Pula (Illyrian Provinces) returned to Austria at the end of 1813.
