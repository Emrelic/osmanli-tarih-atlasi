Karahisâr-ı Sâhib (Afyon) came under Egyptian occupation — the Konya–Kütahya road

1833

Karahisâr-ı Sâhib, on the route of the Egyptian army advancing to Kütahya after the Battle of Konya, remained for a time in the hands of Ibrahim Pasha in 1833. On the map the city is the source-based intermediate point of the occupation line between Konya and Kütahya; no city-level testimony could be found for Akşehir, Ilgın and İshaklı, and these places are shown in the Ottoman colour.
