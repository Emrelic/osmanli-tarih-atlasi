The Alexandrian War: Caesar defeated Ptolemy XIII and left Egypt to Cleopatra

46 BC

When Caesar came to Alexandria and wished to arbitrate between Cleopatra and Ptolemy XIII, the palace forces besieged him; in the street fighting that lasted through the winter relief came to Caesar, and Ptolemy XIII drowned in the Battle of the Nile. Caesar gave Egypt to Cleopatra and her younger brother Ptolemy XIV; Roman legions remained in Alexandria.
