The Sopron plebiscite began — about 65 per cent of the votes were cast for Hungary

14 December 1921

On 14-15 December 1921 Sopron and Brennbergbánya, and on 16 December the eight surrounding villages, voted. Hungary led in the city and Austria in most of the villages; in total Hungary received about 65 per cent. The Allied Generals' Commission handed the region over to Hungary on 1 January 1922 (the Sopron chain on the map remains Hungarian).
