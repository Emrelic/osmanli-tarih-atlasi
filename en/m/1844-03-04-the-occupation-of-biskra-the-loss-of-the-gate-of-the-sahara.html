The occupation of Biskra — the loss of the gate of the Sahara

4 March 1844

Biskra, on the southern foot of the Atlas mountains, was the gate opening onto the Sahara and the centre of the date-palm oases. With its taking, in the years when the resistance of Emir Abd al-Qadir began to break, French control reached the northern edge of the desert for the first time. What followed would be a desert operation advancing oasis by oasis.
