Evacuation of Safi and Azemmour: Portugal withdraws from the Atlantic coast

1 October 1541

After the fall of Agadir on 12 March 1541 and the failure to establish the hoped-for alliance with the Moroccan sultan, John III ordered the evacuation of the two great positions on the Atlantic coast; the evacuation of Safi and Azemmour was completed in the autumn of 1541, in September-October. Thus three positions were lost in a single year and Portugal's fifty-year expansion on the Moroccan coast was reversed; what remained were Ceuta, Tangier, Arzila, Ksar es-Seghir and the newly fortified Mazagan.
