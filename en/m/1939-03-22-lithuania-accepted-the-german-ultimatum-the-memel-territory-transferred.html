Lithuania accepted the German ultimatum — the Memel Territory transferred to Germany

22 March 1939

Upon Ribbentrop's ultimatum, Lithuania ceded the Klaipėda (Memel) Region to Germany; Hitler entered Memel on 23 March. The region was taken by the Red Army on 28 January 1945.
