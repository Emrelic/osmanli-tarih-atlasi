The Declaration of War on Venice — The Preparation of the Morea Campaign

8 December 1714

The decision for war was taken in the Divan on the grounds that Venice supported the Montenegrin rebels, seized Ottoman merchant ships, and oppressed the Morea, which it had taken at Karlowitz, with heavy taxes. The campaign prepared by the Grand Vizier Silâhdar Damad Ali Pasha would end the following year in the recovery of the whole Morea peninsula in less than a hundred days. However, this success would set Austria in motion and open the door in 1716 to a much heavier war.
