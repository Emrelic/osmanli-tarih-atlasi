Kaykawus II takes Malatya, Sivas, Tokat and Amasya; Kilij Arslan withdraws to Erzincan

8 January 1258

When Izz al-Din Kaykawus, with the help of his famous commanders, brought Malatya, Sivas, Tokat and Amasya under his rule, Kilij Arslan IV was obliged to withdraw to Erzincan (656/1258); then, by the yarligh of Möngke Khan, the country was divided in two (657/1259).
