Haile Selassie returned to Addis Ababa — Ethiopia separated from Italian East Africa

5 May 1941

After British-Ethiopian forces took Addis Ababa in early April, Emperor Haile Selassie returned triumphantly to the capital on 5 May 1941; this day is counted as the official end of the Italian occupation (1936-41).
