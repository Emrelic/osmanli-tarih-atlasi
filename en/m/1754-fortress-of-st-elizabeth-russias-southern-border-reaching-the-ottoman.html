Fortress of St Elizabeth — Russia's southern border reaching the Ottoman-Crimean raiding line

1754

Russia built a new fortress bearing the name of St Elizabeth on the land of the Zaporozhian Cossacks. The stated reason for the fortress was to protect the Balkan settlers placed in New Serbia from Ottoman and Crimean raids; that is, the Russian frontier line descended for the first time to the middle of the steppe, right onto the raiding routes. This forward outpost became the starting point of the northern Black Sea expansion that would begin twenty years later with Küçük Kaynarca.
