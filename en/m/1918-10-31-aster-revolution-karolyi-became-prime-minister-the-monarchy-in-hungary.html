Aster Revolution — Károlyi became prime minister, the monarchy in Hungary began to dissolve (the people's republic was proclaimed on 16 November)

31 October 1918

When the defeat of the Central Powers became clear, anti-monarchical sentiment grew stronger in Hungary and a shift to the left took place. On 31 October 1918, as a result of the "Aster Revolution", the radical-minded Count Mihály Károlyi became prime minister, and then the people's republic was proclaimed; Károlyi was elected president in his own right on 11 January 1919.
