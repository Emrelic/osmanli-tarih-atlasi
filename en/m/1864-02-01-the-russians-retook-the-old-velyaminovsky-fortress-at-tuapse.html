The Russians retook the old Velyaminovsky fortress at Tuapse

1 February 1864

In the last year of the occupation of the western Caucasus, Russian troops landed on the Circassian coast and again occupied the site of the Velyaminovsky fortress evacuated ten years before. Within a few months Circassian resistance came to an end, and the people of the coast were for the most part deported to Ottoman lands.
