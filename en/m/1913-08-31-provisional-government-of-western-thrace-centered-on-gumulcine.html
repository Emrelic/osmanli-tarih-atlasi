Provisional Government of Western Thrace centered on Gümülcine proclaimed

31 August 1913

When the Treaty of Bucharest (10 August 1913) left Western Thrace to Bulgaria, the Turkish population of the region proclaimed a provisional government centered on Gümülcine; after the capture of Dedeağaç the government announced its independence under the name 'Independent Government of Western Thrace.' Owing to the intervention of the great powers the Ottoman government did not support it; the Treaty of Istanbul of 29 September 1913 left Western Thrace to the Bulgarians, and the government lived only fifty-seven days, until 25 October 1913.
