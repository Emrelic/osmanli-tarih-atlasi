Henry V is crowned emperor at Rome; the investiture question is bound to a short-lived compromise

13 April 1111

In February 1111 at Sutri, Henry V received the promise of the imperial crown in return for renouncing investiture; the right of investiture and the promise of the crown were wrung from Pope Paschal II, and the coronation ceremony was held on 13 April 1111. After Henry left Rome, a council declared this concession invalid.
