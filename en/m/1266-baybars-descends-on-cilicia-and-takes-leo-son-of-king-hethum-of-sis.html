Baybars descends on Cilicia and takes Leo, son of King Hethum of Sis, prisoner

1266

The Mamluk Sultan Baybars descended on Cilicia in 1266 and took prisoner Leo, son of King Hethum of Sis; by the subsequent agreement the Armenian king was to return the cities and fortresses he had occupied, and Leo was to be released (in 1268 only Behisni remained in Armenian hands).
