The Occupation of Sofia by Russian Forces

4 January 1878

The Russian army, which crossed the Balkans after the fall of Plevna, entered the defenceless Sofia. The city would never return to Ottoman administration; it would become the capital of the autonomous Principality of Bulgaria at the end of 1878 and in practice in 1879. On the map, from this date Sofia leaves direct Ottoman administration and passes to a temporary Russian occupation and then to autonomous Bulgarian rule.
