Constitutio Antoniniana: Roman citizenship was granted to all free inhabitants of the empire

212

In 212 CE Caracalla made the free inhabitants of the provinces Roman citizens; the distinction between citizen and foreigner in the empire was removed. The measure also broadened the tax base.
