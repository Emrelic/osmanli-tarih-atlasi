Gümüshtegin Ghazi dies at Sivas; his son Emir Ghazi becomes head of the Danishmendid dynasty

1104

With Gümüshtegin's death the Danishmendids suffered a great shock; Malatya was surrendered to the Seljuks, and struggles for the throne arose among the members of the dynasty. His eldest son Emir Ghazi took over the government.
