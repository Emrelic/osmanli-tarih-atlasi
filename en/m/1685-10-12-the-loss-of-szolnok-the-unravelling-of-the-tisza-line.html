The loss of Szolnok — the unravelling of the Tisza line

12 October 1685

After the fall of Neuhäusel in August, the Habsburg army turned south and took Szolnok, the bridgehead on the Tisza River. The city was the only safe crossing between Buda and Transylvania; its loss meant the severing of the Ottoman fortresses in the eastern half of Hungary from one another. The cutting of the supply road in the siege of Buda the following year was decisive.
