Peace of Constance: Barbarossa recognises the autonomy of the Lombard communes

25 June 1183

By the peace signed at Constance in 1183 the emperor recognised the communes and the League; Milan gained the right to elect its own consuls (excluding the archbishop), directly dependent on the empire.
