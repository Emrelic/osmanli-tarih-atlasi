The Abbasid embassy reached the camp of the khan of the Volga Bulgars; Almish Khan accepted Islam

12 May 922

The Bulgar Khan Almish, son of Shilki, sent an envoy to the Caliph al-Muqtadir Billah informing him that he wished to become a Muslim. The delegation headed by Sawsan al-Rassi, which included Ibn Fadlan, reached the khan's camp on the Volga on 12 Muharram 310 (12 May 922); with the khan's formal acceptance of Islam, the Volga Bulgar state became a Muslim state subject to the Abbasid caliphate.
