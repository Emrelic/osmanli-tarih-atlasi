Gobero (Tenere circle, Niger): Early Holocene (Kiffian) occupation begins

7699 BC

A Saharan lakeside necropolis with two distinct settlement phases in the Paleo-lake: Early Holocene (Kiffian) hunter-fisher-gatherers, around ~7500 BCE the oldest known cemetery in the Sahara; an arid interval (6200-5200 BCE); then Tenerean herders (5200-2500 BCE).
