The Dejanović principality (Kostadin-ili) became an Ottoman vassal

26 September 1371

When the Serbian state broke up after the defeat on the Maritsa (Çirmen), the brothers Jovan and Konstantin Dejanović founded their own principality, centred on Velbužd (later Köstendil) and including Štip, Strumica, Doiran and the Köprülü region. When Jovan died soon afterward, Konstantin recognized the sovereignty of Murad I and became an Ottoman vassal; the principality lived semi-independently under this tie. When Konstantin died in 1395 at Rovine in the ranks of Bayezid the Thunderbolt, his country passed to Ottoman administration without a fight and became a sancak under the name Kostadin-ili.
