Expansion of the Regency of Algiers toward the Sahara — Touggourt and Ouargla made tributary, the definitive conquest of Tilimsan

1552

According to the TDV article on Tilimsan, in 960 (1553) a large army dispatched from Algiers under Salih Reis definitively took the city and ended the three-century-old Zayyanid dynasty. In the same period (1552) Salih Reis mounted a campaign against the independent sultanates of Touggourt and Wargla; Ottoman forces took Touggourt, advanced on Wargla, and the two sultanates were made tributary; a permanent Ottoman garrison was left at Biskra on the way. Thus the border of the Regency of Algiers for the first time crossed the Tell Atlas and reached the edge of the Sahara. The great battle before Mostaganem, however, took place not this year but in 1558: Hasan Pasha defeated the Spanish Count of Alcaudete there and ended Spanish offensives in North Africa.
