Completion of the Edirnekapı (Mihrimah Sultan) Mosque

1566

Mosque construction, which began after the building permit confirmed in 1563 for Mihrimah Sultan, daughter of Süleyman the Lawgiver, was completed in 1566; the endowment deed of the complex was drawn up in 1570-71. The building is one of Mimar Sinan's great works closest to the walls of Istanbul.
