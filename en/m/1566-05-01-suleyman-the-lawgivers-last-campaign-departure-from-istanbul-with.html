Süleyman the Lawgiver's last campaign — departure from Istanbul with Szigetvár and Eger as targets

1 May 1566

After Ferdinand's death in 1564 and Maximilian II's accession to the throne, clashes on the Transylvanian frontier had increased. In the campaign that the aged sultan planned with Sokullu Mehmed Pasha, the targets were to be the fortresses of Szigetvár and Eger, and the emperor was also to be intimidated. After praying at the Tomb of Eyüp Sultan, he left Istanbul with a great ceremony; he reached Edirne on 15 May and reached Belgrade on the fiftieth day of his march. This was his last departure from the capital, which he would never see again.
