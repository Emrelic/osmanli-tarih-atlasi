Collapse of the Qaraquyunlu State: killing of Jahan Shah

10 November 1467

The Qaraquyunlu ruler Jahan Shah, who was attacked while returning from a campaign against Uzun Hasan because winter had closed in, was killed while fleeing between Bingöl and Kiğı. After this blow the state could not recover; the last ruler, Hasan Ali, was captured and killed in 1469 by Uzun Hasan's son Uğurlu Mehmed. Qaraquyunlu dominion, which had lasted for nearly a century in eastern Anatolia, Azerbaijan and Iraq, thus passed to the Aqquyunlu.
