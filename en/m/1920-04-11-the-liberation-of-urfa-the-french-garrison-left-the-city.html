The liberation of Urfa — the French garrison left the city

11 April 1920

In Urfa, which the British occupied in March 1919 and the French about seven months later, the population rose against the occupying forces on 7 February 1920. According to the TDV article on Şanlıurfa, after two months of fighting the French agreed on 10 April 1920 to evacuate Urfa on the terms of an agreement, and left the city the next day.
