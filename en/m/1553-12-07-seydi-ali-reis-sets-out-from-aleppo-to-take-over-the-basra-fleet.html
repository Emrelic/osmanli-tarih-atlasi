Seydi Ali Reis sets out from Aleppo to take over the Basra fleet

7 December 1553

Seydi Ali, appointed admiral of Süleyman the Lawgiver's Egyptian fleet, is sent from Aleppo to Basra by way of Baghdad; his purpose is to bring the ships at Basra to Suez.
