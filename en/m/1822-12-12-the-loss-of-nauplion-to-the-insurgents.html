The loss of Nauplion to the insurgents

12 December 1822

Nauplion, the port with the strongest fortifications in the Morea, surrendered from hunger after a blockade of over a year. The fortress had been, since the start of the rebellion, Ottoman control's last great support on the peninsula; with its loss the whole of the Morea passed into the hands of the insurgents. The city would soon be the first capital of the new Greek state.
