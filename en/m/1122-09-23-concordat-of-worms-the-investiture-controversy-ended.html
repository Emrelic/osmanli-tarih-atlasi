Concordat of Worms — the Investiture Controversy ended

23 September 1122

Through the mediation of the papal delegation, the peace between Emperor Henry V and Pope Callixtus II was proclaimed on 23 September 1122 at Worms (at Lobwiesen, right next to the city). Henry renounced investiture with ring and staff and accepted the canonical election of bishops and abbots.
