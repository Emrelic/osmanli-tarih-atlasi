The submission of Şâhverdi Khan, ruler of Qaradagh (Ahar), to Cafer Pasha — Marand comes under Ottoman administration

1 September 1588

Cafer Pasha, the Beylerbeyi of Tabriz, extended his rule to Marand in 1588; Şâhverdi Khan, the ruler of Qaradagh, submitted and sent his son to Tabriz. The sources give only the year; the day has been matched with the Ganja campaign of the same year.
