The Red Army occupied Vilnius

19 September 1939

The Red Army, which had entered eastern Poland on 17 September, entered Vilnius, held by Poland, on the night of 18-19 September. By the Soviet-Lithuanian mutual assistance pact of 10 October the city would be handed to Lithuania, and the Lithuanian army would enter on 28 October.
