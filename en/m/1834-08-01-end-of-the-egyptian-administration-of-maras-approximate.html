End of the Egyptian administration of Maraş (approximate)

1 August 1834

When TDV's phrase 'nearly nineteen months' is counted from the occupation of 1833, the Egyptian administration ends around the summer of 1834. That Mustafa Bey of the Küçükalioğulları, unable to resist the Egyptian army in 1834, withdrew toward Maraş also shows that Maraş was at that time a refuge outside Egyptian control.
