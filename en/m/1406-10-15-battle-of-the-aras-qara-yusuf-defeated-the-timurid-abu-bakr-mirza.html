Battle of the Aras: Qara Yusuf defeated the Timurid Abu Bakr Mirza

15 October 1406

After returning from exile following Timur's death in 1405, the Qaraquyunlu ruler Qara Yusuf took Avnik and regained his lands in Eastern Anatolia; he then marched against Timur's grandson Abu Bakr Mirza, who was regarded as the master of Azerbaijan and the two Iraqs, and defeated him on the banks of the Aras. This victory did not give Tabriz to the Qaraquyunlu: the TDV entry on Tabriz states that the city was in the hands of Abu Bakr Mirza in 1408; the battle that won Azerbaijan is the battle of Sardrud on 13 April 1408.
