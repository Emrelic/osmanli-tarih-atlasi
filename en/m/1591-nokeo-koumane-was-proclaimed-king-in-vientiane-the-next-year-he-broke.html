Nokéo Koumane was proclaimed king in Vientiane; the next year he broke the resistance of Luang Prabang and reunited Lan Xang

1591

According to Hall, as Bayinnaung's empire dissolved, Lan Xang (Laos) regained its independence under the leadership of Nokéo Koumane; Koumane was proclaimed king in Vientiane in 1591, subdued Luang Prabang in 1592 and united the country, and Tran Ninh showed allegiance to him.
