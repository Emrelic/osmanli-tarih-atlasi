The Fourth Crusade captured Constantinople

13 April 1204

A week after the halting of the first assault on 6 April, the Crusaders and the Venetians entered Constantinople on 13 April 1204; the city was plundered for three days and half of it was destroyed by fires. The Byzantine Empire was broken up; three small Byzantine states emerged at Nicaea, Trebizond and Epirus.
