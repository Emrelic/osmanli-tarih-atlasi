Tribes around Taif came under Wahhabi influence — Turaba and Hurma broke away from the Sharifate of Mecca

1801

In 1801 the tribes around Taif, dependent on Sharif Ghalib of Mecca, came under Wahhabi influence; the emir of Diriyah gave them to the administration of the Bedouin sheikh Uthman al-Mudayfi, the Sharif's brother-in-law and enemy. The Turaba and Hurma oases in the east also passed to the Saudi side in this wave. This was the precursor of the Wahhabis' capture of Taif in February 1803 and then of Mecca.
