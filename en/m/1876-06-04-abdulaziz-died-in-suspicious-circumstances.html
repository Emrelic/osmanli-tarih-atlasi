Abdülaziz died in suspicious circumstances

4 June 1876

Abdülaziz, who after being deposed was moved at his own request to the Feriye Palace (1 June 1876), was found on 4 June in his room with his wrist veins cut. A committee of nineteen doctors, including embassy physicians, prepared an ambiguously worded report; his death was announced in the newspapers on 6 June as a suicide. The investigation opened by Abdülhamid II, however, reached the conclusion that the death was not suicide but murder, and the accused were tried in 1881 at the Yıldız Court; the matter is still disputed in historiography today.
