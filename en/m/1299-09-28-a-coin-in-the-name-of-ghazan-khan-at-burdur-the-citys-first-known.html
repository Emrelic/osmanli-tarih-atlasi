A coin in the name of Ghazan Khan at Burdur — the city's first known testimony

28 September 1299

The Hamidids, under Ilkhanid pressure, were forced in the year 699 to have a coin struck at Burdur in the name of Ghazan Khan. This coin is the first written/dated testimony to Burdur.
