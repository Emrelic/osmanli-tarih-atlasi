The seizure of the emirate of Ha'il by Saudi-Wahhabi forces — the administration was in the hands of the Ibn Ali family of the Abde clan

1779

When the forces of the First Saudi State seized the emirate of Ha'il, the administration of the city was in the hands of the Ibn Ali family of the Abde clan of the Shammar tribe. This family ruled Ha'il until 1835, when Abdullah b. Rashid seized the emirate; the Rashidid dynasty was founded only at that date. The source names the emir of the city in 1820.
