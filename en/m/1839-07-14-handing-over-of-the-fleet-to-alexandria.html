Handing over of the fleet to Alexandria

14 July 1839

The grand admiral (kaptan-ı derya) Ahmed Fevzi Pasha, suspecting that the grand vizier Koca Hüsrev Pasha would give the fleet to Russia, took the fleet to Alexandria and handed it over to Mehmed Ali. Within the same month the Ottoman State was left without an army, without a sultan and without a fleet.
