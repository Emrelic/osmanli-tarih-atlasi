Darius III was killed by the men of Bessus, satrap of Bactria; the Achaemenid dynasty ended

1 July 329 BC

Darius III, who fled eastward from Ecbatana, was wounded and abandoned by Bessus and his followers in Parthia in July 330 and died; Alexander sent the body to Persepolis. The actual end of the Achaemenid Empire.
