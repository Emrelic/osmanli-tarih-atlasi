Enrico Dandolo is elected doge at the age of 85

21 June 1192

On the death of Doge Orio Mastropiero on 14 June 1192, Enrico Dandolo, blind and 85 years old, was elected doge on 21 June 1192; he is the figure who would steer the Fourth Crusade.
