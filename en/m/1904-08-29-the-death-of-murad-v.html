The death of Murad V

29 August 1904

Murad V, who remained on the throne for about three months in 1876 and was deposed, lived twenty-eight years as a prisoner under close guard after the attempts to restore him to the throne, and died of diabetes. He was interred next to his mother in the Cedid Havatin Tomb in the Yeni Cami. He went down in history as the Ottoman sultan who remained on the throne for the shortest time.
