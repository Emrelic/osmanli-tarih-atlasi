The Cretan fleet seized and plundered Thessalonica

31 July 904

The Arab fleet under the command of Leo of Tripoli, a renegade, took Thessalonica on 31 July 904 after a three-day siege; it returned unpursued with more than 30,000 prisoners and booty. This event showed the weakness of the Byzantine fleet.
