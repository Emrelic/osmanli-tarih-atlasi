The Bazavluk Sich was founded

1593

After the Tatars destroyed the Tomakivka Sich, the Zaporozhian Cossacks founded a new Sich on the island of Bazavluk, where the Dnieper joins its Chortomlyk, Pidpilna and Skarbna branches. The Sich was destroyed in 1638 by a Polish campaign after the suppression of the Cossack uprising. Term: «Sich» (Ukrainian Sich) is the central camp of the Zaporozhian Cossacks on the Dnieper islands, fortified with logs and ditches; it is the headquarters, the seat of the assembly (Rada) and the centre of the elected head of the host (koshovyi), not a state capital. At this date the 'Hetmanate' does not yet exist (1648); the name Zaporozhia means 'beyond the rapids,' and the Sichs stand on the steppe frontier below the Dnieper rapids. There is no ethnic kinship with the Cossacks of Kazakhstan: the Zaporozhian Cossacks are an East Slavic (Ukrainian), Orthodox community; the only thing in common is the word 'kazak' (free warrior) of Turkic origin.
