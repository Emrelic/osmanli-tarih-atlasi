Columbus is stranded in Jamaica; writes his famous letter

7 July 1503

Columbus, whose ships had become unusable, remained on the coast of Jamaica and wrote the 'Lettera Rarissima' of 7 July 1503 to the sovereigns. The rescue took about a year.
