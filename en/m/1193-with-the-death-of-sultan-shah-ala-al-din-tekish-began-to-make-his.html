With the death of Sultan Shah, Ala al-Din Tekish began to make his influence felt in Eastern Iran (Khurasan)

1193

After the death (1193) of his brother Sultan Shah, who had founded an emirate in the cities of Merv and Tus with Qara Khitai help, Tekish began to show his influence in Eastern Iran as well and to interfere in the affairs of Western Iran.
