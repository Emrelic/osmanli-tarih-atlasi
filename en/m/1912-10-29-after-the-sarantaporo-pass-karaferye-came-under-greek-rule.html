After the Sarantaporo pass, Karaferye came under Greek rule

29 October 1912

The Greek army advancing north from Thessaly, after forcing the Sarantaporo (Kırkgeçit) pass, took control of Karaferye. Part of the Muslim population of the city left the region at this time.
