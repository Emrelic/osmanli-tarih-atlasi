Byzantium recovered Vandal Africa: Carthage again passed into the hands of the Eastern Romans

534

With the campaign of Justinian I's general Belisarius, North Africa was taken from the Vandals and Carthage again became a Byzantine city. The city would later be the centre of the Exarchate of Africa.
