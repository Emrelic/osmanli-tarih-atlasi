Lipova (Lippa) surrendered to Caraffa — passed into Austrian hands

22 June 1688

After Antonio Caraffa's three-day siege, Lipova surrendered between 12 and 22 June 1688 and passed into Austrian hands. The Ottomans later retook the city, Austria seized it again in 1691; in the 1695 campaign the Ottomans regained control. Other settlements passing from the Ottomans to the Habsburgs on the same day in the atlas: Arad and Világos (Șiria). For Arad the source gives only the year (MKL, Arad: in Ottoman hands between 1551-1688); the day was taken from Lipova, Világos follows Arad's chain (declared approximation).
