Publication of the General Education Regulation (Maarif-i Umumiyye Nizamnamesi) of 1869

1 September 1869

The Maarif-i Umumiyye Nizamnamesi, published on 1 September 1869, became the first comprehensive text regulating general education in the provinces and the center. By the regulation the Meclis-i Kebir-i Maarif was established; with this arrangement began the restructuring of the sibyan schools, taking them out of being merely a stage of religious education and orienting them toward the teaching of reading and writing.
