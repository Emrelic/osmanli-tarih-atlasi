Omri made Samaria the capital and took Moab

879 BC

Omri founded the first true state of Israel: he conquered the east of the Jordan (including Moab), strengthened the frontier fortresses and made Samaria the new capital. His son Ahab married Jezebel, daughter of Ethbaal, king of Sidon, and thereby tied Israel to Phoenician trade.
