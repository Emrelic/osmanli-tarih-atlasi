British occupation of Egypt

13 September 1882

The Urabi Pasha movement, which rose in Egypt against financial bankruptcy and foreign control, was crushed by the victory of Tel el-Kebir by the British army that landed after the bombardment of Alexandria, and Cairo was occupied. Although Egypt legally remained attached to the Ottomans, it effectively came under British administration; control of the Suez Canal settled at the centre of British imperial strategy.
