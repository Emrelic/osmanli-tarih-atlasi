Rudolph III, last king of the Kingdom of Burgundy (Arles), dies; his country passes to Conrad II (German emperor) — Lyon enters the imperial lands

1032

Rudolph III (993-1032), the last king of the Kingdom of Burgundy (Jurane Burgundy/Arles), died without an heir and left his lands to Emperor Conrad II. This inheritance is the source of the imperial rights, called after the thirteenth century the Kingdom of Arles, extending from the Jura to the Aar and including part of present-day Switzerland as well as Franche-Comté, the Lyonnais, Dauphiné, Savoy and Provence. Since 888 the authority of the kingdom had been recognized in the bishoprics of Besançon, Basel, Lausanne, Geneva and Sion.
