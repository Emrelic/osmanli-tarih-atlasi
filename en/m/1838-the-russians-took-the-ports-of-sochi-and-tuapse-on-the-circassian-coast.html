The Russians took the ports of Sochi and Tuapse on the Circassian coast

1838

On the coast of the Circassians, who did not recognize the Treaty of Edirne and remained in effect independent, the Russians took the ports of Sochi and Tuapse in 1838. The occupation of the Circassian interior was completed in 1861-1864.
