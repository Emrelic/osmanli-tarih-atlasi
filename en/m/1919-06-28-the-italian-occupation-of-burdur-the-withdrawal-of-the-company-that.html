The Italian occupation of Burdur — the withdrawal of the company that headed for Isparta

28 June 1919

The Italians who occupied Antalya advanced toward the interior. According to the TDV article on Isparta, Italian troops occupied Burdur on 28 June and on 15 August headed for Isparta with a company of soldiers. When the people of Isparta rejected the offer to surrender their arms and declared that they would resist, the Italians withdrew the next day.
