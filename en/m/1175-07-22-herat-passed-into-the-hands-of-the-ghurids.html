Herat passed into the hands of the Ghurids

22 July 1175

Herat came under Ghurid rule in 571 (1175–76); the Ghurid Sultan Ghiyath al-Din Muhammad had turned to the city at the invitation of the inhabitants, who were dissatisfied with the administration of the Seljuk Baha al-Din Tughril.
