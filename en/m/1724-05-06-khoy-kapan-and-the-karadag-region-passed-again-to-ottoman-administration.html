Khoy, Kapan and the Karadağ region passed again to Ottoman administration

6 May 1724

During the Iranian campaign begun in 1723, Khoy together with Kapan and the Karadağ region passed again to Ottoman administration on 6 May 1724 and was recorded in the Ottoman survey in 1728. The atlas also regards Kotur, 40 km southwest of Khoy, as having passed from the Safavids to the Ottomans on this day (chain taken over from Khoy: Uluerler 2015, Turkish Studies 10/5, p. 6; no day specific to Kotur in the source — declared approximation).
