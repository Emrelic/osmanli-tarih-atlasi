Autonomy for Crete

22 December 1898

Although the Ottoman-Greek War ended in an Ottoman victory, Britain, Russia, France and Italy proclaimed the autonomy of Crete on 18 December 1897; the island was to be a neutral and autonomous province under Ottoman dominion. After 6 November 1898 the Turkish flag was no longer raised at Candia and Kisamos; with Prince George, son of the Greek king, taking up his post as extraordinary commissioner on 22 December 1898, Crete effectively left Ottoman administration (formally it separated in 1913). Other settlements lost on the same date: Candia (Crete), Chania (Hanya).
