Greek Geometric period (absolute dates raised by 14C): Middle Geometric II begins (Sindos 14C: 930 against the traditional 800)

929 BC

A stratigraphic animal-bone 14C series from Sindos in northern Greece raises the traditional absolute dates of the Geometric sub-phases (based on Sicilian colony dates) by 100-150 years; the beginning of MG II is 930 calBC rather than 800 histBC.
