Administration of Fiume transferred to Croatia, attached to the Hungarian crown

14 February 1776

By a decree of 14 February 1776, Empress Maria Theresa gave the administration of Fiume to Hungary through the Zagreb Council; the Diploma of 1779 made the city a separate body (corpus separatum) attached directly to the Hungarian crown.
