Darius and six nobles killed Gaumata; Darius I ascended the Persian throne

29 September 521 BC

Darius, with his six noble associates, killed the false Bardiya in the fortress of Sikayauvati in Media. Gaumata's death triggered uprisings in many parts of the empire; Darius suppressed them in nineteen battles within a year.
