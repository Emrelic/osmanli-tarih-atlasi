Maurya Empire: the Maurya dynasty ends

183 BC

Chandragupta (c. 321–297 BC), Bindusara (25/27/28 years), Ashoka; the dynasty ends in c. 184 BC with the killing of Brihadratha by Pushyamitra, according to the Puranic total of 137 years (Shunga period).
