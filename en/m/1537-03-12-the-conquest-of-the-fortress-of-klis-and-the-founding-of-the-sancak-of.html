The Conquest of the Fortress of Klis and the Founding of the Sancak of Dalmatia

12 March 1537

Murad Bey, the kethüdâ of Gazi Hüsrev Bey and a convert of Croatian origin, took the strategic fortress of Klis, just north of Split, from Venice. Immediately after the conquest Klis was made the centre of a new sancak and kaza encompassing its surroundings; the borders of this sancak largely drew the outline of Ottoman Croatia. This conquest, which came fifteen years after Knin, completed the administrative skeleton of the Dalmatian interior.
