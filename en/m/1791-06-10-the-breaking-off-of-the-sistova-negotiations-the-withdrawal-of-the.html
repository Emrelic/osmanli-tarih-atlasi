The breaking off of the Sistova negotiations — the withdrawal of the Austrian delegation to Bucharest

10 June 1791

The Austrian plenipotentiary Herbert withdrew to Bucharest on 10 June 1791 and caused the negotiations to be broken off; the next day the term of the armistice also expired. This rupture lasted five weeks, and the peace was saved only by the intervention of Britain and Prussia.
