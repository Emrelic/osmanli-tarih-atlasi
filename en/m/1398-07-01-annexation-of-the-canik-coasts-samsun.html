Annexation of the Canik coasts: Samsun

1 July 1398

In the vacuum created by the capture of Kadı Burhâneddin by the Aqquyunlu Karayülük Osman Bey, Bayezid the Thunderbolt first attached the Canik region on the Black Sea coast to Ottoman administration. Samsun and its surroundings thus became the dynasty's first Black Sea port. The Sivas-Tokat-Kayseri line in the interior would be annexed two weeks later, with the effective dissolution of the State of Kadı Burhâneddin.
