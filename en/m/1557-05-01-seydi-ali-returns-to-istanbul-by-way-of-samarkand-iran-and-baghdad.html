Seydi Ali returns to Istanbul by way of Samarkand, Iran and Baghdad

1 May 1557

Passing from Samarkand, Bukhara and Khwarezm to Khorasan, and at Mashhad and Qazvin to the court of the Safavid Shah Tahmasp I, he returns to Istanbul by way of Baghdad; he writes the Mir'at ul-Memalik.
