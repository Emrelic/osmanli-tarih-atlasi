Treaty of Paris: Monaco returned to Grimaldi sovereignty

30 May 1814

By the Treaty of Paris of 30 May 1814 the rights of the Grimaldis were restored; Monaco left French annexation (the Sardinian protectorate of 1815–1861 is a separate matter, not treated in this record). By this treaty Menton (Principality of Monaco) returned to Grimaldi sovereignty.
