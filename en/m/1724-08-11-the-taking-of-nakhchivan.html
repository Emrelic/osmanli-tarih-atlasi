The taking of Nakhchivan

11 August 1724

Ottoman forces advancing along the Aras took Nakhchivan and Ordubad; Julfa was also added to the liva of Nakhchivan. The city was attached to the newly founded province of Revan as Iran's northwestern gate. Thus the Ottoman frontier once more reached its easternmost line from the time of Süleyman the Lawgiver.
