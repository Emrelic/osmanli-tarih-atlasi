Marriage of Orhan Gazi and Theodora: the Byzantine alliance

1 June 1346

Orhan Gazi, who supported Kantakouzenos in the Byzantine struggle for the throne, married his daughter Theodora; this alliance enabled the Ottoman forces to become closely acquainted with Rumelia.
