Henry of Flanders was crowned Latin Emperor

20 August 1206

Henry, brother and regent of the captive Baldwin, was crowned in Hagia Sophia on 20 August 1206 by the new Latin Patriarch Thomas Morosini. Henry is regarded as the most capable ruler of the Latin Empire.
