Buçuktepe Incident — first Janissary uprising and Murad II's return to the throne

5 May 1446

The Buçuktepe Incident, the first Janissary uprising known to history, which broke out in Edirne, began with a demand for an increase of half an akçe in their pay (ulufe) and ended with the deposition of the young Mehmed II from the throne. Murad II set out in haste from Manisa on 8 Safar 850 and began his second reign. The incident became the first sign that the kapıkulu corps had become a force with a voice over the throne, and shaped the Conqueror's lifelong wariness toward the Janissaries.
