The Wahhabi Occupation of Medina

1 June 1805

After Mecca, Medina too fell into the hands of the Wahhabi forces; the treasury of the Rawda-i Mutahhara was dispersed and the domed tombs in the city were destroyed. Thus both holy cities left Ottoman control at once, and the pilgrim caravans from Damascus and Egypt were unable to set out for years. Medina could be retaken only seven years later, in 1812, by the Egyptian army under Tosun Pasha, the son of Mehmed Ali Pasha.
