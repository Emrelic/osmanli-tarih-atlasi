Chavin de Huantar: ends

499 BC

A ceremonial center in Ancash in the Andes; stamped burnished black pottery (Janabarroide) is concentrated in the range 800–500 BCE (calibrated); according to Rick et al., the center loses its temple function in ~500 BCE, and major construction had declined before this.
