The death of Ahmed II at Edirne

6 February 1695

Ahmed II, whose four-year reign was passed in war, died at Edirne at the age of fifty-two of istiskā (dropsy). His body was brought to Istanbul and interred in the tomb of Süleyman the Lawgiver. His nephew Mustafa II succeeded him.
