Revolt of Sharif Hussein

10 June 1916

Sharif Hussein, the Emir of Mecca, rose in revolt, following secret correspondence with the British, on the promise of an independent Arab kingdom; the Hejaz Railway became a target of raids. Although Fahreddin Pasha's legendary defence of Medina, which lasted until after the armistice, remained a symbol of resistance, the revolt, combined with the British advance on the Palestine front, accelerated the dissolution of Ottoman rule in the Arab provinces. Other settlements lost on the same date: Hurma, Turaba.
