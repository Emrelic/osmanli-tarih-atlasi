Mehmed III acceded to the throne, nineteen princes were strangled

27 January 1595

Mehmed III, upon the death of his father Murad III, reached Istanbul on Friday, 16 Jumada al-Awwal 1003 (27 January 1595) and ascended the throne. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, nineteen princes, four of them adults (Mustafa, Bayezid, Osman and Abdullah), were strangled to death the same night; this was the most numerous fratricide in Ottoman history.
