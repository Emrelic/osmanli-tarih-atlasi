New constitution of the Bukharan People's Soviet Republic adopted

1 September 1921

A new constitution was adopted which, unlike the Russian constitution of 1918, permitted private ownership of land and production and granted the vote also to non-proletarians (except the relatives of the deposed emir and large landowners).
