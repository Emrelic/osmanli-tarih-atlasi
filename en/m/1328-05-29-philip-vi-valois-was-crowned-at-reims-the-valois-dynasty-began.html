Philip VI (Valois) was crowned at Reims: the Valois dynasty began

29 May 1328

When Charles IV died in February 1328 without a male heir, Philip of Valois became regent; he was crowned at Reims on 29 May 1328 and began the Valois dynasty. The claim of King Edward III of England through his mother is one of the sparks of the Hundred Years' War.
