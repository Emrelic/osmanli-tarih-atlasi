Official declaration of the Mamluks and Aleppo as the target of the campaign

30 July 1516

Selim the Grim, when Qansuh al-Ghawri came to terms with the Safavids and drove his army to Aleppo, openly declared the Mamluk sultan and Aleppo to be the target of the campaign. The covert rivalry that had lasted for years between the two Sunni states thus turned into open war. This declaration began the four-week countdown to Marj Dabiq.
