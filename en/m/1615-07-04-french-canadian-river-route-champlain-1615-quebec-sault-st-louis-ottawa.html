French Canadian river route — Champlain 1615 (Quebec – Sault St-Louis – Ottawa River – Lake Nipissing – Huron country)

4 July 1615

Route: Quebec → Sault Saint-Louis (Montréal) → Lake Nipissing → Otoueacha (Huron village) → Carhagouha. The whole journey is shown on the map as a travel line.
