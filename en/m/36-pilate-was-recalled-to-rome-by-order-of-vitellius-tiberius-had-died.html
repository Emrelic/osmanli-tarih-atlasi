Pilate was recalled to Rome by order of Vitellius; Tiberius had died (complaint of the Samaritans)

36

The Samaritans complained about Pilate to Vitellius, the governor of Syria; Vitellius sent Pilate to Rome to answer to the emperor. Tiberius had died before Pilate reached Rome.
