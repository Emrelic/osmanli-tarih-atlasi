Al-Mansur, after the victory of Alarcos, retook Wadi al-Hijara and besieged Toledo

1196

Marching north in the wake of the victory of Alarcos, al-Mansur retook some cities and fortresses such as Wadi al-Hijara (Guadalajara) and Salamanca (1196); he also besieged Toledo but achieved no result.
