Territorial details of the Peace of Schönbrunn: Salzburg and Upper Carinthia (Villach, Tarvis, Lienz) separated from Austria

14 October 1809

After the defeat of 1809, Austria left Salzburg, Upper Carinthia (including Villach and, in East Tyrol, Lienz) and Carniola and Trieste; the Carinthian and Carniolan parts formed Napoleon's Illyrian Provinces. By this peace Ljubljana (Kranj), Gradisca (Gorizia) and Karlovac (Croatia south of the Sava) passed to France; Kraków, Kielce, Radom, Chełm (New Galicia) and Zamość to the Duchy of Warsaw.
