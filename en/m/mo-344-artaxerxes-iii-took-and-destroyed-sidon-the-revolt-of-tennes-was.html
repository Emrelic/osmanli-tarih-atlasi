Artaxerxes III took and destroyed Sidon; the revolt of Tennes was suppressed

344 BC

During the Egyptian-backed anti-Persian revolt of Tennes, king of Sidon, Artaxerxes III captured the city; Tennes betrayed the Persians, the city burned itself down and many people died. The Phoenician cities came under Persian control again and the way was opened for the conquest of Egypt.
