Begtemür, Shah of Ahlat, is killed by his son-in-law Hazar Dinari

18 May 1193

About two and a half months after Saladin's death, Sayf al-Din Begtemür was killed on 18 May 1193 by his son-in-law Badr al-Din Aqsunqur Hazar Dinari, one of his commanders. Hazar Dinari imprisoned Begtemür's seven-year-old son and wife in a fortress in the Erzas region.
