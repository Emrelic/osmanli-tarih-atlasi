Beginning of construction of the Şehzade Mosque and Complex

1 June 1543

Süleyman the Lawgiver ordered the building of the complex in the name of his son Şehzade Mehmed, whom he lost to an epidemic of plague in 1543; construction began in June 1543, first the mausoleum was completed and then work passed to the construction of the mosque. In Mimar Sinan's own phrase, this building is "my apprentice work."
