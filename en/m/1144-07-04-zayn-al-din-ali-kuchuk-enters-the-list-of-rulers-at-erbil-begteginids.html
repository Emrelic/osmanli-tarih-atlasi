Zayn al-Din Ali Kuchuk enters the list of rulers at Erbil (Begteginids)

4 July 1144

The list of rulers of the Begteginids (Atabegs of Erbil) begins with Zayn al-Din Ali Kuchuk in 539 (1144); when Zengi took Edessa in 1144, he had made Ali Kuchuk governor of Edessa.
