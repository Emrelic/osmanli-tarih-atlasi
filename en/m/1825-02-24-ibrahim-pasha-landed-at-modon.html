Ibrahim Pasha landed at Modon

24 February 1825

The landing force, protected by the Egyptian fleet, set foot on the south-western tip of the Morea. This army, equipped with regular (nizami) infantry and field artillery, quickly gained the upper hand over the rebel bands.
