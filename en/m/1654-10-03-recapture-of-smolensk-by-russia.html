Recapture of Smolensk by Russia

3 October 1654

During the Polish-Russian War that began in 1654, the Russian army, which had been besieging Smolensk since July, recaptured it on 3 October 1654 when the Polish garrison surrendered; the city, lost to Poland in 1611, returned to Russia after 43 years. This was one of the most important steps in Russia's process of reversing its losses from the Time of Troubles, and it became permanent with the 1667 Truce of Andrusovo.
