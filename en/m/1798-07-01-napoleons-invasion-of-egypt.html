Napoleon's invasion of Egypt

1 July 1798

The army of General Bonaparte landed at Alexandria and occupied Egypt; this campaign, aimed at Britain's road to India, was the first direct attack by a European power on the central lands of the Ottomans. Nelson's raid at Abukir and Cezzâr Ahmed Pasha's defence of Acre broke the French advance; the occupation ended in 1801, but in the vacuum it left the rise of Mehmed Ali Pasha of Kavala and the age of the 'Eastern Question' began.
