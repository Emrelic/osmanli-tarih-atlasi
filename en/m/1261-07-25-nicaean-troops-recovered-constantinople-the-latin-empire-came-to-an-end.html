Nicaean troops recovered Constantinople: the Latin Empire came to an end

25 July 1261

On 25 July 1261 Nicaean troops burned the Latin quarter on the shore of the Golden Horn, opened a gate in the wall from the inside and entered the city. Fifty-seven years of Latin rule came to an end, and Byzantium was re-established.
