The relinquishing of Zakynthos to Venice in return for an annual tribute

1482

Zakynthos, one of the Ionian islands taken from the Tocco family at the end of Mehmed the Conqueror's reign, was left to Venice by the new sultan, who was occupied with the question of Prince Cem and was seeking tranquillity in the West. The island was transferred to a Venetian administration that recognised Ottoman sovereignty and paid an annual tribute of five hundred ducats. Thus in the Ionian Sea direct Ottoman administration remained only in Santa Maura.
