Agreement on the division of Vienna into four sectors — the Western Allies took up their sectors on 1 September

9 July 1945

The agreement on the occupation zones (Besatzungszonen) and the administration of the city of Vienna is dated 9 July 1945; the US, British and French forces arrived in their sectors on 1 September 1945, and the Allied Council met on 11 September. Four-power control lasted until 1955. The occupation zones are not shown separately on the map.
