First siege of Kefe by joint Ottoman-Crimean forces

14 July 1454

The Crimean khan Hacı Giray, who came to terms with Mehmed the Conqueror in June 1454, besieged Kefe by land with 7000 horsemen; an Ottoman fleet of fifty to sixty galleys also took station offshore. Genoese Kefe had the siege lifted by paying tribute, and the definitive conquest was left to twenty-one years later, 1475. This first joint operation was the first example of Ottoman-Crimean military cooperation, which would be repeated later in the definitive conquest of Kefe.
