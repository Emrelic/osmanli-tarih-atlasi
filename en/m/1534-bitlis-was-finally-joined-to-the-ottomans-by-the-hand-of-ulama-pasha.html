Bitlis was finally joined to the Ottomans by the hand of Ulama Pasha

1534

Tekelü Ulama, whose first siege had been inconclusive, killed Şeref Khan, the ruler of Bitlis, in 940 (1533) and in 1534 definitively annexed Bitlis to Ottoman lands. Şemseddin Bey, Şeref Khan's son, after Bitlis was given to Ulama, fled to the Safavids with the Rojeki tribe. Ottoman dominion over Bitlis was also recognized by the Safavids in the Treaty of Amasya of 1555.
