Michael Angelos founded the Despotate of Epirus; Ioannina became the centre

1204

After the Fourth Crusade, Michael Komnenos Doukas Angelos, a relative of Isaac II and Alexios III, founded an independent Greek state around Arta and Ioannina; the state is the Despotate of Epirus, which would live until 1430. Michael survived at first by relying on diplomacy with Venice and the papacy.
