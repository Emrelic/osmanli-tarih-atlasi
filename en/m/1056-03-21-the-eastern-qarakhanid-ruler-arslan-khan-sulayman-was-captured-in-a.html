The Eastern Qarakhanid ruler Arslan Khan Sulayman was captured in a campaign against the lands of his brother Muhammad

21 March 1056

Arslan Khan Sulayman was captured and imprisoned in 448 (1056) during a campaign he launched against the lands of his brother Muhammad; Muhammad proclaimed himself great khagan and fifteen months later left the rule to his son Husayn.
