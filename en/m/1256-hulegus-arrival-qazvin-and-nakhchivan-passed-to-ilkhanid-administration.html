Hülegü's arrival: Qazvin and Nakhchivan passed to Ilkhanid administration

1256

With Hülegü's arrival in Iran, Mongol administration passed to the Ilkhanid State; Qazvin and Nakhchivan passed to Ilkhanid administration (rule 5 limit, not the source date). In Shirvan (Baku, Shamakhi) the Mongol occupation brought heavy destruction, but the Shirvanshahs continued as a local dynasty; the atlas keeps these two points in the Shirvanshah colour.
