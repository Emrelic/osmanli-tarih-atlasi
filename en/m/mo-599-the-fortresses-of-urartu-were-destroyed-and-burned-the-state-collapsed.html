The fortresses of Urartu were destroyed and burned; the state collapsed

599 BC

The layer of burning seen in fortresses such as Karmir-blur shows that before 600 BC a Scythian/Cimmerian raid, the Medes or another enemy destroyed Urartu. Because the Assyrian sources break off, the date after the last king, Rusa III, is uncertain; after some time Urartu became an Achaemenid satrapy (Armina).
