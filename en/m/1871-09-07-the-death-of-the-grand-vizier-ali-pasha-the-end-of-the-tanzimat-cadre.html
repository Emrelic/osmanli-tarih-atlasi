The Death of the Grand Vizier Âlî Pasha: The End of the Tanzimat Cadre

7 September 1871

With the death of Âlî Pasha, who after Fuad Pasha's death in 1869 had shouldered the Tanzimat policy alone, the generation of master diplomats who had directed the state's foreign policy for thirty years was exhausted. The pro-Russian and arbitrary administration of Mahmud Nedim Pasha, who took his place, strengthened Abdülaziz's personal rule. In the five years after Âlî Pasha's death, the finances went bankrupt, the Balkans rose in revolt, and a change of reign became unavoidable.
