Li Cunxu entered Kaifeng: the Later Liang fell

19 November 923

Li Siyuan, the vanguard of Li Cunxu, who had routed the Liang army at Zhongdu, reached the Fengqiu Gate of Kaifeng at sunrise; Wang Zan, the Liang governor of Kaifeng, surrendered the city, and Li Cunxu entered the city through the Daliang Gate.
