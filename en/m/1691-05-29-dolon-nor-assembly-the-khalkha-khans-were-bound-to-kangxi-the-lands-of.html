Dolon Nor assembly: the Khalkha khans were bound to Kangxi; the lands of Northern Yuan (Khalkha) passed to the Qing

29 May 1691

After Galdan's invasion of 1688 the Khalkha lords had withdrawn to the Qing frontier; Kangxi received the Khalkha khans at Dolon Nor (Duolun). The Tüsheet Khan and the Jebtsundamba Khutughtu appeared before the emperor in his tent and welcomed his pardon; titles and rewards were given to the Khalkha and Mongol lords, and the Khalkha were taken into the Qing's Mongol administration (khoshuu/jasag order).
