Privilegium minus (Regensburg): the March of Austria was raised to a duchy — Melk and Vienna in the Duchy of Austria

17 September 1156

On 17 September 1156, at the Reichstag in Regensburg, Henry II Jasomirgott of Babenberg returned the duchy of Bavaria to the emperor, and the March of Austria was raised to a duchy entirely separate from Bavaria; Henry II had moved his residence from Klosterneuburg to Vienna around 1155.
