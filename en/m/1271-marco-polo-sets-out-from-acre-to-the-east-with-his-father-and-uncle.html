Marco Polo sets out from Acre to the East with his father and uncle

1271

The Venetian merchants Niccolò and Maffeo Polo, with the 15-17-year-old Marco, set out from Acre with the Pope's letter and oil for Kublai. The route extends via Ayas, Mosul, Baghdad, Tabriz and Kerman to Hormuz.
