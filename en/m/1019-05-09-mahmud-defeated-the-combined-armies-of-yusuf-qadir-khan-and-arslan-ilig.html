Mahmud defeated the combined armies of Yusuf Qadir Khan and Arslan Ilig Mansur near Balkh

9 May 1019

The Karakhanid Yusuf Qadir Khan and Arslan Ilig Mansur, who had made war on Mahmud, were defeated near Balkh (410/1019); most of the Karakhanid soldiers who crossed the Oxus drowned. Yusuf Qadir Khan then came to terms with Mahmud.
