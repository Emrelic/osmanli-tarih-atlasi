Kharkiv was founded by Cossack settlers

1654

On the northern edge of the Wild Steppe, at the confluence of the Lopan and Kharkiv rivers, Cossack settlers who had come from the right bank of the Dnieper founded a settlement. Kharkiv soon became the centre of Sloboda Ukraine, attached to Moscow.
