Jacopo I da Carrara was elected protector and governor of Padua: the Commune of Padua passed to a single lord

25 July 1318

The aristocracy of Padua elected Jacopo I da Carrara protector/governor for life (shortly after 25 July 1318); the commune passed to a single lord. The day is not certain, and the first day of the range was taken.
