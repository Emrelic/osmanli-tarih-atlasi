Toghril and Chaghri Beg crossed the Oxus and entered Ghaznavid-ruled Khurasan

12 May 1035

After the murder of the Khwarazmshah Harun (April 1035) the Seljuks could not remain in Khwarazm; together with Musa Inanch Yabgu, Yunus and Ibrahim Inal, with about 10,000 horsemen, they crossed the Oxus in Rajab 426 (May 1035) and reached Ghaznavid territory in Khurasan; the foundations of the Seljuk state were laid.
