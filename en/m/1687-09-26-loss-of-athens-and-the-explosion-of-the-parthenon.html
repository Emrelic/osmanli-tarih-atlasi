Loss of Athens and the explosion of the Parthenon

26 September 1687

A bomb fired by the Venetian artillery blew up the Parthenon, which the Ottomans were using as a powder magazine; the two-thousand-year-old temple was reduced to ruins. The city fell into Venetian hands (it was to be recovered in 1688).
