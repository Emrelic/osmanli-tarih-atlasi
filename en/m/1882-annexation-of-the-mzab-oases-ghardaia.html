Annexation of the Mzab oases (Ghardaia)

1882

The Ibadi towns of the Mzab had agreed in 1852 to pay tribute to France but had kept their internal administration; thirty years later, in 1882, the region was directly annexed to French territory and Ghardaia was placed under military administration. Thus the last autonomous belt of the northern Sahara also legally came to an end.
