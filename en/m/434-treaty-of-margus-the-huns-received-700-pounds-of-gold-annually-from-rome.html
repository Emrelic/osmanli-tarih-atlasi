Treaty of Margus: the Huns received 700 pounds of gold annually from Rome

434

After the death of the Hunnic king Rua (433/4), Attila and Bleda concluded a treaty with the Eastern Roman Empire at Margus: the annual tribute was raised from 350 to 700 pounds of gold, and Hunnic refugees were returned. The Huns gained the right to trade on the Danube frontier.
