Foedus Cassianum: Rome allied with the Latin League

492 BC

By the treaty that bears the name of Spurius Cassius, Rome and the Latin League formed an alliance on the basis of mutual defense, division of booty, and a joint army. This treaty is the framework of Roman–Latin relations until the Latin War (340–338).
