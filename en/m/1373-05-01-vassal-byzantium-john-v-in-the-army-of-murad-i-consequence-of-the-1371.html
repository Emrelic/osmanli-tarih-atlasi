Vassal Byzantium: John V in the army of Murad I (consequence of the 1371 victory of Çirmen)

1 May 1373

According to the TDV, after the victory of Çirmen (Maritsa) on 26 September 1371, the Byzantine Empire had recognized Ottoman dominion and, by accepting to pay tribute and to serve in the Ottoman army, had fallen into the position of a vassal state. Emperor John V, with this obligation, joined the army of Murad I in 1373; his son Andronikos IV, whom he had left behind in Constantinople at that time, rebelled against their fathers together with Prince Savcı Bey in Bursa.
