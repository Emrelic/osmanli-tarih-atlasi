Telepinu came to the throne and issued an edict; relations were defined by a treaty with Kizzuwatna

1524 BC

Telepinu re-established Hittite law with his edict regulating succession; the treaty signed with Kizzuwatna recognized that country as an independent partner rather than a dependent. The Hittite Old Kingdom ends with this edict.
