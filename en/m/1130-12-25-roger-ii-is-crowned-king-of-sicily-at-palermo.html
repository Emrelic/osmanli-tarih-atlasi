Roger II is crowned king of Sicily at Palermo

25 December 1130

In the schism that followed the death of Pope Honorius II (13 February 1130), Roger, who received the royal crown from Anacletus II, was invested on 27 September 1130; on Christmas Day he was crowned king at Palermo. The kingdom comprised the island of Sicily and southern Italy as far as the Abruzzi.
