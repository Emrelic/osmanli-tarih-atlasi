Disappearance of İsa Çelebi

1 June 1406

İsa Çelebi, who with the support of Süleyman Çelebi returned to Anatolia several times, disappeared after his last defeat; his share passed to Çelebi Mehmed, and on the map Mehmed's domain expanded westward.
