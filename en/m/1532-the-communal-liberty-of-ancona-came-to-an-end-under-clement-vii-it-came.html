The communal liberty of Ancona came to an end: under Clement VII it came directly under papal sovereignty

1532

The communal liberty of the Republic of Ancona came to an end in 1532, under Clement VII, when it passed under the direct sovereignty of the Papacy (year precision; day/month not read).
