Stjepan Vukčić Kosača 'Duke of St Sava' — the birth of Herzegovina

1448

Stjepan Vukčić Kosača, who had united Hum and Trebinje in a single principality, rose against the king of Bosnia and proclaimed himself 'Herzog (duke) of St Sava'; the Emperor Frederick III granted the title in 1448. The name Herzegovina ('the land of the Herzog') comes from this title. Herzegovina passed piece by piece to the Ottomans from 1466, became a sancak in 1470, and its remaining lands were annexed at the beginning of 1482.
