Merneptah Stele: the earliest known mention of the name 'Israel' (5th regnal year of the Egyptian pharaoh Merneptah; c. 1209 BCE)

1208 BC

The victory stele erected in the 5th regnal year of the Egyptian pharaoh Merneptah lists 'Israel' among the defeated peoples: «Israel is laid waste, his seed is no more»; Palestine/Canaan is said to have 'become a widow for Egypt'. This is the earliest known written mention of the name 'Israel'. The stele situates Israel as a people in Palestine; it makes no mention of Moses or of the Exodus narrative in the Torah.
