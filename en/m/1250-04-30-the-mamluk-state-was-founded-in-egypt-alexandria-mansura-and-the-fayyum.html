The Mamluk State was founded in Egypt — Alexandria, Mansura and the Fayyum passed to the Mamluks

30 April 1250

After Turanshah, Mamluk power was established with Shajar al-Durr and ʿIzz al-Dīn Aybak; the Egyptian cities (Alexandria, Mansura, the Fayyum) passed to Mamluk administration. The source gives Aybak's accession as 3 July 1250; the atlas took the day over from the end of the Ayyubid register entry (30 April 1250) — the künye date is not a source, and the difference was reported.
