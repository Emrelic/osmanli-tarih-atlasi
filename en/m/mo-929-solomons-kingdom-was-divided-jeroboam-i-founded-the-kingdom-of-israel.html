Solomon's kingdom was divided; Jeroboam I founded the Kingdom of Israel in the north

929 BC

According to the Torah, on Solomon's death his son Rehoboam's increase in taxation led to the secession of the ten northern tribes; Jeroboam was chosen king and made Tirzah the capital. In the south Judah continued under the Davidic dynasty. The separate histories of the two states begin.
