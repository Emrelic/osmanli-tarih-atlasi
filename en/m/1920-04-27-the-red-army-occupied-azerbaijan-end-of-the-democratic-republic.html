The Red Army occupied Azerbaijan — end of the Democratic Republic

27 April 1920

The Red Army entered Azerbaijan and dissolved the parliament and government; the two-year Azerbaijan Democratic Republic came to an end. The oil basin of Baku and Ganja thus passed to Soviet administration. With this occupation Shamakhi, Qabala, Ereş, Shabran, Salyan, Quba, Shaki and Barda passed to Soviet Russian control.
