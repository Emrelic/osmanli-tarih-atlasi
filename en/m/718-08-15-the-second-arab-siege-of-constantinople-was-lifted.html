The second Arab siege of Constantinople was lifted

15 August 718

In the face of famine, cold, epidemic and Bulgar attacks, Maslama withdrew on 15 August 718, exactly one year later, on the order of Caliph Umar II. This success halted the Arab advance in Europe.
