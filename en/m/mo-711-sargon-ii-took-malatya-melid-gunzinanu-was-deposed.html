Sargon II took Malatya (Melid); Gunzinanu was deposed

711 BC

Gunzinanu, king of Melid (Malatya), was defeated by Sargon for having cooperated with Tabal and Urartu, and his kingdom became an Assyrian province. Neo-Hittite dominance in eastern Anatolia thus came to an end.
