The Qing placed the seven Khalkha hoshuns in the same order as the 49 Mongol banners: the Khalkha were bound to Qing administration with khans, wangs, beile and jasags

30 May 1691

At the Dolon Nor assembly, Kangxi told the Khalkha khans, jinongs and noyans, «I have established you in the same order as my 49 banners»; the old jinong and noyan titles were abolished and the ranks of wang, beile and taiji were given together with jasags, the titles of Tüsheet Khan and Setsen Khan were preserved, and Tsewang Jab, brother of the Zasagt Khan, was made a wang. Khalkha became Outer Mongolia, taken into the Qing jasag-banner (hoshuu) order; the aimags of the Tüsheet Khan, Setsen Khan and (after 1725) Sain Noyan and Zasagt are parts of this order.
