Taking of Zabid: first entry of Ottoman seamen into the Red Sea

20 June 1516

The seamen of Ottoman origin Hüseyin Bey and Selman Reis, sent to the Red Sea at the request of the Mamluk Sultan Qansuh al-Ghawri in the face of Portuguese pressure in the Indian Ocean, took Zabid with their fleet. This operation, still conducted in Mamluk service, is the first actual presence of the Ottoman navy on the Red Sea-India line. A year later, when Egypt passed to the Ottomans, the same fleet would be tied directly to Istanbul.
