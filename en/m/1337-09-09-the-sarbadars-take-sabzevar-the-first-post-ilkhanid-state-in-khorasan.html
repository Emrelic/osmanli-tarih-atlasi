The Sarbadars take Sabzevar: the first post-Ilkhanid state in Khorasan

9 September 1337

When Ilkhanid central authority collapsed with the death of Abu Sa'id (30 November 1335), the reaction that had built up in Khorasan against the tax officials burst out at Bashtin: Abd al-Razzaq rose on 13 March 1337 and seized Sabzevar on 9 September 1337 (12 Safar 738). The name "Sarbadar" — "head on the gallows" — is their own expression of the fact that they were ready to be hanged if defeated.
