Conquest of Kemah Castle from the Safavids

19 May 1515

Selim the Grim, who spent the winter at Amasya on his return from Chaldiran, assigned Bıyıklı Mehmed Pasha to the conquest of the fortified Kemah Castle, which held the crossing point of the Euphrates. At the end of the siege, in which the sultan himself took part, the fortress was taken from Safavid hands and the gate of eastern Anatolia was opened. This conquest is the first concrete step that turned the victory of Chaldiran into a lasting territorial gain.
