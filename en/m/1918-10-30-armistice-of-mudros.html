Armistice of Mudros

30 October 1918

The Ottoman State, whose fronts were severed from one another by the collapse of Bulgaria, withdrew from the war with the armistice signed aboard the battleship Agamemnon. The opening of the Straits, and especially Article 7, which made it possible to occupy places where security was threatened, were soon made the legal cloak of de facto partition: Mosul, Alexandretta, then Izmir and Istanbul were occupied. The armistice is regarded as the document of the empire's de facto end. Other settlements lost on the same date: Hama, Damascus, Homs.
