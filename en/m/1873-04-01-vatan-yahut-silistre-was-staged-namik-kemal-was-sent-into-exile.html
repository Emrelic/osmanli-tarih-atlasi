'Vatan yahut Silistre' was staged, Namık Kemal was sent into exile

1 April 1873

When Namık Kemal's patriotic play was staged at the Gedikpaşa Theatre, the audience poured into the streets shouting 'Long live the homeland, long live Kemal'. According to the TDV article on 'Nâmık Kemal', the government, alarmed by this demonstration, closed the newspaper İbret and on 6 April 1873 sent Namık Kemal into exile in Cyprus (Famagusta) without trial.
