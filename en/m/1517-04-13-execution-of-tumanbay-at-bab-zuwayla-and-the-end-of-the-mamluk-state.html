Execution of Tumanbay at Bab Zuwayla and the end of the Mamluk State

13 April 1517

Fourteen days after his capture, Tumanbay was hanged at Bab Zuwayla, the celebrated gate of Cairo; his body was later buried in the madrasa built by his uncle. The Mamluk State, which had ruled Egypt and Syria since 1250, thus officially passed into history. With the breaking of the resistance, cities of Upper Egypt (the Sa'id) such as Asyut and Aswan were also attached to Ottoman administration.
