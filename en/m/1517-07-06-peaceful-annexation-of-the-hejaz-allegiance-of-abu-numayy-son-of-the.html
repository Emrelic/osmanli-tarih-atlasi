Peaceful annexation of the Hejaz: allegiance of Abu Numayy, son of the Sharif of Mecca

6 July 1517

Selim the Grim had first considered sending troops for the seizure of Mecca and its surroundings, but gave this up when the emir of Mecca, Sharif Barakat, announced his obedience. Barakat's twelve-year-old son Abu Numayy came to Cairo with his father's gifts and the keys of Mecca and was received in the sultan's presence. Thus the Hejaz was joined to the Ottomans not as an automatic consequence of Ridaniya, but five and a half months later and without a single shot, peacefully and by way of allegiance (bi'a). Other places joined on the same date: Taif.
