Caesar was assassinated in the Senate on the Ides of March (15 March)

15 March 43 BC

Caesar, who had been proclaimed dictator for life, was stabbed by a group of senators at a Senate meeting in the Theatre of Pompey on 15 March 44. His death prepared the ground for new civil wars and for the rule of Augustus.
