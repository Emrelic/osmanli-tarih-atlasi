The Capture of Buda and Its Handing Over to Zápolya

8 September 1529

On the road to the Vienna campaign the Ottoman army besieged and retook Buda, which had fallen into Ferdinand's hands, and delivered the city to János Szapolyai, the Hungarian king under Ottoman protection. Thus a model of a buffer vassal kingdom was established over Hungary in the Habsburg-Ottoman rivalry. This arrangement would last until 1541; after Zápolya's death Buda would be made a direct Ottoman province.
