Yaghibasan, Malik of Sivas, dies at Çankırı; a new struggle begins among the Danishmendids

4 August 1164

Yaghibasan, who had gone to Çankırı to obtain aid from the Seljuk Malik Shahinshah against Kilij Arslan II, died there on 4 August 1164; he was buried in the cemetery of the madrasa he had built at Niksar. Who succeeded him after his death is disputed.
