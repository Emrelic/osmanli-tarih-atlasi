Trogir left Venetian protection — Hungarian control began again

9 July 1357

Trogir remained under Venetian protection and administration until 9 July 1357; while Louis of Anjou was beginning to tear Dalmatia away from Venice, it left that protection and returned to the sovereignty of the Croatian-Hungarian kingdom.
