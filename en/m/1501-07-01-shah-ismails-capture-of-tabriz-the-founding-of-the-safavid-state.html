Shah Ismail's capture of Tabriz: the founding of the Safavid State

1 July 1501

According to the Safavids and Aqquyunlu entries of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, Ismail, the young sheikh of the Ardabil lodge, with an army raised from his Qizilbash disciples in Anatolia, inflicted a heavy defeat on the Aqquyunlu Sultan Alvand in the Nakhchivan region; Alvand fled to Diyarbekir, Ismail entered Tabriz and ascended the throne, and proclaimed Twelver Shiism the official creed. Thus both Aqquyunlu rule and the region's post-Ilkhanid era of principalities came to a close; the Safavid State, which would last three centuries, was founded.
