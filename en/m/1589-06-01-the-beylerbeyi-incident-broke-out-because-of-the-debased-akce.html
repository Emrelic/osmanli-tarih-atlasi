The Beylerbeyi Incident broke out because of the debased akçe

1 June 1589

When the state made payments by lowering the silver content of the akçe, the sipahis and Janissaries besieged the palace and raised a bloody uprising that went down in history as the Beylerbeyi Incident. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Abdülkadir Şeyhî Efendi, the şeyhülislam was dismissed from office as a result of this event.
