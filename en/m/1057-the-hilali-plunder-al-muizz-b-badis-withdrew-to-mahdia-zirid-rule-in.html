The Hilali plunder: al-Muʿizz b. Bādīs withdrew to Mahdia, Zirid rule in Kairouan came to an end

1057

Around 449 (1057) the Hilalis plundered Kairouan; the Zirids withdrew to Mahdia; the owner of the city until 1229 cannot be traced year by year in the sources (the map shows this period as blank).
