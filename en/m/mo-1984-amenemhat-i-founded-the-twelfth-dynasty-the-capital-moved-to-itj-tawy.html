Amenemhat I founded the Twelfth Dynasty; the capital moved to Itj-tawy

1984 BC

Amenemhat I, who became king while vizier, founded the Twelfth Dynasty and moved the capital from Thebes to a new city between Memphis and the Fayum (Itj-tawy). This dynasty established the most stable period of the Middle Kingdom and the fortress system aimed at Nubia.
