Battle of Sakarya

13 September 1921

The Greek offensive, which had approached to within 50 kilometres of Ankara, was broken in the 22-day, 22-night battle conducted under the order of Mustafa Kemal Pasha, who had assumed the powers of commander-in-chief: 'There is no line of defence, there is a surface of defence.' The Greek army went onto the strategic defensive for the first time; the victory brought Mustafa Kemal the title of Gazi and the rank of field marshal (müşir), and opened the door to the Treaty of Ankara with France and to foreign recognition.
