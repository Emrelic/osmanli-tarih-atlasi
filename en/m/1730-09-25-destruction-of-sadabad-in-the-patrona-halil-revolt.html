Destruction of Sa'dabad in the Patrona Halil revolt

25 September 1730

During the Patrona Halil revolt that broke out on 25 September 1730, hundreds of pavilions and structures at Sa'dabad were destroyed; this complex, the symbol of the Tulip Period, never regained its former splendor.
