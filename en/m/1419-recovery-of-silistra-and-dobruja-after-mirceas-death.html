Recovery of Silistra and Dobruja: after Mircea's death

1419

The disorder that arose in Wallachia after the death of the Voivode of Wallachia Mircea in 1418 gave Çelebi Sultan Mehmed the opportunity to retake Silistra and Dobruja. In the spring of 1419 Silistra, Constanța and the greater part of Dobruja again came under Ottoman administration; the fortresses of Enisala (Yenisale) and Isaccea were repaired and made frontier fortresses (serhat); Dobruja thereafter remained Ottoman until 1878.
