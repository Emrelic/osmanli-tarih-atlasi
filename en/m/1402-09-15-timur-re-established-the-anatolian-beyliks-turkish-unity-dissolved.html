Timur re-established the Anatolian beyliks — Turkish unity dissolved

15 September 1402

After the victory at Ankara, Timur restored the beys who were with him to their former lands. Karamanoğlu Mehmed and Ali Beys were given the Karaman province together with Kayseri, Kırşehir, Sivrihisar and Beyşehir; Germiyanoğlu Yakub Bey II received Kütahya and the Germiyan province; Candaroğlu İsfendiyar Bey received Çankırı and Kalecik in addition to the Kastamonu-Sinop line. The Aydın, Menteşe and Teke provinces also returned to their former dynasties. The Anatolian Turkish unity that Yıldırım Bayezid had built in ten years dissolved within a single summer; on the map these beyliks reappear in their own colours. Other settlements that changed hands in the same process: Aksaray, Denizli, Ereğli (Konya), Ermenek, Ilgın, Karapınar, Kayseri, Konya, Kırşehir, Kütahya, Sivrihisar, Söke.
