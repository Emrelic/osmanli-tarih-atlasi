The giving of Negroponte to Greece — together with the island (Karystos, Oreoi)

1831

Negroponte, which was the scene of intense fighting throughout the Greek war of independence but remained in Ottoman hands, was given to the Greeks in 1831 after the peace treaty entered into force. The Muslim population of the city, which had been an important naval base since 1470, sold all their property at very low prices and migrated en masse to Ottoman lands; the city remained empty for a long time, and the German traveller Fallmerayer in 1842 described it as a ghost city.
