Timurtaş Bey took Kızılcaağaç Yenicesi (Elhovo) and Yanbolu in the Tundzha valley

1369

During Murad I's campaign against Bulgaria and Byzantium, Kara Timurtaş Bey was sent to the Tundzha valley; he took Kızılcaağaç Yenicesi and Yanbolu and returned to Edirne with a great deal of booty.
