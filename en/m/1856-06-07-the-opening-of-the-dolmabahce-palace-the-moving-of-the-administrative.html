The Opening of the Dolmabahçe Palace: The Moving of the Administrative Centre from Topkapı

7 June 1856

The palace that Abdülmecid had commissioned from the Balyan family in 1842 was opened after fourteen years of construction, and the administrative centre of the state was moved from the four-century-old Topkapı to the shore of the Bosphorus. With its European style, crystal staircase and Muayede Hall, the palace became the expression in stone of the Tanzimat's claim to Westernisation. Its enormous cost became one of the most criticised items of the years in which Ottoman finance entered the spiral of foreign borrowing.
