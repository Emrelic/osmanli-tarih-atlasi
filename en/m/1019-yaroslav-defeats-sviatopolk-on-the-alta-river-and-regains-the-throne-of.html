Yaroslav defeats Sviatopolk on the Alta River and regains the throne of Kiev

1019

Yaroslav, who had been defeated on the Bug River in 1018 by Sviatopolk and his father-in-law Bolesław, had fled from Kiev; in 1019 he routed Sviatopolk on the Alta River with the Novgorod army and returned to the throne of Kiev.
