Malikshah's emir Savtegin captured Arran; the Ganja branch of the Shaddadids ended

16 August 1075

In the time of the Shaddadid Fadl III, Savtegin, one of the emirs of Sultan Malikshah, captured Arran (468/1075–76); the region came under Seljuk administration and the Ganja branch of the Shaddadids ended.
