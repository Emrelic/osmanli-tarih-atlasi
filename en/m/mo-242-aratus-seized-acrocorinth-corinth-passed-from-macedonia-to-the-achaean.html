Aratus seized Acrocorinth: Corinth passed from Macedonia to the Achaean League

242 BC

Aratus of Sicyon neutralized the garrison of Antigonus Gonatas on Acrocorinth by a night raid and added Corinth to the Achaean League; Megara, Troezen and Epidaurus also joined the League. Macedonia lost its key fortress in the Peloponnese.
