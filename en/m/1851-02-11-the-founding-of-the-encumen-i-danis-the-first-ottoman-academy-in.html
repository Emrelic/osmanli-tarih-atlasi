The Founding of the Encümen-i Dâniş: The First Ottoman Academy in Istanbul

11 February 1851

The Encümen-i Dâniş, founded by decision of the Meclis-i Maârif-i Umûmiyye, was the first Ottoman scientific academy, formed to prepare scholarly and textbooks in Turkish. It was opened with a ceremony on 18 July 1851 in the building of the Dârülmaârif; among its native and foreign members was Ahmed Cevdet Pasha. Although short-lived, works such as Cevdet Pasha's Târih and Kavâid-i Osmâniyye were products of this institution, and it is regarded as a cornerstone in Turkish's becoming a language of scholarship.
