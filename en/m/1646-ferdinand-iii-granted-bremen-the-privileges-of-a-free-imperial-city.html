Ferdinand III granted Bremen the privileges of a free imperial city

1646

Emperor Ferdinand III recognized Bremen's privileges as a free imperial city in 1646; Sweden opposed this and in 1666 tried to subdue the city by force but failed. When the Elector of Hanover took the archbishopric (1720), he recognized Bremen as a free city.
