Ṭāriq b. Ziyād inflicted a heavy defeat on the Visigothic army

26 July 711

At the end of a battle lasting eight days between the two armies, the Visigothic army suffered a heavy defeat (5 Shawwāl 92 / 26 July 711); the site of the battle is the Guadalete or the bank of the Barbate. It is related that Ṭāriq conquered Malaga, Elvira, Cordoba and Toledo in a short time.
