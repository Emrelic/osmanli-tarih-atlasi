Ta'if fell into the hands of Sharif Hussein

17 September 1916

The forces of Sharif Hussein, who had risen against Ottoman rule during the First World War, captured Ta'if on 17 September 1916; the city remained under Hashemite administration until the Ottoman withdrawal from the Hejaz on 30 October 1918.
