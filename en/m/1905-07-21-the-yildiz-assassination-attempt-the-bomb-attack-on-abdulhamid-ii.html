The Yıldız Assassination Attempt: The Bomb Attack on Abdülhamid II

21 July 1905

The Dashnaktsutyun organisation made an attempt on the sultan's life by detonating a landau fitted with a time bomb, aimed at his carriage as he came out of the Friday selamlık; Abdülhamid's being delayed by a few minutes while talking with the şeyhülislam saved his life. Twenty-six people died in the explosion and more than fifty were wounded. The event hardened still further the sultan's obsession with security and his despotic rule based on the spy organisation; it was also the subject of Tevfik Fikret's poem 'Bir Lahza-i Taahhur'.
