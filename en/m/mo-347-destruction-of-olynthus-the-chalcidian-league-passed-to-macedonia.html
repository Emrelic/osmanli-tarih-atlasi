Destruction of Olynthus: the Chalcidian League passed to Macedonia

347 BC

Although Olynthus asked Athens for help, Philip took the city by treachery and destroyed it; the Chalcidice peninsula was added to Macedonia. Demosthenes's Olynthiacs are based on this appeal.
