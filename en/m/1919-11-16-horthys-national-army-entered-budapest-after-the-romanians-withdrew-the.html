Horthy's National Army entered Budapest — after the Romanians withdrew, the counter-revolution took power

16 November 1919

The Romanian troops left Budapest on 14 November 1919; the National Army under Horthy's command entered the city on 16 November 1919. The way was thus opened for Horthy's installation as regent on 1 March 1920.
