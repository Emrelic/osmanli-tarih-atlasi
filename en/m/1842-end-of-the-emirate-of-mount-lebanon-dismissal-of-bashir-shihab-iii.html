End of the Emirate of Mount Lebanon — dismissal of Bashir Shihab III

1842

Emir Bashir II (III), unable to control the Christian-Druze strife and the internal balances in the Shuf region, was removed from office in January 1842; the local emirate order that had lasted since 1516 (through the Maʿnids and then the Shihabs) came to an end, and the region passed to direct Ottoman administration and then to the system of the double kaymakamate.
