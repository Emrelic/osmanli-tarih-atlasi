The sancak of Lugos came under Habsburg occupation (1691–1695)

1691

The sancak of Lugos remained under Habsburg (German) occupation between 1691 and 1695; Mustafa II reconquered it on 22 September 1695. In the old record, 1689–1695 was a declared gap; the 1691–1695 slice is now painted as Habsburg, and 1689–1691 remains a gap. The date has been taken as a year only.
