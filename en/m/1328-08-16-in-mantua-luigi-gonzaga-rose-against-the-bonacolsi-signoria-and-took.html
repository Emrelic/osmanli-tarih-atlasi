In Mantua Luigi Gonzaga rose against the Bonacolsi signoria and took power; the Gonzaga era began

16 August 1328

On 16 August 1328 Luigi Gonzaga rose against the Bonacolsi signoria that ruled Mantua and took over the city's government. The same city-state continued with the new dynasty; the Gonzagas ruled Mantua until 1708.
