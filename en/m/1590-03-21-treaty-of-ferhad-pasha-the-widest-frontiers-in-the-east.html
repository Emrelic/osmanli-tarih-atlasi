Treaty of Ferhad Pasha — the widest frontiers in the east

21 March 1590

Following the arrival in Istanbul of Shah Abbas's envoy Haydar Mirza (18 January 1590), the treaty, concluded on the condition that the conquered places remain Ottoman and ending the twelve-year war, left Tabriz, Karabakh, Shirvan, Georgia and Luristan in Ottoman hands; the empire reached the widest frontiers of its history in the east.
