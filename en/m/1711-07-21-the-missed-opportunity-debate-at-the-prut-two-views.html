The 'missed opportunity' debate at the Prut — two views

21 July 1711

That peace was settled for when the Ottoman army had completely encircled the Russian army became a subject of debate among historians immediately after the treaty — in the TDV's own words, 'it was understood that a great opportunity had been missed, and this thesis was strongly argued by those of the opposing view.' According to the critics, the tsar could have been captured and the weapons of the Russian army seized; according to the defenders, on the other hand, weakening Russia in a limited way was a 'wiser policy' in order to prevent Sweden from growing stronger in Northern Europe. The TDV reports both views and takes no side.
