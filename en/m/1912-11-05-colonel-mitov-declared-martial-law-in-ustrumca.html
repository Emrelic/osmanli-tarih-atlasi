Colonel Mitov declared martial law in Ustrumca

5 November 1912

The Bulgarian Colonel Mitov entered Ustrumca and declared martial law; on leaving the next day he left the town to a Bulgarian kaymakam and a Serbian commander. Ustrumca changed hands between the allies during the war and was attached to Bulgaria in 1913.
