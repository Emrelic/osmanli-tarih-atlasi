Sanjar-Mahmud agreement: part of the lands directly governed by Muhammad Tapar were left to Mahmud; the Iraq Seljuks separated

7 November 1119

By the agreement made in Sha'ban 513 (November 1119), Sanjar pardoned his nephew Mahmud, whom he had defeated at Saveh (at the request of his mother Safariyya Khatun), and left him part of the lands directly governed by his father Muhammad Tapar; thus the Iraq Seljuk state was founded.
