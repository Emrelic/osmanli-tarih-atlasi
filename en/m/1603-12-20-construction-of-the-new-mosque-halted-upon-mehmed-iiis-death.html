Construction of the New Mosque halted upon Mehmed III's death

20 December 1603

After the death in 1603 of Mehmed III, son of the patroness Safiye Sultan, Safiye Sultan was sent to the Old Palace and construction of the New Mosque was left unfinished; the building waited in this state, uncompleted, for about sixty years.
