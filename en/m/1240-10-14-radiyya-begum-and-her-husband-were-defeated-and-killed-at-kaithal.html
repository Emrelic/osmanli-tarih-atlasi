Radiyya Begum and her husband were defeated and killed at Kaithal; Bahram Shah remained on the throne of Delhi

14 October 1240

Radiyya, who had married Altuniyya, the ruler of Tabarhind, gathered the Turkish commanders and marched on Delhi; in the battle at Kaithal against Bahram Shah's forces she was defeated when some of the troops subordinate to them went over to the other side, and she was captured and killed (25 Rabi I 638 / 14 October 1240).
