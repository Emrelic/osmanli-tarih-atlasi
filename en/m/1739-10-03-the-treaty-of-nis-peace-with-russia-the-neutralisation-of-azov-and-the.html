The Treaty of Niš — Peace with Russia, the Neutralisation of Azov and the Halting of Crimean Raids

3 October 1739

Russia, left alone when Austria consented to peace at Belgrade, was compelled to end the war through the mediation of the French ambassador Villeneuve. With the fortifications of the Fortress of Azov demolished, the region was declared neutral ground between the two states; Russia accepted the condition of keeping no warships or merchant ships in the Black Sea. Thus Russia's aim of access to the sea was postponed by another generation, and the Ottoman State rose from the table with a gain for the last time in the eighteenth century.
