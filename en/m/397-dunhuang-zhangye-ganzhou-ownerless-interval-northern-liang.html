Dunhuang, Zhangye (Ganzhou): ownerless interval → Northern Liang (北涼)

397

At the points Dunhuang, Zhangye (Ganzhou), ownership passes from the ownerless interval to Northern Liang (北涼). Transition source information: Jin shu 129 (Juqu Mengxun): when Duan Ye became Governor of Liang at Jiankang (Shenxi 1 = 397) he made Mengxun governor of Zhangye; in Long'an 5 (401) Mengxun was proclaimed Duke of Zhangye; in Yixi 8 (412) King of Hexi; Wei shu 4a Taiyan 5 (439) Taiwu took Guzang, Muqian surrendered.
