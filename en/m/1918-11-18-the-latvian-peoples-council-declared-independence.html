The Latvian People's Council declared independence

18 November 1918

The Latvian People's Council, meeting in the National Theatre in Riga, proclaimed the Republic of Latvia independent of the Russian Empire; a provisional government was formed under Kārlis Ulmanis. A few weeks later (December 1918) Soviet Russia attacked and the War of Independence began. With this proclamation Riga, Jelgava, Liepāja, Ventspils, Cēsis, Daugavpils and Rēzekne were regarded as the territory of the Republic of Latvia.
