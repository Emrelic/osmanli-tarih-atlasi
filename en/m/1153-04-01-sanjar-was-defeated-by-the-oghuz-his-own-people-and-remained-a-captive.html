Sanjar was defeated by the Oghuz, his own people, and remained a captive for about three years

1 April 1153

Sanjar, who was defeated while trying to punish the Oghuz, fell captive in Muharram 548 (April 1153); he was freed from captivity in Ramadan 551 (October-November 1156). During his captivity the Khwarazm-Shah Atsiz, in the role of a loyal vassal, tried to protect his rights.
