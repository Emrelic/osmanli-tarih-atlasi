The Saltukid Ziya al-Din Ghazi routs the Georgian King Ivane

1131

Ziya al-Din Ghazi, who took part in the campaign mounted against the Georgians when in 1126 they passed İspir and Pasinler and came as far as Oltu, routed King Ivane in 1131.
