Hasan Tahsin Pasha left Salonica to the Greeks

8 November 1912

Hasan Tahsin Pasha, who had withdrawn to Salonica after the defeat at Yenice, left the city to the Greeks as the Bulgarian army approached from the north; the Greek army entered the city around noon on 8 November. Salonica, the largest port city of Ottoman Rumelia and the birthplace of the Committee of Union and Progress, was thus lost.
