Ibrahim Inal defeated the Byzantine army on the plain of Pasinler

1048

The year after Musa Yabgu's son Hasan was ambushed and martyred on the Byzantine frontier in 1047, Toghril Beg's maternal half-brother Ibrahim Inal defeated the Byzantine army on the plain of Pasinler and avenged Hasan.
