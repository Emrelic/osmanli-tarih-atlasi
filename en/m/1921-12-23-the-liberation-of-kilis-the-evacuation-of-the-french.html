The liberation of Kilis — the evacuation of the French

23 December 1921

In accordance with the evacuation timetable envisaged by the Ankara Agreement (20 October 1921), French forces withdrew from Kilis. The city had been occupied by the British three years earlier, on 6 December 1918, and handed over to the French on 29 October 1919.
