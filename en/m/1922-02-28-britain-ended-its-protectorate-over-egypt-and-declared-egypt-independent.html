Britain ended its protectorate over Egypt and declared Egypt independent

28 February 1922

By its declaration of 28 February 1922 Britain unilaterally ended the protectorate in force since 1914 and declared Egypt an independent state; defence, imperial communications and Sudan, among other matters, were reserved to Britain. Two weeks after the declaration (15 March 1922) Sultan Ahmad Fuad took the title of king. On the map the British occupation layer over Egyptian cities ends on this day.
