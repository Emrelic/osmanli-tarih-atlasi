The Execution of Prince Mustafa at Konya Ereğlisi

5 October 1553

Prince Mustafa, who entered his father's tent to join the army assembled for the Nakhchivan campaign, encountered the executioners just as he was about to appear before the sultan and was strangled to death. The killing of the heir apparent, who was greatly loved within the army, aroused deep anger among the Janissaries, and Rüstem Pasha was removed from the grand vizierate. The event passed into literature and popular memory as one of the most contested breaks in Ottoman princely politics.
