Muhammad Tapar was defeated by Berkyaruq in the final clash before Khoy

19 March 1103

Berkyaruq and Muhammad Tapar clashed five times; in the final clash before the city of Khoy (8 Jumada al-Akhira 496 / 19 March 1103) Tapar was defeated and went to Akhlat. Peace was made the following year.
