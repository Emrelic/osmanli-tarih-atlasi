Hafiz Osman appointed calligraphy teacher to Mustafa II

1695

Hafiz Osman, regarded as the most masterly calligrapher of his time, was appointed in 1695 calligraphy teacher to Mustafa II, who had just ascended the throne. In his lifetime he trained about fifty calligraphers and copied 25 Qur'ans.
