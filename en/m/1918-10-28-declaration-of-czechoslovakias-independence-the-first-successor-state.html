Declaration of Czechoslovakia's independence — the first successor state of the Habsburg legacy

28 October 1918

Following Austria-Hungary's loss of strength in the war and the strikes, demonstrations and uprisings within the country, the National Council declared Czechoslovakia's independence on 28 October 1918. A provisional national assembly was formed on the basis of the 1911 electoral situation, and the government with Masaryk as its head took office on 14 November 1918. The new state gained its place in international law by the Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye (10 September 1919). On the map Czechoslovakia appears on 11 November 1918; the declaration of independence is two weeks earlier. With this declaration Hradec Králové (Bohemia), Olomouc and Brno (Moravia) passed to Czechoslovakia.
