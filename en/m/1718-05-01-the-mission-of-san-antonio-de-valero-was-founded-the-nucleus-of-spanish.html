The mission of San Antonio de Valero was founded — the nucleus of Spanish Texas

1 May 1718

In response to the French infiltration westward from Louisiana, Spain decided to establish a permanent way station in Texas. Father Olivares founded the mission of San Antonio de Valero on the bank of the San Antonio River; a few days later the governor Alarcón placed the presidio of San Antonio de Béxar beside it. The mission building would later be known as the Alamo.
