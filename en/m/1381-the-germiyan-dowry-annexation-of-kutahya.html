The Germiyan dowry: annexation of Kütahya

1381

On the marriage of Devletşah Hatun, daughter of Süleyman Shah of the Germiyanids, to Bayezid the Thunderbolt, Kütahya, Simav, Tavşanlı and Eğrigöz passed to the Ottomans as a dowry — the classic example of expansion without war. Other places annexed on the same date: Afyon.
