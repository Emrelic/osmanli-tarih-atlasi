Mustafa IV acceded to the throne

29 May 1807

Immediately after the deposition of Selim III, his nephew Mustafa IV was proclaimed sultan. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Mustafa IV, the new sultan began his reign under the influence of the anti-Nizam-ı Cedid circles that had supported the rebellion. This short period was a phase of instability in which central authority weakened considerably.
