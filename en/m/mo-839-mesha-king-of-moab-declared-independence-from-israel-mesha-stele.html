Mesha, king of Moab, declared independence from Israel (Mesha Stele)

839 BC

The Mesha Stele recounts the liberation of Mesha, king of Moab, from Israelite overlordship and his victories against the dynasty of Omri. The Torah and the stele together show the period in which the house of Omri weakened.
