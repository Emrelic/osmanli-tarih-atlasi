The grand vizierate of Nevşehirli Damad Ibrahim Pasha

9 May 1718

According to the TDV article on 'Damad İbrâhim Paşa, Nevşehirli', during the ongoing peace negotiations with Austria he was brought to the office of grand vizier on 8 Jumada al-Akhira 1130 (9 May 1718) and, as soon as he was appointed, turned to the negotiations that would end the war; two months later the Treaty of Passarowitz was signed (21 July 1718). In the TDV's words, 'he wished to deal with social and financial matters and longed for a period of peace that would make people forget the wars that had ended in defeat for many years'. By making reforms in finance, he reduced the soldiers' pay and created a significant surplus in the state treasury. His grand vizierate, which would last twelve years, formed the political framework of the period that bears his name (the Tulip Period).
