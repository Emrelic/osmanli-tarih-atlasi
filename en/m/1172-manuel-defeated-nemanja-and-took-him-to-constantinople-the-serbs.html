Manuel defeated Nemanja and took him to Constantinople: the Serbs accepted Byzantine superiority

1172

When Stefan Nemanja, Grand Župan of Raška (Rascia), rose against Byzantium, Manuel defeated him in 1172 and took him in triumph to Constantinople; he was later released and caused no trouble until Manuel's death.
