The Placing of Egypt under British Protection and the Proclamation of a Sultanate

18 December 1914

Upon the Ottoman State's entry into the war, England declared that it was officially taking under its protection Egypt, which it had kept in effective occupation since 1882; the Khedive Abbas Hilmi Pasha, who persisted in his allegiance to the Ottomans, was dismissed and Hüseyin Kâmil was brought in his place with the title of 'sultan'. Thus the legal Ottoman sovereignty that had lasted since 1517 was ended unilaterally. The Sublime Porte did not recognise the decision and two months later attempted to retake Egypt with the Canal Operation.
