Battle of Gaugamela: Alexander decisively defeated the army of Darius III

1 October 330 BC

At Gaugamela, Darius's army, made up of many war chariots and cavalry, was scattered in the attack in which Alexander broke through, with his left wing holding on the defensive; Darius fled again. The battle sealed the end of the Persian Empire; Babylon and Susa opened their gates.
