Safiye Sultan gained influence as valide sultan

1 February 1595

Safiye Sultan, the consort of Murad III and the mother of Mehmed III, rose with her son's accession to a decisive position in palace politics as valide sultan. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, in this period influence stemming from the harem reached the point of direct interference in affairs of state.
