The allied army of the Turkish lords defeats the Georgian army near Lukri Castle

1 July 1163

More than 50,000 mujahids, including Eldiguz, Sökmen, Izz al-Din Saltuk, Ibn Aqsunqur and Arslanshah, invaded Georgia; in July 1163 the Georgians were defeated around Lukri Castle, King Giorgi abandoned his baggage and fled, and was forced to return Ani.
