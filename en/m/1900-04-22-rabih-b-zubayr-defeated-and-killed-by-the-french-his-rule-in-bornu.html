Rabih b. Zubayr defeated and killed by the French: his rule in Bornu dissolved

22 April 1900

Rabih, defeated by the French colonial army, was killed; although his son wished to continue the rule, he could not hold on, and Bornu was partitioned among France, Britain and Germany in 1902.
