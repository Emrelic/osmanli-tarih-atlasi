Second Council of Lyon: Byzantium accepted the pope's supremacy

6 July 1274

At the ultimatum of Pope Gregory X, the Grand Logothete George Akropolites accepted at Lyon on 6 July 1274, on behalf of the emperor, papal supremacy and the Roman faith. The union broke down in 1281.
