Austria's Entry into the War — A Struggle on Two Fronts

9 February 1788

In accordance with the secret alliance he had made with Russia in 1781, the Austrian emperor Joseph II declared war on the Ottoman State and moved in the direction of Belgrade and Bosnia. The Ottoman army was suddenly obliged to fight on two fronts at once, Russia in the north and Austria in the west. Despite the victories won against the Austrians at Lazarethane, Mehadia and Șebeș, the western front too would collapse with the loss of Belgrade in 1789.
