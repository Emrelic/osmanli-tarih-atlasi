Robert Guiscard landed at Valona and took Corfu

1081

In 1081 Guiscard marched with his fleet to Valona (Vlorë), took Corfu, and captured Dyrrhachium in 1082; the march on Constantinople was left unfinished when he was called to help Gregory VII in Rome.
