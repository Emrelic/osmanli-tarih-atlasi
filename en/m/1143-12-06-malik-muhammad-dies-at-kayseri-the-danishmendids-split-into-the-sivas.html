Malik Muhammad dies at Kayseri; the Danishmendids split into the Sivas, Malatya and Kayseri branches

6 December 1143

After Malik Muhammad, who according to Michael the Syrian died at Kayseri on 6 December 1143, his brother Yaghibasan established his rule at Sivas, his son Dhu al-Nun at Kayseri, and Ayn al-Dawla at Malatya and Elbistan; the Danishmendids divided into three branches and entered a destructive struggle among themselves.
