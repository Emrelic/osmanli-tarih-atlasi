Toghan Khan Ahmad died; Yusuf Qadir Khan asserted a claim to the great khaganate and asked Mahmud of Ghazna for help

1017

After the death (1017) of the Great Khagan Toghan Khan Ahmad, who had been fighting the nomadic Turks, Yusuf Qadir Khan, son of Harun Bughra Khan, refused to recognise Arslan Ilig Mansur as great khagan, wished to use the title himself, and asked Mahmud of Ghazna for help.
