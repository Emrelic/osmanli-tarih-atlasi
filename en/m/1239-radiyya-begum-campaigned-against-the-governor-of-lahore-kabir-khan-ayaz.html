Radiyya Begum campaigned against the Governor of Lahore, Kabir Khan Ayaz, and brought him to submission

1239

A year after the Gwalior campaign, Radiyya Begum campaigned against the Governor of Lahore, Malik Izz al-Din Kabir Khan Ayaz, and brought him to submission too; immediately afterward the more dangerous rebellion of the Governor of Tabarhind, Ikhtiyar al-Din Altuniyya, began.
