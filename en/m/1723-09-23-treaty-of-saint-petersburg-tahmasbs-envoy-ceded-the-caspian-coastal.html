Treaty of Saint Petersburg — Tahmasb's envoy ceded the Caspian coastal provinces to Russia

23 September 1723

In return for Russian military aid against the Afghans, the envoy of Tahmasp II ceded to Russia Derbent and Baku with their surroundings and the provinces of Gilan, Mazandaran and Astarabad. Tahmasp did not ratify the treaty; Russia kept garrisons in Derbent, Baku and Gilan but never sent troops to Mazandaran and Astarabad. The territorial provisions of the treaty were recognized the following year in the Treaty of Constantinople signed with the Ottomans. Salyan was captured by the Russian army following the capture of Baku in 1723 (Çaycıoğlu, Vakanüvis 2, p. 126); the Treaty of St. Petersburg was signed on 12 September 1723.
