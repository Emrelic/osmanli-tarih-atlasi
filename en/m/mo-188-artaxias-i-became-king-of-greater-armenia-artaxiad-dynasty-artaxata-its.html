Artaxias I became king of Greater Armenia: Artaxiad dynasty, Artaxata its capital

188 BC

Artaxias, a former general of Antiochus III, founded his kingdom in Greater Armenia and became the first king of the Artaxiad dynasty; according to Strabo, Artaxata was founded for him and became the royal residence of the country.
