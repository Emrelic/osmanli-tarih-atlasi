Szigetvár — death of the Lawgiver

7 September 1566

The 72-year-old Lawgiver died in his pavilion a few hours before the fall of the fortress of Szigetvár, which he was besieging in his thirteenth campaign; Sokollu Mehmed Pasha concealed the death for weeks so that the army would not disperse. The fortress was taken, but the state had lost its longest-reigning and most splendid sultan; his son Selim II came to the throne, and the weight of the grand viziers in the administration of the state began to increase.
