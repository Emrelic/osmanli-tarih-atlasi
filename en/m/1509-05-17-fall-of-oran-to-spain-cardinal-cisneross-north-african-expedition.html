Fall of Oran to Spain — Cardinal Cisneros's North African expedition

17 May 1509

Wahran (Oran), which controlled the western Mediterranean sea route and was the port of Tilimsan, had grown through the refuge there of Andalusian Muslims and Jews who fled Granada in 1492. The Kingdom of Castile, which took the port cities of the Maghrib coast one by one in the early sixteenth century, occupied Wahran on 17 May 1509; most of the population was massacred, the rest were taken captive, and no Muslim or Jewish population remained in the city. The Spaniards enlarged the walls and fortresses; Wahran remained in Spanish hands until 1708.
