Separatist native pharaohs emerged at Thebes: Upper Egypt broke away from Ptolemaic rule

204 BC

The native Egyptians who had been armed after the victory at Raphia, in Polybius's words puffed up by the victory, no longer obeyed the king's orders; uprisings broke out in the Delta and Upper Egypt. At Thebes the native pharaohs (Horwennefer, then Ankhwennefer) held Upper Egypt against the Ptolemies for about twenty years.
