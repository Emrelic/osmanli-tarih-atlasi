Conquest of Valona, Berat and Kanina

1417

In 1417 the Ottomans brought under their dominion Valona (Avlonya) on the Adriatic coast, together with the city of Berat and the Kanina Fortress in the interior of the country. A year later Venice's attempt to retake the city failed, and by the establishment of a sancak organization here Ottoman authority became permanent. The taking of Valona made the Ottomans for the first time the owner of a port directly on the Adriatic, giving them an overseas springboard facing Italy.
