Ragusa (Dubrovnik) and the southern Dalmatian cities swore fealty to Orseolo

1000

During Orseolo's Dalmatian campaign, Ragusa, the southernmost of the Dalmatian cities, met the doge's army with its envoys and its archbishop on the islet of San Massimo and offered allegiance to Venice.
