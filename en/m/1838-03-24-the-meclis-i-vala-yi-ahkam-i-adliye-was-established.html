The Meclis-i Vala-yı Ahkam-ı Adliye was established

24 March 1838

Mahmud II had the Meclis-i Vâlâ-yı Ahkâm-ı Adliyye established as a high-level council in which the laws and regulations to be prepared would be discussed. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on the Meclis-i Vâlâ-yı Ahkâm-ı Adliyye, the council actually began work on 31 March 1838, and nearly all the subsequent Tanzimat reforms were prepared and supervised through this body. The institution became one of the most important centres of the modern Ottoman bureaucracy.
