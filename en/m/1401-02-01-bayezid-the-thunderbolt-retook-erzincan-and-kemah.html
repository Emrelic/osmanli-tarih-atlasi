Bayezid the Thunderbolt retook Erzincan and Kemah

1 February 1401

Bayezid the Thunderbolt, taking advantage of Timur's setting out on his Syrian campaign, took Erzincan and Kemah from the hands of the emir of Erzincan, Mutahharten, who was subject to Timur. Although he had not intervened in the destruction of Sivas, this move amounted to a direct challenge to Timur's sphere of influence in eastern Anatolia. The relations of the two rulers thereafter became beyond repair.
