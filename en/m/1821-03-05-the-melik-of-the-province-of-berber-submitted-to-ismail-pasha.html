The melik of the province of Berber submitted to Ismail Pasha

5 March 1821

The army of Ismail Pasha, son of Mehmed Ali Pasha, entered the province of Berber on 3 March 1821; on 5 March the melik Nasir al-Din, who resided in the provincial centre, together with the sheikhs and notables, came out to meet Ismail Pasha and submitted to him.
