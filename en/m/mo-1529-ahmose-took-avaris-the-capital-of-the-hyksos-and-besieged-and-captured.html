Ahmose took Avaris, the capital of the Hyksos, and besieged and captured Sharuhen

1529 BC

Ahmose besieged and took Avaris in the Eastern Delta; the Hyksos fled to Asia, and he pursued them as far as Sharuhen in Southern Canaan. The inscription of Ahmose son of Ebana reports that Sharuhen was besieged for 6 years. Thus Egypt's Delta left Hyksos control and Egypt was in a position to exercise political influence in Canaan.
