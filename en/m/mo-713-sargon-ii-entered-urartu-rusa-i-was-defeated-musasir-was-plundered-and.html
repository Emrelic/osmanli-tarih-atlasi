Sargon II entered Urartu: Rusa I was defeated, Musasir was plundered and the statue of Haldi was taken

713 BC

Sargon's eighth campaign plundered Urartu from the Lake Urmia region southwards; the temple of Haldi at Musasir was robbed. King Rusa I, faced with the heavy defeat, took his own life (according to tradition). Urartu was never again able to make a major attempt against Assyria.
