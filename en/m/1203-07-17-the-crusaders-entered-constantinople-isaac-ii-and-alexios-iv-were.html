The Crusaders entered Constantinople; Isaac II and Alexios IV were seated on the throne

17 July 1203

The armies of the Fourth Crusade attacked Constantinople on 17 July 1203 and made Alexios III flee; Isaac II was raised to the throne again together with his son Alexios IV. The promises that Alexios made to the Crusaders could not be fulfilled afterwards.
