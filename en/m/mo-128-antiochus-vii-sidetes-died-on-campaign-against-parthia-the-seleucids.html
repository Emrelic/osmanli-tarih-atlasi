Antiochus VII Sidetes died on campaign against Parthia; the Seleucids could not recover Mesopotamia

128 BC

Antiochus VII Sidetes recovered Mesopotamia and Media from the Parthians in 130-129, but lost his army and his own life to a revolt in winter quarters and a Parthian attack. This failure ended the Seleucid claim to the East.
