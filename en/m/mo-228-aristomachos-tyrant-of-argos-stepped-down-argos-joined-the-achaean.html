Aristomachos, tyrant of Argos, stepped down; Argos joined the Achaean League

228 BC

Shortly after the death of Demetrius II and the first Roman crossing into Illyria, Aristomachos, tyrant of Argos, together with the tyrants of Hermione and Phlius, resigned under pressure from Aratos, and the city entered the Achaean League.
