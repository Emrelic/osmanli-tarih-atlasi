Cnut the Great was proclaimed king in Norway; Olaf Haraldsson fled

1028

Cnut came to Norway in 1028 with a fleet of fifty ships and was acclaimed king of Norway at the Øreting; King Olaf II Haraldsson fled first to Sweden and then to Rus' (Gardarike).
