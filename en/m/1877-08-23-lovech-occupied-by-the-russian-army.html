Lovech occupied by the Russian army

23 August 1877

In the '93 War, Lovech had been occupied by the Russians on 5 June 1877, and the Ottoman forces under Rifat Pasha had pushed the Russians back on 15 June; the city was abandoned by the Ottomans on 23 August and again came under Russian occupation. The map shows Lovech under Russian occupation (hatched) from this day until the Treaty of Berlin; legally it continues to be Ottoman territory.
