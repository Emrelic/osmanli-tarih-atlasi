The surrender of Kyrenia — the northern coast of Cyprus

17 September 1570

The fall of Nicosia on 9 September, put to the sword, broke the resistance in the north of Cyprus. The fortress of Kyrenia, behind the Pentadaktylos mountains, upon learning the fate of the capital, surrendered on terms eight days later, and its garrison was sent away, being allowed to leave the island. The Venetian flag remained on the island only at Famagusta; that too would hold out for eleven more months.
