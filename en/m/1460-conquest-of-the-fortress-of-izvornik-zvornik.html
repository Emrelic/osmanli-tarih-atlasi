Conquest of the fortress of Izvornik (Zvornik)

1460

The fortress of Izvornik on the Drina was conquered by the Ottomans in 1460. According to the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, because of its favorable position administratively and militarily, it was first made a kaza center, in 1480 it became the center of the sancak bearing the same name, and in 1491 it was fortified with stone walls and, with its six mosques, eight tekkes and baths, turned into an important administrative and commercial center.
