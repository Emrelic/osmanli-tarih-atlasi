The Kingdom of Kaffa joined Abyssinia — the last king Gaki Sherocho taken captive

1 October 1897

The forces of Menelik II under Ras Walda Giyorgis entered Kaffa in March 1897. After heavy fighting Kaffa was conquered; the last king, Gaki Sherocho, was captured and surrendered in October 1897.
