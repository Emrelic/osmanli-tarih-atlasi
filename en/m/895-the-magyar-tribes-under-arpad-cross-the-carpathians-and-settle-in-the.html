The Magyar tribes under Árpád cross the Carpathians and settle in the Carpathian Basin (895-896)

895

The Magyar tribes under Árpád crossed the Carpathians in 895-896 and made Transylvania and the Great Plain their homeland; around 900 they spread beyond the Danube (Pannonia). After the settlement Great Moravia collapsed.
