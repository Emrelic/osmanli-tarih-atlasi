Piye (Pankhy) came down into Egypt: he defeated Tefnakht's coalition and subdued the rulers of Memphis and the Delta

724 BC

Tefnakht of Sais had formed an alliance stretching from the Delta to Memphis and Middle Egypt and had besieged Herakleopolis. Piye, who set out on campaign from Napata, went north in his 21st year, took the allied cities (Hermopolis, Memphis, Heliopolis), and accepted the surrender of the Delta princes; he then returned to Napata. Thus Kush became the head of all Egypt (Twenty-fifth Dynasty).
