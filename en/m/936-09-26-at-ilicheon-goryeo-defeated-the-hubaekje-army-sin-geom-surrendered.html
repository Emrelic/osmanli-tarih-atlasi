At Ilicheon, Goryeo defeated the Hubaekje army; Sin-geom surrendered, Taejo entered the Hubaekje capital, and the Later Three Kingdoms were united

26 September 936

At the call of Gyeon Hwon, the founder of Hubaekje, Taejo assembled at Cheonan in the autumn of 936 and advanced to Ilsan-gun; with the appearance of a 'cloud-like sword' before Ilicheon and the surrender of the Hubaekje generals to Gyeon Hwon, the enemy was routed, 3,200 prisoners were taken and 5,700 were killed; Sin-geom and his brothers surrendered. Taejo entered the Hubaekje capital, ordered that the people not be touched, and thus united the three Han.
