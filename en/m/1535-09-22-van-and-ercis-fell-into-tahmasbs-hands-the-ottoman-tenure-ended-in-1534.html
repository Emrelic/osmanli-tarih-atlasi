Van and Erciş fell into Tahmasb's hands (the Ottoman tenure ended in 1534-35)

22 September 1535

In the region that had given Van to Ottoman domination in 1534, Ulama Pasha held out in the fortress of Van against a Safavid siege; the fortress passed into Safavid hands, and the Safavid forces besieging Erciş had been pushed back with Ottoman help. By the time Süleyman the Lawgiver reached Ahlat (23 September 1535), Van was already in Tahmasb's hands. Van remained Safavid until 1548.
