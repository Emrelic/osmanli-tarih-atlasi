Treaty of Bucharest — Western Thrace and the Kavala basin passed from Bulgaria to Greece

10 August 1913

The Second Balkan War ended with the Treaty of Bucharest signed between Bulgaria and Serbia, Greece and Montenegro. The Kavala, Drama and Serres basin, which had fallen into Bulgaria's hands in the first war, was left to Greece by this treaty. By this treaty Tutrakan (Turtukaya) passed to Romania.
