The Sultanate of Dar al-Kuti of Sultan Mohammed al-Sanusi eliminated by French forces

12 January 1911

The slave-trade-based Sultanate of Dar al-Kuti, with its capital at Ndele, which had come under French protection in 1897, was effectively eliminated by an expedition organized in 1911 after Sultan Mohammed al-Sanusi continued to resist French authority; Sanusi was killed in the fighting, and the sultanate was formally abolished in 1912.
