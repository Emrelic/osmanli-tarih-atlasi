The evacuation of Mocha

22 October 1635

Mustafa Bey evacuated Mocha with the remaining force and arms on 10 Jumada al-Awwal 1045 (22 October 1635); then Hasan b. Qasim, the son of Imam Qasim, appointed his own men to Zabid and Mocha and established Qasimi control on the Tihama coast as well.
