Mahmud defeated the combined army of the Karakhanids Nasr ibn Ali and Yusuf Qadir Khan near Balkh

1 December 1007

In Rabi' al-Awwal 398 (December 1007) Mahmud defeated the combined Karakhanid army near Balkh; the Karakhanids' last major attempt to take Khurasan thus ended in failure.
