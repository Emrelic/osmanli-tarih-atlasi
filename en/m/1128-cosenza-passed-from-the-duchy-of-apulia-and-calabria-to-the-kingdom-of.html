Cosenza passed from the Duchy of Apulia and Calabria to the Kingdom of Sicily (Roger II)

1128

Calabria became part of the Kingdom of Sicily with the passing of the duchy of Apulia and Calabria to Roger II (1128); no dated sentence specific to the city was found (elder-brother center Bari/Melfi chain).
