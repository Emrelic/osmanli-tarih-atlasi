Yusuf b. Tashfin landed at Algeciras; the first crossing to al-Andalus

30 June 1086

At the invitation of thirteen Andalusian emirs, Yusuf landed on the coast on 15 Rabi I 479 (30 June 1086), on condition that Algeciras be given to him, and crossed to Seville.
