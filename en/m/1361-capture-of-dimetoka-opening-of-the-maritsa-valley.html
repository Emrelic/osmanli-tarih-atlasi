Capture of Dimetoka — opening of the Maritsa valley

1361

Dimetoka (Didymoteicho), the key fortress of the Maritsa valley, was taken; the road to the conquest of Edirne was thus opened. It was not possible to march on Edirne before the corridor extending north from Gallipoli had been completed; for this reason the fortress comes before Edirne in the sequence of the Rumelian conquests. The city became for a time the Rumelian headquarters of the sultans. The forces that took Dimetoka then brought the whole of the Middle Maritsa region under control together with small fortresses such as Kuleli Burgaz (Pythion).
