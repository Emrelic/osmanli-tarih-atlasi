Petronius campaigned against Kush and took Pselchis, Premnis and Napata; he placed a garrison at Ibrim

22 BC

The prefect Petronius, with some ten thousand infantry and eight hundred cavalry, defeated thirty thousand Kushite soldiers, took Pselchis, captured the fortress of Premnis (Qasr Ibrim) at the first assault, and razed Napata, where the Candace's palace stood. Finding it difficult to go further, on his way back he left a garrison of four hundred men with two years' provisions at Premnis. When the Candace tried to retake Premnis, Petronius arrived in time and held the fortress.
