Rout at Zlatica — the 'Long Campaign'

24 November 1443

The army that Hunyadi led into the Balkans in midwinter passed Niš and Sofia and pressed up to Zlatica; the Ottoman defence held with difficulty. This atmosphere of rout brought the Peace of Edirne-Szeged to the table.
