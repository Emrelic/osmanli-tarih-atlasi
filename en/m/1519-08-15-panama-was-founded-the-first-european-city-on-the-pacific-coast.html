Panamá was founded — the first European city on the Pacific coast

15 August 1519

Pedrarias Dávila, governor of Darién, founded the city of Panamá on the Pacific side of the isthmus and moved the colonial administration there from the Caribbean coast. The city became the port of departure for the exploration and conquest expeditions directed to the south; Francisco Pizarro's Peruvian expeditions were fitted out from here.
