Second Mohács (Harsány) disaster — loss of Transylvania and of Mohács

12 August 1687

On the plain where the great victory had been won in 1526, a heavy defeat was suffered this time; the army dispersed, and the mutinous soldiers had the grand vizier dismissed. The defeat also led to the loss of Transylvania.
