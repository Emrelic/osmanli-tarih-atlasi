Kálmán conquers Croatia (1097-1102) and Dalmatia (1102-1105)

1102

Kálmán, who crushed the Croatian uprising, conquered Croatia between 1097 and 1102; between 1102 and 1105 he also brought the Dalmatian cities under Hungarian sovereignty (the beginning of Hungarian rule at Zadar is 1105 or 1107). The Dalmatian cities retained their municipal privileges.
