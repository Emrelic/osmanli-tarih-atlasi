Huizong abdicated; Qinzong became emperor at Kaifeng

19 January 1126

Under the Jin threat, Huizong took the title 'Daojun Huangdi' and handed the throne to the crown prince Zhao Huan (Qinzong); although Qinzong refused in tears and fell ill, the next day he received his officials in the Chuigong Hall and became emperor, and Huizong withdrew to the Longde Palace.
