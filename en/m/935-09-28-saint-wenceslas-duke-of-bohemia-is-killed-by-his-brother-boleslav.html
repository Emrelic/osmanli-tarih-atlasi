Saint Wenceslas, duke of Bohemia, is killed by his brother Boleslav

28 September 935

Wenceslas, an ally of the Germans, was killed in 935 at the hands of his brother Boleslav (later proclaimed a saint); this murder led to war with Germany. Under Boleslav and his son Boleslav II, Bohemia's borders were extended as far as Moravia, Silesia and Galicia.
