Rome ordered Rhodes to withdraw its governors from Lycia and Caria; the Lycian League (Patara) left Rhodian rule

166 BC

After the Third Macedonian War, the Roman Senate ordered Rhodes to withdraw its governors in Lycia and Caria by a certain day; Rhodes regarded these losses as lighter than the threat of war and accepted. The Lycian League (center Patara) thus left Rhodian rule and continued as the Lycian League under Roman protection.
