Amalric de Lusignan takes the title King of Cyprus; the same year he is also recognised as king of Jerusalem by the Crusaders at Acre

1197

Amalric took the title of king in 1197 and the same year was recognised as king of Jerusalem by the Crusaders at Acre; the Kingdom of Cyprus became the naval base of the Crusader East.
