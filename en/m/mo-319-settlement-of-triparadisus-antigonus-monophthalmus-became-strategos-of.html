Settlement of Triparadisus: Antigonus Monophthalmus became strategos of Asia

319 BC

After the murder of Perdiccas in Egypt (May 320), Antipater redistributed the satrapies at Triparadisus; Antigonus was placed at the head of the forces of Asia and Seleucus took Babylon. From this position Antigonus set about founding his own empire.
