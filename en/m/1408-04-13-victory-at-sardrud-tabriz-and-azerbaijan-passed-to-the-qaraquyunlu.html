Victory at Sardrud: Tabriz and Azerbaijan passed to the Qaraquyunlu

13 April 1408

Qara Yusuf defeated the Timurid Abu Bakr Mirza for a second time after the Aras, at Sardrud near Tabriz; this victory won Azerbaijan for the Qaraquyunlu. Qara Yusuf then charged Bistam Beg with the conquest of Persian Iraq;
