The Mongols seized Pagan; the Burmese kingdom temporarily dissolved, and the country was divided into principalities ruled by Tai chiefs

1287

Coedès writes that the capture of Pagan by the Mongols in 1287 led to the temporary disappearance of the Burmese kingdom and the division of the country into principalities governed by Tai chiefs; in the same year Mangrai, Ngam Muang and Rama Khamhaeng formed the Tai alliance. Narathihapate was remembered as 'Tarukphyi' ('the one who fled from the Mongols').
