The Peloponnesian War began: the Peloponnesian League mobilized against Athens

430 BC

With Thebes' night raid on Plataea (spring 431), the peace was effectively broken; at the beginning of summer the army of the Peloponnesian League under King Archidamus invaded Attica. The war lasted 27 years between Athens' naval empire and Sparta's land-based league.
