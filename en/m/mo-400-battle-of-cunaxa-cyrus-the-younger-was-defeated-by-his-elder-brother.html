Battle of Cunaxa: Cyrus the Younger was defeated by his elder brother Artaxerxes II and killed

400 BC

Cyrus the Younger marched on Babylon with the Ten Thousand, a force of Greek mercenaries, and was defeated at Cunaxa by his elder brother Artaxerxes II and killed in the battle. The Ten Thousand's march back to the Black Sea is described in Xenophon's Anabasis.
