Opening of the first coffeehouses in Istanbul

1 June 1554

The entry of coffee into Ottoman urban life began with the coffeehouses opened at Tahtakale by Hakem, a merchant from Aleppo, and a person named Shams from Damascus. In a short time a new public space was born in which people from every section of society gathered; in the last period of the Lawgiver there were fifty coffeehouses in Istanbul.
