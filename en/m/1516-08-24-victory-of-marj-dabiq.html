Victory of Marj Dabiq

24 August 1516

The Mamluk army was routed within a few hours at Marj Dabiq north of Aleppo; the aged sultan Qansuh al-Ghawri died during the battle. With the victory, which opened wide the gates of Aleppo and Damascus, Syria, Lebanon and Palestine passed to Ottoman rule; the way was opened for the Egyptian campaign that would bring about the end of the Mamluk state.
