Mamora (al-Mahdiyya) passes into Spanish hands

1614

The port of Mamora (Ma'mura / Mahdiyya) at the mouth of the Sebou river was taken by the Spaniards in 1614 and fortified under the name San Miguel de Ultramar. The TDV's Mulay Ismail entry records that the port was retaken from the Spaniards in a sudden raid in 1092 (1681) — that is, Spanish rule lasted sixty-seven years.
