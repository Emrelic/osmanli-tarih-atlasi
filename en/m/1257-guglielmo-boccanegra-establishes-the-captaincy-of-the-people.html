Guglielmo Boccanegra establishes the Captaincy of the People

1257

After the death of Frederick II in 1250, the bourgeoisie, facing the old and new noble families (Fieschi, Grimaldi, Doria, Spinola), established in 1257 under the leadership of Guglielmo Boccanegra the office of 'Captain of the People' (capitano del Popolo); Boccanegra's attempt at dictatorship was suppressed in 1262.
