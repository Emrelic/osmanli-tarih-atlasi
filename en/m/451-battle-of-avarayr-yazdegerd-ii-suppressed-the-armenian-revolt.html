Battle of Avarayr: Yazdegerd II suppressed the Armenian revolt

451

Armenian nobles under Vardan Mamikonian rose against the Sasanian imposition of Zoroastrianism; their army was defeated at Avarayr and Vardan died. Despite the political defeat, the Armenians were able to preserve their Christianity.
