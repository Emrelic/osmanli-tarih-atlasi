The League treasury was moved from Delos to Athens

453 BC

On the grounds of the defeat in the Egyptian expedition and fear of the Persian fleet, the common treasury was taken from Delos to Athens; the lists of the "one-sixtieth" share given to the Athenian goddess begin in this year. This step is interpreted as a sign that the League had in practice turned into an Athenian empire.
