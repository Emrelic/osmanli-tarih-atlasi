Big River (later Fort George, Chisasibi) — fur trade posts at the mouth of the La Grande

1803

In 1803, on the east coast of James Bay, in the La Grande (Big River) area, the North West Company and the Hudson's Bay Company entered into competition: the Nor'Wester Duncan McDougall established a post, while the HBC post was run by George Atkinson between 1803 and 1806. The North West Company eventually withdrew from the region (sources give 1805 and 1806). The HBC's Big River post closed in 1813; the permanent post named Fort George was established in 1837.
