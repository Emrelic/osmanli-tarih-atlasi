Sargon II was killed in battle in Tabal; Sennacherib ascended the throne

704 BC

Sargon II was killed on a campaign in Anatolia against Gurdi, king of Tabal, and his body could not be recovered. His son Sennacherib moved the court to Nineveh; his father's city of Dur-Šarrukin was abandoned and many provinces rose in revolt.
