Khan Maysalun — the Faisal period ended, Syria passed to the French mandate

24 July 1920

At Khan Maysalun on the Beirut–Damascus road the French inflicted a heavy defeat on the Syrian forces, and Faisal's Arab Kingdom in Damascus came to an end. France effectively established the mandate authority that had been granted to it at San Remo; the region extending from Aleppo to Damascus and from Alexandretta to Deir ez-Zor was bound to the French mandate administration.
