The opening to instruction of the Sahn-ı Semân madrasas

1 June 1470

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Sahn-ı Semân, these eight madrasas, established within the Fatih Complex, were designed as the highest-level educational institution of the period and formed the summit of the Ottoman ilmiye hierarchy. A professorship and graduation from here were regarded in the following centuries as the most prestigious step in the careers of kadı and müderris.
