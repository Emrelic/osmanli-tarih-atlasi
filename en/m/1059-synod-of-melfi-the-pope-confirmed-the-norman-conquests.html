Synod of Melfi: the pope confirmed the Norman conquests

1059

Nicholas II reversed the Eastern policy and at the Synod of Melfi made an alliance with the Normans and confirmed their conquests. This agreement sanctified the Norman rise that threatened the Byzantine presence in southern Italy.
