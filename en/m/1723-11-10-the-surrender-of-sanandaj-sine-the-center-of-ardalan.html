The surrender of Sanandaj (Sine), the center of Ardalan

10 November 1723

Hane Mehmed Pasha, the Baban bey sent against Ardalan after the capture of Kermanshah, took over Sanandaj, the center of the province; the liwas of Mihribân (Merivan), Bâne and Sakız were also joined to the Ottoman province of Ardalan. In 1727 a detailed Ottoman tahrir register was compiled for the province.
