Çeşme raid

6 July 1770

The Russian fleet, which had sailed into the Mediterranean with British support, raided the Ottoman fleet in the port of Çeşme, where it had taken refuge, with fire ships and burned it almost completely. Russia appeared for the first time as a great naval power in the Mediterranean; the disaster was one of the blows that determined the fate of the war leading to Küçük Kaynarca in 1774, and revealed the need for a radical reform of the fleet.
