Nukuleka (first Lapita coastal settlement), Tongatapu: Nukuleka: Tonga's first Lapita settlement

887 BC

Nukuleka is the site of the first Lapita landfall in Tonga and Polynesia (western-type Lapita pottery only here). The Lapita phase is short on Tongatapu (~129–158 years) and passes into Polynesian Plain Ware.
