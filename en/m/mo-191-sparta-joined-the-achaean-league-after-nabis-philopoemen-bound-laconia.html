Sparta joined the Achaean League: after Nabis, Philopoemen bound Laconia to the League

191 BC

After the murder of the Spartan tyrant Nabis (192), the Achaean strategos Philopoemen forcibly added Sparta to the Achaean League. Sparta lost its independence entirely in 188 with the demolition of its walls.
