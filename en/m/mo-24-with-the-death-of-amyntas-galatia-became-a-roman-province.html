With the death of Amyntas Galatia became a Roman province

24 BC

When Amyntas, the Galatian king appointed by Antonius, was killed in Cilicia (25 BC), Augustus turned Galatia into a Roman province and united it with Pisidia and Lycaonia. The capital was Ancyra (Ankara); Augustus's Res Gestae inscription (Monumentum Ancyranum) was written here.
