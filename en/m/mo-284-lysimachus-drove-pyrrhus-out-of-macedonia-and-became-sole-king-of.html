Lysimachus drove Pyrrhus out of Macedonia and became sole king of Macedonia

284 BC

After the winter of 285, when Demetrius surrendered to Seleucus, Lysimachus expelled Pyrrhus from Macedonia and ruled the country alone. His state reached a wide region comprising Thrace, Macedonia and western Anatolia.
