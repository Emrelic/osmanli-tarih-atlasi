Wallachia, Moldavia and Transylvania rose together against the Ottomans

13 November 1594

In mid-1594 the three voivodeships north of the Danube — Wallachia, Moldavia and Transylvania — decided to act jointly against the Ottomans. Aron Tiran, voivode of Moldavia, joined the Holy League formed by the Austrian Emperor and Pope Clement VIII and placed himself under the Principality of Transylvania; Michael the Brave, voivode of Wallachia, joined the movement out of discontent at the heavy burden of taxes. On 13 November 1594 simultaneous revolts against Ottoman rule began in Wallachia and Moldavia, and with the support of Transylvania the movement spilled south of the Danube into Ottoman lands. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, this triple alliance opened the northern front of the Ottoman–Habsburg Long War (1593–1606), which was in progress in the same years.
