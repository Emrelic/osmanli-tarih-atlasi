The Suppression of the Erzurum-Based Rebellion of Abaza Mehmed Pasha

22 September 1628

Abaza Mehmed Pasha, the beylerbeyi of Erzurum, who rose against the Janissaries with the claim of avenging the killing of Osman II, held Eastern Anatolia under his control for years and resisted the centre. He was forced to surrender at the siege of Erzurum by the Grand Vizier Hüsrev Pasha, and was pardoned and appointed to Bosnia. The end of the rebellion was the precondition for Murad IV to be able to march against Iran.
