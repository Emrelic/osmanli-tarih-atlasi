Passage of Nedroma and the Moroccan border belt to French control

1844

After Abd al-Qadir withdrew to Morocco in 1843, Nedroma and the Traras coast northwest of Tlemcen came under French control. The Battle of Isly on 14 August 1844 routed the Moroccan army, and the border between the two countries was drawn by the Convention of Lalla Maghnia of 18 March 1845; Nedroma remained on the Algerian side of this line.
