The Marinid Sultan Abu Yusuf entered Marrakesh; the Almohad state came to an end

9 September 1269

The Marinid Sultan Abu Yusuf Ya'qub entered Marrakesh on 10 Muharram 668 (9 September 1269) and stayed seven months; the Almohad caliph Abu Dabbus and the Almohad notables remaining in Marrakesh were eliminated at this time.
