Pyrrhus returned to the throne of Epirus with Ptolemy's support

295 BC

Pyrrhus, who had remained a hostage in Egypt after Ipsus, returned to Epirus with Ptolemy's help and regained the throne. After this Epirus had a say in Macedonian and Italian affairs.
