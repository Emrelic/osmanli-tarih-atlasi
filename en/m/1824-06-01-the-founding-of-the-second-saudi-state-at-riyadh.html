The Founding of the Second Saudi State at Riyadh

1 June 1824

Six years after the fall of Diriyah in 1818 and the destruction of the first Saudi State, Turki b. Abdullah expelled the Egyptian garrisons from Najd and founded a new Saudi emirate centred on Riyadh. The new structure avoided extending to the Hijaz, preferring to remain in Najd, and kept away from direct clashes with the Ottoman-Egyptian forces. This second state would last until the end of the century and would form the second link of the chain leading to the founding of present-day Saudi Arabia. Other settlements lost on the same date: Diriyah (Najd).
