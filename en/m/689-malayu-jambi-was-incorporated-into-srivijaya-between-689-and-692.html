Malayu (Jambi) was incorporated into Srivijaya (between 689 and 692, according to the testimony of Yijing)

689

The Chinese pilgrim Yijing reports that Malayu (the Jambi region), at which he had called in 671, was swallowed up by Shih-li-fo-shih (Srivijaya) between 689 and 692 (Coedès).
