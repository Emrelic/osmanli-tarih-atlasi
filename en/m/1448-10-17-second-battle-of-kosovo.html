Second Battle of Kosovo

17 October 1448

The army of John Hunyadi, who wished to avenge Varna, was decisively defeated by Murad II in a hard-fought three-day battle. This was the last great pitched battle the Crusaders undertook against the Ottomans in the Balkans; with the victory, Ottoman dominion south of the Danube became irreversibly established.
