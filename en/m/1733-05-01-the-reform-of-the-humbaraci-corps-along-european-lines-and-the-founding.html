The Reform of the Humbaracı Corps along European Lines and the Founding of the Humbarahâne

1 May 1733

Humbaracı Ahmed Pasha, of French origin, who had entered Ottoman service, with the support of the Grand Vizier Hekimoğlu Ali Pasha made the humbaracı corps a salaried unit organised in the European manner. Three hundred soldiers recruited from Bosnia were given regular training based on arithmetic and geometry; new regulations were prepared for organisation, equipment and retirement, and one hundred mortars and fifty thousand shells were manufactured with contemporary technique. The Humbarahâne, where geometry was taught, is regarded as the first nucleus of modern military engineering education in the Ottoman Empire.
