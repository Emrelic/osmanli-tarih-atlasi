The Mar'ashi sayyids surrendered to Timur at Mahaneser: Mazandaran passed to the Timurids

26 October 1392

Timur, who entered Mazandaran in his five-year campaign, pressed the Mar'ashis, who confronted him at Qaratogan, into the fortress of Mahaneser and received their surrender. A great massacre was carried out at Sari and Amul; Sari was given to Iskandar-i Shaykhi, son of Kiya Afrasiyab.
