The Republic of Venice came to an end: the Great Council dissolved itself

12 May 1797

Under Napoleon's pressure the Great Council dissolved itself on 12 May 1797 and Doge Manin resigned; the French occupation began, and the later fate of the Veneto was determined at Campo Formio.
