Chandax fell: Nikephoros Phokas recovered Crete

6 March 961

The Byzantine forces under Nikephoros Phokas captured Chandax (Candia) on 6 March 961 after a siege of a year; the Emirate of Crete, which had lasted some 130 years, came to an end. Part of the island's Muslim population left the island, and part was forced to convert.
