Ardahan fell into the hands of the Turkish army

3 April 1918

In the Elviye-i Selâse operation, the Ottoman army, after Artvin, took Ardahan on 3 April 1918; Ardahan had fallen in 1877 in the War of '93 and had been left to Russia in 1878. Kars would be taken a month later, on 25 April.
