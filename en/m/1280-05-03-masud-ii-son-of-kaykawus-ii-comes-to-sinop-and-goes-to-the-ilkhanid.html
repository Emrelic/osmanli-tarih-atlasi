Mas'ud II, son of Kaykawus II, comes to Sinop and goes to the Ilkhanid capital to appear before Abaqa Khan

3 May 1280

Mas'ud II, son and heir apparent of Kaykawus II, came to Sinop in 679/1280, then went to the Ilkhanid capital and appeared before Abaqa Khan; Abaqa Khan gave him the administration of Erzurum, Erzincan, Sivas, Diyarbakır and Harput.
