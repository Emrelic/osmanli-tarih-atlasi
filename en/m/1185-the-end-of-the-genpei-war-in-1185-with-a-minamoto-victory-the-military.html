The end of the Genpei War in 1185 with a Minamoto victory: the military government (bakufu) at Kamakura became the power of the whole country

1185

Minamoto no Yoritomo had made Kamakura his base in 1180; when he won the Genpei War (1180-85), Kamakura became the center of the military government that held actual power.
