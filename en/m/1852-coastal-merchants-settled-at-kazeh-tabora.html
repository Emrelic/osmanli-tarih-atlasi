Coastal merchants settled at Kazeh (Tabora)

1852

After a conflict with a local ruler, merchants from the East African coast migrated southward; Fundikira, chief of Unyanyembe, gave them a place to settle near his center at Itetemia. This settlement became the nucleus of the Tabora caravan center.
