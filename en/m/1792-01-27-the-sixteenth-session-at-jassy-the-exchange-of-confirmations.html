The sixteenth session at Jassy — the exchange of confirmations

27 January 1792

At the sixteenth and last meeting held on 27 January 1792 the confirmations were reciprocally exchanged. The conference was thus closed; only the exchange of the sovereigns' instruments of ratification remained.
