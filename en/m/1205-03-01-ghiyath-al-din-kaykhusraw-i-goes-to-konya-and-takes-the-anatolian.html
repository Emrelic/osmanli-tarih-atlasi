Ghiyath al-Din Kaykhusraw I goes to Konya and takes the Anatolian Seljuk throne a second time

1 March 1205

Kaykhusraw, who had had to go to his father-in-law's fortress in the Trabzon region after the Crusaders' occupation of Constantinople (April 1204), came to the city during the minority of Kilij Arslan III and sat on the throne a second time (Rajab 601 / March 1205).
