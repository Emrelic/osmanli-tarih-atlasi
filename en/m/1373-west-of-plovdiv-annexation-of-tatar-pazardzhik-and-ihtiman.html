West of Plovdiv: annexation of Tatar Pazardzhik and Ihtiman

1373

After the taking of Plovdiv (Filibe) in 1372, Tatar Pazardzhik at the upper end of the Maritsa valley and Ihtiman, which held the pass opening toward Sofia, were attached to Ottoman administration. This line, which was in the hands of the local lords who became vassals after the Battle of Chirmen, was placed under direct administration, making the Plovdiv-Sofia route continuous. This advance was the preparation for the road leading, twelve years later, to the conquest of Sofia.
