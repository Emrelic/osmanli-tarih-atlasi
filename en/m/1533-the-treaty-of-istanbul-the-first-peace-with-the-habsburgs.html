The Treaty of Istanbul — The First Peace with the Habsburgs

1533

The sultan, who received Ferdinand's envoy Cornelius, consented to a truce, and the first official peace text between the Ottomans and Austria came into being. By the treaty Ferdinand agreed to pay an annual tribute for the Hungarian lands he held, and the archduke of Austria was regarded in protocol as equal to the grand vizier. This unequal status was the clear expression of the Ottoman diplomacy's claim to superiority in the sixteenth century.
