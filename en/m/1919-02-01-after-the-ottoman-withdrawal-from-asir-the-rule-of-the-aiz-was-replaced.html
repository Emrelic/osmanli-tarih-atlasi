After the Ottoman withdrawal from Asir, the rule of the Āiz was replaced by Muhammad ibn Ali al-Idrīsī

1 February 1919

When Ottoman forces withdrew from Asir between October 1918 and February 1919, an authority vacuum arose in the region; the old Āiz family first seized control, but Muhammad ibn Ali al-Idrīsī soon took control of the region.
