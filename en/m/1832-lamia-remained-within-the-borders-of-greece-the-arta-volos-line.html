Lamia remained within the borders of Greece: the Arta-Volos line

1832

The northern border of the independent Greek state was drawn in 1832 on the line extending from the Gulf of Arta to the Gulf of Volos. Lamia (Izdin), which in the Ottoman period (1424-1832) was a kaza center attached to the sancak of Euboea, passed to Greece because it lay south of this line; the rest of Thessaly (Yenişehir, Tırhala) remained Ottoman until 1881.
