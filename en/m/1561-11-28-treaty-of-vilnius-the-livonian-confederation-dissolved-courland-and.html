Treaty of Vilnius — the Livonian Confederation dissolved; Courland and Livonia attached to Poland-Lithuania (except the city of Riga)

28 November 1561

Gotthard Kettler, the last great master of the Livonian Confederation, which was disintegrating under Russian pressure during the Livonian War, placed himself under the protection of Sigismund II Augustus, Grand Duke of Lithuania and King of Poland, by the treaty signed at Vilnius on 28 November 1561 (Pacta Subiectionis); most of Livonia, except the city of Riga, was attached to Poland-Lithuania, while Kettler became the first duke of the Duchy of Courland. This was the last act of the Livonian Order's passage from the Middle Ages to the early modern era.
