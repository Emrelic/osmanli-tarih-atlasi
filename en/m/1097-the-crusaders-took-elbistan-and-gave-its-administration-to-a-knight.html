The Crusaders took Elbistan and gave its administration to a knight

1097

Elbistan, one of the important cities on the Byzantine marches, passed into the hands of the Crusaders in 1097 and its administration was given to a knight named Pierre d'Aulps; it changed hands several times until 1103.
