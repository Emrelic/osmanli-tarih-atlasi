Completion of the first printed book, the Vankulu Dictionary

1 February 1729

According to the TDV article on 'İbrâhim Müteferrika', the Turkish translation of the Sihah al-Jawhari (the Vankulu Dictionary), the press's first work, was completed in early 1729. This was the first book printed in Arabic letters in Ottoman lands and is regarded as the beginning of the history of Turkish printing.
