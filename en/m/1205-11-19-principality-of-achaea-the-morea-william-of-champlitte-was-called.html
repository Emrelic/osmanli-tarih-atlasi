Principality of Achaea (the Morea): William of Champlitte was called 'Prince of Achaea'

19 November 1205

The conquest of the Morea by Geoffrey of Villehardouin and William of Champlitte (the victory at Koundoura) contributed to the passing of most of Greece into the hands of the Crusaders at the beginning of 1205. Innocent III's letter of 19 November 1205 refers to Champlitte with the title 'prince'; thus the Principality of Achaea is documented. Villehardouin took the title of prince before the end of 1209.
