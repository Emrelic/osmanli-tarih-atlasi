Antony gave Cleopatra Cyprus, Phoenicia, Coele-Syria and part of Cilicia as a gift

35 BC

According to Plutarch, when Antony came to Syria he gave Cleopatra Phoenicia, Coele-Syria, Cyprus, most of Cilicia, the balsam-producing part of Judea, and the part of Nabataea reaching to the sea. Thus the Ptolemaic state, for the first time since Ptolemy III, controlled a wide area in the Eastern Mediterranean (despite Rome's anger).
