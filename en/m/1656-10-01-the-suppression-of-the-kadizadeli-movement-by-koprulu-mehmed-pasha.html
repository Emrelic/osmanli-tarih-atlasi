The suppression of the Kadızadeli movement by Köprülü Mehmed Pasha

1 October 1656

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on the Kadızâdeliler, as soon as he took office Köprülü Mehmed Pasha took up the religious conflict between the circle of Kadızâde Mehmed Efendi and the followers of the Sufi orders. After consulting the ulema, although he had death decrees issued for the leaders of the movement, Üstüvânî Mehmed Efendi, Türk Ahmed and Divâne Mustafa, this penalty was commuted to exile, and the three were sent to Cyprus; thus the second phase of the movement ended.
