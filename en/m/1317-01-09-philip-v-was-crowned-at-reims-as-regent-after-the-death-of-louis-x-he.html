Philip V was crowned at Reims (as regent after the death of Louis X, he had proclaimed himself king upon the death of his nephew John I)

9 January 1317

Louis X died on 5 July 1316; the regent Philip V proclaimed himself king after the posthumous son John I, who died 4 days after his birth, and was crowned at Reims on 9 January 1317. The day of the proclamation is not in the source; the atlas takes the day of the coronation.
