Evacuation of the Shipka Pass: breaching of the Balkan line

19 July 1877

The Ottoman forces at Shipka, the most strategic of the passes crossing the Balkan mountains, resisted the Russian attacks fiercely; but when they became convinced that they would be defeated, they secretly evacuated the pass on 19 July. Thus the second line of defense was also opened and General Gurko took Eski Zagra on 22 July. Although Süleyman Pasha, arriving from the Montenegrin front, defeated Gurko and recovered the occupied places south of the Balkans, he was unable to retake Shipka despite attacks lasting months from 21 August. On the map this means that a unified defense of the northern and southern sides of the Balkan range was never re-established: from now on the struggle on the Danube front would centre on Plevna.
