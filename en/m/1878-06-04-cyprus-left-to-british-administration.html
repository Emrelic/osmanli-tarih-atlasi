Cyprus left to British administration

4 June 1878

In return for an alliance against the Russian threat, the administration of Cyprus was transferred to Britain; legally it remained Ottoman land. Other settlements lost on the same date: Nicosia, Famagusta, Kyrenia, Limassol, Larnaca (Tuzla), Paphos, Morphou, Akrotiri, Episkopi, Dhekelia; that is, the whole of the island.
