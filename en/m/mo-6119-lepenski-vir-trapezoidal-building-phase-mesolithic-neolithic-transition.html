Lepenski Vir trapezoidal building phase (Mesolithic-Neolithic transition, LV I-II): begins

6119 BC

The phase of trapezoidal limestone floor structures and decorated boulders (stone sculptures); in the article it is short (80–210 years, Model 2) and is followed by the Starčevo Early Neolithic.
