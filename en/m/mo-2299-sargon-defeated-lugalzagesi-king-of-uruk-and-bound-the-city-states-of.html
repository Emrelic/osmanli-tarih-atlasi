Sargon defeated Lugalzagesi, king of Uruk, and bound the city-states of Sumer to Akkad

2299 BC

Sargon, king of Agade (Akkad), defeated Lugalzagesi, king of Uruk, and gathered the Sumerian cities (Uruk, Ur, Lagash, Umma, the Kish region) into one hand. Akkad became the first empire to rule the whole of southern Mesopotamia.
