Alexander IV was killed with his mother Roxane; the Argead dynasty ended

309 BC

After the peace of 311, on the orders of Cassander (according to Diodorus, the executioner Glaucias), the twelve-year-old Alexander IV and his mother Roxane were killed. The Empire of Alexander was left without a nominal ruler; the Diadochi moved toward proclaiming their own kingdoms (306-305 BC).
