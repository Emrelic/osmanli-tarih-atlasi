The fortress of Stavropol was founded on the Azov–Mozdok line

1777

On the Azov–Mozdok line of fortifications, founded after Küçük Kaynarca to control the steppe between the Kuban and the Terek, ten fortresses were built; one of them was Stavropol. The fortress later became the centre of the Caucasus governorship and of the governorate of Stavropol.
