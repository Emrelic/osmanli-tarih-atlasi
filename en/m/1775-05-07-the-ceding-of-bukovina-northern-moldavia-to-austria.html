The Ceding of Bukovina (Northern Moldavia) to Austria

7 May 1775

Exploiting the weakness caused by Küçük Kaynarca, Austria demanded Bukovina, the region linking Moldavia and Transylvania, and obtained this land without going to war, through threats and bribery. The loss of this fertile and strategic region southwest of Khotin effectively split the voivodeship of Moldavia in two. This cession, made without a single shot being fired, was regarded as one of the clearest signs that the Ottoman State had lost its deterrent power. With the cession of Bukovina in 1775, Suceava and Czernowitz passed to Austria (its day could not be verified).
