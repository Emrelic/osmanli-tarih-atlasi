Šibenik again came under the administration of the Republic of Venice

1412

After Ladislas of Naples sold his rights over Dalmatia to Venice in 1409, Šibenik, which resisted, again came under Venetian administration in 1412. The city remained a Venetian possession in Dalmatia until the Republic fell in 1797.
