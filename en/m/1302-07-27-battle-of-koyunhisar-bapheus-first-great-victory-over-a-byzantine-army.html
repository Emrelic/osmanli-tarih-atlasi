Battle of Koyunhisar (Bapheus) — first great victory over a Byzantine army

27 July 1302

TDV, Osman I entry: on 27 July 1302 Osman Bey fought the Byzantine army at Bapheus (Koyunhisar) and defeated it. This victory is the turning point that raised Osman Bey from being a regional frontier lord to the status of founder of a dynasty. After the battle the plain of Bursa came under Ottoman control and the blockade of Nicaea began.
