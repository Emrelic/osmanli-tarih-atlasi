Chengdu: ownerless interval → Cheng-Han (成漢)

304

At Chengdu, ownership passes from the ownerless interval to Cheng-Han (成漢). Transition source information: Jin shu 121 (Li Xiong): Luo Te opened the gate and let Li Xiong in, 'took Chengdu'; in Yongxing 1 (304) he proclaimed himself 'King of Chengdu'. End: Li Shi (Jiaming 2, 3rd month, 17th day) letter of surrender to Huan Wen.
