Caliph al-Mu'tadid took the governorship of Transoxiana from Isma'il ibn Ahmad and gave it to Amr ibn Layth

19 February 898

At Amr ibn Layth's request, the caliph dismissed the Samanid emir Isma'il ibn Ahmad from the governorship of Transoxiana and appointed Amr in his place (23 Muharram 285 / 19 February 898); this decision led to war between the Saffarids and the Samanids.
