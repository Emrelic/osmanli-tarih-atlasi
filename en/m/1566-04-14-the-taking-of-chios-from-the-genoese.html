The taking of Chios from the Genoese

14 April 1566

Chios, which had been administered for two hundred years as a trading colony belonging to the Genoese Giustiniani family and paid tribute to the Ottomans, was taken over by Piyâle Pasha without a single shot being fired, on the grounds that it had defaulted on its tribute and had given harbour to enemy ships. Together with Chios and Psara, the last Genoese presence on the eastern shore of the Aegean was also liquidated. With the Chians' mastic production and privileges preserved, the island was attached to the province of Cezâyir-i Bahr-i Sefîd.
