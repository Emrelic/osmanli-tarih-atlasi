The Naval Victory of Djerba

14 May 1560

The Ottoman fleet under Piyale Pasha scattered the allied Spanish-Genoese-Maltese fleet, assembled to retake Tripoli, off Djerba with a manoeuvre of splitting into two. The allies lost the greater part of their fleets, and the fortress on the island fell shortly afterwards. Djerba is regarded as the second great naval victory, after Preveza, that cemented Ottoman superiority in the Mediterranean.
