Napoleon's siege of Acre: halting of the Syrian campaign

18 March 1799

Wishing to disperse the forces the Ottomans were preparing to retake Egypt, Napoleon marched into Syria in early 1799; he took El Arish on 18 February and Jaffa in March, and besieged Acre on 18 March 1799. The defense of Cezzar Ahmed Pasha and the support of the British admiral Sidney Smith from the sea thwarted the siege for two months; because the siege guns had been captured at sea, the walls could not be breached. Napoleon withdrew in May, and this defeat marked the farthest point of the French occupation.
