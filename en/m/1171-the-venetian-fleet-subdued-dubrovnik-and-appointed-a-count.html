The Venetian fleet subdued Dubrovnik and appointed a count

1171

In reprisal for Manuel's arrest of the Venetians, the Venetian fleet, setting out in September 1171, attacked Dubrovnik (Ragusa) on the way; the city surrendered and received a "count" from Venice.
