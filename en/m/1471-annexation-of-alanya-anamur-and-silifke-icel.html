Annexation of Alanya, Anamur and Silifke (İçel)

1471

The İçel coastal strip dependent on the Karamanids (the whole of the Mediterranean between Antalya and Mersin) was annexed by the naval campaign of Gedik Ahmed Pasha; Alanya was important with its strategic shipyard dating from the Seljuk period.
