Drake sets out from Plymouth with five ships

15 November 1577

With the secret approval of Queen Elizabeth I, Francis Drake left Plymouth with a fleet under the command of the Pelican (later the Golden Hind); the goal was to raid the Spanish colonies in the Pacific.
