The Xuanwu Gate incident: Li Shimin killed the crown prince Jiancheng and Yuanji

2 July 626

Li Shimin (later Taizong), son of the Tang founder Gaozu, set an ambush at the Xuanwu Gate of the Chang'an palace and killed his brothers, the crown prince Jiancheng and Yuanji; within a few days he was proclaimed crown prince.
