Opening of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey

23 April 1920

After the dissolution of the Chamber of Deputies in Istanbul, the deputies assembled in Ankara and opened the Grand National Assembly of Turkey, and Mustafa Kemal Pasha was elected chairman of the assembly. According to the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi entry on the Grand National Assembly of Turkey, the assembly had an extraordinary character, concentrating both legislative and executive authority in itself. This opening was the turning point at which the political and institutional center of the national struggle moved to Ankara. On the map, with this event 124 settlements (the unoccupied regions of Turkey), including Konya, Sivas, Kayseri, Eskişehir, Erzurum, Trabzon, Samsun, Diyarbakır, Van, Malatya, Denizli and Isparta, pass to the administration of the Grand National Assembly government.
