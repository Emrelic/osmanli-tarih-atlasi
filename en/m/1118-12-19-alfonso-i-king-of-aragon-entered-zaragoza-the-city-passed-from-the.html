Alfonso I, King of Aragon, entered Zaragoza; the city passed from the Almoravids to Aragon

19 December 1118

Zaragoza, whose provisions ran out after a long siege, accepted the terms of surrender on 11 December 1118; Alfonso I entered the city on 18 December. Zaragoza became the capital of the Kingdom of Aragon.
