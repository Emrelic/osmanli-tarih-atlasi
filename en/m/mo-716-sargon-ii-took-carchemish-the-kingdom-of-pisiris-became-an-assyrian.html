Sargon II took Carchemish; the kingdom of Pisiris became an Assyrian province

716 BC

Pisiris, king of Carchemish, tried to form an alliance with Midas (Mita) against Assyria; Sargon took the city and carried out deportations. Carchemish's independence of a century and a half came to an end and the Euphrates frontier passed to Assyria.
