Treaty of Moscow — the GNAT and Soviet Russia determined the eastern border

16 March 1921

The Treaty of Moscow was signed on 16 March 1921 between the Grand National Assembly government and Soviet Russia. According to the TDV entry on Kars, as a result of the border arrangements of this treaty and of the Treaty of Kars of 13 October 1921, Kars remained within the borders of the new Turkish state. According to the entry on Akhaltsikhe, Akhaltsikhe was attached by this treaty to the Tbilisi province of the Georgian Soviet Socialist Republic. The eastern border would be finalized seven months later by the Treaty of Kars signed with the South Caucasian republics. By this treaty Batum and Adjara (Khulo, Sarp, Murvaneti, Makhalakauri) were left to Soviet Georgia.
