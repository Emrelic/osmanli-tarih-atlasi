The Memphis synod issued the Rosetta Decree: Ptolemy V granted tax exemptions to the temples

27 March 195 BC

The synod that the Egyptian priests assembled at Memphis confirmed Ptolemy V's accession and proclaimed the king's tax grants to the temples, the suppression of the revolts, and the establishment of the royal cult. The decree was written in Greek, Demotic and hieroglyphs; the Rosetta Stone is this trilingual text. The text also refers to the suppression of the rebels in the Delta (Lykopolis).
