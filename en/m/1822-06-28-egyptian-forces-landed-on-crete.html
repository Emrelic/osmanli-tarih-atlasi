Egyptian forces landed on Crete

28 June 1822

When the Greek revolt spread to Crete, the Sublime Porte requested that an Egyptian force be sent to the island. Mehmed Ali's influence on the island began with this intervention and, eight years later, culminated in the granting of the governorship of Crete to him.
