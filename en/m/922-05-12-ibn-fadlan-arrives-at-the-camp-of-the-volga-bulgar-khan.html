Ibn Fadlan arrives at the camp of the Volga Bulgar khan

12 May 922

After a journey of about eleven months the delegation reaches the Bulgar khan; Ibn Fadlan's Risala on the Rus merchants, the Oghuz and Bulgar customs comes out of this journey.
