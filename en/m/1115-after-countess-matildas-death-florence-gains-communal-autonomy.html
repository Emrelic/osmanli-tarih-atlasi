After Countess Matilda's death Florence gains communal autonomy

1115

After the death in 1115 of Matilda of Canossa, Florence was for the first time granted urban autonomy; the first consuls are mentioned in 1138. The commune subsequently defeated Fiesole (1125) and the nobles of the county.
