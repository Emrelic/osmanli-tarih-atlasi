Nadir's offensive: loss of Tabriz, Nakhchivan, Hamadan, Kermanshah, Maragheh and Qasr-i Shirin

12 August 1730

The Western Iranian cities taken in 1723-1727 by exploiting the collapse of the Safavid State were lost one by one to the offensive of Nadir Khan, who seized actual power in Iran. In Azerbaijan Ardabil and Khalkhal also fell into Nadir's hands. The loss of Hamadan, Kermanshah and Qasr-i Shirin had a heavy echo in Istanbul; the army's not setting out on campaign but waiting at Üsküdar and the attempt to cover up the loss were among the direct causes of the Patrona Halil Revolt that broke out six weeks later. The map also shows Kotur, which is considered to have shared the same fate as Hoy, as having passed from the Ottomans to the Safavids on this day (Uluerler 2015, Turkish Studies 10/5, p. 6; no day specific to Kotur in the source — approximate information).
