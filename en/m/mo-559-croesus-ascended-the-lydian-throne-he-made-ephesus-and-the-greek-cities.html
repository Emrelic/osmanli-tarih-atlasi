Croesus ascended the Lydian throne; he made Ephesus and the Greek cities of Ionia tributary

559 BC

After the death of his father Alyattes, Croesus made Ephesus tributary, and then the other Greek cities of Ionia, Aeolis and Caria. Lydia was the single political unit from western Anatolia as far as the Halys; Croesus's treasure and his gifts to Delphi became legendary.
