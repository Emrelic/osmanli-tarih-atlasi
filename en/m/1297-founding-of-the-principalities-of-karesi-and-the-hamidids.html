Founding of the principalities of Karesi and the Hamidids

1297

According to the TDV's Karesioğulları entry, Karesi Beg, declaring his independence in the Seljuk frontier organization that had dissolved with the death of his father Kalem Beg, made Balıkesir his center from 696 (1296-97) and soon took from Byzantium the whole of Mysia, including Erdek, Biga, Edremit and Bergama. In the same year (1297) Hamid Beg, making Uluborlu the seat of government, founded the Principality of the Hamidids; Isparta, Burdur, Eğirdir and Yalvaç were added to the principality's first borders from the dissolved Seljuk frontier region. Both principalities are products of the same collapse in Western Anatolia — the disintegration of Seljuk central authority.
