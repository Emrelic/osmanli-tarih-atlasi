Death of Timur Shah, accession of Zaman Shah (Durrani State)

1793

With the death of Timur Shah, who had spent more than twenty years consolidating the country internally, his three sons, the governors of Kandahar, Herat and Kabul, contended for the throne; Zaman Shah, the governor of Kabul, who held the capital, became shah on 20 May 1793. This succession crisis is the de facto beginning of the dynasty's process of fragmentation.
