Cologne (Colonia Claudia Ara Agrippinensium): ownerless interval → Roman Empire (Principate-Dominate, combined)

356

At Cologne (Colonia Claudia Ara Agrippinensium), ownership passes from the ownerless interval to the Roman Empire (Principate-Dominate, combined). Transition source information: Ammianus 16.3.2: Julian entered Cologne, made peace with the Frankish kings, and received the city back as 'most strongly fortified'.
