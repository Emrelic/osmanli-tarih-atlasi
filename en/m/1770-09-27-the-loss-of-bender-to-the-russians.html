The loss of Bender to the Russians

27 September 1770

After the Kartal Plain debacle the Ottoman army had withdrawn south of the Danube, and the fortresses between Moldavia and Özi had been left alone. Bender, which held the crossing point of the Dniester, was taken by assault after a two-month siege; the city was plundered. The loss of the fortress severed the middle link of the northern Black Sea defence line, and Akkerman and Kilia fell after it. At Küçük Kaynarca Bender would be returned, but it would be lost once again in 1806.
