Arrest of Thököly at Várad — the Kingdom of Middle Hungary dissolved, the fortresses of Upper Hungary surrendered

15 October 1685

After the Vienna disaster, the Ottomans had begun to count Thököly too among those responsible for the war and the defeat. On 15 October 1685, on the order of the serdar Melek İbrahim Pasha, Ahmed Pasha, the beylerbeyi of Várad, had Thököly, who had come to help, seized; it was hoped to obtain better peace terms by handing him over to the Habsburgs. When news of the arrest spread, all the fortresses and cities of the Kingdom of Middle Hungary, except Munkács, defended by his wife Ilona Zrínyi, surrendered to the Habsburgs; most of the Kuruc soldiers went over to the Habsburg army. When Thököly was released in Belgrade on 2 January 1686, the kingdom had already ceased to exist.
