Sanganakallu-Kupgal: expansion of the hilltop village (Late Neolithic–Megalithic transition)

1399 BC

Late Neolithic granite hilltop villages in the Tungabhadra basin; growth between 1400-1200 BCE, and the near-total abandonment of the hilltop settlements in ~1200 BCE (iron almost absent).
