Abd al-Mu'min established a camp at Rabat (Ribat al-Fath) for the conquests in al-Andalus

30 April 1150

Abd al-Mu'min al-Kumi established in 545 (1150), at the site of the ribat, a camp to be used as a military base for the conquests in al-Andalus; the city later became Rabat.
