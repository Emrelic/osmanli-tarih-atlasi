The Normans take Sfax (together with Mahdia and Gabès, 1148)

1148

In 1148 Roger II's fleet seized Mahdia, the capital of the Zirids, as well as Gabès and Sfax; these conquests sent the Almohad caliph Abd al-Mu'min on a campaign into the eastern Maghrib.
