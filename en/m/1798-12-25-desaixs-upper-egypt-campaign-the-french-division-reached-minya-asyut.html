Desaix's Upper Egypt campaign — the French division reached Minya, Asyut and Girga

25 December 1798

While Napoleon was staying in Cairo, General Desaix's division advanced south in the Nile valley, pursuing the Mamluk forces: it reached Minya on 21 December 1798, Asyut on Christmas Day, and Girga on 29 December. With this march, which reached Aswan in February 1799, the valley between Cairo and Aswan came under French control.
