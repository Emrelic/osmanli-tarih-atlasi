Diet of Besançon: Burgundian nobles offer homage to Frederick; the papal-imperial conflict rises

1157

At the diet assembled at Besançon in October 1157, the Burgundian nobles swore an oath of allegiance to Frederick; because of the seizure of the Swedish archbishop on his return from Rome, the papal-imperial dispute grew.
