Shimao (Shenmu, Shaanxi): the city is founded

2299 BC

The largest known late Longshan/early Erlitou stone-walled city in China (≥4 km²). Construction and use 2200-1900 BCE from timber 14C in the Huangchengtai east guard wall; decayed wood of the large hilltop building F2 1900-1800 BCE.
