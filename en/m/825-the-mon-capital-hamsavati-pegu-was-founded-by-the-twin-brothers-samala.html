The Mon capital Hamsavati (Pegu) was founded by the twin brothers Samala and Vimala of Thaton (825, according to the chronicle)

825

Coedès writes that the center of gravity of the Mon country shifted westward; that a chronicle gives 825 as the founding date of Pegu (Hamsavati), and that this date may be preferred to the earlier or later dates given by other texts.
