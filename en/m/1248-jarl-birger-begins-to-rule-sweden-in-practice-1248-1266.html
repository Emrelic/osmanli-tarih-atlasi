Jarl Birger begins to rule Sweden in practice (1248-1266)

1248

Jarl Birger, regarded as Sweden's greatest medieval statesman, effectively governed the country from 1248 to 1266 and strengthened royal authority; Magnus Ladulås (1275-1290) continued this.
