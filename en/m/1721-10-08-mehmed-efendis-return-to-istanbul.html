Mehmed Efendi's return to Istanbul

8 October 1721

According to the TDV article on 'Yirmisekiz Çelebi Mehmed Efendi', the observations of the ambassador, returning from his one-year embassy in Paris, influenced the architectural taste of the Tulip Period. His son's later working together with Ibrahim Müteferrika prepared the ground for the founding of the first Turkish printing press, the embassy's most lasting legacy.
