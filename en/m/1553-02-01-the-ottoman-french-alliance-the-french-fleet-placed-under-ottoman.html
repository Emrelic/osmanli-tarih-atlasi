The Ottoman-French Alliance — The French Fleet Placed under Ottoman Command

1 February 1553

By the treaty dated 1 February 1553 the French fleet was placed under the command of the Ottoman fleet; joint operations against Spain in the Western Mediterranean were envisaged. Following the treaty, Turgut Reis set out on 15 June 1553 with forty-five galleys to support France. This was the first official alliance treaty that carried the commercial basis of the 1536 capitulations over into a military cooperation.
