Mas'ud overthrew his brother Malikshah with the help of the Danishmendid Emir Ghazi and took the throne of Konya

16 May 1116

Mas'ud took the rulership from his brother Malikshah, who had ruled in Konya since 503 (1110) and had struggled for six years with Alexios, with the help of his father-in-law, the Danishmendid Emir Ghazi (510/1116); Malikshah was blinded and killed the following year.
