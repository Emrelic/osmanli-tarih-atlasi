Sulayman Shah captured Antioch; the citadel fell on 12 January 1085

12 December 1084

Sulayman Shah, who went to Antioch with his sons Kilij Arslan and Da'ud on the news that the people of Antioch would surrender the city, took the city on 12 December 1084; the citadel, which resisted for a time, fell on 12 January 1085. The city had remained in Byzantine hands for more than a century, until 1084.
