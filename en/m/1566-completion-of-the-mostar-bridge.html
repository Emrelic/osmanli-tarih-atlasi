Completion of the Mostar Bridge

1566

The famous Mostar Bridge over the Neretva river was built around 1566-67 by Mimar Hayreddin; the mention of the bridge in an official document dated 1568 confirms that it had been completed. The structure is one of the best-known examples of classical Ottoman bridge architecture with a single pointed arch.
