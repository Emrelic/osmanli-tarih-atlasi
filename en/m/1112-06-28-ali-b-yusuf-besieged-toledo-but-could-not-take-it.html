Ali b. Yusuf besieged Toledo but could not take it

28 June 1112

During Ali b. Yusuf's second crossing to al-Andalus, Toledo was besieged; the siege was lifted in the face of strong resistance (506/1112-13), though Madrid and some centres in its surroundings were seized.
