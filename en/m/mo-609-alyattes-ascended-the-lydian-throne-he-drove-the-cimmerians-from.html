Alyattes ascended the Lydian throne; he drove the Cimmerians from Anatolia and expanded the kingdom

609 BC

During his long reign Alyattes cleared the Cimmerians from western Anatolia and fought Miletus; he carried the frontiers of the kingdom into the middle of Anatolia until he met Media on the Halys frontier.
