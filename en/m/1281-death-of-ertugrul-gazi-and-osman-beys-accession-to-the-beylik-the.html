Death of Ertuğrul Gazi and Osman Bey's accession to the beylik — the legacy of Söğüt and Domaniç

1281

According to TDV, Ertuğrul Gazi died in the year 680 (1281-82); the entry notes that the dates 1288 and 1289 are also given. Osman Bey inherited from his father not a conquered city but two grants that the Seljuk sultan had made to Ertuğrul, whom he had settled on the most advanced line of his frontier region: winter quarters (yurtluk) at Söğüt and summer pasture (yaylak) at Domaniç. That is, the beylik began not with an inheritance of conquest but with two grants of pasture; on the map these two places are shown as 'inheritance', not 'conquest'.
