The Diriyah Alliance — The Founding of the First Saudi Emirate in Najd

1744

The emir Muhammad b. Saud, who took under his protection in Diriyah the scholar Muhammad b. Abd al-Wahhab, expelled from Uyaynah, formed with him an alliance that placed political authority at the service of the religious call. This agreement began a new history for the Saud family and united the scattered oasis emirates of Najd around a single movement. This power, which within half a century would extend to Karbala, Mecca and Medina, would force the Ottoman State to defend its dominion in the Hijaz.
