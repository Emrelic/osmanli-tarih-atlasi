Karim Khan Zand took control of Isfahan — Afsharid rule in Iran gave way to Zand administration

1751

After the death of Nadir Shah (1747), Isfahan and Persian Iraq were governed in the name of the Afsharid shahs Adil Shah (6 July 1747) and Ibrahim Shah (8 December 1748). Karim Khan Zand returned to Isfahan in January 1751 and took control of the situation. Khorasan remained with the Afsharids under Shahrukh.
