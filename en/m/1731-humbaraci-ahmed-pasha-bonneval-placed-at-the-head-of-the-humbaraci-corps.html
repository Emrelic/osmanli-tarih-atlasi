Humbaracı Ahmed Pasha (Bonneval) placed at the head of the Humbaracı Corps

1731

The French-born officer whose real name was Claude-Alexandre Comte de Bonneval, after serving first in the French and Austrian armies, converted to Islam in Sarajevo, took the name Ahmed, and took refuge in the Ottoman State in 1729. Ahmed Pasha, who was placed at the head of the Humbaracı Corps in 1731 during the reign of Mahmud I, had about 300 men brought from Bosnia drilled in the barracks he had built at Üsküdar Ayazma; their numbers soon exceeded 600, and he himself also gave mathematics lessons. His attempt at an uprising in Hungary with Rákóczi in favour of France shook his influence, and in 1738 he was exiled to Kastamonu. He died in Istanbul on 23 May 1747, and his grave is in the cemetery of the Galata Mevlevi Lodge.
