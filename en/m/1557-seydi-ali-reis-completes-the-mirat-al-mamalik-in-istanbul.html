Seydi Ali Reis completes the Mir'at al-Mamalik in Istanbul

1557

Seydi Ali Reis completed in Istanbul in January 1557 the travel work called Mir'at al-Mamalik, which he wrote during the return journey beginning from Baghdad. The work is a first-hand source recounting an Ottoman seaman's return to his homeland overland from the Indian Ocean via Central Asia.
