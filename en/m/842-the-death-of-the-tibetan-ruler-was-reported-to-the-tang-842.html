The death of the Tibetan ruler was reported to the Tang (842)

842

The death of the Tibetan tsenpo occurred in the 2nd year of Huichang (842); in the 12th month the Tibetan envoys Lun Zan and his colleagues reported the death to the Tang court, and the Tang sent Li Jing, Assistant Director of Works (將作少監), to offer condolences. The Jiu Tang shu does not give the cause of death (assassination).
