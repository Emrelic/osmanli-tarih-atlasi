Nurbanu Valide Sultan died

7 December 1583

Nurbanu Sultan, the mother of Murad III, died on 22 Dhu al-Qa'da 991 (7 December 1583). According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, as valide sultan she had conducted correspondence with the courts of Venice and France, and the Venetian Senate had agreed that same year to send her a gift worth 2000 Venetian gold pieces in return for her services.
