Angrok (Ken Arok) overthrew the king of Kadiri; Singhasari, centered on Tumapel, replaced Kadiri (1222) and united the former territories of Janggala

1222

According to Coedès, Angrok, who took over the government of Tumapel, northeast of Malang, toward the end of Kertajaya's reign (1222), brought the former Janggala under his own power and then rebelled against the king of Kadiri; the founding of the new dynasty at Tumapel is regarded as the reunification of the two sister kingdoms. Janggala included the Malang region and the port of Surabaya. (The Malang area, where Tumapel lies (former Janggala).)
