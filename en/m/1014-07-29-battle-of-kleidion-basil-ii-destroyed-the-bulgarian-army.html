Battle of Kleidion: Basil II destroyed the Bulgarian army

29 July 1014

On 29 July 1014, Nikephoros Xiphias outflanked the Bulgarian position at the pass of Kleidion ('the Key') in the Belasitsa mountains in the Strymon valley. Samuel escaped to Prilep with the help of his son; about 15,000 captured Bulgarian soldiers were blinded.
