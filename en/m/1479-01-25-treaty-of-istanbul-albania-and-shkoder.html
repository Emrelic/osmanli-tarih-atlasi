Treaty of Istanbul — Albania and Shkodër

25 January 1479

The 16-year Ottoman-Venetian war ended; Shkodër and the Albanian coasts remained with the Ottomans. Other places annexed on the same date: Lemnos, Samos, Bozbaba (Agios Efstratios).
