Köprülü Mehmed Pasha appointed grand vizier on conditional acceptance

15 September 1656

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Köprülü Mehmed Pasha, Köprülü Mehmed Pasha, nearly eighty years old, took the seal only after having Valide Turhan Sultan and Mehmed IV accept the heavy conditions he presented — that his appointments not be interfered with and that what was said against him not be believed. He took office on 26 Dhu al-Qa'da 1066 in place of Boynueğri Mehmed Pasha and set about re-establishing state discipline severely.
