Proclamation of the State of Slovenes, Croats and Serbs — the Croatian Sabor severed the Habsburg ties

29 October 1918

On 29 October 1918 the Croatian Sabor declared that Croatia, Slavonia and Dalmatia had severed all their constitutional ties with Austria-Hungary; on the same day the founding of the State of Slovenes, Croats and Serbs was celebrated in Ljubljana. In Bosnia-Herzegovina, the Austro-Hungarian administration transferred authority to the government of the National Council on 1 November 1918. This state lasted until the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes was proclaimed in Belgrade on 1 December 1918.
