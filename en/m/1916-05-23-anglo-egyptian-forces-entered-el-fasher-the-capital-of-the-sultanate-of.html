Anglo-Egyptian forces entered El Fasher — the capital of the Sultanate of Darfur fell

23 May 1916

The Anglo-Egyptian forces, having defeated Sultan Ali Dinar's army at Beringia, entered the capital El Fasher on 23 May 1916; Ali Dinar withdrew toward Jebel Marra. When the Sultan was killed on 6 November 1916, the lands of Darfur were attached to Anglo-Egyptian Sudan.
