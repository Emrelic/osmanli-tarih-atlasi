Van is founded on its new site — the ruined old city is abandoned

2 April 1918

Van was freed from occupation on 2 April 1918. Those who returned, instead of repairing the old Van on the lake shore that had been ruined in the war years, founded a new city about 7 km inland from the shore on the site of the old Norşen and Arak quarters. The old city was abandoned.
