Qajar Muhammad Hasan Khan was defeated and killed by Karim Khan Zand's army; Astarabad, Mazandaran and Gilan passed to Zand administration

13 February 1759

Karim Khan's army inflicted a heavy defeat on the Qajars; Muhammad Hasan Khan was killed as he fled, and his son Agha Muhammad was taken to Shiraz. The Qajar principality's lands on the Caspian coast came under Zand administration.
