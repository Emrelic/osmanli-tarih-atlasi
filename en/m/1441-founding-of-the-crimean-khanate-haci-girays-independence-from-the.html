Founding of the Crimean Khanate: Hacı Giray's independence from the Golden Horde

1441

According to the TDV's Crimea entry, the Crimean Khanate emerged as a product of the fragmentation of the Golden Horde through struggles for the throne in the second half of the fourteenth century; the earliest coin struck by Hacı Giray, the true founder of the khanate, bears the date 845 (1441-42), and this is regarded as the official date of the khanate's founding. In the same year Hacı Giray consolidated his position in Crimea by establishing friendship with Moscow, and local beg families, the Shirin tribe foremost among them, joined his side.
