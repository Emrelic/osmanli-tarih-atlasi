Preliminary peace of Ofen: Styria (Graz) to the Hungarian King Béla IV, Austria to Ottokar II

3 April 1254

When the Babenberg line died out in 1246, King Ottokar II of Bohemia and King Béla IV of Hungary contended for Austria and Styria; in the preliminary peace signed at Ofen on 3 April 1254, Ottokar kept Austria and northern Styria, and Béla took the rest of Styria (Graz).
