Spartocus became archon in the Bosporus: the Spartocid dynasty

437 BC

Spartocus, of Thracian origin, replaced the Archaeanactid dynasty at Panticapaeum (438/7); the Spartocid dynasty founded the Bosporan kingdom and managed grain exports to Athens. The dynasty lasted until 109 BCE.
