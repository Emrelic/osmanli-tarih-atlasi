Conquest of Gemlik (Kios)

1334

The coastal town of Gemlik (Kios) on the Gulf of Gemlik, which opens onto the Marmara, passed into Ottoman hands. When Bursa surrendered in 1326, the city's tekfur had been sent to Gemlik with his family; that is, Gemlik was still in Byzantine hands in 1326. No separate date was found for Armutlu; it is tied to the Gemlik chain.
