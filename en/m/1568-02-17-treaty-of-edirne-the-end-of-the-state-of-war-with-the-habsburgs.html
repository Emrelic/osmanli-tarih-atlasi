Treaty of Edirne — the end of the state of war with the Habsburgs

17 February 1568

Envoys of the Habsburgs, the Safavids, Poland, France and Venice were present before Selim II, who wintered in Edirne after the Szigetvár campaign. It was decided that peace with the Safavids would continue; a treaty was signed that ended the state of war with the Habsburgs. The influence of the grand vizier Sokullu Mehmed Pasha was great in these affairs.
