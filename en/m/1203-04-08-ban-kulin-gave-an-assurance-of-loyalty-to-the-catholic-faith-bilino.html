Ban Kulin gave an assurance of loyalty to the Catholic faith (Bilino Polje)

8 April 1203

When Kulin, Ban of Bosnia (c. 1180-1204), was accused of sheltering the Patarenes (Bogomils), Pope Innocent III asked the Hungarian king to purge them. On 8 April 1203, in the presence of the papal legate, Kulin gave an assurance of loyalty to Catholic doctrine.
