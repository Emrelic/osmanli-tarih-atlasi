The western Anatolian coast passed to Alexander: Ephesus, Miletus and Halicarnassus

333 BC

Ephesus opened its gates under a democratic regime, Miletus was taken at the end of a siege (summer 334); the siege of Halicarnassus ended in the autumn with the fall of the city, and the satrapy of Caria was given to Ada. Alexander thus applied his strategy of seizing the Persian fleet's ports from the land.
