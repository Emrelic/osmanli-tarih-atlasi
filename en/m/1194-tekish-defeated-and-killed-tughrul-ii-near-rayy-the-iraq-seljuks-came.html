Tekish defeated and killed Tughrul II near Rayy; the Iraq Seljuks came to an end and Iraq-i Ajam passed to the Khwarazm-Shahs

1194

In 1194, in the battle near Rayy, the Khwarazm-Shah Tekish defeated the Iraq Seljuk Sultan Tughrul II (Tughrul II was killed in the battle, 590/1194), put an end to the Iraq Seljuks, and gained rule over Iran and Iraq-i Ajam; he began to use the title of sultan. The Caliph al-Nasir li-Din Allah did not accept this and, although he fought over part of Iraq, was unsuccessful.
