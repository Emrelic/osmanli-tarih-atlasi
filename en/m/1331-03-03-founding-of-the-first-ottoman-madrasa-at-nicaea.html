Founding of the first Ottoman madrasa at Nicaea

3 March 1331

Immediately after taking Nicaea (İznik), Orhan Bey appointed Çandarlı Kara Halil kadi here, converted a basilica into a mosque, and began the city's development by having an imaret and a madrasa built beside it. According to the TDV entry Davud-i Kayseri, the construction of the madrasa was completed in 1336, and Orhan Gazi brought Davud-i Kayseri to its professorship at 30 akçe a day. This madrasa is the first institutional step of Ottoman scholarly life; the beylik thus added a scholarly tradition to its conquering power. The transformation of a Byzantine city taken by conquest into a centre of Islamic learning was the first concrete sign of the Ottoman claim to be an established state.
