The caliph proclaimed Alp Arslan sultan in a magnificent ceremony

27 April 1064

The caliph, pleased that Alp Arslan had sent his uncle's widow Sayyida Khatun to Baghdad, proclaimed Alp Arslan sultan in an assembly he convened (7 Jumada al-Ula 456 / 27 April 1064); Alp Arslan had united the lands of Chaghri Beg and Toghril Beg and stood at the head of an empire extending from the Oxus to the Tigris.
