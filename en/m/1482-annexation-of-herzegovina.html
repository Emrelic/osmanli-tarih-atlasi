Annexation of Herzegovina

1482

After the conquest of Bosnia in 1463, the lands of the Duchy of Herzegovina, which had remained standing, passed stage by stage to Ottoman administration: Trebinje and Mostar were taken in 1466, the sancak of Herzegovina was organized in 1470, and the last lands of the duchy on the Adriatic coast, together with Herseknovi (Herceg Novi), were conquered in early 1482 and joined to the sancak. The mountainous region behind the Adriatic thus became wholly Ottoman, and Bosnia and Herzegovina became a single administrative geography. The son of the last duke, who entered the Ottoman court, was Hersekzade Ahmed Pasha, who would later rise to the grand vizierate.
