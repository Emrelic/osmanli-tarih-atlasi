Tzimiskes took Great Preslav by storm: eastern Bulgaria was annexed to Byzantium

13 April 971

John Tzimiskes entered the plain of Preslav on 12 April 971; on the evening of 13 April the Bulgarian capital, defended by Sviatoslav's men, fell. Tsar Boris was taken captive and Byzantine administration spread over Thrace and eastern Bulgaria.
