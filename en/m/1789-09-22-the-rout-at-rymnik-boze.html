The rout at Rymnik (Boze)

22 September 1789

The Ottoman army suffered a heavy rout on 22 September 1789 while crossing the Rymnik river and then on the bank of the Boza water. This second defeat, coming seven weeks after Focșani, in effect broke up the Wallachian front and led to the surrender of the fortresses on the Dniester line in October-November: the TDV entry on Koca Yûsuf Pasha gives the surrender of Izmail on 11 October and of Bender on 14 November. However, the surrender of Izmail in 1789 does not occur in the TDV entry ismail (the fortress is still in Ottoman hands in 1790), and ESBE also writes that after Rymnik only Hacıbey and Akkerman were taken and that Bender surrendered on 3 November (Julian; = 14 November); TDV akkirman also records that the fortress was taken by the Russians in 1789. The surrender of 11 October is most probably that of Akkerman — since the day could not be confirmed for Akkerman in the source read, it is not shown separately on the map.
