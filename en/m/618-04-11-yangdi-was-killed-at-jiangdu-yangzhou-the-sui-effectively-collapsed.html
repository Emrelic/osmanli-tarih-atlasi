Yangdi was killed at Jiangdu (Yangzhou); the Sui effectively collapsed

11 April 618

Yuwen Huaji had the Sui Retired Emperor Yangdi killed in the Jiangdu Palace, made Yang Hao a puppet emperor and proclaimed himself prime minister; the Tang dynasty was founded a few months later.
