Darüşşafaka school begins instruction

28 June 1873

Darüşşafaka, founded by the Cemiyet-i Tedrisiyye-i Islamiyye, actually began instruction on 28 June 1873 to educate orphaned and poor Muslim children free of charge as boarders; the admission age was set at a maximum of ten.
