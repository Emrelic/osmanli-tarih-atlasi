Conquest of Plovdiv and Stara Zagora

1 June 1372

Plovdiv, upstream in the Maritsa valley, and Stara Zagora to its north were taken; the Bulgarian tsardom was made tributary. The Tatar Pazardzhik and Ihtiman route between Plovdiv and Sofia remained in the hands of local lords who became vassals after Chirmen. Other settlements lost on the same date: Haskovo, Klokotnitsa.
