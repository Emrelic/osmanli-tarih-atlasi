Death of Sheikh Hamdullah and the legacy of the calligraphic school

1520

Sheikh Hamdullah of Amasya, as the calligraphy teacher of Bayezid II, brought the school of the aqlam-i sitte (the six scripts) to maturity and became the founder of an Ottoman calligraphic style that would dominate the entire Islamic world. When he died in 1520, he left behind a broad circle of students.
