The Yuan built a circumvallation (huancheng) to besiege Xiangyang

18 August 1270

The Yuan began to draw a siege wall (huancheng) around Xiangyang to besiege it; in the 9th month the Yuan commanders A'zhu, Hada and Liu Zheng fought at the Guanzi shoal against the Song general Fan Wenhu's relief fleet of 2,000 ships.
