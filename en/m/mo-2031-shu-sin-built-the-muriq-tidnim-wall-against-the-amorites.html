Shu-Sin built the "Muriq-tidnim" wall against the Amorites

2031 BC

Shu-Sin, king of Ur III, built a long wall line (Muriq-tidnim, "that which keeps the Westerners at a distance") between the Tigris and the Euphrates to stop the Amorite nomads (Martu) coming from the west. The wall shows that Amorite pressure was straining the state; Ur III soon collapsed under this pressure and an Elamite attack.
