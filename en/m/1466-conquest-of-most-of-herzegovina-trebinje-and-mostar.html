Conquest of most of Herzegovina: Trebinje and Mostar

1466

The lands of Stjepan Vukčić-Kosača, Duke of Herzegovina, who had established an administration separate from Bosnia, passed largely to Ottoman administration three years after the fall of the Kingdom of Bosnia: in the south, Trebinje was taken in 1466 "together with almost the whole of the Herzegovina region". Mostar, which grew up around the two small fortresses of the bridge over the Neretva, must also have been taken shortly before the conquest of Blagaj, the centre of Herzegovina, in 1466-1468; it is recorded under the name "Köprülü Hisar" in the Bosnian summary survey (icmal tahrir) of 1468-1469. The conquest of Herzegovina, with the remaining fortresses of the duchy, was completed in 1483.
