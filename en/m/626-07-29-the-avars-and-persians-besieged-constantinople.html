The Avars and Persians besieged Constantinople

29 July 626

The Avar khagan and his vassal tribes encamped before the walls of Constantinople on 29 July 626; the Persian army had also descended on the Asian shore. The city survived the siege through stiff resistance and the success of the fleet.
