Conquest of Edirne

5 May 1361

The capture of Edirne (Adrianople), the key city of Thrace, determined the main base and direction of the Balkan conquests; the date of the conquest is disputed in the sources between 1361 and 1369. The city was soon made the capital, announcing that the centre of gravity of the state had shifted to Rumelia; it remained the seat of the dynasty (payitaht) for about 90 years, until the conquest of Constantinople.
