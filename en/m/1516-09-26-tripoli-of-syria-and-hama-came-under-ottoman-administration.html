Tripoli of Syria and Hama came under Ottoman administration

26 September 1516

After Aleppo and Hama, Tripoli of Syria on the Syrian coast also passed to Ottoman administration without resistance. The city, one of the busiest ports of the eastern Mediterranean, was Damascus's gate to the sea and the centre of the silk and sugar trade. With its taking, the coastal strip of Bilad al-Sham changed hands in its entirety.
