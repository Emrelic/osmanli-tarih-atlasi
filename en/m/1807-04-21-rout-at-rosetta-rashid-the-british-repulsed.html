Rout at Rosetta (Rashid) — the British repulsed

21 April 1807

The British column that entered the town of Rosetta suffered heavy losses in street fighting; in September the forces evacuated Egypt entirely. This victory consolidated Mehmed Ali's standing both in Egypt and in Istanbul.
