Laudon took the fortress of Novi on the Una

3 October 1788

After Dubica, Laudon marched on the fortress of Novi on the Una and took it on 3 October 1788; the kapudan Cerić and all the garrison were taken prisoner. Upon the loss of the two fortresses a general mobilization was proclaimed in Bosnia. Novi was returned by the Treaty of Sistova of 1791.
