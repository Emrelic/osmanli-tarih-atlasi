Suppiluliuma I defeated Mitanni (Tushratta) and took Aleppo and Carchemish; northern Syria passed to the Hittites

1344 BC

Suppiluliuma defeated the Mitanni king Tushratta and took the cities of Syria, including Carchemish and Aleppo; the western part of the Mitanni lands was added to the Hittite Empire. The internal problems of Egypt in the time of Akhenaten facilitated this expansion.
