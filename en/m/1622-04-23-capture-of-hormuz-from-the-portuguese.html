Capture of Hormuz from the Portuguese

23 April 1622

Safavid forces besieged and retook Hormuz, which had been in Portuguese hands since 1515; Qeshm and Gamrun (Bandar Abbas) on the coast also passed to Safavid administration, and Gulf trade shifted to the new port.
