Yoritomo destroyed the Oshu Fujiwara and conquered Mutsu-Dewa; Hiraizumi came under Kamakura rule (1189)

1189

According to the Cambridge History, the Fujiwara branch in Mutsu-Dewa remained independent of central control until Minamoto no Yoritomo's conquest of 1189; the last leader, Yasuhira (1155-89), died that year.
