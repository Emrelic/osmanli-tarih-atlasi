The expeditions of Harkhuf, governor of Elephantine, to the land of Yam: diplomatic and commercial ties beyond Nubia

2279 BC

Harkhuf, governor of Elephantine, made several expeditions south to the land of Yam for Merenre and Pepi II; his inscription describes the route he mastered and the products he brought back. The letter that the child king Pepi II wrote to him is also preserved in the tomb inscription. These expeditions are records of contact with the pre-Kerma/Early Kerma communities on the Old Kingdom's southern frontier.
