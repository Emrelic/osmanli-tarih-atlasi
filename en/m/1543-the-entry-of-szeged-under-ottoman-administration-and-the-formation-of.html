The entry of Szeged under Ottoman administration and the formation of the liva of Szeged

1543

After Ottoman administration was fully established in Buda in 1541, the notables of Szeged adopted the sovereignty of Ferdinand I and prepared for armed defence. Küçük Bâlî Pasha, the beylerbeyi of Buda, in early 1543 summoned the city's leaders to him and had them executed on grounds of treason, then sent a force to seize the city. At this time the liva of Szeged was formed, and Derviş Bey, the son of Bâlî Pasha, was appointed as the first sancak bey; the liva at first extended between the Danube and the Tisza.
