Tvrtko I took control of the Dalmatian coastal cities (including Šibenik)

1 June 1390

Tvrtko I, King of Bosnia, took control in 1390 of the Dalmatian coastal cities other than Zadar; Šibenik also left Hungarian control and recognized the overlordship of the Bosnian king.
