Aurelian defeated the Palmyrene Empire: Zenobia's eastern lands returned to Rome

272

In 272 Aurelian defeated Zenobia's army at Antioch and Emesa and besieged Palmyra; Zenobia was captured. Syria, Egypt, and the south of Anatolia were again tied to Rome.
