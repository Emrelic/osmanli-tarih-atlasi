The French blockade of the Algerian coast — the three-year siege

16 June 1827

After the fan incident, France put the Algerian coast under blockade. The blockade lasted three years; the ocak's maritime trade and sources of revenue were dried up, and its capacity to resist was eroded before the occupation began.
