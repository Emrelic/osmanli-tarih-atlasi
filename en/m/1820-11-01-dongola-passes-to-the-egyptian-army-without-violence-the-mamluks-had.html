Dongola passes to the Egyptian army without violence (the Mamluks had left in June 1820)

1 November 1820

Dongola, on the great bend of the Nile, passed without violence into the hands of Ismail Pasha's Egyptian army after the Mamluks abandoned it in June 1820. On the map Sudan appears from this date in the subordinate layer: Cairo is the conqueror and Cairo will be the ruler.
