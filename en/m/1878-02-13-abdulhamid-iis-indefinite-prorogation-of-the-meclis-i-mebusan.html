Abdülhamid II's Indefinite Prorogation of the Meclis-i Meb'ûsan

13 February 1878

Upon the sharp criticisms of the deputies, who held the government and the palace responsible for the disastrous outcome of the war, Abdülhamid II prorogued the assembly indefinitely, relying on the authority granted him by the Kanûn-ı Esâsî. The first Ottoman parliament, which had worked for about eleven months, was thus closed, and the era of despotism (istibdat), which would last thirty years, began. The assembly would be able to meet again only after the Revolution of 1908.
