Liquidation of the Knights of Malta — a stop on the French fleet's way

12 June 1798

The French fleet sailing to Egypt called at Malta on its way; the Knights of St. John (Malta) surrendered the island without much resistance. The last stronghold of the knightly order, which had been expelled from Rhodes in 1522 and settled here, thus came to an end. Malta would fall into British hands two years later, while Napoleon headed directly from the island to Alexandria.
