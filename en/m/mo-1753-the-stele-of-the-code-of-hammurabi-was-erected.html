The stele of the Code of Hammurabi was erected

1753 BC

Hammurabi's legal compilation of 282 articles was inscribed on a basalt stele and set up in temples. The stele was carried to Susa by the Elamite king Shutruk-Nahhunte in the twelfth century BC and was found there in 1901; it is today in the Louvre.
