Peace of Szatmár — Rákóczi's war of freedom ended, Transylvania passed to Habsburg administration

29 April 1711

The compromise reached between Sándor Károlyi, commander of the Kuruc army, and János Pálffy, the imperial commander-in-chief, ended the Rákóczi uprising that had lasted since 1703; the privileges of the Hungarian nobles and the constitutional existence of Transylvania were preserved, but the country in effect came under the administration of the Habsburg ruler. The Habsburg period of the Ottoman-vassal Principality of Transylvania, begun in 1699, was thus made final.
