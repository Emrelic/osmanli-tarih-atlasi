A short-lived artillery school attempt led by Baron de Tott

1 October 1772

According to the TDV article on the 'Mühendishâne-i Bahrî-i Hümâyun', the failures of the Ottoman-Russian War of 1768 laid bare the need for technical military education; the short-lived artillery school opened with the contribution of the French officer Baron de Tott began operation in October 1772 but remained ineffective. This attempt is regarded as the forerunner of the Mühendishâne-i Bahrî-i Hümâyun to be founded in 1775.
