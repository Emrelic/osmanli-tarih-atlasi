Michael the Brave's raids on the Danube fortresses — Ruse burned, Brăila taken, Silistra plundered

1595

Michael the Brave, Voivode of Wallachia, who had joined the Holy League, fell in the winter of 1594–95 upon the fortresses and towns on the Danube that were under direct Ottoman administration. According to the TDV article on Ruse, although he could not take the fortress in February 1595, he seized the town and burned it. According to the article on Brăila, he captured Brăila on the left bank of the Danube, and the town remained in his hands until 1601; the article on Murad III also writes that the Wallachian army burned the fortress of Brăila and reached as far as Silistra. According to a survey record cited in the article on Silistra, the Wallachian army crossed the Danube in the spring of 1595 and plundered Silistra; the town's fortress was destroyed and could not be used for a long time. That summer Koca Sinan Pasha would set out on a campaign against Wallachia.
