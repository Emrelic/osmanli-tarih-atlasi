The Third Punic War ended: Carthage was destroyed and the province of Africa was founded

145 BC

After a three-year siege, Publius Cornelius Scipio Aemilianus took Carthage in the spring of 146; the city was burned and destroyed and its lands were added to Rome. The hinterland of Carthage became the province of Africa; Masinissa's Numidia remained a neighbour to the west.
