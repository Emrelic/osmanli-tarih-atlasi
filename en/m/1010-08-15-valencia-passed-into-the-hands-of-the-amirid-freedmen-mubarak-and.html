Valencia passed into the hands of the Amirid freedmen Mubarak and Muzaffar

15 August 1010

With the dissolution of the power of the grandsons of the hajib Mansur, Valencia came under the rule of the Amirid freedmen Mubarak and Muzaffar; taifa emirates began to appear in al-Andalus.
