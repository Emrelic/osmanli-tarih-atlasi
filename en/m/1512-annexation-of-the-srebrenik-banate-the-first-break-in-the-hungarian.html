Annexation of the Srebrenik banate: the first break in the Hungarian line in northern Bosnia

1512

After the Kingdom of Bosnia fell in 1463, Matthias Corvinus launched a counteroffensive and established in the north of the kingdom two banates that halted the Ottoman advance: Jajce in the west and Srebrenik in the northeast. These were Hungary's forward defense line in Bosnia and closed the corridor between the Sava and Bosnia. The banate of Srebrenik was taken in 1512; thus the eastern flank of the line collapsed and the sancak of Izvornik was able to expand northward. Jajce on the western flank would hold out sixteen years more and could be taken only in 1528, after Mohács.
