Kayqubad does not recognise the sultanate of his elder brother Kaykawus I and rebels; he shuts himself in Ankara Castle and surrenders

1212

Ala al-Din Kayqubad, who rebelled against Izz al-Din Kaykawus, proclaimed sultan at Kayseri on his father's death, besieged Kayseri together with the Armenian King Leo and his uncle Tughril Shah, Malik of Erzurum; without result, he withdrew to Ankara Castle and, when provisions ran out, surrendered on condition that neither he nor the people be harmed (608/1212).
