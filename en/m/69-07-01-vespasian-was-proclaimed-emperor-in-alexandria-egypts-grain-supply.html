Vespasian was proclaimed emperor in Alexandria: Egypt's grain supply decided the fate of the civil war

1 July 69

The prefect of Egypt, Tiberius Alexander, had the legions at Alexandria swear allegiance in the name of Vespasian; the oath was taken on 1 July, and Vespasian counted this day as the day of his own accession. The army in Judea swore the oath in Vespasian's presence two days later. Egypt's grain production is regarded as the strategic basis of this undertaking.
