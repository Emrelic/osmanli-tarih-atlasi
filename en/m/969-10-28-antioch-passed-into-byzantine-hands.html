Antioch passed into Byzantine hands

28 October 969

After the Byzantine armies took many fortresses and cities in northern Syria, Michael Bourtzes and Peter Phokas, coming from Cilicia, captured Antioch on 28 October 969. For about 115 years the city was Byzantium's main base in Syria.
