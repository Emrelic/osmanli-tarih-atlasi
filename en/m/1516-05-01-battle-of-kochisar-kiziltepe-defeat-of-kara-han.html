Battle of Koçhisar (Kızıltepe) — defeat of Kara Han

1 May 1516

The Ottoman army under the command of Bıyıklı Mehmed Pasha defeated the Safavid governor Kara Han, in a pitched battle lasting all day at the place called Koruk on the plain of Dede Garkın near Koçhisar; Kara Han's severed head was sent to the sultan's camp. Although the Ottoman forces then entered the city of Mardin, they stayed a few days and left; Mardin Castle, held by Safavid guards, could be taken over only a year later. This victory secured Selim's southern frontier and made it possible for him to turn to the Egyptian campaign. Other places joined on the same date: Palu, Çemişgezek, Siverek.
