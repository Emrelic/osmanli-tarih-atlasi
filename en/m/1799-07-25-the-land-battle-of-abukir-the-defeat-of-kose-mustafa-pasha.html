The land battle of Abukir — the defeat of Köse Mustafa Pasha

25 July 1799

Napoleon, returning defeated from before Acre, defeated the Ottoman forces under Köse Mustafa Pasha at Abukir, and the fortress of Abukir fell into French hands. This coast, where the French fleet had been sunk a year earlier, would two years later be the place where the British army that came to expel the French from Egypt landed (8 March 1801).
