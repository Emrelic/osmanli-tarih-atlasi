Independence of Morocco: French protectorate ended, Salé and Rabat remained with the Kingdom of Morocco

2 March 1956

With the independence treaty signed on 2 March 1956, the French protectorate that had lasted since 1912 ended; Salé and Rabat became land of the independent Kingdom of Morocco. Spain recognized Morocco's independence on 5 April 1956.
