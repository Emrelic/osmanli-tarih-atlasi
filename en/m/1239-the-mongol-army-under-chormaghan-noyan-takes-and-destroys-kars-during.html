The Mongol army under Chormaghan Noyan takes and destroys Kars during the Georgian campaign

1239

In 636/1239 the Mongol army under Chormaghan Noyan took Kars during the Georgian campaign and destroyed it; the city remained under Ilkhanid administration until 1336.
