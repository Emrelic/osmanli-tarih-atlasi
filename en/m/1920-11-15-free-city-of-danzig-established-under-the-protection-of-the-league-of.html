Free City of Danzig established under the protection of the League of Nations

15 November 1920

Danzig, separated from Germany by the Treaty of Versailles, remained from 10 January 1920 under the transitional administration of the Allied great powers; on 15 November 1920 it was established under the protection of the League of Nations as a free city in a customs union with Poland.
