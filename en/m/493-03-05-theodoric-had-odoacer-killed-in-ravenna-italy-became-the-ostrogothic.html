Theodoric had Odoacer killed in Ravenna; Italy became the Ostrogothic Kingdom

5 March 493

End of Odoacer's Kingdom of Italy: all of Italy (including cities such as Genoa, Brindisi and Aricia) passed to the Ostrogothic (East Gothic) Kingdom. After a three-year siege of Ravenna, in March 493 Odoacer and Theodoric agreed on joint rule; then Theodoric killed Odoacer at a banquet. With the approval of Eastern Rome, Italy became the Ostrogothic Kingdom, with Ravenna as its capital.
