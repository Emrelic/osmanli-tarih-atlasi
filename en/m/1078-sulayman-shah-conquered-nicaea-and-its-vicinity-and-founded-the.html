Sulayman Shah conquered Nicaea and its vicinity and founded the Anatolian Seljuk state with Nicaea as capital

1078

Sulayman Shah, son of Qutalmish, took advantage of the throne turmoil in Byzantium, conquered Nicaea and its vicinity, and founded the Anatolian Seljuks, the first Turkish state in Anatolia, with Nicaea as its capital (1078). He extended his frontiers as far as the Bosphorus; in 1081 he made peace with Alexios Komnenos, with the river Drakon as frontier.
