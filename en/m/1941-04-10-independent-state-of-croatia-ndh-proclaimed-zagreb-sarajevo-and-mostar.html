Independent State of Croatia (NDH) proclaimed: Zagreb, Sarajevo and Mostar split from the Kingdom of Yugoslavia

10 April 1941

S. Kvaternik, leader of the Ustaša movement, proclaimed the NDH by radio; the state covered the whole of Bosnia-Herzegovina. With this proclamation Dubrovnik, Bijeljina and Orašje (Bosnia-Herzegovina) passed to the Independent State of Croatia.
