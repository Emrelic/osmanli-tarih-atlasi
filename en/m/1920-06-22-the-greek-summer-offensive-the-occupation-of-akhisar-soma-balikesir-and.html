The Greek summer offensive — the occupation of Akhisar, Soma, Balıkesir and Alaşehir

22 June 1920

When the Allies gave the Greek army permission to advance beyond the Milne line, a great Greek offensive began in western Anatolia. According to the TDV article on the National Struggle, Greek units moving out in three columns on 22 June took Akhisar, Kırkağaç, Soma and Balıkesir. In the south, Alaşehir was occupied on 26 June and Nazilli on 3 July.
