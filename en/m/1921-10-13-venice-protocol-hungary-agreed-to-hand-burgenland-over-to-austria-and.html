Venice Protocol — Hungary agreed to hand Burgenland over to Austria and to hold a plebiscite in Sopron

13 October 1921

At the end of talks held on 11-13 October 1921 under Italian mediation, Hungary agreed to withdraw its troops and leave Burgenland to Austria, while Austria agreed to hold a plebiscite in Sopron and eight villages around it. The first Austrian attempt on 28 August 1921 had failed because of Hungarian irregular bands (the gendarmerie returned to the old border on 10 September).
