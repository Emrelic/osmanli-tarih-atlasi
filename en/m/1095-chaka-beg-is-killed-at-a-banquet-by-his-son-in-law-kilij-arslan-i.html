Chaka Beg is killed at a banquet by his son-in-law Kilij Arslan I

1095

Anna Komnene, who records that Chaka Beg was killed at a banquet by his son-in-law Kilij Arslan, gives 1097 as the date of death, but in view of the siege of Malatya in 1096 and the fall of Nicaea in 1097 this is wrong; the date of death must be 1095 (d. 488/1095 [?]).
