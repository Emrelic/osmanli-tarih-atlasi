Bayezid the Thunderbolt placed Constantinople again under close siege

1394

Constantinople, which had been blockaded intermittently since 1391, was placed again under close siege by Bayezid the Thunderbolt in the spring of 1394. Relations with Byzantium, which had paid tribute to the Ottomans since 1371 and sent military aid, thus turned into open hostility. The siege was lifted with the emergence of the Timur danger and the Battle of Ankara in 1402.
