Golden Bull (Aranybulla): Andrew II grants the nobles the right to resist the king's unlawful acts

1222

In 1222 King Andrew II issued the gold-sealed charter (Aranybulla) granting the nobles (nobiles) the right to resist the king's acts contrary to the laws; it is a milestone in the strengthening of the feudal oligarchy.
