Naram-Sin defeated the Lullubi mountain people in the Zagros (Victory Stele)

2229 BC

The relief stele showing Naram-Sin's victory over the Lullubi (Zagros) people is the best-known work of Akkadian art. The stele was later carried to Susa by the Elamite king Shutruk-Nahhunte and was found there in 1898.
