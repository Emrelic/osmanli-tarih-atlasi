Rome and the Vandals concluded a new treaty: Africa Proconsularis, Byzacena, and part of Numidia were left to the Vandals

442

Gaiseric, who had taken Carthage (439), made Africa Proconsularis, Byzacena, Abaritana, and part of Numidia legally Vandal territory by the treaty he made with Valentinian III in 442; Rome retained only Mauretania and the west of Numidia.
