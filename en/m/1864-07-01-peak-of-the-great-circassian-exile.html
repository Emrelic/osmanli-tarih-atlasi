Peak of the Great Circassian Exile

1 July 1864

With Russia's occupation of the Caucasus between 1858 and 1878, most intensively in 1861-1864, 1.2-1.5 million Circassians were forced to migrate to Ottoman lands. According to the TDV, many researchers describe this event as "the first ethnic cleansing operation of modern history."
