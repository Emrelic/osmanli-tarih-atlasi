Karamanoğlu Mehmed Bey seized Konya and proclaimed Ala al-Din Siyavush (Jimri) sultan

14 May 1277

Karamanoğlu Mehmed Bey, who was fighting against Mongol oppression, proclaimed Ala al-Din Siyavush (Jimri), who claimed to be a son of Kaykaus II, Seljuk sultan, seized Konya on 9 Dhu'l-Hijja 675 (14 May 1277) and seated him on the throne the next day; Mehmed Bey was killed on 20 June 1277, and Siyavush two years later, on 30 May 1279.
