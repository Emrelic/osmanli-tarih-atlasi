The Qarmatian da'i Abu Ghanim plunders Busra, Tiberias and Hit, then enters Kufa; he is soon expelled

906

Abu Ghanim Abdullah ibn Sa'id and his men, sent by Zikrawayh to revive Qarmatism among the Banu Kalb, plundered settlements such as Busra, Adhri'at, Tiberias and Hit and attacked Damascus; failing to achieve success, they entered Kufa but were soon expelled from the city (293/906).
