Sultan Mahmud died in Ghazna; the Ghaznavid state stretched from Azerbaijan in the west to the upper Ganges valley in the east

30 April 1030

Mahmud died in Ghazna on 23 Rabi' al-Akhir 421 (30 April 1030); at his death the state stretched from the lands of Azerbaijan in the west to the upper Ganges valley of India in the east, and from Khwarazm in the north to the shores of the Indian Ocean in the south.
