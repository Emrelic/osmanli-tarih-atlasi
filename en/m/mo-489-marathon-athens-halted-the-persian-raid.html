Marathon: Athens halted the Persian raid

489 BC

The Athenian and Plataean hoplites under Miltiades defeated the Persian force on the plain of Marathon and prevented its landing in Attica. The victory fed the self-confidence of Athenian democracy and the naval policy that led on to the Delian League.
