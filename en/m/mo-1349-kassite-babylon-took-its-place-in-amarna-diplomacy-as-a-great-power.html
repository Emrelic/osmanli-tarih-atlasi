Kassite Babylon took its place in Amarna diplomacy as a great power equal to Egypt

1349 BC

The Kassite kings Kadashman-Enlil I and Burna-Buriash II belonged to the league of "great kings" who conducted marriage and gift diplomacy with the pharaohs of Egypt. The Amarna letters list the members of this league as Egypt, Babylon, Mitanni, Hatti and Aššur.
