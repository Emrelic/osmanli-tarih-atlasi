The British entry into Mosul: the occupation of the vilayet of Mosul in contravention of the armistice

8 November 1918

When the Armistice of Mudros was signed, Mosul was in the hands of Turkish forces. Relying on Article 7 of the armistice, the British entered the city on 8 (or 10) November 1918, and on 15 November the Turkish units were forced to withdraw from the vilayet. The Mosul question could not be resolved at Lausanne either and was left to the Ankara Treaty of 1926.
