The entry of the island of Nikaria (Ikaria) under Ottoman administration

1521

Nikaria, between Samos and Chios, was under the administration of the Knights of Saint John of Rhodes from 1481 to 1521; the first Ottoman period on the island began in 1522 and lasted until 1822. In this period the kaymakam was responsible for the collection and payment of taxes; as in the other Aegean islands, the local council of elders (demogerontia) also continued to exist and at times came into conflict with the Ottoman administration.
