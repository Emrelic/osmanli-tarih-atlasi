The Serbian army occupied Timișoara (Temeşvar) — the Serbian military administration of the Banat began

14 November 1918

Serbian troops occupied Timișoara on 14 November 1918, dispersed the national guards and assumed the military, and shortly afterwards the civil, administration of the Banat. French troops also entered the city in early December; Romanian administration was established only in July-August 1919.
