Strasbourg capitulated to Louis XIV's army: it was joined to France

30 September 1681

During the policy of the Réunions, Louvois's army besieged Strasbourg; the city signed the terms of surrender on 30 September 1681 (deed of capitulation at Illkirch); ratification on 3 October, and Louis XIV's ceremonial entry on 23 October 1681. The city was joined to France, and municipal autonomy was destroyed.
