The caliph invested Toghril Beg with seven black robes of honour and gave him the title 'king of the East and the West'

1058

At a ceremony held in Dhu'l-Qa'da 449 (January 1058), Caliph al-Qa'im-Biamrillah invested Toghril Beg with seven black robes of honour; this meant the bestowal of world sovereignty on the Seljuk ruler.
