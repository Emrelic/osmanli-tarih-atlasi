Macedonia divided by Rome into four autonomous regions (merides)

166 BC

By decision of the Senate (167 BC) the kingdom of Macedonia was divided into four regions centered on Amphipolis, Thessalonike (Thessaloniki), Pella and Pelagonia; marriage and the acquisition of property between the regions were forbidden, and mining operations were restricted.
