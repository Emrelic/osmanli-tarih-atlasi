Bohemond grants the Genoese, who helped at the siege of Antioch, extensive trade privileges

1098

During the First Crusade, Bohemond of Taranto granted the Genoese, who had supported the siege of Antioch, extensive trade privileges in the city; this is how Genoa's colonisation of the eastern Mediterranean began.
