Venice opens a consulate in Aleppo

1 June 1548

In the sixteenth century the commercial center of gravity of the Europeans shifted from Damascus to Aleppo. As a concrete indicator of this change, Venice opened a consulate in Aleppo in 1548; France followed in 1557 and England in 1586. The city became the principal market where Europe's woolen cloth and silver were exchanged for Indian spices and, at the end of the century, Iranian silk.
