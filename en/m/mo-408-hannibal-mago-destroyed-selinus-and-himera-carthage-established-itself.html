Hannibal Mago destroyed Selinus and Himera: Carthage established itself in western Sicily

408 BC

Hannibal Mago, who landed in Sicily at the call of Segesta, took Selinus and Himera in 409 BC; the region became Carthage's epikratia. The Carthaginian-Syracusan conflict lasted two centuries.
