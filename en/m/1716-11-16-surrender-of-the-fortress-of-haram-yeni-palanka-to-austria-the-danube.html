Surrender of the fortress of Haram (Yeni Palanka) to Austria — the Danube bank of the Banat was lost

16 November 1716

After the fall of Temesvár, the Austrian cavalry general Mercy, charged with taking the rest of the Banat, marched on 14 November on Haram (Yeni Palanka) on the Danube bank; when he reached the fortress on 16 November, the 200-man Ottoman garrison raised a white flag and surrendered on condition of free withdrawal. Thus the Danube line of Southern Banat, in Ottoman hands since 1552, also passed to Austria. Another settlement passing from the Ottomans to the Habsburgs on the same day: Vršac — the date of Haram was taken as the basis (Bánlaky 0019/1703; approximate information).
