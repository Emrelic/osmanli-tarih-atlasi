Afonso Henriques, King of Portugal, took Lisbon from the Almoravids with the help of the crusaders

25 October 1147

At the end of the siege that began on 1 July 1147, the garrison agreed on 21 October to surrender on condition of free exit, and the gates were opened four days later; Lisbon became the capital of Portugal.
