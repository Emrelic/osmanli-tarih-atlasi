Valencia was captured by the Almoravids (1102)

5 May 1102

After El Cid's death in 1099, the city had been left to his widow Jimena; Valencia was captured by the Almoravids in 1102. At this date Valencia leaves the Principality of Valencia (El Cid) and passes to the Almoravid State.
