Isma'il ibn Ahmad defeated and captured the Saffarid emir Amr ibn Layth near Balkh; Khurasan passed to the Samanids

19 April 900

In the battle fought near Balkh on 15 Rabi' al-Akhir 287 (19 April 900), Isma'il ibn Ahmad defeated and captured Amr ibn Layth; Amr was sent to Baghdad. The caliph granted Isma'il the diplomas for Khurasan, Tabaristan and Daylam; the Samanids became masters of Khurasan.
