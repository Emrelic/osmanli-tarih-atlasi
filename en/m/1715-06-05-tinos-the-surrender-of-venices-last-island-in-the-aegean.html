Tinos: the surrender of Venice's last island in the Aegean

5 June 1715

The fleet setting out on the Morea campaign anchored off Tinos, the only island remaining in Venetian hands in the Aegean, on its route. The island, which, thanks to its Latin population, had borne the Venetian flag for another hundred and fifty years even though the Duchy of Naxos had fallen in 1566, surrendered without bloodshed after a short siege. Thus Venice's last footprint in the Aegean Sea was erased, and the sea became wholly an Ottoman inland sea.
