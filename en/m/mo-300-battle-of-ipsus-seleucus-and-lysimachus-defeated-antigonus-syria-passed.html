Battle of Ipsus: Seleucus and Lysimachus defeated Antigonus; Syria passed to Seleucus

300 BC

In the decisive battle of the Diadochi wars, Seleucus and Lysimachus defeated Antigonus (Antigonus was killed in the battle). The whole of the East and Syria fell to Seleucus's share; Ptolemy, for his part, occupied Coele-Syria and thereby laid the cause of the first Syrian wars.
