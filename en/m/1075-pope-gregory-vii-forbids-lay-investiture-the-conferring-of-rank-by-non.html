Pope Gregory VII forbids lay investiture (the conferring of rank by non-clerical persons) in the appointment of clergy

1075

The prohibition of lay investiture, issued two years after the election of Hildebrand as pope under the name of Gregory VII in 1073, threatened the empire's power over religious appointments and was the beginning of open conflict with Henry IV.
