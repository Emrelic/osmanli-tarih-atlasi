Athens fell into the hands of the Crusaders: Othon de la Roche became lord of Athens

1204

In the campaign of Boniface, Marquis of Montferrat, the Burgundian Othon de la Roche became lord of Athens at the end of October or in November 1204. Athens became part of Latin Greece as the Duchy of Burgundy (later the Duchy of Athens); the Parthenon was made a Catholic cathedral.
