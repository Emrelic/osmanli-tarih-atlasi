The landing at Sidi Ferruch — the French army on Algerian soil

14 June 1830

After the three-year blockade, the French landing army under the command of Comte de Bourmont landed troops in the bay of Sidi Ferruch in the west of Algiers. The forces advancing toward the city broke the defence line within three weeks; on 4 July the Emperor Fort dominating the city fell, and the next day Dey Hussein surrendered.
