Yaroslav the Wise dies in Kiev; the fragmentation of Rus' begins

20 February 1054

Yaroslav died in Kiev on 20 February 1054. There followed divisions into principalities lasting for centuries: according to EB1911, at least 64 principalities showed a presence, however brief, between 1054 and 1224, and 293 princes laid claim to succession.
