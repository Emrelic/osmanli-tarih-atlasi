Spain's occupation of the islands of al-Hoceima (Alhucemas)

1673

The al-Hoceima islands on the Mediterranean coast were occupied by the Spanish fleet in 1673. Together with Badis and Ceuta, they are among the positions Spain held on the northern Moroccan coast and has retained to the present day. In the same year Mulay Rashid, elder brother of Mulay Ismail, entered Marrakesh and received the pledge of allegiance of the people, and Filali rule was consolidated in the south — this loss on the coast shows that the internal unification as yet had no counterpart on the external front.
