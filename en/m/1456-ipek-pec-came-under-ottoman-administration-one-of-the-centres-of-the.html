İpek (Peć) came under Ottoman administration — one of the centres of the sancak of Dukakin

1456

Immediately after Mehmed the Conqueror's Serbian campaign of 1455, İpek, the centre of the Serbian patriarchate, passed to Ottoman administration; there is no record of this passage in Ottoman sources. The town in time became a small Muslim town and was one of the residences of the sancak bey in the sancak of Dukakin.
