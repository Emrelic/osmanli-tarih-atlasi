Serbian troops entered Szigetvár — the occupation of southern Somogy

19 November 1918

Advancing north of the line drawn by the Belgrade military convention, 220 soldiers of the Serbian army under the command of Obren Petrović entered Szigetvár, the district seat of Somogy, on the night connecting the 18th to the 19th; the villages of the district were also held by this force. The occupation lasted until August 1921; Trianon left Szigetvár in Hungary.
