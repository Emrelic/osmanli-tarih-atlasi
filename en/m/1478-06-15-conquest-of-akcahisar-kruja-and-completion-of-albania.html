Conquest of Akçahisar (Kruja) and completion of Albania

15 June 1478

Ten years after Skanderbeg's death, with the seizure of Akçahisar, Drivasto, Lezhë and Jabyak in 1478, the conquest of Albania was largely completed. The fall of Kruja Castle, which had resisted the Ottoman armies for years and was the symbol of Skanderbeg's resistance, meant the end of the Venetian-Albanian alliance in the region. Shkodër, the remaining place, was to be left to the Ottomans by the Treaty of Istanbul to be signed the following year.
