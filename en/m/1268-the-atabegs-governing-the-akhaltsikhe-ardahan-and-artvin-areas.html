The atabegs governing the Akhaltsikhe, Ardahan and Artvin areas (Atabegate of Samtskhe/Meskheti) take over the government of the region

1268

Between 1268 and 1578 the atabegs, who were the rulers of the Akhaltsikhe, Ardahan and Artvin areas, held the government of the region in their hands, and during this period they remained dependent on the Ilkhanid, Qaraqoyunlu and Aqqoyunlu states.
