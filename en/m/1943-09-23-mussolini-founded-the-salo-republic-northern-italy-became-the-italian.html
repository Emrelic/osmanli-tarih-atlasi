Mussolini founded the Salò Republic: northern Italy became the Italian Social Republic under German protection

23 September 1943

Mussolini, rescued by the Germans, founded the Italian Social Republic (Salò), covering northern and central Italy, under German military control; the Kingdom and the Badoglio government remained in the south. With the founding of the Salò Republic, Venice, Turin, Genoa, Verona, Bologna and the other northern Italian cities passed to the Italian Social Republic.
