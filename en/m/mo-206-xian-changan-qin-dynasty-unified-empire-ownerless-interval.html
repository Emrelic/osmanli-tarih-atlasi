Xi'an (Chang'an): Qin Dynasty (unified Empire) → ownerless interval

206 BC

At the points Xi'an (Chang'an), ownership passes from the Qin Dynasty (unified Empire) to the ownerless interval. Transition source information: Shiji 6, Qin Shi Huang, 26th year: 'for the first time unified the whole land' (change of the identity of the Qin state; Chang'an/Xianyang is the Qin core)
