Talepakemalai (ECA), Eloaua Island, Mussau: Talepakemalai (Mussau) Lapita settlement

1284 BC

Talepakemalai (ECA) in Mussau is the excavation site that yielded the oldest and most varied Lapita ceramic assemblage; the first appearance of the pottery falls in a Bayesian model at ~1510–1060 BC.
