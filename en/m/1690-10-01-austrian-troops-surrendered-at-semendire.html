Austrian troops surrendered at Semendire

1 October 1690

After the taking of Niš on 27 September 1690, the Austrian troops at Semendire (Smederevo) surrendered following a four-day siege (TDV semendire). Bánlaky/Röder says 24 September; the sources conflict, and TDV was taken as the basis.
