Kovno (Kaunas) fortress taken by the Germans — Lithuania came under occupation

18 August 1915

The German 10th Army took Russia's largest fortress on the Neman; the Lithuanian region came under Ober Ost administration, and in 1918 the Taryba declared independence under this occupation.
