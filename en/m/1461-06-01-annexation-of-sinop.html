Annexation of Sinop

1 June 1461

The Candaroğlu surrendered Sinop without a fight; the way was opened for the Trabzon campaign. Other places annexed on the same date: Kastamonu, Bartın, Safranbolu. The TDV entry on the Candaroğulları also mentions Bolu, Eflani and Tosya among the lands of the Candaroğlu principality annexed in 1461; in the atlas these three places are also recorded as passing from the Candaroğlu to the Ottomans on this day.
