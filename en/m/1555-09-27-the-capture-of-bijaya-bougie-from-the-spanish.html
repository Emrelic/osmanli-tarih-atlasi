The capture of Bijâya (Bougie) from the Spanish

27 September 1555

Bijâya, which had been in Spanish hands for forty-three years and divided the coast between Algiers and Constantine in two, surrendered after being besieged by land and sea. With the fall of the fortress, the coast of the Algiers ocak became continuous as far as Oran. In Spain this loss provoked such great outrage that the commander who surrendered the fortress, Alonso de Peralta, was executed in his homeland.
