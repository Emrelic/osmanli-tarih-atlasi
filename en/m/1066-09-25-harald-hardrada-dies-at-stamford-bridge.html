Harald Hardrada dies at Stamford Bridge

25 September 1066

Harald Hardrada, king of Norway, died on 25 September 1066 at Stamford Bridge (Yorkshire) in England; in 1043/44 Harald had married Elisiv, daughter of Yaroslav, Grand Prince of Kiev.
