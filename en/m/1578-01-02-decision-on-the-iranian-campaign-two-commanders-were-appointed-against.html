Decision on the Iranian campaign — two commanders were appointed against the Safavids, shaken by the death of Tahmasb

2 January 1578

With the poisoning of Shah Tahmasb I in 1576 as a result of the disputes among the dynasty and the notables of the state, the Safavids had been drawn into great turmoil; this determined the direction of the campaign to be launched. Lala Mustafa Pasha was charged with entering Iran from the Erzurum side and Koca Sinan Pasha from the Baghdad side. When the dispute between the two viziers intensified, Sokullu intervened: Sinan Pasha was removed from his post, and Lala Mustafa Pasha was charged with the conquest of Shirvan by way of Georgia. This decision is the opening of the Ottoman-Safavid war of 1578-1590.
