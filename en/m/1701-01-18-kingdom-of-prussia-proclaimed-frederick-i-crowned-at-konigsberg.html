Kingdom of Prussia proclaimed — Frederick I crowned at Königsberg

18 January 1701

Frederick III, Elector of Brandenburg, with the consent of Emperor Leopold I, was crowned at Königsberg and took the title «King in Prussia» (18 January 1701). The title was taken from East Prussia because that was outside the Holy Roman Empire, and an elector subordinate to the emperor could be a king only outside the borders of the empire. After this date the scattered lands of the Hohenzollern dynasty began to be called by a single state name.
