Treaty of Fort Laramie — boundaries were drawn on the Northern Plains

17 September 1851

The treaty signed between the United States and the peoples of the Northern Plains defined in writing for the first time the boundaries of the lands of the Lakota, Crow, Mandan, Hidatsa, Arikara, Cheyenne, Arapaho and Assiniboine, and in return made provision for the security of the emigrant roads. The boundaries were soon violated.
