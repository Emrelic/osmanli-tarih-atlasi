The abdication of Ahmed III, the accession of Mahmud I

2 October 1730

According to the TDV article on 'Mahmud I', in the face of the pressure of the rebels Ahmed III abdicated after obtaining guarantees for himself and his children and himself placed his nephew Prince Mahmud on the throne. Mahmud I, who sat on the throne with his uncle's advice 'take the affairs of state into your own hands, trust no one', set about as his first task breaking the influence of the group of rebels who had put him on the throne.
