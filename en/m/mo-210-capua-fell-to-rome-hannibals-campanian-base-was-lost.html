Capua fell to Rome: Hannibal's Campanian base was lost

210 BC

After a two-year siege of Capua, which had gone over to Hannibal after Cannae, Rome took the city despite Hannibal's march on Rome and punished it heavily; Capua's autonomy was abolished. Hannibal withdrew to the defensive in southern Italy.
