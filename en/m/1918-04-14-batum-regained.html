Batum regained

14 April 1918

Advancing eastward after Russia withdrew from the war under the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk, the Ottoman army regained the port of Batum, lost in 1878, forty years later. This port at the eastern end of the Black Sea was the maritime outlet for Baku oil. The gain lasted about eight months; after the Armistice of Mudros the region was to be evacuated.
