Nasir-i Khusraw, returning from the pilgrimage, came to the Fatimid capital Cairo

2 August 1047

The traveler, who made the pilgrimage in Mecca in 438 and returned to Jerusalem, reached the Fatimid capital Cairo in Safar 439 and stayed in Egypt about three years, returning to Mecca by way of Aydhab in 442. According to the TDV he embraced the Isma'ili mission here.
