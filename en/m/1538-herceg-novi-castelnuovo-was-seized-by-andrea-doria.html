Herceg Novi (Castelnuovo) was seized by Andrea Doria

1538

Andrea Doria, commander of the allied fleet united against the Ottomans, took the fortress of Herceg Novi (Castelnuovo) on the Adriatic coast in southern Dalmatia. In the same year the Venetians in Dalmatia had also taken some towns held by the Ottomans, and the Ottoman forces had answered by taking other fortresses. The fortress was retaken a year later; because Ottoman sovereignty legally continued, this interim period is shown on the map with a hatched occupation overlay.
