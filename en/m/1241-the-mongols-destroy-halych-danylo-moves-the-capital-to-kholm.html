The Mongols destroy Halych; Danylo moves the capital to Kholm

1241

The Mongols destroyed Halych; Danylo moved the capital to Kholm (its day is unknown; only the year has been taken as the basis). Encyclopedia of Ukraine ('Galicia-Volhynia'): 'After the Mongols razed Halych in 1241, Kholm became the new capital.'
