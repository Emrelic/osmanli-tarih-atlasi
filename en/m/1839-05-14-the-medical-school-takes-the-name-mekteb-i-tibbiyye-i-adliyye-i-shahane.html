The medical school takes the name Mekteb-i Tibbiyye-i Adliyye-i Shahane

14 May 1839

The medical school, which had moved into the building of the Enderun Aghas' School at Galatasaray, took the name "Mekteb-i Tibbiyye-i Adliyye-i Shahane" on 14 May 1839 with the visit of Mahmud II; the institution was thus formally registered with the identity of a military-administrative school.
