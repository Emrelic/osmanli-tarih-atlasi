Uzun Hasan ended the Qaraquyunlu State — the Lake Van basin passed to the Aqquyunlu

1467

The Aqquyunlu ruler Uzun Hasan caught off guard his implacable enemy, the Qaraquyunlu ruler Jahan Shah, who was marching against him in 1467, and put an end to the Qaraquyunlu State. With the elimination of Jahan Shah, who had ruled since 1438, the Lake Van basin — Van, Bitlis, Bargiri (Muradiye), Hoşap and Kotur — passed from Qaraquyunlu to Aqquyunlu administration. This victory made the Aqquyunlu State the single great power in Eastern Anatolia and Iran, and set Uzun Hasan on the road that would bring him face to face with the Ottomans a generation later.
