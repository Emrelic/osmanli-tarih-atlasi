Death of Mahmud II

28 June 1839

The sultan who, during a reign of thirty-one years, abolished the Janissaries and re-established central administration, died without having defeated his greatest adversary. His illness had not been correctly diagnosed. His death was concealed until 30 June, until military measures were taken in the city for fear that the Janissary mentality would be set in motion.
