Conquest of the island of Lesbos (Midilli)

17 September 1462

The Ottoman army under the command of Mehmed the Conqueror took Lesbos in Dhu al-Hijja 866, after a short but fierce siege, from the Gattilusio family of Genoese origin, and annexed it. The fall of the largest island of the northern Aegean also liquidated the last important Genoese base in the region. The island, thanks to its proximity to the Anatolian coast, would thereafter serve as an advance outpost for the Ottoman navy and would also enter naval history as the place where the Barbarossa brothers were raised.
