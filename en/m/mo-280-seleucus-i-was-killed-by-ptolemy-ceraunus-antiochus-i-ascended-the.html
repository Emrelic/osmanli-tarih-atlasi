Seleucus I was killed by Ptolemy Ceraunus; Antiochus I ascended the throne

280 BC

Seleucus, who had defeated Lysimachus at Corupedium and taken Anatolia, was stabbed by Ptolemy Ceraunus as he crossed into Europe. His son Antiochus I took over the state but would struggle in western Anatolia.
