Ridaniya — conquest of Egypt and the caliphate

22 January 1517

The Ottoman army, which crossed the Sinai desert swiftly, broke Tumanbay's defensive line at Ridaniya and entered Cairo; Tumanbay, who continued to resist, was captured and executed, and the Mamluk state passed into history. Egypt, the Hejaz and the holy cities came under Ottoman rule; the Abbasid caliph and the sacred relics were taken to Istanbul. With the passing of the caliphate to the Ottoman dynasty, the sultans assumed the claim to leadership of the Islamic world; control of the Spice Route also provided the empire with great revenue. Other places annexed on the same date: Acre, Ma'an, Sidon, Karak.
