Manin took the Venice Arsenal: the Republic of San Marco was proclaimed

22 March 1848

Under the leadership of Daniele Manin, Venice rose against Austrian rule and proclaimed the Republic of San Marco; Austrian troops withdrew from the city on 26 March.
