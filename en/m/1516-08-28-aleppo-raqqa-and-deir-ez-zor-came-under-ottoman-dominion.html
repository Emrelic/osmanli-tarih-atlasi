Aleppo, Raqqa and Deir ez-Zor came under Ottoman dominion

28 August 1516

Four days after Marj Dabiq, Selim the Grim entered Aleppo without meeting resistance, and the khutba in the city's mosques was read in his name. The fall of Aleppo, regarded as the commercial and administrative capital of Syria, so quickly shows that Mamluk authority in the region had collapsed. The taking of the city at once opened the road to Damascus and from there to Egypt. Other places joined on the same date: Antioch, Raqqa, Deir ez-Zor.
