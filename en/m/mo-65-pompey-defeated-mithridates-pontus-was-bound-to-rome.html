Pompey defeated Mithridates; Pontus was bound to Rome

65 BC

In 66 Pompey routed Mithridates's army by night on the plain of the Lycus; Mithridates fled from Colchis to the Bosporus. The coastal cities of Pontus came under Roman control, and in 63 the province united with Bithynia was formed.
