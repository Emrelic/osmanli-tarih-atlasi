Rubruck reaches Sudak in Crimea from Constantinople

21 May 1253

The Franciscan friar, who states that he went on his own and not as an envoy of Louis IX, sails into the Black Sea, lands at Soldaia (Sudak) and prepares to enter the Mongol region with a caravan.
