Albrecht IV issued the Wittelsbach Primogeniture Ordinance — the Bavarian duchies were united into a single indivisible duchy

8 July 1506

After the War of the Landshut Succession (1503-1505), Duke Albrecht IV, who reunited the Bavarian duchies in one hand, issued on 8 July 1506 the Primogeniture Ordinance, which provided for the indivisibility of the country and the passing of the throne to the eldest male child. The partitions of Bavaria that had lasted since 1255 thus came to an end; Bavaria, with Munich as its center, continued as a single indivisible duchy among the German states.
