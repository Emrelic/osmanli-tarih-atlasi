The Bulgarians defeated Isaac Angelos

1190

While the Serbian and Bulgarian rulers hoped for support from Barbarossa, who was going to Anatolia, Isaac Angelos was defeated by the Bulgarians near Stara Zagora and barely escaped. The Bulgarians took Sofia and carried the relics of Saint John of Rila to Tarnovo.
