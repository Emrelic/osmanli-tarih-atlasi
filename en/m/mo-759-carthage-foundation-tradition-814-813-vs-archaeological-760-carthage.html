Carthage - foundation tradition 814/813 vs archaeological ~760: Carthage: beginning of continuous occupation (archaeological)

759 BC

The legendary foundation of Carthage (Elissa/Dido) is 814-813; continuous occupation in the city begins in the excavations at about 760 BCE, the earliest tombs belonging to the second half of the 8th century; the 8th-century necropolis has not yet been found.
