Central Lithuania joined Poland — Vilnius became Polish

24 March 1922

The Wilno Sejm voted for annexation on 20 February 1922; the Polish Sejm approved it on 24 March 1922. The Conference of Ambassadors recognized the border in 1923; Lithuania did not recognize it.
