Zaydi Imam al-Mutawakkil Allah invades Hadramawt — the Kathiri Sultanate is made subject to the Yemeni imamate

1659

After the Ottoman withdrawal from Yemen in 1635, the Zaydi imamate grew stronger and, under Imam al-Mutawakkil Allah, sent an army that invaded Hadramawt. The Kathiri rule in inner Hadramawt thus remained subordinate to the Yemeni imamate for nearly half a century; when the influence of the imams and the Kathiris weakened in the first half of the 18th century, administration passed back to the local tribes.
