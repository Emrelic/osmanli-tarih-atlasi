Ejei, the last khan of the Chahar Khanate, surrendered to the Qing with his mother; Ligdan's khanate ended

11 July 1635

After Ligdan Khan died in 1634, Dorgon, Yolo, Hooge and Sahaliyan were sent with ten thousand horsemen to take Ligdan's son Ejei; the army received 1,500 households of the Chahar Nangnang taifei at Shira Jurge and reached Ejei's camp, and Ejei's mother surrendered him.
