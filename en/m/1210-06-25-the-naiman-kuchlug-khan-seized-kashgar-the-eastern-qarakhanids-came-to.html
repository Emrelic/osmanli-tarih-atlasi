The Naiman Kuchlug Khan seized Kashgar; the Eastern Qarakhanids came to an end

25 June 1210

Kuchlug Khan, the Naiman ruler who had captured the Gurkhan, sent Abu'l-Fath Muhammad III to Kashgar to seat him on the Eastern Qarakhanid throne; the notables of the city killed him on the way (607/1210-11), and Kuchlug seized Kashgar and had the rebels killed; the Eastern Qarakhanids came to an end.
