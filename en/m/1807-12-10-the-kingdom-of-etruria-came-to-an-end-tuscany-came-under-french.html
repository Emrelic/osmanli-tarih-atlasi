The Kingdom of Etruria came to an end: Tuscany came under French occupation

10 December 1807

Napoleon dissolved the Kingdom of Etruria; the regent queen left Florence, and in 1808 Tuscany was joined to France as the departments of Arno, Ombrone and Méditerranée.
