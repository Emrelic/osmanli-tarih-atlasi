The Safavids' capture of Bahrain from Portugal

1602

The forces sent by Shah Abbas I joined the rebels on the island and drove the Portuguese garrison out of the fortress; in the TDV's words, the islands were taken from the Portuguese "in 1602 by forces attached to Iran." Bahrain thus left eighty-one years of Portuguese administration, came under Safavid rule and remained attached to Iran for one hundred and fifteen years. This is the first link in the unraveling of Portugal in the gulf; twenty years later Hormuz itself would fall to a joint English-Iranian operation.
