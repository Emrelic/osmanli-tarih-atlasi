William of Villehardouin died: the Principality of Achaea passed to the Angevins

1 May 1278

William, Prince of Achaea, died on 1 May 1278, and by the Viterbo arrangement of 1267 the principality directly belonged to the Angevin dynasty (until 1289). Charles of Anjou added 'Prince of Achaea' to his titles.
