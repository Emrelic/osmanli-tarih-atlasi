Founding of the Principality of Aydın: capture of Izmir, Ayasuluk and Tire

1308

According to the TDV's Aydınoğulları entry, Mubariz al-Din Gazi Mehmed Beg, taking this region from Sasa Beg, the frontier lord of the Germiyanids, became master of Aydın-ili in 1308; he conquered the Muslim quarter of Izmir together with Ayasuluk (Selçuk), Tire and Bodemya. He made Ayasuluk a naval base and divided the lands he had taken among his five sons — in the following decades, especially in the time of his son Umur Beg, the principality would become the decisive power in Aegean seafaring.
