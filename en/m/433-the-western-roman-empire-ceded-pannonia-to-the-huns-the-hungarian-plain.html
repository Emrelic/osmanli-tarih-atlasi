The Western Roman Empire ceded Pannonia to the Huns; the Hungarian plain was joined to the Hun kingdom

433

In 433, when the kingdom of Attila and Bleda began, the Western Roman Empire ceded its lands in Pannonia to the Huns (the date of the transfer is disputed: the assumptions are 425/431/433); EB11 'Attila': Attila's own kingdom comprised present-day Hungary and Transylvania.
