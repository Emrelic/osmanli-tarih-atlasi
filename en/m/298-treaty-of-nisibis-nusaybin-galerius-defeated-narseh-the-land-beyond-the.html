Treaty of Nisibis (Nusaybin): Galerius defeated Narseh; the land beyond the Tigris passed to Rome

298

Narseh's attack on Roman Mesopotamia ended with Galerius's victory; by the peace of 298 the five regions beyond the Tigris (Transtigritania) and Nisibis were left to Rome. The treaty determined the Roman-Sasanian frontier for half a century.
