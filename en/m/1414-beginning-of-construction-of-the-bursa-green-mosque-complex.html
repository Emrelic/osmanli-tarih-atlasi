Beginning of construction of the Bursa Green Mosque Complex

1414

Mehmed I, who reunited the throne after the Interregnum, began the construction of the complex in 1414 and located it at a central point of Bursa, in a way distinguishable from the complexes of his grandfather and father. Its chief architect was Hacı İvaz Pasha.
