The liberation of İzmit — after Adapazarı

28 June 1921

Adapazarı, which came under Greek occupation in March 1921, was retaken on 21 June, according to the TDV article on Adapazarı. According to the TDV article on İzmit, İzmit, which had been occupied on 6 July 1920 first by the British and then by the Greeks, was also liberated on 28 June 1921. As they left the city, the Greeks burned the bazaar quarter.
