Kütahya Settlement (oral) — Syria and Adana to Kavalalı

6 May 1833

The Sublime Porte left, in addition to Egypt, Crete and the Hejaz, the governorships of Damascus, Aleppo, Tripoli (Syria) and Sidon to Mehmed Ali, and the muhassıllık of Adana to İbrahim Pasha. In return the army withdrew south of the Taurus. This was not a treaty but took the form of firmans given by the sultan to a governor — the matter had not been resolved, it had been postponed.
