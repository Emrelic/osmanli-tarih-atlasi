The Qocho (Turfan) Uyghur idiqut Barchuq submitted to Genghis Khan

1209

The Turfan Uyghur state submitted to Genghis Khan in 1209 and continued to exist under Mongol administration until 1368; Barchuq Idiqut served in Genghis's army and in 1211 married Genghis's daughter Al-Khatun. The submission took the form of voluntary vassalage.
