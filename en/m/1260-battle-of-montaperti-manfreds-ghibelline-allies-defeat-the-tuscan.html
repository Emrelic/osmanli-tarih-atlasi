Battle of Montaperti: Manfred's Ghibelline allies defeat the Tuscan Guelfs; Florence becomes Ghibelline again

1260

The victory of Montaperti in 1260 was the peak of Manfred's fortunes; Florence passed again under Ghibelline rule. Manfred's defeat at Benevento (1266) and Tagliacozzo (1268) brought about the return of the Guelfs.
