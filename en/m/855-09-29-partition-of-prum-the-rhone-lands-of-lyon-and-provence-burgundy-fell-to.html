Partition of Prüm: the Rhône lands of Lyon and Provence-Burgundy fell to Charles, the youngest son of Lothair I

29 September 855

On 19 September 855 at Schüller, Lothair I abdicated and divided the Middle Kingdom: Italy was given to Louis II, the northern lands to Lothair II, and the Rhône lands of Provence-Burgundy, including Lyon, to his youngest son Charles. Lothair became a monk at Prüm on 23 September and died on 29 September.
