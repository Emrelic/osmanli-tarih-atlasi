Maraş came under Egyptian occupation

1833

According to TDV, Maraş came under the occupation of Kavalalı Ibrahim Pasha in 1833 and remained under his administration for nearly nineteen months. Since Maraş remained outside the provincial boundaries of the Kütahya arrangement, this administration is de facto, not legal.
