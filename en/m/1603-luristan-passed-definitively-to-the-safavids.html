Luristan passed definitively to the Safavids

1603

Shah Abbas I fully attached Luristan, which had been bound to Ottoman administration by the Treaty of Istanbul (Ferhad Pasha) of 1590, to the Safavids in 1603. The Safavid historian Iskandar Beg Munshi, however, writes that Khorramabad had been occupied earlier by Shah Abbas, in the year 1002 (1593–94), and a Safavid governor appointed to the city; this difference between the two traditions has not been resolved. In the same year the Ottoman fortress of Nihavand, north of Luristan, also fell into the hands of Shah Abbas; Hasan Khan, governor of Hamadan, razed the fortress.
