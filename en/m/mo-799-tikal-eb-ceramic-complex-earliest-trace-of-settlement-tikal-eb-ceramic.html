Tikal: Eb ceramic complex (earliest trace of settlement): Tikal: Eb ceramic complex (first settlement)

799 BC

The earliest ceramic complex at Tikal, Eb (800–600 BC), is found in a pit cut into the bedrock in the North Acropolis; thus the trace of settlement extends to the beginning of the Middle Preclassic in the Maya lowlands. Tzec 600–350, Chuen 350 BC–AD 1.
