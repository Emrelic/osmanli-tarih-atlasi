The Victory of Hisarcık (Grocka) — The Opening of the Road to Belgrade

22 July 1739

The Austrian army advancing to protect Belgrade was ambushed in a narrow defile on the Danube bank and suffered heavy losses in a battle lasting all day. As the forces of Field Marshal Wallis fell back on Belgrade in disorder, the Ottoman army pressed up to the fortress and began the siege. Hisarcık was the most decisive pitched victory won against Austria since Karlowitz and directly secured the recovery of Belgrade.
