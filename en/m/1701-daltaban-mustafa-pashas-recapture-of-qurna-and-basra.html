Daltaban Mustafa Pasha's recapture of Qurna and Basra

1701

Daltaban Mustafa Pasha, who was appointed to the governorship of Baghdad and the seraskership of Basra after the Treaty of Karlowitz, marched south with the forces of Aleppo, Raqqa, Sivas and Karaman and with a river fleet that descended from Birecik. The tribal sheikhs of the region submitted one by one, Dâvud Khan, the Iranian commander of Basra, abandoned the city, and Qurna and Basra came once again under Ottoman administration.
