Jelačić's Croatian army crossed the Drava — the Hungarian war of independence began

11 September 1848

Jelačić, the Ban of Croatia, with the support of the Habsburg court, crossed the Drava, the Hungarian-Croatian border, with an army of about 50,000; on the same day the Batthyány government resigned and the conflict turned into open war.
