The Sparta–Tegea treaty: the core of the Peloponnesian League took shape

549 BC

Sparta defeated Tegea in Arcadia and made it accept a perpetual alliance (symmachia) in return for not ravaging its lands; the same pattern spread to the cities of the Peloponnese and the Peloponnesian League was born under Spartan leadership. Internally the League functioned as Sparta and an assembly of allies (under Spartan command).
