Pope Martin IV excommunicated Michael VIII: the Union of Lyon was broken

18 November 1281

Pope Martin IV, a Frenchman elected on 22 February 1281, under the influence of Charles of Anjou excommunicated Emperor Michael VIII Palaiologos on 18 November 1281; thus the church union of Lyon was broken.
