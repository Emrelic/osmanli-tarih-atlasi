On Alghu's death, Mubarak Shah was proclaimed khan as the first Chagatai khan to become a Muslim

1266

When Alghu (1260-1266), the true founder of the Chagatai Khanate, died in 1266, Mubarak Shah, the son of Ergene Khatun, was proclaimed khan; he was the first ruler among the Chagatai descendants to accept Islam.
