The Loss of Tunis to Charles V

21 July 1535

Charles V, in a great expedition supported by the Genoese and Papal fleets, took Tunis after capturing La Goulette (Halkulvâd) and restored Mulay Hasan to the throne under Spanish protection. A Spanish garrison was placed in the city, and the Ottoman advance in the Central Mediterranean was temporarily halted. The definitive conquest of Tunis would be possible only forty years later, in 1574.
