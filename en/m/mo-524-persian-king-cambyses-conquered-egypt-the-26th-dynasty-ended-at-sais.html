Persian King Cambyses conquered Egypt: the 26th Dynasty ended at Sais

524 BC

The Persian King Cambyses invaded Egypt; the rule of the 26th Dynasty, founded by Psamtik I of Sais in 663 BC, came to an end, and the country was governed by Persian rulers until 404 BC.
