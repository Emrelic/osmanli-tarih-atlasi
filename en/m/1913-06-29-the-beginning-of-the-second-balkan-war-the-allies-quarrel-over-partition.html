The Beginning of the Second Balkan War: The Allies' Quarrel over Partition

29 June 1913

The Balkan allies, who could not share the lands they had taken from the Ottomans under the Treaty of London, fell upon one another; the second war began with Bulgaria's attack on the Serbian and Greek positions. When Romania and Montenegro also entered the front against Bulgaria, the Bulgarian army collapsed. The Ottoman State, exploiting this opportunity, crossed the Midye-Enez line drawn by the Treaty of London and retook Eastern Thrace together with Edirne.
