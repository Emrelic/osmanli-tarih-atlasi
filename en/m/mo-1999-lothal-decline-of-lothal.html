Lothal: decline of Lothal

1999 BC

A Harappan port/production city on the Gujarat coast; according to Rao the Harappans arrived here c. 2450 BCE; calibrated TF dates place the Mature Harappan phase at ~2650-2200 BCE and the Late Harappan (B) phase at ~2315-1865 BCE.
