The ayyars of Zaranj brought Abu Ja'far Ahmad to power; the Khalafid branch of the Saffarids was founded

21 April 923

After the revolts that broke out during Samanid rule in Sistan, the ayyars in Zaranj brought Abu Ja'far Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Khalaf to power in 311 (923); he extended his sphere of rule as far as Bust and Rukhkhaj.
