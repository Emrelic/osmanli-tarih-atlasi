Syphax was defeated and captured; Masinissa united Numidia

202 BC

In 203 Scipio and Masinissa defeated Syphax's army and took Syphax captive; Masinissa seized Cirta and Syphax's kingdom with Rome's support. Numidia became a powerful kingdom through the union of its eastern and western parts.
