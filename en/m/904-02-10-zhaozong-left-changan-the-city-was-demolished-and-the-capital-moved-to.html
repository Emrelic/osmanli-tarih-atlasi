Zhaozong left Chang'an; the city was demolished and the capital moved to Luoyang

10 February 904

Zhu Quanzhong demanded the removal of Zhaozong to Luoyang and had the people of Chang'an migrate according to the registers, and had the houses demolished and their timber floated down the Wei and Yellow rivers to Luoyang; the emperor set out.
