Alexander entered Babylon; Mazaeus surrendered the city and the Satrapy of Babylon passed to Alexander

22 October 330 BC

After Gaugamela, the satrap of Babylon, Mazaeus, opened the gates to Alexander; Alexander ordered the restoration of the temple of Marduk and used the city for 34 days. Babylon passed from Persian rule to Macedonian rule.
