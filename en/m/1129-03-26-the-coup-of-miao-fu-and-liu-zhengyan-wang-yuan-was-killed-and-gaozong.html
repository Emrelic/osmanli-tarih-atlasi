The coup of Miao Fu and Liu Zhengyan: Wang Yuan was killed and Gaozong was forced to abdicate

26 March 1129

The guard commanders Miao Fu and Liu Zhengyan marched on the palace with their soldiers and killed Wang Yuan, the eunuch Kang Lü and more than a hundred people; they forced Gaozong to abdicate in favor of his young son and asked the Empress Dowager Longyou to act as regent from behind the curtain. The next day Gaozong retired with the title 'Ruisheng Renxiao'; six days later the reign title was changed to Mingshou.
