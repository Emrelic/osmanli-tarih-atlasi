Columbus returns to Sanlúcar from his last voyage

7 November 1504

Columbus, who left Jamaica on 28 June 1504, called at Santo Domingo (13 August), set out for Spain on 12 September and arrived at Sanlúcar on 7 November 1504. This was his fourth and last voyage.
