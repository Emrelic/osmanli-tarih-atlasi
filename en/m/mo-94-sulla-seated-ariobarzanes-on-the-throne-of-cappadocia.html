Sulla seated Ariobarzanes on the throne of Cappadocia

94 BC

Against Mithridates VI's holding Cappadocia through a child king, Rome had Ariobarzanes proclaimed king during Sulla's governorship of Cilicia. In the following century Cappadocia became a buffer in the Roman-Persian-Pontic contests.
