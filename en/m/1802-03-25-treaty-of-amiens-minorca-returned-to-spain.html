Treaty of Amiens — Minorca returned to Spain

25 March 1802

The Treaty of Amiens (25 March 1802), signed between France, Spain, the Netherlands and Britain, temporarily ended the War of the Second Coalition; Britain returned almost all the territories it had seized during the war (except Ceylon and Trinidad) — one of them was Minorca, taken from Spain in 1798. The peace lasted only a year and war broke out again in May 1803, but Minorca never again passed into British hands after this treaty.
