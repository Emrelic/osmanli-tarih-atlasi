Old Babylon (First Dynasty of Babylon, Hammurabi): the First Dynasty of Babylon ends (Samsuditana)

1594 BC

Hammurabi (MC 1792-1750) makes Babylon the political center of southern and central Mesopotamia; the First Dynasty of Babylon ends in the last year of Samsuditana (MC 1595/1597).
