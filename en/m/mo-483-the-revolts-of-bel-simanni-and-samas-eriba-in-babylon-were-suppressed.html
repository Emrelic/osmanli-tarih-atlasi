The revolts of Bêl-šimânni and Šamaš-eriba in Babylon were suppressed by Xerxes I

483 BC

Two revolts broke out in Babylon at the beginning of the reign of Xerxes I (Bêl-šimânni, Šamaš-eriba). Xerxes suppressed the revolts; he divided the satrapy of Babylonia and reduced the privileges of the city.
