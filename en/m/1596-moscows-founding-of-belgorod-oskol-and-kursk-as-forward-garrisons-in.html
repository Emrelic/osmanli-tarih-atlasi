Moscow's founding of Belgorod, Oskol and Kursk as forward garrisons in the steppe

1596

Advancing its southern border against the Crimean raids, the Muscovite government in 1596 founded Belgorod, Oskol and Kursk as garrison points in the wild steppe. From this date Belgorod became a fortress town and the centre of the 300-kilometre Belgorod defensive line that would extend over the following century.
