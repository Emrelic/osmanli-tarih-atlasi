Pinarbasi (Konya basin, Karaman): Neolithic occupation begins (first evidenced phase)

9599 BC

An early settlement mound in the Konya basin, beginning at the Pleistocene/Holocene transition and lasting throughout the 9th millennium cal BC; cereal cultivation seems never to have entered here (unlike Boncuklu).
