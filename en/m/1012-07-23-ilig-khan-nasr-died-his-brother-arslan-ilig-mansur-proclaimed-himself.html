Ilig Khan Nasr died; his brother Arslan Ilig Mansur proclaimed himself great khagan and took Talas, Shash, Fergana, Uzgand, Khujand, Ushrusana and Bukhara under his rule

23 July 1012

On the death of Ilig Khan Nasr (403/1012-13), his brother Mansur succeeded him as Arslan Ilig and, taking advantage of the illness of Toghan Khan Ahmad, proclaimed himself great khagan and brought Talas, Shash, Fergana, Uzgand, Khujand, Ushrusana and Bukhara under his rule; his brother Muhammad also recognised him.
