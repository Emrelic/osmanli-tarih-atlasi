Janbirdi al-Ghazali's entry into Ottoman service

8 February 1517

Janbirdi al-Ghazali, the former Mamluk emir, who had withdrawn to Salihiyya after Ridaniya, applied to the sultan and begged for aman. Selim the Grim, thinking that he would be useful in establishing order in Egypt and Syria, accepted him into his service. This choice is the first great example of the Ottoman policy of incorporating the Mamluk cadres into the system rather than liquidating them.
