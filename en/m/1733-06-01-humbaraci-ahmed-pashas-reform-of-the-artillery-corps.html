Humbaracı Ahmed Pasha's reform of the artillery corps

1 June 1733

According to the TDV article on 'Humbaracı', Ahmed Pasha, an officer of French origin who had converted to Islam, undertook in the time of Grand Vizier Hekimoğlu Ali Pasha the reform of the salaried bombardier organisation in the European manner. He established a new structure of 601 men, directly attached to the grand vizier, 300 salaried and 301 with timars. With the technology of the time he had 100 mortars and 50,000 cannonballs produced.
