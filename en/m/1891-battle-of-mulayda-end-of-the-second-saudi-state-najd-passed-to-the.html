Battle of Mulayda — end of the Second Saudi State, Najd passed to the Rashidis

1891

Relations that had soured over a dispute about zakat and the arrest of a Rashidi envoy ended with the army of Muhammad ibn Rashid, emir of Jabal Shammar, inflicting a heavy defeat on 21 January 1891 at Mulayda in the Qasim region on the Saudi forces and their allies under the leadership of Abdul Rahman bin Faisal; this was the actual end of the Second Saudi State, the Al Saud family went into exile in Kuwait and the whole of Najd came under Rashidi rule; the southern districts of Dilam (Harj), Hawta (Hawtat Bani Tamim) and Layla (Aflaj) were included in this. This rule would last until the young Abdulaziz bin Saud retook Riyadh in 1902 (Raid on Riyadh, 16 January 1902).
