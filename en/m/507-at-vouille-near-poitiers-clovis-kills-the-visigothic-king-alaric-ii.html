At Vouillé (near Poitiers) Clovis kills the Visigothic king Alaric II; Visigothic territory as far as the Pyrenees passes to the Franks

507

After completing the subjugation of the Alamanni in 506, Clovis marched the next year against the Visigothic king Alaric II. At Vouillé near Poitiers he killed Alaric with his own hand; the part of the Visigothic kingdom extending to the Pyrenees, except Septimania, was joined to the Frankish state.
