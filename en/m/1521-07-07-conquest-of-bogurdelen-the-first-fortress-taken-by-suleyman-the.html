Conquest of Böğürdelen: the first fortress taken by Süleyman the Lawgiver and the place where the decision on Belgrade was made

7 July 1521

In Süleyman the Lawgiver's first Hungarian campaign, the forces of the Beylerbeyi of Rumelia Ahmed Pasha, sent to the region, took the fortress on 7 July 1521 after a fierce siege. Böğürdelen is the first fortress conquered in the reign of Süleyman the Lawgiver. The sultan himself entered the fortress after the conquest and ordered the development of the town; the decisions concerning the conquest of Belgrade were also made here — that is, the plan for Belgrade, which would fall on 29 August 1521, was made at Böğürdelen. The fortress then remained in Ottoman hands for nearly two hundred years; it was attached first to Rumelia, from 1580 to the province of Bosnia, then briefly to Smederevo, and finally to the sancak of Izvornik. On the map this date is the day the Sava line closed in the Ottomans' favor.
