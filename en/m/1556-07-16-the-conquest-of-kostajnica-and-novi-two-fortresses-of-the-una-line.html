The conquest of Kostajnica and Novi — two fortresses of the Una line

16 July 1556

In the summer of 1556 Kostajnica, besieged by Malkoç Bey, was defended by Captain Pankracije Lusthaller with more than 60 soldiers; the fortress was surrendered only one day after the siege began, on 16 July, and Lusthaller was accused of having sold the fortress. Soon afterwards Novi too was taken and burned; thus the two most important fortresses on the Una fell into Ottoman hands in a single stroke. In the same year the Court War Council (Hofkriegsrat), which would direct frontier defence from the centre, was founded in Vienna.
