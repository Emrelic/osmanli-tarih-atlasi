Territorial details of the Peace of Pressburg: Salzburg to Austria; Brixen, Trent, Tyrol and Vorarlberg to Bavaria

26 December 1805

After Austerlitz, Austria ceded the County of Tyrol (with the prince-bishoprics of Brixen and Trent) and Vorarlberg to the king of Bavaria. In return, the former archbishopric of Salzburg and Berchtesgaden were joined to the Austrian empire.
