Annexation of the western Anatolian principalities: Saruhan, Aydın, Menteşe

1390

Three principalities were annexed in a single campaign: the centre of the Saruhanids, Manisa; the centre of the Aydınids, Aydın (including the upper fortress of Izmir, but the harbour/lower fortress still with the Knights of St. John — until 1402); the centre of the Menteşeids, Muğla-Milas, including the coastal strip of Datça-Bodrum-Marmaris-Fethiye. The Aegean coasts were united in a single hand for the first time. Other places annexed on the same date: Söke.
