Ibrahim Pasha entered Syria — the first crisis began

31 October 1831

The pretext was the governor of Acre, who would not return the fellahin who had fled from Egypt; the real reason was Mehmed Ali's need for Syria's timber, ports and manpower. The army crossed Sinai and swiftly took the Palestinian coast.
