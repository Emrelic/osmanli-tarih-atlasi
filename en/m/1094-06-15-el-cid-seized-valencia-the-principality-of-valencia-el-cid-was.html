El Cid seized Valencia: the Principality of Valencia (El Cid) was established

15 June 1094

Rodrigo Díaz de Vivar (El Cid) took control of Valencia on 15 June 1094 after a siege; he left Ibn Jahhaf in the office of qadi. Valencia left the Muluk al-Tawa'if (Andalusian taifa emirates) record and passed to the Principality of Valencia (El Cid).
