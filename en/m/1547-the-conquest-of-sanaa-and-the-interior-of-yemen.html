The conquest of Sana'a and the interior of Yemen

1547

After Taiz, Sana'a was also taken, and the entire country was attached to the beylerbeylik. However, because of the rugged geography and the sectarian difference, this domination was never comfortable; Yemen became a front that continually swallowed troops for the Ottomans.
