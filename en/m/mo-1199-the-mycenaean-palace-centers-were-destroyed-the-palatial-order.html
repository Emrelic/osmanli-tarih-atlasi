The Mycenaean palace centers were destroyed: the palatial order collapsed in the Peloponnese

1199 BC

At the end of Late Helladic IIIB / beginning of IIIC, the palace centers at Pylos, Tiryns, and Mycenae yield a layer of destruction or abandonment; the palace economy based on Linear B writing comes to an end. The cause of the destruction (earthquake, internal conflict, the Sea Peoples, drought) is disputed; the destruction layers are spread over several decades, not a single year.
