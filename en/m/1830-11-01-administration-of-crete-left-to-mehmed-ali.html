Administration of Crete left to Mehmed Ali

1 November 1830

As compensation for the loss of the Morea, the governorship of Crete was given to Mehmed Ali. The island was governed from Cairo for ten years; on the map it appears in the tributary tone throughout this period. Other places joined on the same date: Candia (Crete).
