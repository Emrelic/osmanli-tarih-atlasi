Merzifonlu Kara Mustafa Pasha appointed grand vizier

5 November 1676

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Merzifonlu Kara Mustafa Pasha, upon the death of Fazıl Ahmed Pasha he was made grand vizier on 28 Sha'ban 1087 (5 November 1676). As a son-in-law and protégé of the Köprülü family, with long experience in the administration of the state, Merzifonlu became one of the most powerful and influential grand viziers of Mehmed IV.
