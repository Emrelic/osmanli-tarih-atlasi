Mehmed IV's accession at the age of seven

8 August 1648

Immediately after Ibrahim's deposition, his son Mehmed, only seven years old, was placed on the throne and became sultan with the title Mehmed IV. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on 'Mehmed IV', such a young accession to the throne intensified the struggle for influence within the palace between his grandmother Kösem Sultan and his mother Turhan Sultan. This power struggle would end in 1651 with the killing of Kösem Sultan.
