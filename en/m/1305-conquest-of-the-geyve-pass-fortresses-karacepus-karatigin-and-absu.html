Conquest of the Geyve Pass fortresses: Karaçepüş, Karatigin and Absu

1305

TDV, Orhan entry: in the Sakarya campaign of 705 (1305) Orhan 'conquered the fortresses of Karaçepüş (Katoikia), Karatigin before Nicaea, and Absu (Hypsu: in the Geyve Pass)'. These three fortresses, by closing the passage between the Sakarya valley and the Nicaea basin, completely cut off Nicaea's help by land. Gazi Abdurrahman was settled at Karaçepüş and Absu in the Geyve pass; the city could now be brought down only by hunger.
