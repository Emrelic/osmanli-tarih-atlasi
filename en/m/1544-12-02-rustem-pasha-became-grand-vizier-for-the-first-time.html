Rüstem Pasha became grand vizier for the first time

2 December 1544

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Rüstem Pasha, after Hadım Süleyman Pasha was dismissed following a disagreement he had with a vizier, Rüstem Pasha was appointed to the grand vizierate on 17 Ramadan 951 (2 December 1544). Rüstem Pasha, whose closeness to the palace increased through his marriage to Mihrimah Sultan, became with this office one of the most influential statesmen of the age of Süleyman the Lawgiver.
