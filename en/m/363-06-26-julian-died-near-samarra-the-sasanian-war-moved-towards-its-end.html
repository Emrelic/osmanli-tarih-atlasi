Julian died near Samarra; the Sasanian war moved towards its end

26 June 363

After Julian's Ctesiphon campaign was halted by flooding, the Roman army retreated, and the emperor, wounded in a Sasanian attack at Maranga, died. Jovian, chosen by the army, was forced to make a treaty with Shapur II.
