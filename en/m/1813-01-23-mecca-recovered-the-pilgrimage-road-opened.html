Mecca recovered — the pilgrimage road opened

23 January 1813

The Egyptian forces that entered Mecca had the khutba read at the Kaaba once again in the name of the Ottoman sultan. The pilgrim caravans, interrupted for ten years, were able to set out again. On the map the Hejaz returns to Ottoman dominion, but since its administration is in Cairo, it is shown in the tributary tone. The other place passing to tributary administration on the same date: Jeddah.
