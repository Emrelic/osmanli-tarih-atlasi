Kilij Arslan I took over Nicaea and ascended the Anatolian Seljuk throne

1093

Kilij Arslan, who was released by Berkyaruq on Malikshah's death, returned to Nicaea with the Yawagiyya Turkmen and took over the city from Abu'l-Ghazi (end of the year 485); he ascended the Seljuk throne without meeting any resistance.
