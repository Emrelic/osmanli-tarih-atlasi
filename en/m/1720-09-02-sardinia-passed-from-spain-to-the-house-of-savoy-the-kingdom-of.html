Sardinia passed from Spain to the House of Savoy — the Kingdom of Sardinia was born

2 September 1720

In the last link of the settlements that closed the War of the Spanish Succession, the island of Sardinia was taken from Spain and given to the House of Savoy; the Duke of Savoy gave up Sicily in exchange and took the title King of Sardinia. Cagliari and Sassari thus emerged from two centuries of Spanish rule. The Kingdom of Sardinia that came into being is the core of the state that would found Italian unity a century and a half later — that is, the root of the Kingdom of Italy that entered Rome in 1870 goes back to here.
