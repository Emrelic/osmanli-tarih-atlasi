Ikaria declares independence

17 July 1912

In July 1912 the people of the small island of Ikaria, between Samos and Chios, rose against the Ottoman garrison and proclaimed a ‘free state’ with its own flag and stamps that lasted about five months. In November 1912 the island joined Greece.
