Jalal al-Din withdrew from Nishapur to Ghazna

1 March 1221

Jalal al-Din, who had come to Nishapur, went to a fortress near Zawzan and from there to Ghazna (Muharram 618 / March 1221); at Ghazna he gathered forces against the Mongols and defeated Mongol units.
