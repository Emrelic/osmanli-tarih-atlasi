The Recapture of the Fortress of Azov from the Cossacks

26 February 1642

The Fortress of Azov, which the Don Cossacks had seized in 1637, was evacuated in the face of the joint operation of Sultanzâde Mehmed Pasha and the Crimean Khan Mehmed Giray IV; the Cossacks set the fortress on fire and withdrew. The recovery of the northern gate of the Black Sea closed the dangerous period in which Cossack raids had reached as far as the Bosphorus of Istanbul. The fortress would be lost, this time to the Russian Tsar Peter I, in 1696.
