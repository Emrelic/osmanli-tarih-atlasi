The County of Tyrol passed to the Habsburgs

26 January 1363

After the death of her son Meinhard III (January 1363), Margarete Maultasch, countess of Tyrol, left the land to the Habsburg dukes; Tyrol joined the Habsburg community of lands without war. On the map, Innsbruck and Landeck pass in this year from the County of Tyrol to Habsburg Austria.
