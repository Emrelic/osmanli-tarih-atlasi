Manuel, despot of Thessalonica, brought the Serres and Drama region under Byzantine administration

1 November 1371

With the death of King Vukašin and Uglješa, despot of Serres, at Chirmen (26 September 1371), Serbian rule in the Serres region came to an end. The Byzantine prince Manuel II Palaiologos, who ruled in Thessalonica, brought Serres and the region including Drama under Byzantine administration in November 1371. The region passed to the Ottomans in September 1383.
