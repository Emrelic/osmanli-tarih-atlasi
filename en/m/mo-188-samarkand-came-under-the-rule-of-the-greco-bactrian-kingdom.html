Samarkand came under the rule of the Greco-Bactrian Kingdom

188 BC

Samarkand, which had been under Seleucid rule, came under the rule of the Greco-Bactrian Kingdom in 189 BC according to TDV; the Greeks remained in the city as a small ruling class.
