The Hocapaşa fire

29 September 1755

According to the TDV article on the 'Fatma Sultan Mosque', the Hocapaşa fire, which broke out at night, devastated the Sublime Porte and its surroundings; it is estimated that the Fatma Sultan Mosque in the district was also damaged in this fire. This disaster, one of the great fires that struck Istanbul in the time of Osman III, caused heavy destruction in the commercial and administrative centre of the city.
