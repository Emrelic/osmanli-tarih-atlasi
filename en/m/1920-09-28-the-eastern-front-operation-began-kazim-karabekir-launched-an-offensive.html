The Eastern Front operation began — Kâzım Karabekir launched an offensive against Armenia

28 September 1920

When the Treaty of Sèvres left Erzurum, Trabzon, Van, Bitlis and Bingöl to Armenia and Armenian attacks in the region increased, the government of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey authorized the operation on 20 September 1920. According to the TDV article on Kâzım Karabekir, Karabekir, commander of the Eastern Front, began the eastern operation on 28 September by ordering an attack by his units holding the passes of Mount Soğanlı. Sarıkamış, Göle and Kağızman were taken; the operation would continue a month later with the liberation of Kars.
