With the death of Charles III, Provence passed to King Louis XI of France under his will: Toulon

11 December 1481

Toulon, the port of Provence, became French with the passage of the county to the French crown (11 December 1481); there is no separate source specific to Toulon, the center to which it was attached, Aix-en-Provence, was taken as the basis (approximate information).
