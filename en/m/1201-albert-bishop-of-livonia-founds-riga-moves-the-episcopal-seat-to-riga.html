Albert, bishop of Livonia, founds Riga / moves the episcopal seat to Riga

1201

After the Germans' landing at the mouth of the Dvina in 1158 and the missionary activity of the archbishop of Bremen in 1186, the bishop of Livonia established his seat at Riga in 1201; soon the Order of the Sword Brothers (1202/1204) was founded.
