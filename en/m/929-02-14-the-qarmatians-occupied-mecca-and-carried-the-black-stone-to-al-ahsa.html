The Qarmatians occupied Mecca and carried the Black Stone to al-Ahsā' (Lahsā'); Qarmatian State of Bahrain as owner of Lahsā' in the first dated testimony

14 February 929

The Ismaili Qarmatians (Qarmatian State of Bahrain) occupied Mecca in 317 AH / 929 and carried the Black Stone to al-Ahsā' (Lahsā'); it was put back in its place twenty years later. In the atlas, Lahsā', which belonged to the Abbasid Caliphate, is assigned to the Qarmatians from this first dated testimony onward.
