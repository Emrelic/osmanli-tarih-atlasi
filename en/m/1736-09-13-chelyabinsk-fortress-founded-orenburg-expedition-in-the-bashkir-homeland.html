Chelyabinsk fortress founded — Orenburg expedition in the Bashkir homeland

13 September 1736

During the Orenburg expedition, Colonel Aleksey Tevkelev built a fortress at the Chelyabi site on the bank of the Miass River; the fortress protected the trans-Ural route against Bashkir uprisings. The founding day is 2 September 1736 (Julian); since the Julian-Gregorian difference was 11 days in the 18th century, the Gregorian equivalent is 13 September 1736.
