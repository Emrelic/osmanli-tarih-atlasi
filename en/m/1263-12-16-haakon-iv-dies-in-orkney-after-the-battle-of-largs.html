Haakon IV dies in Orkney after the Battle of Largs

16 December 1263

Haakon IV, who campaigned in the Hebrides in 1263 to consolidate Norwegian claims in Scotland, died in Orkney after the Battle of Largs; his reign, which had lasted since 1217, ended. His son Magnus Lagabøte ended the war with Scotland.
