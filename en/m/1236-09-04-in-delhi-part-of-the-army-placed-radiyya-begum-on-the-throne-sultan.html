In Delhi part of the army placed Radiyya Begum on the throne; Sultan Rukn al-Din Firuz Shah died in prison

4 September 1236

On the death of Iltutmish his son Rukn al-Din Firuz Shah was placed on the throne; when he left the administration to his mother Shah Turkan, rebellions broke out. The people of Delhi rose up and besieged the palace, and part of the army placed Iltutmish's daughter Radiyya Begum on the throne in the capital. Radiyya used the title 'malika' (1236-1240).
