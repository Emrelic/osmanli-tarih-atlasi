The Swedish King Gustav Vasa founded Helsinki

12 June 1550

In 1550 the Swedish king Gustav Vasa had Helsinki founded at the mouth of the Vantaa river as a new trading town to compete with Reval (Tallinn) in the Baltic trade. In 1640 the city was moved to its present site, toward the sea.
