Messina was conquered; the Strait of Messina between Sicily and Italy came under Muslim control

843

After Platani, Caltabellotta, and Corleone were taken in 840 and the whole Mazara region in 842, Messina in the northeast of the island was conquered the following year (843) and the strategic strait was brought under control.
