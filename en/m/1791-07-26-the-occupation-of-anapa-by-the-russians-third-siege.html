The occupation of Anapa by the Russians (third siege)

26 July 1791

The fortress of Anapa, built in 1781 under Ferah Ali Pasha, was besieged by the Russians three times during the war; it was occupied on 26 July 1791 at the end of the third siege. The occupation was temporary — since the old borders in the Caucasus were accepted by the Treaty of Jassy (1792), the fortress was returned to the Ottoman State.
