The Qarakhanid Harun Tegin was captured by Sanjar and executed; Tirmidh (Termez) remained in Great Seljuk hands

22 May 1102

The Qarakhanid Harun Tegin, who wanted to seize Khorasan by exploiting the confusion among Malik-Shah's sons and had captured Tirmidh, was taken prisoner by the Seljuk Sultan Sanjar and executed (2 Sha'ban 495 / 22 May 1102). On the map, Tirmidh (Termez) is shown as belonging to the Great Seljuk State from this day; this is an inference from the event (approximate information).
