The Istranca fortresses: conquest of Pınarhisar, Kırkkilise (Kırklareli) and Vize

1369

Murad I, who spent the winter of 1369 in the Edirne Palace, moved again against Byzantium in spring and took the fortresses of Pınarhisar, Kırkkilise and Vize at the foot of the Istranca mountains. These conquests caused panic in Constantinople, and Emperor John V decided to go to the pope to seek help.
