Musa Çelebi crossed to Wallachia

18 June 1406

Çelebi Mehmed, who had lost Bursa and Ankara to Süleyman Çelebi and withdrawn to Tokat-Amasya, encouraged his brother Musa to go to Rumelia. Musa, accepting the invitation of the Wallachian voivode Mircea, reached Wallachia by sea; from there he was to enter a struggle that would leave Süleyman between two fronts.
