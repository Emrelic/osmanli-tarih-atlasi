Napoleon annexed Lübeck to the French Empire

1 December 1810

After Blücher surrendered near Lübeck in November 1806, the city had been plundered by the French, and in December 1810 Napoleon annexed the city to his empire. Until this date Lübeck had been a free imperial city since 1226.
