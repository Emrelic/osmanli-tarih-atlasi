The Conquest of Yanıkkale (Győr)

27 September 1594

The grand vizier Koca Sinan Pasha, after a siege of more than a month, received the surrender of Yanıkkale, regarded as the lock on the road to Vienna. The fall of the fortress caused great panic in Europe and was the Ottomans' most brilliant gain in the Long War. However, the fortress could be held for only four years.
