The Ottoman-Safavid war began again — the grand vizier Öküz Mehmed Pasha set out on the Eastern campaign

23 April 1615

When no news came from İncili Mustafa Çavuş, who had been sent to Iran as an envoy after the 1612 Treaty of Nasuh Pasha, the grand vizier Mehmed Pasha was charged with the Eastern campaign and, having completed his preparations, crossed to Üsküdar. Just as he was setting out, news came that İncili Çavuş and the Iranian envoy Burun Qasim Khan had reached Van with an agreement and gifts, but the campaign was not postponed. The grand vizier spent the winter in Aleppo.
