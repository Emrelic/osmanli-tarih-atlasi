Founding of the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes and of Greater Romania — the same day, two states

1 December 1918

As a result of the union with Serbia of the Slavs who separated from the empire with the dissolution of Austria-Hungary, the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes was founded on 1 December 1918; about 250,000 km² of land and 12,000,000 people. Its foundation had been laid in the Corfu Declaration of 20 July 1917, and the assemblies of Podgorica on 26 October, Bosnia-Herzegovina on 28 October and Novi Sad on 25 November had decided to join. On the same day Greater Romania was formed with the union of Transylvania, Bukovina and Bessarabia by the decision of their own peoples. On the map the lands of the Kingdom of Serbia pass to Yugoslavia. With the founding of the kingdom all the lands of the Kingdom of Serbia (Niš, Skopje, Monastir, Ohrid, Pristina, Prizren, Peć and others) passed to the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes.
