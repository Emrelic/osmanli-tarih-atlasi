Battle of Carrhae (Harran): Surena destroyed the Roman army of Crassus

52 BC

Crassus's Roman legions were defeated near Harran by the Parthian cavalry and mounted archers; Crassus was killed. The battle is one of Rome's heaviest defeats in the East and consolidated the Euphrates frontier in Parthia's favour.
