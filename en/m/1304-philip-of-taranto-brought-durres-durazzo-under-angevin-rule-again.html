Philip of Taranto brought Durrës (Durazzo) under Angevin rule again

1304

In Durrës, which had passed to Byzantium in 1284, Philip, Prince of Taranto, son of King Charles II of Naples, re-established Angevin rule in 1304; the Kingdom of Albania (Regnum Albaniae) was proclaimed anew, but in Philip's lifetime it remained limited to this city (Europange: 'The kingdom of Albania was re-established in 1304, when Philip of Taranto was able to restore the Angevin regime in Durazzo'). In 1332 his son Robert gave Durrës to John of Gravina in exchange for the Principality of Achaea; the duchy of Durazzo remained in Angevin hands until Charles Thopia took the city in 1368. Only the year is known.
