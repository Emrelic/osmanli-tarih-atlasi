Selim the Grim's ceremonial entry into Cairo and reading of the khutba

15 February 1517

After the suppression of the street fighting, the sultan entered Cairo on 15 February 1517 in a most splendid ceremony, and the khutba was read in his name in the mosques. The reading of the khutba in Cairo in the name of the Ottoman sultan is the proclamation that Mamluk dominion, which had lasted since 1250, had legally ended. This date is regarded as the symbolic moment of completion of the actual conquest of Egypt.
