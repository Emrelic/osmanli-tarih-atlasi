The viscounties of Carcassonne and Béziers are confiscated by the crown

1247

The viscounty of Carcassonne (together with Béziers) was confiscated by the king in 1247 on account of the role played by Viscount Raymond Roger against Simon de Montfort in the Albigensian Crusade.
