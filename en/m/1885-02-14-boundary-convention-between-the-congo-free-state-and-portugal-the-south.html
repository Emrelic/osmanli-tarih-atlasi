Boundary convention between the Congo Free State and Portugal — the south bank of the Congo mouth to Portugal

14 February 1885

By the convention signed while the Berlin Conference was in progress, the boundary between the Congo Free State and Portugal was drawn along the river itself from the mouth of the Congo (Zaire) River to the Uango-Uango stream; the south bank, where Soyo lies, remained with Portugal, as did Cabinda to the north of the river.
