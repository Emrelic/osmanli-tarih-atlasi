Charles was crowned emperor at Rome by the pope; Byzantium did not recognize it

25 December 800

The Frankish king Charles was proclaimed 'emperor of the Romans' on 25 December 800 by being crowned by Pope Leo III. When Byzantium did not recognize Charles's imperial title, the duality between the Eastern and Western states came clearly into the open.
