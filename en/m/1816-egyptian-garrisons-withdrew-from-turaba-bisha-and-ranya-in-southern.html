Egyptian garrisons withdrew from Turaba, Bisha and Ranya in southern Hejaz

1816

In the months after Tosun Pasha made peace with the Wahhabis and returned to Cairo in November 1815, disorder broke out in southern Hejaz; the garrisons of Mehmed Ali Pasha of Kavala were compelled to withdraw from Bisha, Ranya and Turaba. Ibrahim Pasha's departure from Cairo in August 1816 for a new campaign came after this withdrawal. Who ruled Turaba until the fall of Diriyah (1818) is not written in the source.
