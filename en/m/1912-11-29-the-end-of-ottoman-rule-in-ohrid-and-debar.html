The end of Ottoman rule in Ohrid and Debar

29 November 1912

Eleven days after the fall of Monastir, the Serbian army entered the lake region of western Macedonia; five centuries of Ottoman rule ended in Ohrid, and Debar to the north was also lost in the same operation.
