Pairisades V left his kingdom to Mithridates VI: the Bosporus was bound to Pontus

108 BC

Pairisades V, under Scythian pressure, transferred the kingdom to Mithridates with the help of Mithridates's commander Diophantus; the Bosporus thus became the northern extension of Pontus. The Black Sea grain trade passed to Pontus at this date as a resource of Mithridates.
