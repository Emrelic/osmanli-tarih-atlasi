Wallachian Uprising — Ypsilantis crossing the Prut and the Vladimirescu uprising

22 February 1821

Alexander Ypsilantis, a general in the Russian army, crossed the Prut in the name of the Filiki Eteria and tried to start a general Greek uprising in Wallachia and Moldavia; in the same days Tudor Vladimirescu also mobilized the native peasants. The two movements could not unite: Vladimirescu answered Ypsilantis's call to fight the Turks together with 'I did not rise against the Turks but against the Greeks' and was killed shortly afterwards by Ypsilantis's men. The revolt was suppressed by Ottoman forces in May, but the Phanariot voivodeship order ended and the flame of revolt spread to the Morea.
