Kamgot (Early Lapita settlement), Babase Island, Anir Islands: Kamgot (Anir) early Lapita settlement

1324 BC

An Early Lapita settlement (with stilt houses) on the lagoon edge in the Anir islands; an economy with pigs and dogs brought in (confirmed by parasite eggs, Horrocks et al. 2024).
