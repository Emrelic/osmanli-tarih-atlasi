The Victoria returns to Sanlúcar after circling the world

6 September 1522

The Victoria entered Sanlúcar on 6 September 1522 and anchored at Seville on 8 September; of the 5 ships that set out, only one returned, with about 18 men (the number of men is based on general knowledge).
