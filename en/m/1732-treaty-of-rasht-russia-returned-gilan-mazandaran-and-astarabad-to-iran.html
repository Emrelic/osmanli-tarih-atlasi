Treaty of Rasht — Russia returned Gilan, Mazandaran and Astarabad to Iran

1732

Russia, which after the death of Peter I could not obtain the revenue it had expected from the Caspian coastal provinces and was suffering heavy losses from disease, came to an agreement with the rising Nadir, gave up its rights over Mazandaran and Astarabad and agreed to evacuate Gilan and Talysh; Derbent and Baku remained for the time being in Russian hands. The same winter the Ottomans, too, by the Treaty of Ahmed Pasha, were giving back the greater part of western Iran to Iran: the country partitioned in 1724 was being taken back eight years later from two sides at once.
