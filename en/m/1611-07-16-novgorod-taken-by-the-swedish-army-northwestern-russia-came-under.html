Novgorod taken by the Swedish army — Northwestern Russia came under Swedish control in the Time of Troubles

16 July 1611

The Swedish army of Jacob De la Gardie took Novgorod; until 1617 the town was the capital of the 'Novgorod State' under Swedish protection, and Charles Philip was put forward as candidate for the tsardom.
