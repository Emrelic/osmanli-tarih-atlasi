The Yıldırım Darüşşifa at Bursa — first Ottoman hospital

1400

In the years in which Bayezid the Thunderbolt had the magnificent Great Mosque (Ulucami) built at Bursa, he also gave the city a dar al-shifa (hospital); this was the link devoted to medicine in a broad programme of pious foundations consisting of an imaret, a madrasa, a han and a bridge. This building, the first hospital founded by the state, also functioned as a medical school training physicians, and determined the model of Ottoman health institutions. Completed only two years before the Ankara catastrophe, the work is among the last links of Yıldırım's vision of turning Bursa into an imperial capital.
