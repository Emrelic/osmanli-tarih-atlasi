The Red Army captured Vienna — the Battle of Vienna ended

13 April 1945

The Red Army reached the western edge of Vienna on 5 April 1945; at 14:00 on 13 April the Soviet command announced that the fighting was over. Renner's provisional government proclaimed Austria's independence two weeks later, on 27 April (on the map Vienna switches from Germany to the Second Republic on 27 April).
