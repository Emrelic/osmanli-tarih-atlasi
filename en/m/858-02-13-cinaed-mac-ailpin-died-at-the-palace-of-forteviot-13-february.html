Cináed mac Ailpín died at the palace of Forteviot (13 February)

13 February 858

According to the Chronicle of the Kings of Alba, the historical list of the kings of Alba (Scotland), Cináed mac Ailpín died at the palace of Forteviot on 13 February; the Annals of Ulster place his death in 858. In the atlas, Forteviot is recorded as the Kingdom of Scotland from this first dated attestation.
