The Victory of Banja Luka — The Liberation of Bosnia

4 August 1737

When Austrian forces entered Bosnia and besieged Banja Luka, the governor of Bosnia, Hekimoğlu Ali Pasha, hurried to the rescue with forces he had gathered from the local population and inflicted a heavy defeat on the numerically superior enemy. This victory collapsed Austria's plan to occupy Bosnia at the very first move, and the north of the province was completely recovered. Banja Luka became one of the turning points that restored the morale of the Ottoman army, which after 1683 had been constantly retreating before Austria.
