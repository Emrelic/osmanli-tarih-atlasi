Lepanto (İnebahtı) passes to Venice

1 July 1687

After the fall of Patras, Lepanto at the mouth of the Gulf of Corinth was also abandoned by the Ottomans and passed under Venetian control; it would be returned by the Treaty of Karlowitz in 1699.
