The History of Naîmâ (Ravzatü'l-Hüseyn) was printed at the Müteferrika Press

1734

After Naîmâ's death, Ravzatü'l-Hüseyn, known popularly as the History of Naîmâ, was printed by İbrâhim Müteferrika in 1147 (1734) in two large volumes and 500 copies. The preface written by Müteferrika at the beginning of the first volume was repeated in later editions. The work is one of the first major history books printed in Turkish at the press.
