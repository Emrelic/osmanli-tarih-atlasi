Nakhchivan and Ordubad come under Ottoman administration

1586

After Erivan was taken in 1583 and made a beylerbeyilik center, the forces that descended southeast along the Aras valley brought Nakhchivan and Ordubad under Ottoman administration; the two cities became sancaks of the newly established province of Erivan. According to the Nakhchivan entry of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, lasting Ottoman administration was established in the city for the first time during this war — in the Nakhchivan campaign of 1553 the city had been taken but merely plundered and abandoned. Thus the army road between Erivan and Tabriz was opened, and in September of the same year Tabriz could be entered. Shah Abbas would retake Nakhchivan in 1603.
