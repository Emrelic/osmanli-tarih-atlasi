Bilecik is first mentioned — the border with Byzantium began at Bilecik

20 March 1284

In the events of the year 683 reported by Neşrî, Bilecik appears as the place where the border between the frontier principality of Osman Bey and Byzantium began. This is the first known mention of the settlement in history; it is not its foundation.
