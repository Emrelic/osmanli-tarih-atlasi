Russia built the fortress of Mozdok in Kabarda

1763

In Kabarda, which the Treaty of Belgrade (1739) had left neutral, a struggle had begun between pro-Ottoman and pro-Russian princes. Taking advantage of this confusion, Russia in 1763 had the fortress of Mozdok built on the bank of the Terek, despite the objection of the Ottoman State and of the Kabardian princes. In the following years the fortress became the eastern end of the Russian fortified line extending from Azov to Mozdok and was regarded as one of the causes of the war of 1768-74.
