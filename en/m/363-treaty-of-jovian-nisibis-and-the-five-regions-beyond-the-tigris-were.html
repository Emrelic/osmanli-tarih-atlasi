Treaty of Jovian: Nisibis and the five regions beyond the Tigris were ceded to the Sasanians

363

To save his encircled army, Jovian agreed to cede Nisibis, Singara and the five regions beyond the Tigris to the Sasanians; Rome lost the gains of 298 after half a century. The people of Nisibis withdrew to Roman territory.
