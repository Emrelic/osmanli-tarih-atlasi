The Beginning of the Celali Rebellions: The Uprising of Şeyh Celâl of Bozok

1 March 1519

In the Bozok region a man named Şeyh Celâl proclaimed himself the Mahdi and rose in revolt around Tokat; although the rebellion was suppressed, his name became for a century the name of all brigand and uprising movements in Anatolia. Combined with the disintegration of the timar system and population pressure at the end of the sixteenth century, Celalism turned into the longest-lasting internal security problem of the empire. The uprisings led to mass migrations in Anatolia known as the 'great flight'.
