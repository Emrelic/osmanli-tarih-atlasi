Loss of Temesvár (Timișoara) to Austria

13 October 1716

After the Varadin rout, Prince Eugène marched directly on Temesvár, and after a siege of more than forty days the fortress was surrendered by capitulation. The city, conquered in the time of Süleyman the Lawgiver in 1552 and regarded for 164 years, as a beylerbeylik center, as the eastern pillar of the defense of Hungary, was thus lost. With the fall of Temesvár the whole of the Banat region passed to Austria and the Ottoman frontier was pushed south of the Danube. Other settlements passing from the Ottomans to the Habsburgs on the same day: Csákóvár, Facset and Caransebeș — together with the transfer of the Temesvár eyalet (TDV Timişvar; no day at place level in the source, the date of the center to which they were attached was taken as the basis; approximate information).
