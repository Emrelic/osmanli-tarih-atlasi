Surrender of Strasbourg: German troops occupied the city

28 September 1870

After a six-week siege and bombardment, General Uhrich signed the capitulation at night in Koenigshoffen; in the morning the gates and the Citadel were handed over to Prussian and Baden troops. The city remained under military occupation until the Treaty of Frankfurt (10 May 1871).
