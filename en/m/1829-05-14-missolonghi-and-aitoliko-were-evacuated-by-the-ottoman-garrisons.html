Missolonghi and Aitoliko were evacuated by the Ottoman garrisons

14 May 1829

After the surrender of Lepanto on 30 April 1829, the garrisons of Missolonghi and Aitoliko evacuated the towns on 14 May 1829 and left them to the Greek forces.
