Taking of Suez — gate opening to the Red Sea

24 January 1517

With the entry of the Ottoman troops into Cairo after the victory of Ridaniya, Suez at the northern end of the Red Sea also passed to Ottoman administration. This dockyard-port, from which the Mamluks had sent a fleet to the Indian Ocean, became the base of the southern policy to be conducted against Portugal. Suez is the northern mouth of the line along which, three centuries later, the canal would be opened.
