The rule of Malik Muzaffar al-Din, ruler of the Şebinkarahisar branch of the Mengujekids, ends; Kırşehir is given to him as iqta'

1228

In 1228 Ala al-Din Kayqubad abolished the Erzincan branch of the Mengujekids; Malik Muzaffar al-Din, lord of Şebinkarahisar of the dynasty, was given Kırşehir as iqta' and, together with some places, the town of Efsus as mulk.
