Geneva voted for the Reformation: the rule of the bishop and of Savoy came to an end, the Republic of Geneva was born

21 May 1536

By a vote of the general council on 21 May 1536, Geneva adopted the Reformation; the city remained an independent republic at Bern's side but did not become a member of the Confederation.
