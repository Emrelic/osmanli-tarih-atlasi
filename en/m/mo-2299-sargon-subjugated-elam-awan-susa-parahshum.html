Sargon subjugated Elam (Awan, Susa, Parahshum)

2299 BC

Sargon's year names and inscriptions report the defeat of Elam (Awan, Parahshum); Susa became an Akkadian city administered by Akkadian governors and Akkadian-language documents. This is the first time Elam was bound to a Mesopotamia-centred empire.
