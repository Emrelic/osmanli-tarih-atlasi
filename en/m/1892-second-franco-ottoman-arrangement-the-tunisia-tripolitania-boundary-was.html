Second Franco-Ottoman arrangement — the Tunisia-Tripolitania boundary was extended to Ghadames

1892

A second Franco-Ottoman arrangement determined the boundary between Tunisia and the vilayet of Tripolitania in more detail than before and in the interior as far as Ghadames. The actual document that drew today's boundary is the Convention of Tripoli, to be signed eighteen years later.
