Peace of Vienna: Ottokar II, king of Bohemia, renounces Austria, Styria, Carinthia and Carniola

21 November 1276

After Rudolf of Habsburg was elected king (1273), he placed Ottokar under the imperial ban and made war in June 1276; having detached Henry, duke of Lower Bavaria, from Ottokar, Rudolf forced the king of Bohemia in November 1276 to give up the four provinces. Ottokar was re-enfeoffed only with Bohemia and Moravia, and his son Wenceslaus was betrothed to Rudolf's daughter; Rudolf entered Vienna in triumph.
