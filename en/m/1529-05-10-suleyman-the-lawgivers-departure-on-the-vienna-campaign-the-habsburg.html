Süleyman the Lawgiver's departure on the Vienna campaign — the Habsburg war began again

10 May 1529

After Mohács the Hungarian throne had been split in two, and in 1527 Ferdinand had taken Buda and driven Szapolyai out of the capital. The sultan set out on campaign for the fourth time to support Szapolyai and halt the Habsburg advance; before the campaign, the grand vizier Ibrahim Pasha was given the title of serasker and the beylerbeyilik of Rumelia, and all the planning was done with him. Heavy rains along the way delayed the army's advance; the campaign continued with the taking of Buda and its being given to Szapolyai, and the siege of Vienna.
