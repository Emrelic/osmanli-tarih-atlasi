The Tannu Tuva People's Republic declared independence

14 August 1921

Independence was declared under the leadership of the Tuvan People's Revolutionary Party; the first article of the first constitution stated that the state acted 'in international relations under the protection of Soviet Russia'. With this event Kyzyl (Belotsarsk, Tannu-Tuva) passed to the Tannu-Tuva People's Republic.
