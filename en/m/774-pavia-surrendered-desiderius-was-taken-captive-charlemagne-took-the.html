Pavia surrendered; Desiderius was taken captive; Charlemagne took the title "King of the Lombards" (the Lombard Kingdom ended)

774

At the call of Pope Hadrian I, Charlemagne crossed the Alps in 773 and entered the Lombard Kingdom; in June 774 Pavia surrendered and King Desiderius was sent to a monastery. Charles took the titles "King of the Lombards" and "Patrician of the Romans".
