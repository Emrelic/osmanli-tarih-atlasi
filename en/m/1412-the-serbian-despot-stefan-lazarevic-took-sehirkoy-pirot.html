The Serbian Despot Stefan Lazarević took Şehirköy (Pirot)

1412

While the struggle between Musa Çelebi, who dominated Rumelia during the Interregnum, and his brother Çelebi Mehmed was continuing, the Serbian Despot Stefan Lazarević seized the fortress of Şehirköy, on the road between Niš and Sofia, and held it against Musa Çelebi's attack. The following year, with Musa's death, Çelebi Mehmed formally left the fortress to his vassal Stefan; the Ottomans retook Şehirköy only in 1428, after Lazarević's death.
