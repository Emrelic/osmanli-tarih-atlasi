The people of Basra complete their move to the present site — old Basra is abandoned

2 October 1494

The people had gradually begun to settle in present-day Basra, which they found safer against Bedouin attacks and where they could obtain water more easily; the move to the new site was completed in the year 900 (1494–95). Old Basra, founded in the time of Caliph Umar, was about 25 km from the present city.
