Marriage of Bayezid the Thunderbolt and Despina Hatun

15 January 1390

The vassalage established with the Serbian despotate after Kosovo was sealed by the marriage of Olivera, daughter of Lazar, to Bayezid — a typical example of early marriage diplomacy.
