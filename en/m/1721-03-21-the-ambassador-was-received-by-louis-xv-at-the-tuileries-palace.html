The ambassador was received by Louis XV at the Tuileries Palace

21 March 1721

The ambassador, who entered Paris in a splendid ceremony, presented Ahmed III's letter and gifts to King Louis XV on 21 March 1721. Then he toured Versailles, Saint-Cloud, Trianon and Marly and watched the opera.
