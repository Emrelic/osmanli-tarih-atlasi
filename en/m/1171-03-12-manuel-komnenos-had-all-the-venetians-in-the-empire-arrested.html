Manuel Komnenos had all the Venetians in the empire arrested

12 March 1171

Disturbed by Venice's policy in Italy and Dalmatia, Manuel ordered on 12 March 1171 the arrest of all the Venetians in the empire and the confiscation of their property. Venice responded by sending a fleet.
