Clovis succeeded his father Childeric as king of the Salian Franks; the centre of power was Tournai

481

Clovis succeeded his father Childeric I as king of the Salian Franks in 481 at the age of fifteen. At that date the Salian Franks had advanced as far as the river Somme, and the centre of their power was Tournai.
