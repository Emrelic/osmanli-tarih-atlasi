Waira-jirca ceramic phase (Huánuco basin, Jancao): Wairajirca pottery ends

1129 BC

The Waira-jirca ceramic tradition in the Huánuco basin is modelled as five phases (I–V) at Jancao; the authors place these phases from the late 18th to the late 12th century cal BC.
