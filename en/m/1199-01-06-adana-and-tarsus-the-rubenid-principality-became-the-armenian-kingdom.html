Adana and Tarsus: the Rubenid principality became the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia

6 January 1199

The Rubenid dynasty, which held Adana and Tarsus, took the title of kingdom; this is a change of title of the same dynasty, and no territory changed hands. The coronation day was NOT FOUND in TDV — the date was taken over from the start date of the atlas's entry for the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia (that date is not itself a source).
