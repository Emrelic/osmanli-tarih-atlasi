Napata (Jebel Barkal): begins

1499 BC

Founded in the New Kingdom as a fortified outpost of Egypt; the principal city of the Kushite dynasty (Napata period 1070–300 BCE), it retained its importance throughout the Meroe period (300 BCE–350 CE).
