Pueblo Revolt began: Taos, Picuris and other Pueblo villages targeted the Spaniards and their priests

10 August 1680

The Pueblo villages of New Mexico rose up together on 10 August 1680: Spanish soldiers were killed at Santa Clara, and at Jemez, following the example of Taos and Picuris, a call was made to kill the priests and the Spaniards; the uprising led Governor Otermín to evacuate Santa Fe.
