Old Kingdom (Pyramid Age): ends

2242 BC

The kingdom of the Pyramid Age; according to Erdil et al. 2025, the beginning of the OK is ~2698–2629 BCE (95%), and the transition to the First Intermediate Period ~2283–2204 BCE (95%). The 'Low' chronology is no longer supported in this range.
