Caliph al-Qadir-Billah confirmed Mahmud's sultanate and gave him the title Yamin al-Dawla

1 November 999

After taking Khurasan, Mahmud sent an envoy to Baghdad to inform the Abbasid caliph of his victory; the caliph confirmed his sultanate and gave him the titles 'Yamin al-Dawla wa Amin al-Milla' (Dhu'l-Hijja 389 / November 999).
