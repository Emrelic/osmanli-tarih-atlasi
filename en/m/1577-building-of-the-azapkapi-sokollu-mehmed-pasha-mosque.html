Building of the Azapkapı (Sokollu Mehmed Pasha) Mosque

1577

The mosque, located beside the Şişhane-side pier of the Unkapanı Bridge, was had built by Mimar Sinan by Grand Vizier Sokollu Mehmed Pasha. The building is one of two Sinan mosques constructed in Sokollu's name (the other is the mosque at Kadırga).
