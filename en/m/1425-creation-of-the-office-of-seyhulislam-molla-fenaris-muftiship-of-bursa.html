Creation of the office of şeyhülislam — Molla Fenari's muftiship of Bursa

1425

The appointment in 1425 of the scholar Molla Fenari, who for many years had been kadi of Bursa, to the muftiship of Bursa is regarded, in the TDV's words, as "the beginning of the muftiship and şeyhülislamate among the Ottomans." The TDV, however, notes that this is not identical to the fully institutionalized office of şeyhülislam of the sixteenth century, which represented the entire ilmiye organization.
