Henry VI is crowned king of Sicily at Palermo; the kingdom passes to the Hohenstaufen

25 December 1194

Henry VI entered Palermo on 20 November 1194 and was crowned king on Christmas Day; the next day his son Frederick was born at Jesi. The Kingdom of Sicily passed from the Norman dynasty to the Hohenstaufen dynasty.
