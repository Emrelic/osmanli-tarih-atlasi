Merneptah defeated the alliance of Libyans and Sea Peoples in the western Delta; the name 'Israel' was first mentioned in Canaan

1208 BC

In the 5th year of Merneptah, the Meshwesh under the leadership of the Libu and the 'Peoples of the Sea' (Ekwesh, Teresh, Lukka, Sherden, Shekelesh) invaded the western Delta; the Egyptian army scattered them in a six-hour battle at Perire. The victory stele describing the same events names, in Canaan, Ashkelon, Gezer, Yenoam and 'Israel'; this is the earliest known mention of the name Israel.
