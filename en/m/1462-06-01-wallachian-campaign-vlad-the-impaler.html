Wallachian campaign: Vlad the Impaler

1 June 1462

With the campaign organized against Vlad Țepeș, who refused to pay tribute and killed envoys, Wallachia was returned to vassalage; the night attack and the scenes of impaled terror became legendary in Europe. Other places annexed on the same date: Bucharest.
