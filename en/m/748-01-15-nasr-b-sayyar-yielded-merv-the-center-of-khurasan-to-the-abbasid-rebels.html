Nasr b. Sayyar yielded Merv, the center of Khurasan, to the Abbasid rebels

15 January 748

The Umayyad governor of Khurasan, Nasr, was forced to abandon Merv on 9 Jumada I 130 (15 January 748) and Nishapur in June; before the end of 749 the Abbasid armies had brought all of Khurasan under their control.
