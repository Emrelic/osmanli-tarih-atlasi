Zama: Scipio and Masinissa defeated Hannibal

201 BC

In the battle of Zama, fought after Hannibal's recall to Africa, Scipio's legions and Masinissa's Numidian cavalry defeated the Carthaginian army. With this battle the Second Punic War ended decisively in Rome's favour.
