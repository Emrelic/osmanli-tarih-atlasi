The Russian occupation of Trabzon

18 April 1916

The Russian troops, who broke through the defensive line at Karadere on 14 April and reached Yomra on 16 April, entered Trabzon on 18 April after the Turkish population had evacuated the city. The Russian occupation lasted until 24 February 1918.
