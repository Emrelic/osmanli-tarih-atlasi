Slovenian Partisans liberated Ljubljana

9 May 1945

Units of the 29th Division and the 7th Corps of the Slovenian Partisan Army entered Ljubljana on 9 May 1945 and liberated the city. The administration of the German Operational Zone of the Adriatic Littoral (OZAK) thus came to an end.
