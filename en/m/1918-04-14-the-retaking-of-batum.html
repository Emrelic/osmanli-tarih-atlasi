The retaking of Batum

14 April 1918

When Russia withdrew from the war with the Treaty of Brest-Litovsk, the Ottoman army, advancing eastward, retook the port of Batum, lost in 1878, forty years later. This port at the eastern end of the Black Sea was the sea outlet of Baku oil. The gain lasted only seven months; after the Armistice of Mudros the region would be evacuated.
