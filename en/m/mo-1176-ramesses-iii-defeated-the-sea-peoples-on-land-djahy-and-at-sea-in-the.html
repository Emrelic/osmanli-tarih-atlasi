Ramesses III defeated the Sea Peoples on land (Djahy) and at sea in the Delta

1176 BC

In the 8th year of Ramesses III the Peleset, Tjeker, Denyen, Sherden and other Sea Peoples advanced on the Egyptian frontier by land and sea; the Egyptian army stopped them first on the Canaanite frontier and then in the naval battle at the mouths of the Delta. The reliefs at Medinet Habu document this. Egypt kept Canaan, but after this age its influence in the Eastern Mediterranean began to erode.
