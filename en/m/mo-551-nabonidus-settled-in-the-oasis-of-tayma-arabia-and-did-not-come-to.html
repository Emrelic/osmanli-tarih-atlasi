Nabonidus settled in the oasis of Tayma (Arabia) and did not come to Babylon; the Akitu festival could not be held

551 BC

While Nabonidus stayed in the oasis of Tayma, the crown prince Belshazzar waited in Akkad with the army; in the month of Nisan, because the king did not come, Marduk did not enter Babylon and the New Year (Akitu) festival could not be held. This was one of the reasons the temple priests gave for supporting Cyrus against Nabonidus.
