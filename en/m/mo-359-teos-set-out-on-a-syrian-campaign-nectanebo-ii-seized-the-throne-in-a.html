Teos set out on a Syrian campaign; Nectanebo II seized the throne in a coup, and Agesilaus changed sides

359 BC

Teos gathered Greek mercenaries (the Spartan king Agesilaus and the Athenian Chabrias) to attack Persian Syria; while he was on campaign his nephew Nectanebo II proclaimed himself king. Agesilaus sided with Nectanebo; Plutarch writes that in Greece Agesilaus was criticized for fighting for pay in his old age for a 'barbarian rebel'.
