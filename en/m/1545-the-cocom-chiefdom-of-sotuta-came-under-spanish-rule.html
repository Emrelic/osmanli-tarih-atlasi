The Cocom chiefdom of Sotuta came under Spanish rule

1545

Sotuta, one of the most powerful Maya chiefdoms in the interior of Yucatán, resisted the Spanish advance for a long time under the Cocom dynasty. In the years after the founding of Mérida in 1542 the chiefdom accepted Spanish rule; this allegiance became permanent after the great eastern revolt of 1546–47 was suppressed.
