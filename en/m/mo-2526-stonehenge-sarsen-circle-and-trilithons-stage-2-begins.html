Stonehenge sarsen circle and trilithons (Stage 2): begins

2526 BC

A horseshoe of 5 sarsen trilithons and a sarsen circle of 30 uprights + 30 lintels are erected; a double bluestone circle is added; the whole of Stage 2 spans 2760–2510 and 2470–2300 cal BC.
