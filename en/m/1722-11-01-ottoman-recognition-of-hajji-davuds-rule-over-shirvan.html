Ottoman recognition of Hajji Dâvud's rule over Shirvan

1 November 1722

In November 1722, while the Ottomans were giving the Russian forces an ultimatum to withdraw from the region, they recognized Hajji Dâvud's rule over Shirvan. Hajji Dâvud thus became a vassal ruler dependent on the Ottomans, like the khan of the Crimea.
