A cross is erected; Caminha writes his letter to the king

1 May 1500

On 1 May 1500 a cross was erected on the shore and Mass was held, and Caminha completed his letter reporting the discovery to King Manuel I; the letter was sent to Lisbon with Gaspar de Lemos.
