Hallstatt C -> D transition (Dietfurt Bayesian model; traditional 616): Hallstatt C → D transition (14C model)

669 BC

In southern Germany the transition from Ha C to Ha D lies, according to the Bayesian model of the Dietfurt burial sequence, before 650 cal BC and most probably between 685 and 655; this is decades earlier than the commonly accepted date.
