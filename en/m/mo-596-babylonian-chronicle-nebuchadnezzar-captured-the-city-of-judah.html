Babylonian Chronicle: Nebuchadnezzar captured the city of Judah (Jerusalem) and took its king prisoner (month of Addaru, 7th year; 597 BCE)

596 BC

The Babylonian Chronicle (ABC 5) records that in Nebuchadnezzar's 7th year the king of Akkad marched to the land of Hatti and besieged the 'city of Judah', that on the second day of the month of Addaru he took the city and captured its king, appointed a king of his own choosing there, and collected heavy tribute. According to Jewish belief, this event is the first Babylonian siege, in which King Jehoiachin was exiled; the Torah places this event in Nebuchadnezzar's 8th year (2 Kings 24/12), while Jeremiah places it in his 7th year (52/28).
