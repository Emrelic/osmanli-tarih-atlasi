Abdullah b. Rashid seized the emirate of Ha'il: the founding of the Shammar (Rashidi) dynasty

1835

With the fall of Diriyah to the forces of Mehmed Ali Pasha in 1818, the Jabal Shammar region left Saudi rule and the emirate remained in the hands of the Ibn Ali family. Abdullah b. Rashid, who supported the re-establishment of Ottoman rule, together with his brother Ubayd won the struggle he waged against the Ibn Ali family in 1835, seized the emirate of Ha'il and established the rule of the Rashidi dynasty.
