Alexander entered Egypt; the Persian satrap Mazaces surrendered, and Egypt passed to Macedonian rule

331 BC

Alexander, setting out from Gaza, reached Pelusium on the seventh day, and his fleet had arrived there too. The satrap Mazaces, who after Issus had no Persian support left, did not resist and received Alexander amicably in the cities. Alexander placed a garrison at Pelusium and advanced to Memphis; he left the administration to two native governors (Doloaspis, Petisis) and appointed Macedonian commanders to Memphis and Pelusium. Egypt left Persian rule and joined Alexander's empire.
