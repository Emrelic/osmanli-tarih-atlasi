Alexandria taken over with the fleet — completion of the conquest of Egypt

19 May 1517

Exactly four months after Ridaniya and thirty-six days after Tumanbay's execution, Alexandria passed to Ottoman administration with the arrival at the port of the Ottoman fleet under Cafer Bey; delta ports such as Rosetta (Reşîd) and Damietta were also attached in the same process. That this great Mediterranean port of Egypt was taken over months after the fall of Cairo, and from the sea rather than by land, clearly shows that the conquest was completed not by a single battle but region by region. Alexandria would thereafter become the vital port linking Istanbul and Cairo. Other places joined on the same date: Reşîd (Rosetta).
