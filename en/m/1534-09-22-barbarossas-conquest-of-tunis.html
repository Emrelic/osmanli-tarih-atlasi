Barbarossa's Conquest of Tunis

22 September 1534

The grand admiral (kaptan-ı deryâ) Barbarossa Hayreddin Pasha, after striking the coasts of Italy with his fleet, turned to Tunis, put the Hafsid ruler Mulay Hasan to flight and entered the city. The capture of Tunis, with its position controlling the Strait of Sicily, turned the balance of power in the Western Mediterranean at once in favour of the Ottomans. However, this dominance would not last long, and Charles V would respond the following year with a great fleet.
