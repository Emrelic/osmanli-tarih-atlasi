Osijek passed into the hands of the Kingdom of Hungary (Habsburg Crown): the loss of Slavonia began

26 September 1687

After the rout at Harsány, the bridge fortress of Osijek on the Drava passed to the Habsburg forces on 26 September 1687. The Ottoman military presence and the Turkish population in Slavonia gradually withdrew between 1686 and 1688; the region came under Habsburg administration.
