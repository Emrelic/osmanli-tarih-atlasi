Mahmud made his first campaign into the Ghur region

1011

To bring the Ghurids, who were waylaying travellers, under obedience, Mahmud made his first campaign into the Ghur region in 401 (1011); he forced some local chiefs into submission and appointed teachers to instruct them in the religion of Islam. The second campaign was made in 411 (1020).
