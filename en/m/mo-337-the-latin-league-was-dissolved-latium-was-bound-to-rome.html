The Latin League was dissolved: Latium was bound to Rome

337 BC

At the end of the Latin War (340-338) Rome dissolved the Latin League; the cities were added to Roman citizenship or bound to Rome one by one, and alliance among themselves was forbidden. This is the foundation of Rome's model of expansion in Italy.
