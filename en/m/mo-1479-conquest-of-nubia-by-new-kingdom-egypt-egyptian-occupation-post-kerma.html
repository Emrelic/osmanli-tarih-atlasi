Conquest of Nubia by New Kingdom Egypt / Egyptian occupation (post-Kerma): the Egyptian occupation of Nubia begins

1479 BC

The Kerma kingdom was conquered by the New Kingdom pharaohs; kerma.ch attributes the conquest to Thutmose I (1496-1483) and notes that Egyptian influence was felt south of the Third Cataract only under Thutmose III (1479-1424).
