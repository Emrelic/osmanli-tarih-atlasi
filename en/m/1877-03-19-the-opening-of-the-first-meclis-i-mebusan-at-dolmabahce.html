The Opening of the First Meclis-i Meb'ûsan at Dolmabahçe

19 March 1877

The 115 deputies elected in accordance with the Kanûn-ı Esâsî met for the first time on 19 March 1877 in a ceremony held in the Muayede Hall of the Dolmabahçe Palace; thus the period of representative assemblies began in Ottoman history. Muslim and non-Muslim deputies sharply questioned the government and debated the conduct of the war and the responsibility of the ministers. This bold opposition would be the principal reason that shortened the life of the assembly.
