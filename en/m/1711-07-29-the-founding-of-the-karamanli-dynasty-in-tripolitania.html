The Founding of the Karamanli Dynasty in Tripolitania

29 July 1711

In Tripolitania, exhausted by the quarrels of the ocak agas, the cavalry officer Ahmed Karamanlı seized the administration and made his position official with the beylerbeyilik berat he received from Istanbul. A dynasty lasting from 1711 to 1835 was born through Ahmed Pasha, his son Mehmed Pasha and his grandson Yûsuf Pasha; a semi-independent structure emerged which built its own fleet and signed treaties directly with European states. This period would end only in 1835, with the direct attachment of the province to the centre.
