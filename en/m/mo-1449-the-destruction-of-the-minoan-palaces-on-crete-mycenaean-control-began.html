The destruction of the Minoan palaces on Crete; Mycenaean control began at Knossos

1449 BC

At the end of Late Minoan IB (mid-15th century BCE) the palace centers and villas on Crete suffer destruction; in the subsequent phase at Knossos, the Linear B tablets show a palace administration written in Greek, that is, Mycenaean control. Thus Minoan dominance on Crete ends.
