Before Dorostolon (Silistra) Tzimiskes defeated Svyatoslav

23 April 971

On 23 April 971, Tzimiskes engaged Svyatoslav's Rus army in a great battle on the plain twelve miles south of Dorostolon; the Rus forces were routed and a siege lasting three months began in the city.
