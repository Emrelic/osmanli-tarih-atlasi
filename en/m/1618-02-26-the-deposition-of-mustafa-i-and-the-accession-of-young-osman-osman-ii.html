The Deposition of Mustafa I and the Accession of Young Osman (Osman II)

26 February 1618

On the grounds that he was not of sound mind, Mustafa I was deposed after ninety-six days, and Ahmed I's fourteen-year-old son Osman II became sultan. According to the account based on Hasanbeyzâde, the chief black eunuch (Kızlar Ağası) Mustafa Agha was influential in this deposition decision. With his plan to abolish the Janissary corps and form a new army, Young Osman is regarded as the first radical figure of the Ottoman reform debates. This plan would cost him his life four years later.
