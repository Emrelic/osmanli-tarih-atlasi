Muslim ibn Quraysh is defeated and killed by Suleyman Shah at Kurzahil

20 June 1085

Muslim ibn Quraysh, the Uqaylid who wished to found a united Arab state independent of the Great Seljuk Sultan, fought Suleyman Shah when he had the Antioch region plundered; he was defeated and killed at the place called Kurzahil (24 Safar 478 / 20 June 1085) and was buried at the Aleppo Gate.
