Timur took Herat: end of the Kart dynasty and the passage of Khorasan to the Timurids

1 April 1381

In 1380 Khorasan lay fragmented by the struggle among the Sarbadars, the Karts and the Muzaffarids. After sending his son Miran Shah to the region, Timur himself marched on Herat and, after besieging it for some time, took the city over from the Kart malik Ghiyath al-Din Pir Ali. The revolt that broke out after his departure was suppressed in blood by Miran Shah, and the Kart dynasty, which had ruled in Herat since 1255, was brought to an end. All of Khorasan, from Mashhad to Nishapur, came under Timurid rule on this date.
