Six men of the Khazraj tribe of Yathrib accepted Islam during the pilgrimage season (11th year of prophethood)

620

During the pilgrimage season Muhammad preached Islam to six men of the Khazraj tribe who had come from Yathrib; they accepted it. One of them, As'ad ibn Zurara, promised to meet again at Aqaba a year later.
