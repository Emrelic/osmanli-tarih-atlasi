When the Khwarazmshah Ala al-Din Muhammad took Ghazna, the Kabul region also passed from the Ghurids to the Khwarazmshahs

1215

After annexing Sighnaq to his lands in 1215, Ala al-Din Muhammad took Ghazna and gave it to his son Jalal al-Din; the Ghurid dynasty came to an end. The Kabul region, as a dependency of Ghazna, followed the same change of hands.
