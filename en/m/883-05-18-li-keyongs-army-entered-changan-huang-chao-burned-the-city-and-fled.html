Li Keyong's army entered Chang'an; Huang Chao burned the city and fled

18 May 883

Huang Chao's forces in Chang'an dispersed after he was defeated three times in a single day at Weinan; Li Keyong and the allied armies entered the city through the Guangtai Gate, and Huang Chao set fire to the palaces and fled.
