Montenegro evacuated Shkodër; the international corps of the great powers entered the city

14 May 1913

Under pressure from the great powers, Montenegro evacuated Shkodër, which it had taken on 23 April 1913, on 14 May 1913; an international corps entered the city and it became part of the founding administration of Albania.
