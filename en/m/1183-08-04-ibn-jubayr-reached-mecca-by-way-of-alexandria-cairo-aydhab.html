Ibn Jubayr reached Mecca by way of Alexandria-Cairo-Aydhab

4 August 1183

He observed the period of Saladin the Ayyubid in Alexandria (26 March), in Cairo and in Upper Egypt; from Aydhab he crossed the Red Sea to Jeddah and reached Mecca on 4 August 1183.
