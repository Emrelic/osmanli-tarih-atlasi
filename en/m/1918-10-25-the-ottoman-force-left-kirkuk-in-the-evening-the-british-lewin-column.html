The Ottoman force left Kirkuk in the evening, the British Lewin column entered

25 October 1918

On 25 October 1918 the Ottoman force at Kirkuk held its position until evening, then began to withdraw toward Altun Kopri; General Lewin's British column occupied Kirkuk and a line to its north after dark.
