The Taka expedition and the founding of the Kassala post

1840

During the 1840 Taka expedition of Ahmed Pasha Abu Widan, ruler of the Sudan, a military and administrative post was established; the town of Kassala grew up around it. The expedition failed to bring the Hadendoa of the region into full submission.
