Muş passed from Qaraqoyunlu rule to the administration of Timur (789/1387)

22 January 1387

Muş, which had passed to Qaraqoyunlu rule after the Mongol period, came under the administration of Timur, who held Qaraqoyunlu lands, in 789 (1387). In the atlas, Muş is recorded as passing from the Qaraqoyunlu to the Timurids in this year.
