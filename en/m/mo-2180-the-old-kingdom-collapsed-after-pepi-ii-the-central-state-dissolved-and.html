The Old Kingdom collapsed: after Pepi II the central state dissolved and the First Intermediate Period began

2180 BC

After the very long reign of Pepi II the Sixth Dynasty came to an end; the kingdom broke up into regions in which the nomarchs (provincial governors) were effectively independent. Tomb inscriptions (Asyut, Elephantine and others) show local governors dating by their own names and mobilizing their own troops. The short-lived Seventh and Eighth Dynasties at Memphis could not control the whole country.
