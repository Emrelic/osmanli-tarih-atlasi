An agreement between the bishop and the nobles gives rise to the Genoese commune's autonomy

1056

The agreement of 1056 between the bishop and the noble families of viscount origin, together with the Obertenghi margraves' relinquishing of their jurisdiction, gave birth to Genoa's urban autonomy; the trading associations (compagne) turned into communal government.
