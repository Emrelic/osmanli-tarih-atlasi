Sparta won the First Messenian War: Messenia was annexed to Sparta

719 BC

In the tradition transmitted by Pausanias the First Messenian War lasted twenty years, and with the fall of Ithome the Messenian plain passed to Sparta; its people were reduced to helot status. This conquest was regarded as the fundamental event that determined Sparta's agrarian power and military society.
