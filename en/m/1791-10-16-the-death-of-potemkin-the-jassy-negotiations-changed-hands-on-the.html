The death of Potemkin — the Jassy negotiations changed hands on the Russian side

16 October 1791

Potemkin, the Russian plenipotentiary, died on the road on 16 October 1791 while on his way to the Nikolaşet Monastery. In his place Count Bezborodko conducted the negotiations; the peace negotiations could begin in an orderly manner only after his arrival, in November.
