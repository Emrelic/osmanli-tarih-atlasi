The Silla king Gyeongsun entered Gaegyeong with his retinue; Taejo received him with ceremony and married him to his daughter Princess Nangnang

10 December 935

After applying to Goryeo in the 10th month of 935, the Silla king Kim Bu (Gyeongsun) left the capital in the 11th month and set out with a large retinue; all the districts along the way hosted him. On his entry into Gaegyeong, Taejo received him with ceremony, and he stayed at the Yuhwa Palace.
