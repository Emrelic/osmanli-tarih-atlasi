The County of Champagne was attached to the French crown through the marriage of Joan I to Philip IV: Troyes (together with Meaux)

1284

Through the marriage (1284) of Joan I, countess of Champagne and queen of Navarre, to Philip (IV), heir to France, Troyes, the capital of Champagne, was joined to the crown lands; for Meaux, Treccani says in the same year (1284) 'it passed to the king of France'. The official annexation of the county is in 1314 with the accession of Louis X (the end date in the atlas's state entry).
