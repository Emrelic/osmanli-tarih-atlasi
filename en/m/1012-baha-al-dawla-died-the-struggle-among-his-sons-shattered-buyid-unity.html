Baha al-Dawla died; the struggle among his sons shattered Buyid unity

1012

With the death of Baha al-Dawla in 1012, a long struggle for supremacy began among his sons, and the unity founded by the Buyids was shattered; the conflict between the Daylamites and the Turks in Iraq grew.
