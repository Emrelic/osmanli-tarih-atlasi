Naval Battle of Diu: Portuguese supremacy in the Indian Ocean

3 February 1509

The Mamluk-backed fleet of Malik Ayaz, who governed the region on behalf of the Gujarat Sultanate, suffered a heavy defeat off Diu against the fleet of the Portuguese governor-general Francisco de Almeida, who was trying to establish supply bases on these coasts. This defeat gave Portugal lasting naval supremacy in the Indian Ocean and broke Mamluk-Gujarati naval power; it is regarded as the beginning of the imbalance of power that forms the background of the Red Sea-Indian Ocean struggle which the Ottomans took over after 1517 (the defense of Jeddah, the 1538 siege of Diu).
