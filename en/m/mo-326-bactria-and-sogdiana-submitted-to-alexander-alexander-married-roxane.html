Bactria and Sogdiana submitted to Alexander; Alexander married Roxane

326 BC

Between 329 and 327 the region beyond the Oxus was taken after fierce guerrilla warfare; Spitamenes was killed, and the Sogdian Rock and the fortress of Chorienes surrendered. By marrying the Bactrian princess Roxane, Alexander bound the local nobles to his rule.
