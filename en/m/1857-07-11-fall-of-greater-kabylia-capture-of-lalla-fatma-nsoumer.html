Fall of Greater Kabylia: capture of Lalla Fatma N'Soumer

11 July 1857

The TDV's Algeria entry records that with the occupation of the mountainous Kabylia (Bilad al-Qaba'il) region at the end of the campaigns of 1853, 1854 and 1857, the French operation of occupying Algeria was largely completed. The 1857 campaign broke the Tizi Ouzou and Akbou line; Lalla Fatma N'Soumer, the symbol of the resistance, was taken captive. The last great center of resistance that had remained standing after Emir Abd al-Qadir's surrender in 1847 was thus extinguished.
