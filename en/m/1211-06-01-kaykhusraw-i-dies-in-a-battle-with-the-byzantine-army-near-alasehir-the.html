Kaykhusraw I dies in a battle with the Byzantine army near Alaşehir; the Byzantine army also suffers heavy losses

1 June 1211

Kaykhusraw died in a battle he fought with the Byzantine army near Alaşehir; the Byzantine army also suffered very heavy losses. His body was first buried at Alaşehir, then taken to Konya and interred in the Tomb of the Sultans beside the Alaeddin Mosque (end of June 1211).
