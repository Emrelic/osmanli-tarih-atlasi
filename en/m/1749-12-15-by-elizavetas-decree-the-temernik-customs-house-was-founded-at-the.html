By Elizaveta's decree the Temernik customs house was founded at the mouth of the Don (the nucleus of Rostov)

15 December 1749

Empress Elizaveta Petrovna issued the decree establishing a customs house on the Don at the mouth of the Temernik River; the Temernik customs house is the beginning of the early history of the city of Rostov-on-Don.
