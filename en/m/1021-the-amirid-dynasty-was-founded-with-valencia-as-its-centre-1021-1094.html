The Amirid dynasty was founded with Valencia as its centre (1021-1094)

1021

Abd al-Aziz b. Abd al-Rahman, a grandson of the hajib al-Mansur, founded in 1021 the Amirid taifa emirate whose centre was Valencia; the dynasty lasted, with some interruptions, until 1094.
