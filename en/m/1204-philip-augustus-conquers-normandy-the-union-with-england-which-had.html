Philip Augustus conquers Normandy: the union with England, which had lasted since 1106, ends; Maine, Touraine, Anjou and Poitou pass to France in 1204-1205

1204

After John Lackland had Arthur killed, Philip Augustus took Normandy in 1203-1204 (siege of Château-Gaillard September 1203-April 1204, Rouen on 24 June 1204). The conquest of Maine, Touraine, Anjou and Poitou in 1204 and 1205 was as easy as 'almost a walkover'; only the castles of Loches and Chinon resisted for another year.
