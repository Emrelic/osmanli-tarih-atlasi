Artaxerxes III died; Artaxerxes IV (Arses) ascended the throne

337 BC

Artaxerxes III died in the summer of 338 of natural causes (despite Diodorus's story of poisoning); Arses came to the throne. The revolts of Egypt and Babylon and the preparations of King Philip of Macedonia for Anatolia coincide with the same period.
