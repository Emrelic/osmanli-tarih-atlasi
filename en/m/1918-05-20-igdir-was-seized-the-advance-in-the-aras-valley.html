Iğdır was seized — the advance in the Aras valley

20 May 1918

Within the framework of the forward operation that began in February 1918, Turkish units defeated Armenian units at Serdarabad north of the Aras and seized Iğdır on 20 May 1918. Iğdır was not part of the Elviye-i Selâse but the Surmali district of the Erivan governorate; the first Ottoman administration in the region in about 180 years was established.
