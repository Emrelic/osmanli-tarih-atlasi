Abu Ibrahim Isma'il ibn Nuh escaped from prison, retook Bukhara and ascended the Samanid throne with the title 'al-Muntasir'

1000

The Samanid prince Isma'il ibn Nuh, whom the Karakhanids had exiled to Uzgand, escaped from prison the following year and retook Bukhara with the help of the hajib Arslan Balu and the pro-Samanid populace. His struggles against the Karakhanids in Transoxiana and against the Ghaznavids in Khurasan were inconclusive.
