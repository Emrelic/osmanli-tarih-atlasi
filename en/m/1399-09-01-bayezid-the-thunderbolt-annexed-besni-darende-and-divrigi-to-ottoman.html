Bayezid the Thunderbolt annexed Besni, Darende and Divriği to Ottoman lands after the death of Barquq

1 September 1399

After the death of the Mamluk Sultan Barquq (June 1399), when the administrative affairs of the Mamluk State fell into disorder, Bayezid the Thunderbolt descended on the Euphrates basin and annexed Besni (Behisni), Darende and Divriği, together with Malatya, to Ottoman lands. Sivas had been left to the Ottomans before this, after the death of Qadi Burhan al-Din in the summer of 1398. Divriği had been under Mamluk rule since 1391 and was a forward outpost attached to the province of Aleppo. Ottoman administration did not last long here: because of the approaching danger from Timur, Divriği was given back to the Mamluks in 1401, and the city's definitive entry under Ottoman administration came after Marj Dabiq in 1516.
