End of the Eretna Beylik and founding of the State of Kadı Burhâneddin

28 March 1381

The Eretnids, who arose in 1335 from an Ilkhanid governorship and ruled Central Anatolia, centred on Sivas and Kayseri, for forty-six years, dissolved through court rivalries. After eliminating his rivals Kılıcarslan and Hacı Şadgeldi, Kadı Burhâneddin, who had become vizier in May 1378, proclaimed his sultanate in the autumn of 1381, ended the beylik and founded the state known by his name. Thus the Ottomans' eastern neighbour changed hands; the Sivas-Kayseri line would remain in Kadı Burhâneddin's hands for the next seventeen years.
