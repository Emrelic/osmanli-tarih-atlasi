Truce of Deulino — Smolensk, Chernigov and Novgorod-Seversk ceded to Poland

11 December 1618

The Truce of Deulino, which ended the Polish-Russian War of 1609-1618, was signed on 11 December 1618 and secured a 14.5-year ceasefire; Russia was compelled to cede to the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth Smolensk (excluding Vyazma) and 29 towns in the Chernigov and Novgorod-Seversk regions — this was the heaviest territorial loss of the Time of Troubles (Smuta). The region would remain in Poland until the Truce of Andrusovo in 1667.
