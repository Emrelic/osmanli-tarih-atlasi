Selim the Grim's arrival in Jerusalem

29 December 1516

To Jerusalem, which had passed to Ottoman administration without resistance after Marj Dabiq, Selim the Grim came after the battle of Gaza with the dignitaries of the state and the army. The sultan visited the Dome of the Rock and the Aqsa Mosque and confirmed the holy status of the city. Jerusalem would remain under Ottoman administration from this date until December 1917, a full four centuries.
