Charax became independent: Hyspaosines became king of Mesene (Characene)

140 BC

After the Parthian invasion of Mesopotamia (141 BC), Charax became independent; Hyspaosines gave the city the name Charax Spasinou, meaning «fortress of Hyspaosines», and became the first king of the small state called Characene (Maishan, Mesene).
