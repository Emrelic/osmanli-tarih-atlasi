The last king, Tarquinius Superbus, was expelled; the monarchy ended and the Republic began

508 BC

According to Livy, Tarquinius Superbus and his family were expelled from the city in 509 BCE and the first consuls (Brutus, Collatinus) were elected. Polybius ties the first Roman–Carthaginian treaty to the year of the first consuls; although the date is considered "tradition" in modern historiography, it falls at the end of the 6th century BCE.
