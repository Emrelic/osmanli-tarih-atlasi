Philip subjugated Thrace together with the Odrysian dynasty

340 BC

In his campaigns of 342-341 BC Philip advanced into the interior of Thrace, made Cersobleptes, king of the Odrysians, tributary, and founded cities such as Philippopolis (Plovdiv). Thrace became the eastern extension of Macedonia.
