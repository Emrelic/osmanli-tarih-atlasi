Isa b. Yazid al-Jaludi eliminated Hamdawayh (205/820): Sana'a returned to the administration of the Abbasid governors

17 June 820

Hamdawayh b. Mahan, who had declared independence, was eliminated by Isa b. Yazid al-Jaludi (205/820); Sana'a and its surroundings were governed by Abbasid governors sent from Baghdad until the year 232 (847). With this date the gap at Sana'a in the atlas (815-820) ends and the Abbasid Caliphate period begins.
