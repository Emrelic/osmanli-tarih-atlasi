French forces took the interior of Ubangi-Shari (the Bambari-Bria line)

1903

The northern campaigns of the French Congo in the early 1900s brought under effective control the interior of Ubangi-Shari, including Bambari, Batangafo, Bossangoa and Bria; the region was formally organized as the colony of Ubangi-Shari on 29 December 1903 (part of French Equatorial Africa).
