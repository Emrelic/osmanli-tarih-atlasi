Ubaid culture (southern Mesopotamia): the Ubaid period ends (Carter & Philip 2010; Egberts et al. 2023 give its end as 4200 BCE)

3799 BC

The painted-pottery culture of crudely sedentary, temple-bearing villages in southern Mesopotamia spanning Ubaid 0 (Oueili) to Ubaid 5 (Terminal); it is followed by the Uruk period.
