The Conquest of the Fortress of Eger

12 October 1596

After a struggle of three weeks, the strong fortress of Eger, which the Ottomans had never been able to take before, fell with the surrender of its garrison. This conquest, at which the sultan was personally present, repaired to some extent the morale damaged by the loss of Esztergom, and the province of Eger was established. Together with the victory of Mezőkeresztes won two weeks later, 1596 became the most successful Ottoman year of the Long War.
