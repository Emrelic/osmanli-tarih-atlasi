Gustav Vasa was elected king at Strängnäs: the Kingdom of Sweden left the Kalmar Union

6 June 1523

After the Stockholm Bloodbath (1520), Gustav Eriksson Vasa, who rose against the Danish king Christian II, was elected king on 6 June 1523 by the Swedish Riksdag that assembled at Strängnäs in May 1523. Sweden and Finland, which was attached to it, thereby left for good the Kalmar Union that had lasted since 1397, and the Vasa period of the independent Kingdom of Sweden began.
