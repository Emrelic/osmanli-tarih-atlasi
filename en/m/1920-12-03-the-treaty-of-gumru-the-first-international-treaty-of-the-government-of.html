The Treaty of Gümrü: The First International Treaty of the Government of the Grand National Assembly

3 December 1920

After the army of the Eastern front under Kâzım Karabekir retook Sarıkamış and Kars, Armenia asked for peace, and by the treaty signed at Gümrü it accepted that it did not recognise Sèvres and renounced its territorial claims in the east. This first treaty signed by the Ankara government was the first international document effectively rejecting Sèvres, and it opened to the Grand National Assembly (TBMM) the road to legal recognition. The eastern frontier was thus secured, and shifting forces to the Western front became possible.
