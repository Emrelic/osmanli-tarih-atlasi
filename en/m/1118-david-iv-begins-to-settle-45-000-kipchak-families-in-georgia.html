David IV begins to settle 45,000 Kipchak families in Georgia

1118

David agreed with 45,000 Kipchak families from the North Caucasus and the southern Russian steppes and brought them to Georgia from 1118, and with their help ended Seljuk rule at Tbilisi in 1121.
