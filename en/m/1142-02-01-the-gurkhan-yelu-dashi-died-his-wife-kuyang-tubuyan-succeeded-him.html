The Gurkhan Yelu Dashi died; his wife Kuyang (Tubuyan) succeeded him

1 February 1142

The first Qara Khitai ruler, the Gurkhan Yelu Dashi (1130-1142), died in February 1142 (or 1143); he was succeeded in turn by his wife Tubuyan (Kuyang), his son Yelu Yilie, his sister Yelu Shi, or the regent Pusuwan. With the victory of Qatwan (1141), all Transoxiana and Khwarazm had been made tributary.
