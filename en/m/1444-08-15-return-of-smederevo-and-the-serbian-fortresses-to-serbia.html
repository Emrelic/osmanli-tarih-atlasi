Return of Smederevo and the Serbian fortresses to Serbia

15 August 1444

The provision of the Treaty of Edirne-Szeged was put into effect: on 15 August 1444 Murad II gave Smederevo back to Đurađ Branković together with its twenty-four important fortresses and the other Serbian lands under occupation (TDV semendire); Niš (TDV nis), Alacahisar (Kruševac) (TDV alacahisar), Şehirköy (Pirot) (TDV sehirkoy) and Priština (TDV pristine) were also left to the Serbs. Vidin, not being Serbian land, did not enter the return — this is the source of the northwestern loss of territory seen on the map around the victory of Varna; a few months later, with the rout of Varna, the treaty would in any case become void.
