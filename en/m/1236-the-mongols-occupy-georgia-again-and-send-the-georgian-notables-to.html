The Mongols occupy Georgia again and send the Georgian notables to Mongolia

1236

When the Mongols occupied Georgia again in 633/1235-36, they sent the Georgian notables to Mongolia; these remained there for years. The Mongols used large numbers of Georgian soldiers in their campaigns.
