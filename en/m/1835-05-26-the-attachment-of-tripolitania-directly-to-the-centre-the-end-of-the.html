The Attachment of Tripolitania Directly to the Centre — The End of the Karamanli Dynasty

26 May 1835

Following the struggles for the throne within the Karamanlı family and the European pressure that increased after France's occupation of Algiers in 1830, the Sublime Porte intervened directly in Tripolitania. The fleet sent from Istanbul entered the city, the last Karamanlı bey was dismissed and brought to Istanbul, and the province was handed over to a governor appointed from the centre. As the dynastic period of 124 years ended, the last piece of land in North Africa directly administered by the Ottoman State was consolidated.
