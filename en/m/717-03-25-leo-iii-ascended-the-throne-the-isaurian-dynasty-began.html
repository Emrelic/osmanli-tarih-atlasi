Leo III ascended the throne: the Isaurian dynasty began

25 March 717

After the period of anarchy of 685-717, the strategos Leo, of Isaurian origin, became emperor on 25 March 717. Immediately afterwards Constantinople had to prepare for the great Arab siege.
