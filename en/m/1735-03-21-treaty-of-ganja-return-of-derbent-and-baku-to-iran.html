Treaty of Ganja: return of Derbent and Baku to Iran

21 March 1735

Russia, seeking an alliance with Iran against the Ottoman threat, returned the Caspian coastal cities it had held for thirteen years to Iran, under the command of Nadir Khan (who had not yet taken the title of shah), by the Treaty of Ganja. According to the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi entry on Derbent, this withdrawal was a result of Russia's not wanting to struggle on two fronts (Ottoman and Iranian) in the south, and it strengthened the military prestige that paved the way for Nadir's accession to the throne a few years later. Tarki (Tarku), under Russian administration, also returned to the Iranian side with this withdrawal (the Ganja day is used on the map). Salyan and Shabran, under Russian administration, likewise return to the Iranian side with this withdrawal (no settlement-specific day in the source, the Ganja day is used on the map — approximate information).
