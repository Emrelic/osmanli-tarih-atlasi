The Armistice of Edirne: The Silencing of the Guns in the '93 War

31 January 1878

With the successive fall of Sofia, Philippopolis and Edirne and the Russian army's pressing up to the shores of the Marmara, the Sublime Porte was obliged to ask for an armistice. The ceasefire signed at Edirne made the Ottomans accept preliminary peace terms such as autonomy for Bulgaria, the independence of Montenegro, Serbia and Romania, and a heavy war indemnity. These terms would be turned a month later into a treaty text at San Stefano.
