After the Kiev uprising Volodymyr Monomakh ascends the throne of Kiev

1113

On the death of Sviatopolk II and the Kiev uprising of 1113, Monomakh, prince of Pereyaslavl, became grand prince of Kiev; he carried out legal and economic reforms and tried to secure unity against the Cumans.
