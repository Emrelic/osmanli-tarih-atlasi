Rasulid rule was recognised in Mecca — Nūr al-Dīn ʿUmar fell victim to an assassination

1250

When the Rasulid Sultan of Yemen Nūr al-Dīn ʿUmar was killed in Janad (February 1250), the rule of his state was recognised between Mecca and Hadramawt; in the year the Ayyubid sultanate ended, Mecca appears under Rasulid suzerainty.
