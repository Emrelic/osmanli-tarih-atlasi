Julius Nepos was murdered in Dalmatia: the last legitimate emperor of the Western Roman Empire

480

Nepos, who had withdrawn to Dalmatia after the overthrow of Romulus, was murdered in 480; after his death no one remained to claim the Western Roman throne, and Odoacer continued his de facto power in Italy in the name of Zeno.
