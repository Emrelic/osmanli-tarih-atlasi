Nasir al-Din Mahmud was proclaimed sultan of Delhi; Balban became deputy of the realm

10 June 1246

Mahmud Nasir al-Din, who came secretly to Delhi from Bahraich after Ala al-Din Mas'ud Shah was killed, was proclaimed sultan on 23 Muharram 644 / 10 June 1246; Qutlugh Khan and Malik Baha al-Din Balban seized power in the palace, and Balban became deputy of the realm (na'ib-i mamlakat) with the title Ulugh Khan.
