First armed uprising in the Morea

2 April 1821

After the crossing of the Prut, the revolt spread to the Morea: the armed action that began at Kalavryta (Julian 21 March) was followed by movements in Patras, Kalamata and the Mani; the Morea soon turned into a general uprising and Ottoman administration lost most of the peninsula. Other settlements lost on the same date: Athens, Patras (Balyabadra), Thebes, Livadeia, Eleusis (Attica), Missolonghi.
