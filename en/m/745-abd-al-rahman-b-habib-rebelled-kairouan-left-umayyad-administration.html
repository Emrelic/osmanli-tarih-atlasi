ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Ḥabīb rebelled: Kairouan left Umayyad administration

745

With the rebellion of ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. Ḥabīb in 745, Kairouan left the Umayyad governorship; a Habibid/Kharijite interim until 761 (its owner is uncertain; the map shows this period as blank).
