The capture of the fortress of Krupa by the Ottomans

1565

The fortress of Krupa on the right bank of the Una fell into Ottoman hands in 1565. In the following two centuries the fortress remained Ottoman against Austrian sieges (1581, 1690, 1692, 1716) and was part of the Una frontier line between Bihać and Novi.
