Michael VIII Palaiologos was proclaimed emperor at Nicaea

1259

Michael Palaiologos, regent of the young John IV Laskaris in the Empire of Nicaea, was proclaimed co-emperor on 1 January 1259; the Palaiologan dynasty (1259-1453) thus began.
