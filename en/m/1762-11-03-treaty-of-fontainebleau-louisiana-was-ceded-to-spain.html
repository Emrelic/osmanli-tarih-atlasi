Treaty of Fontainebleau — Louisiana was ceded to Spain

3 November 1762

Toward the end of the Seven Years' War, France ceded Louisiana west of the Mississippi, and New Orleans, to Spain by a secret treaty. Thus France's presence in North America in effect came to an end: the next year the Treaty of Paris would give the east of the river to Britain.
