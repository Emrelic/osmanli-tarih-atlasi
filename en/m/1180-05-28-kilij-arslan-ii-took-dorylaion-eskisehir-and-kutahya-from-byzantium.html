Kilij Arslan II took Dorylaion (Eskişehir) and Kütahya from Byzantium

28 May 1180

After Myriokephalon (1176), Byzantium's fortresses of Dorylaion and Kütahya in the Anatolian interior passed into the hands of Kilij Arslan II in 576/1180, in the last years of Manuel or after his death.
