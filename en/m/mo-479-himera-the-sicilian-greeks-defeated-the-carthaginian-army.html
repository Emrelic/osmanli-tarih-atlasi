Himera: the Sicilian Greeks defeated the Carthaginian army

479 BC

The alliance of Gelon and Theron routed Hamilcar's Carthaginian army before Himera; Carthage was unable to launch a major expedition against Sicily again for half a century. According to Herodotus and Diodorus, Salamis was won on the same day (tradition).
