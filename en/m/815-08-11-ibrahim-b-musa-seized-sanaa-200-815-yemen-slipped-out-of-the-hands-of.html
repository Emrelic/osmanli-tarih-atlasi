Ibrahim b. Musa seized Sana'a (200/815): Yemen slipped out of the hands of the Abbasid governors

11 August 815

The Alid rebel Ibrahim b. Musa defeated the troops sent by al-Ma'mun and seized Sana'a (200/815); until Hamdawayh b. Mahan defeated him and declared independence (203/818), the city passed out of the actual administration of the Abbasid Caliphate.
