Creation of the esham system

1 June 1775

In the face of the burden of an indemnity of 7.5 million kuruş brought by the Treaty of Küçük Kaynarca, the esham system was created in 1775. In this method, designed to overcome the limits of the malikane, the revenue of a mukataa was divided into shares, and, unlike the malikane, women, non-Muslims and ordinary people could also be shareholders.
