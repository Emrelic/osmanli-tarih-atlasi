Mahmud of Ghazna marched on Sistan; Khalaf agreed to pay 100,000 dinars and to have the khutba read in Mahmud's name

1000

After Khalaf's son Tahir killed Mahmud's uncle Bughrajuk, Sultan Mahmud marched on Sistan; peace was made when Khalaf agreed to pay 100,000 dinars and to have the khutba read in Mahmud's name (390/1000).
