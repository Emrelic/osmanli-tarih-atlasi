The people of Kiev are baptised en masse in the Dnieper; the Christianisation of Rus'

1 August 988

In November 987 Vladimir and his entourage were baptised (in Kiev or at Chersonesos), after which the idols were pulled down; the mass baptism of the people of Kiev took place on 1 August 988. This conversion, given in return for marriage to Anna, sister of the Byzantine emperor Basil II, bound Rus' to the Byzantine world.
