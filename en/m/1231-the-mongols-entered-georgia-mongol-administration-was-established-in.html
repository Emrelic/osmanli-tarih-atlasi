The Mongols entered Georgia; Mongol administration was established in Tiflis

1231

The Mongols entered Georgia in 1231; Queen Rusudan asked Pope Gregory IX for help and, obtaining no result, was forced to leave Tiflis. Mongol administration was established in Tiflis in 1231.
