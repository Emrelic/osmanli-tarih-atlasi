The loss of Herceg Novi to Venice: the mouth of the Bay of Kotor

30 September 1687

During the Morean War, the Venetian fleet and its allies besieged and took the fortress of Herceg Novi at the mouth of the Bay of Kotor in 1687; the town remained with Venice until the Venetian Republic fell in 1797.
