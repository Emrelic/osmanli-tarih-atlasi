Nicomedes I founded Nicomedia (İzmit)

263 BC

After the destruction of Astacus, Nicomedes founded Nicomedia at the eastern end of the gulf; in the following centuries the city became the centre of Bithynia and, in the time of Diocletian, the Roman eastern capital.
