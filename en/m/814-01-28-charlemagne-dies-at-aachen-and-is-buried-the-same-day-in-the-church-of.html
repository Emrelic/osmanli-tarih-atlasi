Charlemagne dies at Aachen and is buried the same day in the Church of the Virgin Mary

28 January 814

Charlemagne spent most of his last years at Aachen (Aix). He fell feverish at the beginning of 814, and as a result of pleurisy he died on 28 January 814 after receiving communion, and was buried the same day in the Church of the Virgin Mary at Aachen; his son Louis the Pious succeeded him.
