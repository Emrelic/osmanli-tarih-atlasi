The Greek occupation of Bursa

8 July 1920

The northern column of the summer offensive turned toward the Marmara after Balıkesir. According to the TDV articles on Bursa and the National Struggle, Bursa came under Greek occupation on 8 July 1920. The desecrations of the tombs of the first Ottoman rulers provoked a great reaction throughout the country.
