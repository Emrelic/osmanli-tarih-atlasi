Sökmen II (Shah-i Arman), Shah of Ahlat, dies during the siege of Mosul

10 July 1185

When Saladin besieged Mosul again in 1185, news came that his ally Sökmen had died (9 Rabi' al-akhir 581 / 10 July 1185). Saladin did not return empty-handed from the Ahlat campaign and took Mayyafariqin, which had been left without a ruler.
