Bahram Shah died; Khusraw Shah abandoned Ghazna to the Ghurids and withdrew to Lahore

13 February 1157

When Bahram Shah died in 552 (1157), his son Khusraw Shah succeeded him; Sanjar's capture by the Oghuz and the Ghaznavids' being deprived of Sanjar's aid allowed the Ghurids to expand their spheres of domination rapidly; Khusraw Shah abandoned Ghazna and settled in Lahore, and the Ghaznavids continued to rule in Indian territories.
