The rout at Tuz Khurmatu — Ottoman troops withdrew to Kirkuk

1 April 1918

Toward the end of the First World War, the Ottoman troops, defeated by British forces at Tuz Khurmatu in April 1918, withdrew to Kirkuk; the town came under British occupation.
