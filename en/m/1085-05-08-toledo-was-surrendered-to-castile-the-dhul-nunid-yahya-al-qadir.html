Toledo was surrendered to Castile; the Dhu'l-Nunid Yahya al-Qadir withdrew to Valencia

8 May 1085

At the end of a siege lasting about nine months, Toledo was surrendered peacefully to the Kingdom of Castile of Alfonso VI (8 May 1085); the Dhu'l-Nunid ruler Yahya al-Qadir went to Valencia with his family and retinue and reigned there. On the map, from this day Valencia is shown as the Muluk al-Tawa'if (Andalusian taifa emirates) instead of the Dhu'l-Nunids (Taifa of Toledo).
