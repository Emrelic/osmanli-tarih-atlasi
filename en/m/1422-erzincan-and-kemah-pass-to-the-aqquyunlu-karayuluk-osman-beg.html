Erzincan and Kemah pass to the Aqquyunlu: Karayülük Osman Beg

1422

After the death of Qara Yusuf, Yar Ali, grandson of Mutahharten, had become governor of the city at the wish of the people of Erzincan. In his time the Aqquyunlu ruler Karayülük Osman took Erzincan and added it to the Aqquyunlu lands. After this date Erzincan became a place that changed hands now between the Aqquyunlu and the Qaraquyunlu, now among the Aqquyunlu princes.
