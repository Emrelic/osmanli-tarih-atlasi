The Kuleli Incident: The First Coup Attempt against Abdülmecid in Istanbul

14 September 1859

A secret society of mixed military and civilian membership, reacting against the Tanzimat policy and the equality that the Reform Edict granted to non-Muslims, was raided in a meeting at the Kılıç Ali Pasha Mosque while it was planning to depose the sultan. The event was named after the Kuleli Barracks, where the accused were tried; the death sentences were commuted to exile. It is accepted as the first palace coup attempt in Ottoman history undertaken by an organisation founded on ideological grounds.
