Arakan King Basawpyu took Chittagong; the city remained mostly in Arakanese hands until 1666

1459

Basawpyu (Kalima Shah, 1459-82), successor of Narameikhla, occupied Chittagong in 1459. According to Harvey, the city was mostly in Arakanese hands until 1666; when Bengal was strong Arakan paid tribute to Bengal, and when Arakan was strong it took tribute from the Ganges delta.
