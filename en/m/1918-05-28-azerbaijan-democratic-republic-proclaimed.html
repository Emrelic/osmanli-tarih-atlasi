Azerbaijan Democratic Republic proclaimed

28 May 1918

Two days after Georgia's secession, the Azerbaijan Democratic Republic was proclaimed; the first center was Ganja. On the same day the Republic of Armenia was also founded in Erivan, and Transcaucasia was divided into three separate states. With this proclamation Shamakhi, Qabala, Ereş, Shabran, Salyan, Quba, Shaki and Barda passed to the Azerbaijan Democratic Republic.
