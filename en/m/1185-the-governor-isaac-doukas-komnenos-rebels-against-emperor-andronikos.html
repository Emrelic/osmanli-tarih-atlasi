The governor Isaac Doukas Komnenos rebels against Emperor Andronikos and declares Cyprus independent

1185

While Cyprus continued as a province of Byzantium, the governor Isaac Doukas Komnenos rebelled against Emperor Andronikos Komnenos and declared independence, and Cyprus passed out of Byzantine rule in 1185.
