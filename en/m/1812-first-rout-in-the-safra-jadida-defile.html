First rout in the Safra-Jadida defile

1812

The Egyptian vanguard, ambushed in narrow mountain passes, was scattered. The defeat became the tactical lesson of the campaign: it was understood that marching in the Hejaz was not possible without the support of the Bedouin tribes.
