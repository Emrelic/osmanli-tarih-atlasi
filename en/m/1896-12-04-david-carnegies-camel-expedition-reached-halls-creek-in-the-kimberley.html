David Carnegie's camel expedition reached Halls Creek in the Kimberley; he met the first white person since 21 July

4 December 1896

David Carnegie's camel expedition, which had set out from Coolgardie on 9 July 1896, reached Halls Creek on 4 December. Carnegie writes that the first person they met was Sergeant Brophy of the police, and that they had not seen a white face since 21 July; the journey, counting deviations, had covered 1,413 miles.
