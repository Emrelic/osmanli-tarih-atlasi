The conquest of Ganja — the end of the Caucasus campaign

4 September 1725

Ganja, the final objective of the Caucasus operation, was taken over as the centre of Karabakh between the Kura and the Aras. With the completion of the line of Shirvan, Revan, Nakhchivan and Ganja, the Ottoman State reached in the Caucasus the extent it had had in 1590. However, this gain proved short-lived: the rise of Nadir Shah would retake the entire region within ten years.
