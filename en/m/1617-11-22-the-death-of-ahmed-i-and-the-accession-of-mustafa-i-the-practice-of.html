The Death of Ahmed I and the Accession of Mustafa I — The Practice of Ekberiyet

22 November 1617

After a stomach illness lasting fifty-one days, the twenty-eight-year-old Ahmed I died, and his brother Mustafa I was placed on the throne in place of his son. Thus the practice of succession from father to son was abandoned, and a de facto transition was made to the system of 'ekberiyet', in which the eldest male member of the dynasty takes the throne. This first reign, lasting ninety-six days, is also the beginning of the era in which princes were raised in the 'kafes'.
