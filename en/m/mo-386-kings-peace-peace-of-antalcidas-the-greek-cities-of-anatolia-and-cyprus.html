King's Peace (Peace of Antalcidas): the Greek cities of Anatolia and Cyprus were left to Persia

386 BC

By the peace dictated by the Persian king Artaxerxes II, the Greek cities in Asia and Cyprus were reckoned as belonging to Persia and the Greek cities were granted autonomy. Sparta enforced the peace as Persia's spokesman.
