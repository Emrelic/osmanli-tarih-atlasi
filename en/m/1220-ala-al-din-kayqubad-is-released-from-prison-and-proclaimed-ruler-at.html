Ala al-Din Kayqubad is released from prison and proclaimed ruler at Sivas

1220

On the death of Izz al-Din Kaykawus I, Kayqubad, released from prison, was proclaimed sultan at Sivas (616/1220). Against the Mongol invasion he had Konya, Sivas and Kayseri surrounded with walls.
