Nasr II took Termez on the death of Alp Arslan, then marched on Balkh and captured that as well

15 December 1072

On the death of Alp Arslan during his Transoxiana campaign, the Western Qarakhanid Shams al-Mulk Nasr II took Termez in Rabi II 465 (December 1072), then marched on Balkh and captured that too; the Seljuk–Qarakhanid struggle continued in the time of Malikshah.
