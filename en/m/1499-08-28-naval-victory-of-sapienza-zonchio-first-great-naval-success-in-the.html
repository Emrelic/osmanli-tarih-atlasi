Naval Victory of Sapienza (Zonchio) — first great naval success in the Mediterranean

28 August 1499

Bayezid II, who found his hands free with the end of the Cem question, declared war on Venice in 1499; the Ottoman fleet defeated the Venetian fleet off Sapienza (Zonchio) and won its first great naval victory in the Mediterranean. With this victory the navy announced that it could now stand against Venice at sea.
