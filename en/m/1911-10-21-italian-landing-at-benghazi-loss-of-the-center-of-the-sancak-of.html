Italian landing at Benghazi: loss of the center of the sancak of Cyrenaica

21 October 1911

Benghazi, the center of Cyrenaica, was occupied on 21 October 1911 in the fourth and most important landing on the coast. Thus, three weeks after the declaration of war, Italy held all the major ports of the province of Tripolitania (Tripoli, Tobruk, Derna, Benghazi); but it could not enter the hinterland of any of them. The remaining year of the war was spent in inconclusive thrusts inland from these four points on the coast.
