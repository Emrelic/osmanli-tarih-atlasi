Chengdu: ownerless interval → Shu Han

221

At Chengdu, ownership passes from the ownerless interval to Shu Han. Transition source information: Sanguozhi 32 (Xianzhu zhuan): Liu Bei besieged Chengdu for tens of days and Liu Zhang surrendered (under the heading Jian'an 19 = 214); in Zhangwu 1 (221) he became emperor. End: Sanguozhi 28 (Deng Ai): Liu Shan sent the imperial seal and surrendered, Ai entered Chengdu, and Liu Shan came out with the prince and notables.
