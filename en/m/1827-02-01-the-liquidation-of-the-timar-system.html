The Liquidation of the Timar System

1 February 1827

One year after the abolition of the Janissary Corps, about 5,200 timar-holding sipahis in fifty-three sancaks in Rumelia and Anatolia were converted, together with their timar revenues, into cavalry of the Asâkir-i Mansûre. Thus the timar regime, the four-century backbone of the Ottoman order of land, taxation and army, effectively came to an end. The vacated timars were transferred to the treasury, and the way was opened to the transition to a centrally salaried army and a cash tax system.
