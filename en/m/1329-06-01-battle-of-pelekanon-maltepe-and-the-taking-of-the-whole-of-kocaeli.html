Battle of Pelekanon (Maltepe) and the taking of the whole of Kocaeli

1 June 1329

TDV, Orhan entry: on the first day of the battle Orhan Gazi sent a force of three hundred men to draw the Byzantine army toward himself; on the second day the battle grew as the units under Pazarlu's command descended onto the plain, and when the emperor was wounded by an arrow, panic broke out in the Byzantine army. The result is decisive: 'Orhan seized the whole of Kocaeli; after the victory the people of Nicaea had no hope left.' The coastal towns from Hereke to Üsküdar came under Ottoman rule, and Gebze was made the centre of the region. This was the last Byzantine imperial army that came to save Constantinople.
