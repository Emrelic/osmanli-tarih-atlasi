The Ilkhanids discover Mu'in al-Din Suleyman Parwana's treachery and put an end to his life

4 June 1277

After Baybars's departure from Anatolia, Abaqa Khan, who came with a great army, had 200,000 people massacred on the ground of cooperation with the Mamluks; realising Parwana's treachery too, they put an end to his life and began to administer the Anatolian Seljuk state with officials sent from Tabriz (Parwana's death 676/1277).
