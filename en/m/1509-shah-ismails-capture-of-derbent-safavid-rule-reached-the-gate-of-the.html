Shah Ismail's capture of Derbent — Safavid rule reached the gate of the Caucasus

1509

With the defeat and death of the Shirvanshah Farrukh Yassar in 1500, Baku and Shamakhi had fallen to the Safavids, while Derbent had remained separate for a time. Shah Ismail took the city in 1509 and settled one of the Rumlu tribes there; thus Safavid rule reached the northern gate of the Caucasus, this narrow pass between the Caspian and the mountains. The link became permanent when Shirvan came under direct Safavid administration in 1538. Derbent would pass into Ottoman hands in the campaign of 1578 and be the seat of a beylerbeylik of seven sancaks until 1607. The TDV's record is as follows: "The establishment and growth of the Safavid State marked the beginning of a new period in Derbent. Shah Ismail took the city in 1509 and settled one of the Rumlu tribes there. When Shirvan came under direct Safavid rule in 1538..."
