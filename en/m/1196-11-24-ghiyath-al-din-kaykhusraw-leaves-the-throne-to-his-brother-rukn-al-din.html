Ghiyath al-Din Kaykhusraw leaves the throne to his brother Rukn al-Din Suleyman Shah and goes into exile

24 November 1196

Kaykhusraw I, struggling with his brothers, was obliged to leave the throne to Suleyman Shah II (Rukn al-Din) (593/1196) and left Konya; Suleyman Shah took his brothers' lands and established the unity of the country.
