Reggio Calabria passed from the Angevins to Aragon-Sicily (Peter III landed in Calabria)

1283

After the Sicilian Vespers (1282) Reggio remained in Angevin hands; in early 1283 King Peter III of Aragon landed in Calabria and Reggio became the Aragonese base in the war against the Angevins (in July 1284 Charles of Anjou besieged the city but could not take it).
