Founding of the Province of Habesh — the Red Sea coast came under Ottoman administration

1557

The province, established to prevent Portugal's penetration into the Red Sea via Abyssinia, was formally founded on 15 Sha'ban 962 (5 July 1555) according to the TDV article on the Province of Habesh, and its sancak seat was Suakin. Özdemir Pasha began operations in the autumn of 1555; he took the Dahlak islands, which control the port of Massawa, and on 2 April 1557 took the city of Massawa and then Arkiko. The conquest continued with the province of Tigre coming under Ottoman rule in 1558 and Debarwa in 1559.
