Battle of the Yenişehir Plain — Çelebi Mehmed withdrew to Amasya

1405

Çelebi Mehmed, who had lost Bursa and Ankara, was defeated by Emir Süleyman on the plain of Yenişehir, lost all his gains in western Anatolia and withdrew to his base at Tokat-Amasya. After this defeat he avoided direct confrontation and turned to consolidating his authority in the interior of Anatolia. The longest quiet phase of the Interregnum began in this way.
