The signing of the Armistice of Giurgiu by Coburg and the exchange of texts

21 September 1790

The text of the armistice was signed also by the Austrian commander-in-chief Prince Coburg on 21 September 1790, and the copies were handed over reciprocally. Thus the armistice came into force for both sides.
