Battle of Issus: Alexander defeated Darius III; Darius abandoned his family and fled

1 November 332 BC

On a narrow coastal plain the great army of Darius III was defeated by Alexander; the Persian king abandoned his family and treasure and fled. The victory at Issus brought Syria and the Phoenician coast, and then Egypt, into Alexander's hands.
