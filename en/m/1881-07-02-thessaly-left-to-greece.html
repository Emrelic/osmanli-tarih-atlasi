Thessaly left to Greece

2 July 1881

In accordance with the decisions of the Congress of Berlin, Thessaly and the area around Arta were transferred to Greece. Other settlements lost on the same date: Arta, Larissa, Trikala, Lamia. With this transfer Volos, Kalambaka and Karditsa (Thessaly) passed to Greece.
