Xi'an (Chang'an): ownerless interval → Former Qin (前秦)

351

At Xi'an (Chang'an), ownership passes from the ownerless interval to Former Qin (前秦). Transition source information: Jin shu 112 (Fu Jian / Fu Jian zaiji): in Yonghe 7 Fu Jian proclaimed himself Heavenly King and set up a court in Chang'an (Du Hong's occupation of Chang'an ends).
