Shah Abbas retook Tiflis — the end of Ottoman administration in Kartli and Kakheti

1603

Shah Abbas I retook Tiflis from the Ottomans in 1603 and proclaimed Kartli a khanate; Safavid administration was also established in Kakheti. The Ottoman administration in eastern Georgia, which had begun with Lala Mustafa Pasha's campaign in 1578, thus came to an end; Tiflis would pass into Ottoman hands again only in 1723.
