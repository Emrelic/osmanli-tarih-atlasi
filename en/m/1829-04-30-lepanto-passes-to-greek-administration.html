Lepanto passes to Greek administration

30 April 1829

Lepanto, on the northern shore of the Gulf of Corinth, which had remained in Ottoman hands until the Greek revolt, was lost in April 1829 and came under Greek administration; the name of the town was changed to the ancient Nafpaktos. The Muslim and Jewish population of the city disappeared entirely and it became a small town.
