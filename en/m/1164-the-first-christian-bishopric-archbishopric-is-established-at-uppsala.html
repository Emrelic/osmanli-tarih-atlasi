The first Christian bishopric/archbishopric is established at Uppsala

1164

With the destruction of the temple at Uppsala and the establishment there of the first Christian bishopric (1164), Christianity triumphed in Sweden; the archbishopric of Uppsala, following the Danish archbishopric of Lund, is also dated to 1164.
