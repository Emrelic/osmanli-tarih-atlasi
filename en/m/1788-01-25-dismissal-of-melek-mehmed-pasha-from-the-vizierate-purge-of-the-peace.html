Dismissal of Melek Mehmed Pasha from the vizierate — purge of the peace supporters

25 January 1788

As the second front of the war was about to open, Melek Mehmed Pasha was dismissed from the vizierate on 25 January 1788. According to the TDV, the real reason for the dismissal was that the Grand Vizier Koca Yusuf Pasha wanted to remove the peace supporters from the administration of the state. At the end of the war, on 4 May 1792, Melek Mehmed Pasha would be appointed grand vizier in Koca Yusuf Pasha's place.
