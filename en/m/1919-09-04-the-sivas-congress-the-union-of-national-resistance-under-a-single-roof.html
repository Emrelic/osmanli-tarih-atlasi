The Sivas Congress: The Union of National Resistance under a Single Roof

4 September 1919

The congress, which began its work on 4 September 1919 at the Sivas Sultânî school, united the societies for the defence of rights from all parts of the country under the name of the Society for the Defence of the Rights of Anatolia and Rumelia (Anadolu ve Rumeli Müdafaa-i Hukuk Cemiyeti) and elected a Representative Committee (Heyet-i Temsîliyye). The proposal of an American mandate was rejected after long debates. With the declaration published on 11 September, the National Struggle became a single-centred, legitimate political movement, and an open struggle for power with the Istanbul government began.
