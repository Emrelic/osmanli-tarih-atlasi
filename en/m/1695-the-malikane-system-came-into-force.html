The malikâne system came into force

1695

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Mâlikâne, in the last quarter of the 17th century the increase in war expenses and the decline of revenues through territorial losses plunged the treasury into a heavy financial crisis. In the malikâne system, which came into force in 1106 (1695), tax farmers were granted a lifelong fixed right to taxes, and an incentive to protect production was introduced in place of short-term exploitation.
