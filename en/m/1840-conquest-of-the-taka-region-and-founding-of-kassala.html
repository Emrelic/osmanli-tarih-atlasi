Conquest of the Taka region and founding of Kassala

1840

The governor of the Sudan, Ahmed Pasha Abu Widan, brought the Taka region between the Atbara and Gash rivers under Egyptian administration in 1840 and founded the city of Kassala as a military headquarters. The city became the center of a mudiriyya controlling the caravan route between Sennar and Suakin and the Abyssinian border belt.
