The Latins were massacred in Constantinople; Andronikos Komnenos came to power

1182

With a popular uprising the regency of Manuel's widow ended, the Latin merchants were subjected to massacre, and Andronikos Komnenos rose to power (1182-1185). The massacre increased the West's hostility to Byzantium.
