The British settled in Artvin

17 December 1918

Because the Armistice of Mudros of 30 October 1918 stipulated the Ottoman army's withdrawal behind the 1914 frontier, Artvin was evacuated and was occupied by the British on 17 December 1918. According to the TDV article on Artvin, the British occupation lasted until April 1920.
