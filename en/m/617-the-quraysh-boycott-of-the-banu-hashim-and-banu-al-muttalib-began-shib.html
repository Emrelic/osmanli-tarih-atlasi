The Quraysh boycott of the Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib began (Shi'b Abi Talib, 617-620)

617

All of the Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib, except Abu Lahab and his sons, were forced to live for about three years in hardship in the quarter of Abu Talib; the TDV also relates the report that the boycott lasted a shorter time.
