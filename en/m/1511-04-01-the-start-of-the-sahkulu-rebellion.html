The start of the Şahkulu rebellion

1 April 1511

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Şahkulu Baba Tekeli, Şahkulu, nourished by Safavid propaganda, gathered a large Turkmen mass around him in the Teke region and began a great rebellion of a religious-social character. The rebellion, as one of the most serious internal security crises of the period, deeply shook the central authority of the state.
