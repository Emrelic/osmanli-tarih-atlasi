Treaty of Paris: Bavaria ceded South Tyrol (Trento) to Napoleon, the region was joined to the Kingdom of Italy

28 February 1810

The King of Bavaria ceded South Tyrol to Napoleon; the department of Alto Adige, centered on Trento, was formed and attached to the Kingdom of Italy (partition of 9 June 1810).
