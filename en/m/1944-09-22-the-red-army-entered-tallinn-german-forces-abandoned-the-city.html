The Red Army entered Tallinn — German forces abandoned the city

22 September 1944

During the Tallinn Offensive the Germans abandoned the city; the government of the Estonian National Committee, which had taken over the government buildings on 18 September, could not maintain its administration as the Red Army entered. Estonia came once more under Soviet rule.
