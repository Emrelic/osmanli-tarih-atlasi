Tancred, count of Lecce, is crowned king of Sicily at Palermo

18 January 1190

Tancred was elected king on 8 December 1189 and crowned on 18 January 1190 by Walter, archbishop of Palermo; the struggle with Henry VI of Hohenstaufen thus began.
