Evliya Çelebi recorded that the number of tile workshops in Iznik had fallen to nine

1648

Evliya Çelebi, who saw Iznik in 1648, wrote that the number of tile workshops had fallen to nine in the city, the empire's most important ceramic center; the city's tile industry must have begun to decline around this time. According to the same observation there were eighteen quarters and 600 shops within the walls. TDV does not give a single, definite reason for the decline.
