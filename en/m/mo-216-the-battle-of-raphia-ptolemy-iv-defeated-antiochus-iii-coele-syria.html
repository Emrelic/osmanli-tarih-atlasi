The Battle of Raphia: Ptolemy IV defeated Antiochus III; Coele-Syria remained with the Ptolemies

216 BC

At Raphia the army of Ptolemy IV, with its large number of native Egyptian phalanx troops (hoplites), defeated the army of Antiochus III; Antiochus withdrew from Coele-Syria, and the region remained in Ptolemaic hands until 200. The arming of the native Egyptians later fed the separatist revolts in Upper Egypt.
