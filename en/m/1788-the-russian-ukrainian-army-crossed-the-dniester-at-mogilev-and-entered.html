The Russian Ukrainian army crossed the Dniester at Mogilev and entered Moldavia (Ottoman–Russian War of 1787–1792)

1788

In the war that began with the Ottoman declaration of war in 1787, Russia set up two armies: while Potemkin's army besieged Ochakov, Rumyantsev's Ukrainian army was to threaten Bender between the Dniester and the Bug and to maintain contact with Austria. Rumyantsev detached one column to support the Austrians besieging Khotin, and with his main forces crossed the Dniester at Mogilev and entered Moldavia. The Moldavian leg of the Russian operation, which would extend to Izmail and the Danube, thus began.
