The advance of Muscovy's steppe defence line — Chuhuiv

1638

The Cossack leader Yakiv Ostrianyn and his men, who took refuge in Muscovy after their uprising in Poland, founded a frontier settlement on the Donets. This was one of the first great leaps toward the steppe of the defence line Muscovy had built against Crimean raids; over the following half century, towns such as Sumy, Izium and Bakhmut would arise one after another on the same line. The settlement continued as the seat of a company attached to the Kharkiv regiment until 1765.
