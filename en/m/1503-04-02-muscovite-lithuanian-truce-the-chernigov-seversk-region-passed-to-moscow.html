Muscovite-Lithuanian Truce — the Chernigov-Seversk region passed to Moscow

2 April 1503

The Muscovite-Lithuanian War of 1500-1503 ended with a six-year truce mediated by Vladislaus II, king of Hungary and Bohemia; the Grand Duchy of Lithuania was compelled to cede to the Grand Duchy of Moscow a broad expanse making up roughly one third of the Grand Duchy, including the Chernigov-Seversk region (Chernigov, Novgorod-Seversk, Starodub, the Bryansk area) and some lands around Smolensk and Polotsk. This was one of Moscow's greatest single gains in its process of 'gathering' the Russian lands.
