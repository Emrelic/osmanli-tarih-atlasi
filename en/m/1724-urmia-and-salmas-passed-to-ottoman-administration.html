Urmia and Salmas passed to Ottoman administration

1724

In the years when the Safavid State was dissolving through the Afghan invasion, Urmia once more came under Ottoman dominion. In 1728 an Ottoman tahrir register was compiled for Urmia and the liwa of Salmas attached to it. In Nadir's offensive of 1729-1730 the region was lost; Hekimoğlu Ali Pasha retook Urmia in 1731.
