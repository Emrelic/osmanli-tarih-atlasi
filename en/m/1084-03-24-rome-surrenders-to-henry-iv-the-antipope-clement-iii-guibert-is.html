Rome surrenders to Henry IV; the antipope Clement III (Guibert) is enthroned, Henry is crowned emperor, and Gregory flees Rome

24 March 1084

The Romans surrendered the city to Henry, who had descended into Italy in 1081; Guibert, archbishop of Ravenna, was proclaimed pope on 24 March 1084 as Clement III, and Henry was crowned emperor by the rival pope. Gregory VII, abandoned by thirteen cardinals, was forced to flee Rome; he relied on Norman support.
