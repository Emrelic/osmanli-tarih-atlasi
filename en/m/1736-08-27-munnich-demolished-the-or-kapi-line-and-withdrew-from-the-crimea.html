Münnich demolished the Or Kapı line and withdrew from the Crimea

27 August 1736

Münnich, who returned to Or Kapı from the interior of the Crimea, stayed here five weeks. Because of shortage of provisions and disease spreading in the army, he had the Perekop fortification line demolished and withdrew to Ukraine; when the Russians left, the Tatars rebuilt the line.
