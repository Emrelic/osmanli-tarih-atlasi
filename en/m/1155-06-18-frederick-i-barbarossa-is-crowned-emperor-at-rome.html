Frederick I Barbarossa is crowned emperor at Rome

18 June 1155

Frederick received the imperial crown at Rome on 18 June 1155 in his first Italian expedition, made in 1154; because of his insufficient forces and the hostility that arose, he returned to Germany in the autumn.
