Murad II took Alacahisar; Niš and Şehirköy returned to the Ottomans

1428

After the childless death of the Serbian Despot Stefan Lazarević (1427), Murad II, claiming to be the legitimate heir of Serbia on the basis of Bayezid the Thunderbolt's marriage to a Serbian princess, took action and took Alacahisar (1428). In the same year, after Lazarević's death, the Ottomans also recovered the fortress of Şehirköy — the town had been taken by Stefan in 1412 and defended against Musa Çelebi. According to the TDV article on Niš, the Ottomans retook Niš in the same year as well (831/1428). Thus the line connecting the Great Morava and Nišava valleys came under Ottoman control.
