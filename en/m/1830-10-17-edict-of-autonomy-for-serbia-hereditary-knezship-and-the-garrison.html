Edict of autonomy for Serbia — hereditary knezship and the garrison condition

17 October 1830

The hatt-ı hümâyun issued in accordance with the provision of the Treaty of Edirne concerning Serbia recognized Miloš Obrenović as hereditary knez and made Serbia an autonomous principality free in its internal affairs and dependent on the Ottomans externally. The provisions: the knezship would pass from father to son, a fixed annual tax would be paid, the Serbian assembly would establish its own internal administration, the church would be autonomous; in return, Ottoman garrisons would remain in the fortresses of Belgrade, Smederevo, Šabac, Soko and Ušće (Fethülislâm). This dual structure lasted until the withdrawal of the garrisons in 1867. On the map the city of Belgrade is joined to autonomous Serbia at this date; the fortress of Kalemegdan is shown with an Ottoman garrison until 1867.
