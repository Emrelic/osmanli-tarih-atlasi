With the capture of Smederevo, Hisarcık (Grocka) also passed to the Ottomans

27 August 1439

Smederevo was taken after a three-month siege. Hisarcık (Grocka), 17 km from Smederevo, follows on the map the fate of Smederevo, the center to which it was attached, and passes from the Serbian Despotate to the Ottomans on the same day (it will be returned in 1444).
