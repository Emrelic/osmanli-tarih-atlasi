Klokotnitsa: Ivan Asen II defeated and captured Theodore, Despot of Epirus; Didymoteicho came under Bulgarian rule

1230

In April 1230 at Klokotnitsa on the Adrianople-Philippopolis road, the Bulgarian Tsar Ivan Asen II defeated and captured Theodore Angelos; the power of the Empire of Thessalonica was broken, and Bulgaria grew in Thrace and Macedonia. Didymoteicho remained under Bulgarian rule between 1230 and 1246.
