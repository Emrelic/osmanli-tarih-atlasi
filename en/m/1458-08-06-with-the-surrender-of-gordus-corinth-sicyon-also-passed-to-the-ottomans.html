With the surrender of Gördüs (Corinth), Sicyon also passed to the Ottomans

6 August 1458

In the Morea campaign, the Conqueror took Gördüs (Corinth) peacefully after a three-month siege. Sicyon, 17 km from Corinth, follows in the atlas the fate of its senior center Gördüs and passes to the Ottomans on the same day.
