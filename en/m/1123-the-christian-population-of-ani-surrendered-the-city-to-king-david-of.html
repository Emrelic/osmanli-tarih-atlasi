The Christian population of Ani surrendered the city to King David of Georgia; the city bought by the Saltukid Emir Ali was lost

1123

When the Shaddadid Shawur II tried to sell Ani to the Saltukid Emir Ali, who held Kars, the Christian population rose in revolt, summoned King David of Georgia, who had recently taken Tbilisi, and surrendered the city to him; Shawur II was arrested with his family and sent to Georgia.
