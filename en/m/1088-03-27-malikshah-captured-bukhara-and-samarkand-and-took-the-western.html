Malikshah captured Bukhara and Samarkand and took the Western Qarakhanid Ahmad Khan prisoner to Isfahan

27 March 1088

When the chief qadi Ahmad b. Sulayman was killed by Ahmad Khan, Malikshah, at the call for help of the Shafi'i jurist Abu Tahir b. Alak, captured Bukhara and Samarkand in 481 (1088) and came to Uzgend; he took Ahmad Khan prisoner to Isfahan. Abu Tahir al-Khwarazmi was appointed deputy (naib) in Samarkand.
