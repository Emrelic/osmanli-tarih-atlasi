First Syrian War (274-272): Seleucid forces retook Damascus; peace was made

273 BC

Once Antiochus I had defeated the Galatians in the "Battle of the Elephants" (275) and secured his position, he fought Ptolemy II again: a Seleucid general retook Damascus, Magas attacked Egypt, and peace was finally made in 272. The war marks the beginning of the Ptolemaic-Seleucid contest over Coele-Syria.
