End of Latin rule in the Duchy of Naxos

15 April 1566

Giacomo Crispo, the last duke of the Latin Duchy of Naxos, which had lasted since 1207, was imprisoned in Istanbul following complaints from his people. In 1566 the islands were brought under Ottoman control and the duchy was given to Joseph Nasi; direct administration as a sanjak came only after Nasi's death in 1579. Some small islands remained for a while longer in the hands of the tribute-paying Gozzadini family, while Tinos remained with Venice.
