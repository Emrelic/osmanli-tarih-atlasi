The Rhenish Palatinate is given by Frederick II to Otto of Wittelsbach (the Palatine County in the Wittelsbach dynasty)

1214

On the death in 1214 of Henry, count palatine of the Rhine, the German king Frederick II gave the Palatinate to Otto, the younger son of Duke Louis I of Bavaria (betrothed to Henry's sister Agnes). Since Conrad's settlement in 1155 at the castle of Juttenbühel near Heidelberg, this area had been regarded as the capital of the Palatinate.
