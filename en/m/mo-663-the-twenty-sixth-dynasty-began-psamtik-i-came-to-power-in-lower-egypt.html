The Twenty-sixth Dynasty began: Psamtik I came to power in Lower Egypt at Sais as an Assyrian vassal

663 BC

Among the Delta princes whom Assyria had placed in Egypt, Psamtik I, ruler of Sais, began the Twenty-sixth (Saite) Dynasty. According to Herodotus, Psamtik emerged from among the 'twelve kings', was driven into the marsh, and recovered the Delta. After Assyria and Kush, Egypt again passed into the hands of a native dynasty; the winning of Thebes and Upper Egypt took several years.
