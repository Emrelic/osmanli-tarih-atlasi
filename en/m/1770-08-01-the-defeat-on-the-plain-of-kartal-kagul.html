The Defeat on the Plain of Kartal (Kagul)

1 August 1770

Three weeks after the burning of the fleet at Çeşme, the main Ottoman army on the Danube was dispersed on the plain of Kartal before the far smaller but disciplined units of Field Marshal Rumyantsev. The camp, the baggage and hundreds of cannon fell into enemy hands; the fleeing units suffered great losses while crossing the Danube. The destruction within the same summer of both the fleet and the land army led to the falling one after another of Kili, Akkerman, Ismail and Bender and to the occupation of Crimea.
