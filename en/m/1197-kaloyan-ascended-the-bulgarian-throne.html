Kaloyan ascended the Bulgarian throne

1197

After the murder of Asen (1196) and Peter (1197), their brother Kaloyan (Ioannitsa) ascended the Bulgarian throne in 1197; he ruled until 1207, expanded Bulgaria and established relations with the papacy.
