Elam (Old Elam - Neo-Elam): the Awan dynasty (first Elamite dynasty) begins

2399 BC

The Awan dynasty (c. 2400-2100 BCE), Shimashki, Epartid/sukkalmah (contemporary with Old Babylon); Middle Elam (Untash-Napirisha c. 1340-1300, Chogha Zanbil); Neo-Elam (1100-539). The name Elam first appears in a source in the text of Enmebaragesi, king of Kish (c. 2650 BCE); Neo-Elam II c. 770-646 (conflict with Assyria).
