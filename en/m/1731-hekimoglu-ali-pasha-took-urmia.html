Hekimoğlu Ali Pasha took Urmia

1731

In the Iranian campaign Hekimoğlu Ali Pasha first took Urmia (Rûmiye), and then Tabriz, evacuated to the Ottomans, on 15 November 1731. The TDV does not give the day of the capture of Urmia; only the year, and that it came before Tabriz, is known.
