Piluoge died; his son Geluofeng took over as Prince of Yunnan (748)

748

The founder of Nanzhao, Piluoge (Guiyi), died in the 7th year of Tianbao; the Tang court made his son Geluofeng his successor with the title Prince of Yunnan. According to the text, after Piluoge's death the military governor of Sichuan, Xianyu Zhongtong, and the governor of Yunnan, Zhang Qiantuo, behaved disrespectfully toward Nanzhao.
