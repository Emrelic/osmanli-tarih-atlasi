The Proclamation of the Cihâd-ı Ekber: The Reading of the Jihad Fatwa at the Fatih Mosque

14 November 1914

The five fatwas issued by the Şeyhülislam Ürgüplü Hayri Efendi were read at the Fatih Mosque in the name of the caliph-sultan, and all Muslims were called to jihad against the Entente states. This move, on which Germany placed great hopes, aimed at inciting Muslims in the British and French colonies to revolt. The expected general uprising did not take place; on the contrary, the revolt of the Emir of Mecca, Sharif Husayn, with British support two years later painfully showed the limits of the caliphate's political influence.
