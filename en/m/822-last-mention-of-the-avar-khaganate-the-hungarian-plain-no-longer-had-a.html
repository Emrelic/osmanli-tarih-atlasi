Last mention of the Avar Khaganate: the Hungarian plain no longer had a single owner (blank on the map afterwards)

822

The last political mention of the Avar state is around 822 (Pohl; Curta); afterwards the spheres of dominion of the Bulgars, the East Franks and Great Moravia competed, and no source naming a single owner for the area between the Danube and the Tisza could be found — the map shows this period as blank.

Sources
