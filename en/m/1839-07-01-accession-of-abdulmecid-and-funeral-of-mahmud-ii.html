Accession of Abdülmecid and funeral of Mahmud II

1 July 1839

Upon his father's death, the sixteen-year-old Abdülmecid acceded to the throne. On the same day Mahmud II's funeral cortege was brought to Topkapı Palace; the feared disorder did not occur, and broad masses of Muslims and non-Muslims joined the funeral procession.
