Diocletian drew Egypt's southern border back to the Elephantine/Philae line; Lower Nubia was left to the Nobatae

298

Procopius relates that Diocletian, seeing the cost of holding the vast desert (Dodekaschoinos) at Egypt's far south, left the area south of Elephantine to the Nobatae and the Blemmyes and fixed the border at Elephantine (it is also reported that an annual payment was agreed in return). Roman administration thus withdrew from the Hiera Sykaminos frontier that Augustus had drawn.
