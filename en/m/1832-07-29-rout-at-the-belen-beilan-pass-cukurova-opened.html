Rout at the Belen (Beilan) Pass — Çukurova opened

29 July 1832

The defensive line was broken in the narrow pass in the Amanus mountains. Antioch, Adana and Tarsus passed to the Egyptian forces (the occupation of Maraş, according to TDV, is 1833, a separate item); on the map Çukurova changes to the tributary tone. The road into the interior of Anatolia was now undefended.
