Many Batinis were killed in Aleppo, together with Hasan-i Sabbah's deputies in Syria

18 June 1113

The Seljuk Sultan Muhammad Tapar demanded that the Malik of Aleppo, Alp Arslan al-Akhras, and the ra'is al-ahdath, Sa'id b. Badi', have the Batinis in the city killed; many Batinis were killed, together with Hasan-i Sabbah's deputies in Syria such as Abu Tahir al-Sa'igh, Isma'il the da'i, and the brother of Hakim the astrologer (507/1113).
