Appointment of Mimar Sinan as chief imperial architect

1538

According to the Sinan entry of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, upon the death of the chief imperial architect Acem Alisi in 1537, Sinan, who had drawn attention with the bridge he built over the river Prut during the Moldavian campaign, was appointed to this post on the recommendation of Grand Vizier Lutfi Pasha. Thus began Sinan's period as chief architect, which would last more than fifty years.
