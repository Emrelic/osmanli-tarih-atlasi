Turan Shah II was killed by one of his own men; Muhammad Shah II ascended the throne a second time

26 April 1183

When Turan Shah II was killed in 579 (1183), Muhammad Shah II, son of Bahram Shah, was placed on the throne a second time; the Oghuz had settled in the region of Nasa and Narmashir.
