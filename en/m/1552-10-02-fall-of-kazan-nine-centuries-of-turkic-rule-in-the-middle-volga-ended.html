Fall of Kazan — nine centuries of Turkic rule in the Middle Volga ended

2 October 1552

After about two months of bloody fighting, Kazan fell on 2 October 1552 (Julian; the Gregorian equivalent is 12 October — the TDV's «15 October» appears as a 13-day conversion difference) and the Turkic rule that had lasted in the Middle Volga region since the 6th century AD ended. The city's works of art, calligraphy and architecture were plundered; most of them left not even a trace.
