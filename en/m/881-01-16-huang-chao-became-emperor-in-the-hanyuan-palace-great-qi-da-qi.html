Huang Chao became emperor in the Hanyuan Palace: Great Qi (Da Qi)

16 January 881

After having members of the Tang dynasty killed, Huang Chao entered the palace, proclaimed his empire in the Hanyuan Hall, made the name of the state Great Qi and the reign title Jintong, and issued a general amnesty.
