Plague epidemic in Istanbul

1637

In the time of Murad IV, Istanbul was shaken by a severe plague epidemic that affected a significant part of the population; trade within the city and daily life were disrupted for months. The TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on 'Istanbul' records that the repeated epidemics of the 17th century seriously affected the city's population balance. During the epidemic the palace and the elite moved for a time to gardens and pavilions outside the city.
