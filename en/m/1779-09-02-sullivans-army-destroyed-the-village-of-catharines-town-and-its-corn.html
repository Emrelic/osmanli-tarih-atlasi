Sullivan's army destroyed the village of Catharine's Town and its corn

2 September 1779

The army of the Sullivan expedition reached Catharine's Town, near Seneca Lake, on the night of 1 September 1779; on 2 September it destroyed the village and the corn under cultivation. The village was a settlement of thirty houses, with gardens and abundant corn.
