Raid on Kulacahisar — Osman Bey's first military operation

1285

TDV, Osman I entry: 'He plundered and set fire to the small Kulaca Castle near İnegöl (684/1285).' This is Osman Bey's first datable military action in the sources. No lasting settlement was captured; the castle was plundered and burned, so it is a classic frontier raid rather than a conquest. It is regarded as the starting point of the beylik's military activity.
