Villa de Oropesa was founded — the settlement of the grain valley that would feed Potosí

15 August 1571

By order of the viceroy Francisco de Toledo, Villa de Oropesa was founded in the Cochabamba valley. The valley's fertile soil was organized as the grain region that would feed the numerous labour force of the Potosí silver mines; the town became the granary of colonial Upper Peru.
