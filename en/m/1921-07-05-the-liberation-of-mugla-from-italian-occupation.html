The liberation of Muğla from Italian occupation

5 July 1921

Italy began to withdraw from its occupation zone in Anatolia in the summer of 1921; the evacuation of Antalya had begun on 1 June. According to the TDV article on Muğla, Muğla, which had been under Italian occupation since 23 July 1919, was liberated on 5 July 1921.
