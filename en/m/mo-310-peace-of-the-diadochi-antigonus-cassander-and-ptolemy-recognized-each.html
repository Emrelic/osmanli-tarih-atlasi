Peace of the Diadochi: Antigonus, Cassander and Ptolemy recognized each other's rule over Asia, Europe and Egypt

310 BC

In the peace signed in December 311, Antigonus was recognized as commander of Asia, Cassander as strategos of Europe, Lysimachus of Thrace, and Ptolemy as ruler of Egypt and its surroundings; the principle of the freedom of the Greek cities was adopted. Seleucus's Babylon remained outside this peace.
