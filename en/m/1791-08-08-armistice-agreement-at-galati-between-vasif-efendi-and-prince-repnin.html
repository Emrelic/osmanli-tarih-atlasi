Armistice agreement at Galați between Vâsıf Efendi and Prince Repnin

8 August 1791

A month after the rout at Măcin, on 8 August 1791, agreement was reached at Galați between Ahmed Vâsıf Efendi and the Russian commander Prince Repnin on the basis of the armistice. Three days later the armistice documents would be signed.
