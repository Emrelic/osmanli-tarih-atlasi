Institutionalization of the Imperial Council (Divan-i Hümayun) through the Conqueror's law

1 June 1451

The Divan began to acquire the identity of the "Divan-i Hümayun" in the time of Murad II, when it was bound by regular rules at Edirne. The real transformation came with the law of Mehmed the Conqueror: the greatest innovation was that the sultan's presidency of the divan was completely abolished and transferred to the grand vizier.
