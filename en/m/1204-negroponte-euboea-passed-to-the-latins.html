Negroponte (Euboea) passed to the Latins

1204

In the campaign of Boniface, Marquis of Montferrat, in the autumn of 1204, Latin knights crossed from Boeotia and Attica to the island of Euboea; in the first partition the island was divided between the Venetians and the Crusaders.
