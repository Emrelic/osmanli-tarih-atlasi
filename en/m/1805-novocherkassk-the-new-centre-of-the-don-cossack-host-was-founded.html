Novocherkassk, the new centre of the Don Cossack Host, was founded

1805

The administrative centre of the Don Cossack Host passed from Cherkassk, which had been the centre until then, to Novocherkassk, founded at the locality of Biryuchiy Kut between the Aksay and Tuzlov rivers.
