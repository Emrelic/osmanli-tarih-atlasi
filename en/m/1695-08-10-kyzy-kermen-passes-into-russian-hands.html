Kyzy-Kermen passes into Russian hands

10 August 1695

In the Dnieper line operation, carried out at the same time as Russia's Azov campaign, Sheremetev and Mazepa took Kyzy-Kermen. The 1700 Treaty of Istanbul stipulated that the fortress be demolished and its site left to the Ottomans.
