Near Benevento Pope Honorius II recognises Roger II as duke of Apulia, Calabria and Sicily

22 August 1128

On 22 August 1128, near Benevento, the pope invested Roger as duke of Apulia, Calabria and Sicily; in return, papal control of Benevento and the inviolability of the Principality of Capua were guaranteed. The Duchy of Apulia was united with the County of Sicily.
