Roger, King of Sicily, took Corfu

1147

Roger II's Norman fleet attacked the Byzantine coasts in autumn 1147; it seized the island of Corfu and sacked Corinth and Thebes. The island was retaken in 1148–1149 with the help of the Venetian fleet.
