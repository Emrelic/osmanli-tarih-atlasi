The Persian army under Gobryas (Ugbaru) entered Babylon without a fight; the Neo-Babylonian Empire came to an end

12 October 538 BC

Ugbaru (Gobryas), governor of Gutium, and Cyrus's army entered Babylon without fighting on 16 Tašritu; Nabonidus was captured. The rites in the Esagila were not interrupted. Cyrus entered the city on 3 Arahsamnu (29 October) and the Babylonian empire passed under Persian rule.
