Valdemar I defeats Svend at Grathe Heath and becomes king of Denmark

1157

After a long war of succession, Valdemar I, son of Knud Lavard, defeated Svend at Grathe Heath in 1157 and became sole king; the Valdemarian age of Denmark (with Absalon) began.
