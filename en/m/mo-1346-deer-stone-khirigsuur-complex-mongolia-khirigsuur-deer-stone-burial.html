Deer Stone–Khirigsuur Complex, Mongolia: Khirigsuur (Deer Stone) burial monuments begin

1346 BC

Standing deer-stone monuments and khirigsuur burial monuments encircled by square/round stones; from ca. 1200 BC horses become an integral part of these monuments (evidence of riding).
