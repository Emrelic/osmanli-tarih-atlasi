Agreement of Horodok: Yaroslav and Mstislav divide Rus' along the Dnieper

1026

Yaroslav, defeated by Mstislav at Lystven in 1024, was forced to leave the left bank of the Dnieper, except for Pereyaslavl, to Mstislav; by the agreement reached at Horodok in 1026, the two brothers settled down. Mstislav's centre was Chernigov.
