King Harald Bluetooth is baptised; the effective Christianisation of Denmark

960

According to EB1911, the true Christianisation of Denmark rests on King Harald Bluetooth's baptism in 960. Harald extended the Danevirke, subjugated the German lands south of the Eider, and founded the military colony of Julin (Jomsborg) at the mouth of the Oder.
