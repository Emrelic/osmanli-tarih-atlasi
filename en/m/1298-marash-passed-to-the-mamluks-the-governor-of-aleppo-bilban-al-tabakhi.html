Marash passed to the Mamluks — the governor of Aleppo, Bilban al-Tabakhi, retook it from the Armenians

1298

Marash, held since 1258 by the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia, had passed briefly to the Mamluks in 1293 through Sultan Khalil's treaty with the Armenians, and after their withdrawal had fallen again into Armenian hands. In 697 (1298) Bilban al-Tabakhi, the Mamluk governor of Aleppo, retook the city. Marash thereafter remained Mamluk territory until the rise of the Dulkadirids.
