Balban returned to Delhi with the support of the Turkish emirs

1 February 1254

After Balban fell from favor in March 1253 and was removed, a vacuum arose in the administration of the state; the Turkish emirs also persuaded the sultan and brought Balban back to office. After the dismissal of his rival, the Hindu convert Raihan, Balban came to Delhi on 1 February 1254.
