Abd al-Mu'min died at Salé; Abu Ya'qub Yusuf succeeded him

16 May 1163

Abd al-Mu'min, who had come to Ribat al-Fath for the campaign to al-Andalus, fell ill and died at Salé on 10 Jumada II 558 (16 May 1163); the preparation of the army was left half-finished, and his son Yusuf became caliph.
