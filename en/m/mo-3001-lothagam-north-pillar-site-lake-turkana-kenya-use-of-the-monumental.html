Lothagam North Pillar Site (Lake Turkana, Kenya): use of the monumental cemetery platform begins

3001 BC

The oldest and largest monumental cemetery in East Africa: a burial cavity for >=580 people (119.5 m2), a stone-ringed platform; the first herders of the Turkana basin.
