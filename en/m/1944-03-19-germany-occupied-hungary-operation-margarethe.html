Germany occupied Hungary (Operation Margarethe)

19 March 1944

After Hungary's search for a separate peace with the Allies, German troops occupied the country on 19 March 1944; the Sztójay government was formed and the deportation of Hungarian Jews began. Horthy was overthrown on 15 October 1944 over his attempt to declare an armistice, and the Arrow Cross took power.
