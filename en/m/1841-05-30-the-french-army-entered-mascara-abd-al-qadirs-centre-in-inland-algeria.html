The French army entered Mascara: Abd al-Qādir's centre in inland Algeria fell

30 May 1841

The French army under Marshal Bugeaud, in the campaign of 1841, entered Mascara on 30 May after Tagdemt; Abd al-Qādir made no serious attempt to defend the city.
