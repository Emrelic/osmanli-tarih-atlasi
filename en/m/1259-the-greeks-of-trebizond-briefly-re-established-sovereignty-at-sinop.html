The Greeks of Trebizond briefly re-established sovereignty at Sinop

1259

During the rivalry between Kaykaus II and Kilij Arslan IV, the Greeks of Trebizond briefly re-established sovereignty at Sinop in 657 (1259); by the yarligh of Möngke Khan the Seljuk realm was divided in two in the same year.
