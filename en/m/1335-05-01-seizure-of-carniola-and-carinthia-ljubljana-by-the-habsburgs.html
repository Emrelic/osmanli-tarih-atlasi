Seizure of Carniola and Carinthia (Ljubljana) by the Habsburgs

1 May 1335

With the death without heirs of Henry VI, Duke of Carinthia and Count of Tyrol, on 2 April 1335 at Tyrol Castle, in accordance with a secret agreement made with Louis of Bavaria on 23 November 1330, the Habsburg brothers Albert II and Otto IV received the Duchy of Carinthia and, attached to it, Carniola (the region that includes present-day Ljubljana) by imperial investiture; Tyrol, however, remained separate through Henry's daughter. This was one of the first great steps of Habsburg expansion in the Alpine region, and Carniola would remain (with interruptions) in Habsburg/Austrian hands until 1918.
