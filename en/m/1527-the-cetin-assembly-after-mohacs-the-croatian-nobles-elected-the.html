The Cetin Assembly — after Mohács the Croatian nobles elected the Habsburg Ferdinand king of Croatia

1527

When the throne fell vacant with the death of Louis II, king of Hungary and Croatia, at Mohács, the Croatian nobles assembled at Cetin on 31 December 1526 and on New Year's Day proclaimed Ferdinand, Archduke of Austria, king. In the words of the TDV, the Croats made this election independently of the Hungarian nobles; the election charter was based on the ground of not breaking away from a Christian state in the face of Ottoman raids. Thus the Croatian fortresses neighbouring Ottoman Bosnia were bound to the crown of the Habsburg dynasty; according to the TDV, when the Habsburgs began to organize the Croatian frontier region in the 1530s, the main centre of this line was Bihać.
