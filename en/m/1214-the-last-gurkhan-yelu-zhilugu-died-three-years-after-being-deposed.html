The last Gurkhan, Yelu Zhilugu, died three years after being deposed

1214

Yelu Zhilugu, the Gurkhan who had ruled the Qara Khitai state since 1178, was taken prisoner in 1211 by his son-in-law, the Naiman Kuchlug Khan, who ended the state, yet he continued to exercise sovereignty in practice and died in 1214; all Qara Khitai territory passed into the hands of the Naimans.
