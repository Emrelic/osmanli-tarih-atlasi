Victory of Varna

10 November 1444

The Hungarian-Polish Crusader army, which had marched south in breach of the Treaty of Szeged, was annihilated at Varna by Murad II, who had returned to the head of the army after abdicating; the young king Ladislaus III died on the battlefield. The victory eliminated the last great chance of foreign intervention aimed at liquidating the Ottoman presence in the Balkans and secured the road to the conquest of Constantinople.
