Hannibal crossed the Alps and entered Italy

217 BC

Hannibal crossed the Ebro, the Pyrenees and the Rhône, crossed the Alps in the autumn and descended into northern Italy; he defeated the Roman armies at the Ticinus and the Trebia. The centre of Carthage's war in Italy was thus carried into the heart of Rome.
