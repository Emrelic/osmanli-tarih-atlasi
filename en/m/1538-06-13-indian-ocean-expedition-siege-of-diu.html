Indian Ocean expedition: siege of Diu

13 June 1538

The fleet that set out from Suez crossed the Red Sea and besieged Diu in Gujarat; although the fortress could not be taken, Ottoman power reached the Indian Ocean for the first time and the Portuguese monopoly was shaken.
