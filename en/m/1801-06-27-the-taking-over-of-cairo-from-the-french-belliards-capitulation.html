The taking over of Cairo from the French — Belliard's capitulation

27 June 1801

During the joint Ottoman and British operation directed at Egypt, General Belliard, commander of the French forces in Cairo, signed the capitulation. Together with Cairo, Suez, which the French had seized in November 1798, also passed out of French control. The close of the occupation would be completed two months later in Alexandria, and the evacuation from the country would continue until October.
