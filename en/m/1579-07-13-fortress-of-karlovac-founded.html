Fortress of Karlovac founded

13 July 1579

The fortress of Karlovac was founded in the floodplain between the Kupa and the Korana to protect Croatia and the Austrian hereditary lands against the Ottomans; the fortress was built by Archduke Charles II, commander of the Croatian Military Frontier.
