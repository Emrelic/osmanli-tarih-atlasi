The Treaty of Brest-Litovsk: The Recovery of Kars, Ardahan and Batum

3 March 1918

The treaty signed between Russia, which had withdrawn from the war after the Bolshevik revolution, and the Central Powers closed the Eastern front and regained for the Ottoman State Kars, Ardahan and Batum (Elviye-i Selâse), which it had lost by the 1878 Treaty of Berlin. This was the only territorial gain in Ottoman history obtained after an interval of forty years. However, the region would be lost again after the Armistice of Mudros, and the definitive solution would come with the 1921 Treaty of Kars.
