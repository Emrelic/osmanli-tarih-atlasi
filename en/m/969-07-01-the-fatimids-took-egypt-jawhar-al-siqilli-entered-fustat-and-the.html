The Fatimids took Egypt: Jawhar al-Siqilli entered Fustat, and the foundation of Cairo was laid

1 July 969

The Fatimid commander Jawhar al-Siqilli ended Ikhshidid rule in Egypt, entered Fustat, and had the palace and walls of the new capital, Cairo, built at Manah in the north.
