The Founding of the Istanbul Şehremaneti: The First Modern Municipal Organisation

16 August 1855

The infrastructure crisis created by the allied soldiers massed in Istanbul during the Crimean War and by the growing population exposed the inadequacy of the classical city administration centred on the kadi. With the Şehremaneti, established on European models, cleaning, price controls (narh), market supervision and building works were gathered into one hand; the following year the Sixth Municipal District (Altıncı Daire-i Belediye) was formed in Beyoğlu-Galata as a pilot region. This step is accepted as the beginning of modern municipal government in the Ottoman Empire.
