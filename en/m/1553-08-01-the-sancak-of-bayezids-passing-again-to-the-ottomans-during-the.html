The sancak of Bayezid's passing again to the Ottomans during the Nakhchivan Campaign

1 August 1553

During Süleyman the Lawgiver's third Iranian (Nakhchivan) campaign the sancak of Bayezid was taken again under Ottoman administration. In the campaign of 1554 Bayezid became an important base for the army; the sancak definitively entered Ottoman sovereignty in 1578.
