Ferdinand I was crowned king of Hungary at Székesfehérvár (Istolni Belgrad)

3 November 1527

Ferdinand, who had taken Buda from Szapolyai in September 1527, was crowned in Székesfehérvár, the coronation city of the Hungarian kings, and the period of the two kings, with the rival Szapolyai, became consolidated.
