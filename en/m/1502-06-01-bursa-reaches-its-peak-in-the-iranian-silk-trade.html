Bursa reaches its peak in the Iranian silk trade

1 June 1502

Caravans coming from Tabriz carried the silks of Gilan, Astarabad and Sari to Bursa, where Genoese, Venetian, Florentine and Jewish merchants bought them in competition. In 1479 the value of silk imported from Iran reached 150,000 Venetian gold ducats; in 1502 more than 1,000 weaving looms were operating in the city.
