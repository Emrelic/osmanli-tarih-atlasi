Passing of Akyazı and the Island of İmralı into Ottoman hands (year not in the sources)

1324

It is not known in which year Akyazı and the Island of İmralı passed to the Ottomans. For Akyazı, all that is known is that it was after 1305: in 1305 it was still Byzantine, and in the following years it was taken through Konuralp's raids. For İmralı there is not even such a lower limit. The year 1324 on the map and in this list is the atlas's placeholder; it rests on no source.
