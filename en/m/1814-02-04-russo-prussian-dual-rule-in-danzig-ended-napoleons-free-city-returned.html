Russo-Prussian dual rule in Danzig ended: Napoleon's free city returned to Prussia

4 February 1814

Danzig, which had been separated from Prussia by the Peace of Tilsit (9 July 1807) and made a free city under French protection, was abandoned by Napoleon's forces after the siege of 1813. Between 2 January and 4 February 1814 Russo-Prussian dual rule continued in the city; when Tsar Alexander gave up his plan to take Danzig and the mouth of the Vistula, the city passed to Prussian administration.
