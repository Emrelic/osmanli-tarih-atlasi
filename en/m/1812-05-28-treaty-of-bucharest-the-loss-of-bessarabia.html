Treaty of Bucharest — the loss of Bessarabia

28 May 1812

By the treaty that ended the 1806-1812 Russian war, the area east of the Prut (Bessarabia) was left to Russia. Other settlements lost on the same date: Akkerman, Kiliya, Khotyn, Bender, Izmail. The atlas also regards Chișinău, Bălți, Cahul and Orhei within Bessarabia as having passed from Moldavia (an Ottoman vassal) to Russia on the same day. Soroca also passes to Russia on the same day.
