Alfred took London from the Danes; the Alfred–Guthrum peace changed the boundaries in Alfred's favor

886

Alfred captured London in 885 or 886 at the end of the measures he took against the rebellious Danes; a peace was made between Alfred and Guthrum that changed the Wedmore boundaries in Alfred's favor.
