Loss of Kırklareli after the battle of Kırkkilise

24 October 1912

After the defeat of the Eastern Army in the battle of Kırkkilise in Eastern Thrace, the city, defended by Kölemen Abdullah Pasha, was left to the Bulgarian forces. The city was liberated from an occupation lasting nine months only in the Second Balkan War, on 21 July 1913.
