The Black Sea Raid: The Bombardment of Russian Ports by the Ottoman Fleet

29 October 1914

The Goeben and Breslau (Yavuz and Midilli), which had escaped from Germany and joined the Ottoman fleet as though 'purchased', put out into the Black Sea under Admiral Souchon, bombarded Russian ports and sank ships. This order, which Enver Pasha gave secretly from the cabinet, effectively ended neutrality. Within a week Russia, England and France declared war, and the Ottoman State had irrevocably entered the First World War.
