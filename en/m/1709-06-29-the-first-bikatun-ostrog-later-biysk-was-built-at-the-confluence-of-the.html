The first Bikatun ostrog (later Biysk) was built at the confluence of the Biya and Katun

29 June 1709

In 1709 Russian Cossacks founded the first Bikatun ostrog (the predecessor of the later Biysk fortress) at the confluence of the Biya and Katun rivers; on the south-western edge of Kuznetsk uyezd, the ostrog began a new stage of Russian colonisation toward the Altai.
