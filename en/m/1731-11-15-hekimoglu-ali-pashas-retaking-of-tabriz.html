Hekimoğlu Ali Pasha's retaking of Tabriz

15 November 1731

Hekimoğlu Ali Pasha retook Tabriz, which had fallen into Safavid hands in 1730, on 15 November 1731; Urmia was taken in the same operation. But by the treaty of 10 January 1732 Tabriz was left to Iran. The map also shows Kotur, which is considered to have shared the same fate as Hoy, as having passed from the Safavids to the Ottomans on this day (TDV Hoy; no day specific to Kotur in the source — approximate information).
