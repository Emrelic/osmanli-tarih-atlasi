By Möngke Khan's yarligh the Seljuk realm is divided in two between Kaykawus II and Kilij Arslan IV

1259

After Baiju's departure from Anatolia, Kaykawus II was again seated on the throne at Konya (1 May 1257); by Möngke Khan's yarligh the country was divided between Kaykawus and Kilij Arslan. Kaykawus, who did not accept Mongol rule, took refuge with Byzantium in 660/1262.
