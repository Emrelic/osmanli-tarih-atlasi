Vânî Mehmed Efendi's death in exile

12 October 1685

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Mehmed Efendi, Vânî, Vânî Mehmed Efendi, counted among the principal figures who had the sultan accept the Vienna campaign, was disgraced under public pressure after the campaign ended in a debacle and was exiled to the town of Kestel in Bursa. He died there on 14 Dhu al-Qa'da 1096 (12 October 1685) and was buried at the entrance of the mosque he had had built.
