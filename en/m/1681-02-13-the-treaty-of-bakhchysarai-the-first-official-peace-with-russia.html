The Treaty of Bakhchysarai — The First Official Peace with Russia

13 February 1681

As a result of negotiations conducted through the mediation of the Crimean Khan after the Chyhyryn campaigns, a twenty-year peace was signed between the Ottoman State and Russia, with the Özü (Dnieper) river accepted as the frontier. Being the first comprehensive treaty between the two states, it is regarded as the founding document of Ottoman-Russian relations. Thanks to the peace, the eastern front was secured and the army could be directed to Central Europe.
