The Declaration of War on Russia — The Ottoman-Russian War of 1787-1792 Began

16 August 1787

After Catherine II's ostentatious journey to Crimea and her taking Georgia under her protection, the Divan took the decision for war with the aim of reversing the annexation, and the Russian envoy was again shut up in Yedikule. However, the army was not ready, the treasury was empty, and the search for allies had been fruitless. The war, which would last five years, would end with the fall of Özi through a massacre, the loss of Ismail and the heavy terms of the Treaty of Jassy.
