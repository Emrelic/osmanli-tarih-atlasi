Uprising of Düzmece Mustafa

15 August 1421

Mustafa Çelebi, who claimed to be a son of the Thunderbolt, proclaimed a sultanate in Rumelia with Byzantine support; Murad II suppressed the uprising in 1422 and had his rival executed.
