Divriği given back to the Mamluks because of the Timurid danger

1401

Divriği, which Bayezid the Thunderbolt had taken from the Mamluks in 1398, was left to the Mamluks again in 1401 upon Timur's approach to Anatolia. For this reason, after Timur's destruction of Sivas (1400), Divriği appears on the map in Mamluk colour; in the same months Bayezid was taking Erzincan and Kemah in the east, that is, Mamluk land was left between the Ottoman lands in the west (Sivas) and in the northeast (Kemah). Divriği passed definitively to the Ottomans only after Marj Dabiq in 1516.
