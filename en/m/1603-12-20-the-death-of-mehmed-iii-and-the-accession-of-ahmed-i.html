The Death of Mehmed III and the Accession of Ahmed I

20 December 1603

Upon the death of Mehmed III on 20 December, his fourteen-year-old son Ahmed I ascended the throne. His being the first sultan to take the throne at a young age and without having gone out to a provincial governorship (sancak) shows that the practice of raising princes in the provinces had effectively come to an end. In the reign of Ahmed I, fratricide would be abandoned and a transition made to the principle of 'ekberiyet' (the eldest male member succeeding to the throne).
