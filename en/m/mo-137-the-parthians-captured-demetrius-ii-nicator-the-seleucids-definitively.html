The Parthians captured Demetrius II Nicator; the Seleucids definitively lost Babylonia and Mesopotamia

137 BC

The Parthian army of Mithridates I captured the Seleucid king Demetrius II and held him at the Parthian court. Mithridates annexed Mesopotamia to the Parthian Empire; the Seleucid state, squeezed against the coast, withdrew into Syria.
