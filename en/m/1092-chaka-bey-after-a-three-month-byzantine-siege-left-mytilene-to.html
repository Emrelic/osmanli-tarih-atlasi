Chaka Bey, after a three-month Byzantine siege, left Mytilene to Byzantium on condition that his soldiers be allowed to depart alive

1092

In the spring of 1092 Emperor Alexios Komnenos sent John Doukas by land and Dalassenos by sea to relieve Mytilene; although the three-month siege did not succeed, Chaka Bey, realizing he could hold out no longer, left Mytilene to Byzantium on condition that his soldiers leave the island unharmed.
