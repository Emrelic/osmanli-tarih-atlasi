Narameikhla returned to the Arakan throne with troops given by the sultanate of Gaur (Bengal); the kings began to use Muslim titles

1430

Narameikhla, who had remained in exile in Bengal for many years, was restored to his throne in 1430 with troops given by the king of Gaur. Harvey records that from then on the Buddhist Arakan kings used Muslim titles beside their own names and medallions with the Persian kalima.
