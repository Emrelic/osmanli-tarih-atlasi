Martial law in Suakin — the city passed to British command

9 February 1884

After Baker's force for the relief of Tokar was routed at El Teb on 4 February, Admiral Hewett landed marines at Suakin on 6 February. On 9 February 1884 martial law was proclaimed in the city and British officers were given full military and civil authority over it; the next day responsibility for the city was formally transferred from Baker to Hewett.
