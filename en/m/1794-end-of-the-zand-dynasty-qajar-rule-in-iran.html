End of the Zand dynasty — Qajar rule in Iran

1794

With the defeat of Lutf Ali Khan, the last ruler of the Zand dynasty, the dynasty founded in 1751 came to an end, and all of Iran fell into the hands of Agha Muhammad Khan. The formal founding of the Qajar State is dated two years later, to Agha Muhammad Khan's assumption of the crown of shahanshah at Tehran (1796); but the effective takeover occurred in 1794.
