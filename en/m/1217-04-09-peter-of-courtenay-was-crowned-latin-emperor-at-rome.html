Peter of Courtenay was crowned Latin Emperor at Rome

9 April 1217

Peter of Courtenay, chosen after Henry's death, was crowned by Honorius III on 9 April 1217 at Rome in the church of San Lorenzo; the pope held the ceremony outside the city walls so as not to confuse the titles of the Eastern and Western empires. He then set out for Dyrrachium with Venetian ships.
