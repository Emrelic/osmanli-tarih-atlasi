The Red Army's entry into Tbilisi: fall of the Democratic Republic of Georgia

25 February 1921

Georgia, which had declared independence on 26 May 1918, was occupied by Soviet Russia in February 1921; on 25 February 1921 the Bolsheviks entered Tbilisi and established the Soviet regime. With this event Gori, Telavi and Dusheti (eastern Georgia) passed to Soviet Russian control.
