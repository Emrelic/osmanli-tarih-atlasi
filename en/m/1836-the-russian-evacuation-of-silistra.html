The Russian evacuation of Silistra

1836

After the Treaty of Edirne of 1829 the Russians did not evacuate Silistra and held the fortress until 1836. After the withdrawal of the Russian soldiers, the governor Selim Pasha had a large single-domed mosque built in the middle of the half-ruined city.
