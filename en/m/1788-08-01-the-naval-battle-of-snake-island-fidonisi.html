The naval battle of Snake Island (Fidonisi)

1 August 1788

Cezayirli Gazi Hasan Pasha defeated the Russian fleet in the battle of Snake Island fought in August 1788. However, this success was not enough to save the fortresses in the northern Black Sea; at the end of the same year Özi fell.
