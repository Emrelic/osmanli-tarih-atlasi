Rout at Racova (Vaslui) in Moldavia

10 January 1475

The voivode of Moldavia, Stephen the Great (Ştefan cel Mare), drew the Ottoman forces under the command of the beylerbeyi of Rumelia, Hadım Süleyman Pasha, onto marshy ground and inflicted a heavy defeat on them. This rout, one of the heaviest land defeats of the Conqueror's reign, aroused a great echo in Europe and won Stephen fame in the crusading world. The sultan was to set out on campaign in person the following year in reprisal.
