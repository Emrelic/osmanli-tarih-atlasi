The Andalusian Arabs settled in Crete: the Emirate of Crete was born

2 April 827

The Andalusian Arabs, who had been expelled after the Revolt of the Suburb in Cordoba, came to Crete under the command of Abu Hafs Umar b. Shu'ayb, seized the island gradually and founded the city of Rabdh al-Khandaq (Chandax). The line of Abu Hafs ruled on the island until 961.
