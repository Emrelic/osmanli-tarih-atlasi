The liberation of Maraş — the French evacuated the city

11 February 1920

Maraş, which the British had occupied on 22 February 1919, had been left to the French on 29 October 1919 by an agreement between the two states. The attitude of the Armenians within the French troops and the lowering of the Turkish flag on the citadel stirred the population to revolt; the Society for the Defence of Rights and the National Forces were organized in the city. According to the TDV article on Kahramanmaraş, at the end of the fighting the French evacuated the city on 11 February 1920 and began to withdraw in the direction of İslahiye, and Maraş was liberated on 11–12 February. The city was awarded the Medal of Independence in 1925.
