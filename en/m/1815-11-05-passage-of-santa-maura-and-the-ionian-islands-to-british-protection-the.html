Passage of Santa Maura and the Ionian Islands to British protection — the post-Napoleonic order

5 November 1815

Santa Maura (Ayamavra) and the other Ionian Islands, which had passed to France in 1797, were left under British protection under the name 'United States of the Ionian Islands' by a separate treaty signed in Paris on 5 November 1815 between Britain, Russia, Austria and Prussia, following Napoleon's final defeat — this is a document distinct from the general Treaty of Paris of 20 November 1815. The protection would last until 1864, after which the islands would be ceded to Greece.
