The Qarmatians of Bahrain occupied Kufa two years after Basra; they could not hold out against the Buyids and withdrew

24 May 985

After the death of the Buyid Adud al-Dawla (372/983), the Qarmatians of Bahrain, wishing to display their strength in southern Iraq, attacked Basra in 373 (983-84) and made it pay tribute, and two years later they occupied Kufa; but they could not hold out against the Buyids and withdrew, and never again had a voice in Iraq.
