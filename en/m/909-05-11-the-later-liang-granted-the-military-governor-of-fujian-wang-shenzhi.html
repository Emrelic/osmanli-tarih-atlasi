The Later Liang granted the military governor of Fujian, Wang Shenzhi, the title 'King of Min'

11 May 909

By an edict of the Later Liang, the military governor of Fujian, Wang Shenzhi, became 'King of Min'; in the same edict Wang Chuzhi of Yiding received the title 'King of Beiping' and Liu Yin of Guangzhou the title 'King of Nanping'.
