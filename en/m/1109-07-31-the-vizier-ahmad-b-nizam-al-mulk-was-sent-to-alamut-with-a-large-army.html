The vizier Ahmad b. Nizam al-Mulk was sent to Alamut with a large army; no result was achieved because of the winter

31 July 1109

The Seljuk Sultan Muhammad Tapar dispatched his vizier Ahmad b. Nizam al-Mulk against Alamut with a large army in order to eliminate Hasan-i Sabbah; the campaign remained inconclusive because of the winter. The actual siege was to come in 1117.
