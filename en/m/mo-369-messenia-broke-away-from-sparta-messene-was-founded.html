Messenia broke away from Sparta; Messene was founded

369 BC

In Epaminondas's Peloponnesian campaign after Leuctra (winter 370/69), the helots of Messenia were freed and the strongly walled city of Messene was founded at the foot of Mount Ithome. Sparta lost, for the first time in centuries, the lands of Messenia that formed its economic basis.
