Battle of Ankara — the Interregnum began

28 July 1402

In the heavy defeat suffered on the Plain of Çubuk against the army of Timur, Bayezid the Thunderbolt was taken prisoner and died in captivity a few months later. Timur revived the Anatolian principalities; Ottoman lands were divided among Bayezid's sons and a struggle for the throne that would last 11 years (the Interregnum) began. This period, the gravest crisis the state had experienced since its foundation, engraved the importance of a centralized structure on Ottoman memory.
