Serbian troops withdrew from Szigetvár — the Hungarian gendarmerie took over the town

20 August 1921

Following the entry into force of Trianon, Serbian forces began to withdraw from Barcs and Szigetvár on 17 August and took away everything that could be moved. Around noon on 20 August, Hungarian gendarmerie units entered; in the afternoon the municipal council and officials took the oath, and at five o'clock the military handover took place in the town hall.
