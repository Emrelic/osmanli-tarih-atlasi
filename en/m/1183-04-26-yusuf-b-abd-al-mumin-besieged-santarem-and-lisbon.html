Yusuf b. Abd al-Mu'min besieged Santarém and Lisbon

26 April 1183

Yusuf, who was cooperating with Castile, besieged first Santarém and then Lisbon in the year 579 (1183) in order to punish the Portuguese, who were harassing the Muslim cities; when he was gravely wounded and died, the campaign was left half-finished.
