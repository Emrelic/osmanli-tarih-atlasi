The Russian occupation of Lahijan — the easternmost point in Gilan

1725

The Russians were never able to pass beyond Lahijan in Gilan; the city was occupied in 1725. Mazandaran and Astarabad, which had been ceded by the Treaty of Saint Petersburg, never came in fact into Russian hands. In the same year the Ottoman army completed its share in Azerbaijan by taking Tabriz and Ganja.
