Burning of the palaces of Persepolis (April)

329 BC

In April 330 Alexander had several palace buildings burned in Persepolis; the reasons given are that the treasury should not fall into enemy hands and that he was not yet sole ruler. It is seen archaeologically that the Palace of Xerxes also burned; the whole city was abandoned.
