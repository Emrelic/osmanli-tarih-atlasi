Mursili I took and destroyed Aleppo (Yamhad)

1599 BC

Mursili I, grandson and successor of Hattusili, took and destroyed the kingdom of Aleppo, which had resisted for decades. The end of Yamhad secured Hittite supremacy in northern Syria and opened the way for Mursili's Babylonian campaign.
