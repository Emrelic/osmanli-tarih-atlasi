The fortress of Faş (Poti) was destroyed by the forces of Imereti, Guria and Mingrelia

1640

The fortress of Faş was razed in 1640 by the forces of Imereti, joined by the princes of Guria and Mingrelia. The Ottoman presence at the mouth of the Rioni was interrupted until the fortress was rebuilt in 1724.
