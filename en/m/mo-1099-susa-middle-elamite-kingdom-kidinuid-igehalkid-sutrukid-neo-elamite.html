Susa: Middle Elamite Kingdom (Kidinuid, Igehalkid, Sutrukid) → Neo-Elamite Kingdom

1099 BC

At Susa, ownership passes from the Middle Elamite Kingdom (Kidinuid, Igehalkid, Sutrukid) to the Neo-Elamite Kingdom.
