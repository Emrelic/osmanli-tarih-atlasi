George I Rákóczi took over Szatmár

1 February 1644

In the 1644 campaign of George I Rákóczi, prince of Transylvania, the German garrison of Szatmár entered Rákóczi's service and the fortress fell into Transylvania's hands. The Peace of Linz of 16 December 1645 left the fortress to Rákóczi's heirs for as long as a Rákóczi sat on the throne of Transylvania.
