The capture of the fortress of Cetin by Austria

1790

In 1790 the Habsburg army attacked Cetin, Bužim and Velika Kladuša and took Cetin. The mobilization declared by the governor of Bosnia and the counter-offensive failed to retake the fortress; Article 4 of the Treaty of Sistova ceded Cetin to the Habsburgs. The frontiersmen of Bosnia recaptured the fortress in brief raids in 1809 and 1813.
