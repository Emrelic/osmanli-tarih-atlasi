Proclamation of the Second Constitutional Era

23 July 1908

Faced with the movement that grew with the officers affiliated with the Committee of Union and Progress taking to the mountains in Macedonia, Abdülhamid II restored the Kanûn-ı Esâsî; the 'proclamation of freedom' was received with enthusiasm and elections were held. Stability, however, did not come: after the Incident of 31 March (April 1909) was suppressed, Abdülhamid was deposed; Bulgaria's independence, the annexation of Bosnia and Crete's turning toward Greece also took place in these turbulent months.
