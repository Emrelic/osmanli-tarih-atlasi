Evacuation of Hattusa: Hattusa is abandoned; the Hittite Empire collapses (about 1200)

1199 BC

The central administration evacuated Hattusa, and the city was burned only AFTERWARDS; drought (1198-1196±3) may have been part of the conditions of the collapse, but there is NO proven causality.
