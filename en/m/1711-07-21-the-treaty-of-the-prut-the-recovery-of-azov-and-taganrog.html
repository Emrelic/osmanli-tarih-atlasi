The Treaty of the Prut — The Recovery of Azov and Taganrog

21 July 1711

The seven-article treaty signed upon the surrender of the Russian army besieged on the banks of the Prut stipulated the return of the Fortress of Azov to the Ottomans together with its lands and munitions. The fortifications of Taganrog, Kamenka and Yenikale would be demolished, and the parties would not build new fortresses in these regions. The system of fleet and bases that Peter I had built over sixteen years at Voronezh and Azov collapsed; Russia's descent to the Black Sea was delayed by about fifty years.
