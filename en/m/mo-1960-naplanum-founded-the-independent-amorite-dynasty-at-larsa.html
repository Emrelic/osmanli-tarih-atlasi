Naplanum founded the independent Amorite dynasty at Larsa

1960 BC

The Amorite chief Naplanum, by proclaiming his dominion at Larsa, founded the second great dynasty of the south after Ur III. In its centuries-long rivalry with Isin, Larsa would first bind Ur, then Uruk and Isin.
