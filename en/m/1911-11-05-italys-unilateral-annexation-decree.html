Italy's unilateral annexation decree

5 November 1911

While the war was going on, Italy issued a decree on 5 November 1911 and declared that it had annexed Tripolitania and Cyrenaica. The Ottoman State did not recognize this and the war continued for another year; the annexation gained legal validity only with the Treaty of Ouchy of 18 October 1912. For this reason the decree does not change the Ottoman color on the map — legal ownership remains with the Ottomans until 1912, and Italian control is shown as a hatched occupation.
