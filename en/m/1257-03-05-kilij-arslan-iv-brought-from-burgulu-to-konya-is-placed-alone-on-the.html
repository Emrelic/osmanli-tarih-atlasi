Kilij Arslan IV, brought from Burgulu to Konya, is placed alone on the Seljuk throne

5 March 1257

Kilij Arslan IV, who after the defeat of Sultanhanı (654/1256) was at Burgulu (Uluborlu), was brought to Konya and placed alone on the Seljuk throne on 16 Safar 655 / 5 March 1257; when Kaykawus II came to Konya on 14 Rabi' al-akhir 655 / 1 May 1257, he sent his commander Ali Bahadır against Kilij Arslan.
