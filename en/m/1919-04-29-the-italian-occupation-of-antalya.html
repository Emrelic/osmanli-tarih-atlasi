The Italian occupation of Antalya

29 April 1919

After the Armistice of Mudros, as the Entente powers settled into their occupation zones in Anatolia, Italy turned to southwestern Anatolia. According to the TDV article on Antalya, the city and its surroundings were occupied by the Italians on 29 April 1919, relying on the provisions of the armistice. The occupation would last about two years.
