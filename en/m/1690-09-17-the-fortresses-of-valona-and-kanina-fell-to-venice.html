The fortresses of Valona and Kanina fell to Venice

17 September 1690

While the Ottoman counteroffensive continued in Serbia, the Venetian fleet and forces took Valona and Kanina on the Albanian coast; the two fortresses would be demolished and abandoned in March 1691.
