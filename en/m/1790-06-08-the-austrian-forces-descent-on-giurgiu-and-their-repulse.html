The Austrian forces' descent on Giurgiu and their repulse

8 June 1790

The Austrian forces descended along the Danube as far as Giurgiu on 8 June 1790, but were repulsed by the Ottoman soldiers. Three months later, in the same place, in the tent of the grand vizier, an armistice with Austria would be signed.
