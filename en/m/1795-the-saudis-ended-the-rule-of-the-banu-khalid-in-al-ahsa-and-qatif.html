The Saudis ended the rule of the Banū Khālid in al-Ahsā' and Qatīf

1795

The Saudi emir Abdulaziz increased his pressure on al-Ahsā' and the Banū Khālid after 1792 and in 1795 took control of the region, appointing there as emir a member of the Najīm tribe, loyal to him. The region was then attached directly to Dir'iyya. With this conquest Qatīf passed from the hands of the Banū Khālid to the First Saudi State.
