Alp Arslan took Ani on the Arpaçay, subject to Byzantium, and made King Bagrat of Georgia a tributary

1064

Around 1064 Alp Arslan took Ani, a fortified city subject to Byzantium, together with many fortresses and cities in eastern Anatolia and Georgia, and made King Bagrat of Georgia a tributary; the caliph issued a declaration of thanks for these successes.
