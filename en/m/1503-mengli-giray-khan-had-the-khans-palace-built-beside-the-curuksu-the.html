Mengli Giray Khan had the Khan's Palace built beside the Çürüksu — the foundation of Bakhchysarai

1503

In the valley that was the summer hunting and resting region of the governors of the Golden Horde and of the first Crimean khans, Mengli Giray Khan I had the palace now known as the Khan's Palace built beside the Çürüksu and thus laid the foundation of Bakhchysarai. The city developed around the palace and along the narrow Çürüksu valley, flourished at the end of the sixteenth century, and became the centre of the Crimean Khanate.
