Peace after Zama: Carthage lost Hispania, the Mediterranean islands and her fleet, and undertook an indemnity of 10,000 talents

200 BC

After Zama (202) Rome confined Carthage to her own lands in Africa: the conditions of giving up Hispania and the Mediterranean islands, surrendering the fleet (except ten ships), paying 10,000 talents over 50 years and not making war without Rome's permission were accepted. Masinissa's Numidia became an independent neighbour.
