Gyges overthrew Candaules and founded the Mermnad dynasty

684 BC

According to Herodotus, Gyges, then a royal bodyguard, overthrew King Candaules, seized the Lydian throne and founded the Mermnad dynasty. After the collapse of Phrygia under Cimmerian attack, Lydia began to become the main power of western Anatolia.
