Timur took Malatya and left it to Karayülük Osman — when Timur withdrew, the Dulkadirids retook it

1400

Bayezid the Thunderbolt had returned to Bursa, leaving Malatya, which he had taken in 1399, to Nasreddin Mehmed of the Dulkadirids. Timur, who entered Anatolia in 1400, took first Sivas and Elbistan, then Malatya; the city and its surroundings were plundered and its administration was left to Karayülük Osman, who was with Timur. When Timur left the region, the Dulkadirids again came to dominate Malatya; Ottoman dominion thus ended before Ankara.
