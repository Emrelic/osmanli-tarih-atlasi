The Death of Mustafa III and the Accession of Abdülhamid I

21 January 1774

In place of Mustafa III, who died at the most critical moment of the Russian war, his brother Abdülhamid I, who had spent forty-three years in the cage of the palace, ascended the throne at the age of forty-nine. The new sultan, who took over one of the most difficult periods of the state, was obliged to face the heavy terms of the Treaty of Küçük Kaynarca to be signed in the same year. His reign of fifteen years would pass with the loss of Crimea and a new Russian war, and would end with the stroke he suffered while reading the news of the fall of Özi in 1789.
