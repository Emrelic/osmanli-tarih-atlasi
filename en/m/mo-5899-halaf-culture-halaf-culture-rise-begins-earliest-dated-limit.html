Halaf culture: Halaf culture: rise begins (earliest dated limit)

5899 BC

A Late Neolithic/Chalcolithic culture with fine painted pottery in northern Mesopotamia (beginning with the transition to Early Halaf in the Tell Sabi Abyad sequence); with Ubaid influence it gives way to the Northern Ubaid.
