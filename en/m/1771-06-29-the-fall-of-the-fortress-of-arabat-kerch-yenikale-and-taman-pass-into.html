The fall of the fortress of Arabat; Kerch, Yenikale and Taman pass into Russian hands

29 June 1771

The Russian column under Shcherbatov crossed the Genichesk strait by a bridge built from boats brought by the Azov flotilla, and, marching about a hundred versts along the Arabat spit, took the fortress of Arabat. In July the same column also captured Kerch, Yenikale and Taman. Thus both sides of the Kerch strait came under Russian control.
