Shaybani Khan took Bukhara and Samarkand

1500

Muhammad Shaybani Khan, taking advantage of the disorder among the Timurids, took Bukhara and Samarkand and founded the Shaybanid (Uzbek) dynasty. Between 1500 and 1507 he captured nearly all the cities of Transoxiana, Khwarazm and Khorasan.
