Execution of Sultan Ibrahim

18 August 1648

On the grounds of extravagance and the crisis of government, an alliance of the corps and the ulema deposed Ibrahim; ten days later he was strangled by a fatwa. Mehmed IV, aged seven, came to the throne; power effectively passed into the hands of the grand valide Kösem Sultan.
