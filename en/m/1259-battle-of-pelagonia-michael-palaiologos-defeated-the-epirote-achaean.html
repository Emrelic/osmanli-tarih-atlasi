Battle of Pelagonia: Michael Palaiologos defeated the Epirote-Achaean-Sicilian alliance

1259

Michael VIII routed the combined army of Sicily, Epirus, Achaea and the Serbian kings on the plain of Pelagonia (western Macedonia); William of Villehardouin, Prince of Achaea, was captured. The victory prepared the end of the Latin Empire.
