King Ptolemy was killed by Caligula; the independence of Mauretania ended

40

Ptolemy, son of Juba II, was killed at Lugdunum in 40 CE on Caligula's orders; the Aedemon revolt then broke out in Mauretania, and in 44 Claudius divided the country into two provinces, Tingitana and Caesariensis.
