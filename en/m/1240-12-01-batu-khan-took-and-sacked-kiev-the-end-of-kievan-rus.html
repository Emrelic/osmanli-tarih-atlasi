Batu Khan took and sacked Kiev; the end of Kievan Rus'

1 December 1240

In December 1240 the Mongol-Tatar army of Batu Khan captured Kiev and massacred its population; Kiev, Volodymyr, and Halych were destroyed. Southern Russia was incorporated into the Mongol Empire (the Golden Horde). This event is regarded as the effective end of Kievan Rus'.
