The Death of Abdülmecid and the Accession of Sultan Abdülaziz

25 June 1861

Upon the death from tuberculosis of Abdülmecid, who in his reign of twenty-two years had put his signature to the Tanzimat and Reform edicts, his brother Abdülaziz ascended the throne. In the first years the new sultan gave hope with measures of economy and his simple way of living; he made the navy one of the few leading naval powers in the world. However, towards the end of his reign the heavy foreign debt, the bankruptcy of 1875 and the Balkan rebellions accumulated and prepared the ground for the deposition movement of 1876.
