Firman: governorship of Egypt left hereditary to the Kavalalı family

24 May 1841

The sultan's firman recognized the governorship as passing from father to son within the dynasty; in return the strength of the army was reduced to eighteen thousand, taxation was tied to Istanbul, and the authority to mint coins and to conclude treaties was removed. Egypt remained a province but was now the domain of a separate dynasty. The administration of the Hejaz and the Haramayn returned to the Sublime Porte. Other settlements returning from the tributary layer to the direct layer on the same date: Mecca, Medina, Jeddah, Yanbu.
