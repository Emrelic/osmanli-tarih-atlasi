The fleet rebuilt by Kılıç Ali Pasha put to sea

13 June 1572

After the heavy defeat at Lepanto (7 October 1571), ships were built in the shipyards throughout the winter. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, on 1 Safar 980 (13 June 1572) the new fleet, consisting of 250 galleys and about 300 volunteer reis galleys, was launched in a show of strength.
