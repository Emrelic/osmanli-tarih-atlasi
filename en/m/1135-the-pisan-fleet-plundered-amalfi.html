The Pisan fleet plundered Amalfi

1135

In the campaign of 1135 (and 1136-37), the Pisans, coming to the aid of Robert, Prince of Capua, at Naples against Roger II, King of Sicily, destroyed Amalfi and plundered the island of Ischia.
