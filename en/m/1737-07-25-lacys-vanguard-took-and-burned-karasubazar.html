Lacy's vanguard took and burned Karasubazar

25 July 1737

Lacy, who entered the Crimea by crossing the Sivash, repelled the khan's attack on 12 July (Julian). Two days later General Douglas, in the vanguard, dispersed the Tatar camp before Karasubazar, took the city without resistance and burned it. Lacy began to withdraw the next day.
