Bori Tegin was removed; Sebuk Tegin became ruler of Ghazna, and the Ghaznavid state turned into a dynasty passing from father to son

977

In place of Bori Tegin, who was removed by the Turkish commanders because of his incompetence, Sebuk Tegin, in whom Alp Tegin had confidence, was installed (366/977); he is regarded as the true founder of the independent Ghaznavid state.
