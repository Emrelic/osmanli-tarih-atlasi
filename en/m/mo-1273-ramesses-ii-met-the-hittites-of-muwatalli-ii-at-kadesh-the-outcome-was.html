Ramesses II met the Hittites of Muwatalli II at Kadesh; the outcome was a draw

1273 BC

Ramesses II met the Hittite army before Kadesh on the Orontes; the Egyptian army came close to being shattered when the Hittite chariots set an ambush, and Ramesses counterattacked with his own guard and drove them back. The battle gave no decisive result; Kadesh and Amurru remained under Hittite control, and Egypt's northern frontier again remained on the Canaanite coast.
