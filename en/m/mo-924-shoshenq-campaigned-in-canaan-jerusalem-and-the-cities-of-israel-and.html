Shoshenq campaigned in Canaan: Jerusalem and the cities of Israel and Judah entered the Karnak list

924 BC

Shoshenq's Canaanite campaign corresponds with the biblical account that reports he took tribute from Jerusalem in the 5th year of King Rehoboam (1 Kings 14:25); at the Bubastite Portal at Karnak about 150 place names are listed (roughly fifty to sixty belong to Israel, a hundred to Judah). Josephus also relates that Shishak took Jerusalem and carried off the treasure. This campaign was Egypt's longest foreign operation of the Third Intermediate Period.
