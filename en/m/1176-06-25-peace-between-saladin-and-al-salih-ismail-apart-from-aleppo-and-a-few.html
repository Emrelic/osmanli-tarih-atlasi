Peace between Saladin and al-Salih Isma'il: apart from Aleppo and a few fortresses, the Zengid realm remained with Saladin

25 June 1176

A definitive agreement was reached (15 Dhu'l-Hijja 571 / 25 June 1176); under the treaty, to which the Anatolian Seljuks and the Artuqids were also party, al-Salih Isma'il was left with only Aleppo and a few fortresses.
