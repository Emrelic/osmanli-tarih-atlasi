The surrender of Silistra to Diebitsch — the Russian occupation that would last until 1836

30 June 1829

At the beginning of the campaign of 1829, the new Russian commander-in-chief Diebitsch chose Silistra as his first objective. The garrison resisted stubbornly the siege works directed by General Schilder; Diebitsch left part of his forces at the siege and marched behind the grand vizier. The siege continued and the fortress surrendered. The Russians kept Silistra in their hands until 1836, even after the war had ended.
