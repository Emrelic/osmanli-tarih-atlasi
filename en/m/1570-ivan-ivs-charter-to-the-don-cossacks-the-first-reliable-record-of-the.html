Ivan IV's charter to the Don Cossacks — the first reliable record of the Don Cossack Host

1570

Information about the presence of Cossacks on the Don begins in the middle of the sixteenth century. According to the Russian military encyclopedia, the seniority of the Don Cossack Host is counted from 1570: in that year Tsar Ivan IV sent a charter (gramota) to the Cossacks living on the Northern Donets, asking them to help Ivan Novosiltsev, who was being sent via Azov as an envoy to the Ottoman sultan, and promising to reward them in return. This is the first reliable record of the service of the Don Cossacks to the Muscovite state. The main centre of the Cossacks was first Upper Razdory and then Cherkassk.
