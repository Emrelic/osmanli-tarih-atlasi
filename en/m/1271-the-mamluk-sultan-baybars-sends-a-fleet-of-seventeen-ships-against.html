The Mamluk Sultan Baybars sends a fleet of seventeen ships against Cyprus; the attack fails because of a storm

1271

In 1271, while Baybars was taking the last fortresses and cities of the Crusaders in Palestine and Syria, he sent a fleet of seventeen ships against Cyprus, but the attack failed because of a storm that arose.
