The occupation of Monastir by Serbian forces

18 November 1912

In the First Balkan War, Monastir, one of the most important administrative and military centres of Macedonia, passed into the hands of Serbian forces after five days of fighting. The loss of the city meant the effective end of the Ottoman State's presence in Macedonia.
