Preveza taken from the French — Battle of Nicopolis

23 October 1798

When the Republic of Venice was destroyed in 1797, the Ionian islands and the mainland Preveza, Parga and Vonitsa fell into French hands. Ali Pasha of Tepelena, governor of Ioannina, exploiting the war opened by the French invasion of Egypt, marched on Preveza and inflicted a heavy defeat on the French garrison at the ruins of Nicopolis outside the city. Thus this port, left to Venice at Passarowitz, came under Ottoman administration eighty years later. Butrint fell on 25 October when the French garrison blew up the fortifications and withdrew to Corfu (Curlin, Preveza B, 2010; the date of Preveza is taken as the basis on the map) and came under Ottoman administration.
