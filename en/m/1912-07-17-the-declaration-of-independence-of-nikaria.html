The declaration of independence of Nikaria

17 July 1912

On the small island of Nikaria between Samos and Chios, the people, in the climate of uncertainty created by the Italian occupations, rose up and expelled the Ottoman gendarmerie from the island, and declared a 'free state' lasting five months with their own flag, stamp and assembly. In November 1912 the island was united with Greece. Despite its smallness, this event is a clear indication of how central administration was dissolving in the Aegean.
