At Világos the Hungarian army surrendered to the Russians — the Hungarian Revolution was suppressed

13 August 1849

Görgei, encircled by the Russian intervention, surrendered to the Russian forces at Világos after Kossuth handed authority over to him (11 August); this is regarded as the effective end of the revolution. Kossuth and his comrades took refuge with the Ottomans (see 16 August 1849).
