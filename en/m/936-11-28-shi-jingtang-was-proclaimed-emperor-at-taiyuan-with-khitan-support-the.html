Shi Jingtang was proclaimed emperor at Taiyuan with Khitan support: the Later Jin was founded

28 November 936

Shi Jingtang, the military governor of Taiyuan under the Later Tang, with the help of the Khitan ruler Taizong, was crowned as emperor of the 'Great Jin' at an altar erected south of Taiyuan (Jinyang); the text of Khitan Taizong's edict addresses him as 'my son, Prince of Jin'.
