Abu Tahir hands the administration of Bahrain to a Persian whom he presented as the mahdi; the new mahdi is killed eighty days later

24 January 931

Abu Tahir al-Jannabi, who had taken Oman (318/930), the following year handed the administration of Bahrain to a Persian whom he had brought from Isfahan and presented to the people as the mahdi. When the Persian presented himself as a descendant of the ancient Persian Khosraus and a reformer of the Persian religion and put forward ideas against the Arabs, he was killed by Abu Tahir eighty days after being installed; this caused great bewilderment and dispersal among the Bahrain Qarmatians.
