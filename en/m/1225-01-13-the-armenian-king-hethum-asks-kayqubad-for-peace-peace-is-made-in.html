The Armenian King Hethum asks Kayqubad for peace; peace is made in return for an annual tribute of 40,000 gold pieces and military aid

13 January 1225

After merchants' complaints about the Franks and the Armenians, Kayqubad declared war in 622 (1225) on the Armenians and the Crusaders; the Armenian King Hethum sent an envoy and asked for peace. The king would pay the sultan 40,000 gold pieces every year and, whenever the sultan wished, send 1,000 horsemen and 500 crossbowmen.
