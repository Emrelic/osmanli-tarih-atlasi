Kemankeş Kara Mustafa Pasha became grand vizier — the period of fiscal reform began

24 December 1638

Kemankeş Kara Mustafa Pasha, who became grand vizier on 17 Sha'ban 1048 (24 December 1638) after Tayyar Mehmed Pasha fell as a martyr in the Baghdad Campaign, applied harsh fiscal discipline in the period of Sultan Ibrahim: he reduced the numbers of Janissaries and sipahis by two thirds, corrected the coinage standard, and tightened the narh (price-control) system. By the figure the TDV gives, this saving reached 6,000 purses a year — but this strict control earned him enemies in palace circles.
