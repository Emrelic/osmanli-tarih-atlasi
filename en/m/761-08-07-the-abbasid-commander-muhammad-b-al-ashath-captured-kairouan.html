The Abbasid commander Muhammad b. al-Ash'ath captured Kairouan

7 August 761

After the appointment of Abd al-Rahman b. Rustam as governor there, the city was captured by Muhammad b. al-Ash'ath al-Khuza'i, whom Caliph al-Mansur had appointed to the governorship of Ifriqiya and the Maghrib (Jumada I 144).
