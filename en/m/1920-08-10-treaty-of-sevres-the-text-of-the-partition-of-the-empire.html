Treaty of Sèvres: the text of the partition of the empire

10 August 1920

The text that the Ottoman government signed with the Allied powers and that effectively liquidated the empire. Provisions: Eastern Thrace and the region of Izmir to Greece; an independent Armenia and an autonomous Kurdistan envisaged in Eastern Anatolia; Syria left to France, and Iraq and Palestine to Britain, as mandates; the Straits handed over to an international commission; the army reduced to 50,700 men, the capitulations restored, finances placed under Allied control. The Grand National Assembly in Ankara did NOT RECOGNIZE the treaty AT ALL and declared its signatories traitors to the homeland; the text did not enter into force and was replaced in 1923 by Lausanne. This is the reason it has no counterpart on the map — Sèvres was never legally implemented.
