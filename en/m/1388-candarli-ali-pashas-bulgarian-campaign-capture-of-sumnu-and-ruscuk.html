Çandarlı Ali Pasha's Bulgarian campaign: capture of Şumnu and Rusçuk

1388

The fortresses of the Bulgarian Tsardom along the Danube were taken over in a single campaign; it should not be confused with the defeat suffered at Bileća in Bosnia in the same year — that was a simultaneous success on a separate front. Tırnova and Vidin were the last Bulgarian centres still held.
