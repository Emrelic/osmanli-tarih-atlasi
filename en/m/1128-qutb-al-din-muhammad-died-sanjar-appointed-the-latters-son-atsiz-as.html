Qutb al-Din Muhammad died; Sanjar appointed the latter's son Atsiz as Khwarazm-Shah by diploma

1128

Upon the death of Qutb al-Din Muhammad, Sanjar appointed the latter's son Atsiz as Khwarazm-Shah by means of a diploma (1128-1156); Atsiz seized regions such as Jand and Mangyshlak and extended his influence beyond the Syr Darya.
