Kilij Arslan II dies during the siege of Aksaray; Kaykhusraw I succeeds him

26 August 1192

Kilij Arslan II, who was besieging Aksaray with Ghiyath al-Din Kaykhusraw during his struggle with his sons, fell ill and died on 15 Sha'ban 588 / 26 August 1192; Kaykhusraw took the throne at Konya.
