The proclamation of Albania's independence — İsmail Kemal in Valona

28 November 1912

As the armies of the Balkan War entered the Albanian vilayets, İsmail Kemal proclaimed Albania's independence in Valona. The coastal and inland territory extending from Durrës, Elbasan, Berat, Gjirokastër and Krujë to Ayasaranda thus left Ottoman rule and formed the nucleus of the new Albanian state.
