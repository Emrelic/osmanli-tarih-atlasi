The Samanid emir Isma'il b. Ahmad captured Talas (Taraz); the Samanids reached their widest frontiers in the east

23 March 893

To protect Transoxiana against the raids of the non-Muslim Turks, Isma'il b. Ahmad launched a campaign extending as far as Taraz (Talas) and captured the city; most of the Turkish emirs and dihqans there became Muslims, and Oghulchaq, who lost Talas, the center of the Eastern Qarakhanids, moved the capital to Kashgar.
