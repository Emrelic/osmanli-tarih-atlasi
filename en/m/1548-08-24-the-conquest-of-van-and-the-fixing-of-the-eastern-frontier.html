The conquest of Van and the fixing of the eastern frontier

24 August 1548

The fortress of Van, besieged on the return from Tabriz, was, with its position rising on a rock on the eastern shore of the lake, the undisputed key to the region, and it surrendered after a short siege. The city was immediately made the seat of a beylerbeylik; the sancaks of Erciş, Adilcevaz and Hakkâri were attached to it. Unlike Tabriz, Van was retained and remained for four hundred years the Ottomans' most advanced permanent position in the east.
