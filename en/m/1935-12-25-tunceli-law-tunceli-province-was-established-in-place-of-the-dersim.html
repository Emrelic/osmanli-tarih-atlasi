Tunceli Law — Tunceli province was established in place of the Dersim sanjak (center Kalan)

25 December 1935

By Law No. 2885 it was decided to establish a new province called Tunceli in the Dersim region; the administrative center of the province was the settlement of Kalan, which later took the name Tunceli. The region remained within the Republic of Turkey; what changed was the administrative status and name.
