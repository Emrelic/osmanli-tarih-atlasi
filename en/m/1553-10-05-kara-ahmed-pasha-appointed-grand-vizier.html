Kara Ahmed Pasha appointed grand vizier

5 October 1553

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Kara Ahmed Pasha, Kara Ahmed Pasha, who was serving as second vizier, was promoted to the grand vizierate on 27 Shawwal 960 (5 October 1553) after the sudden dismissal of the existing grand vizier. This change points to a period, coinciding with the days of Prince Mustafa's execution, in which the balances of power within the palace changed hands rapidly.
