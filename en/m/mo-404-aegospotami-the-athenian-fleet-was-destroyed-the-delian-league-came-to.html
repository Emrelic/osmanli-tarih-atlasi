Aegospotami: the Athenian fleet was destroyed; the Delian League came to the brink of collapse

404 BC

In September 405 Lysander seized by a surprise attack the Athenian fleet moored at Aegospotami; almost the whole fleet and thousands of prisoners were lost. The Hellespont route was closed and Athens's grain supply was cut off; a few months later Athens surrendered.
