The Russian 6th Infantry Corps crossed the Prut and entered the Danubian Principalities (Ottoman–Russian War of 1828–1829)

7 May 1828

Following Russia's declaration of war, Field Marshal Wittgenstein's army went into operation. The 6th Infantry Corps crossed the Prut and entered Moldavia and Wallachia, its vanguard under General Geismar heading for Lesser Wallachia. A few days later the 7th Corps besieged Brăila, while the 3rd Corps prepared to cross the Danube between Izmail and Reni. The Danubian Principalities would remain under Russian occupation until 1834.
