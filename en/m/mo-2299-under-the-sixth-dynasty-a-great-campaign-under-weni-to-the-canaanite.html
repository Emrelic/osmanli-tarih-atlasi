Under the Sixth Dynasty, a great campaign under Weni to the Canaanite frontier with Nubian troops

2299 BC

According to Weni's autobiography, the king gathered an army of tens of thousands against the 'Asiatic Sand-dwellers'; the army was composed of troops levied from Southern Egypt (as far as Elephantine), from the North, and from the Nubian tribes (Irtjet, Medja, Yam, Wawat, Kau). The text shows that the Old Kingdom used Nubia as a pool of soldiers and could mount a campaign as far as Southern Canaan.
