Mursili II defeated and partitioned Arzawa (western Anatolia)

1317 BC

At the beginning of his reign Mursili II defeated the powerful Arzawa confederation of western Anatolia, divided it into sub-kingdoms and made it a Hittite vassal. The Hittite frontier in western Anatolia approached the Aegean coast.
