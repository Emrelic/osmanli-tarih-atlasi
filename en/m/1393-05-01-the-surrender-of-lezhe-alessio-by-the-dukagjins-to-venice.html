The surrender of Lezhë (Alessio) by the Dukagjins to Venice

1 May 1393

Paul and Lek Dukagjin, who had held Lezhë (Alessio) since 1387 as the center of the Albanian Principality of Dukagjin, seeing that they could not defend this port at the mouth of the Drin against Ottoman pressure, ceded the fortress to Venice and withdrew to the mountainous region east of the city. The city then remained in Venetian hands until it was taken in 1478, during Mehmed the Conqueror's Shkodër campaign, by Dâvud Pasha, the beylerbeyi of Rumelia — both Skanderbeg's convening of the anti-Ottoman alliance (Lidhja e Lezhës) here on 1 March 1444 and his death here on 17 January 1468 took place in a Venetian fortress.
