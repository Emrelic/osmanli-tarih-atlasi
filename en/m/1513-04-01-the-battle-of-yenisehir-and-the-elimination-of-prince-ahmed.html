The Battle of Yenişehir and the Elimination of Prince Ahmed

1 April 1513

Selim the Grim defeated his elder brother Prince Ahmed, the strongest rival for the throne, on the plain of Yenişehir near Bursa, and removed him by taking him prisoner. Thus the fraternal strife that had continued since 1511 came to an end, and the internal division in Anatolia was over. Having secured his rear, Selim gained the freedom to turn to the Safavid question.
