Passage of Assab into Italian state ownership — the foundation of Italy's first colony

10 March 1882

The Red Sea port of Assab had been purchased in 1869 from local Afar leaders by a private Italian shipping company (Rubattino); with Britain's approval, the Italian government assumed direct state ownership of the port on 10 March 1882, and with a law enacted in July formally declared Assab Italy's first colony. This was the starting point of Italy's expansion along the Red Sea coast, which would later turn into the Colony of Eritrea (1890).
