Failure of the siege of Aden: first Red Sea attempt against Portugal

2 August 1516

Selman Reis and Hüseyin Bey conducted a siege lasting eight days to take Aden, the gate of the Red Sea opening onto the Indian Ocean, but were forced to withdraw in the face of the city's strong fortifications. The failure to take Aden would for many years obstruct the southern strategy to be conducted against Portugal. This attempt is the Ottomans' first test in oceanic policy.
