The Mykytyn Rih Sich is first mentioned

1628

The name of the fourth Sich, founded at Mykytyn Rih on the site of present-day Nikopol, first appears in 1628; it was captured by Bohdan Khmelnytsky in 1648. This is the first known testimony of the place, not its foundation.
