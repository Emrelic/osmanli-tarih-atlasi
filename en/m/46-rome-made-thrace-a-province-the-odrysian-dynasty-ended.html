Rome made Thrace a province: the Odrysian dynasty ended

46

After the killing of Rhoemetalces III, King of Thrace, by his wife and the ensuing disorder, Claudius turned Thrace into a Roman province in 46. It is one of the last steps completing Rome's provincial order in the east of the Balkans.
