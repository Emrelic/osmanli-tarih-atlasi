Mentuhotep II defeated Herakleopolis and reunified Egypt (Sematawy)

2015 BC

Toward the 39th year of his reign Mentuhotep II eliminated Herakleopolis, bringing Upper and Lower Egypt under a single government, and added to his titulary the epithet 'Uniter of the Two Lands' (Sematawy). The central administration moved to Thebes; the powers of the nomarchs were curtailed.
