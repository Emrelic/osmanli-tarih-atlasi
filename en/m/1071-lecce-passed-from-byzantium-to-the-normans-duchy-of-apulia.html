Lecce passed from Byzantium to the Normans: Duchy of Apulia

1071

With the Norman conquest of Bari in 1071, Byzantium's last stronghold in Italy fell; Lecce, tied to the Bari chain, was joined to the duchy of Apulia (no dated sentence specific to the city was found).
