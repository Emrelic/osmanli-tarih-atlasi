Hewett (Adwa) Treaty: Bogos ceded to Abyssinia

3 June 1884

When the Mahdist uprising besieged the Egyptian garrisons in the Sudan, Britain, in order to obtain Abyssinia's help, signed a treaty with Yohannes IV at Adwa on 3 June 1884. The second article of the treaty gave Bogos back to Abyssinia, in return for which Abyssinia would facilitate the evacuation of the besieged garrisons via Massawa. Keren thus left twelve years of Egyptian administration; five years later the region would be joined to Italian Eritrea.
