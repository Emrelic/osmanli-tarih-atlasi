Sökmen dies; his son Ibrahim succeeds him

1 October 1104

Sökmen, who fell ill shortly after the victory at Harran, died in October 1104; his son Ibrahim succeeded to the Hisn Kayfa branch, and on Ibrahim's death in 1108 his brother Dawud became Artuqid ruler.
