The Assyrian army plundered Thebes; Tantamani withdrew to the south, and Kushite rule in Egypt collapsed

662 BC

The armies of Ashurbanipal pursued Tantamani from Memphis as far as Thebes and plundered the city; the temple treasures of Thebes were carried to Nineveh. Tantamani withdrew to Nubia; Kushite rule in Upper Egypt dissolved. This plundering is mentioned in the Old Testament as 'No-Amon' (Nahum 3:8).
