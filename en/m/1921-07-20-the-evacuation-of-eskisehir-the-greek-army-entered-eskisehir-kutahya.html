The evacuation of Eskişehir — the Greek army entered Eskişehir, Kütahya and Bilecik

20 July 1921

The Turkish army, caught unprepared in the Kütahya–Eskişehir battles, withdrew east of the Sakarya on the order of Mustafa Kemal Pasha. According to the TDV articles, the Greeks entered Eskişehir on 20 July 1921, Kütahya on 21 July, and Bilecik on 22 July. The Greek King Constantine presided over a war council at Kütahya on 28 July, and the army prepared to attack in the direction of Ankara.
