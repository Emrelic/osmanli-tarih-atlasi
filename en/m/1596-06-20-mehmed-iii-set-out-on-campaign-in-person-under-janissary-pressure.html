Mehmed III set out on campaign in person under Janissary pressure

20 June 1596

Despite the opposition of Grand Vizier Damad Ibrahim Pasha, under the pressure of the Janissaries Mehmed III set out from Istanbul on campaign on 20 June 1596 as the first sultan since Süleyman the Lawgiver to take command of the army in person. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, this meant the revival of the old tradition of ghaza.
