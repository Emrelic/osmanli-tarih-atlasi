Mehmed IV has cirit played for days at the circumcision festivities in Edirne

1675

Mehmed IV arranged cirit contests lasting for days at the circumcision festivities of his princes held in Edirne in 1675; this is specially mentioned in the TDV's Cirit entry as one of the most spectacular examples of the Ottoman court's support of the sport of cirit.
