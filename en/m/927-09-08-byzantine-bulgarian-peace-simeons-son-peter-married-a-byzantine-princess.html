Byzantine-Bulgarian peace: Simeon's son Peter married a Byzantine princess

8 September 927

With the peace signed in the autumn of 927 after Simeon's death, Simeon's son and successor Peter married Maria Lekapene, granddaughter of Romanos I; Byzantium recognized him as 'emperor of the Bulgarians' and also accepted the independent Bulgarian Patriarchate. A long period of peace began between the two states.
