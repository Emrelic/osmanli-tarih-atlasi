Acceptance of the title Khadim al-Haramayn and khutba in Medina

12 July 1517

After the second reception of the Meccan delegation, Selim the Grim officially took upon himself the protection of the Haramayn and began to use the title 'servant of the two noble sanctuaries' (hadim al-Haramayn al-sharifayn). Sharif Barakat put on the robe of honour (hil'at) sent to him and had the khutba read in the sultan's name; with the Medinan delegation's going to Istanbul, Barakat's emirate was confirmed by a diploma (berat). The sultan sent the people of the Haramayn two hundred thousand gold pieces and abundant provisions, beginning the tradition of the surre to be repeated every year. Other places joined on the same date: Yanbu, Tabuk.
