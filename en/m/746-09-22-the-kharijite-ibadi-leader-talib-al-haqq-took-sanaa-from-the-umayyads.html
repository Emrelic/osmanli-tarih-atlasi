The Kharijite (Ibadi) leader Talib al-Haqq took Sana'a from the Umayyads after Hadramawt (129/746)

22 September 746

Abdallah b. Yahya al-Hadrami (Talib al-Haqq) rose against the Umayyads with the support of the Ibadis of Oman and proclaimed himself caliph; after Hadramawt he seized Sana'a. He then marched on Mecca and Medina, was defeated by the forces of Marwan II and was killed near Sana'a (130/748).
