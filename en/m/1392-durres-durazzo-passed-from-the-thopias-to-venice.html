Durrës (Durazzo) passed from the Thopias to Venice

1392

Charles Thopia's son George Thopia ceded Durrës, which Charles had taken in 1368, to Venice in 1392 (TDV Draç: 'was ceded to Venice by Thopia's son George', no year); Venice confirmed the city's statutes in August 1392. Venice held Durrës until the Ottomans took it in 1501. Only the year is known; the day is unconfirmed.
