The plague epidemic that began in the 1570s continued to affect Istanbul

1 June 1600

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Tâun, the epidemic that began in the 1570s continued its effect until the beginning of the 17th century and struck Anatolia, the Balkans, Egypt and North Africa, above all Istanbul. Periods of epidemic constituted a chronic threat that frequently interrupted palace and city life.
