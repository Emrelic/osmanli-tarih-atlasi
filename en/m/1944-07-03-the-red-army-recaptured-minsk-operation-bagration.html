The Red Army recaptured Minsk — Operation Bagration

3 July 1944

In Operation Bagration, launched on 22 June, the Belorussian fronts broke through Army Group Center and encircled Minsk; the city was recaptured on 3 July, and nearly 50,000 German soldiers remained encircled in the east. The German civil administration of Belarus (Generalbezirk Weißruthenien) collapsed.
