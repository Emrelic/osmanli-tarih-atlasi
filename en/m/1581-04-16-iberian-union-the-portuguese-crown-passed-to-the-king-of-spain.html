Iberian Union: the Portuguese crown passed to the King of Spain

16 April 1581

The succession struggle opened by the childless death of Cardinal-King Henrique was closed in Spain's favor at the Battle of Alcântara (25 August 1580), and the Portuguese Cortes assembled at Tomar recognized Philip II as King of Portugal on 16 April 1581. According to the terms of Tomar, Portugal would keep its own institutions, currency and language, only Portuguese would be appointed to the administration, and there would be a separate Portuguese council in Madrid — that is, the union was a personal union, not an annexation.
