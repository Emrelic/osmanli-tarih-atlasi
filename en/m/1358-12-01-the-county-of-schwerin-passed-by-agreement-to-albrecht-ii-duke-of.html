The County of Schwerin passed by agreement to Albrecht II, Duke of Mecklenburg

1 December 1358

By a contract dated 1 December 1358, Albrecht II took the County of Schwerin by paying Count Nicolaus 10,000 marks of silver; from that date the city became land of the duchy of Mecklenburg (its settling as the capital became permanent in the 16th century).
