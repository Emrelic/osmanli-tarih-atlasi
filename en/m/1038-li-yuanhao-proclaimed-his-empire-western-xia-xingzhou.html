Li Yuanhao proclaimed his empire (Western Xia, Xingzhou)

1038

After the Tangut ruler Li Yuanhao had seized the prefectures of Xia, Yin, Sui, You, Jing, Ling, Yan, Hui, Sheng, Gan, Liang, Gua, Sha and Su, he stayed at Xingzhou (later Xingqing) and established an order resting on the river and the Helan Mountains; in order to attack the Fuyan region of the Song, he swore a blood oath with the tribal chiefs, had an altar built, and became emperor (at the age of 30).
