Necho II entered Syria-Palestine; he defeated Josiah at Megiddo and advanced toward the Euphrates

608 BC

Necho II, aiming to seize the western provinces of the collapsing Assyria, marched on Syria; on his way he had Josiah, king of Judah, killed at Megiddo. Herodotus says 'Necho defeated the Syrians at Magdolus and took Kadytis, the great Syrian city'. Thus for a short time Egypt controlled Syria-Palestine as far as the Euphrates.
