Death of Süleyman Pasha, conqueror of Rumelia

1 September 1357

Süleyman Pasha, Orhan Bey's eldest son and the architect of the conquests in Rumelia, died in the autumn of 1357, the most widely accepted of the dates given in the sources as 758 (1357), 759 and 760 (1359), falling from his horse during a hunt. The death of the prince, who established the permanent Ottoman presence in Rumelia through his crossings between 1352 and 1354, left the order of succession to his brother Murad. The tomb of Süleyman Pasha, buried at Bolayır, remained the oldest dynastic monument of the Ottomans in Europe.
