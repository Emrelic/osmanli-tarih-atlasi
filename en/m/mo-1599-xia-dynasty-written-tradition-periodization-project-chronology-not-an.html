Xia dynasty (written tradition / Periodization Project chronology — NOT an archaeological date): the Xia ends, the Shang begins (written tradition)

1599 BC

In the traditional periodization the Xia is ~2070-1600 BCE (471 years). This date is the product of a written tradition similar to the Shiji/Zhushu Jinian and of the project's calculation; there is no contemporary written document.
