Emir Süleyman crossed into Anatolia: Bursa fell to him

1 March 1404

Hearing of the defeat of Îsâ Çelebi, Emir Süleyman crossed into Anatolia with Çandarlı Ali Pasha at the head of a strong army and seized Bursa; records of March 1404 show that Karadeniz Ereğli, too, was under his rule. Çelebi Mehmed could not resist Süleyman, who soon dominated both Rumelia and most of Anatolia. Other settlements that passed from Çelebi Mehmed to Emir Süleyman in the same process: Akyazı, Anadolu Hisarı, Absu (Hypsu), Adranos (Orhaneli), Akhisar (Pamukova), Armutlu, Aydos Castle, Ayvalık, Balıkesir, Behramkale (Assos), Bergama, Beykoz, Biga. On the map, from this date until Çelebi Mehmed's retaking of Bursa (1410), the two shores appear in a single colour, in Emir Süleyman's share.
