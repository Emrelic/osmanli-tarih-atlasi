Andrey Bogolyubsky is murdered by boyars at Bogolyubovo

1174

Andrey Bogolyubsky was murdered by his boyars at Bogolyubovo; a struggle for the throne began in 1174-1177 (the exact day is contradictory in the sources, 28/29 June).
