Fall of Laghouat: the opening of the gate to the Sahara

4 December 1852

General Pélissier besieged Laghouat with a force of six thousand men on 21 November 1852, and on 4 December the city fell to a bloody assault. Laghouat was the oasis town holding the passage between the Tell and the Sahara; its fall opened the French advance to the desert caravan routes and prepared the way for the Mzab confederation to be bound to taxation within a year.
