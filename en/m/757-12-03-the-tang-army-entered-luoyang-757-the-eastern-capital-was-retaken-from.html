The Tang army entered Luoyang (757): the Eastern Capital was retaken from the rebels

3 December 757

The rebel commander An Qingxu, defeated at the place called Xindian in Shaanxi, and his followers fled to Hebei; Prince Guangping entered Luoyang and drew up his army south of the Tianjin Bridge; more than 300 false officials, such as Chen Xilie and Zhang Ji, awaited their punishment in white garments.
