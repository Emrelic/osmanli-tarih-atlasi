The Austrian Bundesheer entered Burgenland — the territory Trianon left to Austria was actually taken over

13 November 1921

Trianon (1920) and Saint-Germain (1919) had left the German-speaking strip of Western Hungary to Austria; the first attempt at takeover on 28 August 1921 failed because of the resistance of Hungarian irregular units. After the Venice Protocol (13 October 1921), the Allied General Commission approved the entry of the Bundesheer on 11 November, and the takeover of northern Burgenland began two days later; the south was taken over on 6 December 1921, and the Sopron region remained in Hungary by plebiscite.
