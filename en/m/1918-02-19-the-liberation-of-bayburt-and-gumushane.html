The liberation of Bayburt and Gümüşhane

19 February 1918

In the advance that began with the abrogation of the Armistice of Erzincan, Kelkit was liberated on 17 February and Bayburt on 19 February; the Russian occupation of Gümüşhane ended on 28 February 1918.
