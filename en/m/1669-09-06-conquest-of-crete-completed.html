Conquest of Crete completed

6 September 1669

The siege of Candia, the last act of the Cretan war that began in 1645, concluded with the determined three-year operation of Fâzıl Ahmed Pasha; the Venetian commander Morosini surrendered the city by treaty. The struggle, lasting about a quarter of a century, went into the record as the longest siege in Ottoman history; the last great Latin fortress of the Eastern Mediterranean also came under Ottoman rule. Three small rocky fortresses on the northern coast of the island — Suda, Spinalonga and Grambousa — still remained with Venice; because they are established on separate islets, they cannot be shown on the map as separate from the rest of Crete.
