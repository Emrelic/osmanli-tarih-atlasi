The Loss of Revan to the Safavids

1 April 1636

After the withdrawal of the Ottoman army, the forces of Shah Safi besieged Revan and retook it within eight months. The fortress's being lost so quickly showed how difficult lasting dominion in the Caucasus was logistically. Revan would also be left in Iran by the 1639 Treaty of Qasr-i Shirin, and the Ottomans would give up their claim to Azerbaijan.
