Battle of the Granicus: Alexander defeated the Persian satraps

333 BC

Alexander, king of Macedonia, crossed into Asia in May 334 and defeated the Persian satraps at the Granicus in early June. The gates of western Anatolia were opened; Sardis and the cities of Ionia surrendered to Alexander.
