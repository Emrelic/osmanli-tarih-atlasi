Aurelian defeated the Gallic Empire: Rome was reunited

274

In 274 Aurelian defeated Tetricus at Catalaunum; Gaul, Britannia, and Hispania returned to Rome. The empire was reunified after 14 years of division, and Aurelian took the title "Restitutor Orbis" ("restorer of the world").
