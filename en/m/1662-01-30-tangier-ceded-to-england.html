Tangier ceded to England

30 January 1662

The second article of the marriage treaty (23 June 1661), made to secure English support in Portugal's war of independence, left Tangier to England as the dowry of Catherine of Braganza. The fleet of the Earl of Sandwich anchored on 29 January 1662, and the next day the official transfer was made with the ceremony of the Earl of Peterborough. The English would abandon the city in 1684.
