Hejaz campaign began — the army marched to Yanbu by sea from Suez and by land along the pilgrimage road

3 September 1811

Recovering the Haramayn, under Saudi-Wahhabi rule since 1803, was Mahmud II's greatest problem of legitimacy; since the power of the Sublime Porte was insufficient, the task was given to the governor of Egypt. Mehmed Ali sent his sixteen-year-old son Tosun at the head of the army.
