Bregenz passed to the Habsburgs: Hugo XVII sold the remainder of the Montfort-Bregenz property

1523

Half of Bregenz was sold to Duke Sigmund in 1451 and the remaining half to Archduke Ferdinand I in 1523; the map shows the change to full sovereignty in 1523, 1451-1523 being a period of half-sovereignty (no day was found).
