Renewal of the peace with the Habsburgs — the end of the war over Transylvania and Temeşvar

5 May 1562

The war, which had begun with the passing of Transylvania to Ferdinand in 1551 and was carried on through campaigns such as the conquest of Temeşvar and the siege of Eğri, was closed with the decision to continue the peace with the Habsburgs. The agreement mutually renewed the earlier commitments of the two sides; but it brought no solution to the disorders on the frontier, and after Ferdinand's death in 1564 events in Transylvania increased again.
