The Yedisan and Budjak Nogais passed under Russian protection

12 August 1770

While the siege of Bender continued, the Russian commander Panin signed an agreement with the notables of the Yedisan and Budjak Nogai hordes. The Nogais announced that they had separated from the Ottoman State and come under the protection of the Russian empress. About twelve thousand Nogais emigrated from Budjak; the hordes were settled in 1771-1772 on the right bank of the Kuban river. With the passing of the steppes north of the Crimea to the Russian side, the land road to the peninsula was opened.
