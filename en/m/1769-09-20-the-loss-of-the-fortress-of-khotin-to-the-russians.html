The Loss of the Fortress of Khotin to the Russians

20 September 1769

Although two attempts by the Russian army under Prince Golitsyn had been repulsed, the army withdrew upon the defeat the grand vizier suffered between Kamaniçe and Khotin, and the garrison evacuated the fortress. The loss without resistance of the strongest Ottoman fortification north of the Danube opened the gates of Wallachia and Moldavia to the Russians. The two principalities were occupied in the same year; Khotin could be recovered only in 1774 by the Treaty of Küçük Kaynarca.
