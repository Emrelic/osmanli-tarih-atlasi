The Treaty of San Stefano: The Greater Bulgaria Project

3 March 1878

The Treaty of San Stefano, which ended the '93 War, established a huge autonomous Bulgaria extending to the Aegean and including Macedonia; recognised the independence of Serbia, Montenegro and Romania; ceded Kars, Ardahan, Batum and Doğubayazıt to Russia; and in addition imposed a war indemnity impossible to pay. This text, which brought Russia down to the Mediterranean, drew the fierce reaction of England and Austria. Before being put into effect it was rearranged within four months by the Congress of Berlin; nonetheless it remained the document that proclaimed the end of Ottoman Rumelia.
