Anglo-Qatari Treaty — Qatar came under British protection

3 November 1916

During the First World War, by the treaty signed on 3 November 1916 between Major Percy Cox, the British political resident in the Persian Gulf, and Sheikh Abdullah bin Jassim Al Thani of Qatar, Qatar agreed to make its foreign relations subject to British approval, in return receiving the guarantee of British protection against attacks by sea; this treaty formed the legal basis of Qatar's relationship of British protection, which would last until 1971, and was formally ratified in 1918.
