Bitlis passed under Safavid protection — Ulama and Fil Pasha lifted the siege

1532

When Tekelü Ulama took refuge with the Ottomans and was appointed beylerbeyi over the land of Bitlis, Şeref Khan, the Ottoman-subject ruler of Bitlis, took refuge with the Safavids. Ulama and Fil Pasha, the Beylerbeyi of Diyarbekir, besieged the fortress in the year 938; the fortress held out for three months, and the Ottoman forces, learning that Shah Tahmasb was approaching, lifted the siege. The Shah attached Bitlis and the districts of Ahlat, Muş and Hınıs to Şeref Khan.
