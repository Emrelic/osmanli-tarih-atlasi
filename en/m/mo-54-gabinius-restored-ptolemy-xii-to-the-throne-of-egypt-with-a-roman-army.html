Gabinius restored Ptolemy XII to the throne of Egypt with a Roman army; a Roman garrison remained at Alexandria

54 BC

The exiled Ptolemy XII (Auletes) bought entry into Egypt by paying the Syrian governor Gabinius a large bribe; Gabinius abandoned his Parthian campaign, entered Egypt from Pelusium, and brought Auletes back to the throne. This is the moment when Egypt's independence became in effect dependent on Roman guarantee. Gabinius was later tried in Rome.
