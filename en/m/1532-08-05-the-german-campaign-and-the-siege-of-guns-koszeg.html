The German Campaign and the Siege of Güns (Kőszeg)

5 August 1532

Süleyman the Lawgiver marched with his army against Austria in order to force Charles V into a pitched battle; the small fortress of Güns resisted for twenty-five days and delayed the army's advance. Since the emperor did not come out to fight, the raiders (akıncı) struck the environs of Vienna, and the army turned back as the season advanced. The campaign brought no territorial gain, but by bringing the Habsburgs to the table it prepared the ground for the following year's peace.
