Battle of Ayn Salm: Sulayman Shah was defeated and killed by Tutush, the Seljuk ruler of Syria

4 June 1086

Sulayman Shah, who was besieging Aleppo, was routed when Emir Chubuk went over to the other side in the army of Tutush facing him, and through the manoeuvres of Artuk Beg (18 Safar 479 / 4 June 1086); Sulayman Shah died in the battle or by suicide and was buried in the Aleppo cemetery.
