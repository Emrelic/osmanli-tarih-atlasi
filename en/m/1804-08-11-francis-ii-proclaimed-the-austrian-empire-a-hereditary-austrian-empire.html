Francis II proclaimed the Austrian Empire — a hereditary Austrian empire

11 August 1804

In response to Napoleon's becoming emperor of the French, Francis II issued a patent and took the title of hereditary emperor of Austria for the Habsburg lands. Since he still held the Holy Roman crown, the two imperial titles ran side by side until 1806.
