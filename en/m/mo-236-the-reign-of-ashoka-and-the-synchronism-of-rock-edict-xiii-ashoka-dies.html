The reign of Ashoka and the synchronism of Rock Edict XIII: Ashoka dies

236 BC

The reign of Ashoka: according to CHI 1922, accession ≤274, coronation ≤270, conquest of Kalinga ≤262, death 237/236 BC. Rock Edict XIII names five Hellenistic kings as living (Antiochus, Ptolemy, Antigonus, Magas, Alexander); because of Magas's death in 258, CHI provisionally places the edict at 'about 258–257 BC'.
