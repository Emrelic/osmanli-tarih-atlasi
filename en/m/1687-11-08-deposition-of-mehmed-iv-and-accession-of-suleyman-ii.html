Deposition of Mehmed IV and accession of Süleyman II

8 November 1687

Following the disasters at the front and the mutiny of the army, Mehmed IV was deposed after a reign of thirty-nine years; in his place his brother Süleyman II, who had been kept in the cage for forty years, was brought to the throne.
