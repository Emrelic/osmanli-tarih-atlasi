Peace of Caltabellotta: Reggio Calabria returned from Aragon-Sicily to the Angevins (Naples)

31 August 1302

The Peace of Caltabellotta (31 August 1302) brought Frederick III's renunciation of his mainland Calabrian possessions and the island's remaining with the Aragonese line as the Kingdom of Trinacria; Reggio passed to the Angevin Kingdom of Naples.
