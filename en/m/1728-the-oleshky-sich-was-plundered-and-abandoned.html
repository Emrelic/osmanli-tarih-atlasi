The Oleshky Sich was plundered and abandoned

1728

The pro-Russian Samara Cossacks raided and plundered the Oleshky Sich and set the barracks on fire. The Sich was not re-established; the Cossacks returned to Kamianka in 1730 and in 1734 swore allegiance to Russia and founded the New Sich.
