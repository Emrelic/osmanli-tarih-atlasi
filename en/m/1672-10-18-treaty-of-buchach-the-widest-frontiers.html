Treaty of Buchach — the widest frontiers

18 October 1672

With the Treaty of Buchach, signed after the conquest of Kamianets in the Polish campaign, Podolia was annexed to the Ottomans and part of Ukraine was taken under protection. The empire thus reached its widest frontiers in Europe; this point is regarded as the zenith and at the same time the last link of the age of expansion.
