Murad III issues a decree for the writing of the Hünername

2 January 1578

By a decree dated 2 January 1578, Murad III ordered Seyyid Lokman to write the Hünername, which takes as its subject the lives of the sultans from Osman Gazi to his own time; the work's miniatures were prepared by Nakkaş Osman and his team.
