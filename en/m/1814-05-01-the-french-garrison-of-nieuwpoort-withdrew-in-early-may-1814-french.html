The French garrison of Nieuwpoort withdrew in early May 1814: French rule in West Flanders ended

1 May 1814

After the armistice of 12 April 1814, Ostend, Diksmuide, Nieuwpoort and Ypres had remained in French hands; the rest of the Lys (West Flanders) department was evacuated by the French in early May, before the first Peace of Paris (30 May 1814). The region remained attached to the General Government of Belgium until 1 August 1814.
