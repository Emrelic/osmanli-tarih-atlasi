The Đinh dynasty ended; Lê Hoàn ascended the throne of Đại Cồ Việt (Hall: Đinh 968-79, Lê 979-1009)

979

According to Hall, the independent kingdom of Annam-Tonkin continued after the Ngô dynasty with the Đinh dynasty (968-79); the Đinh was very short-lived. The Early Lê dynasty (979-1009) began strongly; its first king, Lê Hoàn, invaded Champa in 982. The capital is Hoa-lu.
