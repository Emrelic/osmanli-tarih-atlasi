Advance into Macedonia: conquest of Monastir and Ohrid

1385

Monastir (Bitola) on the Via Egnatia and Ohrid on the lakeshore came under Ottoman administration during the raids in the direction of Albania. Both were key points on the road extending to the Adriatic; Ohrid was moreover the oldest Orthodox archiepiscopal centre of the Balkans. TDV records that there is no certain information on the date of the conquest of Ohrid, and that the dates 787 (1385) and 797 (1395), after the death of Marko Kraljević, have been put forward; 1385 has been taken as the basis here. In the following centuries Monastir was to become one of the most important military centres of Rumelia.
