The Treaty of Istanbul — The Ceding of Azov to Russia

14 July 1700

At Karlowitz only a two-year armistice could be made with Russia; the lasting peace was signed in Istanbul after difficult negotiations lasting a year and a half. The Ottoman State officially relinquished to Russia the Fortress of Azov, which it had lost in 1696, and the lands around it, and gave up the annual tax the Crimean khan received from Moscow. In return, Russia obtained the right to keep a permanent envoy in Istanbul; thus the Russian presence on the northern shore of the Black Sea was legally registered.
