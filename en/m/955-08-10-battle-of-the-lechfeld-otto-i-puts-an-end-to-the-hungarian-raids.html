Battle of the Lechfeld: Otto I puts an end to the Hungarian raids

10 August 955

Otto marched against the Hungarians besieging Augsburg and, with the great victory he won at the Lechfeld on 10 August 955, delivered Germany from the Hungarian raids; in the same year he also defeated the Slavs.
