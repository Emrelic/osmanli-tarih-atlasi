The destruction of the Russian battalion at Salyan — Tahmasb's troops entered the mouth of the Kura

11 September 1724

The officers of the Russian battalion that had settled at the mouth of the Kura after the capture of Baku were killed at a banquet given by the naib of Salyan; the remaining force withdrew to Baku, and a force of about four thousand men of Tahmasb II entered Salyan. The Russians were able to return to the region only a few years later, by appointing the naib themselves. It is one of the rare occasions during the occupation on which the Russians were repelled by force of arms inside Iran.
