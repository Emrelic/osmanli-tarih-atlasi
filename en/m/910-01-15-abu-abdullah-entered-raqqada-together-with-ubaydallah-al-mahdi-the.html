Abu Abdullah entered Raqqada together with Ubaydallah al-Mahdi; the Fatimid imamate effectively began

15 January 910

Abu Abdullah captured Raqqada, the capital of the Aghlabids, and expelled the last emir, Ziyadatallah (296/909); on 29 Rabi' al-Akhir 297 (15 January 910) he entered Raqqada together with Ubaydallah.
