Leuctra: the Theban army defeated the Spartans; Sparta lost its land supremacy

370 BC

Epaminondas's deep massing of the right wing routed the Spartan force of King Cleombrotus at Leuctra; with the death of about 400 Spartiates, Sparta became unable to make good its loss of population. A period of hegemony under Theban leadership began.
