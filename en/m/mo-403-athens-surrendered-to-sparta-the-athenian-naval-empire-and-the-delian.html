Athens surrendered to Sparta; the Athenian naval empire and the Delian League came to an end

403 BC

Athens, having lost its fleet at Aegospotami, surrendered through starvation; the Long Walls were demolished, the naval empire was dissolved, and the rule of the Thirty, installed by the Spartans, came to the city. In the islands and on the Thracian coasts that had been members of the League, decarchies established by Lysander and Spartan garrisons were installed.
