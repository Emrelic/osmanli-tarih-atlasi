The Kirman Seljuks and the forces of Fars were defeated by the Oghuz; the Oghuz plundered Jiruft

8 June 1179

A large group of the Oghuz who had captured Sultan Sanjar entered Kirman; Turan Shah II came out against the Oghuz with the help of the Salghurid Tekele, ruler of Fars, but was heavily defeated (575/1179); the Oghuz moved south and plundered Jiruft, and the next year a great famine broke out in Kirman.
