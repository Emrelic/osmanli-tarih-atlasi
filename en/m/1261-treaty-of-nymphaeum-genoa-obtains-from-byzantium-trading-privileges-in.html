Treaty of Nymphaeum: Genoa obtains from Byzantium trading privileges in the Black Sea and the eastern Mediterranean

1261

By the Treaty of Nymphaeum signed in 1261 with the Byzantine emperor Michael VIII, Genoa obtained extensive privileges in the Black Sea and the eastern Mediterranean; this treaty is the foundation of the colony of Pera-Galata and of the colonisation of the Crimea (Caffa).
