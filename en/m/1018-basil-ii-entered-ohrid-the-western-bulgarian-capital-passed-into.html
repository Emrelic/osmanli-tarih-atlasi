Basil II entered Ohrid: the western Bulgarian capital passed into Byzantine hands

1018

The Bulgarian dynasty, which had resisted for four years after the death of Samuel, surrendered to Basil II at the gates of Ohrid in early 1018. The archbishopric of Ohrid, for its part, continued to exist under Byzantine rule as the center of Slavic Christianity.
