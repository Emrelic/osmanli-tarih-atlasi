The fan incident — the pretext for the occupation

29 April 1827

In the negotiations that grew tense over France's unpaid old grain debt to Algiers, Dey Hussein struck the French consul Deval with the fan in his hand. This incident, arising from a debt dispute, was made the official pretext of the occupation that would begin three years later.
