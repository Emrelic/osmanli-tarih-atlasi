The Conquest of Bihać

19 June 1592

Telli Hasan Pasha, the beylerbeyi of Bosnia, after taking Ripač, the outer fortress of Bihać, in November 1591, captured the city and the fortress itself in 1592; after a ten-day siege the commander Lamberg surrendered. This conquest pushed the Bosnian frontier forward along the Una river and was the last great Ottoman gain on the eve of the Long War, which would break out a year later with the martyrdom of Telli Hasan Pasha before Kulpa.
