The Turkish commander Toghril killed eleven princes and seized the throne of Ghazna; shortly afterwards he was killed, and Farrukhzad was placed on the throne

1052

After Mawdud, Mas'ud II, Ali and Abd al-Rashid ruled briefly (440/1049); the Turkish commander Toghril killed eleven princes, Abd al-Rashid among them, and took over the head of the state (1052); shortly afterwards he was killed by another Turkish commander, and Farrukhzad, son of Mas'ud I, was seated on the throne.
