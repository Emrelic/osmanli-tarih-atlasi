Spain's recovery of the rock of Badis (Peñón de Vélez)

1564

The TDV's Saadians entry records that the Spaniards' seizure of Badis was a heavy blow to Maghribi seafaring (1564). This rock position on the northern Moroccan coast provided a base for piracy in the western Mediterranean and for the Ottoman-Spanish rivalry.
