Tibet sent an envoy to the Tang for the first time (Songtsen Gampo, 634)

634

The Tibetan ruler Songtsen Gampo (Qizong Nongzan), who had come to the throne at a young age, sent the first embassy to the Tang court in the 8th year of Zhenguan; the Tang source mentions the capital of Tibet as the city of Luoxie (Lhasa).
