The Albigensian Crusaders take Carcassonne

1209

The viscounties of Carcassonne and Béziers were taken by the Crusaders in the Albigensian Crusade in 1209 on account of the role played by Viscount Raymond Roger against Simon de Montfort; the viscounty was confiscated by the crown in 1247.
