Mitanni-Egypt alliance: peace between Artatama I and Thutmose IV; a Mitanni princess went to Egypt as a bride

1399 BC

After hostility, the Mitanni king Artatama I made peace with the pharaoh of Egypt and sent his daughter to Egypt. The alliance established an Egyptian-Mitanni balance in northern Syria against the Hittite threat and prepared the diplomacy of the Amarna period.
