San José Mogote: village settlement and abandonment of the ceremonial centre after Rosario: end of the Rosario phase: most of the population goes to Monte Albán

499 BC

San José Mogote, a village of at least 7 ha in the Tierras Largas phase (1500–1150 bc) in the Valle (Oaxaca, Etla arm); in the Rosario phase it is the centre of a group of 18–23 villages. At the end of Rosario ~95% of the population left to take part in the founding of Monte Albán; the 40-ha centre at Mound 1 was abandoned until the beginning of Monte Albán II (100 BC).
