Battle of Gaza (Khan Yunus): defeat of Janbirdi al-Ghazali

21 December 1516

Selim the Grim sent the grand vizier Hadım Sinan Pasha to Gaza with a vanguard of four thousand men. Janbirdi al-Ghazali, whom Tumanbay had appointed to the governorship of Damascus, tried to halt the Ottoman vanguard on the Gaza-Khan Yunus line but suffered a heavy defeat and fled to Egypt. With the dispersal of the last Mamluk buffer force in Palestine, the road to the Sinai desert was entirely opened.
