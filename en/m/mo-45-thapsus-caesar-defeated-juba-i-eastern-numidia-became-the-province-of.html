Thapsus: Caesar defeated Juba I; eastern Numidia became the province of Africa Nova

45 BC

In the spring of 46 Caesar routed the army of Scipio and Juba I at Thapsus; Juba committed suicide. The eastern part of Numidia became the province of Africa Nova, governed by Sallust, and the western part was given to Bocchus and Sittius.
