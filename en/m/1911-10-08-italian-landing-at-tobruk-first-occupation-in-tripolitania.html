Italian landing at Tobruk: first occupation in Tripolitania

8 October 1911

Italy declared war on 29 September 1911 and its fleet blockaded the coast on 25-26 September. The first land operation was made at Tobruk on 8 October: the small Ottoman garrison withdrew to the interior and the port was occupied without a fight. Tobruk was the Ottoman port closest to the Egyptian border; its fall opened the eastern flank of Cyrenaica and made it easier for Italian forces to march westward along the coast.
