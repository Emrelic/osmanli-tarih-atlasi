The occupation of Kos

20 May 1912

Kos, the last link of the Dodecanese operation, was a fertile and populous island immediately opposite the Bodrum peninsula. With the occupation, all the islands in the southeastern corner of the Aegean came under Italian control, and the southwestern coast of Anatolia was surrounded along its whole length by a chain of foreign islands.
