Shalmaneser III launched campaigns against Urartu (Arame); Urartu appeared for the first time in Assyrian sources

857 BC

In the first years of his reign Shalmaneser III marched into the land of King Arame of Urartu; the Assyrian annals mention Urartu under the name "land of Urartu" for the first time in these campaigns. It is the first dated record of the state that would be Assyria's chief rival in this region in the following centuries.
