The founding of the Hendese-i Mülkiyye Mektebi

3 November 1883

After the Ottoman-Russian War of 1877-78, the Hendese-i Mülkiyye Mektebi was founded by Abdülhamid II on 3 November 1883 with the aim of training engineers from the Turkish and Muslim population. The school institutionalized civil engineering education as a separate institution alongside the military engineering schools; the opening ceremony of its own building was held on 29 October 1884. In 1909 it was taken from military administration, attached to the Ministry of Public Works (Nâfia Nezâreti), and took the name Mühendis Mekteb-i Âlîsi.
