The Romanian army entered Szatmár (19 April) and Oradea (Varad, 20 April)

20 April 1919

On Easter Saturday, 19 April 1919, in Satu Mare, a local delegation headed by Dr. Ilie Carol Barbul welcomed the first patrol of the 2nd Cavalry Division under the command of Colonel Ressel at the bridge over the Someș. The next day, 20 April 1919, Romanian troops under the command of General Traian Moșoiu entered Oradea.
