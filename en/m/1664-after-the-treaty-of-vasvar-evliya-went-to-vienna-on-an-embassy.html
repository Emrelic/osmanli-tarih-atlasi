After the Treaty of Vasvár, Evliya went to Vienna on an embassy

1664

After the Treaty of Vasvár of 1664 he went to Vienna with the embassy of Kara Mehmed Pasha and toured the Habsburg capital. The year is not separately stated in the TDV.
