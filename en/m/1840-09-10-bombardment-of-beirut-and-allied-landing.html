Bombardment of Beirut and allied landing

10 September 1840

The British-Austrian fleet bombarded Beirut; arms were distributed to the people who rose against Egyptian rule in the mountains of Lebanon. Ibrahim Pasha's control of Syria collapsed between internal revolt and external intervention.
