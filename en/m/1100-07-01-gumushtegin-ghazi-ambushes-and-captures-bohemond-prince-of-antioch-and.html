Gümüshtegin Ghazi ambushes and captures Bohemond, Prince of Antioch, and takes him to Niksar and imprisons him

1 July 1100

Gümüshtegin the Danishmendid, besieging Malatya, ambushed and defeated the forces of Bohemond, Prince of Antioch, who had come to its aid; Bohemond was taken first to Sivas, then to Niksar, and imprisoned. The siege was lifted when Baldwin, Count of Edessa, approached.
