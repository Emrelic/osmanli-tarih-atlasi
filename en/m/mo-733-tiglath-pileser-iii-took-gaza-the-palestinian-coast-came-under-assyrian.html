Tiglath-Pileser III took Gaza; the Palestinian coast came under Assyrian control

733 BC

Tiglath-Pileser turned to the Palestinian coastal strip, forced Hanunu, king of Gaza, to flee to Egypt, and brought the city under his dominion. In the following years the Philistine city-states became an Assyrian vassal strip.
