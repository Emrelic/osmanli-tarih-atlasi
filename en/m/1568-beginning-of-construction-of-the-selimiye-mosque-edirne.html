Beginning of construction of the Selimiye Mosque (Edirne)

1568

The construction of the Selimiye, which Mimar Sinan called "my masterpiece," began in the name of Selim II in 1568 and, lasting six years, was completed in 1574 (its completion is already recorded in the chronology). The western shops of the arasta and the primary school were completed after Selim II's death, in the reign of Murad III, by Davud Agha.
