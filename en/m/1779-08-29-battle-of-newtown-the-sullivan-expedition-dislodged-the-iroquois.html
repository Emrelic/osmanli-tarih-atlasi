Battle of Newtown: the Sullivan expedition dislodged the Iroquois warriors, including the Cayuga and Seneca, from their entrenchment near Chemung

29 August 1779

On 29 August 1779, Major General Sullivan's army encountered, near Chemung, an entrenchment prepared by forces made up largely, according to Sullivan's letter, of Indian warriors of "seven nations" (together with the separate commands of the Butlers, Brant and McDonald); under artillery fire and flanking pressure from General Poor, the entrenchment was abandoned.
