Descent into Western Thrace: conquest of Gümülcine

1363

After the taking of Edirne, the frontier forces that turned to the west of the Maritsa took Gümülcine (Komotini), the centre of Western Thrace, under the command of Gazi Evrenos Bey. The city became the forward headquarters from which Evrenos Bey would carry his raids into Macedonia. The part of Thrace extending to the Aegean coast thus came under Ottoman control, and the road to Thessalonica was opened.
