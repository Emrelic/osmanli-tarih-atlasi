Bombardment of Alexandria and the British landing

11 July 1882

When the officers' movement led by Urabi Pasha brought the khedive under its control, Britain intervened on the pretext of the security of the Suez route. Admiral Seymour's fleet bombarded the coastal batteries of Alexandria on 11-12 July 1882; after the fire and looting in the city, British troops landed on 15 July. The Ottoman State could not prevent this operation in a province over which it was legally sovereign, and it also left unanswered the call to take part in the intervention. Thus the first step of an occupation that would last thirty-two years was taken.
