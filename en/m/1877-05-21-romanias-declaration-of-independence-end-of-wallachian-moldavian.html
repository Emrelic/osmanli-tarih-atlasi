Romania's declaration of independence — end of Wallachian-Moldavian vassalage

21 May 1877

Two weeks after the declaration of the War of '93, the Principality of Romania declared its independence and entered the war on Russia's side; its share in the siege of Plevna was decisive in the Ottoman defeat. More than four hundred years of Wallachian and Moldavian vassalage thus effectively came to an end; independence gained international recognition at the Congress of Berlin (1878). From this date the map shows Romania not as a vassal but as an independent state in its own color. With the declaration of independence the whole of Wallachia and Moldavia (Iași, Galați, Brăila, Giurgiu, Calafat, Botoșani and others) became independent Romania.
