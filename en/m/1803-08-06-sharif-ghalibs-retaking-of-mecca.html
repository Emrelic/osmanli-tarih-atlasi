Sharif Ghalib's Retaking of Mecca

6 August 1803

Sharif Ghalib, who had withdrawn to Jeddah, besieged Mecca with the forces he had gathered and, after a siege of twenty-five days, retook the city from the Wahhabis; the khutba was again read in the name of the Ottomans. This second dominion too would not be lasting; the Saudi forces would take the city again in 1806.
