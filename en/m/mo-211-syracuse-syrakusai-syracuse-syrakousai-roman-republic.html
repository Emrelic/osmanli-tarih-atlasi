Syracuse, Syrakusai: Syracuse (Syrakousai) → Roman Republic

211 BC

At Syracuse, Syrakusai, ownership passes from Syracuse (Syrakousai) to the Roman Republic. Transition source information: Livy 25.40: Marcellus settled Sicily 'captis Syracusis'; Eutropius 3.14: Marcellus took the greater part of Sicily and Syracuse.
