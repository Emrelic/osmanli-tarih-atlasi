The Qarakhanid Harun Bughra Khan captured Bukhara

7 May 992

Harun Bughra Khan, who had a secret agreement with Abu Ali al-Simjuri and Fa'iq al-Khassa, captured the Samanid capital Bukhara in Rabi I 382 (May 992); he fell ill shortly afterwards and died.
