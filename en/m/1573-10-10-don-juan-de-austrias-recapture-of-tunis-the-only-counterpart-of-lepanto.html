Don Juan de Austria's recapture of Tunis: the only counterpart of Lepanto on land

10 October 1573

Although the Holy League dissolved after Lepanto (1571), Spain alone bore down on North Africa: in the TDV's words, "On 10 October 1573 the Spaniards undertook a new operation in order to take control in Tunis. They took Tunis and left 8000 soldiers there." This operation under the command of Don Juan de Austria is the only place where the Lepanto victory found a counterpart on land; but it was not lasting, and the city was retaken eleven months later. What the occupation overthrew was an established Ottoman administration: on 2 Dhu al-Hijja 980 (5 April 1573) the commander of Tunis was Kılıç Ali Pasha, the kaymakam Ramazan Pasha, the beylerbeyi Haydar Pasha.
