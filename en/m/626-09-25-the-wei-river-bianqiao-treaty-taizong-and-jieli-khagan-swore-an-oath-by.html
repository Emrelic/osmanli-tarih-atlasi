The Wei River (Bianqiao) treaty: Taizong and Jieli Khagan swore an oath by sacrificing a white horse

25 September 626

When the army of the Eastern Turkic Khagan Jieli advanced as far as the north of the Wei River, Taizong spoke with the khagan at the head of the bridge, and then a treaty was made by sacrificing a white horse on the Bianqiao bridge; the Turkic army withdrew.
