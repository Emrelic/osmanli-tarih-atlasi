The Qarmatians besiege Damascus; the leader Sahib al-Naqa Yahya is killed during the siege

1 July 903

Yahya, known as Sahib al-Naqa, one of the sons of Zikrawayh ibn Mihrawayh, was killed during the siege of Damascus in Sha'ban 290 (July 903); his brother Sahib al-Shama took his place and, after an unsuccessful attack on Aleppo, seized Hama, Homs, Ma'arrat al-Nu'man and Baalbek.
