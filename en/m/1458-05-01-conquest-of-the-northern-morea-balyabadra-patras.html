Conquest of the northern Morea: Balyabadra (Patras)

1 May 1458

The forces of Mehmed the Conqueror, who turned to the Despotate of the Morea in the same years as the annexation of Serbia, took Balyabadra (Patras) in the north-west of the peninsula. This port, which holds the mouth of the Gulf of Corinth, is the lock of the entry into the Morea by land; its taking prepared the ground for the final conquest two years later. The city was the last great landing place by which the Byzantine despots made contact with the Latin world.
