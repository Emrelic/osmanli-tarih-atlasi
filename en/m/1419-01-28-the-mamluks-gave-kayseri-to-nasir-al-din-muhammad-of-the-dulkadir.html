The Mamluks gave Kayseri to Nasir al-Din Muhammad of the Dulkadir

28 January 1419

The Mamluk army, marching against Karamanoğlu Mehmed Bey, occupied Kayseri; the Mamluk commander left the city and its surroundings to Nasir al-Din Muhammad of the Dulkadir and returned to his country.
