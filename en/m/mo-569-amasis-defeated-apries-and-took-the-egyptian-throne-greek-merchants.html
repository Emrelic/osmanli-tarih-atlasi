Amasis defeated Apries and took the Egyptian throne; Greek merchants were settled at Naucratis

569 BC

Apries, returning from his Libyan campaign with Greek mercenaries, was defeated at Momemphis by Amasis, who had taken command of the native troops in the army; Amasis ruled Egypt from Sais and assigned Naucratis to the Greeks as their sole trading port. Toward the end of his rule (526 BCE) the Persian threat grew.
