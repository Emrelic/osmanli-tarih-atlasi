Greek and Bulgarian armies besieged Constantinople; the Prince of the Morea, Geoffrey II, saved it

1236

In 1236 Ivan Asen and John Vatatzes besieged Constantinople again; the Latins were at their last gasp, but the Prince of Achaea, Geoffrey II of Villehardouin, hurried from the Morea and joined in the defence of the city, and the siege was lifted.
