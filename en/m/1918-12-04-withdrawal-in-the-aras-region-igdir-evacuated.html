Withdrawal in the Aras region: Iğdır evacuated

4 December 1918

In accordance with the provision of the Armistice of Mudros requiring withdrawal behind the 1914 frontier, Yakup Şevki Pasha, by an order of 21 November, ordered units outside the Brest-Litovsk boundary to be withdrawn by 4 December; Iğdır and its surroundings were completely evacuated on this date. Afterwards the Aras-Turkish Government was overthrown by the Armenians in late December 1918.
