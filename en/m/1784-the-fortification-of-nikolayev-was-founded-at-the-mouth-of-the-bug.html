The fortification of Nikolayev was founded at the mouth of the Bug

1784

A fortification was founded on the order of Prince Potemkin at the confluence of the Southern Bug and Ingul rivers. In 1788 a shipyard was opened there, and the settlement became one of the shipbuilding centres of the Black Sea fleet.
