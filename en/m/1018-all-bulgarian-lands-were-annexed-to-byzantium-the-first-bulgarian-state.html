All Bulgarian lands were annexed to Byzantium: the First Bulgarian State came to an end

1018

After Ivan Vladislav was killed before Dyrrhachion, at the beginning of 1018 Basil II accepted at the gates of Ohrid the surrender of the family of the last tsar, and the Bulgarian lands as far as the Danube became entirely a Byzantine province. This situation lasted until 1186-1187.
