Malik-Shah (Shahinshah), sent by the Great Seljuk Sultan Muhammad Tapar, takes the throne of the Anatolian Seljuks at Konya

1110

After the death of Kilij Arslan I, because of the attacks of Alexios Komnenos, Muhammad Tapar sent Kilij Arslan's eldest son Malik-Shah to Anatolia; Malik-Shah came to Konya, took the throne and struggled with Alexios for six years.
