Yusuf Qadir Khan died; his sons Arslan Khan Sulayman and Bughra Khan Muhammad took on the government of the state

7 December 1032

Yusuf Qadir Khan died in Muharram 424 (December 1032) and was buried in the Janbaza al-Khaqaniyya cemetery in Kashgar; in the east Arslan Khan Sulayman (1032-1056) assumed the great khaganate as the first Eastern Karakhanid ruler.
