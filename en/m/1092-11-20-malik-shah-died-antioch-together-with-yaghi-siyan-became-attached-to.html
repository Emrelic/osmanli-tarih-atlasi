Malik-Shah died; Antioch, together with Yaghi-Siyan, became attached to Tutush of the Syrian Seljuks

20 November 1092

Following the death of the Great Seljuk Sultan Malik-Shah, the state was divided among his heirs; Yaghi-Siyan, governor of Antioch, became attached to Tutush, the ruler of Syria.
