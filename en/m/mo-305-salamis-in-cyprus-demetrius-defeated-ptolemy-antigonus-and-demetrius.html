Salamis in Cyprus: Demetrius defeated Ptolemy; Antigonus and Demetrius took the title of king

305 BC

In the spring of 306 Demetrius Poliorcetes defeated Ptolemy's fleet off Cyprus and brought Cyprus into Antigonus's hands; in the summer months Antigonus and Demetrius took the title of king, and the other Diadochi soon assumed the same title. Ptolemy recovered Cyprus in 294.
