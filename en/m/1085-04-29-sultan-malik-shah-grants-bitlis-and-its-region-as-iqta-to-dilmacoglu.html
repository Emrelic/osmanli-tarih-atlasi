Sultan Malik-Shah grants Bitlis and its region as iqta' to Dilmaçoğlu Mehmed Beg; the Dilmaçoğulları Principality begins

29 April 1085

After the Seljuk army's commander-in-chief Fakhr al-Dawla Ibn Jahir had taken Bitlis and its region, Sultan Malik-Shah granted it as iqta' to Dilmaçoğlu Mehmed Beg, who had taken part at Manzikert (478/1085). The principality ruled at Bitlis and Erzen from 1085 to 1394.
