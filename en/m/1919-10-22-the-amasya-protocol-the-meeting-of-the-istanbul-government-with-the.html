The Amasya Protocol: The Meeting of the Istanbul Government with the Representative Committee

22 October 1919

After the fall of Damat Ferid and the formation of the government of Ali Rızâ Pasha, the Minister of the Navy Salih Pasha was sent to Amasya to meet the Representative Committee. By the protocols reached, the Istanbul government effectively recognised the existence of the Anatolian movement; the convening of the Meclis-i Meb'ûsan, the holding of free elections and the preservation of the national borders were accepted. This is the first official political gain the National Struggle obtained against Istanbul.
