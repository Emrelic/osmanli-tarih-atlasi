Tarhuncu Ahmed Pasha's balanced-budget reform

20 June 1652

Grand Vizier Tarhuncu Ahmed Pasha, in order to overcome the financial crisis caused by the long years of war, implemented a harsh austerity programme that also covered palace expenses and prepared the first known balanced budget in Ottoman history (the tarhuncu memorandum). According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on 'Tarhuncu Ahmed Pasha', this reform earned the treasury a significant surplus, but it created wide discontent among the palace circle and the high bureaucracy. This discontent would result a year later in his execution.
