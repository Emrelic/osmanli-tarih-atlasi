The Tervingi Goths crossed the Danube and settled on Roman territory

376

Under Hunnic pressure, Fritigern's Tervingi Goths obtained permission from Valens in 376 to cross the Danube and settle on Roman territory; problems of supply and administration turned into revolt. The result was the defeat at Adrianople (Edirne) in 378.
