The annexation of the County of Nice to France

31 January 1793

After the annexation of Savoy in November 1792, the French National Convention approved the annexation to France of the County of Nice, which belonged to the Kingdom of Sardinia, on 31 January 1793; the region became the department of Alpes-Maritimes. After Napoleon's defeat, Nice returned to Sardinia by the First Treaty of Paris of 30 May 1814.
