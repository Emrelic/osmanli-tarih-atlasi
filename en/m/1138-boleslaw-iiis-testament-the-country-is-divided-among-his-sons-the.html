Bolesław III's testament: the country is divided among his sons, the seniorate system begins

1138

Bolesław III, who died in 1138, divided the state among his sons and introduced the seniorate, which gave central authority to the eldest brother bearing the title 'grand duke'; this arrangement led to the period of fragmentation (interregnum) of 1138-1320.
