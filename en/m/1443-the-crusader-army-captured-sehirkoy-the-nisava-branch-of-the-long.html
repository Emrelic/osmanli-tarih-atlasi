The Crusader army captured Şehirköy — the Nišava branch of the 'Long Campaign'

1443

The Crusader army under King Vladislav and the Serbian Despot Đurađ Branković captured Şehirköy in 1443. In the continuation of the same campaign, the Ottoman forces would be routed in November at the Zlatica pass. After the Treaty of Szeged (1444), Şehirköy was given back to the Serbs by Murad II, but it passed to the Ottomans after the death of Đurađ Branković in 1456.
