Romanian troops reached Lugoj (Lugos) — Romanian administration in the east of the Banat

22 July 1919

The train carrying the Romanian troops reached Lugoj at 2 p.m. on 22 July 1919; the people of the city welcomed the army at the station, the prefect of Caraș-Severin, George Dobrin, delivered the welcoming speech, and General Jitianu replied. Romanian administration was established in July 1919 in the part of the Banat that fell to Romania; the legal transfer took place with the Treaty of Trianon in 1920.
