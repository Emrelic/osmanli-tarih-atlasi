At Augsburg King Rudolf made his sons Albrecht and Rudolf dukes of Austria and Styria: Vienna, Graz, Linz, Freistadt, Gmünd, Maribor, Hartberg and Wiener Neustadt as Habsburg fiefs

27 December 1282

At the court assembly at Augsburg on 27 December 1282, Rudolf I enfeoffed his sons Albrecht and Rudolf with the duchies of Austria and Styria (together with Carniola and the Windic March). The Austrian lands that had been in the hands of the Reich between 1276 and 1282 were from this day Habsburg territory; the Habsburgs' roughly 650-year rule in Austria begins.
