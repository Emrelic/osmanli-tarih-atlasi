The attachment of Montenegro to Ottoman administration — Cetinje

1499

When the Crnojević dynasty, which ruled Zeta, gave up its sovereignty in 1496, the mountainous region of Montenegro, which was the backyard of Shkodër, was taken under direct Ottoman administration and organised first as a separate sancak, then as a part of the sancak of Shkodër. The monastery of Cetinje was left in place as the religious centre of the region. Because of the harshness of the geography, administration here never worked as tightly as on the plains; two centuries later, the vladika rule born from the same monastery would become the nucleus of de facto independence.
