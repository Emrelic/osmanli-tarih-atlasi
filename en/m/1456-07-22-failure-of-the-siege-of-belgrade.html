Failure of the siege of Belgrade

22 July 1456

Mehmed the Conqueror, after the conquest of Constantinople, besieged Belgrade, regarded as the lock of Central Europe; however, the reinforcements that John Hunyadi brought down the Danube and the volunteers gathered by the crusade preacher Capistrano prevented the fortress from falling. The sultan, despite the personal heroism he displayed before the siege, was wounded and forced to withdraw. This failure was to close the Hungarian frontier to Ottoman advance for sixty-five years; Belgrade could be taken only in 1521 by Süleyman the Lawgiver.
