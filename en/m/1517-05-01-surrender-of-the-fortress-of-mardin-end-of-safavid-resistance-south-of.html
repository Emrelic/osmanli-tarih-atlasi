Surrender of the fortress of Mardin: end of Safavid resistance south of Diyarbekir

1 May 1517

When Bıyıklı Mehmed Pasha, marching on the Diyarbekir region after Chaldiran, besieged Mardin, the Safavid forces withdrew into the fortress in October 1515; although the city passed into Ottoman hands, the fortress held out. The counterattack of the Safavid governor Qara Khan with reinforcements ended in the Battle of Koçhisar (Kızıltepe) in May 1516 — in the battle that lasted all day on the plain of Dede Garkın, Qara Khan was beheaded and his army dispersed. The fortress, in the TDV's words, surrendered "only nine months later."
