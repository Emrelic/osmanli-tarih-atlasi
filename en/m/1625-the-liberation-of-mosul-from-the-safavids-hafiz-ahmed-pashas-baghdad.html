The liberation of Mosul from the Safavids — Hafız Ahmed Pasha's Baghdad campaign

1625

Murad IV sent an army under the grand vizier Hafız Ahmed Pasha against Baghdad. While the army was near Diyarbekir, the vanguard of Çerkez Hasan, Beylerbeyi of Karaman, chased the pro-Iranian forces gathered at Altınköprü as far as Kirkuk and brought the region, including Kirkuk, under control; the Safavids were again unable to hold Mosul for long. Hafız Ahmed Pasha reached first Mosul and then Kirkuk at the beginning of September and besieged Baghdad on 13 November 1625; the siege was inconclusive and Baghdad remained in Safavid hands. The TDV article on Kirkuk, however, attributes the retaking of the city from the Safavids to 1630 and to Hüsrev Pasha.
