Ottoman-Safavid War (1603-1612) began — Shah Abbas's counteroffensive, loss of Tabriz

21 October 1603

Shah Abbas, having reorganized his army, took advantage of the Celali disorders to launch an offensive; Tabriz fell, and the gains in the Caucasus were lost within a few years. Other settlements lost in the same campaign and in its aftermath: Nakhchivan, Khoy, Marand, Salmas; the lords of Karadagh (Ahar) and of the Mukri also went over to the Safavid side.
