Daniil, Prince of Moscow, annexed Kolomna and Serpukhov to Moscow

1301

Daniil took Kolomna from the Principality of Ryazan (the captured Prince of Ryazan ceded the fortress to be released) and Serpukhov; Moscow reached the Oka River, and this was the first territorial expansion of the Principality of Moscow.
