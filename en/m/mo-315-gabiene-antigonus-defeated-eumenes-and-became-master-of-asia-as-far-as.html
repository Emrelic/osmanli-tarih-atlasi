Gabiene: Antigonus defeated Eumenes and became master of Asia as far as Iran

315 BC

In the winter of 316/5 Eumenes's army was routed at Gabiene; its commander was handed over to Antigonus by his own soldiers and executed. Antigonus became sole master of a wide region from the Persian plateau to Anatolia; Seleucus fled to Egypt.
