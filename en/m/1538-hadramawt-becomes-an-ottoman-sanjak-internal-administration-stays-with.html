Hadramawt becomes an Ottoman sanjak — internal administration stays with the Kathiri chiefs

1538

Hadramawt, which came under Ottoman rule during Hadim Süleyman Pasha's India expedition, was organized as a sanjak attached to Yemen. In practice, however, the chiefs of the Kathiri tribe kept the administration of inner Hadramawt in their own hands; Ottoman authority remained limited to the coast and the tax tie.
