Crucifixion of Jesus: execution in the time of Pilate (calculated date of 7 April 30 per the TDV; 27 and 33 are alternatives)

7 April 30

According to Christian belief, Jesus Christ was crucified and died at Golgotha with the approval of Pontius Pilate, and rose on the third day. According to Islamic belief, ʿĪsā was neither killed nor crucified; it only appeared so to them, and God raised him to Himself (An-Nisa 4/157-158). Historical record: Tacitus writes that Christus was executed in the reign of Tiberius by the procurator Pontius Pilate; the TDV calculates the day of death of Jesus, who died on Friday, 14 Nisan by the Jewish calendar, as 11 April 27, 7 April 30 or 3 April 33 CE, and says that the year 30 is the closest to the truth.
