Koca Sinan Pasha Set Out on the Austrian Campaign — The First Campaign of the Long War

19 July 1593

After the defeat and martyrdom of the Bosnian beylerbeyi Hasan Pasha before Sisak, the grand vizier Koca Sinan Pasha persuaded the sultan to go to war with brilliant words and promises and set out with 12,000 Janissaries and other units. Thus the first campaign of the Ottoman-Austrian war, which would last thirteen years, was opened.
