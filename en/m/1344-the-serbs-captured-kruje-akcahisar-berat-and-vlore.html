The Serbs captured Krujë (Akçahisar), Berat and Vlorë

1344

TDV Kruya: 'in 1344 the Serbs captured Krujë, Berat and Vlorë'. TDV Arnavutluk says that Dušan occupied Albania between 1343 and 1346, and that with the collapse of his empire in 1355 the local lords grew stronger. Only the year is known; the day is unconfirmed.
