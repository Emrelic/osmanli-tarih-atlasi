Re-founding of the Darülfünun-i Shahane as a permanent institution

31 August 1900

After two earlier failed attempts (1863 and 1870), the Darülfünun was re-founded on 31 August 1900 as a permanent institution under the name Darülfünun-i Shahane; the TDV describes this date as the direct foundation of today's Turkish university system.
