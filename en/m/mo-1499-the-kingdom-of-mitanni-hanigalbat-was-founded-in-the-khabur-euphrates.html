The kingdom of Mitanni (Hanigalbat) was founded in the Khabur-Euphrates region

1499 BC

The Hurrian-Mitanni kingdom rose in Upper Mesopotamia in the vacuum left by the fall of Old Babylon and the Hittite decline. The capital was Washukanni; within a few decades the kingdom made Aššur and northern Syria its vassals.
