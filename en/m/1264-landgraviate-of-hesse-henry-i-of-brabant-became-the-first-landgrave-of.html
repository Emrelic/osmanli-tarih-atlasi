Landgraviate of Hesse: Henry I of Brabant became the first landgrave of Hesse, peace with Meissen 1264

1264

The history of Hesse as an independent country begins with Henry I ('the Child'); peace with Meissen was made in 1264. Henry had a castle built in Kassel, which became his usual residence in the following years; in 1292 he was raised to the imperial principality (Reichsfürstenstand).
