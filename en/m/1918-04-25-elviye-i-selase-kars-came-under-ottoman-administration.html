Elviye-i Selâse: Kars came under Ottoman administration

25 April 1918

At Brest-Litovsk the return of the three sancaks lost by the 1878 Treaty of Berlin — Kars, Ardahan and Batum — had been agreed; the Ottoman army entered Ardahan on 3 April, Batum on 14 April and Kars on 25 April 1918, and administration was effectively established. These lands, known by the name 'Elviye-i Selâse', were regarded as compensation for the loss ongoing since the War of '93. After the armistice they would be lost again, and they would be definitively attached to Turkey only with the 1921 Treaty of Kars.
