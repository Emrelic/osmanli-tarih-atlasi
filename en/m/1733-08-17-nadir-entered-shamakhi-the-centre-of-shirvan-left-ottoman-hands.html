Nadir entered Shamakhi — the centre of Shirvan left Ottoman hands

17 August 1733

Nadir entered Shamakhi, which had fallen to the Ottoman share together with the central part of Shirvan by the Treaty of Istanbul of 24 June 1724, on 17 August 1733. Ottoman administration in the Caucasus thereafter disintegrated rapidly; Ganja and Tiflis were lost in 1735.
