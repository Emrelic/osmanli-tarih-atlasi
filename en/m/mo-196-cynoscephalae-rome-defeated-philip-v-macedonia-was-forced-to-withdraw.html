Cynoscephalae: Rome defeated Philip V; Macedonia was forced to withdraw from Greece

196 BC

In June 197 Flamininus's Roman legions routed the Macedonian phalanx at Cynoscephalae. In the peace of 196 Philip gave up all his positions in Greece (Demetrias, Chalcis, Corinth), surrendered his fleet and paid 1,000 talents.
