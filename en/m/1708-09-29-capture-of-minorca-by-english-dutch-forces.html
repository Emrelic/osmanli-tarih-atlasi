Capture of Minorca by English-Dutch forces

29 September 1708

During the War of the Spanish Succession, English-Dutch forces acting as allies of the Austrian archduke Charles VI landed on the island on 14 September 1708 under General James Stanhope, and on 28-29 September broke the Spanish defence and took control of Minorca (the official surrender of the main fortress, Fort St. Philip, came on 24 November). Mahon, Minorca's strategic harbour, would be the focus of the English-French-Spanish struggle for the next century.
