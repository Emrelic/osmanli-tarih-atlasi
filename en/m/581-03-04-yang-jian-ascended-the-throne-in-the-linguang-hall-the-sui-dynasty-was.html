Yang Jian ascended the throne in the Linguang Hall: the Sui dynasty was founded

4 March 581

After the Northern Zhou emperor issued the edict transferring the throne to Yang Jian, Yang Jian entered the palace in the Zhou capital and became emperor in the Linguang Hall; the reign title was changed to Kaihuang. The Chang'an region passed from the Northern Zhou to the Sui.
