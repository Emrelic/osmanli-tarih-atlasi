Ebussuud Efendi examines the question of the gedik (tradesmen's monopoly right) from a legal standpoint

1 June 1555

The question of the "gedik," the monopoly and privilege right granted to a fixed number of persons in a trade, came onto the agenda again in the time of the Lawgiver; Şeyhülislam Ebussuud Efendi examined the subject in depth in 1555-56 and presented the legitimacy of the practice to the sultan.
