Sennacherib invaded Judah; he took Lachish and besieged Jerusalem

700 BC

Suppressing Hezekiah's revolt against Assyria, Sennacherib took forty-six cities of Judah, conquered Lachish and besieged Jerusalem. Hezekiah saved the city by paying a heavy tribute; the Lachish reliefs in Nineveh depict this campaign.
