The decision for war with Russia — upon the Russian attack on Azov

2 May 1736

When the Russians attacked the fortress of Azov in Dhu'l-Qa'da 1148 (March 1736) and marched on Or Kapı, the Ottoman government, in a council convened, partly at the instigation of the French ambassador Villeneuve, was obliged to decide on war against Russia. Grand Vizier Seyyid Mehmed Pasha was appointed serasker of the army. Russia had been freed from the Iranian front by the Ganja agreement of March 1735 and had again put the aim of access to the Black Sea on its agenda.
