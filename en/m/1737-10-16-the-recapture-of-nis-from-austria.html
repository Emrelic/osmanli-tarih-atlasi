The recapture of Niš from Austria

16 October 1737

Niš, which the Austrian army under Seckendorf had taken in Rabî' al-awwal 1150 (July 1737), was retaken in October of the same year by the Beylerbeyi of Rumelia, Köprülüzâde Hâfız Ahmed Pasha. After Banja Luka, this was the second development that frustrated Austria's campaign of 1737.
