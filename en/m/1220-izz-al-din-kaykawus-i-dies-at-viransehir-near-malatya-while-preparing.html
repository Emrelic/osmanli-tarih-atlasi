Izz al-Din Kaykawus I dies at Viranşehir near Malatya while preparing an Aleppo campaign

1220

Izz al-Din Kaykawus, who marched on Aleppo, where after the death (1216) of the Ayyubid al-Malik al-Zahir his child son had come to the throne, allied with the Artuqid Nasir al-Din Mahmud of Amid and Kökböri, lord of Irbil; as he came to Malatya he fell ill and died at Viranşehir near Malatya (Shawwal 616 / January 1220).
