Abolition of the Mutasarrifate of Mount Lebanon

11 July 1915

The autonomous mutasarrifate order, established by the Règlement Organique of 1861 and based on the international protocol renewed in 1864, was unilaterally annulled by the Ottoman State during the First World War; the Ministry of the Interior began to appoint Muslim mutasarrifs directly to the region. The period of autonomy, during which eight mutasarrifs served between 1861 and 1915, thus came to a close.
