Omani invasion of Bahrain: end of Safavid rule

1717

During the dissolution of the last years of the Safavid State, the Omani fleet under the Ya'rubid dynasty invaded the island and Safavid rule ended. The following thirty-five years are the most turbulent period of the gulf: the island changed hands several times among Oman, Iran and local Arab forces.
