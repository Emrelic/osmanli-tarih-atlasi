First surrender of Thessaloniki

9 April 1387

After a long blockade the city surrendered (it would be returned to Byzantium in 1403 and conquered definitively in 1430). Other places annexed on the same date: Kavala. Other settlements lost on the same date: Langadas, Eleftheroupoli (Pravishte).
