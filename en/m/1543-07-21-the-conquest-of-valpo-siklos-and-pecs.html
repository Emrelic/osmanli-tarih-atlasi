The Conquest of Valpo, Šikloš and Pécs

21 July 1543

The first objective of the Hungarian campaign launched two years after the annexation of Buda was to secure the southern flank of the new province between the Drava and the Danube. After the fortresses of Valpo and Šikloš were taken, Pécs, the centre of Baranya, surrendered. The city would become the most populous and most developed centre of Ottoman Hungary, and the historian İbrahim Peçuylu would take his name from here.
