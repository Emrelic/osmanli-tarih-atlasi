Svyatoslav is defeated and killed by the Pecheneg chieftain Kurya; a struggle for the throne begins among his sons

972

Svyatoslav, who had defeated the Volga Bulgars and the Khazars, was defeated by Kurya, the Pecheneg chieftain and ally of Byzantium, and fell on the battlefield in 972; a power struggle then began among his sons.
