Sökmen takes over Hasankeyf; the Hisn Kayfa branch of the Artuqids is founded

1102

On the death of Kurbuqa, emir of Mosul, Sökmen, who supported Musa in Mosul, helped him defeat Chökürmish in return for Musa's promise; when Musa was killed he went to Hasankeyf and took over the city. Thus the Hisn Kayfa branch of the Artuqids was founded (1102-1232).
