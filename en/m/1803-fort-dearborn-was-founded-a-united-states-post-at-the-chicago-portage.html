Fort Dearborn was founded — a United States post at the Chicago portage

1803

The United States Army founded Fort Dearborn at the Chicago portage, which joins Lake Michigan and the Mississippi basin. The portage had been known and used since the 1670s; the fort marked the westward extension of United States control in the Northwest Territory.
