The Greek army's occupation of Eastern Thrace — Edirne and Kırklareli

20 July 1920

Greece set about effectively seizing Eastern Thrace as well before the Treaty of Sèvres was signed. According to the TDV article on the National Struggle, the Greek army took the whole of Thrace on 20 July; this development led the sultan in Istanbul to convene the Council of the Sultanate two days later. Kırklareli came under occupation on 26 July, and Edirne too came under Greek administration in the same month.
