Conquest of Enez and Samothrace — completion of the Western Thracian coast

24 January 1456

Enez (Ainos), at the point where the Maritsa river flows into the sea, was taken from the Gattilusio family of Genoese origin, who had held it for eighty years; the island of Samothrace, attached to it, was also seized in the same operation. With the fall of Enez the coastal strip of Western Thrace became continuous Ottoman territory from Thessalonica to Gallipoli. Only Lesbos (Midilli) remained in the hands of the Gattilusio family; it too was to be taken six years later.
