Capture of Istanköy (Kos) by the Knights of Saint John

1315

According to the TDV's Istanköy entry, after settling in Rhodes the Knights of Saint John also took Istanköy (1315) and built new fortresses. The island thus came under the rule of the knights, which lasted until 1523; under what administration Kos was after the fall of the city of Rhodes in 1310 is not detailed in the TDV.
