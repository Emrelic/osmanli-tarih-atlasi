Ayn al-Dawla died; Bori Tegin Ibrahim (Tamghach Khan) became great khagan and chose Samarkand as capital

3 May 1052

Ayn al-Dawla Muhammad ibn Nasr probably died in 444 (1052); his brother Bori Tegin Ibrahim became great khagan with the title Tamghach Bughra Qara Khan, did not go to Uzgand, and made Samarkand the capital (1052-1068).
