The Granting of the Konya-Baghdad Railway Concession to the Germans

27 November 1899

Following Kaiser Wilhelm II's visit to Istanbul and Damascus in 1898, the Anatolian Railway Company was given the preliminary concession for the line to extend from Konya to Baghdad and Basra. The Baghdad Railway project meant, for the Ottomans, strengthening central control over the Arab vilayets, and for Germany, reaching the Persian Gulf. This project, which England and Russia opposed fiercely, became one of the most contested files of the imperialist rivalry leading to the First World War.
