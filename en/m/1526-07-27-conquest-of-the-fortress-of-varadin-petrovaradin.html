Conquest of the Fortress of Varadin (Petrovaradin)

27 July 1526

The army setting out on the Mohács campaign crossed from Belgrade into Hungarian lands; first the outer quarters of Varadin were taken, and after a fourteen-day siege the fortress too fell. The Lawgiver ordered that the villages on the road not be burned, because these were now regarded as Ottoman land.
