Bregalnica basin: General Stefanović entered İştip

29 October 1912

Serbian troops under the command of General Stefanović took İştip without encountering resistance; irregular bands entered the city first. After Kumanovo, the Bregalnica basin of eastern Macedonia passed under Serbian control.
