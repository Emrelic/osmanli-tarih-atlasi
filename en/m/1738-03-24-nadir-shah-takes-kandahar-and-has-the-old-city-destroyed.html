Nadir Shah takes Kandahar and has the old city destroyed

24 March 1738

The Afsharid ruler Nadir Shah took Kandahar, held by the Ghilzai Afghans, after a long siege. The city was destroyed and its people moved to Nadirabad, founded southeast of the old site; old Kandahar never again became a city.
