The Zaporozhian Cossacks founded the Oleshky Sich with the permission of the Crimean khan

1711

When the Russian offensive forced the evacuation of the Sich at Kamianka, the Zaporozhian Cossacks settled, with the permission of the Crimean khan, at Oleshky on the left bank of the Konka river on the khanate's lands. After the Treaty of the Prut, Hordiienko's Cossacks joined them. The khan did not allow the Sich to have walls or cannon.
