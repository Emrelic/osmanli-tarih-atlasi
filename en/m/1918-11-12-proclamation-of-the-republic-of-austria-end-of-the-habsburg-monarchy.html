Proclamation of the Republic of Austria — end of the Habsburg monarchy

12 November 1918

The day after Emperor Charles I withdrew from affairs of state on 11 November, the Provisional National Assembly adopted the law on the form of state and government and the republic was proclaimed. The First Republic, centered on Vienna, took the place of Habsburg Austria. With this proclamation Vienna, Linz, Salzburg, Innsbruck, Klagenfurt, Bregenz and the other German-Austrian cities became the territory of the Republic of Austria (Deutschösterreich).
