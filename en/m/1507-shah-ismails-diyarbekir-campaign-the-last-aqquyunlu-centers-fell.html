Shah Ismail's Diyarbekir campaign: the last Aqquyunlu centers fell

1507

Shah Ismail, who had taken Azerbaijan and the Iranian plateau, turned west in 1507 to the Diyarbekir region, where the Aqquyunlu dynasty was born. The line extending from Amid to Mardin and from Urfa to Harput came under Safavid administration in two moves, and the Aqquyunlu effectively disappeared. This campaign also brought the Safavid border for the first time up against the Ottoman and Mamluk borders; the geographical ground of the tension leading to Chaldiran was thus laid.
