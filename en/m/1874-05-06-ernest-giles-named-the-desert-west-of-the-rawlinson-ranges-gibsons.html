Ernest Giles named the desert west of the Rawlinson Ranges “Gibson's Desert” in memory of his lost companion Alfred Gibson

6 May 1874

Ernest Giles's Western Australian expedition (begun in August 1873) entered the desert west of the Rawlinson Ranges; his companion Alfred Gibson was lost there, riding Giles's horse. In his journal entry of 6 May 1874, Giles writes that he named this region, lying between the Rawlinson Ranges and the next permanent water, “Gibson's Desert.”
