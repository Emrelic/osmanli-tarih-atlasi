Battle of Lade: the Persian fleet defeated the Ionian fleet; Miletus fell

493 BC

The Persian fleet, predominantly Phoenician, routed the Ionian fleet at Lade; Miletus was besieged and destroyed, and its people were deported to Mesopotamia. The Ionian Revolt came to an end and Ionia came under Persian control again.
