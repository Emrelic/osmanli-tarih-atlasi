Istemi Yabgu sent an embassy to Byzantium: the Gokturk-Byzantine alliance

567

Istemi Yabgu, whose relations with the Sasanians had broken down, sent an embassy to Constantinople in 567; this is the first embassy sent from Central Asia to Constantinople. The Byzantines also sent an envoy to Istemi Yabgu's center; the Gokturk-Byzantine alliance put the Sasanians in a difficult position.
