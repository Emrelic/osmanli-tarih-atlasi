Clovis defeats Syagrius at the Battle of Soissons; the Roman rump principality south of the Somme passes to the Franks

486

Clovis attacked the Roman general Syagrius in 486; Syagrius, defeated at the Battle of Soissons, took refuge with the Visigothic king Alaric II and was handed over to him. Clovis thereafter moved his residence to Soissons and gradually seized Belgica Secunda (capital Reims).
