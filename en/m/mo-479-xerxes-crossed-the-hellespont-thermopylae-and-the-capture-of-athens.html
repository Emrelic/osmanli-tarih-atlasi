Xerxes crossed the Hellespont; Thermopylae and the capture of Athens

479 BC

With his great army Xerxes defeated the Spartan-allied force of Leonidas at Thermopylae and entered Attica; Athens was evacuated and burned together with the Acropolis. The Persian army advanced as far as central Greece.
