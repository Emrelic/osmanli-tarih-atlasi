Annihilation of the Sinkat garrison — Sinkat passed to the Mahdist forces

8 February 1884

The Sinkat garrison, which had been besieged for months by the forces of Osman Digna and whose provisions were exhausted, attempted with its commander Tawfiq Bey to break through the siege and reach Suakin; it was annihilated outside the fort on 8 February 1884, and Sinkat fell into the hands of the Mahdist forces. The news reached Suakin on 12 February.
