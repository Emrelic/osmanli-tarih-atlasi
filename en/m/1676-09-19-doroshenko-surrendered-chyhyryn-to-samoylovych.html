Doroshenko surrendered Chyhyryn to Samoylovych

19 September 1676

Petro Doroshenko, hetman of Right-Bank Ukraine under Ottoman protection, surrendered his capital Chyhyryn on 19 September 1676 to Samoylovych, hetman of the Left Bank, and to Russian forces. The Ottomans removed Doroshenko and installed Yuri Khmelnytsky in his place; the siege of 1677 to drive out the Russian garrison in Chyhyryn failed, and the fortress was taken only on 21 August 1678.
