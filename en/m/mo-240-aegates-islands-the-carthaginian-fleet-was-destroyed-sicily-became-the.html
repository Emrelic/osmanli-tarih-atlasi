Aegates Islands: the Carthaginian fleet was destroyed; Sicily became the first Roman province

240 BC

Lutatius Catulus routed Hanno's Carthaginian fleet off the Aegates in March 241; then by the Peace of Lutatius Carthage evacuated Sicily and agreed to pay a war indemnity of 3,200 talents. Sicily (except Hieron's Syracuse) became Rome's first province.
