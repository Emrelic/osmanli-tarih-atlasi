The Loss of Ayamavra (Lefkada) to Venice

6 August 1684

The fleet of Venice, which had joined the Holy League, bombarded the town and then occupied the Island of Ayamavra in the Ionian Sea. With the fall of Preveza in the same year, Ottoman control at the mouth of the Adriatic was broken. These losses were the opening steps of the operation in which Morosini would seize the whole of the Morea within three years.
