The Conquest of the Fortress of Chyhyryn

21 August 1678

After the operation in which Melek Ibrahim Pasha had failed to achieve a result the previous year, the Grand Vizier Merzifonlu Kara Mustafa Pasha, commanding in person, besieged the fortified fortress of Chyhyryn (19/21 July) and captured it on 21 August. The fall of the fortress, a symbol of the Ottoman-Russian rivalry over the Cossack hetmanate in Ukraine, forced Russia to peace. This success was also one of the sources of the self-confidence with which Merzifonlu would turn toward Vienna.
