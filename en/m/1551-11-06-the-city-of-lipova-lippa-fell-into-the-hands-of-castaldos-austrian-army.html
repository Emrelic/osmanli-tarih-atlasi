The city of Lipova (Lippa) fell into the hands of Castaldo's Austrian army — the fortress surrendered on 5 December

6 November 1551

Lipova, which Sokollu Mehmed Pasha had taken in October 1551, was besieged by General Castaldo's army on 4 November; two days later the city fell into the hands of the Austrians. Ulama Pasha defended the fortress for nine more days with a garrison of 5,000 and withdrew by agreement on 5 December 1551. Until the fall of Timișoara in 1552, Lipova remained in Habsburg hands.
