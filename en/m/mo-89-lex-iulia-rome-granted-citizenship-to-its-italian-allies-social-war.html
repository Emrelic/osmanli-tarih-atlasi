Lex Iulia: Rome granted citizenship to its Italian allies (Social War)

89 BC

After the uprising of Rome's Italian allies (91-88), the Lex Iulia (90) and the Lex Plautia Papiria (89) granted Roman citizenship to the loyal allies and to those who surrendered. Thus the Italian peninsula as far as the Po became a single citizen society.
