Beginning of the sixteen-year Ottoman-Venetian War

28 July 1463

The Venetians, in order to halt the Ottoman advance in the Morea and the Aegean, began the war in 1463 by allying with the Karamanid and Aqquyunlu rulers. This strategy of encirclement, set up with Uzun Hasan in the east, Venice in the west and Karaman in between, forced the Conqueror to fight on several fronts at once. The struggle, which lasted sixteen years, was to cover all the great operations of the period from Otlukbeli to the Albanian campaigns, and to end only with the Treaty of Istanbul of 1479.
