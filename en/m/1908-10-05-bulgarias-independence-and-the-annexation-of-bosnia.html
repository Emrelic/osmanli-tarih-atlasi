Bulgaria's independence and the annexation of Bosnia

5 October 1908

In the confusion of the constitutional revolution, Bulgaria declared independence and Austria-Hungary annexed Bosnia-Herzegovina. With this proclamation all the lands of the Principality of Bulgaria (Varna, Shumen, Ruse, Burgas, Sliven and others) became the independent Kingdom of Bulgaria.
