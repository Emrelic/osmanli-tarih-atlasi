Egypt rebelled against Persian rule; Xerxes I suppressed the revolt and governed Egypt more harshly

485 BC

The Egyptian revolt, which had begun before the death of Darius I, was suppressed in the first years of Xerxes I; Xerxes appointed his brother Achaemenes satrap and began to govern Egypt more harshly than in the time of Darius.
