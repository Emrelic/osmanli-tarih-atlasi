The Loss of Yanıkkale (Győr)

29 March 1598

Units under Miklós Pálffy and Adolf von Schwarzenberg retook Yanıkkale by blowing up the fortress gates in a surprise attack. The loss of the fortress, which Koca Sinan Pasha had obtained with great effort four years earlier, collapsed the Ottoman forward line in Hungary. Although the enemy forces that besieged Buda in the same year were repulsed, the balance of the war was gradually turning into a war of attrition.
