Recapture of Semendire (Smederevo) from the Habsburgs (the crown of the Kingdom of Hungary) — the War of 1737-39

1 August 1738

According to the TDV article on Semendire, the fortress, ceded to Austria at Passarowitz in 1717, was recaptured through the Ottoman advance on the Danube-Sava front of the Ottoman-Austrian War that began in 1737; Habsburg (crown of the Kingdom of Hungary) forces were able to hold on in the region until August 1738, after which date Semendire came under Ottoman administration again. The Treaty of Belgrade (18 September 1739), signed a year later, would confirm this gain — together with Belgrade and northern Serbia.
