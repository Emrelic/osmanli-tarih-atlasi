Dias, having rounded the southern tip of Africa, anchors at Mossel Bay

3 February 1488

Dias, driven by a storm to the south of Africa, found present-day Mossel Bay on St Blaise's day (3 February 1488) under the name Bahia dos Vaqueiros; he had rounded the Cape of Good Hope without realizing it.
