The Battle of Panion: Antiochus III defeated Scopas; Coele-Syria passed from the Ptolemies to the Seleucids

199 BC

In the Fifth Syrian War Antiochus III routed the Ptolemaic commander Scopas of Aetolia at Paneion; according to Josephus, Antiochus recovered the cities of Coele-Syria that Scopas had seized and took Samaria as well, and the Jews joined Antiochus of their own accord. Coele-Syria (Syria-Palestine) thereafter passed under Seleucid rule; the Ptolemaic state withdrew from the region.
