The Sable Incident — Grand Vizier Ahmed Pasha's fur levy prepared Ibrahim's end

1647

While Sultan Ibrahim's passion for sable furs and amber grew in the palace, Grand Vizier Ahmed Pasha around 1647-1648 began a practice of forcibly collecting the price of sable furs from the dignitaries of the state; the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi records that officials regarded this as an open 'extortion'. When the Janissary officers collectively refused to pay, this resistance became the direct trigger of the process leading to the sultan's deposition — on the morning of 7 August 1648 the assembled officials first killed Ahmed Pasha and the next day deposed Ibrahim.
