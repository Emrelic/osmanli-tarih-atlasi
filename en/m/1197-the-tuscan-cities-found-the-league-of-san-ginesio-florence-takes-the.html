The Tuscan cities found the League of San Ginesio; Florence takes the lead

1197

In the anti-imperial, pro-papal league of Tuscan cities founded at San Ginesio in 1197 on the death of Henry VI, Florence came to the fore; this is the direction of Florentine foreign policy from then on.
