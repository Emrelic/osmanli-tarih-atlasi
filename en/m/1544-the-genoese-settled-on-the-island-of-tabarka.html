The Genoese settled on the island of Tabarka

1544

Following an agreement made by Charles V with the ruler of Tunis, permitting the building of a fortress on the coast and coral fishing, a Genoese community from Pegli was settled on the island of Tabarka. The island became a trading base ruled by the Lomellini family.
