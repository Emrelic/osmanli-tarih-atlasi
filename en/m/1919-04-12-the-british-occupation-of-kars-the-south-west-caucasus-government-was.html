The British occupation of Kars — the South-West Caucasus Government was dissolved

12 April 1919

In accordance with the provision of the Armistice of Mudros requiring withdrawal to the borders of 1914, the Ottoman army had evacuated Kars. On 5 November 1918 the population of the region founded the Kars Islamic Council against Armenian and Georgian attacks, and through the congress of January 1919 turned it into the National Provisional Government of the South-West Caucasus. According to the TDV article on Kars, the British occupied Kars on 12 April 1919 and dissolved this government, arrested its members and sent them into exile, and left control of the city to the Armenians.
