Death of Osman Gazi and Orhan Bey's accession to the beylik

1324

Osman Gazi, who gave the beylik its name, died in the year 724 (1324), as attested by the endowment deeds (vakfiye) of Asporça Hatun and Mekece. His son Orhan, who had been at the head of the army on campaigns since 1305, took the head of the beylik on his father's death without any struggle for the throne. The founding generation's passing the standard on without upheaval shows that the dynasty passed its first great test on the road from frontier beylik to state with calm.
