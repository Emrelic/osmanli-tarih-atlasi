Mas'ud made peace: Dihistan was left to Chaghri Beg, Nasa to Toghril Beg, and Farawa to Musa Yabgu

1 August 1035

After the defeat, Sultan Mas'ud gave Dihistan to Chaghri Beg, Nasa to Toghril Beg and Farawa to Musa Yabgu; the Seljuks were to be considered subordinate and autonomous, and a diploma, a robe of honour and a banner were sent (August 1035). The Seljuk begs did not take the peace seriously.
