Achaean War: the Achaean League was defeated by Rome and the leagues were dissolved

145 BC

The Achaean League, which rebelled against Rome over a border dispute with Sparta, was defeated at Leucopetra in 146; Corinth was destroyed and the League was effectively dissolved, and part of Greece came under the supervision of the province of Macedonia.
