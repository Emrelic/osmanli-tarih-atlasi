Barbarossa's Aegean islands campaign: the Duchy of Naxos came under Ottoman control

1537

The Duchy of Naxos, governed since 1207 under Venetian suzerainty by Latin dukes, came under Ottoman control through Barbaros Hayreddin Pasha's islands campaign. The status of the island was not touched; the duchy continued as an Ottoman vassal, and by the Ottoman-Venetian treaty of 1540 the right of sovereignty was officially transferred to the Ottomans.
