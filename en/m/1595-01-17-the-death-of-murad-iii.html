The Death of Murad III

17 January 1595

After a reign of twenty-one years, Murad III died in the days when the Wallachian army burned Brăila and reached as far as Silistra, and was buried in the cemetery of Hagia Sophia. His son Mehmed III, summoned from Manisa, reached Istanbul ten days later and ascended the throne (27 January 1595).
