Octavian entered Alexandria; the Ptolemaic state came to an end and Egypt passed to Rome

1 August 29 BC

After Antony's fleet and cavalry went over to Octavian, Octavian entered Alexandria; Antony took his own life, and Cleopatra died shortly afterwards. Octavian spared the people of Alexandria, did not visit the tombs of the Ptolemies, saying 'I wished to see a king, not corpses', and gave Egypt as a tributary country to Cornelius Gallus. With this date the three-hundred-year Ptolemaic kingdom came to an end.
