Death of Nedim during the Patrona Halil revolt

28 October 1730

Nedim, who was a müderris at the Sekban Ali Pasha Madrasa when the Patrona Halil Revolt broke out, died in the days following the revolt. The day of death is not known; however, since the qassam hujja concerning his estate was drawn up on 15 Rabi' al-Akhir 1143 (28 October 1730), he died before this date. There are different accounts concerning the cause of death: some of the sources write that he died of a fear sickness (illet-i vehime) or of a trembling illness caught through drink and opium, while Müstakimzade writes that during the upheaval he climbed onto the roof of his house out of fear and died falling from it. With his death the cultural brilliance of the Tulip Period also effectively came to an end.
