The Battle of Gaza: Ptolemy and Seleucus defeated Demetrius (Syria briefly in Ptolemaic hands)

311 BC

Demetrius, son of Antigonus, was defeated before Gaza by the combined army of Ptolemy and Seleucus; Ptolemy entered Coele-Syria. With Antigonus's counterstroke the region was soon lost again, and the peace (311 BCE) fixed this.
