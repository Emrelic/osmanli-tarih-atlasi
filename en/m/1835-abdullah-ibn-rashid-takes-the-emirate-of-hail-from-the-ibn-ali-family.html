Abdullah ibn Rashid takes the emirate of Ha'il from the Ibn Ali family — the Shammar (Rashidi) Emirate is founded

1835

Abdullah ibn Rashid, of the Abde branch, the largest branch of the Shammar tribe, won in 1835 the struggle he had begun together with his brother Ubayd against the Ibn Ali family that ruled Ha'il, and seized the emirate. Thanks to its position on the Mesopotamia–Hijaz trade and pilgrimage route, Ha'il became the center of the Rashidi rule over Jabal Shammar, which lasted until 1921.
