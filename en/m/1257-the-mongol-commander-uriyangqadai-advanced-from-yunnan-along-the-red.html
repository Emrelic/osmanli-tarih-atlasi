The Mongol commander Uriyangqadai advanced from Yunnan along the Red River to Thăng Long (1257): the first Mongol invasion of the Trần

1257

Uriyangqadai, who had crushed Nanzhao (Yunnan), advanced along the Red River to Thăng Long in the summer and autumn of 1257 with a force of about 25,000 men (5,000 Mongols, 20,000 Yunnanese); his aim was to attack the Southern Song from the south. Taylor describes the event as an unprecedented crisis for the Trần state.
