The Khwarazm-Shahs came to rule Sarakhs and its surroundings together with all of Khurasan

1193

Sarakhs, which had remained for a time in the hands of the Oghuz after the Seljuk period, came under the rule of the Khwarazm-Shahs together with all of Khurasan (1193); Tekish had destroyed the city's walls in 1190 during the dispute with Sultan Shah.
