Preston handed the administration of Kars to the Armenian governor

30 April 1919

The British commander Preston, who dissolved the South-West Caucasus Government on 12-13 April 1919, abolished the provisional shura government on 30 April 1919 and handed the administration of the city to the Armenian Karganov, who was made Governor of Kars; the period of Armenian administration in Kars began and lasted until Kâzım Karabekir's victory on 30 October 1920.
