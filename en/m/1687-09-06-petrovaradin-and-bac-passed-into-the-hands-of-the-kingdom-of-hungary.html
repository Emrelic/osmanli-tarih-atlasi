Petrovaradin and Bač passed into the hands of the Kingdom of Hungary (Habsburg Crown): withdrawal from Bačka after Harsány

6 September 1687

The army of Sarı Süleyman Pasha, retreating from the rout at Harsány, passed through Petrovaradin on 1 September 1687 and left the fortress to Cafer Pasha; soon afterwards Petrovaradin and Bač in Bačka passed into the hands of the allied Habsburg forces. When the Sava was accepted as the frontier at Karlowitz, Petrovaradin remained on the Austrian side.
