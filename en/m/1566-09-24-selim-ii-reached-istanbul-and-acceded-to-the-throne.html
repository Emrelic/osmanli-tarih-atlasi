Selim II reached Istanbul and acceded to the throne

24 September 1566

After the death of Süleyman the Lawgiver at Szigetvár, the news was kept secret for weeks by Sokollu Mehmed Pasha in order not to disturb order. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, while Selânikî gives the date of Selim's arrival in Istanbul as 15 Rabi al-Awwal 974 (30 September 1566), most contemporary historians accept 9 Rabi al-Awwal (24 September) as correct. The death of Süleyman the Lawgiver was officially announced only on 24-25 October 1566 in Belgrade.
