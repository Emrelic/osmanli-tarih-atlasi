Southern Kurdistan operation: Column C entered Kifri

28 April 1918

In the Southern Kurdistan operation the British Column C entered Kifri on the morning of 28 April 1918 without resistance. When Kirkuk was abandoned at the end of May, Kifri and Tuz Khurmatu were also held as British forward outposts.
