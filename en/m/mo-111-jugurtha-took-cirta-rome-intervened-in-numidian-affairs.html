Jugurtha took Cirta: Rome intervened in Numidian affairs

111 BC

In 112 BC Jugurtha besieged Cirta and killed Adherbal; the Italian merchants killed in the city pushed Rome to declare war on Jugurtha. The war lasted until 105.
