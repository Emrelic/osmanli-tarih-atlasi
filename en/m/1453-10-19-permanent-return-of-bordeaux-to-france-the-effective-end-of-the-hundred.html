Permanent return of Bordeaux to France — the effective end of the Hundred Years' War

19 October 1453

With the death of John Talbot and the destruction of his army at the Battle of Castillon on 17 July 1453, English resistance in Gascony collapsed; besieged Bordeaux surrendered to the French forces on 19 October 1453. This surrender is regarded as the moment the Hundred Years' War, which had lasted 116 years, effectively ended (no formal peace treaty was ever signed), and it narrowed England's dominion on the Continent to Calais.
