Beginning of the Jomon: Sosoki (Incipient) period: Sosoki ends

9649 BC

The incipient phase of the Jomon culture (Japanese archipelago): pottery, temporarily sedentary hunting and gathering. The beginning is placed ~15,500 years ago with the first protruding-line (Ryusenmon) vessels.
