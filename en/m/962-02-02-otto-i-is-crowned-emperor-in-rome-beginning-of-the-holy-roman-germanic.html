Otto I is crowned emperor in Rome; beginning of the Holy Roman-Germanic Empire

2 February 962

Otto crossed into Italy at the call of Pope John XII for help against Berengar; after arriving at Pavia at Christmas 961, he was crowned emperor in Rome on 2 February 962 and confirmed the rights and privileges of the papacy. The Roman people swore to obtain the emperor's approval in the election of the pope. At this date East Francia (the German Kingdom) came to an end and Germany (Holy Roman) was established in its place: East Frankish cities such as Frankfurt, Hamburg and Dorestad (Wijk bij Duurstede) passed to Germany (the Holy Roman Empire).
