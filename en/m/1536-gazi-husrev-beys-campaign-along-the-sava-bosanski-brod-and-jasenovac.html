Gazi Husrev Bey's campaign along the Sava: Bosanski Brod and Jasenovac

1536

After Jajce and Banja Luka were taken in 1528, the fortresses along the Sava River remained for some time longer in the Croatian-Slavonian frontier tied to the Habsburg crown. Gazi Husrev Bey, who in 1536 became sancak bey of Bosnia for the third time, took Požega and its surroundings that same year and also added the fortresses of Brod and Jasenovac on the Sava bank to Ottoman administration. Thus the northern frontier of Bosnia reached the Sava; the region was completed two years later with the capture of Dubica.
