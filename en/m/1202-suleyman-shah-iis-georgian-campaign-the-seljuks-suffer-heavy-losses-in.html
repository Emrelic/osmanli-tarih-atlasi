Suleyman Shah II's Georgian campaign: the Seljuks suffer heavy losses in a Georgian raid on the camp; Behram Shah, Mengujekid lord of Erzincan, is taken prisoner

1202

Suleyman Shah II, who in 1202 set out on campaign against the Georgians attacking Turkish lands, was struck by a Georgian raid while resting in camp and suffered heavy losses; among the prisoners was Behram Shah, the Mengujekid lord of Erzincan.
