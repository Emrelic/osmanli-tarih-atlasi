Sanxingdui (Guanghan, Sichuan) civilization: the Sanxingdui civilization forms (cross-dating)

1599 BC

A civilization in the Sichuan basin producing bronze, gold, jade and bronze masks, independent of the Shang. According to Zhu, the civilization took shape in the 2nd phase (Period II; great city wall) and ended at the end of the Shang / beginning of the Zhou (~1050).
