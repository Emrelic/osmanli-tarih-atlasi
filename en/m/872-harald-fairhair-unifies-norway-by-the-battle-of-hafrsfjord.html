Harald Fairhair unifies Norway by the Battle of Hafrsfjord (traditionally 872)

872

According to tradition, Harald Fairhair defeated his rivals at the Battle of Hafrsfjord in 872 and became the first king of Norway; however, the SNL regards this only as a historiography that took shape later (twelfth century), and dates Harald's reign from about 865 to 930-933.
