Founding of the Sultanate of Darfur: the Keira dynasty and Sulayman Solong

1695

According to the TDV, the founding of the Sultanate of Darfur falls after the disorder that followed Bumu al-Kasir's invasion of the region in the late seventeenth century. The Fur, under the leadership of the Kira (Keira) dynasty headed by Sulayman Solong, took rule from the hands of the Daju and Tunjur and established a firm administration. Sulayman Solong reigned from 1695 to 1715. The sultans of Darfur corresponded with the Sublime Porte in the following centuries; Abdulmejid and Abdulaziz confirmed their rule.
