Thutmose I made an Asiatic campaign reaching the banks of the Euphrates (Naharin)

1498 BC

According to the inscriptions of Ahmose-Pennekhbet and Ahmose son of Ebana, after his Nubian campaign Thutmose I launched a great campaign to Naharin; Thutmose III later adds his own stele beside the boundary stele that his father had set up on the Euphrates. This is the first recorded period in which Egyptian influence extended to the Euphrates.
