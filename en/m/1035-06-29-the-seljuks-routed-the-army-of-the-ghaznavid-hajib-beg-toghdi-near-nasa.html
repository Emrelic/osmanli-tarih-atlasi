The Seljuks routed the army of the Ghaznavid hajib Beg Toghdi near Nasa

29 June 1035

The army of 17,000 men under the hajib Beg Toghdi, sent by Sultan Mas'ud, was defeated near Nasa on 19 Sha'ban 426 (29 June 1035); this victory gave the Seljuks confidence that they could found a state in the region. (TDV Ghaznavids places the same clash, under the name Hisar-i Taq, in 426/1035.)
