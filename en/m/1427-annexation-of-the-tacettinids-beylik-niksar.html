Annexation of the Tacettinids Beylik: Niksar

1427

Under pressure from Yörgüç Pasha, the beylerbeyi of Amasya, Taceddinoğlu Hüsameddin Hasan Bey handed over the keys of his country, centred on Niksar, in the year 830 (1427); he was given a sancak in Rumelia. The beylik, founded in the second half of the fourteenth century and whose borders at times extended to Kelkit, Amasya, Samsun, Ordu and Giresun, thus came to an end. In the same year the lands belonging to the Hacıemiroğulları were also surveyed (tahrir) and attached to the sancak of Canik; the period of the beyliks in the central Black Sea region was closed.
