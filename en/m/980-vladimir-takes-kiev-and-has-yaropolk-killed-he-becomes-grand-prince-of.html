Vladimir takes Kiev and has Yaropolk killed; he becomes Grand Prince of Kiev

980

Vladimir, who had fled to Novgorod and brought Varangians from Scandinavia, took Novgorod, Polotsk and then Kiev in 980; Yaropolk was killed. For 35 years Vladimir extended the borders of Rus'.
