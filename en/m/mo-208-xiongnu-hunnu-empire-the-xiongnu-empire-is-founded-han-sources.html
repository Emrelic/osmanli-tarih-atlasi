Xiongnu (Hunnu) empire: the Xiongnu Empire is founded (Han sources)

208 BC

The first steppe empire of the Central Asian steppes, founded with the rise of Modun (Maodun) Chanyu in 209 BCE; a long rivalry with the Han; the formal empire ends with the defeat of the Northern Xiongnu in 91 CE.
