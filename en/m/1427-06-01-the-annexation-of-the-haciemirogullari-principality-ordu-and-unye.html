The annexation of the Hacıemiroğulları Principality — Ordu and Ünye

1 June 1427

The Turkmen principality, founded around Ordu by Bayram Bey and expanded in the 1350s by his son Hacı Emir, was a small but strategic coastal state with its centre at present-day Eskipazar: it was the only independent area of domination between Samsun and Trebizond. Emir Süleyman had declared his allegiance in Bayezid the Thunderbolt's Canik operation of 1398, and after the Battle of Ankara the principality had become free again. In the time of Murad II, in the same years as the liquidation of the Tâceddinoğulları, the principality's lands were attached to direct Ottoman administration, and the Black Sea coast from Samsun to Giresun was united in a single hand.
