The Erzurum and Aşkale region changed hands in the Qaraquyunlu-Timurid contest

1408

In the power vacuum that followed Timur's death in 1405, the Qaraquyunlu ruler Qara Yusuf moved between 1406 and 1408 against the Timurid governors in eastern Anatolia; Erzurum and, around it, Aşkale passed out of Timurid control in this struggle and came under Qaraquyunlu influence.
