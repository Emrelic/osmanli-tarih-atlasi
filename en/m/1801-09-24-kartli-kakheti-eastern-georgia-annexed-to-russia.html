Kartli-Kakheti (Eastern Georgia) annexed to Russia

24 September 1801

The tsardom of Kartli-Kakheti, which had accepted Russian protection in 1783 and suffered Iran's devastating campaign in 1795, was abolished; by an order dated 12 September 1801 it was declared a province of Russia and the Russian administrative system began to be installed. Georgia lost its independence from this date; within a few years Mingrelia (1803), Imereti and Guria (1804) also came under Russian rule and Russia reached the Ottoman Caucasus frontier. With this event Gori, Dusheti (Kartli) and Telavi (Kakheti) passed to Russia.
