Battle of Konya: the grand vizier taken prisoner

21 December 1832

The Ottoman army, dispersed in foggy weather, suffered a heavy defeat, and the grand vizier Reşid Mehmed Pasha was taken prisoner. The central army of the state no longer existed; there was no force left to stand between Istanbul and the Egyptian forces.
