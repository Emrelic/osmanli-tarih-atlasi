The rout of Serav (Pul-i Shikasta) — Halil Pasha's Iranian campaign

10 September 1618

Halil Pasha, who became commander in place of Öküz Mehmed Pasha, marched on Iran again in 1618. The battle fought at the locality of Pul-i Shikasta between Tabriz and Ardabil ended in the defeat of the Ottoman army. After this rout, which is also referred to in the sources as the Battle of Serav, the Treaty of Serav was signed in the same month.
