The execution of Nevşehirli Damad Ibrahim Pasha

1 October 1730

According to the TDV article on 'Damad İbrâhim Paşa, Nevşehirli', the body of the grand vizier, who was killed in the palace during the Patrona Halil Rebellion, was dragged through the streets of Istanbul and abandoned near the Fountain of Ahmed III in the Sultanahmet Square. His twelve-year grand vizierate and the Tulip Period thus ended in a bloody manner.
