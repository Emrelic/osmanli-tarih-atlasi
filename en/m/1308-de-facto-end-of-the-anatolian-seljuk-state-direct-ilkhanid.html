De facto end of the Anatolian Seljuk State: direct Ilkhanid administration

1308

According to the TDV's Mesud II entry, Giyath al-Din Mesud II, who died in Kayseri in 708 (1308) after a long illness, is according to the generally accepted view the last ruler of the Anatolian Seljuk State — the candidates placed on the throne after his death received the recognition neither of the internal authorities nor of the Ilkhanids. The region was already being governed de facto by Ilkhanid officials throughout Mesud's reign; with his death this direct administration also lost its formal cover in centers such as Konya, Niğde and Aksaray.
