The Victory of Alaçayır and the Suppression of the Kalenderoğlu Rebellion

9 August 1608

Kalenderoğlu, the Celali leader who had put the regions of Manisa and Bursa under tribute, was defeated at Alaçayır by Kuyucu Murad Pasha and was forced to flee to Iran. With this victory the last of the great Celali armies in Anatolia was dispersed. Kuyucu Murad Pasha's harsh purge, in which he is rumoured to have filled wells that he had dug with the rebels he had killed, secured order in the short term but could not make up for Anatolia's loss of population and production.
