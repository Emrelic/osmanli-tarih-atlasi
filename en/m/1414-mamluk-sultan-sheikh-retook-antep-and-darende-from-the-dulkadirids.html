Mamluk Sultan Sheikh retook Antep and Darende from the Dulkadirids

1414

The Mamluk Sultan Sheikh, worried by the Dulkadirids' friendship with the Ottomans, set out on campaign in 1414; he retook Antep and Darende, which he had earlier given of his own accord to the Dulkadirids.
