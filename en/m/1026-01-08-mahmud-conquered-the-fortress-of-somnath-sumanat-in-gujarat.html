Mahmud conquered the fortress of Somnath (Sumanat) in Gujarat

8 January 1026

Mahmud set out from Ghazna with 30,000 horsemen in Sha'ban 416 (October 1025) and conquered the fortress of Somnath on 16 Dhu'l-Qa'da 416 (8 January 1026); the echoes of the victory spread through the whole Islamic world, and the caliph gave him the title 'Kahf al-Dawla wa'l-Islam'.
