Hezarfen Ahmed Çelebi's winged flight (legend)

1 June 1638

According to the account related by Evliya Çelebi, Hezarfen Ahmed Çelebi managed to glide from the Galata Tower as far as Üsküdar with wings he had made himself; although its historical certainty is disputed, it became a symbol of technical imagination. The sultan, who had at first rewarded Hezarfen, later exiled him to Algiers because of the stir the event caused.
