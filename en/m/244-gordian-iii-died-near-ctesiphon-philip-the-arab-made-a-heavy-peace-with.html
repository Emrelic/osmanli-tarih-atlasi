Gordian III died near Ctesiphon; Philip the Arab made a heavy peace with Shapur

244

Gordian III, after an initially successful Mesopotamian campaign, was defeated by Shapur and died. His successor Philip the Arab made peace with the Persians; in his own inscription (ŠKZ) Shapur counts Philip's accession as his own doing and reports that he took tribute.
