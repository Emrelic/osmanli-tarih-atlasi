Kuwait left British protection and became fully independent

19 June 1961

With notes exchanged between Kuwait and the United Kingdom on 19 June 1961, the British protection in force since the 1899 agreement ended and Kuwait became fully independent. On the map the British layer over Kuwait ends on this day.
