Recapture of Bordeaux by England — John Talbot's last Gascon campaign

23 October 1452

Bordeaux, lost to the French in 1451, surrendered on the night of 22-23 October 1452 to the English army under the elderly commander John Talbot, after its people rose up against the French garrison and opened the gates; this was England's last and short-lived victory in Gascony in the Hundred Years' War. Bordeaux would return permanently to France a year later after the Battle of Castillon.
