Treaty of Turkmenchay: Erivan and Talysh pass to Russia

22 February 1828

When the 1826-1828 Russo-Persian War ended in a heavy Iranian defeat, by the treaty signed at Turkmenchay the khanates of Erivan and Nakhchivan and the Talysh region on the Caspian coast (Lankaran, Astara) were left to Russia; a heavy war indemnity was also imposed. According to the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi entry on Erivan, this loss is the moment when the Russo-Iranian border in the Caucasus was drawn in a form that would remain largely valid to the present day, and it also triggered the migration of the Armenian population to Erivan. By this treaty the Erivan khanate (Echmiadzin, Iğdır, Norapat, Beri) and the Nakhchivan khanate (Julfa, Sharur) passed to Russia.
