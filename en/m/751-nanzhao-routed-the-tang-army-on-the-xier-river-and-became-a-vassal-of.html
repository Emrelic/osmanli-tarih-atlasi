Nanzhao routed the Tang army on the Xi'er River and became a vassal of Tibet

751

The army of about 80,000 men of the military governor of Sichuan, Xianyu Zhongtong, was defeated by the Nanzhao king Geluofeng at Lunan and on the shore of the Xi'er River (Erhai) (Zizhi tongjian: 60,000 dead); Geluofeng had the dead collected and a victory monument made, and became a vassal of Tibet.
