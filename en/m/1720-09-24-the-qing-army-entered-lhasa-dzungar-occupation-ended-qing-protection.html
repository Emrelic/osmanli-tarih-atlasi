The Qing army entered Lhasa — Dzungar occupation ended, Qing protection began

24 September 1720

Of the two armies Kangxi sent to Tibet, the Sichuan column under Galbi reached Lhasa after a difficult march and took the city without resistance; the Dzungars had concentrated all their forces at Dam against the Kokonor army coming from the north. With the Dzungars' withdrawal from Tibet, the Qing established a lasting protectorate order in Lhasa.
