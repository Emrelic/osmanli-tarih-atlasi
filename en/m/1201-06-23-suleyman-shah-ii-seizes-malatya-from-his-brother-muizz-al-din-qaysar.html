Suleyman Shah II seizes Malatya from his brother Mu'izz al-Din Qaysar Shah

23 June 1201

Suleyman Shah, who moved against his brother Mu'izz al-Din Qaysar Shah, Malik of Malatya, who resisted submitting to him, seized the city on 19 Ramadan 597 / 23 June 1201.
