Ya'qub ibn Layth was defeated for the first time by Abbasid forces at Dayr al-Aqul, near Baghdad

1 April 876

In Rajab 262 (April 876), at Dayr al-Aqul, fifty miles from Baghdad, Ya'qub was defeated for the first time in his life by Abbasid forces; he died while preparing to take his revenge.
