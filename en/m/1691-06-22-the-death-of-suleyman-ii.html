The death of Süleyman II

22 June 1691

Süleyman II, who was placed on the throne in 1687 after spending about forty years in the kafes, died at Edirne toward noon on Friday, 22 June 1691, while, ill, he was following the army's preparations; his reign lasted three years, eight months and two days. The grand vizier had given in advance the necessary instructions for his brother Ahmed to be placed on the throne in the event of the sultan's death. The funeral prayer was performed the same day at the Alay Köşkü; his body was brought to Istanbul and buried in the tomb of Süleyman the Lawgiver, on his right side.
