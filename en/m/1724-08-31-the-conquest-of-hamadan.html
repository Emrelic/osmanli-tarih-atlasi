The conquest of Hamadan

31 August 1724

The army marching east from Kermanshah took Hamadan, at the western threshold of the Iranian plateau, after a long siege. The city was the last great stopping place on the road to Isfahan, the easternmost point the Ottoman armies had been able to reach since the Campaign of the Two Iraqs. The Treaty of Istanbul with Russia, signed the same year, set down on paper the partition of the dissolving Iran between the Ottomans and Russia.
