At the end of the Later Three Years' War, Mutsu and Dewa were united in the hands of Kiyohira; the Oshu Fujiwara dynasty began at Hiraizumi

1087

After Minamoto no Yoshiie ended the Later Three Years' War (1083-87) by defeating Kiyohara no Iehira, Mutsu and Dewa were united under the rule of Kiyohara no Kiyohira; Kiyohira took his father's surname Fujiwara and made Hiraizumi his center (Oshu Fujiwara). The region remained independent of central control until Yoritomo's conquest of 1189.
