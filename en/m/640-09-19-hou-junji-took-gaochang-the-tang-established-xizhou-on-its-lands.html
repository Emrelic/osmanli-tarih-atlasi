Hou Junji took Gaochang; the Tang established Xizhou on its lands

19 September 640

Hou Junji, commander-in-chief of the Jiaohe route, captured Gaochang (Turfan) and the Tang established the prefecture of Xizhou there; Qu Zhisheng, king of Gaochang, was brought as a prisoner before Taizong in the 12th month, and the army returned to China.
