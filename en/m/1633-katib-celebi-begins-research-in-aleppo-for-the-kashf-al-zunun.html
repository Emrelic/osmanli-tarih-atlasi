Katib Çelebi begins research in Aleppo for the Kashf al-Zunun

1633

Katib Çelebi began in 1633 in Aleppo to gather the material for the Kashf al-Zunun, which would later be his bibliographical masterpiece. During this work, which would last twenty years, he combed the libraries and bookshops of the cities he went to one by one.
