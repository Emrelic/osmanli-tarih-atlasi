Proto-Elamite writing system (Susa): Proto-Elamite writing appears

3199 BC

The undeciphered Proto-Elamite writing system at Susa, contemporary with Uruk III; it spreads to eight sites on the Iranian plateau. The 'c. 3100 BCE' in the brief was not found in a single source; the sources give a beginning of 3200.
