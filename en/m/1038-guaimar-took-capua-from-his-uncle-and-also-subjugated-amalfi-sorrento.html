Guaimar took Capua from his uncle and also subjugated Amalfi, Sorrento, and Gaeta

1038

Guaimar takes Capua from his uncle Pandulf IV; then he also subjugates Amalfi, Sorrento, and Gaeta, and receives recognition from the Emperor Conrad II.
