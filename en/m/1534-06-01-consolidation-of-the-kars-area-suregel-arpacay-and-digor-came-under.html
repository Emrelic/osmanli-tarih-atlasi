Consolidation of the Kars area: Şüregel, Arpaçay and Digor came under Ottoman administration

1 June 1534

During the Campaign of the Two Iraqs, the fortresses of Pasin, Şüregel and Oltu fell into Ottoman hands; it is highly probable that Kars Fortress was also attached by the force at this time. The Arpaçay and Digor districts to the north and east of Kars came under Ottoman control in the same operation.
