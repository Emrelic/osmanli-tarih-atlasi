Roman-Sasanian treaty partitioning Armenia (Theodosius I - Shapur III)

387

The greater (eastern) part of Armenia was left to Sasanian influence and the smaller western part to Roman influence; the Armenian Arsacid kingdom was effectively on the road to extinction. The partition determined the Roman-Iranian frontier for about two centuries.
