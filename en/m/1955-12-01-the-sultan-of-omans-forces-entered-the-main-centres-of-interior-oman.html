The Sultan of Oman's forces entered the main centres of interior Oman; the Ibadi imamate effectively came to an end

1 December 1955

In December 1955 the Sultan of Oman moved to reassert his authority, and his forces entered the main inhabited centres of interior Oman (Bahla, Izki, Nizwa); the Ibadi imamate's rule over interior Oman effectively ended. The source gives only the month; the map shows 1 December 1955.
