Treaty of Tartu: Soviet Russia recognised Estonia's independence, Petseri remained in Estonia

2 February 1920

By the treaty ending the Estonian War of Independence, Soviet Russia recognised Estonia's independence. The border drawn ran somewhat east of today's border and left Petseri (Pechory) in Estonia in the southeast.
