Sennacherib destroyed Babylon, plundered its temples and let the Euphrates flood the city

688 BC

After Babylon's repeated revolts, Sennacherib took and destroyed the city, carried off the statue of Marduk and let the waters of the canals flood the city. Babylon remained in ruins for half a century, until Esarhaddon's restoration; the destruction is the harshest move of Assyria's Babylonian policy.
