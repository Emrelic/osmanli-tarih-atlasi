The fez was declared the official headgear

1 June 1829

Mahmud II, making a radical change in the dress of officials and soldiers, declared the fez the official headgear in place of the turban. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Fes, this decision was announced by a hatt-ı hümâyun and put into practice together with the Western-style reforms of the Asâkir-i Mansûre-i Muhammediyye. The change also meant the end of the traditional hierarchy of dress.
