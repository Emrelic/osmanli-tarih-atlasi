Conversion of the ruler of the Qasim Khanate to Christianity and his removal from the khanate

1573

The ruler at the head of the khanate took the name 'Simeon' and became a Christian; the Russians, taking into account the reaction of the Muslim subjects, removed him from the khanate. This person was soon afterward declared nominal ruler in Moscow by Ivan the Terrible with the title 'grand prince of the tsar and of Russia' (1575) — the most striking intersection between the Qasim Khanate and the throne of Moscow.
