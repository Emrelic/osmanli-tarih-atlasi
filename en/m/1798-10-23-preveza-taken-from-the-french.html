Preveza taken from the French

23 October 1798

When the Republic of Venice fell in 1797, the Ionian Islands, along with Preveza, Parga and Vonitsa on the mainland, passed to France. Ali Pasha of Tepelena, governor of Yanina, took advantage of the war that broke out over the French occupation of Egypt and took Preveza in October 1798. Thus this port, left to Venice at Passarowitz, came under Ottoman rule eighty years later; Butrint too passed to the Ottomans in the same operation.
