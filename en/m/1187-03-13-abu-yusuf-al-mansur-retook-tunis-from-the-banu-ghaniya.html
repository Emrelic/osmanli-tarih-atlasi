Abu Yusuf al-Mansur retook Tunis from the Banu Ghaniya

13 March 1187

The Banu Ghaniya had invaded Bijaya, Algiers, Miliana, and Tunis in 580 (1184); al-Mansur retook Tunis in 583 (1187) but could not expel the Banu Ghaniya from the region entirely.
