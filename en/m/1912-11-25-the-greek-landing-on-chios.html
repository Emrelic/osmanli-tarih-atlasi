The Greek landing on Chios

25 November 1912

The Greeks made a landing on Chios, which had been bombarded on 7 April 1912 in the First Balkan War; the Ottoman garrison surrendered on 3 January 1913. The island was left to Greece by the Treaty of Lausanne (24 July 1923).
