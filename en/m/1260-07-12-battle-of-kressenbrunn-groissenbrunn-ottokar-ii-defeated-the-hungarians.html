Battle of Kressenbrunn (Groißenbrunn): Ottokar II defeated the Hungarians; Béla gave up Styria (Graz)

12 July 1260

At the end of 1259 a revolt against Hungarian rule broke out in Styria; with the support of Ottokar II the Hungarian army was defeated at Kroissenbrunn in the Marchfeld on 12 July 1260, Béla IV gave up Styria, and Styria was united with Austria in Ottokar's hands.
