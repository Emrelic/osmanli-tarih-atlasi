The Muscovite Tsardom took Astrakhan — the Lower Volga came under Russian control

1556

In 1556 the Muscovite Tsardom also took Astrakhan, making, in the words of the TDV, 'a move that would have very important consequences for the future'; with the occupation of the Astrakhan Khanate the Lower Volga came under Russian control. The Crimean Khan Devlet Giray wanted to prevent these occupations but could not succeed. The Ottomans answered this development with the Astrakhan campaign of 1569; but, failing to gain control of the Lower Volga region, they left the struggle against the Muscovite Tsardom to the Crimean khan.
