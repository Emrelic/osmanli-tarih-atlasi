The Defeat at Slobozia — The Encirclement of the Danube Army

14 October 1811

The greater part of the Ottoman army that crossed the Danube before Rusçuk was hemmed in at Slobozia by Kutuzov's encircling manoeuvre from the opposite bank of the river and left to starve. Thousands of soldiers were obliged to surrender; the force in the grand vizier's hands effectively melted away, and the Sublime Porte was obliged to ask for an armistice a month later. This defeat is the direct reason for the acceptance of the ceding of Bessarabia in the 1812 Treaty of Bucharest.
