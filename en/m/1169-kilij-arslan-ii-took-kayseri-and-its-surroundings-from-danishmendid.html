Kilij Arslan II took Kayseri and its surroundings from Danishmendid rule and annexed them to Seljuk territory

1169

With the weakening of Danishmendid rule (the disorders of the time of Zunnun), Kilij Arslan II annexed Kayseri and its surroundings to Seljuk territory in 564 (1169); with the achievement of political unity in Anatolia in 570 (1175), Kayseri became an important centre of the Seljuks and the residence of the sultans.
