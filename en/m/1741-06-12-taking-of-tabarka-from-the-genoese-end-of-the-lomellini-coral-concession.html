Taking of Tabarka from the Genoese: end of the Lomellini coral concession

12 June 1741

After Charles V's agreement of 1544 with the Bey of Tunis, the Genoese Lomellini family had settled on the island of Tabarka and for two hundred years held the coral concession; the island had remained a Christian trading outpost in the middle of Ottoman Tunisia. Ali Pasha of the Husainid dynasty, learning of the family's attempt to dispose of the island, sent forces and seized it on 12 June 1741; about fifteen hundred Christians were transferred to Tunis. After this date Tabarka came under the administration of the Regency of Tunis.
