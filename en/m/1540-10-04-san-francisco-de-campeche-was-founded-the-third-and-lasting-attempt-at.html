San Francisco de Campeche was founded — the third and lasting attempt at the conquest of Yucatán

4 October 1540

After two unsuccessful expeditions to take Yucatán, Francisco de Montejo (the son) founded the town of San Francisco de Campeche on the site of the Maya town of Can Pech. This port became the supply base for the forces that would march into the interior of the peninsula, and formed the point of departure of the campaign that ended two years later with the founding of Mérida.
