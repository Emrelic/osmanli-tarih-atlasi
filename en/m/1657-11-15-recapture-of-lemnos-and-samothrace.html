Recapture of Lemnos and Samothrace

15 November 1657

After the liberation of Tenedos on 31 August 1657, Köprülü Mehmed Pasha directed the fleet northward and recaptured Lemnos and Samothrace, which Venice had held for sixteen months. According to the Lemnos entry of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, the island had passed into Venetian hands in July 1656 and was retaken in November 1657. Thus the Venetian blockade at the mouth of the Dardanelles was completely broken and Istanbul's grain route was secured again; this is the most concrete gain of the first year of the grand vizier's tenure with extraordinary powers.
