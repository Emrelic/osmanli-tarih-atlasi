Abdülhamid I suffered a stroke and died while reading the news of the fall of Ochakov

7 April 1789

According to the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi article abdulhamid-i, Abdülhamid I, whose health had deteriorated markedly with the heavy course of the Russo-Austrian war, died of a sudden stroke while reading the grand vizier's letter reporting that Ochakov Fortress had been occupied by the Russians (11 Rajab 1203 / 7 April 1789). His body was buried in the mausoleum he had built at Bahçekapı in Istanbul. On the same day his nephew Selim III came to the throne.
