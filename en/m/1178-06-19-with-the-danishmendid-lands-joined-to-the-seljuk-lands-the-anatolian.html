With the Danishmendid lands joined to the Seljuk lands, the Anatolian Seljuk State became a great state

19 June 1178

One of Kilij Arslan II's important achievements was the elimination of the Danishmendids; the cities of central Anatolia such as Kayseri, Sivas, Niksar, Tokat, Amasya, and Malatya were annexed to the Seljuk lands in 574 (1178), and political and national unity was established.
