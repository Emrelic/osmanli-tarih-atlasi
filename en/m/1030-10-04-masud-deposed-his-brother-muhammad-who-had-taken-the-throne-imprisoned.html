Mas'ud deposed his brother Muhammad, who had taken the throne, imprisoned him, and became sultan

4 October 1030

Mas'ud defeated his brother Muhammad, who had taken the throne after Mahmud's death, had him blinded with a hot needle and imprisoned, and became sultan (3 Shawwal 421 / 4 October 1030).
