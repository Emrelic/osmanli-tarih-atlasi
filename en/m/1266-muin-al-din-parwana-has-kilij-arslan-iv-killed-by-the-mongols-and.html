Mu'in al-Din Parwana has Kilij Arslan IV killed by the Mongols and places his young son Kaykhusraw III on the throne

1266

When Kilij Arslan was forced to agree to being given Sinop as mulk, the vizier Mu'in al-Din Parwana had him killed by the Mongols (664/1266) and installed in his place his child son Kaykhusraw III; Parwana became the unrivalled master of the state.
