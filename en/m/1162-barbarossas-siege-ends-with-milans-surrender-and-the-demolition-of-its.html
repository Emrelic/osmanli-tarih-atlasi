Barbarossa's siege ends with Milan's surrender and the demolition of its walls

1162

Milan, which had been placed under the imperial ban in 1155 and besieged and forced to surrender in 1158, surrendered in 1162 after a long siege before its enemies (Lodi, Como, Pavia, Cremona, Novara and the imperial army); the walls were demolished, and the people had to live outside the city for four years.
