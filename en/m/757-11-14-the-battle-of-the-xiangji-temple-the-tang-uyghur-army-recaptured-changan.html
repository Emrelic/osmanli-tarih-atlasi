The battle of the Xiangji Temple: the Tang-Uyghur army recaptured Chang'an

14 November 757

The mixed Tang-Uyghur-Arab army of about 200,000 men under the commander-in-chief Prince of Guangping (Li Chu) defeated the rebel generals northwest of the Xiangji Temple (Jiu Tang: sixty thousand heads), and the next day the Western Capital Chang'an was recaptured.
