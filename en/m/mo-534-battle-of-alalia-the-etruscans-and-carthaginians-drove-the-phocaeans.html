Battle of Alalia: the Etruscans and Carthaginians drove the Phocaeans out of Corsica

534 BC

At Alalia the Phocaean Greeks won a victory on paper but lost most of their fleet; they abandoned Corsica and withdrew to Elea in southern Italy. Etruscan (Caere) domination was established on the island.
