The Emirate of Transjordan was founded — Abdullah b. Husayn proclaimed his emirate

1 February 1921

Abdullah, the son of Sharif Husayn, came north by way of Ma'an and proclaimed the emirate of Transjordan; Britain soon recognized this administration within the framework of the mandate. Amman became the centre of the emirate, and the Transjordanian plateau, including Kerak, was attached to this new administration.
