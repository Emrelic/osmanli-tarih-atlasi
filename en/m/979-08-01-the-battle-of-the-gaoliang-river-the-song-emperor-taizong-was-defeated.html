The Battle of the Gaoliang River: the Song Emperor Taizong was defeated by the Khitan before Youzhou

1 August 979

Song Taizong reached Youzhou (the Southern Capital of the Liao, present-day Beijing) in the 6th month and stationed his commanders for a siege on four sides; in the great battle fought with the Khitan at the Gaoliang River in the 7th month the Song army was routed and began to retreat the next day.
