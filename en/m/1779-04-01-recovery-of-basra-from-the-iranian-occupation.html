Recovery of Basra from the Iranian occupation

1 April 1779

Basra, which Sadiq Khan, brother of Karim Khan Zand, had seized in 1776, was evacuated when a struggle for the throne began in Iran after Karim Khan's death on 2 March 1779; Sadiq Khan returned to Iran with a claim to the sultanate. The city remained for a short time in the hands of the Muntafiq tribe and then came under Ottoman administration again. The former mutesellim Süleyman Agha, who had returned from Shiraz, asked for the governorship of Basra and, under the name Süleyman Pasha the Great, became governor of Basra, Baghdad and Shahrizor. Gulf trade returned to Ottoman control; during the years of occupation part of the trade had shifted to Kuwait, and from 1778 the desert post had also been dispatched from Kuwait.
