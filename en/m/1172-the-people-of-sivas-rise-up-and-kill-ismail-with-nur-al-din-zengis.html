The people of Sivas rise up and kill Isma'il; with Nur al-Din Zengi's support Dhu al-Nun takes the Danishmendid throne at Sivas

1172

In Sivas the people rose against Isma'il because of famine and his not distributing the stocks in the granaries; Isma'il and his wife were killed. The notables of the city invited Dhu al-Nun, who had taken refuge with Nur al-Din; with Nur al-Din's help Dhu al-Nun took the Danishmendid throne at Sivas in 567/1172, but soon afterwards, under pressure from Kilij Arslan II, fled to Niksar.
