The Russian army crossed the Dniester without a declaration of war and began the occupation of the Danubian Principalities (Ottoman–Russian War of 1806–1812)

23 November 1806

In October 1806 General Michelson was ordered to occupy Moldavia and Wallachia; war had not yet been officially declared. Russian forces began to cross the Dniester, and the commanders of the fortresses of Khotin, Bender, Akkerman and Kilia surrendered their fortresses without fighting. Only the commandant of Izmail did not yield to Russian persuasion. The Porte's declaration of war came after this occupation.
