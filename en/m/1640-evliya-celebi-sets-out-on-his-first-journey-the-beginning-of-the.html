Evliya Çelebi sets out on his first journey: the beginning of the Seyahatname

1640

Evliya Çelebi made his first journey outside Istanbul in 1640, to Bursa, beginning the forty-year traveling life that would form the basis of his ten-volume Seyahatname. The work ends abruptly in the tenth volume, unfinished, with the author's death around 1684.
