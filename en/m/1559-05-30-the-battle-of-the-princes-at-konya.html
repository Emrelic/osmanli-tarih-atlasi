The Battle of the Princes at Konya

30 May 1559

Prince Bayezid, who had entered the struggle for the throne, came before Konya with a force of about 30,000 men and was defeated by his brother Prince Selim in a clash lasting two days. The routed Bayezid took refuge in Iran with Shah Tahmasb by way of Amasya. The battle turned the succession crisis in the last years of Süleyman the Lawgiver into a foreign-policy problem.
