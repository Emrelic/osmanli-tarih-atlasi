Eastern campaign of Antiochus III (209-204): the Parthian and Bactrian kings were made tributary to the Seleucids

204 BC

Antiochus III marched on Media, Parthia and Bactria to recover the Seleucids' lost eastern lands: Arsaces II and Euthydemus were made tributary. After this campaign Antiochus took the title "the Great".
