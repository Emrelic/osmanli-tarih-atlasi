The Russian naval port of Sevastopol was founded in the Akhtiar bay

1784

After the annexation of Crimea to Russia in 1783, the bay where the Crimean Tatar village of Akhtiar (Akyar) stood was chosen for a military port; the port was founded the next year and took the name Sevastopol. In 1804 the city became the main port of the Russian Black Sea fleet.
