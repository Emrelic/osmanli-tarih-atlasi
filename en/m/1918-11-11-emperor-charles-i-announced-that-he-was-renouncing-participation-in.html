Emperor Charles I announced that he was renouncing participation in affairs of state (for Austria)

11 November 1918

Charles announced on 11 November 1918 that he was renouncing participation in affairs of state in Austria; he did not expressly abdicate the throne. The next day the Provisional National Assembly proclaimed the republican form. The separate proclamation for Hungary (Eckartsau) is dated 13 November.
