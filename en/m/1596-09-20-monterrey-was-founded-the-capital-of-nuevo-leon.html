Monterrey was founded — the capital of Nuevo León

20 September 1596

After two unsuccessful attempts, Diego de Montemayor founded Ciudad Metropolitana de Nuestra Señora de Monterrey at the head of the Santa Lucía spring. This settlement on the northern frontier became the foothold of New Spain's lasting expansion toward the northeast (Nuevo León).
