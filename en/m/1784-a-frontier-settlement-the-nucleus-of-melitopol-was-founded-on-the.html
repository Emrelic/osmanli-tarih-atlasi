A frontier settlement, the nucleus of Melitopol, was founded on the Molochna

1784

After the annexation of Crimea, a frontier settlement was founded on the bank of the Molochna River. The settlement took the name Novooleksandrivka in 1816 and was made a town under the name Melitopol in 1841.
