Franco-Monegasque treaty: Monaco gave up its rights over Menton (and Roquebrune) for 4 million francs; Menton passed to France

2 February 1861

After the cession of Piedmont-Sardinia, together with Nice, to France in 1860, the Prince of Monaco ceded his rights of sovereignty over Menton and Roquebrune to France. Menton had been a free city under Sardinian protection since 1848.
