The Second Iranian Campaign and the retaking of Tabriz

27 July 1548

In the campaign launched at the instigation of Elqas Mirza, the brother of Shah Tahmasb who had rebelled against him and taken refuge in Istanbul, the army entered Tabriz for the second time. Because the Safavids again refused to engage in a pitched battle and withdrew, burning the land, the army could remain in the city for only a few weeks. The campaign made it certain that Tabriz could not be held and directed Ottoman strategy toward establishing a lasting border line, that is, toward fortifying Van.
