Bıyıklı Mehmed Pasha took Mosul, Sinjar, and Imadiya from the Safavids

24 August 1516

Bıyıklı Mehmed Pasha, who defeated Kara Khan at Koçhisar, took the surrounding fortresses from the Safavids while besieging Mardin: Mosul, Ruha, Birecik, and Sinjar were captured, and Imadiya and the other Kurdish rulers accepted Ottoman allegiance. The fortress of Mardin would be taken over after the Pasha's return from Marj Dabiq.
