Ibrahim Pasha appointed grand vizier

27 June 1523

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Ibrâhîm Pasha, Makbul, Ibrahim Pasha, who was promoted from chief of the privy chamber to the beylerbeylik of Rumelia and on the same day to the grand vizierate, became grand vizier on 13 Sha'ban 929 (27 June 1523). This was an unprecedented appointment, moving directly from palace service to the grand vizierate, and showed Ibrahim Pasha's extraordinary closeness to Süleyman the Lawgiver.
