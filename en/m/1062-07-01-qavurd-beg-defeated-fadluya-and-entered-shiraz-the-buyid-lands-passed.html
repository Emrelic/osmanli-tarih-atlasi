Qavurd Beg defeated Fadluya and entered Shiraz; the Buyid lands passed entirely to the Seljuks

1 July 1062

Malik Qavurd defeated Fadluya, emir of the Shabankara, entered Shiraz and had the khutba read in the name of Sultan Toghril Beg (July 1062); the lands under Buyid administration in Iran and Iraq came entirely under Seljuk rule.
