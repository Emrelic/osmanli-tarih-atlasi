Ounjougou (Ravin de la Mouche) - the oldest pottery of Africa: Africa's earliest pottery (Ounjougou)

9249 BC

The first pottery sherds, associated with a lithic industry with small bifacial arrowheads, in unit HA1; the overlying HA2 sediment gives a terminus ante quem: 9758+-70 BP (ETH-28746) and 9510+-70 BP (ETH-31279).
