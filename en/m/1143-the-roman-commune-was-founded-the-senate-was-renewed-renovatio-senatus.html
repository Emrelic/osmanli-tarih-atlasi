The Roman Commune was founded: the senate was renewed (renovatio senatus), the Pope's temporal sovereignty was suspended

1143

The people of Rome re-established the commune and the senate on the Capitol; under the influence of Arnold of Brescia, papal sovereignty was in effect not recognized until 1155 (atlas state entry: 1143; Treccani: 1144).
