Uruk: Gutium (Gutian) Rule → ownerless interval

2111 BC

At the points Uruk, ownership passes from Gutium (Gutian) Rule to the ownerless interval. Transition source information: Uruk breaks away from the Akkad state: in the late years of Sharkalisharri (MC 2205-2181) or a little later | Source: Sallaberger, W. & Schrakamp, I.
