The Hungarian constitutional government was restored — Gyula Andrássy prime minister

17 February 1867

On Ferenc Deák's recommendation Franz Joseph appointed Gyula Andrássy to the Hungarian premiership, putting the pre-1848 Hungarian constitutional order back into operation; the government took the oath on 20 February. This step opened the way to the adoption of the Ausgleich.
