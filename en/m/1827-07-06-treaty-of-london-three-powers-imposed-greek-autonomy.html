Treaty of London: three powers imposed Greek autonomy

6 July 1827

Britain, France and Russia decided on joint action to have the Ottomans and the Greeks accept an armistice and the condition of autonomy; it was announced that their fleets would intervene if this was refused. The Sublime Porte rejected the proposal.
