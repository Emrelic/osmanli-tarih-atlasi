Roger II is proclaimed duke by the army at Reggio

1127

On the death of William II, duke of Apulia, Roger, claiming the inheritance, was acclaimed duke of Apulia by his army at Reggio at the end of 1127 as he was returning to Sicily; Pope Honorius II excommunicated him.
