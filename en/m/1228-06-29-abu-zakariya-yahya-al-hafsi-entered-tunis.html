Abu Zakariya Yahya al-Hafsi entered Tunis

29 June 1228

Abu Zakariya, brother of Abu Muhammad Abdallah, the Almohad governor of Ifriqiya, entered Tunis on 24 Rajab 625 (29 June 1228); he gained control of the whole of Ifriqiya.
