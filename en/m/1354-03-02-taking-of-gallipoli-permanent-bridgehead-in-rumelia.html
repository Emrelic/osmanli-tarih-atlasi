Taking of Gallipoli — permanent bridgehead in Rumelia

2 March 1354

The Ottomans took Gallipoli in 1354, a place whose surroundings they had come to know between 1349 and 1352 as allies of Kantakouzenos; the great earthquake that brought down the city's walls had provided the opportunity. The narrow bridgehead at Çimpe thus turned into a real base on the Rumelian side of the Straits, and the gate opening to Thrace was opened wide. Gallipoli was to be lost in 1366 to the Savoyard crusade, but with the second conquest in 1376 it was to come definitively under Ottoman dominion. Other places joined on the same date: Kilitbahir.
