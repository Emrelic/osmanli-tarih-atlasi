The Meccan khutba was changed from the Fatimids to the Abbasid caliph and Sultan Alp Arslan

20 October 1069

When supplies did not arrive because of the famine in Egypt, the Emir of Mecca Abu Hashim Muhammad b. Ja'far had the khutba read in the name of the Abbasid Caliph al-Qa'im bi-Amr Allah and the Seljuk Sultan Alp Arslan; Mecca passed to the suzerainty of the Great Seljuks.
