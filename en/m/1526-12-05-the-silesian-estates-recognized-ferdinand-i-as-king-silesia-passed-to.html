The Silesian estates recognized Ferdinand I as king: Silesia passed to Habsburg Austria

5 December 1526

Ferdinand of Habsburg, elected in October 1526 to the Bohemian crown left vacant by the death of Louis II at Mohács, was recognized as king, and his wife Anna as queen, on 5 December 1526 by the estates of Upper and Lower Silesia in a general assembly, on condition of the confirmation of their privileges. The Silesian duchies thus came under Habsburg rule together with the Bohemian crown.
