The effective end of the tradition of fratricide

23 December 1603

At his accession, Ahmed I, unlike the previous sultans, did not have his brother Prince Mustafa killed but spared his life; this decision is regarded as the beginning of the effective adoption of the principle of seniority (ekber-erşed) in the Ottoman system of succession. The TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on 'Mustafa I' relates that from this date Mustafa led a life of seclusion in the palace. The practice brought a de facto end to the accession murders that had continued for centuries.
