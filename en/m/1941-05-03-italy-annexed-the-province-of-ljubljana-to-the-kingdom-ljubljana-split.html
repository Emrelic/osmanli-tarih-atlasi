Italy annexed the Province of Ljubljana to the Kingdom — Ljubljana split from Yugoslavia

3 May 1941

Italy, which occupied Ljubljana and the western half of Slovenia with the collapse of Yugoslavia in April 1941, annexed the region to the Kingdom under the name Province of Ljubljana by a decree dated 3 May 1941; the high commissioner was Grazioli. Ljubljana left Yugoslavia and came under Italian administration (until September 1943).
