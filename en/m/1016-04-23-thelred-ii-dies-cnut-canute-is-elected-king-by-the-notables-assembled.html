Æthelred II dies; Cnut (Canute) is elected king by the notables assembled at Southampton

23 April 1016

After the death of Æthelred II on 23 April 1016, the Dane Cnut was elected king by the council of notables at Southampton; London, however, remained loyal to Edmund. Cnut won in the great battle at Assandun, and after Edmund's death in November 1016 he was elected king of all England at the beginning of 1017.
