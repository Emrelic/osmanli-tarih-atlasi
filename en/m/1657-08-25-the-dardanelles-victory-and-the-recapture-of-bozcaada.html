The Dardanelles Victory and the Recapture of Bozcaada

25 August 1657

The fleet mobilised by Köprülü Mehmed Pasha defeated the Venetian fleet before the Strait on 19 July 1657; then the forces under Kurd Pasha retook Bozcaada on 25 August, and on 30 August the Venetians evacuated the island completely. In November Lemnos was also liberated, the Dardanelles blockade was broken and Istanbul's supply route was reopened. Köprülü crossed to the island himself and had the fortifications repaired; this success is the first great military gain of the Köprülü era.
