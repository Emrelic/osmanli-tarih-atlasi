Foundation of Rome (dates of the WRITTEN TRADITION): foundation of Rome (written tradition, Dionysius)

751 BC

The written tradition places the foundation of Rome, by Olympiad reckoning, in the 1st year of the 7th Olympiad (Cato, Dionysius) - between 7.1 and 12.4 across authors. Varro's 753 was NOT read FROM THE SOURCE in this session.
