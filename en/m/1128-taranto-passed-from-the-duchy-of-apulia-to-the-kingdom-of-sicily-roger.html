Taranto passed from the Duchy of Apulia to the Kingdom of Sicily (Roger II)

1128

In 1128 Roger II took the duchy of Apulia and Bohemond II's principality of Taranto/Otranto/Brindisi (May-June 1128, secondary); on the same day as the Bari and Salerno chains.
