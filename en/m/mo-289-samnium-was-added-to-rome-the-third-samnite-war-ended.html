Samnium was added to Rome: the Third Samnite War ended

289 BC

Manius Curius Dentatus decisively defeated the Samnites and the Sabines; Samnium became Rome's ally. Rome became master of central Italy and came face to face with the Greek cities of southern Italy (Tarentum).
