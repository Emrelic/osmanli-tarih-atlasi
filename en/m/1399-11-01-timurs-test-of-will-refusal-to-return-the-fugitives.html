Timur's test of will: refusal to return the fugitives

1 November 1399

Under Timur's pressure, the Jalayirid Sultan Ahmed and the Qaraquyunlu leader Qara Yusuf took refuge in Ottoman territory; Timur demanded the return of these two fugitives, and Bayezid the Thunderbolt refused. Thereupon letters containing mutual grave insults began to be exchanged between the two rulers. The ideological ground of the war was laid by this correspondence, before a single soldier had moved.
