Emir Süleyman took Ankara from Çelebi Mehmed

1406

Emir Süleyman besieged in 1406 Ankara, which had remained in Çelebi Mehmed's hands after Timur's withdrawal from Anatolia. The commander of the fortress, Yakub Bey, held out for a time, but through a stratagem of the grand vizier Çandarlı Ali Pasha he surrendered the fortress to Süleyman. Thereupon Çelebi Mehmed came to an agreement with the Karamanid Mehmed Bey and moved against Süleyman.
