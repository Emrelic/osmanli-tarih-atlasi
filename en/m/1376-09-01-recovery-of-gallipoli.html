Recovery of Gallipoli

1 September 1376

Andronikos IV, who obtained Ottoman support in the Byzantine struggle for the throne, returned Gallipoli in exchange; the bridge between Rumelia and Anatolia was established anew. Other places annexed on the same date: Çimpe.
