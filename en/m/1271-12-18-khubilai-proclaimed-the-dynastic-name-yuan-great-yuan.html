Khubilai proclaimed the dynastic name 'Yuan' (Great Yuan)

18 December 1271

On the 15th day of the 11th month, a decision on official court dress was taken at the court ceremonies, the application of the Jin Taihe laws was prohibited, and the state name 'Great Yuan' was proclaimed.
