The dismissal of Koca Yûsuf Pasha from the grand vizierate

7 June 1789

The rout at Galați was the visible cause of Koca Yûsuf Pasha's removal from the grand vizierate on 7 June 1789. In his place Kethüdâ Hasan Pasha, known for his successes on the Austrian front, was appointed. In the remaining three years of the war the grand vizierate would change hands four times.
