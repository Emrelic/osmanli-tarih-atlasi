The Kherson shipyard was founded at the mouth of the Dnieper

1778

At the mouth of the Dnieper, which passed to Russia after Küçük Kaynarca, a shipyard was founded near the fortification of Aleksandr-shants, which had been built in 1737 and soon destroyed, and was given the name Kherson. The city became a district centre in 1784 and a governorate centre in 1803.
