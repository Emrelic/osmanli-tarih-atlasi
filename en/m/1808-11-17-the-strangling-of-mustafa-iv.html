The strangling of Mustafa IV

17 November 1808

When Alemdar Mustafa Pasha was attacked and died and the rebels wished to march on the palace, the deposed Mustafa IV was strangled on the order of his brother Mahmud II. His body was buried at Bahçekapı in the tomb of his father, Abdülhamid I; a rumour spread among the rebels that the coffin was empty.
