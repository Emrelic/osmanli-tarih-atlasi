Timur's withdrawal from Anatolia

15 March 1403

Timur, receiving the news that Bayezid the Thunderbolt had died at Akşehir, regarded his business in Anatolia as finished and turned eastward. Behind him remained the beyliks that had declared loyalty to him and the Ottoman princes set against one another; some cities, such as Sivas, were left in the hands of emirs subject to Timur, such as Mezid Bey. Timur never returned to Anatolia, but the fragmented order he established lasted ten years.
