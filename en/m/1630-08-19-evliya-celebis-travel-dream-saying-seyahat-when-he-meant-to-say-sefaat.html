Evliya Çelebi's travel dream — saying 'Seyahat' when he meant to say 'Şefaat'

19 August 1630

According to the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, Evliya Çelebi attributes the beginning of his travelling life, which would last forty years, to a dream he had on the night of Ashura in the month of Muharram 1040 (19 August 1630): in his dream he sees the Prophet together with a large congregation in the Ahî Çelebi Mosque in Istanbul, and in his excitement, instead of saying 'şefaat yâ Resûlellah' (intercession, O Messenger of God), says 'seyahat yâ Resûlellah' (travel, O Messenger of God). According to the account, the Prophet smiled at this slip of the tongue and gave him the good tidings of intercession, travel and pilgrimage alike. He never married and devoted his life to this promise of 'travel'.
