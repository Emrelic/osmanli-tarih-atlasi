Annexation of Novgorod by Moscow

15 January 1478

Grand Prince Ivan III removed the symbolic bell of the popular assembly (veche) in Novgorod, replaced the local officials with his own governors and in effect bound the city to Moscow; Novgorod ceased to be an independent republic. The land of Novgorod stretched from the Baltic Sea to the White Sea and the Urals. In the atlas, Novgorod is recorded as passing from the Novgorod Republic to Moscow from this day.
