The Great Offensive began

26 August 1922

After a year of preparation following the Sakarya, the Turkish army went over to the general offensive on the Afyon front. According to the TDV articles on the National Struggle and Kütahya, the Great Offensive began from Afyon on the morning of 26 August 1922, and the resistance of the Greek army was soon broken.
