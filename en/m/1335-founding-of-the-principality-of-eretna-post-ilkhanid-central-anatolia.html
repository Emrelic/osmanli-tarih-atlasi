Founding of the Principality of Eretna: post-Ilkhanid Central Anatolia

1335

According to the TDV's Eretnaoğulları entry, Ala al-Din Eretna, an Ilkhanid official of Uyghur origin, after the death in 1335 of the Ilkhanid ruler Abu Sa'id Bahadur Khan, turned the Ilkhanid lieutenancy in Anatolia into a de facto independent administration; at first appearing as deputy of the Mamluk sultan, he removed the Mamluk name from the khutba in 1340 and declared full independence around 1341. The principality, whose center was first Sivas and then Kayseri, established until his death (1352) a broad rule over Central Anatolia comprising Amasya, Tokat, Çorum, Niğde, Aksaray and Erzincan, and was brought to an end in 1381 by Kadi Burhan al-Din.
