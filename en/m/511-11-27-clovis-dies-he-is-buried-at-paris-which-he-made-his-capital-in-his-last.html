Clovis dies; he is buried at Paris, which he made his capital in his last years, and the unity of the kingdom is left to the Merovingian inheritance

27 November 511

Clovis spent his last years in Paris and made it the capital of the kingdom; by eliminating the minor Frankish kings at centres such as Cambrai and Cologne, he became the sole king of all the Frankish tribes. He died in 511.
