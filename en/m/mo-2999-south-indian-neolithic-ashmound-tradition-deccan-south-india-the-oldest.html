South Indian Neolithic (Ashmound tradition, Deccan): South India: the oldest occupation layer at Kodekal (charcoal)

2999 BC

A pastoral Neolithic in the southern Deccan marked by cattle-dung ash mounds (ashmounds); Kodekal Layer 4 (before the ash mound) 'early 3rd millennium'.
