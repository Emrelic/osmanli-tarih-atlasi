Conquest of Nicopolis

1395

After the battle of Rovine in 1395 against the Wallachians, Bayezid the Thunderbolt took the fortress of Nicopolis on the bank of the Danube; some anonymous Bulgarian chronicles state that the conquest took place on 3 June 1395. The date of the conquest is not uniform in the sources: the entry on the Battle of Nicopolis in the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi writes that Bayezid took Silistra and Nicopolis in a Danube campaign in 1394. The fortress would be besieged in the famous Crusade of 1396, but would not fall. Other places annexed on the same date: Ohrid.
