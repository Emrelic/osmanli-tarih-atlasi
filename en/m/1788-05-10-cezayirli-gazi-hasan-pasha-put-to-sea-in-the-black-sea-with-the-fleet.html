Cezayirli Gazi Hasan Pasha put to sea in the Black Sea with the fleet

10 May 1788

The kaptan-ı derya Cezayirli Gazi Hasan Pasha put to sea in the Black Sea with the fleet on 10 May 1788. The aim was to exert pressure on the Özi and Kılburun line and to break the superiority of the Russian fleet in the northern Black Sea; this operation was the beginning of the naval engagements that continued through the summer.
