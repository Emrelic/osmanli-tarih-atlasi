Attila took the Danube fortresses: Viminacium and Singidunum (Belgrade) passed to the Huns

441

With the Eastern Roman army engaged in the Sicilian and Vandal expedition, Attila crossed the Danube in 441; Margus, Viminacium, and Singidunum were taken. Sirmium also fell in these campaigns, and the Huns reached the interior of the Balkans.
