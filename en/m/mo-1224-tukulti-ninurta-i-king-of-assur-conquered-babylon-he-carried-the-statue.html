Tukulti-Ninurta I, king of Aššur, conquered Babylon; he carried the statue of Marduk to Aššur

1224 BC

Tukulti-Ninurta I (c. 1243-1207) defeated the Kassite king Kashtiliash IV and took Babylon, had its walls demolished and carried the statue of Marduk to Aššur. Babylon remained under Aššur's control for about seven years; Assyria became the most powerful state of the age.
