The principality of Mingrelia (Dadiani) was left within the Ottoman sphere of influence

29 May 1555

By the provisions of the Treaty of Amasya dividing Georgia, the principality of Dadiani (Mingrelia and Svaneti) in western Georgia, together with Imereti and Guria, was left within the Ottoman sphere of influence. On the map, Zugdidi, the centre of Mingrelia, appears from this day as a principality subject to the Ottomans; the actual declaration of submission would come in 1578.
