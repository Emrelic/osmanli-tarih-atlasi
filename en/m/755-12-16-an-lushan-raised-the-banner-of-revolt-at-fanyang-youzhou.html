An Lushan raised the banner of revolt at Fanyang (Youzhou)

16 December 755

The military governor of Fanyang (Youzhou), An Lushan, marched on Chang'an with an army of about 150,000 men supported by the Tong-luo, Xi, Khitan and Shiwei, under the pretext of 'punishing Yang Guozhong'; the An-Shi rebellion, which was to last more than seven years, began.
