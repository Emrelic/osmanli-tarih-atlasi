Ivan Vladislav died before Dyrrhachion

1018

Ivan Vladislav (1015-1018), the Bulgarian tsar who had killed Gavril Radomir and taken the throne, was killed in a futile assault on Dyrrhachion (Dyrrachium). When the news reached Constantinople, Basil II set out on his last campaign, and at the gates of Ohrid the family of the last tsar surrendered.
