The Treaty of Edirne with Russia

24 June 1713

After the promises given at the Prut were not fulfilled, war was declared twice more, and finally, when the Russians accepted the commitments, a lasting peace was signed at Edirne. The treaty confirmed the Prut terms; Russia would not interfere in Polish affairs, would withdraw from Cossack lands, and would not obstruct the return to his country of the Swedish king Charles XII. The closing of the northern front in this way made it possible for the Ottoman State to turn its face to the Morea and Venetian front. Other places that joined on the same date: Khotin.
