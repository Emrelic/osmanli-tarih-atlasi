Portuguese fleet's attack on Jeddah and Selman Reis's defence

18 April 1517

A Portuguese fleet of some thirty galleys and galleons attacked Jeddah; Selman Reis directed the city's defence, prevented the enemy from landing and sank several Portuguese ships. This defence prevented the port of Mecca, and thus the pilgrimage road, from falling into the hands of a Christian state. A few months later Jeddah would be officially incorporated into Ottoman administration with the allegiance of the Hejaz.
