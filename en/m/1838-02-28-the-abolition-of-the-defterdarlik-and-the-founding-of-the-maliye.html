The Abolition of the Defterdarlık and the Founding of the Maliye Nezâreti

28 February 1838

The office of defterdar, the head of the classical Ottoman financial order, was abolished, the Maliye Nezâreti (Ministry of Finance), a ministry in the European fashion, was established, and the scattered treasuries were gathered into one hand. In the same year the other units of the Sublime Porte were also reorganised under the name of nezâret, and a transition was made to the structure of a modern council of ministers. This arrangement was one of the most important steps preparing the administrative infrastructure of the Tanzimat Edict to be proclaimed a year later.
