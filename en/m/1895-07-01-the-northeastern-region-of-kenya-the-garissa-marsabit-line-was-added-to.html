The northeastern region of Kenya (the Garissa-Marsabit line) was added to the British East Africa Protectorate

1 July 1895

The British East Africa Protectorate, established with the transfer of the Imperial British East Africa Company's concession to the Crown in 1895, expanded to include northeastern regions such as Garissa, Kitui, Marsabit and Wajir; the placing of these interior regions under effective administration extended over the second half of the 1890s.
