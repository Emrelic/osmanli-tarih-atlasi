The March Revolution began in Pest — the Hungarian Revolution of 1848-49

15 March 1848

Demonstrations that began in the Pilvax café, following the revolution in Vienna (13 March), led in Pest to the acceptance of the Twelve Points by the governor; the Hungarian national movement against the Habsburg monarchy began.
