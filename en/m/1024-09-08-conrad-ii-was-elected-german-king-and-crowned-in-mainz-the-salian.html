Conrad II was elected German king and crowned in Mainz — the Salian dynasty began

8 September 1024

After the death of Henry II, the German princes assembled on 4 September 1024 at Kamba on the right bank of the Rhine; the election was led by Archbishop Aribo of Mainz. The elder Conrad won against his nephew the younger Conrad and was crowned king by Aribo in Mainz on the feast of the Nativity of Mary (8 September).
