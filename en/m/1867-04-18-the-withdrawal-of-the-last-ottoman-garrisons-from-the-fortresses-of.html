The Withdrawal of the Last Ottoman Garrisons from the Fortresses of Belgrade and Serbia

18 April 1867

Upon the pressure of the great powers and the insistent demand of Prince Mihailo, the Sublime Porte consented to evacuate the last military bases remaining in its hands in Serbia; on 18 April 1867 the Ottoman standard at the Fortress of Belgrade was lowered and the keys were handed to the prince. Thus the de facto Ottoman military presence that had continued since 1521 ended, and the last great obstacle on Serbia's road to independence was removed. The sultan's suzerainty was reduced to a matter of merely a symbolic flag and tribute.
