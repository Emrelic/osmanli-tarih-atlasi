Belgrade and Mačva given to Dragutin by the Hungarian king

1284

In 1284 the Hungarian king gave the administration of Mačva and Belgrade to the Serbian king Stefan Dragutin, who had abdicated in 1282; Belgrade remained in Dragutin's hands for more than thirty years (the Kingdom of Srem under Hungarian suzerainty).
