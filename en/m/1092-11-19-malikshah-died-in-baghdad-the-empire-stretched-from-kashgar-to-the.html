Malikshah died in Baghdad; the empire stretched from Kashgar to the Aegean and Yemen

19 November 1092

About a month after Nizam al-Mulk was killed by the Batinis, Malikshah died in Baghdad (16 Shawwal 485 / 19 November 1092); the state stretched from Kashgar to the Aegean islands and from the Aral Sea and the Caucasus to Yemen and Aden, and the Karakhanids too were subject to the empire. After his death a struggle for the sultanate began among the members of the dynasty: the eldest son Berkyaruq was proclaimed sultan in Rayy by the ghulams attached to Nizam al-Mulk (485/1092; the day is not given in TDV, after Malikshah's death), and a struggle began with Terken Khatun and the Turkish emirs, who wished to place her young son Mahmud on the throne.
