Spain occupies the Alhucemas (al-Hoceima) Islands

1673

The al-Hoceima islands off the Mediterranean coast fell into Spanish hands in 1673. Together with Badis and Ceuta, they are among the positions Spain held on the northern coast of Morocco. Around the same time, Mawlay Ismail had defeated the forces of his nephew Ahmad ibn Muhriz, entered Marrakesh and received the oath of allegiance; the struggle for the throne was continuing.
