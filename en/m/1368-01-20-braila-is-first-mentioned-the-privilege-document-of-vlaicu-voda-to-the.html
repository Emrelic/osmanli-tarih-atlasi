Brăila is first mentioned — the privilege document of Vlaicu Vodă to the merchants of Brașov

20 January 1368

Vladislav, voivode of Wallachia, gave the merchants of Brașov a document granting them facilities in carrying goods by way of Brăila. This document is the first known written testimony to Brăila; the place appears in it not yet as a city but as the Danube end of the road. It is not the date of foundation.
