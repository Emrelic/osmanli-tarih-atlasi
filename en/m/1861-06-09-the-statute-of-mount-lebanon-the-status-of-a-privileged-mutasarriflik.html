The Statute of Mount Lebanon: The Status of a Privileged Mutasarrıflık for Lebanon

9 June 1861

The statute, prepared under the pressure of the great powers after the massacres of 1860, made Mount Lebanon a privileged mutasarrıflık directly attached to the Sublime Porte and governed by an administrative council set up according to the balance between the sects. It was stipulated that the mutasarrıf must be an Ottoman subject and a Catholic Christian and that his appointment be submitted to the approval of the great powers; the first mutasarrıf was Dâvud Pasha. This order would last until 1915 and would form the nucleus of the modern Lebanese state.
