Henry (II) gains Poitou, Guyenne and Gascony by marrying Eleanor

1152

Henry Plantagenet became Duke of Normandy in 1150 and Count of Anjou in 1151 on his father's death; in 1152 he married Eleanor (Aliénor), who had been divorced from Louis VII, King of France, and thereby obtained Poitou, Guyenne and Gascony — this is the southern half of the Angevin Empire. The marriage drew the hostility of Henry's sovereign, Louis VII.
