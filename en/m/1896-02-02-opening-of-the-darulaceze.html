Opening of the Darülaceze

2 February 1896

The Darülaceze, prepared following the orders Abdulhamid II had given from 1890, was opened on 2 February 1896 by Grand Vizier Halil Rifat Pasha. The institution, which sheltered destitute children, the elderly and the needy, with its 200-bed hospital, its workshops and its separate places of worship such as a mosque, a church and a synagogue, was the largest social assistance complex of its time.
