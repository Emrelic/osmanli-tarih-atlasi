Hulagu set out from Tabriz for the Syrian campaign

12 September 1259

Hulagu, after sending the vanguard units of Ket-Buqa Noyan ahead, set out from Tabriz on 12 September 1259; Edessa, Harran, Hama, Homs and Damascus submitted to the Mongols in a short time, and Aleppo was besieged and taken.
