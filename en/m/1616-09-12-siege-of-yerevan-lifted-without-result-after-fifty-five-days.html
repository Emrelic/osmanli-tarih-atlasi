Siege of Yerevan — lifted without result after fifty-five days

12 September 1616

The grand vizier, who set out from Aleppo in April 1616 with a large army, reached Yerevan by way of Erzurum and Kars. Because it was thought the fortress would surrender easily, heavy cannon had not been brought; the siege lasted fifty-five days without materiel. After the Janissary agha Muslu Agha and Tekeli Mehmed Pasha fell as martyrs, with the reluctance of the soldiers and the advance of the season, the siege was lifted and the army withdrew to Erzurum. Mehmed Pasha was dismissed in November 1616, and Halil Pasha took his place.
