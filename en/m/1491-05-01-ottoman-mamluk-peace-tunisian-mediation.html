Ottoman-Mamluk peace — Tunisian mediation

1 May 1491

In the Ottoman-Mamluk war that began in 1485 the parties could not win a decisive victory; through the mediation of the Hafsid ruler of Tunis, Uthman, an agreement was reached in 1491. The treaty returned the frontiers largely to their prewar state, and the reckoning between the two Islamic states was postponed for a generation. The wearing struggle of six years was among the principal reasons for Bayezid II's cautious foreign policy and for his emphasis on internal arrangements rather than turning eastward.
