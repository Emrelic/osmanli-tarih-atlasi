Gutium: according to the Sumerian King List the Gutians held kingship in Sumer after Akkad

2152 BC

The Gutians, descending from the Zagros, became dominant over most of Mesopotamia after the fall of the Akkadian state. The Sumerian King List counts Gutium as a dynasty between Akkad and Uruk V and Ur III (21 kings, about 91-124 years); although the source may be distorted, Gutian rule is documented as a weak succession rather than a strong foreign threat.
