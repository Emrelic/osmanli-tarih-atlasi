Graaff-Reinet – Lange Kloof – Plettenberg Bay – Mossel Bay – Swellendam – Cape Town (Barrow's coastal return of 1797-98)

9 December 1797

Route: Graaff-Reinet → Lange Kloof (Joubertina) → Plettenberg Bay → Mossel Bay → Swellendam → Hottentots Holland Kloof → Cape Town. The whole journey is shown on the map as a travel line.
