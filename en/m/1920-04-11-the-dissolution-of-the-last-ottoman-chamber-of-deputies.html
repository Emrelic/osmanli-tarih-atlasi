The dissolution of the last Ottoman Chamber of Deputies

11 April 1920

After the official occupation of Istanbul by the Allied forces and the arrest of many deputies, the Chamber of Deputies became unable to work and was officially dissolved by decree of Mehmed VI. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Meclis-i Mebusan, this dissolution symbolised the effective end of the Ottoman constitutional parliamentary tradition. The closure strengthened the need for a new national assembly to convene in Ankara.
