Shaddadid Fadl IV dies in the war undertaken by Kurti, emir of Erzen, to retake Dvin

1130

Fadl IV, who had retaken Ani after besieging it and added Ganja and Dvin to his lands, died in 524/1130 in the war undertaken by Kurti, emir of Erzen, to retake Dvin; his brother succeeded him, but his son Mahmud came to Ani and seized the government.
