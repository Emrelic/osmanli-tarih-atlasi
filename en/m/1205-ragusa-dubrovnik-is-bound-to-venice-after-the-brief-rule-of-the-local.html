Ragusa (Dubrovnik) is bound to Venice after the brief rule of the local count Damiano Giuda

1205

Ragusa, which had oscillated between the Kingdom of Sicily (1187) and Byzantium (1192), submitted to Venice in 1205 after the brief rule of the local count Damiano Giuda; Venetian rule lasted about 150 years.
