Abdication of Bayezid II and accession of Selim the Grim

24 April 1512

Under pressure from Prince Selim, who had the support of the Janissaries in the struggle for the throne with his brothers, Bayezid II abdicated on 24 April 1512, and Selim I acceded to the throne the same day as the ninth Ottoman ruler. The aged sultan, on his way to Dimetoka, where he was born, died on the road in the village of Abalar near Çorlu on 25 Rabi al-Awwal 918 (10 June 1512). A cautious and inward-looking reign of thirty-one years was replaced by the era of the Grim, which would double the frontiers of the empire in eight years.
