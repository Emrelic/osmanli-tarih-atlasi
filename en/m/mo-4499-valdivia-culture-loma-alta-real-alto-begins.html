Valdivia culture (Loma Alta / Real Alto): begins

4499 BC

The Early Formative Valdivia village culture with pottery on the coast of southwestern Ecuador; Loma Alta is one of the oldest villages in the Valdivia Valley (the first two phases), with traces of maize, manioc, Jerusalem artichoke and chili pepper.
