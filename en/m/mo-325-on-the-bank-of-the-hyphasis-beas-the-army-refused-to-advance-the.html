On the bank of the Hyphasis (Beas) the army refused to advance: the eastern limit of Alexander

325 BC

Because of the monsoon rains and exhaustion, the Macedonian army refused to cross beyond the Hyphasis; Alexander had twelve altars erected and was forced to turn back. The Hyphasis became the eastern extremity of the Macedonian conquests in India.
