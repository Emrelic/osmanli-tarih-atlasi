Zhaozong was killed at Luoyang

22 September 904

Zhaozong, who had moved to Luoyang, was killed at night in the Jiaodian Hall on the orders of Zhu Quanzhong by the generals Zhu Youqong and Shi Shucong and the Communications Commissioner Jiang Xuanhui.
