The capture of Khotin by Austro-Russian forces

1 September 1788

In the war that began in 1787, the combined forces of Austria and Russia besieged Khotin, and the fortress was taken by these forces in September 1788. According to the Treaty of Sistova, Austria held the fortress in trust until peace with Russia was concluded; Khotin was returned to the Ottomans by the Treaty of Jassy of 1792.
