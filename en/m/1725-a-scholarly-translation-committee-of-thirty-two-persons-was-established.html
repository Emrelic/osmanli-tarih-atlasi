A scholarly/translation committee of thirty-two persons was established

1725

During the grand vizierate of Damad Ibrahim Pasha, a committee of thirty-two scholars and scribes, 'which can be regarded as the academy of the age' in the TDV's phrase, was established in Istanbul. The committee brought works of history, philosophy and geography from Arabic, Persian, Greek and Latin into Turkish — among these were Yanyalı Esad Efendi's translation of Aristotle's Physics with a team of twenty-five, and the poet Nedim's presiding over a committee translating a history. The day is not specified in the TDV; the year is declared.
