Qaraquyunlu Jahan Shah's seizure of Timurid Iran (Rey, Isfahan, Fars, Kerman)

1452

According to the TDV's Qaraquyunlu entry, the Qaraquyunlu ruler Jahan Shah, taking advantage of the struggles for the throne among the Timurid princes, brought the provinces of Rey, Isfahan, Fars and Kerman under his administration in the years 856-857 (1452-1453); this is the culminating point of the expansion process that began after Shahrukh Mirza's death in 1447 and opened with the annexation of Sultaniyya-Qazvin. The Timurid lands in south-central Iran thus passed to the Qaraquyunlu; Jahan Shah's rule would reach in 1466 a breadth encompassing Azerbaijan, Arran, Persian Iraq, Arab Iraq, Fars, Kerman and Eastern Anatolia.
