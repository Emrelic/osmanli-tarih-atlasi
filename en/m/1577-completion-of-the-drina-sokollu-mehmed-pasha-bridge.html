Completion of the Drina (Sokollu Mehmed Pasha) Bridge

1577

The bridge, built by Mimar Sinan under the patronage of Grand Vizier Sokollu Mehmed Pasha, was completed in 1577-78 according to the date in its inscription; some researchers suggest that construction began in 1571-72. The bridge is a symbol of Sokollu's attachment to Višegrad, where he was born.
