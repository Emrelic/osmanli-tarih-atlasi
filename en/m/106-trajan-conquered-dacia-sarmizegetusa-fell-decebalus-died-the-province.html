Trajan conquered Dacia: Sarmizegetusa fell, Decebalus died, the province of Dacia was established

106

In the Second Dacian War (105–106) Trajan took Sarmizegetusa; Decebalus committed suicide to avoid capture. Dacia became a Roman province and its gold mines were tied to the Roman economy. The earlier phases of the war (101–105) are in separate entries in the chronology.
