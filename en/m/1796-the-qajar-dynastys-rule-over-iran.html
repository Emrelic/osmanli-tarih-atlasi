The Qajar dynasty's rule over Iran

1796

Agha Muhammad Khan, chief of the Qajar tribe, overthrew Zand rule and broke the last Afshar resistance in Khorasan, reunifying Iran under a single hand and making Tehran the capital. The forty-nine-year period of fragmentation thus came to an end; from then until 1923 the Ottoman Empire's eastern neighbor would be Qajar Iran. According to the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, in 1796 the Afshar ruler Shahrokh came out to meet Agha Muhammad Shah before Mashhad, and the city came under Qajar rule (day not in the source).
