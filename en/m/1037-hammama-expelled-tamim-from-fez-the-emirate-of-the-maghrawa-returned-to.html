Hammama expelled Tamim from Fez: the Emirate of the Maghrawa returned to Fez

1037

After the Banu Ifran took Fez from the Maghrawa emir Hammama in 1033, Hammama, who had withdrawn to Oujda and Tenes, expelled the Banu Ifran emir Tamim from Fez four years later; Fez remained in the Emirate of the Maghrawa until the Almoravid conquest in 1070.
