Seydi Ali reaches the port of Surat in Gujarat

28 September 1554

Seydi Ali, having lost his fleet, comes to Surat in the Sultanate of Gujarat, then goes to Ahmedabad; the fleet remains on the Indian coast and he decides to return to Istanbul by land.
