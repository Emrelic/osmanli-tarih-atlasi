The Cibali Fire

1 September 1633

The fire that broke out in Cibali and spread rapidly under the effect of the wind reduced a large part of Istanbul to ashes; according to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on 'Istanbul', the sources of the period relate that thousands of houses burned. The association of the fire with the wooden house fabric and with tobacco smoking became the direct justification for the harsh ban on tobacco and coffeehouses that Murad IV would proclaim shortly afterwards. The disaster was only one of the repeated great fires in the Istanbul of the period.
