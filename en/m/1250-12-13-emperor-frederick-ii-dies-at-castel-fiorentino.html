Emperor Frederick II dies at Castel Fiorentino

13 December 1250

Frederick II died of an intestinal fever on 13 December 1250 at Castel Fiorentino in Apulia (near Foggia); the throne of Sicily was left to his son Conrad IV, and the regency to Manfred.
