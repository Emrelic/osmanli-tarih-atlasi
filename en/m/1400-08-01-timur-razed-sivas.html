Timur razed Sivas

1 August 1400

Timur, who marched on Sivas guided by the Aqquyunlu Qarayuluk Osman Bey and the emir of Erzincan, Mutahharten, besieged the city for eighteen days and, after taking it over with the promise that no one's blood would be shed, carried out a great massacre; he had the defenders thrown into trenches and had them covered over alive. The city's important works, beginning with the walls, were destroyed, and Sivas collapsed demographically. This was the first great blow that Timur dealt to Ottoman lands, two years before the Battle of Ankara.
