Dholavira: Dholavira Stage VII ends

1499 BC

A Harappan city on Khadir island in Kachchh; according to the UNESCO/ASI scheme, an occupation of about 1,500 years in 7 stages beginning c. 3000 BCE; Stage VII (~1500-1450 BCE) was a final settlement without urban character, and the site was never inhabited afterwards.
