The capture of Ada Kale from Austria

17 August 1738

The Ottoman army, which set out on campaign in the spring of 1738, took Mehadia and besieged Orsova and Ada Kale, crossed the Danube and raided Temesvár. Ada Kale was taken on 17 August 1738. Yeğen Mehmed Pasha, who then came to Niš, launched raids on Belgrade; a year later came the victory of Hisarcık and the Treaty of Belgrade.
