Recapture of Tokar by Anglo-Egyptian forces — Osman Digna's withdrawal

19 February 1891

Tokar on the Red Sea coast, held since 1884 by Osman Digna, the local commander of the Mahdist movement, was recaptured in February 1891 by an operation of Anglo-Egyptian forces; Osman Digna was compelled to withdraw into the mountains. This was a heavy blow to the Mahdist State's presence on the Red Sea coast, and the region came under joint Anglo-Egyptian administration.
