The Deposition of Murad V and the Accession of Abdülhamid II

31 August 1876

Murad V, who ascended the throne after Abdülaziz's deposition, became unable to perform his duties because of his mental health, shaken by his uncle's death and by the Circassian Hasan Incident; of his reign of ninety-three days, his judgement was sound for only seven days. He was deposed by a fatwa of the şeyhülislam, and his brother Abdülhamid II was brought in his place. The new sultan came to the throne having given Midhat Pasha his word that he would proclaim the constitutional regime; an era that would last thirty-three years was beginning.
