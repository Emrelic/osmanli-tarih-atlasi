The Mongols seized and destroyed Kiev

1240

After the Kipchaks were dispersed, the Mongols descended into the steppes of Ukraine in December 1240, and Kiev, the greatest principality of the region, was seized and destroyed; Batu's armies then plundered Poland, Hungary, Austria and Dalmatia.
