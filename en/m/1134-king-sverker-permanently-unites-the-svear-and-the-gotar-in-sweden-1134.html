King Sverker permanently unites the Svear and the Götar in Sweden (1134-1155)

1134

During the reign of King Sverker (1134-1155) the Swedes and the Götar came together, in an arrangement in which, for a hundred years, joint kings were drawn from each in turn; central monarchical organisation was strengthened.
