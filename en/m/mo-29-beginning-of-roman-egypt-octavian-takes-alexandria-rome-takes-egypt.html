Beginning of Roman Egypt - Octavian takes Alexandria: Rome takes Egypt (Octavian)

29 BC

August 30 BCE: Octavian takes Alexandria; the official 'kratesis' is calculated from 1 Thoth = 29 August 30 BCE (closed with the 18-day 'reign' of Antony's children).
