Sultan Malik-Shah comes as far as Kartli on a Georgian campaign; he entrusts responsibility for the region to Sarhang Savtegin

1076

Malik-Shah, together with the Georgian commander Ioane, son of Liparit, who had remained loyal to him, campaigned against Georgia and came as far as Kartli, returned with many captives and much booty, and entrusted responsibility for the region to Sarhang Savtegin (1076). King Giorgi II won his wars with Savtegin and recovered some places.
