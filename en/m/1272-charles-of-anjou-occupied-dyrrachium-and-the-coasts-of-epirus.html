Charles of Anjou occupied Dyrrachium and the coasts of Epirus

1272

Charles of Anjou occupied Corfu in 1267 and Dyrrachium and the coasts of Epirus in 1272; he then came to an agreement with the Serbs and Bulgarians. This threat forced Michael VIII into a church union with the papacy.
