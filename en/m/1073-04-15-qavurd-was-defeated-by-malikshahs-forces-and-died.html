Qavurd was defeated by Malikshah's forces and died

15 April 1073

Qavurd, who heard of Alp Arslan's death (November 1072) in Oman, marched from Kirman to Hamadan; Malikshah and Nizam al-Mulk had already drawn the Turkmen tribes to their own side; the defeated Qavurd was captured in the mountains of Hamadan and killed (Sha'ban 465 / April 1073).
