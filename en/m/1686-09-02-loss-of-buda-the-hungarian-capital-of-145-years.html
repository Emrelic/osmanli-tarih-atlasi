Loss of Buda — the Hungarian capital of 145 years

2 September 1686

At the end of a siege lasting seventy-eight days Buda fell; the last beylerbeyi, Abdurrahman Abdi Pasha, died a martyr on the walls. The loss of the city, under Ottoman rule since 1541, is regarded as the symbol of the loss of Hungary. Other settlements lost on the same date: Pest.
