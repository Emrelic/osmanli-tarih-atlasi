The Signing of the Ottoman-German Secret Alliance Treaty in Istanbul

2 August 1914

As soon as war broke out in Europe, a secret alliance treaty was signed between the Ottoman State and Germany in the mansion of the Grand Vizier Said Halim Pasha; the text envisaged that the Ottomans too would take Germany's side if Russia entered the war against Germany. The treaty was concealed even from the cabinet and was known to only four persons. Three months later the Ottoman State would be dragged effectively into the war by the Black Sea raid, and the fate of the empire would be bound by this document.
