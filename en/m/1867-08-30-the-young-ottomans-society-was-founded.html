The Young Ottomans Society was founded

30 August 1867

Ottoman intellectuals who fled to Europe under the leadership of Mustafa Fazıl Pasha organised, with a thirteen-article regulation, under the name Young Turkey. According to the TDV article on the 'Yeni Osmanlılar Cemiyeti', the group spread its demand for constitutional government by issuing newspapers such as Muhbir and Hürriyet in Paris, London and Geneva.
