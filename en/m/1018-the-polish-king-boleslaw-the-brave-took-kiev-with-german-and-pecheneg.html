The Polish King Bolesław the Brave took Kiev with German and Pecheneg support

1018

In August 1018 Bolesław seized Kiev with German and Pecheneg forces; Bolesław, who defeated Yaroslav on the Bug River, put his son-in-law Sviatopolk on the throne. Yaroslav was able to retake Kiev only in 1019.
