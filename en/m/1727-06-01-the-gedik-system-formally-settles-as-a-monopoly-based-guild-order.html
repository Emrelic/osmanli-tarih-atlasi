The gedik system formally settles as a monopoly-based guild order

1 June 1727

According to the TDV, the systematic transformation in which the gedik right acquired the meaning of "monopoly, privilege and a kind of patent" can be followed from 1727; this order, granting the right to work to a fixed number of persons in a trade, settled under the name "gedik right" in shops, most of them waqf property.
