The statesmen proclaim Izz al-Din Kaykawus, malik of Uluborlu, sultan in the village of Altuntaş in Akşehir

1246

The statesmen proclaimed Izz al-Din Kaykawus, who was malik of Uluborlu (Borgulu) at the time of Ghiyath al-Din Kaykhusraw II's death, sultan in the village of Altuntaş in Akşehir (643/1246).
