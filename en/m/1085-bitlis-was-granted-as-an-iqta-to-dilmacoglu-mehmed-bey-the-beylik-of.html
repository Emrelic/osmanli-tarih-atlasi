Bitlis was granted as an iqta to Dilmaçoğlu Mehmed Bey: the Beylik of the Dilmaçoğulları was founded

1085

When Malik-Shah took the Bitlis region from the hands of the Marwanids, he granted it as an iqta to Dilmaçoğlu Mehmed Bey; the beylik was founded as a subject of the Great Seljuks (1084 according to some sources).
