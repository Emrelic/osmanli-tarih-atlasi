Shahverdi, ruler of Luristan, declared allegiance to Shah Abbas

1592

Shahverdi, ruler of Luristan, who in 1589 had submitted to the Ottomans and become a subordinate of the beylerbeyi of Baghdad, was forced, according to the account of the Safavid historian Iskandar Beg Munshi, to declare allegiance to the Safavid crown in the year 1000 (1591–92). The same source writes that in these years he went back and forth between the two sides, troubled the governors of Hamadan and raided Burujird, and that in the year 1002 (1593–94) Shah Abbas occupied Khorramabad and appointed a governor to the city. The TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, on the other hand, places the full attachment of Luristan, which had been bound to Ottoman administration by the Treaty of Istanbul of 1590, to the Safavids in the year 1603. The two traditions give different dates; this item reflects the declaration of allegiance in the Safavid tradition, while the 1603 item shows the definitive loss. On the map there is no change of owner with this item: Luristan appears on the Ottoman side until 1603.
