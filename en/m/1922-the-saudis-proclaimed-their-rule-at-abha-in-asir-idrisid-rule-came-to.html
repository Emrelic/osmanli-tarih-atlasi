The Saudis proclaimed their rule at Abha in Asir: Idrisid rule came to an end

1922

The Saudi emir Abdulaziz sent his son Faisal to the Asir region and proclaimed his rule over Abha; the rule of Muhammad ibn Ali al-Idrīsī in Asir thus came to an end.
