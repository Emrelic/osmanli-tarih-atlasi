Lamian War: the Greek cities rebelled against Macedonian hegemony after Alexander's death

322 BC

With the news of Alexander's death (June 323), Athens and the Aetolian League rose against Antipater; Antipater was besieged at Lamia. The war ended with a Macedonian victory at Crannon (September 322), and Macedonian supremacy within the framework of the League of Corinth was re-established.
