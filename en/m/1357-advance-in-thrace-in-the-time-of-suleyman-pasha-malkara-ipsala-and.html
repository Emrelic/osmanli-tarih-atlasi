Advance in Thrace in the time of Süleyman Pasha: Malkara, İpsala and Tekirdağ

1357

After a bridgehead was established at Gallipoli, Süleyman Pasha moved north out of the peninsula and took the plain between the Marmara coast and the mouth of the Maritsa step by step. Malkara, İpsala and Rodosçuk (Tekirdağ) thus formed an uninterrupted corridor linking Gallipoli and the Maritsa valley. It was not possible to march on Edirne without this corridor; the advance, halted by the prince's sudden death in 1357, would be continued by Lala Şahin and Hacı İlbey.
