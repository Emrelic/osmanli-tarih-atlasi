The Chortomlyk Sich was founded

1652

The Zaporozhian Cossacks founded the Chortomlyk Sich, also called the 'Old Sich,' on the cape where the Dnieper joins the Chortomlyk river. The Sich was destroyed by the Russian army on 25 May 1709 because they had supported Mazepa.
