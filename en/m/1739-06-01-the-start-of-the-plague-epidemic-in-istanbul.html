The start of the plague epidemic in Istanbul

1 June 1739

According to the TDV article on 'Tâun', the plague epidemic that was effective in the Islamic geography between 1739 and 1743 struck Istanbul as well in this period. The article notes that the epidemics of the 18th century ran a comparatively milder course than those of the previous centuries. The years 1713, 1719, 1728-1729 and 1759-1765 are also counted among the epidemic periods.
