An unprecedented winter — the freezing of the Bosphorus

1621

While preparations for the Polish campaign continued, Istanbul experienced an unprecedented winter: the strait froze, and food and drink could not be found in the city. Grand Vizier Güzelce Ali Pasha, who feared for his life because of the distress in the market, died shortly after the weather improved and the situation returned to normal, on 15 Rabî' al-âkhir (9 March), and Hüseyin Pasha of Ohrid was brought in his place. The imperial tent was pitched on 7 Jumâdâ al-âkhira (29 April) on the plain of Dâvud Pasha; the campaign that would take Osman II to Khotin began after this winter.
