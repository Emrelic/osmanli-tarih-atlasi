When the defeated Qara Khitai army returned to the capital Balasagun, the people closed the gates; the city was besieged and plundered for three days

1210

The Khwarazmshah Muhammad b. Tekish defeated the army of the Qara Khitai commander Tayangu on the plain of Ilamish and took him prisoner. When the helpless army returned to the capital Balasagun, the inhabitants closed the citadel gates; the city, placed under siege, was plundered for three days and its people were massacred. The Qara Khitai were to be destroyed a year later at the hands of the Naiman Kuchlug.
