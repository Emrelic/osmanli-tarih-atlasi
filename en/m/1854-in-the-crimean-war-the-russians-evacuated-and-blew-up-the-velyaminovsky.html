In the Crimean War the Russians evacuated and blew up the Velyaminovsky fortress at Tuapse

1854

With the entry of the Anglo-French fleet into the Black Sea in the Crimean War, the Russian fortresses on the Circassian coast, which had become indefensible, were evacuated; the Velyaminovsky fortress, founded at Tuapse in 1838, was also abandoned and blown up. The coast remained for about ten years again under the control of the Circassian tribes.
