In the time of Wawa (927-28) the administrative center of the Central Javanese kingdom of Medang moved eastward; Sindok took the throne in 929 (East Javanese period)

929

Coedès writes, from the locations where the inscriptions are found, that the administrative center of the kingdom moved eastward during the reign of Wawa (927-928); that Rakai Halu Sindok, named in the inscription of 919, took the throne ten years later, in 929. Thus the center of Medang shifted to the Brantas basin. (The Brantas basin is East Java.)
