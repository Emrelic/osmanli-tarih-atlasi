Arslan-Shah I (son of Kirmanshah) ascended the throne of Kirman; Iranshah had been killed by fatwa and popular uprising

16 November 1101

Iranshah, who had strayed into Batini ideas, was killed by a fatwa of the Sunni ulama and a popular uprising (494/1101); on 22 Muharram 495 (16 November 1101) Arslan-Shah I, son of Kirmanshah, ascended the throne and ruled for forty-two years, seized Fars, and Yazd came under Kirman's rule.
