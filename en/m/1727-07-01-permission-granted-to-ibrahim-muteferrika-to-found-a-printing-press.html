Permission granted to Ibrahim Müteferrika to found a printing press

1 July 1727

According to the TDV article on 'İbrâhim Müteferrika', with the firman of Ahmed III and the fatwa of Şeyhülislam Yenişehirli Abdullah Efendi, permission was given for the founding of the first Turkish printing press. The press began operation in Istanbul's Yavuzselim quarter. Until his death in 1747 Müteferrika printed seventeen works in twenty-three volumes.
