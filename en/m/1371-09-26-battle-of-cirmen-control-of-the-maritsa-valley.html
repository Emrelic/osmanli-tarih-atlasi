Battle of Çirmen — control of the Maritsa valley

26 September 1371

The army of the Serbian lords was scattered in a night attack; the local Serbian and Bulgarian lords in Macedonia and the Rhodopes began, one by one, to pay tribute to the Ottomans — the beginning not of a direct conquest but of a wave of vassalization. In the Ottoman tradition this battle is also recounted under the name 'Sırpsındığı' and in the year 1364, as the night attack of Hacı İlbey on the Crusader army that had come to retake Edirne; the TDV states that this date is wrong and that the two accounts belong to the same battle.
