Treaty of Lausanne — Patmos left to Italy, Greek sovereignty in the Aegean islands confirmed

24 July 1923

By article 15 of Lausanne, Turkey renounced in favor of Italy all rights and titles over the Twelve Islands (Dodecanese), including Patmos; the islands had been under de facto Italian occupation since 1912. Article 12, in turn, confirmed Greek sovereignty over Lemnos, Samothrace, Lesbos, Chios, Samos and Ikaria. Agios Efstratios and Fournoi had been in Greek hands since 1912 and remained in Greece after Lausanne; these two islands themselves are not listed in article 12 of Lausanne.
