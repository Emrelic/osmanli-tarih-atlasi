Philaretos gathers Armenian priests at the city of Hunu (Elbistan region), has a catholicos elected and makes the city a catholicosal seat

1073

Taking advantage of the capture of Romanos Diogenes at the Battle of Manzikert in 1071, Philaretos gathered Armenian priests at the city of Hunu and had a catholicos elected. Thus the religious centre of an Armenian administration detached from Byzantium in the Marash-Elbistan-Malatya region was established.
