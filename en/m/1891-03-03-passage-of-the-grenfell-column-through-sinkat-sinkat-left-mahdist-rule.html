Passage of the Grenfell column through Sinkat — Sinkat left Mahdist rule and returned to Anglo-Egyptian administration

3 March 1891

After the recapture of Tokar in February 1891, the Anglo-Egyptian column under Grenfell set out from Afafit via Sinkat on 3 March 1891 and reached Suakin on 7 March; the column found the region, which had not been entered since 1884, completely evacuated by the enemy. On 8 March a general amnesty was proclaimed in the name of the Khedive to the sheikhs assembled at Suakin, and Sinkat was once again attached to Anglo-Egyptian administration.
