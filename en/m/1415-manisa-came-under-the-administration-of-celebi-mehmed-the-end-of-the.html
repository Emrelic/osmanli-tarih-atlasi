Manisa came under the administration of Çelebi Mehmed — the end of the Manisa branch of the Saruhanids

1415

At the end of the Interregnum, the land of Saruhan came under the administration of Çelebi Mehmed and the Manisa branch of the Saruhanids passed into history. TDV places this transfer after 1411 and before 1415; Ottoman administration could be fully established shortly before 818 (1415). The date has therefore been written with the latest end of the range.
