Annexation of Dobruja

1 September 1393

The lands of Dobruja, extending to the Danube delta, were annexed. Other places annexed on the same date: Silistra, Constanța, Babadag, Balchik, Hârșova, Tulcea, Mangalia, Isaccea, Tutrakan, Măcin, Küçük Kaynarca.
