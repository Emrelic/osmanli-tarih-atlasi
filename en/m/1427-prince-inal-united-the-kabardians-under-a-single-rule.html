Prince Inal united the Kabardians under a single rule

1427

Among the Kabardian branch of the Circassians, who had withdrawn eastward after the Mongol-Tatar invasions, Prince Inal (1427-1456) united the greater part of the people under his own rule; the princes of Kabarda, Besleney and Chemguy base their lineage on him. The Kabardian princes until the end of the eighteenth century also had influence over the Chechens, Ingush, Karachay-Balkars and Ossetes and over the Abazas north of the Caucasus mountains. The date is the beginning of Inal's years of rule; the day of the unification is not known.
