Şehirköy returned to Ottoman rule — the death of Đurađ Branković

1456

Şehirköy, which had been captured by the Crusader army in 1443 and left to the Serbs by Murad II after the Treaty of Edirne–Szeged of 1444, returned to Ottoman rule in 1456 with the death of the Serbian Despot Đurađ Branković. In the same year and for the same reason Niš also definitively came under Ottoman administration; the line of the Nišava valley opening onto the Sofia road was thus completed. The treaty had been signed for a term of ten years, and after Varna (1444) and the Second Battle of Kosovo (1448) the life of the Despotate lasted only as long as Branković's life.
