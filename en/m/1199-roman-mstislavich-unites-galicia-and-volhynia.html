Roman Mstislavich unites Galicia and Volhynia

1199

Roman Mstislavich, prince of Volhynia, who took Halych on the death in 1199 of Volodymyr Yaroslavich, the last Rostislavich prince of Halych, united Galicia and Volhynia and founded the Principality of Galicia-Volhynia; the Romanovich dynasty ruled until 1340.
