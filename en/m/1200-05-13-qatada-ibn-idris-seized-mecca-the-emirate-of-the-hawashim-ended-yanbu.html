Qatāda ibn Idrīs seized Mecca: the emirate of the Hawāshim ended, Yanbu' was attached to the Sharifate of Mecca

13 May 1200

Qatāda ibn Idrīs, born in the Yanbu' valley and in control of the Yanbu' region, seized Mecca together with the tribes that supported him; the rule over Mecca held since 455 (1063) by the Hawāshim branch of the Banū Hāshim came to an end. Qatāda later had a fort built at Yanbu', and his authority extended to the Yanbu' coast.
