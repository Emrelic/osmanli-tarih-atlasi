Jalal al-Din Khwarazmshah came to Lahore and asked Iltutmish for land; Iltutmish refused

15 February 1222

Jalal al-Din Khwarazmshah, fleeing from the Mongols, came to Lahore and asked Iltutmish to give him suitable land (619/1222). Iltutmish, wary of Jalal al-Din's fame and influence, refused the request by a diplomatic maneuver and directed him against Qabacha; the Khwarazmians plundered Qabacha's lands and made them tributary.
