Baghdad passes to the Qaraquyunlu: defeat of the Jalayirid Sultan Ahmad

1411

According to the TDV's Qaraquyunlu entry, Qara Yusuf inflicted a heavy defeat on the Jalayirid Sultan Ahmad near Tabriz on 28 Rabi' al-Akhir 813 (30 August 1410); after Sultan Ahmad's death, in 814 (1411) he proclaimed his son Pir Budak sultan in Tabriz, and in the same year his eldest son Shah Mehmed conquered Baghdad. Thus Baghdad, which had been the Jalayirid capital for nearly a century, together with the surrounding Basra-Mosul-Kirkuk line, came under Qaraquyunlu rule.
