The Danishmendid Ayn al-Dawla took Gerger, Kâhta, Adıyaman, and Palu from the Crusaders

1150

The Malik of Malatya, Ayn al-Dawla, who attacked Crusader lands together with the Artuqid Kara Arslan, captured Gerger, Kâhta, Adıyaman, and Palu in 1150 and took many prisoners. In the same year Yaghibasan, Malik of Sivas-Amasya, took Ünye, Samsun, and Bafra.
