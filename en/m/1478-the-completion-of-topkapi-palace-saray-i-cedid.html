The completion of Topkapı Palace (Saray-ı Cedîd)

1478

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Topkapı Palace, the sources of the period diverge on the start and end dates of the construction; according to the date 883 (1478) on the inscription of the Bâb-ı Hümâyun, the construction, begun by order of Mehmed the Conqueror probably in 1465, had been largely completed by this year, and the palace took the place of the centre of the state in the old palace. Thus the sultan moved to the new residence that would be the administrative and ceremonial heart of the state for three centuries.
