The Battle of Carchemish: Nebuchadnezzar defeated the Egyptian army; Egypt lost Syria

604 BC

The crown prince Nebuchadnezzar crossed the Euphrates with the Babylonian army against the Egyptian army at Carchemish and routed it, destroyed the fugitives in the region of Hamath, and took the whole region of Hamath. Egypt withdrew from Syria and returned behind the Sinai frontier; Babylon became master of Syria-Palestine.
