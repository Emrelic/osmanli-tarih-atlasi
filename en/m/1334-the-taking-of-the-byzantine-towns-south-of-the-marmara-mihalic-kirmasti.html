The taking of the Byzantine towns south of the Marmara — Mihaliç, Kirmasti, Gölyazı

1334

After Nicaea and Nicomedia had been taken, the towns remaining in Byzantine hands west of the Bursa plain came under Ottoman administration one by one. Gölyazı around Lake Uluabat, and Kirmasti and Mihaliç along the Susurluk stream, completed the corridor connecting the southern shore of the Marmara to the lands of Karesi. These towns surrendered without undergoing a long siege, once their surroundings had been cut off; the Byzantine presence in the region was thus squeezed into the Gulf of Gemlik.
