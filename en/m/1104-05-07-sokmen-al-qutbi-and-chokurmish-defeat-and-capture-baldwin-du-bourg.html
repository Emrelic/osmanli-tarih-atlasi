Sökmen al-Qutbi and Chökürmish defeat and capture Baldwin du Bourg, Count of Edessa, and Joscelin near Harran (Balikh)

7 May 1104

The Crusader Baldwin du Bourg, Count of Edessa, and Joscelin, Count of Tell Bashir, were defeated by the Muslims and taken prisoner at the Battle of Harran on 9 Sha'ban 497 / 7 May 1104. Sökmen, the hero of the victory, fell ill and died in October of the same year.
