Portuguese capture of Bahrain: end of Jabrid rule

1521

When the Jabrid ruler Muqrin b. Zamil refused to pay tribute to Hormuz, the Portuguese fleet and the forces of the Kingdom of Hormuz, a Portuguese vassal, landed on the island; Muqrin was defeated in battle and Bahrain came under Portuguese administration in 1521. The island was thereafter governed for eighty years through Hormuz, mostly by Sunni Iranian governors. Bahrain's pearls and strategic position would make the island a direct target of the Ottoman-Portuguese rivalry in the gulf.
