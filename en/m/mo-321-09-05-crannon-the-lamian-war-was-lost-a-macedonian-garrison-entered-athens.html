Crannon: the Lamian War was lost; a Macedonian garrison entered Athens and democracy was suspended

5 September 321 BC

On 5 September 322 at Crannon the Greek alliance was defeated by Antipater and Craterus; Athens agreed to accept a Macedonian garrison at Munychia and to limit the citizenship qualification to property owners. Demosthenes committed suicide; classical Athenian democracy effectively came to an end.
