Ibn Battuta sets out from Mecca for Iraq and Iran

17 November 1326

After completing the pilgrimage he goes with the Iraqi caravan by way of Najaf, Baghdad and Basra to Isfahan and Shiraz; then he returns to Baghdad and visits Tabriz. Between 1327 and 1330 he makes three more pilgrimages.
