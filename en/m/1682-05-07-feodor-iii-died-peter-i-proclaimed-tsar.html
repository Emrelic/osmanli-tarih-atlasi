Feodor III died, Peter I proclaimed tsar

7 May 1682

After Feodor III died childless, the representatives of the estates who assembled in the Kremlin proclaimed the ten-year-old Peter tsar. After the streltsy uprising incited by the Miloslavsky faction, Peter and his elder half-brother Ivan V were made joint tsars, and government passed to the regent Sophia. Peter ruled alone after 1696.
