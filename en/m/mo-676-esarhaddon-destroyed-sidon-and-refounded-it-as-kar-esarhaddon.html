Esarhaddon destroyed Sidon and refounded it as Kar-Esarhaddon

676 BC

When Abdi-Milkutti, king of Sidon, rebelled against Assyria, Esarhaddon destroyed the city, put the king to death by beheading, and in place of the city founded an Assyrian provincial centre called Kar-Esarhaddon ("Harbour of Esarhaddon").
