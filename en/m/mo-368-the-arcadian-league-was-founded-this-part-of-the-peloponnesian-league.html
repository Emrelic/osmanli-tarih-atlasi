The Arcadian League was founded: this part of the Peloponnesian League was dissolved

368 BC

After Leuctra, the Arcadian cities, with Theban support, formed a federal league and built the new capital Megalopolis. Thus Sparta's Peloponnesian League effectively dissolved; Sparta withdrew to Laconia alone.
