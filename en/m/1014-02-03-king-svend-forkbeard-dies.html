King Svend Forkbeard dies

3 February 1014

Svend Forkbeard, who rose against Harald Bluetooth and came to the throne (by 987 at the latest), died on 3 February 1014; he had conquered England in 1013. His son Knud (Cnut) later founded the North Sea Empire.
