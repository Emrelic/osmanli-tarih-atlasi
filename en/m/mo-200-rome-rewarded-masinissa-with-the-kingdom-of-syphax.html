Rome rewarded Masinissa with the kingdom of Syphax

200 BC

In the peace after Zama Rome assigned Masinissa to the kingdom of Syphax and to the frontier lands taken from Carthage; Masinissa's claims on the "Emporia" region and on the Carthaginian frontier later became a cause of war.
