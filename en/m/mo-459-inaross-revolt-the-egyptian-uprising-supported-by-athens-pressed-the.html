Inaros's revolt: the Egyptian uprising supported by Athens pressed the Persians at Memphis and collapsed at Prosopitis in 454

459 BC

The Libyan Inaros raised almost all of Egypt against Artaxerxes and asked the Athenians for help; the Athenian fleet entered the Nile and took two-thirds of Memphis, besieging the remaining 'White Fortress'. The Persian commander Megabyzus besieged the Athenians on the island of Prosopitis for a year and a half and destroyed them (by draining the channel); Inaros was captured and crucified. Amyrtaeus in the Delta marshes, however, could not be caught.
