Pskov taken by the German Army — the occupation of Russian lands began

9 July 1941

In the third week of Barbarossa the Wehrmacht entered Pskov; the city remained under German military administration until 23 July 1944. Source: Pskov city chronology, memorialmuseums.org «Memorials in Pskov» — search summary [summary].
