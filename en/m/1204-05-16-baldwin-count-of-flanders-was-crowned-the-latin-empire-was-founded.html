Baldwin, Count of Flanders, was crowned: the Latin Empire was founded

16 May 1204

Eight days after the election, on 16 May 1204, the Crusaders crowned Baldwin, Count of Flanders and Hainaut, as emperor in Hagia Sophia. The Venetian Thomas Morosini was appointed Latin Patriarch, and the imperial lands were divided between the Crusaders and the Venetians.
