Cetin again became an Ottoman fortress

1670

In 1670 Cetin again became an Ottoman fortress and was substantially fortified in the eighteenth century: the medieval fortress was surrounded with bastioned walls. Until 1790 the fortress held the northwestern extremity of the Bosnian frontier.
