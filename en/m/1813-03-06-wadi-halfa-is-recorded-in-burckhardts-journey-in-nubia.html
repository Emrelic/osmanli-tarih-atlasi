Wadi Halfa is recorded in Burckhardt's journey in Nubia

6 March 1813

The Swiss traveller J. L. Burckhardt writes in his journal of 6 March 1813 that he passed through Wadi Halfa and that some trade was carried on there. It is the first dated testimony that can be found.
