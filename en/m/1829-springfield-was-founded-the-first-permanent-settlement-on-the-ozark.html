Springfield was founded — the first permanent settlement on the Ozark plateau

1829

Springfield was founded on the Ozark plateau, at the eastern end of the Osage lands in southwestern Missouri. The settlement became the center of the migration toward the land opened up after the Osage left the region in 1825, and became the main town of southwestern Missouri.
