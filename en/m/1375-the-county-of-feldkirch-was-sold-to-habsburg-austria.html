The County of Feldkirch was sold to Habsburg Austria

1375

Rudolf, the last count of the Montfort-Feldkirch line, sold all his property to the Habsburgs, dukes of Austria, in 1375; the actual transfer was completed with his death (1390). Feldkirch was the first major part of Vorarlberg to pass into Habsburg hands.
