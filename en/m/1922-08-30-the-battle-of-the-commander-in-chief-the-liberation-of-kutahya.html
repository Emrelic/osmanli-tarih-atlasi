The Battle of the Commander-in-Chief — the liberation of Kütahya

30 August 1922

The main forces of the Greek army were encircled between Afyon and Dumlupınar. According to the TDV article on the National Struggle, the encircling operation was completed on 30 August with the Battle of the Commander-in-Chief, directed personally by the Commander-in-Chief Mustafa Kemal Pasha. According to the TDV article on Kütahya, on the same day a cavalry division liberated Kütahya, which the retreating Greek forces had burned and destroyed.
