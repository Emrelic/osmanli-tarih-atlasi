Ottoman-Genoese ahidname — renewal of the 1352 treaty

8 June 1387

With Genoese envoys from Pera, the 1352 commercial agreement dating from the time of Orhan Gazi was renewed; taxes between the two states, the treatment of captives and free trade were fixed anew. This is regarded as the first officially known ahidname of friendship and trade between the Ottomans and the Genoese — not a military alliance but an entirely commercial agreement.
