Diodotus, satrap of Bactria, broke away from the Seleucids; the Graeco-Bactrian Kingdom was born

249 BC

Diodotus, satrap of Bactria and Sogdiana, took advantage of the weakness of Antiochus II and declared his independence. Graeco-Bactria forced the eastern frontier of the Seleucid state to withdraw from Parthava and Bactria.
