Victory of Nicopolis

25 September 1396

The great Crusader army, assembled at the call of the Hungarian king Sigismund and joined also by French and Burgundian knights, was heavily defeated by Bayezid the Thunderbolt before Nicopolis on the bank of the Danube. The victory made Ottoman dominion over Bulgaria definitive; for a generation no great Crusade was organized in Europe.
