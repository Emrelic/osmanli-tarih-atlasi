Occupation of In Salah (Tidikelt) by the French

29 December 1899

In Salah, the center of the Tidikelt cluster of oases in the middle of the Sahara, was taken by French forces on 29 December 1899. This is the first great step of the French expansion southward from the Algerian coast to spill into the desert; within two years Touat and Gourara would follow the same course.
