Antiochus I defeated the Galatians in the "Battle of the Elephants"

274 BC

Antiochus I, having defeated the Galatians with his elephants, took the epithet Soter ("saviour"); the Galatians returned to a settled order of tribute and raiding in central Anatolia. Conflicts with Pergamum and Bithynia continued in the following centuries.
