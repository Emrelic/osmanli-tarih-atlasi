The conquest of the fortresses of Lugoj and Caransebeş — continuation of the Temesvár campaign

1 August 1552

The fortress of Temesvár surrendered on 26 July 1552 after three weeks of resistance. In August the fortresses of Lugoj and Caransebeş were also taken; fifteen fortresses were captured along the Mureş valley, and a new beylerbeylik with its centre at Temesvár was established.
