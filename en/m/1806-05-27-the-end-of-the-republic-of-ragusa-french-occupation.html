The end of the Republic of Ragusa — French occupation

27 May 1806

The French, who had settled on the Dalmatian coast by the Treaty of Campo Formio (1797), seized Dubrovnik on 27 May 1806 and ended the small merchant republic. Thus the three-and-a-half-century tributary status of Dubrovnik, which had preserved its autonomy and trade privileges by paying tribute to the Ottomans since 1458, came to an end; the city and the republic's island of Mljet pass on the map to direct French administration.
