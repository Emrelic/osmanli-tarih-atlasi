The surrender of Or Kapı to Münnich — the Russian army entered the Crimea

1 June 1736

Münnich reached the front of the Or Kapı lines on 16 May (Julian) and stormed the lines at dawn on 20 May; the surprised Tatar army withdrew, and the fortress of Or surrendered the next day. The Russian army marched into the interior of the peninsula two days later; Or Kapı remained in Russian hands until the end of August.
