On his deathbed Henry III recognized Henry of Navarre (Henry IV) as his successor: the Bourbon dynasty begins

1 August 1589

Henry III, struck down by an assassin, as the last king of the Valois line solemnly recognized Henry of Navarre as his successor on 1 August 1589; with his death (2 August) the Bourbon dynasty came to the French throne.
