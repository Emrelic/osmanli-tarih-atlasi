King Tancred dies in Palermo; the de facto end of the Norman dynasty

20 February 1194

Tancred died on 20 February 1194; he had lost his elder son Roger III a few months earlier (24 December 1193). The accession of his younger son William III could not prevent Henry VI's conquest.
