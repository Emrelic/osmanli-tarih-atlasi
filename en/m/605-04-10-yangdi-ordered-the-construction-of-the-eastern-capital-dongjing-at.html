Yangdi ordered the construction of the Eastern Capital (Dongjing) at Luoyang

10 April 605

Yang Guang ordered Yang Su, Yang Da and Yuwen Kai to build the Eastern Capital on the Yi and Luo rivers and to settle the townspeople of Yuzhou there; Luoyang was founded as a second capital.
