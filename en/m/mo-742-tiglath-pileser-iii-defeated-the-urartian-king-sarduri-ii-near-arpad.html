Tiglath-Pileser III defeated the Urartian king Sarduri II near Arpad

742 BC

Sarduri II's attempt to lead the Syrian states to independence ended in the victory of Tiglath-Pileser III. Urartu lost its influence in Syria; Assyria left no rival for Anatolia and Syria.
