Completion of the Büyükçekmece Bridge

1567

The bridge, begun at the order of Süleyman the Lawgiver, was completed in 1567-68 by his son Selim II after the sultan's death during the Szigetvár campaign. Mimar Sinan himself also repaired those sections of the bridge, which he built at the point where Lake Büyükçekmece meets the sea, that had been damaged by flooding.
