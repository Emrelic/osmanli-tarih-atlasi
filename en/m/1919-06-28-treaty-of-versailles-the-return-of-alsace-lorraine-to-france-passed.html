Treaty of Versailles — the return of Alsace-Lorraine to France passed into law

28 June 1919

The Paris Peace Conference convened on 18 January 1919, and the Treaty of Versailles was signed with Germany on 28 June. Article 51 of the treaty returned Alsace-Lorraine (Strasbourg, Metz, Colmar, Mulhouse), which had been German since 1871, to French sovereignty — but it tied the transfer not to the day of signature but, retroactively, to the armistice of 11 November 1918. The same series of conferences produced Saint-Germain with Austria (10 September 1919), Neuilly with Bulgaria (27 November 1919), Trianon with Hungary (4 June 1920) and Sèvres with the Ottoman Empire (10 August 1920).
