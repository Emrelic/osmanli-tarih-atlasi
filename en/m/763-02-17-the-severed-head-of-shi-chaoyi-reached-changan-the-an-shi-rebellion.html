The severed head of Shi Chaoyi reached Chang'an: the An-Shi rebellion ended

17 February 763

Shi Chaoyi hanged himself in a forest near the Wenquan Fortress as the army of Li Huaixian, governor of Youzhou, pursued him; Li Huaixian sent his head to the Tang and the rebellion ended after more than seven years.
