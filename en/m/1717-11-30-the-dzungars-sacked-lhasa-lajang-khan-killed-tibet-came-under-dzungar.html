The Dzungars sacked Lhasa — Lajang Khan killed, Tibet came under Dzungar occupation

30 November 1717

The Dzungar army under Tsering Dondup attacked Lhasa, defended by the Khoshut ruler Lajang Khan, from all directions after midnight; the Dzungars, who found support from inside, took and plundered the city and also looted the Potala. Lajang Khan was killed as he fled; the Dzungars set up a puppet Tibetan government headed by sTag-rtse-pa.
