Loss of Szigetvár

3 February 1689

Szigetvár was the fortress where Süleyman the Lawgiver died during the siege in 1566 on his thirteenth and last campaign; for this reason it had a symbolic place in Ottoman memory. A hundred and twenty-three years later, after all the fortresses around it had fallen, the fortress, protected by marshes, surrendered too. The mausoleum where the Lawgiver's internal organs were buried and the town around it were destroyed after it passed to Habsburg rule. Another settlement passing from the Ottomans to the Habsburgs on the same day in the atlas: Barcs — follows Szigetvár's senior-center chain (Bánlaky 0017/1449; declared approximation).
