Yinxu (Anyang, Late Shang capital): Di Yi/Di Xin phase: end of the Shang dynasty (final phase of Yinxu)

1080 BC

Late Shang capital; the oracle bones were dated by Bayesian 14C according to the phases of the kings: Wu Ding phase 1254-1197, Di Yi/Di Xin phase 1121-1041; end of the Shang ~1041.
