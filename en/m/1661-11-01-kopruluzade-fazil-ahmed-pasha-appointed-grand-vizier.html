Köprülüzade Fazıl Ahmed Pasha appointed grand vizier

1 November 1661

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Köprülüzade Fazıl Ahmed Pasha, upon the death of his father Köprülü Mehmed Pasha he was made grand vizier on 8 Rabi al-Awwal 1072 (1 November 1661). Fazıl Ahmed Pasha, known, unlike his father, as gentle in temperament, pious and a patron of scholars and artists, had libraries and charitable institutions founded in Istanbul and the conquered lands during his grand vizierate of more than fifteen years.
