Stefan Nemanja made the Serbian state independent

1180

Taking advantage of the confusion created by Manuel's death, Stefan Nemanja made Raška (Rascia) the nucleus and laid the foundation of a great Serbian state that also included Zeta and Hum; he retained the title 'Grand Župan'. His dynasty is called the 'Nemanjić'.
