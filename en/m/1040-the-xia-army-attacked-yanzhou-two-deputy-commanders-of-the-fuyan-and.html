The Xia army attacked Yanzhou; two deputy commanders of the Fuyan and Huanqing routes were captured

1040

In the 1st month of the 1st year of Kangding, Zhao Yuanhao (Li Yuanhao) attacked Yanzhou (Yan'an) and seized the deputy commanders-in-chief of the Fuyan and Huanqing routes. In the 3rd month of the same year the Song court discussed the offense and defense plans for Shaanxi and entrusted Han Qi with the repair of the Shaanxi cities.
