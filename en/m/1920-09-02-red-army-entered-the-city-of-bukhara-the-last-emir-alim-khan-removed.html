Red Army entered the city of Bukhara: the last emir Alim Khan removed from the throne

2 September 1920

The Red Army entered the city of Bukhara on 2 September 1920 and the last Manghit emir Alim Khan was removed from the throne. The emir withdrew to the east of the country, to Dushanbe, and for half a year directed the struggle against the Soviets from there; the Red Army took Dushanbe on 21 February 1921, and the emir passed farther east and from there to Afghanistan. The khanate was formally abolished on 6 October 1920 (see the Bukharan People's Soviet Republic).
