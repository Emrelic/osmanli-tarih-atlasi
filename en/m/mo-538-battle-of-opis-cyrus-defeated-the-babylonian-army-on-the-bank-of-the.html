Battle of Opis: Cyrus defeated the Babylonian army on the bank of the Tigris

538 BC

Cyrus's Persians met the Akkadian army at Opis on the Tigris and defeated it; the people of Babylon withdrew, and there was plunder and massacre. The battle opened the way for the fall of Babylon two weeks later.
