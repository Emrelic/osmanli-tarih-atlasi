Přemysl Ottokar I is crowned king of Bohemia (ruler in 1197, crowned in 1198)

1198

Přemysl Ottokar I, who became the undisputed ruler of Bohemia in 1197, was crowned king in 1198; in 1212, by the Golden Bull of Sicily, the royal title became hereditary and relations with the empire were regulated.
