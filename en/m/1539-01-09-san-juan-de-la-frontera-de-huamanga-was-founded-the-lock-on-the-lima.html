San Juan de la Frontera de Huamanga was founded — the lock on the Lima–Cuzco road

9 January 1539

The resistance that Manco Inca conducted from Vilcabamba was cutting the road between Lima and Cuzco. To secure this road, Francisco Pizarro founded San Juan de la Frontera de Huamanga midway between the two capitals; the word 'frontera' (frontier) in the town's name states its purpose of foundation directly.
