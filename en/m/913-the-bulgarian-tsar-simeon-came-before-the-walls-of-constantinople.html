The Bulgarian Tsar Simeon came before the walls of Constantinople

913

Simeon pressed up to the walls of Constantinople with the aim of obtaining the Byzantine crown; the government came to an agreement with him by making heavy concessions, and he was given the title 'Basileus of the Bulgarians'.
