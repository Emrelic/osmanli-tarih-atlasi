The Babenbergs became lords of the city of Vienna about 1135 (Vienna, March of the Babenbergs)

1135

AEIOU: the Babenbergs (976-1246) became lords of the city of Vienna about 1135; the city, first mentioned in 881, had before this long been under Hungarian influence (the end year is not given in the source).
