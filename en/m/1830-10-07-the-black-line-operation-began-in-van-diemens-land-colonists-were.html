The Black Line operation began in Van Diemen's Land: colonists were mobilised to capture the Tasmanian Aborigines

7 October 1830

Governor Arthur's Colonial Secretary's Office notice of 25 September 1830 announced that the public would move out en masse on 7 October 1830 and that Major Douglas would occupy the stations along the line the same day. The aim was to capture the Aboriginal tribes and drive them towards the Tasman Peninsula.
