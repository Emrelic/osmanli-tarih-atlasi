The Hünkâr Mosque at Sarajevo — the earliest dated testimony of the settlement

1458

İshak Bey, sancak bey of Bosnia, had a palace built for himself in the first Muslim settlement on the Sarajevo plain, the Hünkâr Mosque in the name of Sultan Mehmed the Conqueror, and a bath beside it. The mosque is dated 862 (1458); it is the earliest dated building of Sarajevo known.
