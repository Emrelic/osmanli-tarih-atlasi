Kashta, king of Kush (Napata), advanced into Upper Egypt; he extended his influence to Elephantine and Thebes

749 BC

The Kushite kings at Napata grew stronger toward the north by taking advantage of Egypt's fragmentation in the Third Intermediate Period. From the monuments it is clear that Kashta established influence in Upper Egypt as far as Elephantine; Amenirdis I of the Kushite dynasty (Piye's sister) was installed at Thebes with the title 'God's Wife of Amun', and this office became Kush's instrument over Thebes. Thus the ground was prepared on which the Twenty-fifth Dynasty would be founded.
