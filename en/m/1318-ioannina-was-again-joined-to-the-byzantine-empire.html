Ioannina was again joined to the Byzantine Empire

1318

Ioannina, which changed hands many times in the second half of the 13th century, was again joined to Byzantium in 1318, but preserved its autonomy to a certain degree; the centre of the Despotate of Epirus shifted to Arta.
