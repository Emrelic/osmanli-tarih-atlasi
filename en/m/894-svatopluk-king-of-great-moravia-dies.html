Svatopluk, king of Great Moravia, dies

894

Svatopluk, who after overthrowing Rastislav and handing him over to the Franks had extended Moravia from the Oder to the Gran, died in 894; strife began among his sons, and Magyar raids brought about the collapse of the state.
