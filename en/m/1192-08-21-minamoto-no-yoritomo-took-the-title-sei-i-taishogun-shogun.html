Minamoto no Yoritomo took the title sei-i taishogun (shogun)

21 August 1192

In 1192 the court gave Yoritomo the title 'barbarian-subduing great general' (sei-i taishogun), formally recognizing the actual military power at Kamakura; the Cambridge History regards this year as one of the possible turning points for the formal founding of the bakufu.
