The acceptance of Ottoman domination by the fortress of Bayezid in the Campaign of the Two Iraqs

23 June 1534

When Grand Vizier Ibrahim Pasha came to the vicinity of Bingöl in the Campaign of the Two Iraqs, Van, Toprakkale, Avnik and Bayezid fortresses, together with many Kurdish tribes in the region, agreed to enter Ottoman domination. Bayezid had joined the Ottomans in 1514 in Selim the Grim's campaign, but when the army returned, the Safavids had retaken the region.
