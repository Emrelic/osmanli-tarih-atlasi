Régie Administration established (tobacco monopoly)

1884

Tobacco revenues were transferred to the Régie company, with foreign capital, within the Ottoman Public Debt Administration; the conflicts of 'Régie smuggling' between the peasants and the gendarmes (kolcu) continued for decades. Other settlements lost on the same date: Zeila.
