Rebellion of Savcı Bey — joint revolt of a prince and the Byzantine heir

15 May 1373

Savcı Bey, son of Murad I, and Andronikos Palaiologos, son of the Byzantine Emperor John V, rose against their fathers simultaneously in Bursa and Constantinople at the beginning of May 1373. This alliance, which united the heirs of the two dynasties, was swiftly suppressed; Savcı Bey was captured, blinded and executed. The date 787 (1385) given for the rebellion by the Ottoman tradition is erroneous; the event falls just after the years in which Byzantium entered Ottoman vassalage and shows how intertwined the two courts were.
