Salerno fell to Robert Guiscard; the Lombard Principality of Salerno ended

1076

After a long resistance Salerno surrendered to Robert Guiscard in December 1076; Prince Gisulf II withdrew into exile at the papal court. Guiscard made Salerno the capital of his own duchy.
