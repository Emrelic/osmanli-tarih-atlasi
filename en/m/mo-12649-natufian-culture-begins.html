Natufian Culture: begins

12649 BC

Late Epipaleolithic: large sites, stone structures, graves; 27 AMS dates at Shubayqa 1: first settlement ~14,600-14,200 cal BP, abandonment of ~900 years, reoccupation 13,300-13,000 and ~12,380-11,500 cal BP (2σ).
