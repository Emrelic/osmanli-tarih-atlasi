The Uqaylid Quraysh has the khutba read in the name of Tughril Beg

5 November 1054

When Tughril Beg came to the region on account of his Baghdad campaign, the Uqaylid emir Quraysh had the khutba read in Tughril Beg's name in Sha'ban 446 / November 1054; after the killing of the Marwanid Sulayman he was gravely wounded in 447/1055.
