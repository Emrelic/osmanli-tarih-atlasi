Diriyah fell — the First Saudi State ended

9 September 1818

After a siege of six months Diriyah surrendered and was destroyed. The emir Abdullah b. Saud was sent to Istanbul and executed. The Ottoman flag reached so deep into the interior of Arabia for the first time; the Najd garrison remained until 1824. Other places joined on the same date: Diriyah (Najd).
