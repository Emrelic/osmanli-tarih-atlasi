Commercial Convention of Balta Limanı

16 August 1838

Britain was granted the most privileged position even in internal trade, and the yed-i vahit (monopoly) system was abolished; while domestic production was left unprotected, the treaty became the document of the full opening of the Ottoman market to free trade.
