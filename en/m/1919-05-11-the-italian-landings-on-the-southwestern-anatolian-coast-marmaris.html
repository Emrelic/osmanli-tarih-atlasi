The Italian landings on the southwestern Anatolian coast — Marmaris, Bodrum, Kuşadası

11 May 1919

Italy, which had withdrawn from the Paris Peace Conference in protest at the pro-Greek attitude of its allies, landed troops on the southwestern coasts after Antalya. According to the TDV, Marmaris was occupied by the Italians on 5 May, Bodrum on 11 May and Kuşadası on 13 May. In the same days the Greeks also landed at Fethiye; the rivalry of the two states over western Anatolia prepared the road to the occupation of Izmir.
