Bukharan People's Soviet Republic proclaimed

8 October 1920

After the Red Army overthrew the Emirate of Bukhara on 28-31 August 1920 and drove Emir Alim Khan to Eastern Bukhara, the Bukharan People's Soviet Republic was proclaimed under the chairmanship of Faizullah Khojaev.
