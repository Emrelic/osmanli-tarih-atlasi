Franz Joseph and Elisabeth were crowned king and queen of Hungary at Buda

8 June 1867

The Crown of St Stephen was placed on Franz Joseph's head in the Matthias Church by the Primate and by Prime Minister Andrássy; by this ceremony the Ausgleich was tied to the king's authority in Hungarian law.
