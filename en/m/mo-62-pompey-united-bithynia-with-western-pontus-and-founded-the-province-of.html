Pompey united Bithynia with western Pontus and founded the province of Bithynia et Pontus

62 BC

In his eastern settlement (65-63) Pompey created the province of Bithynia et Pontus by uniting Bithynia with the western half of Pontus; the interior districts of Pontus in the east were given to local princes.
