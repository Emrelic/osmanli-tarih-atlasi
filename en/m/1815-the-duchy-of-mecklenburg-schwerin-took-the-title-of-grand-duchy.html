The Duchy of Mecklenburg-Schwerin took the title of grand duchy

1815

Friedrich Franz I, Duke of Mecklenburg-Schwerin, took the title of grand duchy ('großherzogliche Würde') in 1815; the entry for the Duchy of Mecklenburg (1348-1815) ends at this point, and the country continues as a grand duchy within the German Confederation.
