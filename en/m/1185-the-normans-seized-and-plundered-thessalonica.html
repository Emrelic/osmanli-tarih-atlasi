The Normans seized and plundered Thessalonica

1185

The Normans, coming from Sicily and southern Italy, attacked Thessalonica by land and sea in August 1185 with a fleet of 200 ships and an army; the city was dreadfully plundered. In the capital this news hastened the uprising against Andronikos.
