Jalal al-Din Khwarazmshah defeated the Georgian-Armenian-Kipchak alliance near Lake Betak and entered Tiflis

20 November 1229

Jalal al-Din, who was busy with the siege of Ahlat, learned from his vizier Sharaf al-Mulk that the Georgians were preparing to seize Tiflis; he confronted the allies, made up of Georgians, Armenians, and Kipchaks, near Lake Betak, neutralized the Kipchaks by diplomatic means, routed the Georgian army, and entered Tiflis (627/1229).
