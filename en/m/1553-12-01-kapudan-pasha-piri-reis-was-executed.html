Kapudan pasha Piri Reis was executed

1 December 1553

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Pîrî Reis, when the accusations that, while lifting the siege of Hormuz, he had plundered the property of Muslims in return for bribes and had abandoned his army on the battlefield reached Egypt, the famous seaman and cartographer was executed at Cairo at the end of the year 960 (late 1553). The death of Piri Reis, whose property was confiscated, is regarded as a result of the rivalry among pashas in the Basra and Indian Ocean naval policy of the time.
