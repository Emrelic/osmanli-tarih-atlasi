Akça Koca took Aydos and the fort of Samandıra

1328

After the capture of Kandıra, Akça Koca, together with Konuralp and Gazi Abdurrahman, took the fortress of Aydos near Kartal, and then the fort of Samandıra to its north; the region of Samandıra was given to him as property. Akça Koca died near Kandıra in 1328; the region where he had served as frontier lord was given to Prince Murad.
