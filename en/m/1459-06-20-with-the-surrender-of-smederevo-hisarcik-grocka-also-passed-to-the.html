With the surrender of Smederevo, Hisarcık (Grocka) also passed to the Ottomans

20 June 1459

Helena, widow of Lazar Branković, surrendered the fortress of Smederevo to the Ottomans; the Serbian Despotate came to an end. Hisarcık (Grocka), with Smederevo as its senior center, passes to the Ottomans on the same day.
