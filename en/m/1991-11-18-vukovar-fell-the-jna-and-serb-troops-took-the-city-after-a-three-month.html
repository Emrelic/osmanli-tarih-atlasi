Vukovar fell — the JNA and Serb troops took the city after a three-month siege

18 November 1991

The remaining Croatian defenders of besieged Vukovar surrendered on 18 November 1991; the city fell into the hands of the Yugoslav People's Army and Serb forces. The city later passed through the period of the UN Transitional Administration in Eastern Slavonia (UNTAES).
