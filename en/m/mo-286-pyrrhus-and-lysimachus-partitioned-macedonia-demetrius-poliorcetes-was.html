Pyrrhus and Lysimachus partitioned Macedonia; Demetrius Poliorcetes was overthrown

286 BC

In the summer of 287, when Demetrius crossed to Asia, Pyrrhus and Lysimachus together divided Macedonia into two regions; Demetrius was brought down. This partition lasted only two years.
