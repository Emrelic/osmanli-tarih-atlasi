The Khwarazmians entered Jerusalem: Jerusalem passed out of the Crusaders' hands

11 July 1244

Khwarazmian troops, who had ravaged the surroundings of Damascus, entered Jerusalem on 11 July 1244; the city passed out of the Crusaders' hands. In October of the same year, in the battle near Gaza, the Egyptian Ayyubids, supported by the Khwarazmians, routed the Syrian Ayyubids and their allies the Crusaders, and Jerusalem definitively came under the rule of the Egyptian Ayyubids.
