The conquest of the fortress of Sinj

1513

Ottoman raiding forces on the Bosnian frontier took the fortress of Sinj, extending into the interior of Dalmatia, from the Kingdom of Hungary. This was an early link in the Dalmatian frontier line, which would expand nine years later with Gazi Husrev Bey's conquest of Knin and its surroundings; the fortress would be lost to Venice in 1686.
