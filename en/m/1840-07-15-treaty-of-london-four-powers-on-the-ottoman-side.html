Treaty of London: four powers on the Ottoman side

15 July 1840

The treaty, signed with France left out, gave Mehmed Ali an ultimatum: if he accepted within ten days, the hereditary governorship of Egypt and Acre for life; if within twenty days, Egypt only; if he refused, nothing. The governor refused.
