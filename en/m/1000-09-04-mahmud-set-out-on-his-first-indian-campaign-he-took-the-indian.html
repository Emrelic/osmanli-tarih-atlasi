Mahmud set out on his first Indian campaign; he took the Indian fortresses in the Lamghan region

4 September 1000

In Shawwal 390 (September 1000) Mahmud set out on his first Indian campaign, took several fortresses held by the Indians in the Lamghan region east of Kabul, and returned to Ghazna.
