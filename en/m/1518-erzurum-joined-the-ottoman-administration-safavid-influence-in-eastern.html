Erzurum Joined the Ottoman Administration — Safavid Influence in Eastern Anatolia Was Broken

1518

Although Ottoman influence in the region grew after the victory at Chaldiran (1514), the full seizure of Erzurum was delayed until Iranian influence was broken; the city came under Ottoman administration only in 1518-19, after the Egyptian campaign. Erzurum had come under Safavid rule in 1502, when Shah Ismail ended the Aqquyunlu Principality; that sixteen-year period was thus closed. In 1534, during the Campaign of the Two Iraqs, the Erzurum beylerbeylik would be established. Between 1518 and 1519, Aşkale, together with the Erzurum region, came under Ottoman administration (its day could not be verified).
