Fortress of Tsaritsyn first mentioned in a document

2 July 1589

The decree of Tsar Feodor Ivanovich dated 2 July 1589 mentions the fortress of Tsaritsyn, built to protect the southern border of the Volga; this date is regarded as the city's founding day.
