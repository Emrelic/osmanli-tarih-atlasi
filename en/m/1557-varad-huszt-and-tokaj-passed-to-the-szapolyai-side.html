Várad, Huszt and Tokaj passed to the Szapolyai side

1557

Following Isabella and János Zsigmond's return to Transylvania in 1556, the supporters of Szapolyai besieged Nagyvárad in 1556. In 1557 the aristocrats of the Upper Tisza took the most important royal fortresses — Várad, Huszt and Tokaj; only Gyula and Világos remained in Ferdinand's hands. Várad thereafter became a fortress of the Principality of Transylvania and remained so until the Ottomans took it in 1660.
