Disaster at Shaykan: destruction of Hicks Pasha's army

5 November 1883

Hicks Pasha, a retired British officer who commanded an Egyptian force of about ten thousand men sent from Cairo, was surrounded by the Mahdist forces at Shaykan on 5 November 1883 while advancing in the Kordofan desert without water or guides, and the army was almost completely annihilated. This disaster broke the backbone of Egyptian rule in the Sudan: within a month Darfur and Bahr al-Ghazal surrendered and all of Kordofan passed to the Mahdi. The road to the fall of Khartoum begins here.
