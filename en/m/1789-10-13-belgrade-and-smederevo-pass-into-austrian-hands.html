Belgrade and Smederevo pass into Austrian hands

13 October 1789

In the third year of the war the Austrian commander Loudon took the Ottoman frontier fortresses along the Danube. Smederevo surrendered on 13 October 1789 on condition that the garrison's 300 soldiers leave after laying down their arms; Belgrade, which had been an Ottoman frontier fortress for fifty years, also passed into Austrian hands the same autumn. Between 1789 and 1791 Loudon had entrenchments built commanding the citadel and suburb of Smederevo. Both cities were returned to the Ottoman State by the Treaty of Sistova signed on 4 August 1791; for this reason both are shown on the map as occupation, not annexation. The day of Belgrade's surrender does not appear in any of the four TDV entries read (the entry gives only the year); the date here is the day of Smederevo, and for Belgrade it is not an estimate but the only known day of the same operation.
