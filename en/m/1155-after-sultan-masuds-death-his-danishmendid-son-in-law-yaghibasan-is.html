After Sultan Mas'ud's death, his Danishmendid son-in-law Yaghibasan is given Amasya, Ankara and Cappadocia

1155

When Kilij Arslan II came to the throne on Mas'ud's death (1155), of Mas'ud's two sons-in-law belonging to the Danishmendid dynasty, Yaghibasan was given Amasya, Ankara and Cappadocia, and Dhu al-Nun the cities of Kayseri and Sivas; his brothers claimed the throne and rose in revolt.
