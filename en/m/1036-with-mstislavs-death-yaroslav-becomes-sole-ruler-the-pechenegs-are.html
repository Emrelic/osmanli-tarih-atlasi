With Mstislav's death Yaroslav becomes sole ruler; the Pechenegs are heavily defeated

1036

After Mstislav's death in 1036, Yaroslav annexed his lands as well (except Polotsk) and became the sole ruler of Kievan Rus'; at about the same time he heavily defeated the Pechenegs who attacked Kiev (1037) and began construction of the Cathedral of Hagia Sophia.
