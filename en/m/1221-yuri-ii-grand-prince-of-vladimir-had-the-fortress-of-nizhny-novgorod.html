Yuri II, Grand Prince of Vladimir, had the fortress of Nizhny Novgorod built

1221

The Russian prince Yuri, son of Vsevolod, had the fortress of Nizhny Novgorod (Gorky) built in 1221 as part of Russian pressure on the Volga Bulgar lands.
