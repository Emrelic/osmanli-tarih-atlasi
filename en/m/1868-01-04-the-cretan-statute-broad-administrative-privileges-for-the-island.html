The Cretan Statute: Broad Administrative Privileges for the Island

4 January 1868

To end the three-year revolt, the Grand Vizier Âlî Pasha crossed to the island in person, and by the statute issued with the sultan's approval the Cretans were granted mixed administrative councils, local courts, the use of Greek in official transactions, and tax relief. This arrangement kept Ottoman dominion on the island standing for thirty more years. However, the privileges granted to the Christian population weakened the position of the Muslim Cretans and sowed the seeds of the crisis of 1896-1898.
