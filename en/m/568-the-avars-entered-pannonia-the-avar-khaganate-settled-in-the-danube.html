The Avars entered Pannonia: the Avar Khaganate settled in the Danube basin

568

After the fall of the Gepid state, the Avars under Bayan settled in Pannonia; the Danube–Tisza basin became the centre of the Avar Khaganate. In 626 the khaganate would besiege Constantinople and, together with the Slavs, launch raids into the Balkans.
