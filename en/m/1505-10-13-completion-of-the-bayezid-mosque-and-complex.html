Completion of the Bayezid Mosque and Complex

13 October 1505

Construction of the Bayezid Mosque and Complex, built for Bayezid II, according to an archive document began in 906 (1500) and was completed on 14 Jumada al-Ula 911 (13 October 1505). Its architect is disputed: for a long time, relying on Ayvansarayi's record, it was considered the work of Mimar Hayreddin, then it was attributed to Mimar Kemaleddin, and according to some the two worked together; the same document, however, shows the master to be Yakub Shah b. Sultan Shah. The mosque is counted among the important monuments of the transition from pre-classical Ottoman architecture to the classical period.
