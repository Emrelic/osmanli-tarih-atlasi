Hafiz Osman develops his own calligraphic style

1679

Hafiz Osman, who came from the line of Sheikh Hamdullah, developed in 1679 in the aqlam-i sitte a style of his own, later to be called the "Hafiz Osman school." This style has continued to the present day in two separate branches.
