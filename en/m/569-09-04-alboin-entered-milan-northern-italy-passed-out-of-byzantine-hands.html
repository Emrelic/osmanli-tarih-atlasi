Alboin entered Milan: northern Italy passed out of Byzantine hands

4 September 569

Alboin entered Milan on 4 September 569; the bishop Honoratus fled to Genoa. Pavia fell after a long siege and the Po valley passed largely into the hands of the Lombards; Byzantium retained only some coastal and fortress cities.
