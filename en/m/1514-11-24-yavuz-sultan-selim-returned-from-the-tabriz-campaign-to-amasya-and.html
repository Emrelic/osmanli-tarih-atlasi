Yavuz Sultan Selim returned from the Tabriz campaign to Amasya and spent the winter there

24 November 1514

Yavuz Sultan Selim, who stayed nine days in Tabriz, gave up spending the winter in Iran because of food shortages and the reluctance of the soldiers, and returned by way of Kars and Bayburt. The sultan, who spent the Feast of Ramadan at Niksar, reached Amasya and passed the winter there. He intended to march again against the Safavids the next year; at Amasya pressure from the Janissaries to return to Istanbul began, and the campaign continued in the spring of 1515 with the conquest of Kemah.
