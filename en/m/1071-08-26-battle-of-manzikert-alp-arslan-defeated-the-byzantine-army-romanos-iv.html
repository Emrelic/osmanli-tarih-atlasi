Battle of Manzikert: Alp Arslan defeated the Byzantine army, Romanos IV was captured

26 August 1071

The Turkish forces of Sultan Alp Arslan defeated at Manzikert on 26 August 1071 the Byzantine army, which consisted for the most part of Norman, Frankish, Oghuz and Pecheneg mercenaries. Emperor Romanos IV was captured; the process of the opening of Anatolia to the Turks began.
