Seydi Ali Reis's journey to India

7 December 1553

Seydi Ali Reis, who was charged with bringing the fleet at Basra to Egypt, was driven by storms to India; the Mir'âtü'l-memâlik, in which he related the adventure of his return, is among the first Ottoman travel books.
