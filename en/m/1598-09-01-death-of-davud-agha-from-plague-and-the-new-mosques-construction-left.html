Death of Davud Agha from plague and the New Mosque's construction left unfinished

1 September 1598

Dalgıç Ahmed Çavuş was appointed chief architect in place of Davud Agha, who died of plague in September 1598 when he had raised the New Mosque only a cubit above the ground. The TDV notes that in an alternative account, based on a chronogram poem, the date of death also appears as 1599-1600, and that the exact date cannot be established.
