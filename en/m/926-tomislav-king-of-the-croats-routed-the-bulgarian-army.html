Tomislav, King of the Croats, routed the Bulgarian army

926

The Bulgarian Tsar Simeon attacked Croatia, an ally of Byzantium, but his army was defeated and scattered by Tomislav, King of the Croats. This victory consolidated the authority of the Croatian kingdom over the Dalmatian cities.
