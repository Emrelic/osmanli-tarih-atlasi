Byzantium retook Krujë (Akçahisar), Durrës and Vlorë from the Angevins

1284

Durrës and Krujë, which Charles of Anjou had taken in 1271-1272, were retaken after Charles's death by the Byzantine emperor Andronikos II (TDV Kruya: 'he retook Valona, Durrës and Krujë'). Only the year is known.
