Tantamani came down from Kush and retook Thebes and Memphis; he besieged the Assyrian vassals

663 BC

Taharqa's successor Tantamani, according to the Dream Stele, after being crowned at Napata, went down the river to the north: he made an offering to Khnum at Elephantine, came to Thebes and Memphis, where he took the city by massacring those who opposed him, while the lords of the Delta hid or submitted. This undertaking threatened the Delta order of Psamtik I and Assyria and brought on the Assyrian punitive campaign.
