Battle of Savra — opening of the gates of Albania

18 September 1385

At the call for help of the Albanian lord Karlo Thopia, hard pressed against the Balšić dynasty, the Ottoman forces routed the army of Balša II on the plain of Savra; Balša lost his life on the battlefield. This battle, entered upon at the invitation of a native lord, gave the Ottomans their first lasting influence in the Albanian lands extending to the Adriatic coast. This policy, which turned the setting of the region's lords against one another into an opportunity, was to be the unchanging method of Balkan conquests throughout the next century.
