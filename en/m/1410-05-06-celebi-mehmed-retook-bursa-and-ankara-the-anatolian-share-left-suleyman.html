Çelebi Mehmed retook Bursa and Ankara — the Anatolian share left Süleyman Çelebi

6 May 1410

Following Musa Çelebi's successes in Rumelia (Yambol, 13 February 1410), Emir Süleyman Çelebi, who was in Anatolia, crossed to Rumelia. After Süleyman's departure from Anatolia, Mehmed Çelebi retook the Bursa region, including Ankara, and as a sign of his accession to the throne had coins struck dated 813. On the map, the Anatolian side of the Marmara passes from the share of Emir Süleyman to the colour of Çelebi Mehmed.
