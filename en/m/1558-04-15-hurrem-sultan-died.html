Hürrem Sultan died

15 April 1558

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Hürrem Sultan, Hürrem Sultan, who as the haseki sultan of Süleyman the Lawgiver had held great influence within the palace for years, died in Istanbul on 26 Jumada al-Akhira 965 (15 April 1558) and was buried in her tomb in the Süleymaniye Mosque complex. Hürrem Sultan, who had consolidated her authority over the harem after the death of Valide Sultan Hafsa in 1534, with her death also removed the balancing effect in the way of the rivalry for the throne between her sons Bayezid and Selim.
