Mustafa I died after fifteen years of oblivion

20 January 1639

Mustafa I, deposed for the second time on 10 September 1623, lived in a room where he was kept shut up for the fifteen years until his death; there is no information in the sources about these years. He died on 15 Ramadan 1048 (20 January 1639) — some of the sources regard this as a natural death, while others claim that Murad IV had a hand in it.
