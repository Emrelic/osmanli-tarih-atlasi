Musharrif al-Dawla, supported by the Turks, took control of Iraq; Sultan al-Dawla gave up Iraq

1021

Musharrif al-Dawla ibn Baha al-Dawla, supported by the Turks, took control of Iraq in 1021; Sultan al-Dawla was obliged to give up his rule over Iraq. With Musharrif al-Dawla's death in 1025, a struggle for the throne began again in Iraq.
