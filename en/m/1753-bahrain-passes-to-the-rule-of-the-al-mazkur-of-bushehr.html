Bahrain passes to the rule of the Al Mazkur of Bushehr

1753

The Al Mazkur family, originally from Oman, settled in Bushehr and counted among the Huwala, took Bahrain under its administration in 1753, operating from its bases in Bushehr. The family led the Arab communities on Iran's gulf coast and governed the island in Iran's name; its last representative, Nasr Al Mazkur, had been appointed to the governorship of Bahrain by the Zand administration. This is the island's last administration before the Al Khalifa.
