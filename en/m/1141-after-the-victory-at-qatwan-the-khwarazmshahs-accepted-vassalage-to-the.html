After the victory at Qatwan, the Khwarazmshahs accepted vassalage to the Qara Khitai and agreed to pay tribute

1141

After the Qara Khitai defeated Sanjar at Qatwan and became masters of all Transoxiana, the Khwarazmshahs in the same year likewise became vassals of the Qara Khitai and agreed to pay tribute (30,000 dinars a year). This vassalage lasted until 1173, when Tekish came to the throne of Khwarazm with Qara Khitai support.
