The Treaty of Jassy — The Definitive Loss of Crimea and the Turla Frontier

10 January 1792

By the treaty signed at the end of the five-year war, the Turla (Dniester) river was accepted as the frontier; the left bank of the river was left to Russia and the right bank to the Ottomans, and Özi and its surroundings were definitively lost. The Ottoman State abandoned entirely the hope of recovering Crimea and legally recognised the annexation of 1783; Wallachia and Moldavia were returned with a two-year tax exemption. With the acceptance also of the obligation of a war indemnity of twelve million kuruş, the north of the Black Sea was permanently ceded to Russia. Other places that joined on the same date: Ismail.
