The Failure of the Siege of Corfu

20 August 1716

The serasker of the Morea, Kara Mustafa Pasha, by land, and the Kaptanıderyâ Canım Hoca Mehmed Pasha, with the fleet by sea, pressed upon Corfu; the island was Venice's key base in the Adriatic. However, the extremely strong fortifications of the fortress, the reinforcements brought by the Venetian fleet, and the news of the defeat from the Danube front led to the lifting of the siege. The failure to take Corfu extinguished the mood that had risen with the reconquest of the Morea and shifted the centre of gravity of the war to Hungary.
