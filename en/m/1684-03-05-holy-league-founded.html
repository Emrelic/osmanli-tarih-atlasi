Holy League founded

5 March 1684

Under the leadership of the papacy, the Holy League (Mukaddes İttifak) against the Ottomans was formed among Austria, Poland and Venice; in 1686 Russia also joined. The empire thus entered a multi-front war that would last sixteen years.
