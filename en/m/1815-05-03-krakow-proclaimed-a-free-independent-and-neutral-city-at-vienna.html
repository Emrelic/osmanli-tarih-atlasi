Kraków proclaimed a free, independent and neutral city at Vienna

3 May 1815

At the Congress of Vienna, Russia, Austria and Prussia made the city of Kraków, together with its surroundings, a free state under the protection of the three powers; the Final Act of Vienna recorded this arrangement.
