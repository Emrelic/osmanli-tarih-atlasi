The Mürzsteg Programme: International Reform Supervision in Macedonia

2 October 1903

After the Ilinden uprising, the Russian and Austrian emperors met at Mürzsteg and prepared a new reform package for Macedonia. The programme envisaged placing two foreign civil agents beside the Ottoman inspector-general, reorganising the gendarmerie by European officers, and changing administrative boundaries on the basis of nationality. Although the Sublime Porte resisted on the grounds that its sovereign rights were being violated, it was obliged to accept in the face of the joint pressure; the programme became one of the fundamental factors that accelerated Unionist organisation among young officers.
