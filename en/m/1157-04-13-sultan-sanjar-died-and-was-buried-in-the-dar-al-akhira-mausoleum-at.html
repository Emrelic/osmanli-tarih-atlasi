Sultan Sanjar died and was buried in the Dar al-Akhira mausoleum at Merv; the Great Seljuk State came to an end

13 April 1157

Shortly after being freed from captivity in Ramadan 551 (October-November 1156), Sanjar died in Rabi I 552 (14 or 24 Rabi I 552 / 26 April or 6 May 1157) and was buried in the Dar al-Akhira mausoleum that he had had built at Merv; with his death the Great Seljuk State withdrew from the stage of history. The Khwarazm-Shah Il-Arslan came to the fore as the most powerful ruler of Eastern Iran.
