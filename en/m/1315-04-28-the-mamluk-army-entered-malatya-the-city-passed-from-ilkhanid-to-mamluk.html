The Mamluk army entered Malatya — the city passed from Ilkhanid to Mamluk administration

28 April 1315

The Mamluks dispatched an army under Sayf al-Din Tengiz, governor of Damascus, against Malatya; the army entered the city on 22 Muharram 715 (28 April 1315). Malatya had until then been under Ilkhanid rule and was the key point of the line, west of the Euphrates, where the Ilkhanid and Mamluk spheres of influence met. The change of hands of the city shows the Mamluks' advance toward the Upper Euphrates in the long contest between the two states. After this date Malatya would for a time come under the administration of the Eretnids, and from 1338 return again to Mamluk rule.
