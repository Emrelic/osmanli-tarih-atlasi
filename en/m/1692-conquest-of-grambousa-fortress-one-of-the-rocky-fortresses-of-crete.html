Conquest of Grambousa Fortress — one of the rocky fortresses of Crete remaining with Venice

1692

Although the conquest of Crete was regarded as largely complete with the surrender of Candia in 1669, the rocky island-fortress of Grambousa at the northwestern tip of the island remained in Venetian hands for twenty-three more years. With its capture in 1692, one of the three Venetian remnants in Crete was cleared; Suda and Spinalonga would await the Morea campaign of 1715.
