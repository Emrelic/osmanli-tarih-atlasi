An Lushan proclaimed himself emperor of the 'Great Yan' at Luoyang

5 February 756

An Lushan, who had taken Luoyang (the Eastern Capital) in January 756, proclaimed himself emperor of the Great Yan (Da Yan) on the first day of the new year, made Shengwu his reign title, and established the Yan court organization.
