Troy 'Proto-IV' (hypothesis) and Troy IV: the Troy IV phase begins

2059 BC

A gap of 110±20 years (2170-2060) between Troy III and IV and a possible 'Proto-IV' phase; Troy IV begins from 2060±10, IVa 2060, IVc 2010±10, end of IV at the earliest 1930±10.
