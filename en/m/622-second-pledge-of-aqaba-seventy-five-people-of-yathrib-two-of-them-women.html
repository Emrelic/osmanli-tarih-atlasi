Second Pledge of Aqaba: seventy-five people of Yathrib, two of them women, met Muhammad at Aqaba after the pilgrimage (13th year of prophethood)

622

Seventy-five Muslims of Yathrib (two of them women) came to Mecca in the pilgrimage season and met Muhammad secretly at Aqaba after the pilgrimage; when the people of Yathrib invited him to their city, the Messenger of God set out the terms of the Second Pledge of Aqaba. This meeting was the direct preparation for the Hijra.
