Sullivan's army entered the village of Kendaia; about twenty houses were burned to ashes, and crops and fruit trees were destroyed

5 September 1779

Sullivan's army reached the village of Kendaia on 5 September 1779; according to Sullivan's report the village had been abandoned. Rogers's journal says the village was destroyed, and Sullivan's official report says about twenty houses were reduced to ashes and that the army spent about a day destroying corn and fruit trees.
