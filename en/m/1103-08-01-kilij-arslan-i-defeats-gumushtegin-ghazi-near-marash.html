Kilij Arslan I defeats Gümüshtegin Ghazi near Marash

1 August 1103

Gümüshtegin's release of Bohemond for ransom and the displeasure of Kilij Arslan, who demanded a share of the ransom, set the two allies against each other; Kilij Arslan routed Gümüshtegin near Marash.
