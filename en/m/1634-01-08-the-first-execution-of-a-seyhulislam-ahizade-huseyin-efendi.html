The First Execution of a Şeyhülislam: Ahîzâde Hüseyin Efendi

8 January 1634

Şeyhülislam Ahîzâde Hüseyin Efendi was executed near Büyükçekmece, the grounds cited being that he had mediated in the military disorder and had sent the sultan a written warning concerning judicial procedure. Being the first Ottoman şeyhülislam to be executed, he broke the deep-rooted tradition that the severest punishment for members of the ilmiye was exile. The event is regarded as a symbol of the hardening authority of Murad IV and of the disruption of the balance between the state and the ulema.
