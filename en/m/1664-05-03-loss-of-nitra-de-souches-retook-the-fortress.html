Loss of Nitra — De Souches retook the fortress

3 May 1664

Nitra, which had surrendered after brief resistance following the conquest of Uyvar (Érsekújvár) in 1663, was besieged by the imperial commander De Souches on 15 April 1664; the fortress surrendered on 3 May 1664. Since the Treaty of Vasvár left captured places as they stood, Uyvar remained Ottoman while Nitra remained in Habsburg hands.
