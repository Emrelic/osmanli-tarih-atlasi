The foundation of the Sultanahmet Mosque was laid

9 August 1609

By order of Ahmed I, the foundation of the mosque, to be built by the architect Sedefkâr Mehmed Agha, was laid with a ceremony at the Hippodrome. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on 'Sultan Ahmed Mosque and Complex', the building also became the subject of contemporary criticism as the first great imperial mosque built directly from the treasury without a conquest victory. The complex was designed to include, together with the mosque, a madrasa, a soup kitchen and a tomb.
