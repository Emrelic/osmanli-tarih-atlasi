The Army of Islam of the Caucasus captured Baku

15 September 1918

The Army of Islam of the Caucasus under the command of Nuri Pasha, which had come to the aid of the Democratic Republic of Azerbaijan in accordance with the Treaty of Batum of 4 June 1918, captured Baku, which was in Russian hands. After the Armistice of Mudros the Ottoman forces withdrew from the city; the republic came to an end on 27 April 1920 with the occupation by the Red Army.
