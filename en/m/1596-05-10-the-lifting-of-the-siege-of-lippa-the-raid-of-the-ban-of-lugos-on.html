The lifting of the siege of Lippa — the raid of the ban of Lugos on Temesvár

10 May 1596

In the spring of 1596 the pasha of Temesvár besieged Lippa. In the night between 10 and 11 May, on the order of György Palatics, ban of Lugos of Transylvania, the soldiers of György Buday raided the suburbs of Temesvár and set them on fire; the fact that György Borbély fired all the cannon in the fortress also convinced the pasha that relief had arrived. On 11 May the pasha withdrew from before Lippa and hurried to protect his own seat. At this time Lugos was in the hands of the Principality of Transylvania.
