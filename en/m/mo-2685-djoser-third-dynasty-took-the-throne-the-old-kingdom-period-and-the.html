Djoser (Third Dynasty) took the throne; the Old Kingdom period and the Step Pyramid at Saqqara

2685 BC

With Djoser's (Netjerikhet) rule the Memphis-centered Old Kingdom begins; the Step Pyramid, the first great example of stone architecture, was built for him. In this period the state governs the Nile Valley and the Delta from the center; the Sinai mines and the Nubian frontier (Elephantine) are under the state's control.
