Pîrî Reis drew the oldest surviving world map

1513

Pîrî Reis drew his world map dated 919 (1513) at Gallipoli; according to the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, among the map's sources were 'old maps reaching him from the Eastern and Western worlds and the map of Christopher Columbus' — that is, it is thought to bear traces of Columbus's original navigation chart, which was considered lost at that time. He presented the map to Sultan Selim the Grim in Cairo in 923 (1517). Only one fragment, showing the western/Atlantic coasts (Spain, Portugal, West Africa, the east coast of South America), has survived to the present day; this fragment was rediscovered in the Topkapı Palace in 1929 and is on the UNESCO Memory of the World list.
