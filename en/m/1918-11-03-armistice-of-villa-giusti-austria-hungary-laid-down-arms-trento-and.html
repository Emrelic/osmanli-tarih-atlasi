Armistice of Villa Giusti — Austria-Hungary laid down arms, Trento and Trieste passed to Italy

3 November 1918

Emperor Charles's signing of an armistice with the Italians at Villa Giusti on 3 November 1918 accelerated the empire's break-up. The TDV explicitly records this day as Austria's laying down of arms: "Austria, which accepted defeat, laid down arms on 3 November, and Germany on 11 November." On the map Trento and Trieste pass from Austria to Italy on this day — the first transfer of territory visible on the map in the dissolution. By this armistice Brixen, Cortina d'Ampezzo, Gorizia and Gradisca, south of the Brenner–Toblach–Julian Alps line, passed to Italian occupation.
