Bahrain's treaty with Britain: entry into the protectorate order in the gulf

21 May 1861

According to the TDV's record, "on 21 May 1861 Britain and Ali, brother of Sheikh Muhammad, representing Bahrain, signed an agreement prohibiting the slave trade and piracy in the region"; in British documents the same treaty (the Perpetual Truce of Friendship and Peace) bears the date 31 May 1861. The treaty is the first link of the protectorate order that bound Bahrain's foreign relations to Britain, and the island remained outside this order even after the Ottomans resettled in al-Hasa and Qatif in 1871.
