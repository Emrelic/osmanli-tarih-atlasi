Eugène signed an armistice at Mantua: the Kingdom of Italy collapsed, Austria took Milan

16 April 1814

With the Armistice of Schiarino-Rizzino, Napoleon's Kingdom of Italy in effect came to an end; Lombardy and the Veneto came under Austrian control (official transfer by the Final Act of Vienna, 9 June 1815).
