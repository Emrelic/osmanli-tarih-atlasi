The Iranian Occupation of Basra

16 April 1776

Sadiq Khan, the brother of the Zand ruler Karim Khan, captured Basra after a siege lasting a year, and the Ottoman State lost its most important port on the Persian Gulf. The occupation directly shook the Indian trade route, the date and pearl revenues, and the southern defence of Iraq. The city could be retaken only in 1779, during the struggles for the throne that broke out in Iran with the death of Karim Khan.
