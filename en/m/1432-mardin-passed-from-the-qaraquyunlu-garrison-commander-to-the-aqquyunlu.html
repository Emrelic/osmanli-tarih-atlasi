Mardin passed from the Qaraquyunlu garrison commander to the Aqquyunlu Qarayuluk Osman Bey

1432

Mardin, which Qara Yusuf took in 1409, was governed by the Qaraquyunlu through governors; Emir Nasir, who had been left as garrison commander, surrendered the city in 835 (1432) to the Aqquyunlu Qarayuluk Osman Bey. Aqquyunlu rule lasted until the beginning of the sixteenth century.
