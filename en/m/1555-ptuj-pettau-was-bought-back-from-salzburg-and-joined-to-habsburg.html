Ptuj (Pettau) was bought back from Salzburg and joined to Habsburg Steiermark

1555

Ptuj, which Maximilian I had left in 1511 to the archbishop of Salzburg with a right of pledge/sale, was bought back by King Ferdinand in 1555 and attached to the duchy of Steiermark; Salzburg's seigniory on the Dravsko polje came to an end.
