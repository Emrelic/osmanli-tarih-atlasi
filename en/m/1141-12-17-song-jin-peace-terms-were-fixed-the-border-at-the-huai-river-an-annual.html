Song-Jin peace terms were fixed: the border at the Huai River, an annual 250,000 taels of silver and 250,000 bolts of silk

17 December 1141

With the reception of the Jin envoy Xiao Yi and his colleagues at the Song court, the terms of the peace oath were fixed: the border would run through the middle of the Huai River, the prefectures of Tang and Deng would be ceded to the Jin, and the Song would pay each year 250,000 taels of silver and 250,000 bolts of silk; in the same month the peace agreement was completed and its declaration written.
