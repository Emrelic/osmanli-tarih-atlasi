Opening of the Grand National Assembly and Sèvres

23 April 1920

Upon the formal occupation of Istanbul and the dissolution of the Meclis-i Meb'ûsan, the Grand National Assembly opened in Ankara on 23 April 1920; the assembly, which gathered extraordinary powers in itself, became the nucleus of the new state based on the national will. The Treaty of Sèvres signed by the Istanbul government in August partitioned Anatolia; the Grand National Assembly declared those who signed the treaty traitors to the homeland, and Sèvres was never implemented.
