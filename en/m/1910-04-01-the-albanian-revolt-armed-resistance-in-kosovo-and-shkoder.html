The Albanian Revolt: Armed Resistance in Kosovo and Shkodër

1 April 1910

The Unionist government's centralist tax policy, its campaign of collecting arms and the debate over the Latin alphabet set in revolt the Albanian tribes, who until then had been regarded as among the elements most loyal to the state. Although the army under Mahmud Şevket Pasha suppressed the revolt harshly, the unrest flared up again in 1911 and 1912. These revolts wore down the Ottoman army on the eve of the Balkan War and also accelerated the birth of the Albanian national movement.
