Rim-Sin I ascended the Larsa throne

1821 BC

Rim-Sin I (c. 1822-1763), during his 60-year reign, bound Uruk, Isin and Nippur to Larsa and made a dynasty of Elamite origin the sole master of the south. His reign ended with the conquest of Larsa by Hammurabi (1763 BC).
