The British occupied Sokoto: the Sokoto Caliphate lost its capital

15 March 1903

After Britain declared northern Nigeria a colony in 1900, its governor Lugard took the emirates one by one, then inflicted a heavy defeat on the Sokoto army and occupied the capital on 15 March 1903. With the killing of the fleeing caliph Tahir at Burmi in July, the political dominion of the caliphate ended.
