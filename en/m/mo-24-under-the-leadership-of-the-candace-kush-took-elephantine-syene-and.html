Under the leadership of the Candace, Kush took Elephantine, Syene and Philae; Augustus's statues were overthrown

24 BC

When part of the Roman troops in Egypt were withdrawn for Aelius Gallus's Arabian expedition, the Kushites entered the Thebaid; they defeated the garrison of three cohorts at Syene, took Syene, Elephantine and Philae, enslaved the inhabitants, and toppled the statues of Caesar. Strabo writes that the queen who ruled Kush at that time was a one-eyed woman with the title 'Candace'.
