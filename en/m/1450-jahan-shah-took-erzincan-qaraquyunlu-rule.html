Jahan Shah took Erzincan: Qaraquyunlu rule

1450

The Qaraquyunlu ruler Jahan Shah sent an army to Erzincan in 854 (1450) and took the city from the Aqquyunlu. This loss led to the falling out between the Aqquyunlu ruler Jahangir Mirza and his brother Hasan Beg (Uzun Hasan).
