The death of the crown prince Şehzade Mehmed — the allegation of poisoning is disputed

22 December 1756

Şehzade Mehmed, son of Ahmed III and in the position of crown prince, died, and his death opened the way to the throne for his brother Mustafa III. Two entries of TDV diverge on this death: the entry «Mustafa III» writes that the prince died suddenly and suspiciously, probably by poisoning; the entry «Osman III», however, writes that the death was from illness and treats Köse Mustafa Pasha's later becoming grand vizier and prospective son-in-law as evidence of the groundlessness of the accusation. The atlas does not choose between the two views.
