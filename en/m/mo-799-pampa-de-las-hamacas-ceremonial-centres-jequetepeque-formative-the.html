Pampa de las Hamacas ceremonial centres (Jequetepeque, Formative): the ceremonial centres are abandoned

799 BC

A series of Formative ceremonial centres in the middle Jequetepeque valley; the location of the centres shifts eastward over time. Hamacas phase 1500–1250, Tembladera 1250–800 (abandonment ~800), Lechuzas 800–550 cal BC.
