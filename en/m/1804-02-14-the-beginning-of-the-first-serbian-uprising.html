The Beginning of the First Serbian Uprising

14 February 1804

When the rebel Janissary dahis who had seized the administration in Belgrade massacred the Serbian notables en masse, an armed resistance began under the leadership of Karađorđe (Kara Yorgi). The movement, which at first remained loyal to the sultan and was directed only against the dahis, soon turned into a national rebellion demanding autonomy and independence. The Serbian forces captured Belgrade in 1806; this uprising became the first link of the nationalist independence movements in the Balkans.
