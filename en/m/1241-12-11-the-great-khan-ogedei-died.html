The Great Khan Ogedei died

11 December 1241

Ogedei Khan died on 11 December 1241 after falling into an alcohol-induced coma; his body was buried on Mount Buldaq-Qasir on the Upper Irtysh. His wife Toregene Khatun controlled the administration for about five years until the quriltai elected a new khan; Guyuk was elected khan in 1246.
