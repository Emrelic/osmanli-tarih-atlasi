End of the Artuqid dynasty: surrender of Mardin to the Qaraquyunlu

1409

The Artuqids, who had ruled in Mardin since 1106 and were the longest-lived branch of the dynasty, could not hold out in the face of Aqquyunlu pressure. Malik Ahmed, realizing that he could not defend the city, came to terms with the Qaraquyunlu ruler Qara Yusuf and surrendered Mardin to him. Artuqid dominion of over three centuries thus ended; Mardin and its surroundings were the scene of the Qaraquyunlu-Aqquyunlu struggle until its passage to the Ottomans a century later.
