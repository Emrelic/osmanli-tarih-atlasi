The death of Mehmed I and the accession of Murad II

25 June 1421

Mehmed I, who ended the Interregnum and gathered the Ottoman lands again in a single hand, died at Adrianople on 25 June 1421, and his son Murad ascended the throne at Bursa. According to the arrangement made with Byzantium before his death, his younger sons were to be sent as hostages to the emperor's side, and the emperor in turn would not release the pretender to the throne, Mustafa Çelebi, whom he held; Murad II refused to send his brothers to the emperor.
