Battle of Myriokephalon: Kilij Arslan II routed the army of Manuel Komnenos

17 September 1176

Manuel Komnenos, who had had fortifications built before Uluborlu, marched on Konya with a great army because Kilij Arslan had not halted the Turkmen raids; at the place called Myriokephalon he suffered a heavy defeat (17 September 1176). Byzantium lost its hope of recovering Anatolia; it was a turning point for the Turkification of Anatolia.
