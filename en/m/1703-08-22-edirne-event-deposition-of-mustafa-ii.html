Edirne Event — deposition of Mustafa II

22 August 1703

Anger at the sultan's residing in Edirne and at the influence of Feyzullah Efendi turned into a great uprising of soldiers and tradesmen; the şeyhülislam was lynched, the sultan was deposed and the court returned to Istanbul.
