Ceibal (Seibal, Pasion, Guatemala): Real 1 phase: first sedentary community

999 BC

In the Middle Preclassic, the first formal ceremonial complex (E-Group) together with pottery, ~950 BCE; among the earliest examples of monumental architecture in the Maya lowlands in the Pasion region. Early Middle Preclassic Real 1 (1000-850), Real 2 (850-800), Real 3 (800-700); Late Middle Preclassic Escoba 1 (700-600), 2 (600-450), 3 (450-350).
