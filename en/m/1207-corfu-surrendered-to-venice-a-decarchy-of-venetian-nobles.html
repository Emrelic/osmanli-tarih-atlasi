Corfu surrendered to Venice: a decarchy of Venetian nobles

1207

The people of Corfu, who had resisted the attack of the Venetian fleet in 1205, surrendered only in 1207; the island was governed in the name of the Republic by a decarchy (a rule of ten men) of Venetian nobles.
