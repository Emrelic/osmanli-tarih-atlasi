Mostar taken by the Yugoslav Partisans: control of the Independent State of Croatia collapsed in Herzegovina

14 February 1945

Units of the Yugoslav People's Liberation Army took Mostar in February 1945; with the capture of Drežnica on 16 February the Mostar operation was completed and most of Herzegovina fell into the hands of the Partisans.
