Psamtik I brought Upper Egypt under his control by having his daughter Nitocris adopted by the God's Wife of Amun at Thebes

655 BC

In his 9th year Psamtik I arranged for his daughter Nitocris to be sent to Thebes as the adopted daughter of Shepenwepet (God's Wife of Amun at Thebes), daughter of the Kushite king Taharqa; Shepenwepet transferred her estate to Nitocris, and the temple wealth of Thebes thus passed into the hands of the Saites. The adoption stele shows that in Psamtik's 9th year he gained full control of Thebes and that Tantamani had lost Upper Egypt by that date.
