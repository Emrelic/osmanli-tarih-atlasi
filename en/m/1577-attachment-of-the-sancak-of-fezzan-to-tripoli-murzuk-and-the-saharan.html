Attachment of the sancak of Fezzan to Tripoli: Murzuk and the Saharan caravan junction

1577

As a result of pressure from the beylerbeyilik of Tripoli, Fezzan, the caravan junction of the Sahara, accepted Ottoman allegiance, with Murzuk as its center. According to the Fezzan entry of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, the Awlad Muhammad dynasty that ruled the region was left free in its internal affairs on condition of paying regular taxes; Fezzan was organized as a sancak attached to the province of Tripoli, taken in 1551. Thus the trade in slaves, gold and camels along the Tripoli-Murzuk-Bornu route came under Ottoman control. This indirect administration would pass to the Karamanlis in 1711 and in 1842 to a direct kaza organization.
