The Republic of Florence became a duchy — Alessandro de' Medici

27 April 1532

Under pressure from Pope Clement VII, the governing commission of Florence (the Balìa) adopted a new constitution; the five-century republican regime was abolished and Alessandro de' Medici was proclaimed hereditary duke, taking power in practice on 1 May. Florence thus changed from an independent city republic into the hereditary duchy of the Medici dynasty.
