Hadrian began building a Wall on the northern frontier in Britannia

122

After visiting Britannia in 122 CE, Hadrian had a frontier wall built along the Tyne–Solway line. The Wall symbolizes Rome's defensive frontier policy; an advance was attempted with the Antonine Wall (142) but did not last.
