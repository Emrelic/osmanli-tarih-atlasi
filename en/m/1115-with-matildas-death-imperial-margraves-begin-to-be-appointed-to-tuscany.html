With Matilda's death imperial margraves begin to be appointed to Tuscany

1115

After the death of Countess Matilda of Canossa in 1115, the emperors tried to appoint a series of German margraves to Tuscany; these margraves, who remained weak because of the autonomy of the cities, were replaced after Frederick I by imperial 'delegates' and 'vicars'.
