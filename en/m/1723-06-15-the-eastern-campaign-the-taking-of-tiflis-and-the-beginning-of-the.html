The Eastern Campaign: The Taking of Tiflis and the Beginning of the Iranian War

15 June 1723

Upon the collapse of the Safavid State through the Afghan invasion and Russia's descent to the Caspian shores, the Ottoman State sent an army to the east so that the vacuum would not be left entirely to Russian influence. Tiflis was captured and Georgia was attached to Ottoman administration; the advance then continued in the direction of Ganja, Revan and Nakhchivan. This campaign, conducted behind the peaceful appearance of the Tulip Era, was the beginning of a long eastern adventure that would last thirteen years and end with the return of every place gained.
