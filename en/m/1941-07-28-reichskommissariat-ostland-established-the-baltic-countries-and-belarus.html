Reichskommissariat Ostland established — the Baltic countries and Belarus came under German civil administration

28 July 1941

By Hitler's decision of 17 July 1941, the occupied territories of the East were transferred to civil administration; Ostland, headed by Hinrich Lohse, comprised Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania and Belarus (seat at Riga). The Estonian civil administration was established on 5 December 1941; eastern Belarus remained under military administration.
