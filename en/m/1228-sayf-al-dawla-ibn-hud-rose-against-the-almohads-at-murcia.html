Sayf al-Dawla Ibn Hud rose against the Almohads at Murcia

1228

At Murcia, Muhammad b. Yusuf b. Hud of the Hudids proclaimed his emirate against the Almohads in the year 625 (1228); he brought Cordoba, Seville, Granada, Málaga and Almería under his authority.
