Topal Osman Pasha defeated Nadir at Dujum — the siege of Baghdad was lifted

19 July 1733

Against Nadir, who had besieged Baghdad in January 1733, the army sent to the aid of Baghdad under the command of Topal Osman Pasha routed Nadir's forces at Dujum near Samarra; the wounded Nadir withdrew to Hamadan, and the siege that had lasted for months was lifted. But the victory was short-lived: at the end of the same year Nadir entered Iraq again and defeated the Ottoman forces at Akderbend, north of Kirkuk; Topal Osman Pasha lost his life during these battles and was buried in Kirkuk.
