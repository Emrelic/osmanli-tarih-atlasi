The Ottoman Bank Raid: The Dashnak Militants' Action at Galata

26 August 1896

An armed group of the Armenian Dashnaktsutyun organisation, in order to draw Europe's attention to the Armenian question, raided and took hostage the headquarters of the Ottoman Bank at Galata and threatened to blow up the building. The crisis ended, through the intervention of the ambassadors, in exchange for the militants' being taken out of the country on a ship. The bloody events that took place in Istanbul in the days following the raid were decisive in the Armenian question's turning into a lever of international intervention.
