The Ministry of Foreign Affairs was established

1 June 1836

Mahmud II abolished the office of Reisülküttab, which conducted foreign affairs, and established the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in its place. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on Hariciye Nezâreti, Mehmed Akif Pasha was appointed the first minister of the new ministry, and the organisation began operation in the mansion of Rıfat Bey, the ambassador to Vienna. This arrangement was the beginning of the conduct of Ottoman foreign policy on the basis of a modern ministry.
