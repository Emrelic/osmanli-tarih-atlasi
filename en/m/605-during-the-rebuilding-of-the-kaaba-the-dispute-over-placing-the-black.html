During the rebuilding of the Kaaba, the dispute over placing the Black Stone was settled by Muhammad's arbitration

605

While the Quraysh were rebuilding the Kaaba, the dispute over who should put the Black Stone in its place grew so bitter that it neared war; the proposal to abide by the decision of the first person to enter the Kaaba was adopted, and that person, Muhammad, had the tribal chiefs lift the stone together on a cloak and set it in its place with his own hands.
