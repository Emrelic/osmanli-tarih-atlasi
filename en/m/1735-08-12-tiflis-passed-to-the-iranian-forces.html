Tiflis passed to the Iranian forces

12 August 1735

Following Nadir's growth in strength, the Iranian forces retook Tiflis and brought Ali Mirza to the governorship of Kartli-Kakheti. Nadir changed the Ottoman administrative arrangement there; he governed Kartli directly with an Iranian governor, and gave it to Teimuraz in 1744.
