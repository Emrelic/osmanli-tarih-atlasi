Battle of Benevento: Charles of Anjou defeats and kills Manfred; the kingdom passes to Anjou

26 February 1266

On 26 February 1266 at Benevento, Charles, count of Anjou, routed Manfred's army; Manfred died on the battlefield. The Kingdom of Sicily passed from the Hohenstaufen to the House of Anjou.
