By his Italian campaigns Pippin III takes the Exarchate of Ravenna from the Lombards and donates it to the pope; the Papal State is born

756

At the call of Pope Stephen II, Pippin III made two campaigns to Italy, in 754 and 756; by donating to the pope the Exarchate of Ravenna that he took from the Lombard king Aistulf, he became the creator of the papacy's temporal state. Pippin gave the pope the title 'Patrician of the Romans'.
