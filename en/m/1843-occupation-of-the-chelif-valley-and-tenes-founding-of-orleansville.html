Occupation of the Chelif valley and Tenes: founding of Orléansville

1843

In order to pursue Abd al-Qadir, the French founded in April 1843 a new city named Orléansville in the middle of the Chelif valley; in the same year the coastal port of Tenes was also occupied. Thus the interior corridor between Algiers and Oran came under French control and the resistance in the Dahra mountains was encircled. On 16 May of the same year Abd al-Qadir's nomadic camp, the 'smala,' was found by chance and dispersed.
