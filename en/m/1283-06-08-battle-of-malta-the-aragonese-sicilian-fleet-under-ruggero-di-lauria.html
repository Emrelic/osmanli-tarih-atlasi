Battle of Malta: the Aragonese-Sicilian fleet under Ruggero di Lauria defeated the Angevin fleet; Malta passed under Aragonese rule

8 June 1283

The Aragonese-Sicilian fleet under Ruggero di Lauria defeated the Angevin (Neapolitan) fleet under Guillaume Cornut off Malta; the island of Malta passed from Angevin to Aragonese-Sicilian rule. Other settlements lost on the same date: Mdina (Città Notabile), Gozo (Cittadella, Rabat).
