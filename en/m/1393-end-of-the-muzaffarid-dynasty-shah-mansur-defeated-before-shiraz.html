End of the Muzaffarid dynasty: Shah Mansur defeated before Shiraz

1393

Shah Mansur, the last powerful ruler of the Muzaffarids, who had ruled in Yazd, Fars, Isfahan and Kerman since 1318, united with his relatives and moved against Timur. In the battle near Shiraz, when one of his emirs went over to the other side, he lost most of his forces and was defeated and killed. When Timur afterwards had all the members of the dynasty killed as well, Muzaffarid dominion came to an end and Fars and Kerman were joined to Timurid rule.
