Sultan Malik-Shah comes to the Caucasus with a great army and brings the region wholly into submission

1086

After King Giorgi II went to Isfahan and accepted Malik-Shah's suzerainty, Malik-Shah came to the Caucasus once more in 1086 with a great army and brought the region wholly into submission; Eastern Georgia had been given the name 'Didi Turkoba' (Great Turkish Time).
