Recapture of Dongola: beginning of the Nile campaign

23 September 1896

After the Italians were defeated at Adwa, Britain marched the Egyptian army south along the Nile under Kitchener's command. As the railway advanced through the desert the supply problem was solved, and the province of Dongola was recaptured on 23 September 1896. Eleven years of Mahdist rule ended here; the campaign would continue for two more years and reach Omdurman.
