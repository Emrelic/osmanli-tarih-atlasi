Sellasia: Antigonus Doson defeated Cleomenes; Sparta suffered foreign occupation for the first time

221 BC

In the summer of 222 at Sellasia the Macedonian-Achaean alliance defeated the Spartan army of Cleomenes; Cleomenes fled to Egypt. Doson entered Sparta and restored the Lycurgan order; the Spartans for the first time in their history experienced occupation by a foreign army.
