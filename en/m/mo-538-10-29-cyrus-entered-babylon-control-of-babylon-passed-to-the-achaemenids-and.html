Cyrus entered Babylon; control of Babylon passed to the Achaemenids and Gobryas was appointed governor

29 October 538 BC

Cyrus entered Babylon on 3 Arahsamnu; he greeted the people of the city and made Gubaru (Gobryas) governor of the city. The Akkadian divine images that Nabonidus had gathered in the city were returned to their places in Kislîmu-Addaru. His son Cambyses took part in the rite of Nabû in his capacity as crown prince.
