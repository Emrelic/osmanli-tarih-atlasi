Fall of Jizzakh to Russia — the last fortress of the Emirate of Bukhara on the Syr Darya line

18 October 1866

During Russia's advance in Central Asia, after General Chernyayev's unsuccessful attempt of February 1866, Russian forces under General Dmitri Romanovsky, appointed in his place, captured Jizzakh, the Emirate of Bukhara's last great fortress in the Syr Darya valley, in October 1866 after a seven-day siege and a fierce assault; this fall effectively ended Bukharan resistance in the region and opened the road to Samarkand for the Russian army.
