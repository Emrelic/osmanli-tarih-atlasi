The Kar-Kiya dynasty founded in Gilan: the last independent rulers of the Caspian coast

1371

Ali Kiya, returning to Gilan with his family, established Kar-Kiya rule centered on Lahijan in the year 773 (1371-72); the dynasty held the southwestern coast of the Caspian for more than two centuries and lasted until the annexation of the region by the Safavid Shah Abbas I in 1592.
