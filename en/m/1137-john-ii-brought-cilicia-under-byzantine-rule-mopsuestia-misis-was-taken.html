John II brought Cilicia under Byzantine rule: Mopsuestia (Misis) was taken

1137

With his campaign of 1137-1138, John II Komnenos brought under obedience the Cilician plain and the Taurus regions held by the Armenian Rubenid principality; Byzantine superiority over Antioch was also partly secured.
