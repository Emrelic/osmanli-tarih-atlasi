Wang Jian became emperor at Chengdu: the Great Shu (Former Shu)

3 November 907

Wang Jian, Prince of Shu, after Zhu Wen overthrew the Tang, called for the revival of the Tang, but when no one responded he discussed the proclamation of an empire with his generals, mourned for the Tang for three days with the people, and in the 9th month became emperor and named the state 'Great Shu'.
