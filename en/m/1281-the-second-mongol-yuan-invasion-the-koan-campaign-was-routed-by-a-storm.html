The second Mongol (Yuan) invasion (the Koan campaign) was routed by a storm off Kyushu

1281

The second great invasion fleet of 1281 was also largely destroyed by a storm off the coast of Kyushu; the two failed invasions accelerated the financial collapse of the Kamakura bakufu.
