Choe Tan's northwestern revolt: the regent of Seogyeong (Pyongyang) and the governors of five districts were killed, and the region moved toward being handed over to the Mongols

29 October 1269

Choe Tan, a clerk of the northwestern regional command, and his companions, using Lim Yeon's deposition of the king as a pretext, rose up and killed the regent of Seogyeong and the governors of the districts of Yong, Yeong, Cheol, Seon and Ja; Choe Tan told the Mongol envoy that this was against 'Goryeo's preparations to withdraw to the islands'. The region was later attached to the Mongols and administered under the name Dongnyeong-bu (1270).
