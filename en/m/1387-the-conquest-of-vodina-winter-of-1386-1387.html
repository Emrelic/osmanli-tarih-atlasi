The conquest of Vodina (winter of 1386-1387)

1387

Vodina in Macedonia, west of the Vardar, came under Ottoman administration through a conquest for which the Ottoman chronicles give different years. Hoca Sâdeddin places the conquest in 1386; TDV finds it more appropriate, with strong probability, to accept the winter of 1386-1387. Since the commander who made the conquest is not expressly named in the source, no person has been written.
