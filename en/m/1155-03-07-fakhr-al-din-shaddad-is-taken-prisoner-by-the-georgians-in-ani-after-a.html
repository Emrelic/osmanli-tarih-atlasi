Fakhr al-Din Shaddad is taken prisoner by the Georgians; in Ani, after a revolt of Armenian nuns, his brother Fadl V is installed in his place

7 March 1155

After the Georgian King Demetrius defeated the Saltukid army, Armenian nuns, reacting to Fakhr al-Din Shaddad's conduct, rose in revolt and installed his brother Fadl V at Ani in place of Saltuk (550/1155); Fadl V's emirate ended in 1161 with the surrender of Ani to the Georgian king.
