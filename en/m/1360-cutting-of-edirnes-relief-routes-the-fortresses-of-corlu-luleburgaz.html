Cutting of Edirne's relief routes: the fortresses of Çorlu, Lüleburgaz, Babaeski and Keşan

1360

Prince Murad, who resumed the campaign of conquest after the rescue of Halil, sent the raiders (akıncı) as far as the approaches to Constantinople in order to isolate Edirne from help that might come from outside. The fortresses of Çorlu, Lüleburgaz and Babaeski on the Constantinople-Edirne road, and Keşan at the road junction of Southern Thrace, were seized. The surrender of Edirne came after this ring of siege had been completed.
