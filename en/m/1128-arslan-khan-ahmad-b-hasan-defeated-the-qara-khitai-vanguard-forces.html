Arslan Khan Ahmad b. Hasan defeated the Qara Khitai vanguard forces entering the Balasagun–Khotan–Kashgar region

1128

When the Qara Khitai army under Yeh-lü Ta-shih first entered the region of Balasagun, Khotan and Kashgar in 1128, the Eastern Qarakhanid ruler Arslan Khan Ahmad b. Hasan defeated them and halted their advance for a time.
