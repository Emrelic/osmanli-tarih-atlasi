Strymon and Mesta valleys: annexation of Kyustendil, Petrich, Nevrokop and Drama

1374

The Strymon and Mesta valleys, held by the Serbian and Bulgarian local lords who had begun to pay tribute to the Ottomans after the victory of Chirmen, were taken under direct Ottoman administration through the advance of the frontier lords. With the annexation of Kyustendil, Petrich, Nevrokop and Drama, the eastern half of Macedonia was opened, and Thessalonica and Serres came within the ring of siege. The expansion of Rumelia toward the south-west on the map is the result of this operation.
