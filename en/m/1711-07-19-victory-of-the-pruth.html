Victory of the Pruth

19 July 1711

In the war that arose over the matter of the Swedish king who took refuge with the Ottomans after the defeat at Poltava, the army of Tsar Peter I was surrounded on the bank of the Pruth and was forced to surrender. With the treaty signed, Azak was recovered; the decision not to annihilate the army and to release the tsar went down in history as a decision debated from that day to this.
