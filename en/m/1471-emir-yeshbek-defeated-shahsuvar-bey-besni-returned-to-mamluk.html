Emir Yeshbek defeated Shahsuvar Bey; Besni returned to Mamluk administration (approximate)

1471

In the spring of 1471 Emir Yeshbek defeated Shahsuvar Bey on the Savron stream; the Mamluks retook Kadirli and Kozan. Shahsuvar was executed in Cairo in 1472, and the Mamluks reinstated Shah Budak as bey. With this return, Besni also came under Mamluk administration (TDV besni: 'Toward the end of the fifteenth century it again fell into the hands of the Mamluks').
