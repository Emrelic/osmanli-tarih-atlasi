Treaty of Brest-Litovsk: Kars, Ardahan and Batum regained

3 March 1918

The treaty signed between the Central Powers and Russia, which had withdrawn from the war after the Bolshevik Revolution, closed the Eastern Front and returned to the Ottoman State Kars, Ardahan and Batum (Elviye-i Selâse, the three sanjaks), which it had lost by the 1878 Treaty of Berlin. After the Armistice of Mudros, however, the region was to be lost again, and the final settlement came with the 1921 Treaty of Kars.
