Toghril Beg died in Rayy; Alp Arslan succeeded him

4 September 1063

Toghril Beg, who had no children, died in Rayy on 8 Ramadan 455 (4 September 1063); the vizier al-Kunduri proclaimed the heir Sulayman ruler, but Alp Arslan was proclaimed sultan in Qazvin, defeated Qutalmish near Damghan, took the throne in Rayy, and made Nizam al-Mulk his vizier.
