Chengdu: Shu Han → Cao Wei

263

At the points Chengdu, ownership passes from Shu Han to Cao Wei. Transition source information: Sanguozhi 28: Deng Ai entered Chengdu, Liu Shan surrendered — Chengdu was de facto under Wei command (later the Zhong Hui/Deng Ai affair). The end limit (Western Jin 265, 'Sima Yan') was not read.
