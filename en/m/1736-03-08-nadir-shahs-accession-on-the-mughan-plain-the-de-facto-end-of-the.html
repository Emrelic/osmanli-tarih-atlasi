Nadir Shah's accession on the Mughan Plain: the de facto end of the Safavid dynasty

8 March 1736

According to the Nadir Shah entry of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, the assembly of about twenty thousand people gathered on the Mughan Plain insistently asked him to take charge of Iran; Nadir accepted this offer on the condition that the extreme Shiite doctrine that had caused the wars between the Safavids and the Ottomans be abandoned and the moderate Ja'fari school adopted, and he ascended the throne on 24 Shawwal 1148 (8 March 1736) with the title of Nadir Shah. This meant the de facto end of the two-century-old Safavid dynasty and the founding of the Afsharid dynasty.
