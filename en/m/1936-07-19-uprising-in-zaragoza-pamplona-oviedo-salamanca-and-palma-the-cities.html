Uprising in Zaragoza, Pamplona, Oviedo, Salamanca and Palma: the cities fell to the Nationalists

19 July 1936

The military uprising broke out on 19 July 1936 in Zaragoza, Pamplona, Vitoria, Oviedo, Salamanca and Palma (Majorca). The Gran Enciclopèdia Catalana counts these cities among the uprisings of 19 July. Majorca (Palma) passes to Nationalist rule from this date.
