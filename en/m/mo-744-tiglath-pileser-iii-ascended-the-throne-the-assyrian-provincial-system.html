Tiglath-Pileser III ascended the throne; the Assyrian provincial system was established

744 BC

Tiglath-Pileser III, who came to the throne through a revolt in Kalhu, established the provincial system by putting Assyrian governors in place of vassal kingdoms; he bound Babylon through a personal union and launched campaigns against Damascus, Gaza and Israel. The administrative order used by the later empires (Babylonian, Persian, Seleucid) derives from this.
