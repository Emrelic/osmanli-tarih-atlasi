Battle of Geok Tepe — the Akhal Teke Turkmen were defeated by Russia

24 January 1881

Russian forces under General Mikhail Skobelev broke the resistance of the Akhal Teke Turkmen by taking the fortress of Geok Tepe on 24 January 1881; the Trans-Caspian Turkmen region, including the Dehistan plain, Abiverd and Nisa, entered the process of joining the Russian Empire (the Transcaspian Region was established in 1881). With this event Kizil Arvat and Ashgabat (Akhal Turkmen lands) passed to Russia.
