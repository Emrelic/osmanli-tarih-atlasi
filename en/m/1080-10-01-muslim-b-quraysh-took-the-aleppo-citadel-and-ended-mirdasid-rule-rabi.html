Muslim b. Quraysh took the Aleppo citadel and ended Mirdasid rule (Rabi' II 473)

1 October 1080

The Uqaylid Muslim b. Quraysh entered the city of Aleppo on 26 Dhu'l-Hijja 472 (18 June 1080); in Rabi' II 473 (October 1080) he took the citadel and ended Mirdasid rule in Aleppo.
