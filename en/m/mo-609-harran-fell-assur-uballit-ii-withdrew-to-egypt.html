Harran fell; Aššur-uballit II withdrew to Egypt

609 BC

After the fall of Nineveh the last king of Assyria, Aššur-uballit II, had withdrawn to Harran; when the Medes and Babylonians marched on Harran, the Assyrian and Egyptian forces abandoned the city and withdrew beyond the Euphrates. Nabopolassar took Harran and plundered it.
