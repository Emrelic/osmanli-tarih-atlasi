The League of Corinth was founded: the Greek cities were united under the hegemony of Philip II

337 BC

After Chaeronea, Philip gathered the Greek cities at Corinth under a common peace (koine eirene) and a common defence pact; troops and the command of a hegemon were decided for the campaign against Persia. Only Sparta did not join.
