Klimonas and Shillourokambos (Cyprus, Early Aceramic Neolithic): the first sedentary villages on Cyprus (Klimonas)

9149 BC

The villagers established at Klimonas lived 11,100-10,600 years ago (highest probability 10.8-10.6 cal kyBP). Shillourokambos Early A 10.4-10.0, Early B-C 10.0-9.6, Middle A ~9.5 cal kyBP (caption of Fig. 3, comparison).
