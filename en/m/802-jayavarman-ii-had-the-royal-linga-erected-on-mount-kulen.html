Jayavarman II had the royal linga erected on Mount Kulen (Mahendraparvata) and freed the Khmer kingdom from Javanese domination; the Angkor period began

802

According to the Sdok Kak Thom stele, the Brahman Hiranyadama performed the devaraja ritual so that the land of Kambuja would never again be dependent on Java and would have a single chakravartin ruler. Coedès dates this consecration to 802; the kingdom, moving among Hariharalaya (Roluos), Amarendrapura and Mahendraparvata (Phnom Kulen), was united around Angkor.
