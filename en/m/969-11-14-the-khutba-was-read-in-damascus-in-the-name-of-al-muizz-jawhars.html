The khutba was read in Damascus in the name of al-Mu'izz; Jawhar's commander Ja'far b. Falah took Syria

14 November 969

After the conquest of Egypt, the Fatimid caliph al-Mu'izz li-Din Allah sent Ja'far b. Falah to Syria; in Muharram 359 the khutba was read in Damascus in the name of the Fatimid caliph.
