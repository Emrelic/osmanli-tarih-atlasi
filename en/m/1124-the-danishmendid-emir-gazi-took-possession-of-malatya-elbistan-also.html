The Danishmendid Emir Gazi took possession of Malatya; Elbistan also passed to the Danishmendids

1124

After Belek's death, Emir Gazi took Malatya from the Seljuk malik Tuğrul Arslan after a long siege; with the seizure of the Malatya region, Elbistan also came under Danishmendid administration.
