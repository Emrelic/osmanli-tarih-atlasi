The capture of Hunza — the defeat of the zaque Quemuenchatocha

1 August 1537

Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada, who had climbed from the Magdalena River to the plateau, overran Hunza, the centre of the zacazgo, one of the two heads of the Muisca Confederation, and took the zaque Quemuenchatocha prisoner. The gold seized in the town was the real booty that fed the rumour of El Dorado; the northern half of the land of the Muisca thus came under Spanish rule.
