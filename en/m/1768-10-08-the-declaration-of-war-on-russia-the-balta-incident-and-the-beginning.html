The Declaration of War on Russia — The Balta Incident and the Beginning of the War of 1768-1774

8 October 1768

When Russian and Cossack units pursuing the Bar Confederation in Poland raided and burned the town of Balta on the Ottoman frontier, the Divan took the decision for war, and the Russian envoy was imprisoned in Yedikule. The war, entered without serious military preparation and declared six months in advance, went badly from the very start. This struggle, which would last six years, would turn, with the raid on Çeşme, the defeat at Kartal and the occupation of Crimea, into one of the heaviest series of defeats in Ottoman history.
