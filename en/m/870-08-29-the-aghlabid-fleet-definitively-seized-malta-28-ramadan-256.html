The Aghlabid fleet definitively seized Malta (28 Ramadan 256)

29 August 870

The fleet under the command of Ahmad b. Umar, one of the Aghlabid commanders in Sicily, definitively seized Malta (28 Ramadan 256 / 29 August 870); the island had been under Byzantine (Eastern Roman) rule since 533, and after this date Muslim rule became permanent.
