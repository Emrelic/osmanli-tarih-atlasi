Revolt of Peter Delyan: many fortresses, including Skopje, fell into the hands of the rebels

1040

The discontent that grew when taxes were changed from payment in kind to money turned into a revolt under the leadership of Peter Delyan, a grandson of Samuel. The rebels advanced south from Belgrade and, with the support of the local boyars, took many fortresses, including Skopje.
