The Sultanate of Sennar (Funj) surrendered

14 June 1821

The Funj dynasty of three centuries surrendered without fighting. The camp established in the same year at the point where the two branches of the Nile meet soon turned into Khartoum, the administrative centre of Sudan. Other places joined on the same date: Sennar.
