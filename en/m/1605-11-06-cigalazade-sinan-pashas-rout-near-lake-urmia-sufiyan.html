Cigalazade Sinan Pasha's rout near Lake Urmia (Sufiyan)

6 November 1605

The position of Sinan Pasha, who marched in the 1605 campaign season to retake Tabriz, was shaken when the beylerbeyi of Erzurum, Köse Sefer Pasha, moved separately from the main army and was taken prisoner. Shah Abbas's sudden attack inflicted a heavy defeat on the army around Lake Urmia; Sinan Pasha withdrew first to Van, then to Diyarbekir. His having the beylerbeyi of Aleppo, Canbolatoğlu Hüseyin Pasha, executed for being late with aid kindled a great revolt in Anatolia; after Sinan Pasha's death Shah Abbas took Ganja, Shirvan and Shamakhi.
