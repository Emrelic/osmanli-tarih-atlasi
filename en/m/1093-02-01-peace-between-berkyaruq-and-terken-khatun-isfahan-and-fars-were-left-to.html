Peace between Berkyaruq and Terken Khatun: Isfahan and Fars were left to Terken Khatun and her son Mahmud

1 February 1093

Through the efforts of Berkyaruq's supporters Terken Khatun was forced to make peace: the provinces of Isfahan and Fars were left to Terken Khatun and Mahmud, and Berkyaruq was recognised as sultan in the other provinces (486/1093).
