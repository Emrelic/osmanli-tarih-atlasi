King William II dies childless; the succession crisis begins

1189

William II, who had no children, died in 1189 at the age of only 36; his uncle's daughter Constance (wife of Henry VI of Hohenstaufen) had been designated as his successor, but the nobles elected Tancred, count of Lecce.
