The Bahrain islands pass to the Jabrids

1417

The TDV entry on Bahrain notes that the island "remained successively under the rule of the Uyunids, Salghurids, Tabids and Jabrids, and during the Jabrid period the majority passed from Shiism to Sunnism," giving no date for any of them. In the general literature the Jabrid dynasty was founded at the beginning of the fifteenth century by overthrowing the last Jarwanid ruler in Qatif, and at its zenith (Ajwad b. Zamil, d. 1496) it controlled the entire Arab coast of the Basra gulf, al-Hasa, Qatif and the Bahrain islands. After Ajwad the Awal islands passed to Muqrin b. Zamil — he is the ruler who would be defeated by the Portuguese in 1521.
