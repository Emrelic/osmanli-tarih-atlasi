Ak-Mechet Fortress taken by Russian forces — the Syr Darya line of the Khanate of Kokand collapsed

28 July 1853

Russian forces under General Vasily Perovsky took the fortress of Ak-Mechet, belonging to the Khanate of Kokand on the bank of the Syr Darya, in July 1853 after a long siege; the fortress later became, under the name Perovsk, an outpost of Russian Turkestan.
