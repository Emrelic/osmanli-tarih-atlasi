Treaty of Verdun: the Carolingian Empire is divided into three (West Francia, Middle Francia, East Francia)

10 August 843

By the treaty reached at Verdun in August 843, the sons of Louis the Pious divided the empire: Charles the Bald took the west of the Meuse-Saône-Rhône line (West Francia, including the Spanish March), Louis the German the east of the Rhine (Bavaria, Thuringia, Franconia, Saxony; also the area around Speyer-Worms-Mainz), and Lothair took Italy, the imperial title, and the strip in the Rhine-Rhône valleys.
