Treaty of Küçük Kaynarca

21 July 1774

With Küçük Kaynarca, which ended the war of 1768–1774 and is regarded as one of the heaviest treaties in Ottoman history, 'independence' was granted to the Crimean Khanate — in effect a Russian sphere of influence was created. Russia established itself on the Black Sea coast, gained for its merchant ships the right of passage through the Straits and a permanent embassy in Istanbul; the ambiguous clause of protection over Orthodox subjects opened the door to the pretexts for intervention of the following century. For the first time a land whose people were Muslim was being removed from Ottoman dominion.
