The occupation of Bucharest by the Austrian army (Coburg)

1 November 1789

After the defeat at Rymnik, the Austrian army occupied Bucharest, the center of Wallachia, at the beginning of November 1789. The city was returned to the Ottoman side by the Treaty of Sistova on 4 August 1791.
