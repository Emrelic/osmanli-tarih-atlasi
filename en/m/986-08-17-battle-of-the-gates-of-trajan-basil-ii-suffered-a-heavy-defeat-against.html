Battle of the Gates of Trajan: Basil II suffered a heavy defeat against the Bulgarians

17 August 986

Basil II's march on Serdica (Sofia), because it was badly prepared and his commanders were at odds, ended in a heavy defeat at the Gates of Trajan pass on 17 August 986; the emperor barely escaped.
