Xaghra Brochtorff Circle (Gozo, Malta): last burials

2419 BC

A cave/tomb complex at Xaghra on Gozo which began in the Ġgantija period with a small rock-cut tomb and then became monumental with collective burial with Tarxien material and altar-like stone arrangements.
