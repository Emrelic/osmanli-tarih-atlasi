Çimpe Fortress — the crossing into Rumelia

1 March 1352

Süleyman Pasha, son of Orhan Gazi, had taken the fortress of Çimpe, which had been given in the Byzantine civil war, as a base in 1352; after the great earthquake that ruined Gallipoli in March 1354, the abandoned fortresses were swiftly fortified and the peninsula was transformed into a permanent Ottoman bridgehead. This step, supported by the settlement of Turkmen population brought from Anatolia, is the beginning of the Ottoman presence on the European continent, which was to last over 550 years.
