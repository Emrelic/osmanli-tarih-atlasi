The devshirme system begins to be applied in practice

1 June 1421

The devshirme method, not seen in earlier Islamic states, began first in the form of making use of non-Muslim youths from among war captives under the pencik law. According to the TDV, "it is understood that it was applied in the time of Çelebi Mehmed but became law in the reign of his son Murad II."
