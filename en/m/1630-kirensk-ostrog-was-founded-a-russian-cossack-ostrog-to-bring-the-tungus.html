Kirensk ostrog was founded: a Russian Cossack ostrog to bring the Tungus under yasak

1630

In 1630 Russian Cossacks founded the Kirensk ostrog on the Lena (the same year as Ilimsk, with Bratsk in 1631); the ostrog was founded to subject the Tungus.
