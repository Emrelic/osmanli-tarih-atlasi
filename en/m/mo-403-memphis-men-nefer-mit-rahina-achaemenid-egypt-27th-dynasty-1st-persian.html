Memphis (Men-nefer / Mit Rahina): Achaemenid Egypt (27th Dynasty, 1st Persian rule) → ownerless interval

403 BC

At the points Memphis (Men-nefer / Mit Rahina), ownership passes from Achaemenid Egypt (27th Dynasty, 1st Persian rule) to the ownerless interval. Transition source information: Battle of Pelusium 525; Cambyses was proclaimed king of Egypt at the end of the month; the 1st Persian rule ended in ~402; "Egypt was governed from Memphis by a satrap" [Bresciani, Iranica Egypt i; Dandamayev, Iranica Cambyses]
