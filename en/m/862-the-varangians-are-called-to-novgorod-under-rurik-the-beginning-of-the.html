The Varangians are called to Novgorod under Rurik: the beginning of the Rurikid dynasty

862

The Slavic and Finnic tribes expelled the Varangians (Rus) in 859, but when they fell into quarrels among themselves they called them back three years later (862); the brothers Rurik, Sineus and Truvor accepted the invitation. Rurik's capital was Novgorod, on the banks of the Volkhov; the dynasty of the Rus princes descends from here.
