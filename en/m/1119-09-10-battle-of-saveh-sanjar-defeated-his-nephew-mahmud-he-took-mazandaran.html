Battle of Saveh: Sanjar defeated his nephew Mahmud; he took Mazandaran, Tabaristan, Qumis, Damghan and Rayy

10 September 1119

The Battle of Saveh between Sanjar and his nephew, the Iraq Seljuk sultan Mahmud, ended in Mahmud's defeat (2 Jumada al-Akhira 513 / 10 September 1119); Sanjar added Mazandaran, Tabaristan, Qumis, Damghan and Rayy to his own country, assigned Saveh, Abeh, Qazvin, Zanjan and Abhar to Mahmud's brother Tughril, and half of Fars-Khuzestan-Isfahan to Saljuq. The caliph began to have the khutba read in Sanjar's name.
