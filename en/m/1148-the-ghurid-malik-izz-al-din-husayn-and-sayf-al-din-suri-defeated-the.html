The Ghurid Malik Izz al-Din Husayn and Sayf al-Din Suri defeated the Ghaznavid sultan Bahram Shah and seized Ghazna

1148

The Ghurid emirs, who were under Ghaznavid suzerainty, grew strong and attacked Ghaznavid territory; Malik Izz al-Din Husayn and his successor Sayf al-Din Suri defeated the Sultan of Ghazna, Bahram Shah, and took the title «sultan» (1148); after Sayf al-Din Suri seized the throne of Ghazna, his brother Baha al-Din Sam moved the capital to Firuzkuh.
