The leaving of Parga to Ottoman administration

10 May 1819

Parga had been in Venetian hands since 1401 and had never come under Ottoman administration; after the destruction of Venice, it passed in turn to France and Britain. The TDV article on Ali Pasha of Tepelena summarises the situation thus: the city surrendered to the British and was abandoned to Ali Pasha's administration only in 1819. The transfer resulted in the migration of most of its people to the Ionian islands. Thus the last of the Preveza-Parga-Vonitsa trio left with Venice at Passarowitz was also added to the empire; Parga would remain Ottoman for ninety-four years.
