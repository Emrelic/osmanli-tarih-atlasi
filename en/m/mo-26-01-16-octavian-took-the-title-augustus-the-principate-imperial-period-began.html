Octavian took the title "Augustus": the Principate (imperial period) began

16 January 26 BC

On 16 January 27 the Senate gave Octavian the title Augustus; Octavian received the command (imperium) of most of the provinces for a long term. This date is counted as the beginning of the Roman Empire.
