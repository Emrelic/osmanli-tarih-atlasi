King Robert II annexed the Duchy of Burgundy to the French crown after a war of thirteen years

1015

After the death of Duke Henry in 1002 a war broke out over the Duchy of Burgundy; despite the resistance of Otto-William, Count of Burgundy, Robert II, King of France, annexed the duchy to the kingdom in 1015 at the end of a war lasting thirteen years and gave it to his son Henry (later Henry I).
