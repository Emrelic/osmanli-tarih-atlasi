The taking of Çuha Island (Kythira)

7 September 1715

Çuha Island at the southern tip of the Morea was taken over by the fleet following the completion of the peninsula. The island, which overlooks the passage between the Ionian Sea and the Aegean, was the forward outpost Venice used to control these waters. However, it did not remain the only island held by the Ottomans: three years later it was returned to Venice at Passarowitz.
