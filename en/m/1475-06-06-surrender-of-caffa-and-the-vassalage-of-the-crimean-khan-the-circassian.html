Surrender of Caffa and the vassalage of the Crimean khan — the Circassian coast (Sochi, Tuapse, Maykop) was attached to the Crimean Khanate as a loose protectorate

6 June 1475

After a four-day siege Caffa surrendered to Ahmed Pasha, and Mengli Giray accepted the vassalage of the Ottoman sultan. The area around Sochi, Tuapse and Maykop, homeland of the Circassian tribes who recognized the Crimean khans as suzerains, is shown in the atlas from this date as attached to the Crimean Khanate as a loose protectorate.
