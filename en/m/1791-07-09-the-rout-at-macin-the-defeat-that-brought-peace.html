The rout at Măcin — the defeat that brought peace

9 July 1791

The Ottoman army suffered a general rout at Măcin and fell into great disarray. This defeat, coming when the armistice with Austria had long since been signed, made the decision to sit down at the table with Russia also unavoidable: an armistice was signed at Galați a month later.
