Louis XI took Artois (Arras) from the Burgundian inheritance

1477

With the death of Charles the Bold at Nancy (5 January 1477), King Louis XI of France occupied Artois; Arras resisted and was taken by storm, and its walls were demolished. The Treaty of Arras (23 December 1482) left Artois in France as the dowry of Marguerite; by the Treaty of Senlis of 1493 it returned to the Habsburgs.
