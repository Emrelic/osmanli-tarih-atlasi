The founding of Sloboda Ukraine — Sumy and the belt of steppe settlement

1652

Peasants and Cossacks from the Bila Tserkva regiment in Right-Bank Ukraine, under Colonel Herasym Kondratiev, founded a new town on the Psel; four years later the Muscovite voivode fortified the fortress. This wave of migration is the beginning of the Sloboda Ukraine order, which opened the empty steppe on Muscovy's southern border to settlement: tax exemption was granted to those who came, and border watch duty was expected in return. From 1658 to 1765 Sumy was the seat of the regiment bearing its own name.
