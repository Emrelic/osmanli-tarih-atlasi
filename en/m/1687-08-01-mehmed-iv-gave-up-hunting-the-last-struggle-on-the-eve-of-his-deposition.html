Mehmed IV gave up hunting — the last struggle on the eve of his deposition

1 August 1687

After the Mohács defeat (August 1687), the army, whose pay had not been disbursed, rebelled and marched on Istanbul. Mehmed IV had Süleyman Pasha, who had fled with the seal of the grand vizierate, killed and showed his head to the soldiers, and announced that he had repented by dispersing his hunting equipment and greyhounds — but this gesture was not enough to save his throne, and he was deposed a few months later.
