Darius III ascended the Persian throne (murder of Arses)

335 BC

The court official Bagoas killed Artaxerxes IV; Artašata, the satrap of Armenia, became king as Darius III (Codomannus). In October 336 Philip was assassinated in Macedonia and Alexander came to the throne.
