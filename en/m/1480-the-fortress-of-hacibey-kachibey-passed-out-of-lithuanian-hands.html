The fortress of Hacıbey (Kachibey) passed out of Lithuanian hands

1480

The port of Kachibey on the northwestern coast of the Black Sea had been fortified at the beginning of the fifteenth century by the Lithuanian grand duke Vytautas. In 1480 the fortress passed out of Lithuanian hands and took the name Hacıbey. At the end of the fifteenth century the coast came under the control of the Crimean Khanate, subject to the Ottomans; the region's direct attachment to the Ottoman administrative organization dates from 1538.
