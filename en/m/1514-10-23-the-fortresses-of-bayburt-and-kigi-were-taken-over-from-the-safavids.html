The fortresses of Bayburt and Kiği were taken over from the Safavids — Erzincan and Bayburt were given to Bıyıklı Mehmed Bey

23 October 1514

While Yavuz Sultan Selim, returning from Tabriz after the victory of Çaldıran, was in camp, the news came that the fortresses of Bayburt and Kiği had been taken over. Bıyıklı Mehmed Bey, sancak bey of Trebizond, and Mustafa Bey, sancak bey of Ioannina, took Bayburt, defended by Qara Maqsud-i Sultani, one of Shah Ismail's emirs, in October 1514. Erzincan, which came under Ottoman rule without a fight, was given together with Bayburt as a beylerbeylik to Bıyıklı Mehmed Bey on 23 October 1514, and Bayburt was made a sancak centre.
