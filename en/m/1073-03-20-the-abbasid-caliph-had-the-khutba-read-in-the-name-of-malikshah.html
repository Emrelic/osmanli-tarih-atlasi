The Abbasid caliph had the khutba read in the name of Malikshah

20 March 1073

After Alp Arslan was killed on 24 November 1072, the heir Malikshah took the throne; the caliph had the khutba read in his name on 8 Rajab 465 (20 March 1073), and on 2 Safar 466 (7 October 1073) sent a diploma and banner confirming his sovereignty.
