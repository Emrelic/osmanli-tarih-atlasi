Caesar's Gallic campaign began: the Helvetii were defeated

57 BC

In 58 BC Caesar, as governor of Cisalpine and Transalpine Gaul, halted the migration of the Helvetii near Bibracte; he then defeated the Germans of Ariovistus. The campaign would last eight years and add Gaul to Rome.
