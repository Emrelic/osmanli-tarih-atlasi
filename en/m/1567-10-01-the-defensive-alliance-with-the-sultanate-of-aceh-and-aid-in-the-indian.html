The Defensive Alliance with the Sultanate of Aceh and Aid in the Indian Ocean

1 October 1567

The Acehnese envoys, who had asked Süleyman the Lawgiver for help against Portuguese attacks, presented their allegiance to Selim II upon the sultan's death at Szigetvár, and a defensive agreement was signed with him. The sultan ordered cannon, muskets, munitions and craftsmen to be sent from Alexandria; seventeen warships were prepared to be dispatched to the Indian waters. The growth of the Yemen rebellion prevented the fleet from setting out, but the Ottoman gunners and craftsmen who reached Aceh left a lasting mark in the region.
