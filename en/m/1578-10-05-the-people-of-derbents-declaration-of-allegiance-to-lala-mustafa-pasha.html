The people of Derbent's declaration of allegiance to Lala Mustafa Pasha — the Iron Gate under Ottoman administration

5 October 1578

During the Shirvan campaign, a seven-man delegation from the people of Derbent went to the commander-in-chief Lala Mustafa Pasha, who was at Ereş, and declared their allegiance. Derbent (the Iron Gate), which closes the narrow pass between the Caucasus wall and the Caspian Sea, played a key role in the retention of Shirvan as the compulsory passage of the land routes coming from the north. Ottoman domination here lasted until 1607.
