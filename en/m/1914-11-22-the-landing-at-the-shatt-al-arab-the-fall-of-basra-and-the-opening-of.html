The landing at the Shatt al-Arab — the fall of Basra and the opening of the Iraqi front

22 November 1914

At the opening of the First World War, the British expeditionary force that came from India landed at Fao at the mouth of the Shatt al-Arab and soon took Basra. The fall of the city meant the opening of the Iraqi front and the passing of the northern end of the Gulf under British control.
