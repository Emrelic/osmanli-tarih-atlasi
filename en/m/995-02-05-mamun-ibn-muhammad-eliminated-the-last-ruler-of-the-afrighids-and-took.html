Ma'mun ibn Muhammad eliminated the last ruler of the Afrighids and took the title Khwarazmshah; the Ma'munids were founded

5 February 995

Ma'mun ibn Muhammad, the Samanid governor of Gurganj, eliminated the last ruler of the Afrighids and became master of all Khwarazm with the title of khwarazmshah (385/995); the dynasty lasted until 1017.
