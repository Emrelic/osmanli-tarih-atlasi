Kilij Arslan II takes the Uluborlu, Kütahya and Dorylaeum (Eskişehir) region from Byzantium

28 May 1180

When Emperor Manuel did not abide by the terms of the treaty and did not have the fortress of Dorylaeum demolished, Kilij Arslan II had the Meander valley laid waste in 573/1177; Uluborlu, Kütahya and Dorylaeum passed to Seljuk rule in 576/1180.
