The capture of Drežnik by Austria — the western front of the Dubica War

1788

In the Ottoman–Austrian war of 1788–1791, Habsburg forces took Drežnik, to the west of Bihać. By Article 4 of the Treaty of Sistova, Drežnik and Cetin remained under Habsburg administration and never returned to Bosnia.
