The founding of the Fire Brigade Corps (Tulumbacı Ocağı)

1 June 1720

According to the TDV article on 'Tulumbacı', following the success shown with the tulumba (fire pump) in Istanbul fires by Gerçek Dâvud, who had returned from the 1715 Morea campaign, Grand Vizier Nevşehirli Damad Ibrahim Pasha charged him with establishing an official fire-fighting corps. The corps, founded in the Acemi Oğlanları Barracks with six officers and fifty men, reached 150 persons in 1724 and 461 in 1755. Dâvud remained at the head of the corps until his death in 1733.
