The Order of the Sword Brothers (Livonian Order) is recognised by the papacy

1202

Pope Innocent III recognised the Order of the Sword Brothers in 1202 or 1204; the seat of the order was Wenden (Cēsis). The order opened a period of uninterrupted warfare against the Russians and the Lithuanians.
