The ratification of the Treaty of Jassy by the grand vizier's instrument

18 January 1792

The Treaty of Jassy, signed on 10 January 1792, was ratified on 18 January by an instrument given by the grand vizier. The ratification would be followed by exchanges of confirmations and instruments of ratification, and the peace would become legally final at the beginning of February.
