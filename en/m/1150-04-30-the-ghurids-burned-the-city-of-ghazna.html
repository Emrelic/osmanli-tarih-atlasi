The Ghurids burned the city of Ghazna

30 April 1150

The ever-strengthening Ghurids burned the city of Ghazna as an act of revenge (545/1150–51); although Bahram Shah regained control of the city in 547 (1152), the Ghaznavids entered a process of collapse.
