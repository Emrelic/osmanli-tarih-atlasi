Shutruk-Nahhunte plundered Babylonia; he carried the stelae of Hammurabi and Naram-Sin to Susa

1157 BC

Shutruk-Nahhunte, king of Middle Elam, attacked Babylonia and plundered cities such as Sippar; booty such as the stele of the Code of Hammurabi and the Victory Stele of Naram-Sin was carried to Susa. His son Kutir-Nahhunte ended the Kassite dynasty in 1155 BC.
