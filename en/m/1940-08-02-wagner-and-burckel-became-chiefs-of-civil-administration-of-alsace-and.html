Wagner and Bürckel became chiefs of civil administration of Alsace and Lorraine: the region was effectively joined to the Reich

2 August 1940

After France's defeat Alsace and Moselle were joined to Germany de facto, without a treaty: Alsace was placed under the civil administration of Wagner, Gauleiter of Baden, and Moselle under that of Bürckel, Gauleiter of Saar-Palatinate (Moselle was attached to Gau Westmark on 30 November 1940). The armistice text contains no annexation clause; legally French sovereignty continues (Ordinance of 9 August 1944). By these appointments Metz (Moselle), Colmar and Mulhouse (Alsace) passed de facto to German civil administration.
