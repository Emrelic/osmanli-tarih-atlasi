Gungunum, king of Larsa, took Ur from Isin

1924 BC

Gungunum (c. 1932-1906) made Larsa a great power for the first time: his year names proclaim the capture of Ur and a success against Elam. With the passing of Ur, Isin's confinement to the north began.
