Albanian soldiers overthrew the governor of Egypt

1 May 1803

Albanian troops whose pay (ulufe) had not been paid rose in revolt and forced the governor Hüsrev Pasha to flee from the citadel. In the vacuum created by the French withdrawal, the Mamluk beys, the Albanian corps and the governors appointed by the Sublime Porte entered a three-cornered struggle; Mehmed Ali came to the fore by establishing balances within this triangle. Other settlements lost on the same date: Taif.
