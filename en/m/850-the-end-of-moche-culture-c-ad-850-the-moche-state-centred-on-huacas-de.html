The end of Moche culture (c. AD 850): the Moche state centred on Huacas de Moche disintegrated

850

Archaeologists of the north coast generally place the end of Moche culture at about AD 850; new radiocarbon dates show that occupation at Huacas de Moche continued into the ninth century and beyond, and, through the textile finds at Huaca del Sol, into the tenth century, although the culture changed considerably in the process.
