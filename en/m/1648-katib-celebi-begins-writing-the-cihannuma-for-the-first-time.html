Katib Çelebi begins writing the Cihannüma for the first time

1648

Katib Çelebi began the first composition of the Cihannüma in 1648, in order to fill the gap in Ottoman geographical knowledge in the face of the West's geographical discoveries. This first version was left unfinished because sufficient sources could not be found about some European countries.
