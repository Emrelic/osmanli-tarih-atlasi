Kırcaali Detachment broken in the Rhodopes, Kırcaali fell

20 October 1912

After the Bulgarian declaration of war, Bulgarian forces descending from the Rhodopes defeated Mehmed Yaver Pasha's Kırcaali Detachment and entered Kırcaali. The routed detachment withdrew toward Gümülcine; the eastern part of the Rhodopes was lost in the first week of the war.
