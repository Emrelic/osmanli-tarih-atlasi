Skanderbeg captured Krujë (Akçahisar) — the Albanian resistance begins

1443

After the defeat at Zlatitsa (İzladi) (TDV Kruya: 847/1443), Skanderbeg occupied Krujë. After the death of his father Gjon Kastrioti in 1437, the Ottomans had given the city not to him but to Hasan Bey, whom they appointed. Only the year is known; the day is unconfirmed.
