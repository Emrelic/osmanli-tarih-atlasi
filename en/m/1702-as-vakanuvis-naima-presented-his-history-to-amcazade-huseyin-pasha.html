As vakanüvis, Naîmâ presented his history to Amcazâde Hüseyin Pasha

1702

Mustafa Naîmâ, a palace scribe from Aleppo, had been appointed vakanüvis by Amcazâde Hüseyin Pasha before the year 1114 (1702), and in the same year he presented his work to the pasha. The widespread opinion that he was the first official historian of the state is reinforced by his successor Râşid Efendi's referring to him as vakanüvis. The product of this work, Ravzatü'l-Hüseyn, covers approximately the years 1592-1660.
