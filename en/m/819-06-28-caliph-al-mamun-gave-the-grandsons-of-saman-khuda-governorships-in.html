Caliph al-Ma'mun gave the grandsons of Saman-khuda governorships in Transoxiana; the foundation of the Samanid dynasty was laid

28 June 819

In return for their services in suppressing the revolt of Rafi' ibn Layth, al-Ma'mun appointed the sons of Asad ibn Saman-khuda to governorships: Nuh to Samarkand, Ahmad to Fergana, Yahya to Shash, and Ilyas to Herat; the family was divided into four branches.
