Tsar Simeon died: the age of Bulgaria's great wars closed

27 May 927

Simeon, who was compelled to seek peace through the mediation of the pope, died suddenly on 27 May 927. His death changed the balance in the Balkans; for the next sixty years Bulgaria played no active role in the region.
