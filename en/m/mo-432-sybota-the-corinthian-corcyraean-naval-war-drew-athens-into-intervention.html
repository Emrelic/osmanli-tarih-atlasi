Sybota: the Corinthian–Corcyraean naval war drew Athens into intervention

432 BC

In the naval war that Corinth fought with its colony Corcyra over Epidamnus, Athens supported Corcyra. Corinth's resentment toward Athens was one of the direct causes of the Peloponnesian War.
