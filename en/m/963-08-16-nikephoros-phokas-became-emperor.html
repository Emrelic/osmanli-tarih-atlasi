Nikephoros Phokas became emperor

16 August 963

Nikephoros, proclaimed emperor by the army of Caesarea, entered the capital on 14 August 963; after street fighting with the palace guards he ascended the throne on 16 August. His reign is remembered for the conquests of Crete, Cilicia and Antioch.
