Completion of the Zal Mahmud Pasha Mosque

1580

In the construction of the complex, begun after the marriage of Zal Mahmud Pasha and Shah Sultan in 1574, the building of the mosque was completed in 1580; the madrasas continued until 1582, and the fountain until 1590. The complex is notable for its original plan with two symmetrical minarets on the slopes of Eyüp.
