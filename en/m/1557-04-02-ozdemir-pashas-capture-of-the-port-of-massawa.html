Özdemir Pasha's capture of the port of Massawa

2 April 1557

Özdemir Pasha, beylerbeyi of the Province of Habesh, after taking the Dahlak islands, took the city of Massawa on the opposite shore on 2 April 1557; Arkiko then also came under Ottoman administration. Thus Abyssinia's principal port opening onto the Red Sea fell into Ottoman hands.
