Franz Joseph I was crowned king of Hungary at Budapest — the dual monarchy (Austria-Hungary) was completed

8 June 1867

The Compromise (Ausgleich) of 1867 granted Hungary a separate government and parliament in its internal affairs; foreign policy, the army and common finances remained joint. Franz Joseph was crowned king of Hungary on 8 June 1867.
