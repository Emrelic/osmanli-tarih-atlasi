Qavurd Beg took Bardasir, the center of Kirman, ending Buyid rule in Kirman

16 June 1048

When Qavurd Beg, son of Chaghri Beg, entered the north of Kirman with 5,000–6,000 horsemen and besieged the center, Bardasir, Bahram b. Lashkarsitan, who ruled in the name of the Buyids, surrendered the city (440/1048); the foundation of the Kirman Seljuks was laid. Abu Kalijar died in the same year.
