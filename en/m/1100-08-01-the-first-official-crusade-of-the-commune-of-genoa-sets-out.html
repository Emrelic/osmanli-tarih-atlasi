The first official crusade of the Commune of Genoa sets out

1 August 1100

After private crusading ventures (1097; Guglielmo Embriaco's expedition of 1098-99), the first official crusader fleet of the Commune of Genoa set out on 1 August 1100; Genoese colonialism in the East began.
