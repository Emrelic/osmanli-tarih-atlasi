Muhammad Bakhtiyar Khalji died; Izz al-Din Muhammad succeeded him

1206

Muhammad Bakhtiyar, returning with heavy losses from the Kamrup-Tibet expedition, fell ill from grief and died, or, according to another view, was killed by an emir named Ali Mardan (1206); Izz al-Din Muhammad succeeded with the support of the commanders.
