Franco-Russian Alliance formalized

4 January 1894

France and Russia established a secret alliance by confirming, through an exchange of letters, the provisions of the military convention of 1892. If Germany or a state supported by it attacked one of the parties, the other would go to war against Germany with all its strength. The alliance was valid as long as the Triple Alliance lasted and established the bloc order of the First World War.
