Sultan Mahmud died; the vizier and atabeg Aqsunqur placed Mahmud's son Dawud on the throne

1131

The Iraq Seljuk sultan Mahmud died in 525 (1131); Aqsunqur al-Ahmadili placed Mahmud's son Dawud on the throne, and the khutba was read in his name in Iraq-i Ajam and Azerbaijan, but it did not last long.
