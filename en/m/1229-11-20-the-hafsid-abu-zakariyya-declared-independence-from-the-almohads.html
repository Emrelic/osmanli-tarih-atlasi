The Hafsid Abu Zakariyya declared independence from the Almohads

20 November 1229

At the beginning of 627, Abu Zakariyya claimed that the beliefs of the Almohad caliph Idris al-Ma'mun were contrary to Sunnism, stopped the khutba and declared his independence with the title 'emir', thus becoming the true founder of the Hafsid dynasty.
