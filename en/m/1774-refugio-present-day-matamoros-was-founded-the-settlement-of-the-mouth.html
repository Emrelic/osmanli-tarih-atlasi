Refugio (present-day Matamoros) was founded — the settlement of the mouth of the Rio Grande

1774

The Nuevo Santander colonization programme, which José de Escandón carried out from the 1740s onward, filled the lower basin of the Rio Grande with ranches and towns. Nuestra Señora del Refugio de los Esteros was founded on the south bank of the river as one of the last links of this programme; it took the name Matamoros in the nineteenth century.
