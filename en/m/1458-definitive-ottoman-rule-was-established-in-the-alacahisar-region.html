Definitive Ottoman rule was established in the Alacahisar region

1458

Alacahisar, left to Branković under the treaty made with the Hungarian delegates in 1444, came under Turkish rule again in the years following the victory at Varna. This second conquest of the city is documented not by a record of war but by a fiscal record: the timar registers of some villages dependent on Alacahisar are dated to the end of 1453/beginning of 1454; but this is only village testimony — in 1454 the Ottoman forces were defeated near Alacahisar, and in 1455 Alacahisar was left to Branković as a border. According to the TDV, definitive Ottoman rule in the region was established in 1458.
