Treaty of Zadar — Dubrovnik passed from Venice to Hungarian protection

18 February 1358

The Treaty of Zadar, signed on 18 February 1358, ended Venice's 150-year dominion over Dalmatia; Dubrovnik (Ragusa) thus came out of Venetian rule and acquired the status of a semi-autonomous republic under the protection of the Hungarian King Louis I.
