Şehzade Mosque opened for worship

1 August 1548

After construction lasting about five years, the Şehzade Mosque was opened for worship in August 1548. With its plan scheme of twin minarets and a central dome, it is regarded as a harbinger of Mimar Sinan's later great works; the complex consists of a mosque, mausoleum, madrasa, imaret, tabhane and caravanserai.
