When the Mongols are defeated by the Mamluks and withdraw to Iran, the Mamluks attack Cilicia and devastate the country

1260

When the Ilkhanid Mongols were defeated by the Mamluks and withdrew to Persia, the Mamluks attacked Cilicia and devastated the country; this is the actual beginning of the Mamluk attacks.
