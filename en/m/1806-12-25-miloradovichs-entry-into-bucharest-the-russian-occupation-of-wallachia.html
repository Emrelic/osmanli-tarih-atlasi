Miloradovich's entry into Bucharest — the Russian occupation of Wallachia

25 December 1806

When the Russian army entered Moldavia, Mustafa Pasha, commandant of Ruse, sent a force to Bucharest, and this force held the city. The detachment of General Miloradovich drove them out of the city; the Ottoman force withdrew to Giurgiu. From this date until the Treaty of Bucharest of 1812, Bucharest remained under Russian occupation.
