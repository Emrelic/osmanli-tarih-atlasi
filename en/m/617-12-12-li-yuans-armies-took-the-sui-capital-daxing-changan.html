Li Yuan's armies took the Sui capital Daxing (Chang'an)

12 December 617

After Li Yuan's army reached 200,000 men at the Changle Palace, it defeated the Sui defenders who resisted the city; Daxing was taken, the commanders who resisted were executed, and a few days later Yang You (Gongdi) was proclaimed Sui emperor and Yangdi was regarded as the 'Retired Emperor'.
