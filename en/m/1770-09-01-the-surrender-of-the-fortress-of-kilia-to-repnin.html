The surrender of the fortress of Kilia to Repnin

1 September 1770

The column of Repnin, which descended to the mouth of the Danube after the rout at Kartal, came before Kilia on 10 August (Julian). After the fire of the breaching batteries and a ten-day defense, the garrison surrendered the fortress on condition that they leave freely with their arms and the goods of the people. Kilia was returned to the Ottomans by the Treaty of Küçük Kaynarca (1774).
