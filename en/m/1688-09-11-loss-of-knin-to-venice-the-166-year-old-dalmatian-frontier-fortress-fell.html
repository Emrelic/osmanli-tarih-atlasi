Loss of Knin to Venice: the 166-year-old Dalmatian frontier fortress fell

11 September 1688

In the multi-front war opened after the Vienna defeat of 1683, Venetian forces advanced into the interior of Dalmatia. Knin, besieged by the Dalmatian general Girolamo Cornaro, was surrendered by Beylerbeyi Mehmed Pasha after a twelve-day siege. Knin was the key fortress of the Dalmatian frontier line, which Gazi Hüsrev Beg had conquered in 1522 and which had remained in Ottoman hands for 166 years; its loss largely opened the region to Venice.
