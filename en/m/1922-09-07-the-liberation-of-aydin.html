The liberation of Aydın

7 September 1922

With the approach of the Turkish army to Izmir, the Greek forces in the Menderes valley also withdrew. According to the TDV article on Aydın, the city was liberated by the Turkish forces on 7 September 1922, partly destroyed and with its population greatly reduced. The retreating Greeks had burned most of the city.
