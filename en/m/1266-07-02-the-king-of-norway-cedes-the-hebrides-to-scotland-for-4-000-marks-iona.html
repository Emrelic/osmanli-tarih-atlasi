The King of Norway cedes the Hebrides to Scotland for 4,000 marks: Iona (Hebrides)

2 July 1266

With Alexander III's defeat of the Norwegian fleet at Largs (1263), Norwegian rule over the Hebrides was shaken; in 1266 the King of Norway ceded the islands to Scotland for 4,000 marks.
