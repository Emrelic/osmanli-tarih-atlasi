The Attachment of Algiers to the Ottoman State

1 September 1519

Hayreddin Reis, who was resisting Spanish pressure in North Africa, appealed to Istanbul in order to hold out, offered Algiers to the sultan, and the khutba was read in the name of Selim the Grim. Thus Algiers was officially added to Ottoman territory, and soldiers and cannon were sent to Hayreddin with the title of beylerbeyi. This attachment laid the foundation of the Ottoman naval dominance in the Western Mediterranean that would last for centuries. Other places that joined on the same date: Constantine, Cherchell (Şerşel), Médéa (Titteri), Sétif.
