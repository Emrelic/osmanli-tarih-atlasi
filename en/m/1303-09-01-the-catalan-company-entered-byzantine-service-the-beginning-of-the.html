The Catalan Company entered Byzantine service — the beginning of the Anatolian campaign

1 September 1303

The Byzantine Empire, losing territory to the Turkish beyliks in western Anatolia, took into its service as mercenaries a Catalan force of about 6,500 men under Roger de Flor. According to the TDV article on Byzantium, the force came to the empire's aid in 1303 and the following year relieved Alaşehir, which was besieged by the Germiyanids. But the company soon began to plunder the places it passed through; Byzantium moved it across to Thrace and had Roger de Flor killed in 1305.
