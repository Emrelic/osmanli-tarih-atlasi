The Tang dynasty came to an end: Aidi handed the throne to Zhu Wen

1 June 907

The Tang emperor Zhaoxuan (Ai) issued the edict of abdication; the ceremonial delegation came to Daliang (Kaifeng) and presented the seals to Zhu Wen, and Zhu Wen became emperor. The Tang completed its life of 289 years; China entered the Five Dynasties period.
