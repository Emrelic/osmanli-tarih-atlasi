Jalal al-Din Khwarazm-Shah was killed in the mountains of Amid; the Khwarazm-Shah dynasty collapsed

1231

With Jalal al-Din, who was killed in a mountain village of Amid where he had taken refuge after the defeat at Yassıçimen (628/1231), the Khwarazm-Shah dynasty, which had played an important role in Turco-Islamic history, collapsed; the leaderless Qangli-Qipchaq and Khwarazmian units later entered the service of Kayqubad in Anatolia.
