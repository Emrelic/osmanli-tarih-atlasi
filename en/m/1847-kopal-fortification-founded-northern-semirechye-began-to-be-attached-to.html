Kopal fortification founded — Northern Semirechye began to be attached to Russia

1847

At the request of the Kazakhs of the Senior Zhuz, Russia founded the Kopal fortification at the foot of the Zhetysu Alatau. According to the source, this facilitated the annexation of Northern Semirechye, up to the Ili River, to Russia.
