The Marinids founded an independent principality in the Far Maghrib under the leadership of Abd al-Haqq b. Mahyu

1196

After the death of Mahyu, his son Abu Muhammad Abd al-Haqq took over in 592 (1196) as the founder of the dynasty; the Marinids had been living independently in the Far Maghrib since 1196.
