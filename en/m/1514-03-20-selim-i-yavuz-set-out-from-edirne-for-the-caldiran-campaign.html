Selim I (Yavuz) set out from Edirne for the Çaldıran campaign

20 March 1514

Yavuz Sultan Selim, leaving his son Prince Süleyman in Manisa as his deputy in Istanbul, set out from Edirne for his campaign against the Safavid State. The army passed through Anatolia by the Sivas–Erzincan–Erzurum line and would meet Shah Ismail's forces at Çaldıran at the end of August.
