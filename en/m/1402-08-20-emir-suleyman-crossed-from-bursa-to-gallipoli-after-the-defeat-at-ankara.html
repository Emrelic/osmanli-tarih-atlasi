Emîr Süleyman crossed from Bursa to Gallipoli after the defeat at Ankara

20 August 1402

After the Battle of Ankara, Süleyman Çelebi, hearing that Timur's grandson Muhammad Sultan wanted to capture him, left Bursa with his brother Kasim, his sister Fatma and whatever he could take from the treasury, and crossed to Gallipoli. Because of developments in the Balkans he could not return to Anatolia until early 1404; the Ottoman lands in Rumelia remained his share.
