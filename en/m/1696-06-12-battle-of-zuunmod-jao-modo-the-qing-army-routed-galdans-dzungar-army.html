Battle of Zuunmod (Jao Modo): the Qing army routed Galdan's Dzungar army

12 June 1696

The Qing army under Fiyanggu inflicted a heavy defeat on Galdan at Jao Modo; Galdan's wife Anu died. The defeat strengthened Khalkha's attachment to the Qing, and Galdan died in 1697.
