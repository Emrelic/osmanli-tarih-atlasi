Teres founded the Odrysian kingdom: the first great state of Thrace

449 BC

Thucydides writes that Teres founded the first great kingdom in Thrace; his son Sitalkes extended the kingdom from the Danube to the Aegean. The Odrysian kingdom pursued a policy of balance among Athens, Macedonia, and Persia.
