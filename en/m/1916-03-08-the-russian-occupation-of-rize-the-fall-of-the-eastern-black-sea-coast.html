The Russian occupation of Rize: the fall of the eastern Black Sea coast

8 March 1916

The Russian troops, who went on the offensive from Batum in the direction of Arhavi on 7 February 1916, advanced westward along the coast and occupied Pazar on 4 March, Çayeli on 5 March, Rize on 8 March and Of on 26 March. Trabzon was taken by land at the end of this advance, on 18 April 1916.
