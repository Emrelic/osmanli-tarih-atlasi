Abdulaziz b. Saud's recapture of Riyadh from the Rashidis

15 January 1902

Abdulaziz, a young member of the House of Saud who had gone into exile in Kuwait ten years earlier, raided Riyadh with a small detachment and seized the city by killing the deputy governor of the Al Rashid (Shammar). According to the Riyadh entry of the TDV İslâm Ansiklopedisi, the progressive weakening of the Rashidis and their inability to send reinforcements to the city facilitated the success of this raid; the event is regarded as the founding moment of the Second Saudi State, which over the next thirty years would form the core of present-day Saudi Arabia.
