The Second Athenian League was founded (decree of Aristoteles)

376 BC

Athens called the Aegean cities into a league against Sparta on the principle of autonomy and no tribute; the decree was passed on the motion of Aristoteles and the list of member cities was inscribed on stone. The founding of the League made Athens a naval power again.
