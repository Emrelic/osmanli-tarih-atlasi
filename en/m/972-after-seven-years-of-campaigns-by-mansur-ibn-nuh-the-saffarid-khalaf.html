After seven years of campaigns by Mansur ibn Nuh, the Saffarid Khalaf agreed to have the khutba read in the name of the Samanids

972

When Khalaf al-Saffar did not send the taxes, the Samanid emir Mansur I conducted campaigns against the Saffarids that lasted seven years; in the end Khalaf agreed to have the khutba read in the name of the Samanids (361/972).
