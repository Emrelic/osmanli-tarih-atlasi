Sultan Mas'ud died; his son Kilij Arslan II ascended the throne at Konya

7 March 1155

Mas'ud died in 550 (1155); the Seljuk realm at that time extended over the regions of Eskişehir, Ankara, Çankırı and Kastamonu and the Elbistan area. Kilij Arslan II, who succeeded him, eliminated the Danishmendids.
