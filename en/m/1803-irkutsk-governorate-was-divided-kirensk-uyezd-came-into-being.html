Irkutsk Governorate was divided: Kirensk uyezd came into being

1803

In 1803 Irkutsk Governorate was divided into the uyezds of Irkutsk, Nizhneudinsk, Verkhneudinsk, Kirensk, Nerchinsk and Yakutsk; the Kirensk division later continued as an okrug until the end of the 19th century.
