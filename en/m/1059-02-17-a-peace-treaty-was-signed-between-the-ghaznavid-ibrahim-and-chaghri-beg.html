A peace treaty was signed between the Ghaznavid Ibrahim and Chaghri Beg; the frontiers were kept as they stood

17 February 1059

Chaghri Beg, who had long struggled with the Ghaznavids, added Balkh, Tokharistan, Tirmidh, Walwalij, Qubadhiyan and Wakhsh to his country and signed a peace with the Ghaznavid Sultan Ibrahim (451/1059): the two sides would keep the places they held and would not attack each other. The peace lasted about fifty years.
