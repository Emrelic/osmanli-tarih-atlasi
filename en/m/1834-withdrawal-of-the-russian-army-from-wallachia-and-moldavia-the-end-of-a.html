Withdrawal of the Russian army from Wallachia and Moldavia: the end of a six-year occupation

1834

In the Ottoman-Russian war of 1828-1829 the Russians had occupied Wallachia and Moldavia, and after the Treaty of Edirne they had not withdrawn until the war indemnity was paid. When the indemnity was reduced to five million in January 1834, the Russian forces evacuated the Danubian Principalities and new voivodes were appointed to the two principalities. Ottoman suzerainty returned on paper, but Russia retained the influence it had established through the Règlement Organique.
