Ibn Tumart proclaimed himself the Mahdi

13 November 1121

When Ibn Tumart was expelled from the Almoravid capital Marrakesh, he gathered followers and proclaimed himself the Mahdi in the month of Ramadan of the year 515 (November 1121); the Almohad movement began in the Daran (Atlas) mountains.
