Kaloyan received a royal crown at Tarnovo from the papal legate

8 November 1204

At the end of his negotiations with Pope Innocent III, Kaloyan was crowned king (rex) at Tarnovo on 8 November 1204 by the papal legate Cardinal Leo with a crown brought from Rome; the next day the archbishop of Tarnovo was consecrated as 'primate'.
