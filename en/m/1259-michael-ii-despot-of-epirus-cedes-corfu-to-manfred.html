Michael II, Despot of Epirus, cedes Corfu to Manfred

1259

In 1259 Michael II Angelos Komnenos, Despot of Epirus, ceded Corfu to Manfred, king of Sicily; in 1267 the island passed with the Kingdom of Sicily to Charles of Anjou and became the naval base of Angevin influence in the Balkans.
