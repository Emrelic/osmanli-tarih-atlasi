Gaozong arrived at Hangzhou and took up residence (1129)

5 March 1129

Gaozong, who had fled from Yangzhou by way of Zhenjiang, Changzhou, Pingjiang and Xiuzhou, arrived at Hangzhou on the 壬戌 day of that month and took up residence there; the next day he issued an edict blaming himself and asking for direct counsel.
