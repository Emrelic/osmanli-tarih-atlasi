Arslan Ilig Mansur abdicated; Yusuf Qadir Khan became great khagan of the Karakhanids

15 March 1024

When Mansur, preferring the life of a dervish, abdicated his throne in 415 (1024), Yusuf Qadir Khan succeeded him; Ali Tegin and Ahmad set about struggling against him.
