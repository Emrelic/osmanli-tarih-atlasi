Mantinea: Epaminondas died; Boeotian hegemony came to an end

361 BC

The Theban army won the battle, but Epaminondas was wounded and died; Xenophon ends by saying that there was "more uncertainty and confusion in Greece than before". Thebes's brief hegemony ended in the same year, and the Athens-Sparta-Thebes balance was preserved.
