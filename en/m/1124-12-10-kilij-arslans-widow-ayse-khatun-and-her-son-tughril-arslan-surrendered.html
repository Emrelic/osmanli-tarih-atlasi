Kilij Arslan's widow Ayse Khatun and her son Tughril Arslan surrendered Malatya to the Danishmendids and withdrew to the fortress of Minshar

10 December 1124

After the death of the Artuqid Belek (518/1124), Kilij Arslan's wife Ayse Khatun and her son Tughril Arslan surrendered Malatya to the Danishmendids and withdrew to the fortress of Minshar (1 Dhu al-Qa'da 518 / 10 December 1124).
