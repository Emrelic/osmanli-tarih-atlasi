Victory at Akdere (Valea Albă) — Moldavian campaign

26 July 1476

Mehmed the Conqueror, who set out on campaign in person to avenge the Racova rout of a year earlier, inflicted a great defeat on the Moldavian forces with the Ottoman army at the place called Akdere and entered Suceava, the centre of the principality. The fortresses, however, could not be taken; because of supply shortages, the plague that broke out among the Janissaries and Hungarian preparations, the army was forced to withdraw. The definitive allegiance of Moldavia could be secured only in the following years and with the taking of Kili (Chilia) and Akkirman (Cetatea Albă) in 1484.
