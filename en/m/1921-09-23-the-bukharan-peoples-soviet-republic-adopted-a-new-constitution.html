The Bukharan People's Soviet Republic adopted a new constitution

23 September 1921

The Second All-Bukharan Kurultai adopted the new constitution. The constitution gave the vote to female and male citizens who had reached the age of 20; excluded from this were large landowners and capitalists, relatives of the emir's family, former high officials of the emirate, convicts and the mentally ill. Private property was also recognised.
