Boncuklu Mound (Konya basin, Cumra): Neolithic occupation begins (early phase)

8299 BC

At Boncuklu, cultivation was adopted by local hunter-gatherers in the second half of the 9th millennium; the community is interpreted as the ancestor of Catalhoyuk East (Catalhoyuk East from 7100 cal BC).
