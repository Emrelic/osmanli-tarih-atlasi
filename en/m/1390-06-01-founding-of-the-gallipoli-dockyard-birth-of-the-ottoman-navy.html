Founding of the Gallipoli Dockyard — birth of the Ottoman navy

1 June 1390

The first properly organized dockyard of the Ottoman State was founded in 1390 by Bayezid the Thunderbolt at Gallipoli, on the site of the dockyard left over from Byzantium. The Ottomans, inheriting the seafaring legacy of the western Anatolian beyliks, thus for the first time became a naval power that built its own ships. Located at the key point of the Dardanelles, the dockyard served for a century and a half as the main base of the Ottoman navy, from the siege of Constantinople to the Aegean campaigns.
