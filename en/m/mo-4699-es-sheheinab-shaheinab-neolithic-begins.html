Es-Sheheinab (Shaheinab) Neolithic: begins

4699 BC

A Neolithic settlement near the Nile in central Sudan (domestic animals, ceramics, chisel/gouge); it defines the 'Shaheinab Neolithic' as a type site. The general range of the culture is 5000–3800 BCE.
