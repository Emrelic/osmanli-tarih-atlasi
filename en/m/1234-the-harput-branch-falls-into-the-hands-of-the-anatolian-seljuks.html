The Harput branch falls into the hands of the Anatolian Seljuks

1234

Ala al-Din Kayqubad took Harput in 631/1234 against the Ayyubids' advance on Anatolia; the rule of the Artuqid branch at Harput came to an end (after Kösedağ the city fell into the hands of the Ilkhanids).
