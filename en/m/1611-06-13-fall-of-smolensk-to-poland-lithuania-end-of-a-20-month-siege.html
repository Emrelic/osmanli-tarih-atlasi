Fall of Smolensk to Poland-Lithuania — end of a 20-month siege

13 June 1611

In the Time of Troubles (Smuta), the army of King Sigismund III of Poland blew a breach in the walls of Smolensk, which it had besieged since September 1609, with a mine on 13 June 1611 and took the city; the 20-month resistance ended. Smolensk remained in Poland-Lithuania until 1654, and this fall prepared the ground of the Truce of Deulino (1618), which would be signed seven years later.
