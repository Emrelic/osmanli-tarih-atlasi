Muş and Bingöl (Çapakçur) passed from the Safavids to Ottoman administration

25 February 1515

With the taking of Eastern Anatolia from the Safavids after Chaldiran, Muş came under Ottoman administration in 921/1515; neighbouring Çapakçur (Bingöl) was added to the Ottomans in the same process.
