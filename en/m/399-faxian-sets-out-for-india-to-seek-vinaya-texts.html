Faxian sets out for India to seek Vinaya texts

399

The Chinese Buddhist monk Faxian and his companions set out from Chang'an in 399 and go through the Gansu corridor to Dunhuang. Their goal is to find complete copies of the Vinaya (Buddhist discipline) books in India.
