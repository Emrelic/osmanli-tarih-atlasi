Baden crossed the Morava at Pasarofça — Pasarofça fell to Austria

1 August 1689

Margrave Ludwig von Baden crossed the Morava at Pasarofça in August 1689 and advanced toward Niš; the town remained in Austrian hands until the summer of 1690 (TDV suleyman-ii says that Pasarofça was retaken in 1690). The day and the reading of Röder were not re-verified in this session.
