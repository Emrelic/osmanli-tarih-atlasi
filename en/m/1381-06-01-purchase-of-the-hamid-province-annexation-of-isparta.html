Purchase of the Hamid province: annexation of Isparta

1 June 1381

Hamidoğlu Hüseyin Bey sold a part of his principality (Akşehir-Beyşehir-Isparta) to Murad I for cash; the rest of the Hamid province would be annexed by Yıldırım in 1391. Other settlements lost on the same date: İshaklı.
