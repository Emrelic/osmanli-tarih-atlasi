Ibrahim ibn Muhammad was killed by Inal Tegin; Tughril Qara Khan Mahmud became great khagan of the Eastern Karakhanids

17 February 1059

Ibrahim ibn Muhammad, who at his mother's instigation marched against Inal Tegin, the ruler of Barskhan, was defeated and killed (451/1059); the Western Karakhanid Tamghach Khan Ibrahim had meanwhile retaken Fergana and Shash. Tughril Qara Khan Mahmud, son of Yusuf Qadir Khan, was made great khagan.
