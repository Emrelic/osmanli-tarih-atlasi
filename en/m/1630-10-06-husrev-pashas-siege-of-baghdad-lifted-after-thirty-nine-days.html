Hüsrev Pasha's siege of Baghdad — lifted after thirty-nine days

6 October 1630

After the victory of Mihriban (5 May 1630), the grand vizier Hüsrev Pasha, who entered Hamadan and burned and destroyed the city, instead of marching on Qazvin turned back to the real target, Baghdad, by the decision of the war council assembled at Dergezin. The army, which reached the vicinity of Baghdad in early August, besieged the city on 6 October. Heavy losses were suffered in the sortie of the Iranian garrison on 26 October and in the general assault of 9 November; this second siege after the loss of Baghdad was also lifted without result. The army withdrew to Mosul.
