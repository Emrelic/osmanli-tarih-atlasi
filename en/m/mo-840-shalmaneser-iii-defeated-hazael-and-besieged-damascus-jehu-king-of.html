Shalmaneser III defeated Hazael and besieged Damascus; Jehu, king of Israel, paid tribute

840 BC

Shalmaneser III defeated Hazael, king of Aram-Damascus, on Mount Senir (Hermon) and besieged Damascus; Jehu, king of Israel, is shown on the Black Obelisk presenting tribute to Assyria. The Qarqar coalition broke up and Assyria became the dominant power in the west.
