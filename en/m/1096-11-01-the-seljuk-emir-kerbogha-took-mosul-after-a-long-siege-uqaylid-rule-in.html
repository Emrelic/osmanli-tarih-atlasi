The Seljuk emir Kerbogha took Mosul after a long siege; Uqaylid rule in Mosul ended

1 November 1096

When Muhammad, son of Muslim b. Quraysh, asked the Seljuk emir Kerbogha for help against his brother Ali, Kerbogha seized him, took Nisibis, and after a long siege captured Mosul; Uqaylid rule in Mosul ended in Dhu al-Qa'da 489 / November 1096.
