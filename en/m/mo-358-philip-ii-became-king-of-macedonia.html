Philip II became king of Macedonia

358 BC

After Perdiccas III died in a war with the Illyrians (360/359 BC), Philip became first regent and then king; he warded off the Illyrian, Paeonian and Thracian threats and carried out an army reform (the sarissa phalanx). The rise of Macedonia begins with this reign.
