Return of Gábor Bethlen's seven counties to the crown completed

30 March 1630

The Peace of Nikolsburg had left seven Upper Hungarian counties, including Szatmár, to Gábor Bethlen for life. After Bethlen's death on 15 November 1629, the return of the counties to the crown began with the instruction of 30 November 1629 and was completed on 30 March 1630.
