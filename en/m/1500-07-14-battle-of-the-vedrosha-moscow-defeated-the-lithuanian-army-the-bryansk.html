Battle of the Vedrosha — Moscow defeated the Lithuanian army; the Bryansk and Seversk lands remained with Moscow

14 July 1500

In the Muscovite-Lithuanian war of 1500-1503, the Muscovite army under Daniil Shchenya defeated the army of Hetman Konstanty Ostrogski on the Vedrosha River near Dorogobuzh and captured the hetman. The cities of Bryansk and Seversk, taken in the spring of the same year, remained in Moscow's hands; by the end of the war about one third of Lithuanian territory had passed to Moscow.
