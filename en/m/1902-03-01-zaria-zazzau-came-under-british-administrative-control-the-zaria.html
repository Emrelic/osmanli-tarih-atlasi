Zaria (Zazzau) came under British administrative control — the Zaria emirate of the Sokoto Caliphate was attached to the Northern Nigeria Protectorate

1 March 1902

The Zaria emirate, dependent on the Sokoto Caliphate, was brought under British Northern Nigeria administration in March 1902; the British stationed a Resident and a garrison in Zaria. Emir Kwasau surrendered in July 1902 and was removed from office in September.
