Konuralp's conquest of the Bolu region — Mudurnu, Konurapa and Bolu

1325

Konuralp, one of the commanders of Orhan Gazi, turned toward the mountainous region east of the Sakarya and brought the Mudurnu, Konurapa and Bolu region under Ottoman administration; the town on the plain of Düzce was called by his name. The exact date of the conquest is not known; the account rests on the report of Aşıkpaşazade. The region was at that time a frontier zone that changed hands between the Candaroğulları and Byzantium; the conquest brought the Ottomans for the first time close to the western shore of the Black Sea. After the Battle of Ankara the region would pass again to the Candaroğulları, but it would be definitively retaken in the time of Mehmed the Conqueror.
