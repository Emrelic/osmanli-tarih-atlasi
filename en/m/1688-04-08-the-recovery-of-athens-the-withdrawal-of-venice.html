The recovery of Athens — the withdrawal of Venice

8 April 1688

Morosini, who in September 1687 had bombarded the Acropolis and blown up the Parthenon, realised after spending the winter in Athens that he could not hold on in the city. Upon the plague epidemic, the difficulty of supply and the recovery of the Ottoman forces, the Venetian garrison evacuated the city and withdrew to the Morea. Athens thus returned to Ottoman administration without a fight, but the destruction the Parthenon had suffered could not be undone.
