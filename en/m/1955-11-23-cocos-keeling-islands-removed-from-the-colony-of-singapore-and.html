Cocos (Keeling) Islands removed from the Colony of Singapore and transferred to Australia

23 November 1955

On 23 November 1955, the day of proclamation of the Order in Council issued under Britain's Cocos Islands Act of 1955 and of Australia's Cocos (Keeling) Islands Act 1955, the islands ceased to be part of the Colony of Singapore and came under the authority of the Commonwealth of Australia. From this day Home Island (the Clunies-Ross family estate and the Cocos-Malay settlement) is under Australian administration.
