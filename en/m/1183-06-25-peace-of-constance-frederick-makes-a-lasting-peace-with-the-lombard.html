Peace of Constance: Frederick makes a lasting peace with the Lombard League

25 June 1183

In June 1183 a treaty was concluded with the Lombard League at Constance (a lasting peace replacing the six-year truce of 1177). In the meantime Henry the Lion, duke of Saxony, had been deprived of his duchy and sent into exile, and the duchy had been broken up; in 1184 Frederick's son Henry was betrothed to Constance, daughter of King Roger of Sicily.
