Berkyaruq died; Muhammad Tapar removed his son Malikshah II and reunited the Seljuk state

21 December 1104

Berkyaruq died in Rabi' al-Akhir 498 (December 1104); Muhammad Tapar removed Berkyaruq's young son Malikshah, took the throne, and reunited the state.
