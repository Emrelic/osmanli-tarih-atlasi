Thessalonica was annexed to the Empire of Nicaea: the Empire of Thessalonica came to an end

1246

In 1246 John Vatatzes crossed into Thrace and seized the lands occupied by the Bulgarians and Thessalonica; the Thessalonian state of Theodore Angelos's sons John and Demetrios was destroyed, and Macedonia again came under Byzantine/Nicaean rule.
