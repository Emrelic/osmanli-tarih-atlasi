The circumcision festival of Prince Mehmed at the Hippodrome began

29 May 1582

Prince Mehmed, the son of Murad III, was circumcised with magnificent ceremonies that began on 6 Jumada al-Awwal 990 (29 May 1582) and lasted fifty-six to fifty-seven days. According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam, the festivities held at the Hippodrome were among the most splendid palace celebrations of the period.
