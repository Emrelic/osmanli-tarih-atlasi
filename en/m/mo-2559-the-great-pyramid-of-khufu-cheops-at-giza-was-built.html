The Great Pyramid of Khufu (Cheops) at Giza was built

2559 BC

The pyramid of the Fourth Dynasty ruler Khufu at Giza is the symbol of the Old Kingdom's mobilization of labor and resources. The Egyptian priestly account that Herodotus relays portrays Khufu as a tyrant who closed the temples and forced the people to haul stone; as such it is the view of a later age, not a contemporary document.
