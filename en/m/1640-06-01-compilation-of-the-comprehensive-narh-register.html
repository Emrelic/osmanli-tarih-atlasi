Compilation of the comprehensive narh register

1 June 1640

Although the practice of narh (price ceiling) goes back very far among the Ottomans, the most comprehensive narh register that has come down to us dates from 1640; this register, covering food, clothing, services, standards and hygiene conditions, in the TDV's words "functioned as a complete market regulation law."
