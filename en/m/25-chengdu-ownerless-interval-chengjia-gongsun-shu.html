Chengdu: ownerless interval → Chengjia (成家, Gongsun Shu)

25

At Chengdu, ownership passes from the ownerless interval to Chengjia (成家, Gongsun Shu). Transition source information: Hou Han shu 13 (Gongsun Shu): in the Gengshi period he became 'King of Shu' and made Chengdu his capital; in Jianwu 1 (25 CE), 4th month, he proclaimed himself emperor; its end in Jianwu 12 (Wu Han's campaign, siege of Chengdu).
