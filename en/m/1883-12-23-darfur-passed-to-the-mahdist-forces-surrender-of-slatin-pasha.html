Darfur passed to the Mahdist forces — surrender of Slatin Pasha

23 December 1883

Slatin Pasha, governor-general of Darfur, had been dealing since 1882 with the Mahdist uprising of the Rizeigat tribe; thinking his soldiers' defeats were attributed to his being a Christian, he openly became a Muslim in 1883 and took the name Abd al-Qadir. After the destruction of Hicks Pasha's army in Kordofan in November 1883, resistance lost its meaning and he surrendered to Emir Madibbo on 23 December 1883. The small Egyptian posts in Darfur soon followed the same path; ten years of Turco-Egyptian rule ended. With this event Jebel Marra (Darfur) passed to the administration of the Mahdist state.
