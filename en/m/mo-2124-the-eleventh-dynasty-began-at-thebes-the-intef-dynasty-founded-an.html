The Eleventh Dynasty began at Thebes: the Intef dynasty founded an independent kingdom in Upper Egypt

2124 BC

The nomarchs of Thebes, of the line of Intef, took the royal title and founded their own kingdom at the southern end of Upper Egypt (from Elephantine to Abydos). A later Intef states in his inscription that he took the Thinite nome, in which Abydos lies, and 'opened all its fortresses'; thus the Thebes-Herakleopolis border was drawn around Abydos.
