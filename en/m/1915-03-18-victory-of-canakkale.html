Victory of Çanakkale

18 March 1915

The Allied armada, aiming to bring down Istanbul and reach Russia, was repulsed from the Straits on 18 March by the fire of the minefields and the forts; the line laid by the minelayer Nusret determined the fate of the battle. The Gallipoli land operation that began in April was also halted at Anafartalar and Conkbayırı; the Allies completely evacuated the peninsula in January 1916. The name of Lieutenant Colonel Mustafa Kemal was heard on this front; the victory, won at the cost of about a quarter of a million martyrs and casualties, is regarded as the place where the cadres and the spirit of the National Struggle were born.
