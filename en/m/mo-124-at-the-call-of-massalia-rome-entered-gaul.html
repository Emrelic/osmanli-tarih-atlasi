At the call of Massalia, Rome entered Gaul

124 BC

When Massalia, under pressure from the Salluvii and the Ligurians, asked Rome for help, Rome sent an army to Southern Gaul in 125. This intervention led to the founding of Narbo Martius (118) and the taking of Gallia Transalpina.
