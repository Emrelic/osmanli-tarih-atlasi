Narameikhla moved the capital from Launggyet to Mrohaung (Mrauk U); this city remained the capital for four centuries

1433

According to Harvey, soothsayers had said that if the capital were moved the king would die within the year; Narameikhla nonetheless founded Mrohaung in 1433 and died the next year. The city was the capital of Arakan between 1433 and 1785 (Harvey's chronology: Launggyet 1237-1433, Mrohaung 1433-1785).
