Laying of the foundation of the New Mosque (Eminönü)

9 April 1598

Under the patronage of Safiye Sultan, mother of Mehmed III, the foundation of the mosque was laid with stone blocks after the ground was strengthened with piles. Two separate TDV entries give different dates for this event (the Yenicami Complex entry 9 April 1598, the Davud Agha entry 23 August 1597); here the date in the building's own specific entry is taken as the basis.
