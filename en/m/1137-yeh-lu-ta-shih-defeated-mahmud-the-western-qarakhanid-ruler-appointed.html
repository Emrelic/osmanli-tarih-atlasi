Yeh-lü Ta-shih defeated Mahmud, the Western Qarakhanid ruler appointed by Sanjar, near Khujand

1137

In 531 (1137), near Khujand, the Western Qarakhanid ruler Mahmud b. Muhammad, who had been appointed by the Seljuk Sultan Sanjar, was defeated by Yeh-lü Ta-shih.
