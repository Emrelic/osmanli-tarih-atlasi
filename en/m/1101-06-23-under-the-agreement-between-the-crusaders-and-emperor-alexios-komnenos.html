Under the agreement between the Crusaders and Emperor Alexios Komnenos, Ankara is handed over to Byzantium

23 June 1101

During the First Crusade Raymond of Toulouse had occupied Ankara without meeting serious resistance, and the Turks in the fortress had been put to the sword; under the agreement between the Crusaders and Alexios Komnenos the city was handed over to the Byzantine Empire on 23 June 1101. Byzantine administration lasted about twenty years.
