Sanjar marched as far as Ghazna against Bahram Shah, who did not send the tribute; Bahram Shah was pardoned and left in his place

1135

When Bahram Shah did not pay the annual tribute of 250,000 dinars, Sultan Sanjar marched on Ghazna (529/1135); he pardoned Bahram Shah, who had fled to India, and left him in his place.
