Lima was founded — the capital of Spanish Peru moved to the Rímac valley

18 January 1535

Since Cuzco, in the mountains, lay far from Spain's sea communications, Francisco Pizarro founded Ciudad de los Reyes (Lima) in the coastal Rímac valley. The city soon became the capital of the Viceroyalty of Peru and the centre of Spanish rule in South America.
