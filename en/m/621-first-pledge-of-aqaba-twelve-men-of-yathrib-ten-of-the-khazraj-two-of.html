First Pledge of Aqaba: twelve men of Yathrib (ten of the Khazraj, two of the Aws) met Muhammad at Aqaba; Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr was sent to Medina

621

After the return of the six men a year earlier, twelve men of Yathrib met Muhammad secretly at Aqaba and made the First Pledge of Aqaba; Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr was sent with them to Yathrib to teach Islam.
