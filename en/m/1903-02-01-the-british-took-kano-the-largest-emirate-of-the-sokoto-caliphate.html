The British took Kano — the largest emirate of the Sokoto Caliphate passed to the Northern Nigeria Protectorate

1 February 1903

The force of Colonel Morland, under the orders of Lugard, High Commissioner of Northern Nigeria, set out from Zaria and captured Kano in February 1903; the Kano emirate, dependent on the Sokoto Caliphate, came under British administration. Sokoto was taken a month later, on 15 March 1903.
