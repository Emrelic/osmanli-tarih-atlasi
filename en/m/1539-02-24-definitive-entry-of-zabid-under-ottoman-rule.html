Definitive entry of Zabid under Ottoman rule

24 February 1539

The fleet, which had lifted the siege of Diu on 5 November 1538, called at the Yemeni coast on its return journey and Zabid was definitively attached to Ottoman administration; Hadım Süleyman Pasha left the city on 10 March 1539 and reached Jeddah on 1 April. The city had been governed in the name of the Ottomans since 1517 by the former Mamluk beys; with this date it passed to direct administration.
