Annexation of Crimea by Russia

19 April 1783

The 'independent' Crimea created by Küçük Kaynarca (Kuchuk Kainarji) was annexed to Russia by Catherine II's manifesto, with the disorder under the last khan, Şahin Giray (Shahin Giray), used as a pretext. The three-century Ottoman-Crimean partnership came to an end; the loss of the northern steppes removed Istanbul's strategic depth and set off the first great wave of migration of tens of thousands of Crimean Tatars into Ottoman lands. On this day the Crimean peninsula (Alushta, Kefe/Caffa, Sudak, Yalta, Balaklava, Inkerman, Mangup, Arabat) joined Russia; Taman and Kuban also passed to Russia in the same annexation (TDV Kırım: 8 April 1783 Julian = 19 April Gregorian).
