Mansura: the Muslims decisively defeated the Crusader army of Louis IX

7 April 1250

In the battles that lasted from the beginning of March to 7 April 1250, the Crusaders were decisively defeated; the king and his entourage were to be released in return for the restitution of Damietta and a heavy ransom.
