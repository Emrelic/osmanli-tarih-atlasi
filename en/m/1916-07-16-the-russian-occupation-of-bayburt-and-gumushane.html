The Russian occupation of Bayburt and Gümüşhane

16 July 1916

With the breaking of the Kop defense, Russian troops occupied Bayburt on 16 July 1916, Gümüşhane on 19 July and Kelkit on 22 July; Erzincan fell after these, on 24 July.
