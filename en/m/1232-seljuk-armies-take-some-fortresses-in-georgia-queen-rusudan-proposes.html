Seljuk armies take some fortresses in Georgia; Queen Rusudan proposes peace and agrees to betroth her daughter Tamar to Kaykhusraw II

1232

In 1232, in the time of Ala al-Din Kayqubad I, Kamal al-Din Kamyar and Mubariz al-Din Chavli campaigned in Georgia and took some fortresses; Queen Rusudan, unable to resist the Seljuk army, proposed peace and agreed to betroth her daughter Tamar to Ghiyath al-Din Kaykhusraw II, Kayqubad's son.
