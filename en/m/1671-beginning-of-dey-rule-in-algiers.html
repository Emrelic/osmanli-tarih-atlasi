Beginning of dey rule in Algiers

1671

The Algiers ocak and the taife-i reis (corps of corsair captains) deposed the last agha and elected Hacı Mehmed dey for life. After this date Algiers was governed not by a beylerbeyi appointed by Istanbul but by a dey chosen by the local ocak from among its own members; the Ottoman tie remained limited to the reading of the sultan's name in the khutba and to obligations of the standard and of campaign service. Algiers thus changed from a province into an ocaklık — the same transformation was experienced by Tunis in 1705 with the Husaynids and by Tripolitania in 1711 with the Karamanlis.
