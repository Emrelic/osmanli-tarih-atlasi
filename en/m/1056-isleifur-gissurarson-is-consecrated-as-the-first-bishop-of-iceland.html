Ísleifur Gissurarson is consecrated as the first bishop of Iceland; Skálholt becomes the episcopal seat

1056

Ísleifur Gissurarson was consecrated by the Archbishop of Bremen as the first bishop of Iceland; Skálholt became the episcopal seat (only the year is known).
