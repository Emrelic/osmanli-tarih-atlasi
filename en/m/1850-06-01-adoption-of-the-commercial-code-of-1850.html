Adoption of the Commercial Code of 1850

1 June 1850

Within the framework of the legal reforms of the Tanzimat period, new laws were taken from Western states through translation and borrowing: the penal code came into force in 1840, the commercial code in 1850, the land code in 1857. The Commercial Code was the first modern codification of Ottoman commercial law.
