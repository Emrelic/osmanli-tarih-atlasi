Arghun Khan proclaims Mas'ud II sultan and has Kaykhusraw III killed

11 April 1282

After Ahmad Tegüder divided the Seljuk lands between Mas'ud II and Kaykhusraw III, Arghun Khan, victorious in the struggle for the Ilkhanid throne, proclaimed Mas'ud II, who was at Tabriz, sultan (681/1282) and had Kaykhusraw III, who was waiting at Erzurum, killed.
