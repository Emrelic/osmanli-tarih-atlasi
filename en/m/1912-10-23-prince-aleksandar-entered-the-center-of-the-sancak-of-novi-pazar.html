Prince Aleksandar entered the center of the sancak of Novi Pazar

23 October 1912

Serbian forces took Novi Pazar in the first week of the war and joined up with the Montenegrin forces coming from the west. The sancak of Novi Pazar, which had separated Serbia and Montenegro for a hundred years, thus became a bridge between the two allies.
