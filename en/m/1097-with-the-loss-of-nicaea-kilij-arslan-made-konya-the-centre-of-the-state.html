With the loss of Nicaea, Kilij Arslan made Konya the centre of the state

1097

After the surrender of Nicaea (19 June 1097) and the defeat suffered near Eskişehir (July 1097), the Seljuks abandoned the Marmara and western Anatolia to Byzantium and withdrew to central Anatolia; Konya was chosen as capital.
