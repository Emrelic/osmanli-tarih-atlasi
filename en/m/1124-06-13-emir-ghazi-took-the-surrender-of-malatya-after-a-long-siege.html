Emir Ghazi took the surrender of Malatya after a long siege

13 June 1124

On the death of Belek (1124), the Danishmendid Emir Ghazi, taking advantage of the disputes among the Seljuks between Tughril Arslan, the Malik of Malatya, and Suleyman, the Emir of Harput, assaulted Malatya on 13 June 1124 and received the city's surrender after a long siege.
