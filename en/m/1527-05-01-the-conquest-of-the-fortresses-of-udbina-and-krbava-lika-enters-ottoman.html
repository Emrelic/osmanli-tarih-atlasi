The conquest of the fortresses of Udbina and Krbava — Lika enters Ottoman administration

1 May 1527

The plain of Krbava had been emptied in 1524, and only Karlović's garrison remained in Udbina. In April 1527 Karlović won a last victory before the fortress, but at the end of May Udbina, together with the fortresses of Mrsinj and Komić, fell into the hands of the pasha of Bosnia. In the same year the Gospić area was given to the Senković aghas. Lika and Krbava thereafter became part of the sancak centred on Obrovac, which took the name Kırka in 1580.
