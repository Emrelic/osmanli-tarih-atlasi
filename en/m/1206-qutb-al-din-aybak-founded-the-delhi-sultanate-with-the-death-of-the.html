Qutb al-Din Aybak founded the Delhi Sultanate; with the death of the Ghurid Muizz al-Din, an independent sultanate was born

1206

After the Ghurid Sultan Muizz al-Din Muhammad b. Sam was murdered in 1206, the Turkish commander Qutb al-Din Aybak took the throne and the Ghurid territories in India became an independent sultanate (the Mamluk/Slave dynasty). The real founder of the sultanate, however, is considered to be his son-in-law Iltutmish.
