Council of Chalcedon: Dioscorus, Patriarch of Alexandria, was condemned; the Church of Egypt separated from the imperial Church

8 October 451

When the Council of Chalcedon condemned Dioscorus, the Patriarch of Alexandria in Egypt, most Egyptian Christians did not accept its decisions; thus in Egypt the Coptic (Egyptian) Church and the imperial Church (the Melkites) parted ways. This division laid the ground for Egypt's political estrangement from Byzantium in the following centuries.
