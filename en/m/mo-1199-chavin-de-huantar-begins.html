Chavín de Huántar: begins

1199 BC

A ceremonial centre in the Andes at Ancash; stamped burnished black pottery (Janabarroide) is concentrated in the range 800–500 BCE (calibrated); according to Rick et al., the centre loses its temple function around 500 BCE, and major construction had declined before that.
