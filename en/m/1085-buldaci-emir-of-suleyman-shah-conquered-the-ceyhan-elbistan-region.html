Buldaci, emir of Suleyman Shah, conquered the Ceyhan (Elbistan) region

1085

The Ceyhan (Elbistan) region, a target of the raids of the Turkmen ghazis, was conquered in 1085 by Buldaci, one of the emirs of the Anatolian Seljuk Sultan Suleyman Shah.
