Cannae: Hannibal encircled and destroyed the Roman army; southern Italy began to go over to Carthage

215 BC

In 216 at Cannae in Apulia, Hannibal's encirclement destroyed a Roman army of about seventy thousand men. Capua, Tarentum and many cities of southern Italy went over to the Carthaginian side.
