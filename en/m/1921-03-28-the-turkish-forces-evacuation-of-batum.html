The Turkish forces' evacuation of Batum

28 March 1921

When the British withdrew from the Caucasus in July 1920 they had evacuated Batum, and the Georgian government had seized the city. According to the TDV article on Batum, the Treaty of Moscow signed with Soviet Russia on 16 March 1921 left Batum to Georgia. Turkish forces evacuated the city on 28 March 1921, and Batum became the centre of the Autonomous Republic of Adjara, established within Georgia.
