The conquest of Szolnok and the failure of the siege of Eger

4 September 1552

After the capture of Timișoara, the two Ottoman armies, having united, took Szolnok on the Tisza almost without fighting, as the mercenary guards abandoned the fortress. The fortress of Eger, besieged next, however, could not be taken in the face of the thirty-eight-day resistance of the two-thousand-man garrison under István Dobó, and the army withdrew as winter set in. That in the same campaign one fortress fell easily while the other held out was the first clear sign that the frontier in Hungary no longer advanced of its own accord in the Ottomans' favour.
