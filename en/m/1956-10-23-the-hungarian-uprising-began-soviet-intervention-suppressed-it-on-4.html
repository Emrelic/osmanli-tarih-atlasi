The Hungarian Uprising began — Soviet intervention suppressed it on 4 November

23 October 1956

Excessive repression led to a popular uprising on 23 October 1956; Soviet troops intervened on 4 November. Territory and borders did not change; the regime continued under Kádár (macaristan-halk).
