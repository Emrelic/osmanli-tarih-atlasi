Darius crossed the Ister (Danube) and set out on the Scythian campaign; Thrace was added to the Persian satrapies

512 BC

Darius crossed into Thrace by a bridge over the Bosphorus, subjugated the Getae, crossed the Danube and set out on a campaign against the Scythians. Although he could not defeat the Scythians, Megabazus brought Thrace and the Macedonian coast under Persian sovereignty.
