The Death of Selim the Grim at Çorlu

21 September 1520

Selim I, who in eight years had more than doubled the territory of the state, set out from Istanbul on 18 July 1520 for a new campaign; because of a large tumour that appeared on his back he could not go beyond Çorlu. Despite the physicians' intervention his illness worsened, and after a hopeless treatment lasting two months he died there on 8-9 Shawwal 926 (21-22 September 1520). He had stopped Iran with Chaldiran, Marj Dabiq and Ridaniya, had eliminated the Mamluk state, and had brought the caliphate and the guardianship of the Two Holy Sanctuaries (Haramayn) to the Ottomans. He left behind a single male child: Süleyman.
