Tiryaki Hasan Pasha's Defence of Kanizsa

10 September 1601

Archduke Ferdinand and his allies, wishing to retake Kanizsa, which had been conquered a year earlier, besieged the fortress for more than two months; Tiryaki Hasan Pasha resisted with his force of 9,000 men. Despite receiving no help from outside, he repulsed the general assaults and, with sorties, forced the enemy to withdraw after heavy losses. The defence became epic in Ottoman historiography, and the 'Siege of Kanizsa' became a literary genre in its own right.
