The First Entry into Tabriz — The Opening of the Campaign of the Two Iraqs

13 July 1534

Ibrahim Pasha, who moved ahead in the capacity of serdar-ı ekrem, entered the Safavid capital Tabriz without encountering resistance, as Shah Tahmasb avoided a pitched battle and withdrew into the interior. Süleyman the Lawgiver also reached the city in September; however, because of the onset of winter and the excessive lengthening of the supply line, the army turned in the direction of Baghdad, and Tabriz could not be held for long. The city, taken for the second time after Chaldiran, became the place where the Ottomans learned that in the east taking is easy and holding is hard.
