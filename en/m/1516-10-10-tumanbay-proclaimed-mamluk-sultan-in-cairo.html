Tumanbay proclaimed Mamluk sultan in Cairo

10 October 1516

After Qansuh al-Ghawri, who died at Marj Dabiq, the Mamluk emirs raised Tumanbay, who was to be the last sultan of the state, to the throne. The new sultan of Circassian origin took over a state that had lost Syria, whose treasury was exhausted and which lacked firearms. Nevertheless, by establishing a line of defence before Cairo he would organize a last resistance against the Ottoman advance.
