Sale of Alanya to the Mamluk Sultan Barsbay

1427

Alanya, which Karamanoğlu Mecdüddin Mahmud Beg had seized in 1293, had become a separate principality governed by a branch of the Karamanids; Ibn Battuta records in 1333 that the administration was in the hands of Karamanoğlu Yusuf Beg. In 1427 Karaman b. Savcı Beg sold the city to the Mamluk Sultan Barsbay for five thousand gold pieces, and Alanya came under Mamluk administration. This key port of the Mediterranean thus became a Mamluk-held obstacle on the Ottoman road opening to the south; when Gedik Ahmed Pasha besieged it in 1471, the last bey, Kılıçarslan, surrendered the city.
