Julius Caesar had Corinth refounded as a Roman colony

43 BC

Shortly before Caesar's assassination, Roman citizen colonists (mostly freedmen) were settled at Corinth; the city became one of the important centres of the Roman province of Achaea. The decision to refound dates from 44 BC, the settlement from 44/43 BC.
