Cerro Sechín: Cerro Sechín: earliest calibrated dates

2269 BC

A central platform (phases 1–4) and a stone façade with reliefs in the Casma valley; the oldest dated examples are around 2400–2040 BC; the fill layers in which the reliefs were buried are ~1740–1433 BC.
