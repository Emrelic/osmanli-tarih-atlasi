With the annexation of Danishmendid lands by the Seljuks, Kırşehir again passed to Seljuk administration

1173

Kırşehir, which had passed for a time into the hands of the Danishmendids, again passed to Seljuk administration when Danishmendid lands were annexed by the Seljuks (1173) in the time of Kilij Arslan II.
