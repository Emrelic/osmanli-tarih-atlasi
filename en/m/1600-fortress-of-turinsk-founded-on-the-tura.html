Fortress of Turinsk founded on the Tura

1600

In 1600 the Russian Cossack-yasak fortress (ostrog) of Turinsk was founded in the area of present-day Turinsk; the fortress is one of the way-station fortifications of Russia's advance in Western Siberia.
