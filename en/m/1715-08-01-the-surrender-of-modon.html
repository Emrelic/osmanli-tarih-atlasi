The surrender of Modon

1 August 1715

After the fall of Nauplia, the fortress of Modon at the southwestern tip of the Morea was besieged by the army from the land and by the fleet from the sea, and surrendered in a short time. This port, which Venice had used for two hundred years in its eastern Mediterranean campaigns, was the obligatory stopping point where ships took on water and provisions. Its loss ended the Venetian fleet's freedom of movement in the Aegean.
