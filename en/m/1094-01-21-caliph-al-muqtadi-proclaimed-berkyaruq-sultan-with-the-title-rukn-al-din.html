Caliph al-Muqtadi proclaimed Berkyaruq sultan with the title 'Rukn al-Din'

21 January 1094

After the revolt of Isma'il ibn Yaquti, Malik of Azerbaijan, was suppressed (Sha'ban 486 / September 1093), Berkyaruq went to Baghdad, was proclaimed sultan by Caliph al-Muqtadi-Biamrillah, and the khutba was read in his name (487/1094).
