Death of Kınalızade Ali Çelebi: author of the Akhlaq-i Alai, the classic text of Ottoman moral philosophy

22 January 1572

Kınalızade Ali Çelebi, author of the Akhlaq-i Alai, the best-known classic text of Ottoman moral philosophy, died on 22 January 1572 while serving as kazasker of Anatolia. More than seventy copies of his work are found in Istanbul libraries.
