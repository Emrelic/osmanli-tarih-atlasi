The Edirne Incident began

17 July 1703

According to the TDV article on the 'Edirne Vak'ası', the movement, which began with the rising of soldiers who could not receive their ulufes, quickly turned into a wide rebellion in which Janissaries, madrasa students, tradesmen and the people took part. The real target of the rebellion was Şeyhülislam Feyzullah Efendi, who was alleged to interfere excessively in the affairs of state. A crowd of about 60,000 people marched from Istanbul to Edirne.
