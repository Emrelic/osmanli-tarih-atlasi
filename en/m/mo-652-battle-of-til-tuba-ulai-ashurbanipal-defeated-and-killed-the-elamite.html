Battle of Til-Tuba/Ulai: Ashurbanipal defeated and killed the Elamite king Teumman

652 BC

Ashurbanipal defeated and killed the Elamite king Teumman on the bank of the Ulai (Karun/Karkheh) near Susa; his head was carried to Nineveh. The battle prepared the end of the role Elam played in the Assyrian-Babylonian rivalry.
