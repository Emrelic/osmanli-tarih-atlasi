The Livonian Order transferred the Klaipėda (Memel) lands to the Prussian branch of the Teutonic Order

1328

The Livonian Order ceded the fortress of Klaipėda (Memel) at the mouth of the Neman and its surrounding lands to the Prussian branch of the Teutonic Order; the bishop of Courland also transferred his share to the same branch in 1392. Klaipėda remained with the Order through the peaces of Toruń in 1411 and Melno in 1422, and passed to the Duchy of Prussia in 1525.
