Ashurbanipal died; a struggle for the throne and a revolt in Babylon began in Assyria

630 BC

Ashurbanipal's death left Assyria divided between two heirs. The Babylonian governors rebelled against Sin-šum-lišir and Sin-šar-iškun; the collapse of the empire began within ten years.
