Burning of the first plaza of San Martin Tilcajete by Monte Alban: the plaza is burned (Monte Alban attack)

374 BC

At the end of Early Monte Alban I, the first plaza of the rival center Tilcajete was burned; afterwards a new plaza was built on a more defensible ridge. Its palace (Structure 7) burned with calibrated dates in the range 100 BCE-140 CE, and its temple (Structure 16) in the range 165 BCE-155 CE.
