The Mongol armies entered Khwarazm-Shah territory; fortified places such as Otrar, Sighnaq and Khujand fell

1219

Toward the end of 1219 the Mongol armies entered the lands of the Khwarazm-Shahs; the fortified places of Transoxiana fell one by one, and the places that resisted were subjected to massacre (such as Otrar, Sighnaq and Khujand). In the atlas, Mongol ownership of Khujand begins in 1219.
