Flamininus proclaimed the freedom of the Greeks at the Isthmian Games

195 BC

After Cynoscephalae, the Roman commander Titus Quinctius Flamininus proclaimed at the Isthmian Games of 196 BC that the Macedonian garrisons at Corinth, Chalcis and Demetrias would be withdrawn and that the Greek cities were "free, untaxed and subject to their own laws". Rome thus established itself as arbiter in Greece.
