Tyre passed to Antigonus: Phoenicia was taken from Ptolemy

313 BC

In 315 Antigonus entered Syria, took Phoenicia and besieged Tyre; the city fell in 314. The Phoenician coast and the coast as far as Gaza came into his hands (Gaza was given back to Ptolemy in 312 in the Battle of Gaza).
