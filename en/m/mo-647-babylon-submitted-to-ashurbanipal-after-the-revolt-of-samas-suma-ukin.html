Babylon submitted to Ashurbanipal after the revolt of Šamaš-šuma-ukin

647 BC

Ashurbanipal's brother, the Babylonian king Šamaš-šuma-ukin, rebelled against Assyria in 652; his Elamite and Arab allies gave temporary support. The war, which lasted four years, ended with the fall of Babylon, and Šamaš-šuma-ukin died.
