Zhou Shizong (Chai Rong) died; the Prince of Liang was proclaimed emperor before the coffin

27 July 959

The Later Zhou emperor Shizong died at the age of 39 in the Wanshui Hall; the next day his last will was read and the Prince of Liang was proclaimed emperor before the coffin.
