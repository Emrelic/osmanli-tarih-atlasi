Los Morteros (Chao Valley, Peru) - the oldest adobe monumental architecture: the oldest adobe monumental architecture in the Americas

3749 BC

The oldest known adobe monumental structure on the northern Peruvian coast (Chao Valley); the study dates the 'invention of adobe architecture before 5,100 calendar years B.P.' It is linked to El Niño rains.
