Çelebi Mehmed's campaign against the Candaroğulları: Çankırı was given to Kasım Bey, who was under Ottoman protection

18 February 1417

Çelebi Mehmed marched against the Candaroğlu İsfendiyar Bey, who had helped Sheikh Bedreddin cross over to Wallachia, advanced to Kastamonu, and besieged Sinop, where İsfendiyar had withdrawn. İsfendiyar recognized Ottoman suzerainty; with the Ilgaz mountain accepted as the boundary, Çankırı and its surroundings were given to his son Kasım Bey, who had taken refuge with the Ottomans.
