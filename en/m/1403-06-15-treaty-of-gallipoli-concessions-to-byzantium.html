Treaty of Gallipoli — concessions to Byzantium

15 June 1403

Süleyman Çelebi, wishing to secure his position in Rumelia, came to terms with Byzantium and the Latin states and returned Thessalonica, the Marmara coasts and certain islands; he even agreed to pay tribute.
