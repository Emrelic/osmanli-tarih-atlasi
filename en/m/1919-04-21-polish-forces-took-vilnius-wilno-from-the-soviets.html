Polish forces took Vilnius (Wilno) from the Soviets

21 April 1919

Józef Piłsudski's troops took Wilno from the Red Army in April 1919; Lithuania continued to claim the city, and the Vilnius question poisoned Lithuanian-Polish relations in the interwar period.
