The retaking of Herceg Novi (Castelnuovo) by Barbarossa Hayreddin Pasha

10 August 1539

The Ottomans, who after the victory at Preveza had gained superiority in the central Mediterranean as well, turned to the fortress of Nova (Castelnuovo) on the Adriatic coast, which Andrea Doria had taken a year earlier. At the end of the operation carried out jointly by Barbarossa Hayreddin Pasha and Gazi Hüsrev Bey, Bey of Bosnia, the fortress was retaken and the occupation ended.
