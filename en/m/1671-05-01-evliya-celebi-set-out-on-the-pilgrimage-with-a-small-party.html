Evliya Çelebi set out on the pilgrimage with a small party

1 May 1671

In May 1671 he set out from Istanbul with his private retinue, without a large caravan. He first toured Bursa, Kütahya, Afyon and İzmir, then the Aegean islands and the Menteşe coast.
