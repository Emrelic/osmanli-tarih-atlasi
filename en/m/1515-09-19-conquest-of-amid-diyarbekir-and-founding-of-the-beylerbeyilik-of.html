Conquest of Amid (Diyarbekir) and founding of the beylerbeyilik of Diyarbekir

19 September 1515

After Idris-i Bitlisi won over the Sunni Kurdish lords of the region to the Ottomans, the Safavid forces could not withstand a long siege, and the keys of Amid were handed over to Bıyıklı Mehmed Pasha. The city was made the centre of the newly founded beylerbeyilik of Diyarbekir. The administrative core of Upper Mesopotamia thus passed into Ottoman hands.
