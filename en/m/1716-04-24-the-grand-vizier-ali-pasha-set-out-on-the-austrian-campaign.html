The Grand Vizier Ali Pasha Set Out on the Austrian Campaign

24 April 1716

After war was declared on Austria, the serdâr-ı ekrem Ali Pasha set out on the Austrian campaign with an army of over 100,000, while the fleet turned toward Corfu the same summer. The army would march from Belgrade upon Petrovaradin, and the grand vizier would be martyred in the defeat at the beginning of August.
