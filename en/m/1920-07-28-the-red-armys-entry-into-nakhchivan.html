The Red Army's entry into Nakhchivan

28 July 1920

The British who entered the region at the beginning of 1919 had dissolved the Aras Republic and had left Nakhchivan in the summer of the same year. According to the TDV article on Nakhchivan, the Red Army entered Nakhchivan on 28 July 1920, gave the administration to the Communists, and the Nakhchivan Soviet Socialist Republic was founded. The status of the region was determined by the treaty signed at Kars in 1921 as an autonomous unit attached to Azerbaijan.
