The Plane Tree Incident (Vak'a-i Vakvakiye)

4 March 1656

According to the TDV Encyclopaedia of Islam article on the Çınar Vak'ası, the Janissaries and sipahis who returned from the Cretan campaign without pay rose up in Istanbul and demanded the execution of the statesmen they accused of corruption. Grand Vizier Zurnazen Mustafa Pasha's proposal of confiscation and exile was not accepted; first three officials, then nearly thirty statesmen were executed, and their bodies were hung from the plane trees in the Sultanahmet Square. The event was therefore called the Çınar Vak'ası.
