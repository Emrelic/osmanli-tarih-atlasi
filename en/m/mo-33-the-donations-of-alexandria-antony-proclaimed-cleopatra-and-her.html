The Donations of Alexandria: Antony proclaimed Cleopatra and her children rulers of the eastern kingdoms

33 BC

Antony, having golden thrones set up for the crowd assembled in the gymnasium, proclaimed Cleopatra queen of Egypt, Cyprus, Libya and Coele-Syria, Caesarion her co-ruler, and her children kings of Armenia, Media, Parthia and Phoenicia-Syria-Cilicia. In Rome this ceremony turned into propaganda against Antony and hastened the civil war.
