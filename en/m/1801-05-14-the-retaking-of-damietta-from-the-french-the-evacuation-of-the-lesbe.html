The retaking of Damietta from the French — the evacuation of the Lesbe fortress

14 May 1801

A force under İbrahim Pasha from the grand vizier's army was sent to Damietta. On the morning of 14 May, while the assault on the Lesbe fortress that protected Damietta was being prepared, it was seen that the fortress had been evacuated by the French and the garrison had withdrawn; two small forts dependent on it were also taken. Damietta became the second Nile-mouth city to pass out of French control after Rosetta, which had fallen in April.
