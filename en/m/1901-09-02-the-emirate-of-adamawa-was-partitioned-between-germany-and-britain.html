The Emirate of Adamawa was partitioned between Germany and Britain

2 September 1901

The Emirate of Adamawa (Fombina), dependent on the Sokoto Caliphate, was split in two at the end of the German and British colonial forces' campaigns of 1899-1901: the eastern part (including Garoua, Banyo, Marua, Ngaoundéré) was joined to German Kamerun, the western part (capital Yola) to British Northern Nigeria; Emir Zubayr was sent into exile. With this event Rey Bouba (Adamawa (German share)) passed to German Kamerun administration.
