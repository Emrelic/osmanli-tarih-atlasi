The exchange of the Jassy instruments of ratification — the peace became final in international law

10 February 1792

The sovereigns' instruments of ratification were exchanged on 10 February 1792, and the peace acquired finality in terms of the law of nations as well. The five-year, two-front war was thus formally closed; the loss of the Crimea became final and the new frontier of the Ottoman State became the Turla (Dniester).
