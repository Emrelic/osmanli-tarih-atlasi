Loss of Anabolu (Nauplion) to Venice — turning point of the Morean War

30 August 1686

According to the TDV article on Anabolu, during the siege the surviving Muslim inhabitants of the city fled to the neighbouring islands and to Anatolia (1686). According to the standard academic source (Setton, Venice, Austria, and the Turks in the Seventeenth Century), Königsmarck's forces repelled the attacks of the Ottoman relief army for two weeks; when İsmail Pasha's assault on 29 August ended in heavy defeat, the fortress surrendered within a few days. Anabolu was Ottoman territory taken over by treaty in 1540; with its loss the passage of the Morea peninsula to Venice was hastened.
