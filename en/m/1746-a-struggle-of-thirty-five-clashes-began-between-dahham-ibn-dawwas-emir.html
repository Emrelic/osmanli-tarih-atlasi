A struggle of thirty-five clashes began between Dahham ibn Dawwas, emir of Riyadh, and the Saudi forces

1746

Between 1746 and 1773 Dahham ibn Dawwas, emir of Riyadh, and the forces loyal to the Saud family faced each other thirty-five times; at the end of the struggle Riyadh fell to the Saudis.
