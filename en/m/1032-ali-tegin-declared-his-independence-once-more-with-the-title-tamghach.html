Ali Tegin declared his independence once more with the title Tamghach Bughra Qara Khaqan

1032

Ali Tegin declared his independence in 423 (1032) with the title Tamghach Bughra Qara Khaqan; the dispatch by Mas'ud of Ghazna of the Khwarazmshah Altuntash against Ali Tegin ended in the Battle of Dabusiyya (spring of 423/1032).
