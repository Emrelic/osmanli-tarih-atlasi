The ceremonial exchange of the Sistova instruments of ratification

31 August 1791

The instruments of ratification of the peace with Austria were exchanged with ceremony on 31 August 1791; after this the grand vizier set out for the peace negotiations on the Russian front. Thus the Austrian front that had opened in 1788 was entirely closed.
