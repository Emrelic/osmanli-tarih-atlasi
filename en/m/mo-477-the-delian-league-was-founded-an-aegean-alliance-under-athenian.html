The Delian League was founded: an Aegean alliance under Athenian leadership

477 BC

After Pausanias' manner of command, the Ionian cities asked Athens for leadership; a common treasury was established on Delos with the tribute fixed by Aristides (460 talents). The League gave Athens a permanent fleet and a tax base across the Aegean.
