The fall of Niš to Austria — Seckendorf's Serbian operation

27 July 1737

The Serbian column of the Austrian army, which entered Ottoman lands in three columns in the summer of 1737, descended south through the Morava valley under Marshal Seckendorf and captured Niš. The fall of the fortress was the only great success of the war in Austria's favor; but in October of the same year the beylerbeyi of Rumelia, Köprülüzâde Hâfız Ahmed Pasha, retook Niš without a fight.
