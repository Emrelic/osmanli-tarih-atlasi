Annexation of Amasya to the Ottoman lands

1 June 1393

During Yıldırım Bayezid's campaign in Central Anatolia, the besieged emir of Amasya surrendered the city to the Ottomans, and Bayezid entered the city in 1393. The Sultan left its administration to his son Mehmed Çelebi; as a princely sanjak, Amasya henceforth became one of the most important training grounds of the Ottoman dynasty. Other settlements lost on the same date: Ladik (Amasya), Merzifon, Osmancık, Çorum. This choice would prove decisive in the later Interregnum, when Çelebi Mehmed would conduct his struggle to reunite the state from this very Amasya base.
