The loss of Shahrizor — Sührâb, the lord of Erdelân, inclined toward the Safavids

1550

The Shahrizor region, which had recognised Ottoman administration during the Campaign of the Two Iraqs (1535), was governed by Bige Bey, the emir of Erdelân, who resided in the fortress of Zalm. Following the death of Bige Bey in 1550, his brother Sührâb, who seized the fortress of Zalm and took control of the principality, turned toward the Safavids and broke away from obedience to the Ottomans; the Shahrizor region thus passed out of Ottoman influence. The region was regained only during the Nakhchivan campaign, in 1554, with the siege and capture of the fortress of Zalm.
