Seleucus I founded Antioch (Antakya)

299 BC

After Ipsus, Seleucus founded Antioch on the banks of the Orontes in Syria, naming it after his father; the city became the western capital of the Seleucid Kingdom and one of the greatest cities of Antiquity. Seleucia on the Tigris had been founded earlier as the eastern capital.
