The first loss of Belgrade — Holy League siege

6 September 1688

Belgrade, which had been in Ottoman hands since 1521, surrendered to the Holy League army; the gates of Serbia were opened to the enemy and the danger reached the Niš-Sofia line. Another settlement passing from the Ottomans to the Habsburgs on the same day in the atlas: Hisarcık (Grocka) — follows Belgrade's senior-center chain (TDV belgrad; declared approximation).
