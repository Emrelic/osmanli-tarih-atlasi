The loss of Várad

5 June 1692

Várad was the key fortress opening onto Transylvania, taken in 1660 by Köprülü Mehmed Pasha and made the centre of a province bearing its name. The fortress, left entirely alone after the Salankamen debacle, surrendered from hunger after a blockade lasting nearly two years. With its loss Ottoman influence over Transylvania effectively ended, and Habsburg domination settled east of the Tisza.
