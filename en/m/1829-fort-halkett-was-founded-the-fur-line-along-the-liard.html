Fort Halkett was founded — the fur line along the Liard

1829

The Hudson's Bay Company founded Fort Halkett on the Liard River and thereby extended the fur line from the Mackenzie basin to the west of the Rockies. The post was one of the company's westernmost advanced stations in Kaska Dene territory.
