Cigalazade Sinan Pasha set out on the Iranian campaign

15 June 1604

While the two-front war with Iran and Austria continued, Shah Abbas had besieged and taken Yerevan and entered Kars, but could be stopped only before Akhaltsikhe. Cigalazade Sinan Pasha, appointed commander of the East, set out from Istanbul late and reached the front of Kars on 8 November. Although circumstances were favourable, he did not march against the shah but wintered in Van, and upon the shah's attack he moved to Erzurum; a campaign season was wasted.
