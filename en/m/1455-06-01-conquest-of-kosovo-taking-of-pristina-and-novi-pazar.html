Conquest of Kosovo: taking of Pristina and Novi Pazar

1 June 1455

The Ottoman army, marching on the Serbian Despotate, took Pristina on the plain of Kosovo and Novi Pazar (Yenipazar), the centre of the Raška region; in the same operation Novo Brdo, famed for its silver mines, was also taken. The southern half of Serbia was thus attached to direct Ottoman administration, and the despotate at Smederevo was cut off from its central lands. This conquest was the decisive step on the road to the complete annexation of Serbia four years later.
