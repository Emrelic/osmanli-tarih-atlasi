The British evacuation of Corsica: the island again became France's

1 October 1796

Britain, squeezed in the Mediterranean by Bonaparte's Italian victories and Spain's going over to the French side, evacuated Corsica. The Anglo-Corsican Kingdom ended and the island was again attached to France.
