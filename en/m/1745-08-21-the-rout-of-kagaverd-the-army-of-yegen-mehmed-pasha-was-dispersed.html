The rout of Kağaverd — the army of Yeğen Mehmed Pasha was dispersed

21 August 1745

In the year after the siege of Kars, Nadir Shah routed the Ottoman forces under the command of Yeğen Mehmed Pasha at the locality of Kağaverd. He then returned to Isfahan. His giving up the insistence on the recognition of Jaʿfarism opened the way to the Treaty of Kerden the following year.
