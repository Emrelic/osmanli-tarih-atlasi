Defeat at Lepanto

7 October 1571

The Crusader fleet, united after the conquest of Cyprus, caught the Ottoman fleet in the Gulf of Lepanto and destroyed the greater part of it; the grand admiral (kaptan-ı deryâ) Müezzinzâde Ali Pasha fell as a martyr and only the squadron of Uluç Ali was saved. After the disaster, Grand Vizier Sokollu Mehmed Pasha appointed Uluç Ali Pasha as grand admiral, charging him with gathering the scattered fleet, and began the construction of a new fleet in all the shipyards from Istanbul to Alanya; yet absolute supremacy in the Mediterranean had been psychologically shaken.
