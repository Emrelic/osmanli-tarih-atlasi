Trogir came under Venetian rule

22 June 1420

During Venice's war with Hungary, with the arrival of the fleet of the captain-general Piero Loredan, Trogir passed to Venetian rule on 22 June 1420; the last of the Dalmatian cities to join Venice, it remained under the Venetian flag until 1797.
