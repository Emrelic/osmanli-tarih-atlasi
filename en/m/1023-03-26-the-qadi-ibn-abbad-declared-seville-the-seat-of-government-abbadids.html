The qadi Ibn Abbad declared Seville the seat of government (Abbadids)

26 March 1023

The qadi Abu'l-Qasim Ibn Abbad declared Seville the seat of government in 414 (1023); the Abbadid taifa emirate became one of the most important centres of al-Andalus.
