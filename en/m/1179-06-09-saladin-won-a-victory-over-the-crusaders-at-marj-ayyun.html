Saladin won a victory over the Crusaders at Marj Ayyun

9 June 1179

Saladin won a victory over the Crusaders at Marj Ayyun on 2 Muharram 575 (9 June 1179); he made good the consequences of the defeat in the Gaza-Ascalon raid of 1177.
