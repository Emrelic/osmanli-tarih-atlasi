Italy annexed the Eritrean highlands, including Asmara and Keren

1889

Italy, exploiting the power vacuum born of the death of the Ethiopian Emperor Yohannes II in 1889, effectively annexed the Eritrean highlands, including Asmara and Keren, after the Treaty of Wuchale (2 May 1889); on 1 January 1890 a colony was officially proclaimed under the name 'Eritrea'. With this event Keren passed to Italian administration.
