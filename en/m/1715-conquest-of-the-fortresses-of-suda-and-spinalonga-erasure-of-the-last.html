Conquest of the fortresses of Suda and Spinalonga — erasure of the last Venetian trace in Crete

1715

Twenty-three years after the fall of Grambousa in 1692, with a side branch of the Morea campaign, Suda and Spinalonga, the last two Venetian strongholds in Crete, were also conquered. Thus the conquest of Crete, begun in 1645, was completed down to the last inch of the island at the end of forty-six years.
