The Mongol invasion does not reach Novgorod, but the city recognises Golden Horde overlordship and pays tribute

1239

The Tatar invasion of 1239-40 did not come as far as Novgorod; however, the city was obliged to recognise Golden Horde overlordship and pay tribute to the khan. This relative independence allowed it to prosper through Hanseatic trade.
