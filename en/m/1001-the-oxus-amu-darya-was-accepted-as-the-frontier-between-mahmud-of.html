The Oxus (Amu Darya) was accepted as the frontier between Mahmud of Ghazna and the Karakhanid Nasr ibn Ali

1001

After taking Samanid Bukhara, the Karakhanid Nasr ibn Ali exchanged messages of friendship with Mahmud; the Oxus was accepted as the frontier between the two states (391/1001).
