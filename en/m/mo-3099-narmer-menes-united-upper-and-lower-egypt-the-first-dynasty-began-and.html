Narmer (Menes) united Upper and Lower Egypt; the First Dynasty began and Memphis became the capital

3099 BC

Narmer, the ruler of Upper Egypt (the area around Thinis/Abydos), is according to most Egyptologists the same person as Menes. By bringing Lower Egypt under his rule, the Nile Valley and the Delta became a single state for the first time. According to the old account, Menes founded the site of Memphis by diverting the course of the Nile; the unification is not a single event but the result of a long process continuing from Naqada III.
