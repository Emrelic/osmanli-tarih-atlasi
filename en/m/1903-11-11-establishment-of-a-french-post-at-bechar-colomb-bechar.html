Establishment of a French post at Béchar (Colomb-Béchar)

11 November 1903

The oasis of Béchar on the Moroccan border was taken by French forces on 11 November 1903 and made into a forward post under the name Colomb-Béchar. This step, coming two years after the annexation of the Touat belt, is among the last links of France's advance from Algeria toward Morocco; it opens the road to the establishment of the protectorate over Morocco in 1912.
