Ardashir I attacked Roman Mesopotamia; the Roman-Sasanian wars began

231

Ardashir I, who overthrew the Parthians, attacked the Nisibis and Carrhae region in 230-231 and began the struggle with Rome that would last for centuries; Severus Alexander's campaign of 232 was inconclusive.
