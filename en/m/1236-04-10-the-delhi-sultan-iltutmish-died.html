The Delhi Sultan Iltutmish died

10 April 1236

Iltutmish, who gave the Delhi Sultanate its true character as a state, fell ill before the campaign he had begun against the Isma'ilis and died at Delhi (Shaban 633 / April 1236). After his death the sultanate experienced political turmoil for some thirty years.
