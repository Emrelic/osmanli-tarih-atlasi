Gordion — Tumulus MM (Midas Mound): Midas Tumulus (MM): felling year of the trees of the burial chamber (dendrochronology)

739 BC

The largest Phrygian tumulus at Gordion; the felling year is dated by tree rings of the inner chamber timber (juniper) (the bark year is present), the felling ≈ the construction year of the tumulus.
