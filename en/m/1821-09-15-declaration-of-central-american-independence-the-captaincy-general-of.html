Declaration of Central American Independence — the Captaincy General of Guatemala separated from Spain

15 September 1821

Following the Plan of Iguala in Mexico, the assembly that met in Guatemala declared the independence of the Captaincy General from Spain; the captain general Gabino Gaínza remained at the head of the transitional government. Thus Spanish rule came to an end in the lands of present-day Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua and Costa Rica. After being briefly annexed to the Mexican Empire, the region founded the United Provinces of Central America in 1823.
