Tarentum surrendered to Rome: southern Italy was added to Rome

271 BC

After Pyrrhus's departure the Epirote garrison of Tarentum withdrew and the city surrendered to Rome (272). Together with Bruttium and Lucania, the Greek cities of southern Italy became Rome's allies; thus Rome became master of the Italian peninsula as far as the Po plain.
