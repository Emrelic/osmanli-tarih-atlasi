The Abbasid Caliph al-Mustansir Billah sent Iltutmish a robe of honor and a diploma of investiture and recognized him as sultan of India

1229

In 626 (1229) the Abbasid Caliph al-Mustansir Billah sent a delegation and granted Iltutmish a robe of honor, a diploma of investiture, and the titles 'al-sultan al-mu'azzam nasir amir al-mu'minin' and 'yamin khalifat Allah'; this was the first sign that an Indian ruler had been recognized by the Abbasid caliph as sultan of India.
