The loss of Lika and Krbava — Udbina and Gospić passed into the hands of the Habsburg frontier forces

1689

In the war that continued after the Vienna debacle, the Croatian frontier troops under Herberstein, general of Karlovac, took Udbina in 1689; Gospić also left Ottoman administration in the same year. Ottoman Lika, which had lasted more than 160 years, came to an end, the emptied lands were settled with a new Croatian population, and Udbina became an important fortress of frontier defence against the Ottomans until the Peace of Sistova (1791).
