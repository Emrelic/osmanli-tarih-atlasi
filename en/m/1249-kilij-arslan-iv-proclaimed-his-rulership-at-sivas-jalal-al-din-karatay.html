Kilij Arslan IV proclaimed his rulership at Sivas; Jalal al-Din Karatay proclaimed the three brothers sultans jointly (joint sultanate 1249-1254)

1249

Kilij Arslan IV, who had received a yarligh from Güyük Khan, proclaimed his rulership at Sivas in 1249; to resolve the crisis, Emir Jalal al-Din Karatay proclaimed Izz al-Din Kaykaus II, Rukn al-Din Kilij Arslan IV and Ala al-Din Kayqubad II sultans jointly (1249-1254).
