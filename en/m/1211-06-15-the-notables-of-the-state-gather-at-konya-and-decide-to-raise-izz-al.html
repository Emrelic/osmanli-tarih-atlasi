The notables of the state gather at Konya and decide to raise Izz al-Din Kaykawus, Malik of Malatya, to the Seljuk throne; Kaykawus I is proclaimed sultan at Kayseri

15 June 1211

On the death of Kaykhusraw I (608/1211), the notables of the state, because he was the eldest son, decided to raise Izz al-Din Kaykawus, Malik of Malatya, to the throne and sent a messenger for him to come to Kayseri; his brother Ala al-Din Kayqubad did not recognise his sultanate.
