Bocskai's forces took Szatmár

21 January 1605

As the Bocskai uprising spread to northeastern Hungary, Gyulaffy took Szatmár, which he had besieged, on 21 January 1605. The Peace of Vienna of 23 June 1606 left the county of Szatmár to Bocskai for life; since there was no male heir, the county returned to the crown upon Bocskai's death on 29 December 1606.
