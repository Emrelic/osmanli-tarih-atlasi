Henry II's campaign of the Three Bishoprics: Toul was joined to France

1552

Toul, ruled by prince-bishops and dependent on the German Empire, was joined to France in 1552 (the Three Bishoprics together with Metz and Verdun); it was legally confirmed at Westphalia in 1648. The day of Metz (18 April 1552) is separate; for Toul there is no day in the source.
