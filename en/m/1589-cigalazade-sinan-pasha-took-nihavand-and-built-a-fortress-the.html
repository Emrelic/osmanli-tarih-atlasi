Cığalazâde Sinan Pasha took Nihavand and built a fortress — the submission of Luristan

1589

Cığalazâde Sinan Pasha, the governor of Baghdad, took Nihavand at the end of the year 1588 and had a fortress built there; Şâhverdi, the ruler of Luristan, submitted to the Ottomans. The sources give no day; the date is at the year level.
