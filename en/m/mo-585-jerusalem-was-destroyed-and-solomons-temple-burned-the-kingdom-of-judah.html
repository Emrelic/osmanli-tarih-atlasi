Jerusalem was destroyed and Solomon's Temple burned; the Kingdom of Judah came to an end and its people were deported to Babylon

585 BC

After Zedekiah's revolt against Babylon, Nebuchadnezzar took Jerusalem for the second time and burned it; the Temple was destroyed and most of the population was deported to Babylon (the Babylonian Captivity). The existence of Judah as an independent state ended.
