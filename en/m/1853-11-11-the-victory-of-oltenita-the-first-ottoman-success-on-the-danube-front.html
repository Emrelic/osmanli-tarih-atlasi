The Victory of Oltenița: The First Ottoman Success on the Danube Front

11 November 1853

In the opening phase of the Crimean War, the Serdâr-ı Ekrem Ömer Lütfi Pasha crossed the Danube and repulsed the Russian forces at Oltenița with heavy losses. By halting the Russian advance at the very beginning of the war, the victory showed Europe the strength the Ottoman army had gained through post-Tanzimat training. This success prepared the psychological ground that eased the decision of England and France to enter the war on the Ottoman side.
