Abolition of the Tarku shamkhalate: direct Russian administration

1726

The shamkhal of Tarku, Adil-Girey, who had hosted Peter in 1722 and entered Russian allegiance, rebelled in 1725; Yeropkin destroyed Tarku in the autumn of 1725, the shamkhal was arrested and exiled in mid-May 1726, and the shamkhalate was abolished. Tarku and its surroundings remained under direct Russian administration until the Treaty of Ganja in 1735.
