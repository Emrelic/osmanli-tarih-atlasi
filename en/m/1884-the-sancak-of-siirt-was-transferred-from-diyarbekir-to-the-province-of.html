The sancak of Siirt was transferred from Diyarbekir to the province of Bitlis — the district of Eruh and the village of Şırnak were also attached to Bitlis

1884

When the transition from the eyalet system to the vilayet system was made after the Tanzimat, the sancak of Siirt, which had remained attached to the vilayet of Diyarbekir, was transferred to the vilayet of Bitlis in 1884. According to the TDV article on Şırnak, the lands where present-day Şırnak stands thus lay within the district of Eruh, belonging to the sancak of Siirt of the vilayet of Bitlis. At that time Şırnak was a small village of the district of Eruh; its name appears in the records toward the end of the nineteenth century, while its becoming a district centre belongs to the first years of the Republic. These lands had been annexed to the Ottomans in 1514 after the Çaldıran campaign and had been administered within the sancak of Siirt of the eyalet of Diyarbekir.
