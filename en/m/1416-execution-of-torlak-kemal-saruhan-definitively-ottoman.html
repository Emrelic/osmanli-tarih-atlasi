Execution of Torlak Kemal — Saruhan definitively Ottoman

1416

Torlak Kemal, who led the western Anatolian wing of the movement of Sheikh Bedreddin, was defeated in the Manisa region and executed. After this date the Saruhan region came definitively under Ottoman control, and the beylik that Timur had revived in 1402 passed into history. The last political remnants of the Interregnum in western Anatolia were thus cleared away.
