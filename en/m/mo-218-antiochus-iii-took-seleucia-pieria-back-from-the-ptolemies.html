Antiochus III took Seleucia Pieria back from the Ptolemies

218 BC

Antiochus III besieged Seleucia Pieria, which was in Ptolemaic hands, and took it by bribing officers; Polybius writes that the city had been held since the time of Ptolemy III by a garrison belonging to the Egyptian kings. With this victory the Seleucids regained the port of Antioch and prepared to march on Coele-Syria (the Fourth Syrian War).
