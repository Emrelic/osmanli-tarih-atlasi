The death of Ahmed III

24 June 1736

Ahmed III, who was deposed in 1730 by the Patrona Halil rebellion, spent the last six years of his life in the apartment of Topkapı Palace set aside for deposed sultans and died at the age of sixty-three. He was buried at Bahçekapı in the tomb of his grandmother Turhan Sultan next to the Yeni Cami.
