Ghiyath al-Din Kay Khusraw took Antalya: the Seljuks reached the Mediterranean

5 March 1207

Antalya, which passed into the hands of the Italian Aldobrandini after the Latins' capture of Constantinople, was taken by the Anatolian Seljuk sultan Kay Khusraw I with the support of the Greeks, who were dissatisfied with Latin rule.
