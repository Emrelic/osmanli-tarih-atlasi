The Viceroyalty of New Spain was established

17 April 1535

Charles V, king of Spain, attached the conquered Mexican lands directly to royal administration, established the Viceroyalty of New Spain, and appointed Antonio de Mendoza as the first viceroy. This arrangement ended the order based on the personal disposal of the conquerors. The boundaries of the viceroyalty, whose center was in Mexico, comprised Mexico, the Yucatán peninsula and the regions of Guatemala.
