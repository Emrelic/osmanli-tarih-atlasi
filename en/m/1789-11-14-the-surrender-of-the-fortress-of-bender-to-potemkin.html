The surrender of the fortress of Bender to Potemkin

14 November 1789

In the Ottoman-Russian war of 1787-1792, the fortress of Bender on the Dniester surrendered to the Russian army under Potemkin's command. The fortress was returned to the Ottomans by the Treaty of Jassy of 1792.
