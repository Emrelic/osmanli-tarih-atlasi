Thirteen Hungarian generals were executed at Arad, Lajos Batthyány was shot in Pest

6 October 1849

By Haynau's order thirteen Hungarian generals were executed at Arad, and Batthyány, the first Hungarian prime minister, was shot in Pest. 6 October is a day of national mourning in Hungary today.
