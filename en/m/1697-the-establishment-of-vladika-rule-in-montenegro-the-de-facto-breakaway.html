The establishment of vladika rule in Montenegro — the de facto breakaway of Cetinje

1697

In the years when the Holy League War loosened Ottoman control in the Balkans, the metropolitanate at the head of the monastery of Cetinje became an office passing from father to son in the Petrović family, and the region effectively acquired a separate administration. Thanks to the inaccessibility of the mountains, this vladikate continued for two centuries in a position neither fully independent nor fully dependent; its independence would be recognised in law only by the 1878 Treaty of Berlin.
