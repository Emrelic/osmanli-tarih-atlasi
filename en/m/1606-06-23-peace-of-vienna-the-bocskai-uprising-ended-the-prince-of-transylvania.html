Peace of Vienna — the Bocskai uprising ended, the prince of Transylvania was recognized

23 June 1606

The Habsburg ruler recognized Bocskai's hereditary right over Transylvania and his administration of four counties in Royal Hungary, and confirmed the constitutional and confessional privileges of the Hungarian nobles. The Treaty of Zsitvatorok in November of the same year completed this peace.
