The Cisalpine Republic was ceremonially founded in Milan: Lombardy, Mantua, Bergamo, Brescia and the Cispadane territories united

9 July 1797

Lombardy, Mantua, Bergamo, Brescia, the Cispadane Republic (Modena, Reggio, Bologna, Ferrara) and the Romagna, all conquered by Napoleon, united in a single state.
