Ala al-Din Kayqubad I dies, poisoned by game meat; his son Ghiyath al-Din Kaykhusraw II succeeds him

31 May 1237

Kayqubad, who closed the age of Seljuk ascendancy, was poisoned by game meat he ate at the banquet he gave for envoys on the festival of Ramadan and died the next day (4 Shawwal 634 / 31 May 1237); through the efforts of the statesmen Kaykhusraw II took the throne in place of the heir apparent Izz al-Din Kilij Arslan.
