Conquest of Rhodes

21 December 1522

Rhodes, the base of the Knights of Saint John who threatened the sea routes of the Eastern Mediterranean and the pilgrimage route, surrendered by capitulation after a bloody siege lasting months; the knights were permitted to leave the island freely (they later settled in Malta). The security of the Anatolian coasts was ensured and the Istanbul-Egypt sea route came under uninterrupted Ottoman control.
