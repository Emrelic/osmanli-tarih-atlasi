Pacorus and Labienus invaded Syria and Palestine

39 BC

The Parthian prince Pacorus and Labienus, who had fled from Rome and taken refuge with the Parthians, invaded the Roman provinces of Syria and took Antioch and Palestine. Antony's general Ventidius defeated the Parthians at Gindarus in 38 and recovered the region.
