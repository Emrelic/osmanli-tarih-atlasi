French occupation of Muaskar (Mascara): loss of Abd al-Qadir's capital

1841

With Bugeaud's arrival as governor in 1841, French strategy changed: instead of clinging to the coast, to occupy the interior cities one by one. Mobile columns setting out from Oran, Mostaganem and Médéa took the centers of Abd al-Qadir's regular state; Mascara, a true capital with its mint, gunpowder workshop and granaries, was the most important of them. The emir thereafter gave up establishing a fixed center and turned his army into a nomadic camp called the 'smala.'
