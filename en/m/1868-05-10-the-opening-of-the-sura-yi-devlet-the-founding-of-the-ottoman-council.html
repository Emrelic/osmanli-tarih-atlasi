The Opening of the Şûrâ-yı Devlet: The Founding of the Ottoman Council of State

10 May 1868

By the irade of 5 March 1868 the Meclis-i Ahkâm-ı Adliyye was divided in two, with administrative and legislative affairs left to the Şûrâ-yı Devlet and judicial matters to the Dîvân-ı Ahkâm-ı Adliyye; the institution was opened with a ceremony at the Sublime Porte, and Midhat Pasha was appointed as its first president. It was charged with preparing draft laws, resolving administrative disputes and conducting the trials of officials. With its mixed Muslim and non-Muslim membership, it is regarded as the first institutional application of the idea of the separation of powers in the Ottoman Empire.
