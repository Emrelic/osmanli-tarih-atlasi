Before 3000 BC

  • 14961 BC Afontova Gora-2 (AG-2), Yenisei, near Krasnoyarsk: Afontova Gora-2: Upper Paleolithic Siberian settlement (off the horizon)
  • 14914 BC Yuchanyan Cave: begins
  • 13799 BC Odai Yamamoto I (Aomori) — among the oldest pottery sites in Japan: invention of the oldest pottery (pre-Jomon/early)
  • 13749 BC Beginning of the Jomon: Sosoki (Incipient) period: Sosoki (Jomon incipient phase) begins
  • 12649 BC Natufian Culture: begins
  • 12599 BC Monte Verde II: begins

  • 9319 BC Hallan Çemi: owner period ends — Epipaleolithic–Pre-Pottery Neolithic communities (Northern Levant, Euphrates, Southeastern Anatolia)
  • 9249 BC Ounjougou (Ravin de la Mouche) - the oldest pottery of Africa: Africa's earliest pottery (Ounjougou)
  • 9149 BC Klimonas and Shillourokambos (Cyprus, Early Aceramic Neolithic): the first sedentary villages on Cyprus (Klimonas)

  • 8099 BC Boncuklu Mound (Konya basin, Cumra): occupation ends (late phase)
  • 7999 BC Pinarbasi (Konya basin, Karaman): occupation ends
  • 7699 BC Gobero (Tenere circle, Niger): Early Holocene (Kiffian) occupation begins
  • 7349 BC Holocene occupation of the Fayum (around Lake Qarun): begins
  • 7049 BC Peiligang Culture: begins
  • 6899 BC Ba'ja: owner period ends — PPNB — Pre-Pottery Neolithic B (Southern Levant)
  • 6699 BC Knossos (Neolithic settlement): begins
  • 6499 BC Ubaid culture (southern Mesopotamia): Ubaid culture: the first dated Ubaid phase in southern Mesopotamia begins
  • 6119 BC Lepenski Vir trapezoidal building phase (Mesolithic-Neolithic transition, LV I-II): begins
  • 5974 BC Lepenski Vir trapezoidal building phase (Mesolithic-Neolithic transition, LV I-II): ends

  • 5779 BC Lepenski Vir: ends
  • 5499 BC Tepe Yahya: Period VII begins (Iranica; provisional table)
  • 5499 BC Halaf culture: the latest date of the Halaf period (upper limit)
  • 4999 BC Early Khartoum (Khartoum Mesolithic): ends
  • 4849 BC Holocene occupation of the Fayum (around Lake Qarun): ends
  • 4799 BC Banpo (Yangshao, Banpo phase): the Banpo settlement (Yangshao)
  • 4790 BC Carnac megalithic complex, Le Plasker section (Plouharnel): begins
  • 4723 BC Mehrgarh: owner period ends — Mehrgarh Neolithic (Kachi Plain, Balochistan)
  • 4699 BC Es-Sheheinab (Shaheinab) Neolithic: begins
  • 4586 BC Vinča-Belo Brdo: begins
  • 4514 BC Vinča-Belo Brdo: ends
  • 4499 BC Valdivia culture (Loma Alta / Real Alto): begins
  • 4349 BC Coxcatlán phase (Tehuacán Valley): ends
  • 4299 BC Banpo (Yangshao, Banpo phase): ends
  • 4199 BC Esh-Shaheinab: owner period ends — Shaheinab Neolithic (Central Sudan)
  • 3999 BC Susa: the city is founded (approximate, Iranica)
  • 3949 BC Takarkori rock shelter (Tadrart Acacus): ends
  • 3819 BC Anau: owner period ends — Anau IA (pre-Namazga/Meana phase, Kopet Dag)
  • 3799 BC Ubaid culture (southern Mesopotamia): the Ubaid period ends (Carter & Philip 2010; Egberts et al. 2023 give its end as 4200 BCE)
  • 3749 BC Los Morteros (Chao Valley, Peru) - the oldest adobe monumental architecture: the oldest adobe monumental architecture in the Americas
  • 3639 BC Hierakonpolis HK11C (Naqada IC-IIB occupation): Hierakonpolis HK11C occupation (Naqada IB/IC)

  • 3463 BC Hierakonpolis HK11C (Naqada IC-IIB occupation): HK11C occupation ends (Naqada IIB/IIC)
  • 3199 BC Proto-Elamite writing system (Susa): Proto-Elamite writing appears
  • 3139 BC Los Millares: the settlement and forts begin
  • 3099 BC Narmer (Menes) united Upper and Lower Egypt; the First Dynasty began and Memphis became the capital
  • 3001 BC Lothagam North Pillar Site (Lake Turkana, Kenya): use of the monumental cemetery platform begins
