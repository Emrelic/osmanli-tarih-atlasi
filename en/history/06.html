6th century

  • 507 At Vouillé (near Poitiers) Clovis kills the Visigothic king Alaric II; Visigothic territory as far as the Pyrenees passes to the Franks
  • 27 November 511 Clovis dies; he is buried at Paris, which he made his capital in his last years, and the unity of the kingdom is left to the Merovingian inheritance
  • 534 Byzantium recovered Vandal Africa: Carthage again passed into the hands of the Eastern Romans
  • 550 Teotihuacan: period ends — Teotihuacan City-State
  • 567 The Avars destroyed the Gepid kingdom; the Avar Khaganate began on the Hungarian plain
  • 567 Istemi Yabgu sent an embassy to Byzantium: the Gokturk-Byzantine alliance
  • 568 The Avars entered Pannonia: the Avar Khaganate settled in the Danube basin
  • 1 April 568 Alboin's Lombards assembled to march on Italy (1 April 568)
  • 4 September 569 Alboin entered Milan: northern Italy passed out of Byzantine hands
  • 570 Muhammad was born in Mecca (the year is disputed among 569/570/571; day: 12 Rabi' al-awwal, Monday)
  • 17 January 577 The Northern Zhou army reached Bingzhou (Taiyuan/Jinyang); the Northern Qi ruler fled to Ye
  • 4 March 581 Yang Jian ascended the throne in the Linguang Hall: the Sui dynasty was founded
  • 582 Maurice established the Exarchate of Africa: a military-civil administration centred on Carthage
  • 582 Maurice established the Exarchate of Ravenna: the Byzantine lands in Italy were placed under a military governor
