14th century BC

  • 1399 BC Mitanni-Egypt alliance: peace between Artatama I and Thutmose IV; a Mitanni princess went to Egypt as a bride
  • 1399 BC Sanganakallu-Kupgal: expansion of the hilltop village (Late Neolithic–Megalithic transition)
  • 1362 BC Aššur-uballit I freed himself from Mitanni overlordship; Assyria became independent and a "great king"
  • 1349 BC Kassite Babylon took its place in Amarna diplomacy as a great power equal to Egypt
  • 1349 BC Suppiluliuma I ascended the Hittite throne; the New Kingdom period began
  • 1349 BC Ugarit (Ras Shamra): Kingdom of Ugarit period begins
  • 1347 BC Amenhotep IV (Akhenaten) moved the capital of Egypt to Akhetaten (Amarna)
  • 1346 BC Deer Stone–Khirigsuur Complex, Mongolia: Khirigsuur (Deer Stone) burial monuments begin
  • 1344 BC Suppiluliuma I defeated Mitanni (Tushratta) and took Aleppo and Carchemish; northern Syria passed to the Hittites
  • 1339 BC In the Amarna age Egypt's rule in Northern Syria dissolved; Amurru and Kadesh slipped into the Hittite sphere of influence
  • 1327 BC Suppiluliuma appointed his sons to head the kingdoms of Carchemish and Aleppo
  • 1324 BC Kamgot (Early Lapita settlement), Babase Island, Anir Islands: Kamgot (Anir) early Lapita settlement
  • 1317 BC Mursili II defeated and partitioned Arzawa (western Anatolia)
