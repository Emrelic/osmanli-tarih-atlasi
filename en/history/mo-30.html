30th century BC

  • 2999 BC Norte Chico (Caral-Supe) Late Archaic complex: begins
  • 2999 BC South Indian Neolithic (Ashmound tradition, Deccan): South India: the oldest occupation layer at Kodekal (charcoal)
  • 2999 BC Dholavira: Dholavira occupation begins
  • 2949 BC Dadiwan (Qin'an, Gansu): Phase V (Changshan) ends
  • 2919 BC Troy I (Hisarlik, beginning of the Early Bronze Age): the Troy Ia phase begins (Weninger 1995)
