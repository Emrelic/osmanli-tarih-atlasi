7th century BC

  • 700 BC In the Judean campaign Sennacherib met the Kushite-Egyptian army at Eltekeh
  • 700 BC Sennacherib invaded Judah; he took Lachish and besieged Jerusalem
  • 700 BC Sennacherib took Sidon; King Luli fled to Cyprus
  • 699 BC Median State (centered on Hegmatana): Median state: the period of Deioces begins (Herodotean tradition)
  • 688 BC Sennacherib destroyed Babylon, plundered its temples and let the Euphrates flood the city
  • 684 BC Gyges overthrew Candaules and founded the Mermnad dynasty
  • 677 BC Ecbatana, Hamadan: ownerless interval → Median Kingdom
  • 676 BC Esarhaddon destroyed Sidon and refounded it as Kar-Esarhaddon
  • 670 BC Esarhaddon entered Egypt and took Memphis; Taharqa withdrew to the south
  • 669 BC Hallstatt C -> D transition (Dietfurt Bayesian model; traditional 616): Hallstatt C → D transition (14C model)
  • 663 BC The Twenty-sixth Dynasty began: Psamtik I came to power in Lower Egypt at Sais as an Assyrian vassal
  • 663 BC Tantamani came down from Kush and retook Thebes and Memphis; he besieged the Assyrian vassals
  • 663 BC Ashurbanipal sacked Thebes
  • 662 BC The Assyrian army plundered Thebes; Tantamani withdrew to the south, and Kushite rule in Egypt collapsed
  • 655 BC Psamtik I brought Upper Egypt under his control by having his daughter Nitocris adopted by the God's Wife of Amun at Thebes
  • 652 BC Battle of Til-Tuba/Ulai: Ashurbanipal defeated and killed the Elamite king Teumman
  • 651 BC The Cimmerians sacked Sardis; Gyges died
  • 647 BC Babylon submitted to Ashurbanipal after the revolt of Šamaš-šuma-ukin
  • 644 BC Ashurbanipal destroyed Susa; the New Elamite Kingdom collapsed
  • 630 BC Battus of Thera founded Cyrene
  • 630 BC Ashurbanipal died; a struggle for the throne and a revolt in Babylon began in Assyria
  • 624 BC Cyaxares (Umakištar) ascended the Median throne
  • 613 BC Cyaxares and Nabopolassar concluded an alliance treaty before Aššur
  • 613 BC Ashur (beginning of documents of the Old Assyrian period and the fall of 614 BCE): the city of Ashur is taken by the Medes/Babylonians
  • 609 BC Harran fell; Aššur-uballit II withdrew to Egypt
  • 609 BC Alyattes ascended the Lydian throne; he drove the Cimmerians from Anatolia and expanded the kingdom
  • 608 BC Necho II entered Syria-Palestine; he defeated Josiah at Megiddo and advanced toward the Euphrates
  • 608 BC Jarablus, Carchemish, Megiddo, Samaria (Samaria / Sebaste), Damascus: Neo-Assyrian Empire period ends
  • 604 BC The Battle of Carchemish: Nebuchadnezzar defeated the Egyptian army; Egypt lost Syria
  • 7 September 604 BC Nabopolassar died and Nebuchadnezzar II ascended the Babylonian throne
  • 1 December 603 BC Nebuchadnezzar II took Ashkelon, captured its king and reduced the city to ruins
