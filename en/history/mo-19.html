19th century BC

  • 1899 BC Assyrian merchants founded the karum of Kanesh (Kültepe); the Old Assyrian trade network extended to Anatolia
  • 1899 BC Susa: Elam — Shimashki Dynasty → Elam — Sukkalmah (Eparti) Dynasty
  • 1866 BC Senusret III at Semna moved Egypt's southern border to the Second Cataract; he forbade the passage of Nubians
  • 1835 BC Level II of the karum of Kanesh (Kültepe) was destroyed; Anitta rose among the Anatolian city-states
  • 1821 BC Rim-Sin I ascended the Larsa throne
  • 1811 BC Middle Kingdom: ends
  • 1807 BC Ashur: Old Assyrian State (city-state of Assur) → Kingdom of Upper Mesopotamia (Shamshi-Adad I)
  • 1801 BC Uruk: ownerless interval → Kingdom of Larsa
