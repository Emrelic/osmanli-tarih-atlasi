11th century

  • 1001 Basil II took Sofia
  • 1001 The Oxus (Amu Darya) was accepted as the frontier between Mahmud of Ghazna and the Karakhanid Nasr ibn Ali
  • 1003 Vidin surrendered to Byzantium after a siege of eight months
  • 1003 Mahmud of Ghazna took Sistan and captured Khalaf; the Saffarids came to an end
  • 3 August 1003 The Oghuz of Arslan Yabgu, son of Seljuk Beg, together with the Samanid Isma'il, defeated the Karakhanid Ilig Khan Nasr
  • 1004 Muslim raiders briefly occupy Pisa
  • 1005 The last Samanid emir, Isma'il al-Muntasir, was killed by an Arab tribe near Merv; the Samanids passed into history
  • 1 September 1006 The two armies sent into Khurasan by the Karakhanid Nasr ibn Ali were driven back by Mahmud
  • 1 December 1007 Mahmud defeated the combined army of the Karakhanids Nasr ibn Ali and Yusuf Qadir Khan near Balkh
  • 1008 Batumi under the rule of the Kingdom/Kingdoms of Georgia — a dated attestation in the source
  • 1009 Lý Công Uẩn took the throne after the Lê dynasty; the Lý dynasty was founded (1009-1225)
  • 15 August 1010 Valencia passed into the hands of the Amirid freedmen Mubarak and Muzaffar
  • 1011 Mahmud made his first campaign into the Ghur region
  • 1012 Baha al-Dawla died; the struggle among his sons shattered Buyid unity
  • 23 July 1012 Ilig Khan Nasr died; his brother Arslan Ilig Mansur proclaimed himself great khagan and took Talas, Shash, Fergana, Uzgand, Khujand, Ushrusana and Bukhara under his rule
  • 1013 Mahmud took Lahore from the Hindu Shahis; Lahore became the favorite city of the Ghaznavids
  • 1013 Sulayman al-Musta'in and his supporters entered Cordoba and deposed Hisham II again; at least sixty scholars were killed in the city (403/1013; the source gives the year, no day).
  • 3 February 1014 King Svend Forkbeard dies
  • 29 July 1014 Battle of Kleidion: Basil II destroyed the Bulgarian army
  • 1015 King Robert II annexed the Duchy of Burgundy to the French crown after a war of thirteen years
  • 15 July 1015 Grand Prince Vladimir dies at Vyshhorod
  • 1016 The Hammudids seized Cordoba and the throne of al-Andalus
  • 23 April 1016 Æthelred II dies; Cnut (Canute) is elected king by the notables assembled at Southampton
  • 1017 Toghan Khan Ahmad died; Yusuf Qadir Khan asserted a claim to the great khaganate and asked Mahmud of Ghazna for help
  • 1018 Basil II entered Ohrid: the western Bulgarian capital passed into Byzantine hands
  • 1018 The Polish King Bolesław the Brave took Kiev with German and Pecheneg support
  • 1018 All Bulgarian lands were annexed to Byzantium: the First Bulgarian State came to an end
  • 1018 Ivan Vladislav died before Dyrrhachion
  • 20 December 1018 Mahmud conquered Kanauj (Kanyakubja)
  • 1019 Yaroslav defeats Sviatopolk on the Alta River and regains the throne of Kiev
  • 9 May 1019 Mahmud defeated the combined armies of Yusuf Qadir Khan and Arslan Ilig Mansur near Balkh
  • 27 April 1020 Mahmud made his second campaign into Ghur and brought the Afghans under obedience
  • 1021 The Amirid dynasty was founded with Valencia as its centre (1021-1094)
  • 1021 Musharrif al-Dawla, supported by the Turks, took control of Iraq; Sultan al-Dawla gave up Iraq
  • 26 March 1023 The qadi Ibn Abbad declared Seville the seat of government (Abbadids)
  • 15 March 1024 Arslan Ilig Mansur abdicated; Yusuf Qadir Khan became great khagan of the Karakhanids
  • 8 September 1024 Conrad II was elected German king and crowned in Mainz — the Salian dynasty began
  • 1025 Naval expedition of the Chola king Rajendra I: the ports of Srivijaya (Palembang), Pannai and Kadaram (Kedah) were plundered, and the king Sangramavijayottungavarman was taken prisoner
  • 4 March 1025 Mahmud and Yusuf Qadir Khan met near Samarkand; the Seljuk Arslan Yabgu was arrested and imprisoned in the fortress of Kalinjar
  • 1026 Agreement of Horodok: Yaroslav and Mstislav divide Rus' along the Dnieper
  • 8 January 1026 Mahmud conquered the fortress of Somnath (Sumanat) in Gujarat
  • 22 February 1026 Ali Tegin became independent ruler of Transoxiana; the foundation of a separate Karakhanid branch in the west was laid
  • 11 February 1027 Mahmud made his last Indian campaign against the Jats; he built a river fleet of 1,400 boats at Multan
  • 1028 Cnut the Great was proclaimed king in Norway; Olaf Haraldsson fled
  • 1030 The County of Aversa was founded: Rainulf Drengot became a Norman count
  • 1030 Yaroslav the Wise conquers the land between Lake Peipus and the Baltic and founds Yuryev (Tartu)
  • 30 April 1030 Sultan Mahmud died in Ghazna; the Ghaznavid state stretched from Azerbaijan in the west to the upper Ganges valley in the east
  • 29 July 1030 Battle of Stiklestad: King Olav II Haraldsson falls in Trøndelag
  • 4 October 1030 Mas'ud deposed his brother Muhammad, who had taken the throne, imprisoned him, and became sultan
  • 1032 Rudolph III, last king of the Kingdom of Burgundy (Arles), dies; his country passes to Conrad II (German emperor) — Basel enters the imperial lands
  • 1032 Rudolph III, last king of the Kingdom of Burgundy (Arles), dies; his country passes to Conrad II (German emperor) — Geneva enters the imperial lands
  • 1032 Rudolph III, last king of the Kingdom of Burgundy (Arles), dies; his country passes to Conrad II (German emperor) — Lyon enters the imperial lands
  • 1032 Ali Tegin declared his independence once more with the title Tamghach Bughra Qara Khaqan
  • 1032 The Orseolo family is exiled; the tendency towards a hereditary dynasty is definitively broken
  • 7 December 1032 Yusuf Qadir Khan died; his sons Arslan Khan Sulayman and Bughra Khan Muhammad took on the government of the state
  • 12 May 1035 Toghril and Chaghri Beg crossed the Oxus and entered Ghaznavid-ruled Khurasan
  • 29 June 1035 The Seljuks routed the army of the Ghaznavid hajib Beg Toghdi near Nasa
  • 1 August 1035 Mas'ud made peace: Dihistan was left to Chaghri Beg, Nasa to Toghril Beg, and Farawa to Musa Yabgu
  • 12 November 1035 Knud the Great dies; the North Sea Empire disintegrates
  • 1036 With Mstislav's death Yaroslav becomes sole ruler; the Pechenegs are heavily defeated
  • 1037 Hammama expelled Tamim from Morocco: the Emirate of the Maghrawa returned to Morocco
  • 1038 Guaimar took Capua from his uncle and also subjugated Amalfi, Sorrento, and Gaeta
  • 1038 Li Yuanhao proclaimed his empire (Western Xia, Xingzhou)
  • 1038 King István I dies; a period of turmoil in the Árpád dynasty
  • 1040 Revolt of Peter Delyan: many fortresses, including Skopje, fell into the hands of the rebels
  • 1040 Stefan Vojislav began the independence movement in Duklja
  • 1040 The Xia army attacked Yanzhou; two deputy commanders of the Fuyan and Huanqing routes were captured
  • 23 May 1040 Battle of Dandanqan: the Seljuks routed the Ghaznavid army; Khurasan slipped out of Ghaznavid hands
  • 1041 Muhammad, restored to the throne, was killed; Mawdud became Ghaznavid sultan

  • 3 May 1052 Ayn al-Dawla died; Bori Tegin Ibrahim (Tamghach Khan) became great khagan and chose Samarkand as capital
  • 24 October 1052 Nasir-i Khusraw returned to Balkh seven years later by way of Basra and Isfahan
  • 1054 Fulad Sutun, master of Shiraz, had the khutba read in the name of Toghril Beg
  • 20 February 1054 Yaroslav the Wise dies in Kiev; the fragmentation of Rus' begins

  • 1056 An agreement between the bishop and the nobles gives rise to the Genoese commune's autonomy

  • 1057 The Hilali plunder: al-Muʿizz b. Bādīs withdrew to Mahdia, Zirid rule in Kairouan came to an end
