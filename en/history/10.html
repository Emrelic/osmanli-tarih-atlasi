10th century

  • 902 The captive Amr ibn Layth was killed in Baghdad; Fars was given to Amr's grandson Tahir
  • 1 July 903 The Qarmatians besiege Damascus; the leader Sahib al-Naqa Yahya is killed during the siege
  • 10 February 904 Zhaozong left Chang'an; the city was demolished and the capital moved to Luoyang
  • 31 July 904 The Cretan fleet seized and plundered Thessalonica
  • 22 September 904 Zhaozong was killed at Luoyang
  • 906 The Qarmatian da'i Abu Ghanim plunders Busra, Tiberias and Hit, then enters Kufa; he is soon expelled
  • 907 The Later Liang made Qian Liu 'King of Wuyue'
  • 1 June 907 The Tang dynasty came to an end: Aidi handed the throne to Zhu Wen
  • 3 November 907 Wang Jian became emperor at Chengdu: the Great Shu (Former Shu)
  • 11 May 909 The Later Liang granted the military governor of Fujian, Wang Shenzhi, the title 'King of Min'
  • 15 January 910 Abu Abdullah entered Raqqada together with Ubaydallah al-Mahdi; the Fatimid imamate effectively began
  • 911 Treaty of Saint-Clair-sur-Epte: Charles the Simple cedes present-day Normandy to the Normans
  • 911 The Ganzhou Uyghur army captured Dunhuang
  • 913 The Bulgarian Tsar Simeon came before the walls of Constantinople
  • 25 July 918 Wang Geon (Taejo) ascended the throne in the Bojeong Hall at Gaegyeong and named the country Goryeo
  • 21 June 921 Ibn Fadlan leaves Baghdad with the embassy of Caliph al-Muqtadir
  • 12 May 922 Ibn Fadlan arrives at the camp of the Volga Bulgar khan
  • 12 May 922 The Abbasid embassy reached the camp of the khan of the Volga Bulgars; Almish Khan accepted Islam
  • 21 April 923 The ayyars of Zaranj brought Abu Ja'far Ahmad to power; the Khalafid branch of the Saffarids was founded
  • 15 June 923 Battle of Soissons: King Robert I dies, Raoul, Duke of Burgundy, is elected king
  • 19 November 923 Li Cunxu entered Kaifeng: the Later Liang fell
  • 926 Tomislav, King of the Croats, routed the Bulgarian army
  • 15 May 926 Li Cunxu was killed at Luoyang by rebellious soldiers
  • 27 May 927 Tsar Simeon died: the age of Bulgaria's great wars closed
  • 8 September 927 Byzantine-Bulgarian peace: Simeon's son Peter married a Byzantine princess
  • 929 In the time of Wawa (927-28) the administrative center of the Central Javanese kingdom of Medang moved eastward; Sindok took the throne in 929 (East Javanese period)
  • 14 February 929 The Qarmatians occupied Mecca and carried the Black Stone to al-Ahsā' (Lahsā'); the Qarmatian State of Bahrain appears as owner of Lahsā' in the first dated attestation
  • 930 The first Althing of Iceland convenes at Þingvellir
  • 24 January 931 Abu Tahir hands the administration of Bahrain to a Persian whom he presented as the mahdi; the new mahdi is killed eighty days later
  • 932 The Buyid Ali occupied Isfahan; the Buyid dynasty began to establish rule in western Iran
  • 934 Byzantium took Melitene
  • 28 September 935 Saint Wenceslas, duke of Bohemia, is killed by his brother Boleslav
  • 10 December 935 The Silla king Gyeongsun entered Gaegyeong with his retinue; Taejo received him with ceremony and married him to his daughter Princess Nangnang
  • 26 September 936 At Ilicheon, Goryeo defeated the Hubaekje army; Sin-geom surrendered, Taejo entered the Hubaekje capital, and the Later Three Kingdoms were united
  • 28 November 936 Shi Jingtang was proclaimed emperor at Taiyuan with Khitan support: the Later Jin was founded
  • 11 January 937 Li Congke burned himself at Luoyang with his family: the Later Tang came to an end
  • 24 August 944 Caliph al-Muttaqi-Lillah granted the administration of Egypt to Muhammad ibn Tughj for thirty years and accepted the succession of his son Unujur as heir (333/944; first day of the Hijri year).
  • 19 December 945 The Buyid Ahmad (Muʿizz al-Dawla) entered Baghdad; the city passed to Buyid rule
  • 947 Hasan ibn Ali al-Kalbi becomes governor of Sicily: the Kalbid Emirate begins
  • 24 February 947 Khitan Taizong held an enthronement ceremony at Kaifeng; the edict was issued under the name 'Great Liao'
  • 10 August 955 Battle of the Lechfeld: Otto I puts an end to the Hungarian raids
  • 957 Olga goes to Constantinople and is baptised
  • 27 July 959 Zhou Shizong (Chai Rong) died; the Prince of Liang was proclaimed emperor before the coffin
  • 960 King Harald Bluetooth is baptised; the effective Christianisation of Denmark
  • 6 March 961 Chandax fell: Nikephoros Phokas recovered Crete
  • 2 February 962 Otto I is crowned emperor in Rome; beginning of the Holy Roman-Germanic Empire
  • 963 Alp Tegin took Ghazna from the Lawik and laid the foundation of the Ghaznavid state
  • 16 August 963 Nikephoros Phokas became emperor
  • 965 The Volga Bulgars freed themselves from Khazar domination and became fully independent
  • 16 August 965 Tarsus surrendered to Byzantium: Cilicia came under Byzantine rule
  • 966 Mieszko I accepts Christianity; the beginning of the Polish state
  • 967 Mu'izz al-Dawla died; the era of his son Bakhtiyar began in Iraq
  • 6 July 969 The commander Jawhar al-Ṣiqillī entered Fustat and conquered Egypt; the Ikhshidids came to an end
  • 28 October 969 Antioch passed into Byzantine hands
  • 14 November 969 The khutba was read in Damascus in the name of al-Mu'izz; Jawhar's commander Ja'far b. Falah took Syria
  • 10 December 969 Nikephoros Phokas was murdered; John Tzimiskes became emperor
  • 13 April 971 Tzimiskes took Great Preslav by storm: eastern Bulgaria was annexed to Byzantium
  • 23 April 971 Before Dorostolon (Silistra) Tzimiskes defeated Svyatoslav
  • 972 The Fatimid caliph al-Mu'izz appointed Buluggin governor of the Maghrib and settled him in Kairouan; the Zirid period began
  • 972 After seven years of campaigns by Mansur ibn Nuh, the Saffarid Khalaf agreed to have the khutba read in the name of the Samanids
  • 972 Svyatoslav is defeated and killed by the Pecheneg chieftain Kurya; a struggle for the throne begins among his sons
  • 1 July 972 Hasan al-A'sam reoccupied Ramla and defeated a strong Fatimid army
  • 977 Bori Tegin was removed; Sebuk Tegin became ruler of Ghazna, and the Ghaznavid state turned into a dynasty passing from father to son
  • 979 The Đinh dynasty ended; Lê Hoàn ascended the throne of Đại Cồ Việt (Hall: Đinh 968-79, Lê 979-1009)
  • 1 August 979 The Battle of the Gaoliang River: the Song Emperor Taizong was defeated by the Khitan before Youzhou
  • 980 Vladimir takes Kiev and has Yaropolk killed; he becomes Grand Prince of Kiev
  • 24 May 985 The Qarmatians of Bahrain occupied Kufa two years after Basra; they could not hold out against the Buyids and withdrew
  • 17 August 986 Battle of the Gates of Trajan: Basil II suffered a heavy defeat against the Bulgarians
  • 1 August 988 The people of Kiev are baptised en masse in the Dnieper; the Christianisation of Rus'
  • 989 Sharaf al-Dawla died; Baha al-Dawla took the Buyid throne in Baghdad
  • 990 Via Francigena — Sigeric's return stages (Rome – Siena – Lucca – Pontremoli – Piacenza – Vercelli – Aosta – Lausanne – Besançon – Reims – Arras – Guisnes)
  • 31 March 990 Abū'l-Zawwād took Mosul; the Uqaylid dynasty was founded
  • 992 Harun Bughra Khan took Samarkand; the Qarakhanids entered Transoxiana for the first time
  • 7 May 992 The Qarakhanid Harun Bughra Khan captured Bukhara
  • 25 May 992 Mieszko I dies; his son Bolesław the Brave restores unity
  • 1 March 994 Li Shun's rebels took Chengdu (the Wang Xiaobo-Li Shun uprising)
  • 5 February 995 Ma'mun ibn Muhammad eliminated the last ruler of the Afrighids and took the title Khwarazmshah; the Ma'munids were founded
  • 25 January 996 By an agreement made through Sebuk Tegin, the Syr Darya region as far as Qatwan remained with the Karakhanids; Fa'iq became governor of Samarkand
  • 997 After Géza, his son István becomes Grand Prince
  • 3 January 998 Abu'l-Hasan Ali Arslan Khan died; the Karakhanids continued in two branches, the Ali and Hasan lines
  • 3 March 998 Mahmud defeated his brother Isma'il and ascended the Ghaznavid throne
  • 14 October 999 The Qarakhanid Ilig Khan Nasr entered Bukhara; the Samanid family was exiled to Uzgend, and the state effectively ended
  • 1 November 999 Caliph al-Qadir-Billah confirmed Mahmud's sultanate and gave him the title Yamin al-Dawla
  • 1000 Doge Pietro II Orseolo subjugated the Dalmatian cities and became "Duke of Dalmatia"
  • 1000 Ragusa (Dubrovnik) and the southern Dalmatian cities swore fealty to Orseolo
  • 1000 Abu Ibrahim Isma'il ibn Nuh escaped from prison, retook Bukhara and ascended the Samanid throne with the title 'al-Muntasir'
  • 1000 Mahmud of Ghazna marched on Sistan; Khalaf agreed to pay 100,000 dinars and to have the khutba read in Mahmud's name
  • 1000 István I is crowned and founds the Christian Kingdom of Hungary (1000-1001)
  • 1000 The Althing adopts Christianity as the official religion
  • 4 September 1000 Mahmud set out on his first Indian campaign; he took the Indian fortresses in the Lamghan region
