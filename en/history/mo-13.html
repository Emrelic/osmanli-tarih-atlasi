13th century BC

  • 1299 BC Ajalpan phase (Tehuacán Valley): Tehuacán: the Ajalpan phase
  • 1294 BC Adad-nirari I defeated Hanigalbat (the Mitanni remnant) and made it a vassal
  • 1293 BC Seti I campaigned from southern Canaan to the Phoenician coast: Egypt again became dominant over Canaan and the Lebanese coast, and took Kadesh
  • 1284 BC Talepakemalai (ECA), Eloaua Island, Mussau: Talepakemalai (Mussau) Lapita settlement
  • 1274 BC Untash-Napirisha founded the city of Dur-Untash (Chogha Zanbil) and its ziggurat
  • 1273 BC Ramesses II met the Hittites of Muwatalli II at Kadesh; the outcome was a draw
  • 1269 BC Shalmaneser I annexed Hanigalbat; Mitanni disappeared
  • 1258 BC The 'Eternal Treaty' between Ramesses II and Hattusili III: the Egyptian-Hittite war came to an end
  • 1253 BC Anyang / Yinxu (殷墟), Yinxu (Anyang): ownerless interval period begins
  • 1224 BC Tukulti-Ninurta I, king of Aššur, conquered Babylon; he carried the statue of Marduk to Aššur
  • 1224 BC Yinxu (Anyang, Late Shang capital): Wu Ding phase (oracle bone 14C): the first dated royal phase of Yinxu
  • 1219 BC Babylon: Kassite Babylonian Kingdom → Middle Assyrian Empire
  • 1209 BC Babylon: Middle Assyrian Empire → ownerless interval
  • 1208 BC Merneptah defeated the alliance of Libyans and Sea Peoples in the western Delta; the name 'Israel' was first mentioned in Canaan
  • 1208 BC Merneptah Stele: the earliest known mention of the name 'Israel' (5th regnal year of the Egyptian pharaoh Merneptah; c. 1209 BCE)
  • 1206 BC Suppiluliuma II (last known Hittite king) — accession: Suppiluliuma II accedes (last Hittite king)
