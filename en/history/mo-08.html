8th century BC

  • 799 BC El Mirador: begins
  • 799 BC Tikal: Eb ceramic complex (earliest trace of settlement): Tikal: Eb ceramic complex (first settlement)
  • 799 BC Pampa de las Hamacas ceremonial centres (Jequetepeque, Formative): the ceremonial centres are abandoned
  • 759 BC Carthage - foundation tradition 814/813 and archaeological ~760: Carthage: beginning of continuous occupation (archaeological)
  • 751 BC Foundation of Rome (written tradition, Dionysius)
  • 749 BC Kashta, king of Kush (Napata), advanced into Upper Egypt; he extended his influence to Elephantine and Thebes
  • 749 BC Izapa (Chiapas): Middle Formative royal capital and first white clay surface: Izapa: Middle Formative settlement
  • 744 BC Tiglath-Pileser III ascended the throne; the Assyrian provincial system was established
  • 742 BC Tiglath-Pileser III defeated the Urartian king Sarduri II near Arpad
  • 742 BC Nukuleka (first Lapita coastal settlement), Tongatapu: the Tongatapu Lapita phase ends
  • 739 BC Gordion — Tumulus MM (Midas Mound): Midas Tumulus (MM): felling year of the trees of the burial chamber (dendrochronology)
  • 733 BC Tiglath-Pileser III took Gaza; the Palestinian coast came under Assyrian control
  • 733 BC Syracuse, Syrakusai: Syracuse (Syrakousai) period begins
  • 732 BC Tiglath-Pileser III took Galilee and Gilead from Israel and made them Assyrian provinces
  • 724 BC Piye (Pankhy) came down into Egypt: he defeated Tefnakht's coalition and subdued the rulers of Memphis and the Delta
  • 721 BC Samaria (Samaria / Sebaste): Kingdom of Israel (Samaria) → Neo-Assyrian Empire
  • 719 BC Sparta won the First Messenian War: Messenia was annexed to Sparta
  • 719 BC Sargon II defeated Hamath; he killed Yau-bidi at Qarqar and made the kingdom a province
  • 716 BC Sargon II took Carchemish; the kingdom of Pisiris became an Assyrian province
  • 716 BC Jarablus, Carchemish: Neo-Assyrian Empire period begins
  • 713 BC Sargon II entered Urartu: Rusa I was defeated, Musasir was plundered and the statue of Haldi was taken
  • 711 BC Sargon II took Malatya (Melid); Gunzinanu was deposed
  • 709 BC Babylon: ownerless interval → Neo-Assyrian Empire
  • 704 BC Sargon II was killed in battle in Tabal; Sennacherib ascended the throne
