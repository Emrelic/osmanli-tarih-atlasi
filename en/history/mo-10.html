10th century BC

  • 999 BC Ceibal (Seibal, Pasion, Guatemala): Real 1 phase: first sedentary community
  • 999 BC Non Nok Tha: Bronze Age phase (short chronology)
  • 999 BC La Blanca (San Marcos, Pacific Guatemala): Middle Preclassic regional centre, Mound 1: La Blanca: rise of the monumental centre
  • 944 BC Shoshenq I (of the Meshwesh/Libyan line) founded the Twenty-second Dynasty; the capital was Bubastis
  • 944 BC Teouma (Lapita cemetery), Efate: begins
  • 939 BC Damascus: Aram-Damascus (Dimashqa) Kingdom period begins
  • 929 BC Solomon's kingdom was divided; Jeroboam I founded the Kingdom of Israel in the north
  • 929 BC Greek Geometric period (absolute dates raised by 14C): Middle Geometric II begins (Sindos 14C: 930 against the traditional 800)
  • 929 BC Jerusalem: Kingdom of Judah period begins
  • 924 BC Shoshenq campaigned in Canaan: Jerusalem and the cities of Israel and Judah entered the Karnak list
  • 910 BC Adad-nirari II ascended the throne; the Neo-Assyrian recovery began
  • 910 BC Ashur: ownerless interval → Neo-Assyrian Empire
