3rd century BC

  • 300 BC Lysimachus placed his treasury at Pergamum; Philetaerus became the guardian of the fortress
  • 300 BC After Ipsus, Ptolemy occupied Coele-Syria; Seleucus objected (the root of the Syrian Wars)
  • 300 BC Battle of Ipsus: Seleucus and Lysimachus defeated Antigonus; Syria passed to Seleucus
  • 299 BC Seleucus I founded Antioch (Antakya)
  • 295 BC Pyrrhus returned to the throne of Epirus with Ptolemy's support
  • 294 BC Sentinum: the alliance of Etruscans, Umbrians, Samnites and Gauls was defeated by Rome
  • 293 BC Demetrius Poliorcetes was proclaimed king of Macedonia
  • 289 BC Samnium was added to Rome: the Third Samnite War ended
  • 286 BC Pyrrhus and Lysimachus partitioned Macedonia; Demetrius Poliorcetes was overthrown
  • 284 BC Lysimachus drove Pyrrhus out of Macedonia and became sole king of Macedonia
  • 281 BC Bergama, Pergamon: Kingdom of Pergamon (Attalid) period begins
  • 280 BC Philetaerus went over to Seleucus after Corupedium; Pergamum effectively founded an independent dynasty
  • 280 BC Seleucus I was killed by Ptolemy Ceraunus; Antiochus I ascended the throne
  • 279 BC Pyrrhus crossed to Italy to help Tarentum; he defeated the Romans at Heraclea
  • 279 BC The Achaean League was refounded: Patrae, Dyme, Tritaea, Pharae
  • 279 BC Carian War: Ptolemy II took Ionia, Caria, Lycia, Pamphylia and part of Cilicia from Antiochus I
  • 279 BC Pasargadae: ownerless interval ends
  • 278 BC The Aetolian League defended Delphi against the Galatian raid
  • 277 BC Nicomedes I brought the Galatians into Anatolia
  • 275 BC Antigonus Gonatas became king of Macedonia: the Antigonid dynasty was established
  • 274 BC Maleventum (Beneventum): Pyrrhus withdrew from Italy
  • 274 BC Antiochus I defeated the Galatians in the "Battle of the Elephants"
  • 273 BC First Syrian War (274-272): Seleucid forces retook Damascus; peace was made
  • 273 BC The reign of Ashoka and the synchronism of Rock Edict XIII: Ashoka ascends the throne
  • 271 BC Pyrrhus died at Argos
  • 271 BC Tarentum surrendered to Rome: southern Italy was added to Rome
  • 263 BC Nicomedes I founded Nicomedia (İzmit)
  • 263 BC The First Punic War began: Appius Claudius crossed to Messana
  • 263 BC Volsinii fell: the last independent great city of Etruria passed to Rome
  • 262 BC Hieron II made an alliance with Rome: eastern Sicily became Rome's ally
  • 255 BC Luoyang: State of Qin period begins
  • 250 BC Aratus added Sicyon to the Achaean League
  • 249 BC Arsaces I and Tiridates seized Parthava and founded the Parthian (Arsacid) state
  • 249 BC Diodotus, satrap of Bactria, broke away from the Seleucids; the Graeco-Bactrian Kingdom was born
  • 245 BC In the Laodicean War Ptolemy III invaded Syria and Mesopotamia; Seleucia Pieria passed into Ptolemaic hands
  • 242 BC Aratus seized Acrocorinth: Corinth passed from Macedonia to the Achaean League
  • 240 BC Aegates Islands: the Carthaginian fleet was destroyed; Sicily became the first Roman province
  • 237 BC Rome took Sardinia and Corsica from Carthage
  • 236 BC The reign of Ashoka and the synchronism of Rock Edict XIII: Ashoka dies
  • 228 BC Aristomachos, tyrant of Argos, stepped down; Argos joined the Achaean League
  • 225 BC Treaty of the Ebro: Carthage agreed not to cross north of the Ebro
  • 223 BC The Achaean League gave Acrocorinth to Antigonus Doson: Corinth returned to Macedonia
  • 221 BC Sellasia: Antigonus Doson defeated Cleomenes; Sparta suffered foreign occupation for the first time
  • 221 BC Mediolanum (Milan) fell to Rome: the Insubres were defeated, northern Italy opened to Rome
  • 218 BC Hannibal took Saguntum: the fuse of the Second Punic War was lit
  • 218 BC Antiochus III took Seleucia Pieria back from the Ptolemies
  • 217 BC A Roman army landed in Hispania: the first Roman base at Emporion
  • 217 BC Hannibal crossed the Alps and entered Italy
  • 216 BC The Battle of Raphia: Ptolemy IV defeated Antiochus III; Coele-Syria remained with the Ptolemies
  • 215 BC Cannae: Hannibal encircled and destroyed the Roman army; southern Italy began to go over to Carthage
  • 211 BC Syracuse, Syrakusai: Syracuse (Syrakousai) → Roman Republic
  • 210 BC Capua fell to Rome: Hannibal's Campanian base was lost
  • 208 BC Fabius Maximus recovered Tarentum from Hannibal
  • 208 BC Xiongnu (Hunnu) empire: the Xiongnu Empire is founded (Han sources)
  • 206 BC Xi'an (Chang'an): Qin Dynasty (unified Empire) → ownerless interval
  • 205 BC Ilipa and Gades: Hispania passed from Carthage to Rome
  • 204 BC Separatist native pharaohs emerged at Thebes: Upper Egypt broke away from Ptolemaic rule
  • 204 BC Eastern campaign of Antiochus III (209-204): the Parthian and Bactrian kings were made tributary to the Seleucids
  • 202 BC Syphax was defeated and captured; Masinissa united Numidia
  • 201 BC Zama: Scipio and Masinissa defeated Hannibal
