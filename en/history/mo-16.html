16th century BC

  • 1599 BC Mursili I took and destroyed Aleppo (Yamhad)
  • 1599 BC Xia dynasty (written tradition / Periodization Project chronology — NOT an archaeological date): the Xia ends, the Shang begins (written tradition)
  • 1599 BC Sanxingdui (Guanghan, Sichuan) civilization: the Sanxingdui civilization forms (cross-dating)
  • 1594 BC Old Babylon (First Dynasty of Babylon, Hammurabi): the First Dynasty of Babylon ends (Samsuditana)
  • 1589 BC Mursili I was assassinated; the Hittite kingdom was weakened by internal strife
  • 1583 BC Thera (Santorini) Minoan eruption — current Bayesian models: Thera (Santorini) eruption — Manning 2022 model
  • 1569 BC The Kassite dynasty reconsolidated Babylon in the time of Agum-kakrime; the statue of Marduk returned from Hittite captivity
  • 1549 BC Ahmose I took the throne at Thebes; the Eighteenth Dynasty and the New Kingdom began
  • 1531 BC Expulsion of the Hyksos from Avaris (Ahmose's conquest of Avaris): Avaris passes to Ahmose (end of the Hyksos)
  • 1529 BC Ahmose took Avaris, the capital of the Hyksos, and besieged and captured Sharuhen
  • 1524 BC Ahmose campaigned into Lower Nubia and suppressed the Nubian bowmen
  • 1524 BC Telepinu came to the throne and issued an edict; relations were defined by a treaty with Kizzuwatna
  • 1519 BC Erlitou (Erlitou culture, Yanshi): Phase IV ends
  • 1509 BC Ban Chiang: first dated burial (bone collagen, short chronology)
  • 1509 BC Sechín Alto: Sechín Alto: pre-Haldas settlement
