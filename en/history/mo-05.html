5th century BC

  • 499 BC Chavin de Huantar: ends
  • 499 BC San José Mogote: village settlement and abandonment of the ceremonial centre after Rosario: end of the Rosario phase: most of the population goes to Monte Albán
  • 498 BC The Ionian Revolt began: after Aristagoras's Naxos expedition failed, the Greek cities of Ionia rose against Persia
  • 497 BC The Ionians burned Sardis
  • 493 BC Battle of Lade: the Persian fleet defeated the Ionian fleet; Miletus fell
  • 492 BC Foedus Cassianum: Rome allied with the Latin League
  • 490 BC Mardonius subjected Macedonia to Persia
  • 489 BC Marathon: Athens halted the Persian raid
  • 485 BC Egypt rebelled against Persian rule; Xerxes I suppressed the revolt and governed Egypt more harshly
  • 483 BC The revolts of Bêl-šimânni and Šamaš-eriba in Babylon were suppressed by Xerxes I
  • 479 BC Himera: the Sicilian Greeks defeated the Carthaginian army
  • 479 BC Xerxes crossed the Hellespont; Thermopylae and the capture of Athens
  • 479 BC Battle of Salamis: the Greek fleet routed the Persian fleet
  • 478 BC Salamis (29 September 480) and Plataea: the Persian invasion was repulsed
  • 477 BC The Delian League was founded: an Aegean alliance under Athenian leadership
  • 473 BC Battle of Cumae (Kyme): Syracuse defeated the Etruscan fleet, and Etruscan sea power was broken
  • 462 BC The Thasian revolt was suppressed; the Delian League became an empire whose members could not leave
  • 459 BC Inaros's revolt: the Egyptian uprising supported by Athens pressed the Persians at Memphis and collapsed at Prosopitis in 454
  • 453 BC The League treasury was moved from Delos to Athens
  • 449 BC Teres founded the Odrysian kingdom: the first great state of Thrace
  • 445 BC The Thirty Years' Peace: Athens lost its land empire (Boeotia, Megara, Achaea)
  • 437 BC Spartocus became archon in the Bosporus: the Spartocid dynasty
  • 432 BC Sybota: the Corinthian–Corcyraean naval war drew Athens into intervention
  • 430 BC The Peloponnesian War began: the Peloponnesian League mobilized against Athens
  • 412 BC The Sicilian Expedition ended in disaster: the Athenian army was destroyed before Syracuse
  • 408 BC Hannibal Mago destroyed Selinus and Himera: Carthage established itself in western Sicily
  • 407 BC The city of Rhodes was founded: Lindos, Ialysos and Camirus united
  • 404 BC Dionysius I became tyrant of Syracuse
  • 404 BC Aegospotami: the Athenian fleet was destroyed; the Delian League came to the brink of collapse
  • 403 BC Athens surrendered to Sparta; the Athenian naval empire and the Delian League came to an end
  • 403 BC Memphis (Men-nefer / Mit Rahina): Achaemenid Egypt (27th Dynasty, 1st Persian rule) → ownerless interval
  • 401 BC Memphis (Men-nefer / Mit Rahina): ownerless interval period ends
