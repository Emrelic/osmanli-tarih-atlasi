9th century BC

  • 887 BC Nukuleka (first Lapita coastal settlement), Tongatapu: Nukuleka: Tonga's first Lapita settlement
  • 879 BC Omri made Samaria the capital and took Moab
  • 878 BC Ashurnasirpal II made Kalhu (Nimrud) the new capital of Assyria
  • 876 BC Ashurnasirpal II crossed the Euphrates and took tribute from Carchemish and the Neo-Hittite/Aramaean states
  • 859 BC Old Van (Tushpa), Tushpa (Van Fortress): Kingdom of Urartu period begins
  • 857 BC Shalmaneser III launched campaigns against Urartu (Arame); Urartu appeared for the first time in Assyrian sources
  • 852 BC Battle of Qarqar: Shalmaneser III faced the coalition of Damascus, Hamath and Israel
  • 849 BC Ajalpan phase (Tehuacán Valley): ends
  • 840 BC Shalmaneser III defeated Hazael and besieged Damascus; Jehu, king of Israel, paid tribute
  • 839 BC Mesha, king of Moab, declared independence from Israel (Mesha Stele)
  • 836 BC Latium Vetus: Latial II -> III transition (traditional ~770 vs 14C high chronology 850-825): Latium: Latial II → III transition (high chronology)
  • 813 BC Carthage: Carthaginian (Punic) State period begins
  • 809 BC Menua extended Urartu from Lake Van to the Araxes Valley and Lake Urmia
