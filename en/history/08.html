8th century

  • 20 November 708 Qutayba b. Muslim took the surrender of Bukhara
  • 26 July 711 Ṭāriq b. Ziyād inflicted a heavy defeat on the Visigothic army
  • 20 June 712 Dahir, Raja of Sind, died facing Muhammad b. al-Qasim; Rur (Aror), Brahmanabad and Multan were conquered
  • 25 March 717 Leo III ascended the throne: the Isaurian dynasty began
  • 15 August 717 Maslama began the siege of Constantinople (15 August 717)
  • 15 August 718 The second Arab siege of Constantinople was lifted
  • 739 Nanzhao moved its capital to the city of Taihe (739)
  • 22 September 746 The Kharijite (Ibadi) leader Talib al-Haqq took Sana'a from the Umayyads after Hadramawt (129/746)
  • 748 Piluoge died; his son Geluofeng took over as Prince of Yunnan (748)
  • 15 January 748 Nasr b. Sayyar yielded Merv, the center of Khurasan, to the Abbasid rebels
  • 5 August 750 The Umayyad state came to an end: Mecca passed to Abbasid rule
  • 751 The Lombards took Ravenna: the Byzantine Exarchate ended
  • 751 Nanzhao routed the Tang army on the Xi'er River and became a vassal of Tibet
  • 16 December 755 An Lushan raised the banner of revolt at Fanyang (Youzhou)
  • 756 By his Italian campaigns Pippin III takes the Exarchate of Ravenna from the Lombards and donates it to the pope; the Papal State is born
  • 756 Pippin donated the Exarchate of Ravenna to the papacy
  • 5 February 756 An Lushan proclaimed himself emperor of the 'Great Yan' at Luoyang
  • 10 July 756 Tongguan fell: Ge Shuhan was taken prisoner and the road to Chang'an was opened
  • 14 July 756 Xuanzong fled from Chang'an to Shu (Sichuan)
  • 14 November 757 The battle of the Xiangji Temple: the Tang-Uyghur army recaptured Chang'an
  • 3 December 757 The Tang army entered Luoyang (757): the Eastern Capital was retaken from the rebels
  • 7 August 761 The Abbasid commander Muhammad b. al-Ash'ath captured Kairouan
  • 17 February 763 The severed head of Shi Chaoyi reached Chang'an: the An-Shi rebellion ended
  • 18 November 763 Tibetan armies entered Chang'an; they placed the puppet emperor Cheng Hong on the throne
  • 764 The Khazar army captured Tiflis
  • 774 Pavia surrendered; Desiderius was taken captive; Charlemagne took the title "King of the Lombards" (the Lombard Kingdom ended)
  • 775 The Ligor (Nakhon Si Thammarat) stele: the Srivijaya king settled at Ligor on the Malay Peninsula and had Buddhist temples built (775)
  • 24 September 787 The Seventh General (Ecumenical) Council met at Nicaea: iconoclasm was condemned
  • 25 December 800 Charlemagne is proclaimed emperor and crowned by Pope Leo III at Saint Peter's in Rome
  • 25 December 800 Charles was crowned emperor at Rome by the pope; Byzantium did not recognize it
