2nd century

  • 106 Trajan conquered Dacia: Sarmizegetusa fell, Decebalus died, the province of Dacia was established
  • 115 The Jewish revolt in Egypt, Cyrene and Cyprus: under Trajan, Rome suppressed the region with difficulty
  • 122 Hadrian began building a Wall on the northern frontier in Britannia
  • 172 The Bucoli uprising: a revolt in the Delta threatened Alexandria, and Avidius Cassius suppressed it
  • 175 Avidius Cassius proclaimed himself emperor in Syria and Egypt; he was killed three months later
  • 198 Septimius Severus made Mesopotamia a province; Ctesiphon was sacked
