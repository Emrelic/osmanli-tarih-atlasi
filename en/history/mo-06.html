6th century BC

  • 600 BC Nebuchadnezzar attacked the Egyptian frontier; in an open battle both sides suffered heavy losses and Babylon withdrew
  • 599 BC The fortresses of Urartu were destroyed and burned; the state collapsed
  • 599 BC La Blanca (San Marcos, Pacific Guatemala): Middle Preclassic regional centre, Mound 1: the decline of La Blanca
  • 599 BC Marseille, Massalia: Massalia (Marseille) period begins
  • 598 BC The Phocaeans founded Massalia (Marseille)
  • 16 March 596 BC Nebuchadnezzar took Jerusalem; King Jehoiachin was taken captive and Zedekiah took his place
  • 592 BC Psamtik II launched a campaign into Nubia: Kush's pressure on the Egyptian frontier was broken
  • 589 BC Old Van (Tushpa), Tushpa (Van Fortress): Kingdom of Urartu period ends
  • 585 BC Nebuchadnezzar began the siege of Tyre; the siege lasted thirteen years
  • 585 BC Jerusalem was destroyed and Solomon's Temple burned; the Kingdom of Judah came to an end and its people were deported to Babylon
  • 585 BC Jerusalem: Kingdom of Judah period ends
  • 584 BC Battle of the Halys (Kızılırmak): after a solar eclipse Alyattes and Cyaxares made peace; the Halys became the Lydian-Median frontier
  • 574 BC Veii: Etruscan City-States (Twelve-City League) period begins
  • 569 BC Amasis defeated Apries and took the Egyptian throne; Greek merchants were settled at Naucratis
  • 559 BC Croesus ascended the Lydian throne; he made Ephesus and the Greek cities of Ionia tributary
  • 558 BC Achaemenid Empire / Cyrus II's kingdom of Persis: Cyrus II accedes to the Persian throne (559)
  • 555 BC Nabonidus ascended the Babylonian throne by a coup
  • 551 BC Nabonidus settled in the oasis of Tayma (Arabia) and did not come to Babylon; the Akitu festival could not be held
  • 549 BC The Sparta–Tegea treaty: the core of the Peloponnesian League took shape
  • 549 BC Ecbatana (Hamadan): Cyrus's capture of the Median capital: begins
  • 546 BC Croesus crossed the Halys and took Pteria; the Battle of Pteria against Cyrus ended indecisively
  • 545 BC Pasargadae: Achaemenid (Persian) Empire period begins
  • 542 BC Rise of Magadha (Bimbisara and Ajatashatru): Bimbisara ascends the throne of Magadha
  • 538 BC Battle of Opis: Cyrus defeated the Babylonian army on the bank of the Tigris
  • 538 BC Elam (Old Elam - Neo-Elam): the Neo-Elamite period ends
  • 538 BC Ur: Achaemenid (Persian) Empire period begins
  • 12 October 538 BC The Persian army under Gobryas (Ugbaru) entered Babylon without a fight; the Neo-Babylonian Empire came to an end
  • 29 October 538 BC Cyrus entered Babylon; control of Babylon passed to the Achaemenids and Gobryas was appointed governor
  • 537 BC Decree of Cyrus: the Jews exiled in Babylon were able to return to Jerusalem, and the rebuilding of the Temple was permitted
  • 537 BC The Phoenician city-states (Tyre, Sidon, Byblos) accepted Persian rule; they became the core of the Persian fleet
  • 534 BC Battle of Alalia: the Etruscans and Carthaginians drove the Phocaeans out of Corsica
  • 529 BC Cyrus died; his son Cambyses ascended the throne
  • 524 BC Memphis (Men-nefer / Mit Rahina): Achaemenid Egypt (27th Dynasty, 1st Persian rule) period begins
  • 524 BC Persian King Cambyses conquered Egypt: the 26th Dynasty ended at Sais
  • 522 BC Cambyses' Kushite campaign failed from thirst and lack of provisions; Egypt's southern border remained at Elephantine
  • 11 March 521 BC Gaumata (Bardiya) seized the Persian throne; Cambyses died on his return from Egypt
  • 29 September 521 BC Darius and six nobles killed Gaumata; Darius I ascended the Persian throne
  • 18 December 521 BC Darius retook Babylon; Nidintu-Bêl (Nebuchadnezzar III) was captured and killed
  • 8 May 520 BC Darius's army defeated the Median rebel Phraortes (Fravartiš)
  • 28 December 520 BC Darius's general defeated the Margianian rebel Frada; the last great uprising was suppressed
  • 517 BC Persepolis: Achaemenid (Persian) Empire period begins
  • 512 BC Darius crossed the Ister (Danube) and set out on the Scythian campaign; Thrace was added to the Persian satrapies
  • 508 BC The last king, Tarquinius Superbus, was expelled; the monarchy ended and the Republic began
  • 508 BC The first treaty between Rome and Carthage (Polybius 3.22)
  • 507 BC Cleisthenes' reform: the institutional foundation of Athenian democracy was laid
  • 507 BC Rome: Monarchy -> Republic (first consuls) - WRITTEN TRADITION: Rome: the Monarchy ends, the Republic begins (traditional)
  • 504 BC The Peloponnesian League's campaign against Athens collapsed with Corinth's withdrawal
