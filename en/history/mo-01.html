1st century BC

  • 99 BC Teotihuacan: begins
  • 99 BC Kaminaljuyu (Guatemala Valley): Providencia phase / Late Preclassic rise: the Providencia phase ends
  • 95 BC Ptolemy Apion bequeathed Cyrene to Rome
  • 94 BC Sulla seated Ariobarzanes on the throne of Cappadocia
  • 91 BC Sulla and the Parthian envoy Orobazus met on the Euphrates; first Roman-Parthian diplomatic contact
  • 89 BC Lex Iulia: Rome granted citizenship to its Italian allies (Social War)
  • 87 BC Mithridates invaded the province of Asia; the Ephesian Vespers (massacre of 80,000 Romans)
  • 85 BC Athens: Roman Republic period begins
  • 84 BC Peace of Dardanus: Mithridates withdrew from Asia and Bithynia
  • 81 BC Burebista united the Dacian and Getic tribes
  • 73 BC Nicomedes IV bequeathed his kingdom to Rome
  • 69 BC Antiochus I Theos ascended the throne of Commagene
  • 65 BC Pompey defeated Mithridates; Pontus was bound to Rome
  • 63 BC Pompey recognized Commagene as a friend and ally of Rome
  • 62 BC Mithridates VI died at Panticapaeum; the Bosporus was left to Pharnaces
  • 62 BC Pompey united Bithynia with western Pontus and founded the province of Bithynia et Pontus
  • 57 BC Caesar's Gallic campaign began: the Helvetii were defeated
  • 57 BC Rome took Cyprus from Ptolemy and annexed it; Cato was sent to the island
  • 54 BC Gabinius restored Ptolemy XII to the throne of Egypt with a Roman army; a Roman garrison remained at Alexandria
  • 52 BC Battle of Carrhae (Harran): Surena destroyed the Roman army of Crassus
  • 51 BC Alesia: Caesar besieged and defeated Vercingetorix; the resistance of Gaul was broken
  • 48 BC Marseille, Massalia: Massalia (Marseille) → Roman Republic
  • 47 BC Pharsalus: Caesar defeated Pompey
  • 46 BC The Alexandrian War: Caesar defeated Ptolemy XIII and left Egypt to Cleopatra
  • 21 May 46 BC Zela: Caesar defeated Pharnaces II ("veni vidi vici")
  • 45 BC Thapsus: Caesar defeated Juba I; eastern Numidia became the province of Africa Nova
  • 17 March 44 BC Munda: Caesar defeated the sons of Pompey
  • 43 BC Julius Caesar had Corinth refounded as a Roman colony
  • 43 BC Burebista fell victim to assassination: the Dacian kingdom was fragmented
  • 15 March 43 BC Caesar was assassinated in the Senate on the Ides of March (15 March)
  • 41 BC Philippi: Octavian and Antonius defeated Brutus and Cassius
  • 39 BC Pacorus and Labienus invaded Syria and Palestine
  • 35 BC Antonius made Archelaus king of Cappadocia
  • 35 BC Antony gave Cleopatra Cyprus, Phoenicia, Coele-Syria and part of Cilicia as a gift
  • 33 BC The Donations of Alexandria: Antony proclaimed Cleopatra and her children rulers of the eastern kingdoms
  • 32 BC Bocchus II died; Mauretania passed to the control of Octavian
  • 2 September 30 BC The Battle of Actium: Octavian defeated Antony and Cleopatra
  • 29 BC Beginning of Roman Egypt - Octavian takes Alexandria: Rome takes Egypt (Octavian)
  • 1 August 29 BC Octavian entered Alexandria; the Ptolemaic state came to an end and Egypt passed to Rome
  • 28 BC Cornelius Gallus became the first prefect of Egypt; he suppressed the revolts of Heroonpolis and Thebes, and the frontier with Kush was secured
  • 26 BC Rome, Alexandria: Roman Republic → ownerless interval
  • 16 January 26 BC Octavian took the title "Augustus": the Principate (imperial period) began
  • 24 BC With the death of Amyntas Galatia became a Roman province
  • 24 BC Under the leadership of the Candace, Kush took Elephantine, Syene and Philae; Augustus's statues were overthrown
  • 22 BC Petronius campaigned against Kush and took Pselchis, Premnis and Napata; he placed a garrison at Ibrim
  • 20 BC The Peace of Samos: Kushite envoys obtained peace from Augustus; Rome lifted the tribute it had imposed on Kush, and the frontier was drawn in Lower Nubia (Hiera Sykaminos)
  • 19 BC Treaty between Augustus and Phraates IV: the Roman standards of Crassus were returned
