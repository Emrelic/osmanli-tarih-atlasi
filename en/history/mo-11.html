11th century BC

  • 1099 BC Tiglath-Pileser I marched west and took tribute from the Phoenician cities on the Mediterranean coast
  • 1099 BC Slab Grave culture, Mongolia: the Slab Grave culture begins
  • 1099 BC Susa: Middle Elamite Kingdom (Kidinuid, Igehalkid, Sutrukid) → Neo-Elamite Kingdom
  • 1080 BC Yinxu (Anyang, Late Shang capital): Di Yi/Di Xin phase: end of the Shang dynasty (final phase of Yinxu)
  • 1079 BC Conquest of Nubia by New Kingdom Egypt / Egyptian occupation (post-Kerma): the Egyptian occupation of Nubia ends
  • 1069 BC Kingdom of Kush — Napata period: begins
  • 1068 BC The New Kingdom ended: in the last years of Ramesses XI, Smendes (Tanis) in the Delta and the High Priest of Amun at Thebes were effectively independent
  • 1049 BC Sanxingdui (Guanghan, Sichuan) civilization: the Sanxingdui civilization ends
  • 1009 BC Northern Kyushu (Uki-Kunden Shell Midden), arrival of rice cultivation: begins
  • 1009 BC Sechín Alto: Sechín Alto: latest Haldas date
