9th century

  • 802 Jayavarman II had the royal linga erected on Mount Kulen (Mahendraparvata) and freed the Khmer kingdom from Javanese domination; the Angkor period began
  • 809 Khan Krum took Sofia (Serdica)
  • 812 The Carolingian March of Tuscany (Tuscia) is established: Boniface of Bavaria becomes count of Lucca
  • 22 June 813 Battle of Versinikia: Michael Rangabe was defeated by Krum
  • 28 January 814 Charlemagne dies at Aachen and is buried the same day in the Church of the Virgin Mary
  • 14 April 814 Khan Krum died: preparations for the siege of Constantinople were left unfinished
  • 11 August 815 Ibrahim b. Musa seized Sana'a (200/815): Yemen slipped out of the hands of the Abbasid governors
  • 28 June 819 Caliph al-Ma'mun gave the grandsons of Saman-khuda governorships in Transoxiana; the foundation of the Samanid dynasty was laid
  • 1 February 820 Founding of Zabīd: administrative seat of the Ziyadids in Tihāma
  • 17 June 820 Isa b. Yazid al-Jaludi eliminated Hamdawayh (205/820): Sana'a returned to the administration of the Abbasid governors
  • 821 The Tang-Tibetan treaty was sworn at Chang'an (821)
  • 822 Last mention of the Avar Khaganate: the Hungarian plain no longer had a single owner (DECLARED GAP)
  • 825 The Mon capital Hamsavati (Pegu) was founded by the twin brothers Samala and Vimala of Thaton (825, according to the chronicle)
  • 2 April 827 The Aghlabid–Euphemius fleet conquered Mazara (Mazara del Vallo): the conquest of Sicily began
  • 2 April 827 The Andalusian Arabs settled in Crete: the Emirate of Crete was born
  • 18 February 831 Palermo surrendered to the Aghlabid Muslims
  • 838 Al-Mu'tasim entered Anatolia, besieged and captured Ammuriya (Amorion)
  • 840 After the fall of the Uyghur state to the Kyrgyz (840), the Karakhanid dynasty emerged in the Kashgar-Balasagun region
  • 842 The death of the Tibetan ruler was reported to the Tang (842)
  • 14 February 842 Oaths of Strasbourg: Charles the Bald and Louis the German swear an alliance at Strasbourg
  • 843 Messina was conquered; the Strait of Messina between Sicily and Italy came under Muslim control
  • 10 August 843 Treaty of Verdun: the Carolingian Empire is divided into three (West Francia, Middle Francia, East Francia)
  • 849 The city of Pagan (Arimaddanapura) was founded: at the confluence of the Irrawaddy and Chindwin, a new center born of the Pyu collapse
  • 850 The end of Moche culture (c. AD 850): the Moche state centred on Huacas de Moche disintegrated
  • 29 September 855 Partition of Prüm: the Rhône lands of Lyon and Provence-Burgundy fell to Charles, the youngest son of Lothair I
  • 13 February 858 Cináed mac Ailpín died at the palace of Forteviot (13 February)
  • 18 June 860 The Rus appeared before Constantinople for the first time
  • 17 March 861 Ya'qub ibn Layth al-Saffar became master of Sistan; the Saffarid dynasty was founded
  • 862 The Varangians are called to Novgorod under Rurik: the beginning of the Rurikid dynasty
  • 863 At the invitation of Rastislav, Cyril and Methodius come to Moravia; the Moravians become Christian
  • 867 The Aghlabid fleet besieged Dubrovnik; the city was saved by the Byzantine fleet
  • 29 August 870 The Aghlabid fleet definitively seized Malta (28 Ramadan 256)
  • 872 Harald Fairhair unifies Norway by the Battle of Hafrsfjord (traditionally 872)
  • 16 October 874 Caliph al-Mu'tamid gave the administration of Transoxiana to Nasr ibn Ahmad; his brother Isma'il became governor of Bukhara
  • 25 December 875 Charles the Bald is crowned with the imperial crown at Rome
  • 1 April 876 Ya'qub ibn Layth was defeated for the first time by Abbasid forces at Dayr al-Aqul, near Baghdad
  • 878 Battle of Edington (Ethandun) and the Peace of Wedmore: Alfred defeated the Danes (Guthrum)
  • 21 May 878 The Arabs took Syracuse
  • 879 Huang Chao took Guangzhou; the governor of Lingnan, Li Tiao, was taken prisoner (879)
  • 8 January 881 Xizong fled from Chang'an; Huang Chao's vanguard entered the city
  • 16 January 881 Huang Chao became emperor in the Hanyuan Palace: Great Qi (Da Qi)
  • 882 Oleg descends from Novgorod and takes Kiev; the Rurikid dynasty is established in Kiev
  • 18 May 883 Li Keyong's army entered Chang'an; Huang Chao burned the city and fled
  • 886 Alfred took London from the Danes; the Alfred–Guthrum peace changed the boundaries in Alfred's favor
  • 1 December 886 Through an agreement with the Abbasids, rule over Egypt was recognised; Syria, Palestine and al-Jazira were granted to the Tulunids for thirty years (Rajab 273 / December 886; first day of the month).
  • 16 May 888 Isma'il ibn Ahmad defeated his elder brother Nasr near Samarkand and took him prisoner, leaving him in his post
  • 21 June 888 Al-Muwaffaq granted Kirman, Fars and Khurasan to Amr ibn Layth as an iqta'
  • 28 January 893 Charles the Simple is crowned king at Reims; a rival kingship begins against Odo
  • 23 March 893 The Samanid emir Isma'il b. Ahmad captured Talas (Taraz); the Samanids reached their widest frontiers in the east
  • 894 Svatopluk, king of Great Moravia, dies
  • 895 The Magyar tribes under Árpád cross the Carpathians and settle in the Carpathian Basin (895-896)
  • 2 March 895 The Pechenegs brought the steppe between the Don and the Danube under their rule (282/895)
  • 19 February 898 Caliph al-Mu'tadid took the governorship of Transoxiana from Isma'il ibn Ahmad and gave it to Amr ibn Layth
  • 19 April 900 Isma'il ibn Ahmad defeated and captured the Saffarid emir Amr ibn Layth near Balkh; Khurasan passed to the Samanids
