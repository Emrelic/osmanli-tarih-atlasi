7th century

  • 605 During the rebuilding of the Kaaba, the dispute over placing the Black Stone was settled by Muhammad's arbitration
  • 10 April 605 Yangdi ordered the construction of the Eastern Capital (Dongjing) at Luoyang
  • 14 April 605 Work began on opening the Tongji Canal (the first branch of the Grand Canal)
  • 610 First revelation: the first five verses of Surah al-ʿAlaq in the cave of Hira (year 610, last ten days of Ramadan)
  • 5 October 610 Heraclius overthrew Phocas and ascended the throne
  • 617 The Quraysh boycott of the Banu Hashim and Banu al-Muttalib began (Shi'b Abi Talib, 617-620)
  • 12 December 617 Li Yuan's armies took the Sui capital Daxing (Chang'an)
  • 11 April 618 Yangdi was killed at Jiangdu (Yangzhou); the Sui effectively collapsed
  • 620 Abu Talib died (about a month after Khadija, Shawwal)
  • 620 Journey to Ta'if: Thaqif rejected his call and Muhammad was stoned (Shawwal of the 10th year of prophethood, May-June 620)
  • 620 Six men of the Khazraj tribe of Yathrib accepted Islam during the pilgrimage season (11th year of prophethood)
  • 19 April 620 Khadija died (10 Ramadan / 19 April 620); Abu Talib about a month later: the "Year of Sorrow"
  • 621 First Pledge of Aqaba: twelve men of Yathrib (ten of the Khazraj, two of the Aws) met Muhammad at Aqaba; Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr was sent to Medina
  • 622 Second Pledge of Aqaba: seventy-five people of Yathrib, two of them women, met Muhammad at Aqaba after the pilgrimage (13th year of prophethood)
  • 13 September 622 After staying three days in the cave of Thawr, Muhammad and Abu Bakr set out for Yathrib (1 Rabi' al-awwal / 13 September 622)
  • 2 July 626 The Xuanwu Gate incident: Li Shimin killed the crown prince Jiancheng and Yuanji
  • 29 July 626 The Avars and Persians besieged Constantinople
  • 25 September 626 The Wei River (Bianqiao) treaty: Taizong and Jieli Khagan swore an oath by sacrificing a white horse
  • 629 Xuanzang's route (Taxila – Kashmir – Poonch – Rajauri – Jalandhar – Kullu – Mathura – Thanesar – Kannauj)
  • 629 The Khazars captured Tiflis
  • 634 Tibet sent an envoy to the Tang for the first time (Songtsen Gampo, 634)
  • 7 March 634 Khalid b. al-Walid conquered Tadmur (Palmyra) by peace treaty (13/634)
  • 19 September 640 Hou Junji took Gaochang; the Tang established Xizhou on its lands
  • 641 Princess Wencheng was married to Songtsen Gampo (641)
  • 29 July 661 Hasan b. Ali left the caliphate to Mu'awiya; Mu'awiya received the oath of allegiance in Kufa, Kufa passed to Umayyad rule
  • 23 April 682 The Kedukan Bukit inscription: the king of Srivijaya set out on a siddhayatra expedition by water and land on 23 April 682; a month later he returned victorious to Srivijaya
  • 27 August 683 Battle of al-Ḥarra: the Umayyad army defeated the people of Medina
  • 689 Malayu (Jambi) was incorporated into Srivijaya (between 689 and 692, according to the testimony of Yijing)
  • 697 Venice elects its first doge: Paulo Lucio Anafesto
  • 700 Monte Albán: owner period ends — Zapotec State (Monte Alban)
