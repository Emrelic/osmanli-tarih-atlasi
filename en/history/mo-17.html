17th century BC

  • 1649 BC Egypt was divided in two: the Hyksos (Fifteenth Dynasty) ruled Lower and Middle Egypt from Avaris, and the Seventeenth Dynasty at Thebes ruled Upper Egypt
  • 1649 BC Cerro Sechín: the Cerro Sechín period ends
  • 1649 BC Hittite Old Kingdom — reign of Hattusili I (Labarna): the period of Hattusili I (hMC traditional value)
  • 1629 BC Hattusili I crossed the Taurus and campaigned in northern Syria; Alalakh was destroyed, the kingdom of Aleppo (Yamhad) was attacked
  • 1624 BC San José Mogote: village settlement and abandonment of the ceremonial centre after Rosario: the village of San José Mogote is founded
  • 1612 BC Akrotiri (Thera/Santorini) — Minoan eruption: volcanic eruption of Thera (Santorini)
