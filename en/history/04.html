4th century

  • 304 Chengdu: ownerless interval → Cheng-Han (成漢)
  • 309 Shapur II ascended the Sasanian throne from his birth (309-379)
  • 347 Chengdu: Cheng-Han (成漢) period ends
  • 349 The Aksumite king Ezana defeated Kush; the Kingdom of Meroe came to an end (c. AD 350)
  • 349 Meroe: owner period ends — Kingdom of Kush (Napata–Meroe)
  • 351 Xi'an (Chang'an): ownerless interval → Former Qin (前秦)
  • 356 Cologne (Colonia Claudia Ara Agrippinensium): ownerless interval → Roman Empire (Principate-Dominate, combined)
  • 358 Julian settled the Salian Franks in Toxandria as Roman foederati
  • 359 Shapur II besieged and took Amida (August-October 359)
  • 363 Treaty of Jovian: Nisibis and the five regions beyond the Tigris were ceded to the Sasanians
  • 26 June 363 Julian died near Samarra; the Sasanian war moved towards its end
  • 375 The Huns crossed the Don and attacked the Alans and Ostrogoths: the beginning of the Great Migration
  • 376 The Tervingi Goths crossed the Danube and settled on Roman territory
  • 386 Xi'an (Chang'an): Former Qin (前秦) period ends
  • 387 Roman-Sasanian treaty partitioning Armenia (Theodosius I - Shapur III)
  • 391 Theophilus had the Serapeum in Alexandria demolished: the pagan cult centre in Egypt was closed
  • 395 Rome: Roman Empire (Principate-Dominate, combined) → ownerless interval
  • 17 January 395 Theodosius I died; the empire was permanently divided in two, and Egypt remained in the Eastern Roman Empire (Arcadius)
  • 397 Dunhuang, Zhangye (Ganzhou): ownerless interval → Northern Liang (北涼)
  • 399 Faxian sets out for India to seek Vinaya texts
  • 400 Dunhuang: Northern Liang (北涼) → ownerless interval
