22nd century BC

  • 2180 BC The Old Kingdom collapsed: after Pepi II the central state dissolved and the First Intermediate Period began
  • 2166 BC Los Millares: the settlement ends
  • 2159 BC The Ninth and Tenth Dynasties at Herakleopolis (Henen-nesut): the Kheti dynasty ruled Northern Egypt
  • 2153 BC Uruk: ownerless interval → Gutium (Gutian) Rule
  • 2152 BC Gutium: according to the Sumerian King List the Gutians held kingship in Sumer after Akkad
  • 2143 BC Girsu (center of the Lagash state): ownerless interval → Lagash II Period (Ur-Baba, Gudea, Ur-Ningirsu)
  • 2141 BC Akkadian Empire (Sargon): Akkadian Empire: the reign of the last king Shudurul ends
  • 2124 BC The Eleventh Dynasty began at Thebes: the Intef dynasty founded an independent kingdom in Upper Egypt
  • 2111 BC Uruk: Gutium (Gutian) Rule → ownerless interval
  • 2110 BC Utu-hegal, king of Uruk, defeated Tirigan, king of Gutium, and ended Gutian dominion
