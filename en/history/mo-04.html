4th century BC

  • 400 BC Battle of Cunaxa: Cyrus the Younger was defeated by his elder brother Artaxerxes II and killed
  • 395 BC Veii fell to Rome: the first great city of Etruria in the south was lost
  • 395 BC Veii: Etruscan City-States (Twelve-City League) → Roman Republic
  • 391 BC Corinth united with Argos (Argos-Corinth isopoliteia)
  • 388 BC Allia: the Gauls defeated the Roman army and sacked the city
  • 386 BC King's Peace (Peace of Antalcidas): the Greek cities of Anatolia and Cyprus were left to Persia
  • 378 BC Pelopidas liberated Thebes; the Spartan garrison was expelled from the Cadmea
  • 376 BC The Second Athenian League was founded (decree of Aristoteles)
  • 374 BC Burning of the first plaza of San Martin Tilcajete by Monte Alban: the plaza is burned (Monte Alban attack)
  • 372 BC Nectanebo I repelled the Persian invasion (Pharnabazus-Iphicrates)
  • 370 BC Leuctra: the Theban army defeated the Spartans; Sparta lost its land supremacy
  • 369 BC Messenia broke away from Sparta; Messene was founded
  • 368 BC The Arcadian League was founded: this part of the Peloponnesian League was dissolved
  • 361 BC Mantinea: Epaminondas died; Boeotian hegemony came to an end
  • 359 BC Teos set out on a Syrian campaign; Nectanebo II seized the throne in a coup, and Agesilaus changed sides
  • 358 BC Philip II became king of Macedonia
  • 356 BC Philip took Amphipolis from Athens
  • 354 BC The Social War ended: Byzantium, Chios, Rhodes and Cos broke away from the Second Athenian League
  • 349 BC Kaminaljuyu (Guatemala Valley): Providencia phase / Late Preclassic rise: Kaminaljuyú: the Providencia phase
  • 347 BC Destruction of Olynthus: the Chalcidian League passed to Macedonia
  • 344 BC Artaxerxes III took and destroyed Sidon; the revolt of Tennes was suppressed
  • 342 BC Artaxerxes III reconquered Egypt; Nectanebo II fled to Nubia
  • 340 BC Philip subjugated Thrace together with the Odrysian dynasty
  • 340 BC Slab Grave culture, Mongolia: Slab Grave burials end (median)
  • 338 BC Philip II defeated the Scythian king Ateas
  • 337 BC The League of Corinth was founded: the Greek cities were united under the hegemony of Philip II
  • 337 BC Chaeronea: Philip defeated the Athenian and Theban army and took control of Greece
  • 337 BC The Latin League was dissolved: Latium was bound to Rome
  • 337 BC Artaxerxes III died; Artaxerxes IV (Arses) ascended the throne
  • 335 BC Philip II was assassinated at Aegae; Alexander ascended the throne
  • 335 BC Darius III ascended the Persian throne (murder of Arses)
  • 334 BC Thebes was destroyed on Alexander's order
  • 333 BC The western Anatolian coast passed to Alexander: Ephesus, Miletus and Halicarnassus
  • 333 BC Battle of the Granicus: Alexander defeated the Persian satraps
  • 1 November 332 BC Battle of Issus: Alexander defeated Darius III; Darius abandoned his family and fled
  • 331 BC Alexander entered Egypt; the Persian satrap Mazaces surrendered, and Egypt passed to Macedonian rule
  • 331 BC Siege of Tyre: Alexander took the island city by means of a causeway and destroyed it
  • 330 BC Alexander consulted the Oracle of Ammon at the Siwa Oasis
  • 1 October 330 BC Battle of Gaugamela: Alexander decisively defeated the army of Darius III
  • 22 October 330 BC Alexander entered Babylon; Mazaeus surrendered the city and the Satrapy of Babylon passed to Alexander
  • 22 October 330 BC Babylon, Uruk: Achaemenid (Persian) Empire → Empire of Alexander the Great
  • 329 BC Alexander reached Persepolis; the city and treasury fell into Macedonian hands
  • 329 BC Burning of the palaces of Persepolis (April)
  • 329 BC Achaemenid Empire / Cyrus II's kingdom of Persis: the Achaemenid state ends (Darius III, 330)
  • 329 BC Ecbatana, Hamadan: Achaemenid (Persian) Empire → Empire of Alexander the Great
  • 1 July 329 BC Darius III was killed by the men of Bessus, satrap of Bactria; the Achaemenid dynasty ended
  • 326 BC Bactria and Sogdiana submitted to Alexander; Alexander married Roxane
  • 325 BC Hydaspes: Porus was defeated; the Punjab was bound to Alexander
  • 325 BC On the bank of the Hyphasis (Beas) the army refused to advance: the eastern limit of Alexander
  • 325 BC Nanda dynasty (Magadha): Dhana Nanda in Magadha (age of Alexander)
  • 322 BC Lamian War: the Greek cities rebelled against Macedonian hegemony after Alexander's death
  • 10 June 322 BC Alexandria: Ptolemaic (Lagid) Kingdom period begins
  • 321 BC Ptolemy added Cyrene (Cyrenaica) to Egypt
  • 5 September 321 BC Crannon: the Lamian War was lost; a Macedonian garrison entered Athens and democracy was suspended
  • 320 BC Perdiccas's Egyptian campaign failed at the crossing of the Nile; Perdiccas was killed by his own army
  • 320 BC Maurya Empire: Maurya Empire: the Chandragupta period begins
  • 319 BC Settlement of Triparadisus: Antigonus Monophthalmus became strategos of Asia
  • 315 BC Cassander had Thebes refounded
  • 315 BC Gabiene: Antigonus defeated Eumenes and became master of Asia as far as Iran
  • 314 BC Cassander founded Thessalonica
  • 313 BC Tyre passed to Antigonus: Phoenicia was taken from Ptolemy
  • 311 BC The Battle of Gaza: Ptolemy and Seleucus defeated Demetrius (Syria briefly in Ptolemaic hands)
  • 311 BC Ecbatana, Hamadan: Seleucus Nicator's rule begins (Media)
  • 310 BC Peace of the Diadochi: Antigonus, Cassander and Ptolemy recognized each other's rule over Asia, Europe and Egypt
  • 310 BC Babylon, Uruk: Empire of Alexander the Great (Argead nominal) → Seleucid Empire
  • 309 BC Alexander IV was killed with his mother Roxane; the Argead dynasty ended
  • 309 BC Pasargadae, Susa: Empire of Alexander the Great (Argead nominal) → ownerless interval; Alexander IV was killed
  • 308 BC Lysimachus founded Lysimachia: a capital in the Thracian Chersonese
  • 305 BC Salamis in Cyprus: Demetrius defeated Ptolemy; Antigonus and Demetrius took the title of king
  • 304 BC Ptolemy proclaimed himself king; the Ptolemaic dynasty was formally established
  • 304 BC Seleucus I founded Seleucia on the Tigris
  • 304 BC Seleucus I made a treaty with Chandragupta Maurya in India; Arachosia, Gedrosia and Paropamisadae were ceded to the Mauryas
  • 303 BC The siege of Rhodes by Demetrius Poliorcetes ended in failure
  • 302 BC Dura-Europos: Seleucid Empire period begins
  • 301 BC Mithridates I Ktistes founded the dynasty of Pontus
