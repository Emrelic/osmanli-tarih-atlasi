21st century BC

  • 2099 BC Puzur-Inshushinak, the last Awan king in Elam (Susa), freed himself from Akkadian overlordship
  • 2091 BC Shulgi ascended the Ur III throne; he established the state's centralized administrative system
  • 2069 BC Xia dynasty (written tradition / Periodization Project chronology — NOT an archaeological date): the Xia dynasty begins (written tradition, Periodization Project)
  • 2059 BC Troy 'Proto-IV' (hypothesis) and Troy IV: the Troy IV phase begins
  • 2054 BC Mentuhotep II took the throne at Thebes; the Middle Kingdom period began and war against Herakleopolis intensified
  • 2050 BC First Intermediate Period: ends
  • 2050 BC Middle Kingdom: begins
  • 2049 BC Liangzhu: owner period ends — Liangzhu Culture
  • 2031 BC Shu-Sin built the "Muriq-tidnim" wall against the Amorites
  • 2025 BC Ibbi-Sin ascended the throne as the last king of Ur III
  • 2024 BC Uruk, Ur: Ur III (Third Dynasty of Ur) → Kingdom of Larsa
  • 2023 BC Susa: Ur III (Third Dynasty of Ur) → Elam — Shimashki Dynasty
  • 2021 BC Girsu (center of the Lagash state), Umma: Ur III (Third Dynasty of Ur) period ends
  • 2018 BC Nippur: Ur III (Third Dynasty of Ur) period ends
  • 2016 BC Ishbi-Erra broke away from Ur III at Isin; the Dynasty of Isin was founded
  • 2015 BC Mentuhotep II defeated Herakleopolis and reunified Egypt (Sematawy)
  • 2003 BC Uruk, Ur: Kingdom of Larsa → ownerless interval
  • 2001 BC Ishbi-Erra expelled the Elamite garrison from Ur; he took Isin, Ur and Nippur under his dominion
