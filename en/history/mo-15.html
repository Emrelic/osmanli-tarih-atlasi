15th century BC

  • 1499 BC The kingdom of Mitanni (Hanigalbat) was founded in the Khabur-Euphrates region
  • 1499 BC Pampa de las Hamacas ceremonial centres (Jequetepeque, Formative): the Hamacas phase begins (Jequetepeque)
  • 1499 BC Dholavira: Dholavira Stage VII ends
  • 1499 BC Shang dynasty/period boundaries (Erligang-Yinxu framework): the Shang period begins (14C model)
  • 1499 BC Napata (Jebel Barkal): begins
  • 1499 BC Kerma (Kerma / Doukki Gel): Kingdom of Kerma (Classic Kerma) → ownerless interval
  • 1498 BC Thutmose I made an Asiatic campaign reaching the banks of the Euphrates (Naharin)
  • 1479 BC Conquest of Nubia by New Kingdom Egypt / Egyptian occupation (post-Kerma): the Egyptian occupation of Nubia begins
  • 1474 BC Uruk: First Dynasty of the Sealand → ownerless interval
  • 1464 BC Uruk, Ur: ownerless interval → Kassite Babylonian Kingdom
  • 1456 BC Thutmose III defeated the Canaanite coalition led by Kadesh at Megiddo; Canaan-Syria came under Egyptian rule
  • 1449 BC The destruction of the Minoan palaces on Crete; Mycenaean control began at Knossos
  • 1449 BC Kingdom (culture) of Kerma (Kush): ends
  • 1446 BC Thutmose III crossed the Euphrates and entered Mitanni territory; he set up a boundary stele in Naharin
  • 1424 BC Amenhotep II reached Napata in Nubia and erected a southern boundary stele: Egypt's southernmost point
