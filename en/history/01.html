1st century

  • 1 Ban Chiang: period ends — Ban Chiang Culture (Northeast Thailand)
  • 9 Teutoburg Forest: Varus' three legions were annihilated; Rome abandoned the Elbe line
  • 9 Pannonia was organized as a separate Roman province
  • 10 January 9 Wang Mang accedes to the throne: Dunhuang and Zhangye (Ganzhou) pass from the Western Han to the ownerless interval
  • 23 Luoyang, Xi'an (Chang'an): ownerless interval → Gengshi Regime (Xuan Han / Lulin)
  • 25 Chengdu: ownerless interval → Chengjia (成家, Gongsun Shu)
  • 25 Xi'an (Chang'an): Red Eyebrows Regime (Jianshi) period begins
  • 26 Pontius Pilate became prefect of Judaea (26-36)
  • 27 Xi'an (Chang'an): Red Eyebrows Regime (Jianshi) → ownerless interval
  • 7 April 30 Crucifixion of Jesus: execution in the time of Pilate (calculated date of 7 April 30 per the TDV; 27 and 33 are alternatives)
  • 36 Chengdu: Chengjia (成家, Gongsun Shu) → ownerless interval
  • 36 Pilate was recalled to Rome by order of Vitellius; Tiberius had died (complaint of the Samaritans)
  • 40 King Ptolemy was killed by Caligula; the independence of Mauretania ended
  • 43 Claudius invaded Britannia
  • 46 Rome made Thrace a province: the Odrysian dynasty ended
  • 1 July 69 Vespasian was proclaimed emperor in Alexandria: Egypt's grain supply decided the fate of the civil war
  • 1 August 70 The Second Temple burned in Jerusalem (Josephus: the tenth day of the month of Lous/Ab; Britannica: August 70)
