12th century BC

  • 1199 BC The Mycenaean palace centers were destroyed: the palatial order collapsed in the Peloponnese
  • 1199 BC Chavín de Huántar: begins
  • 1199 BC Sanganakallu-Kupgal: abandoned (approximate)
  • 1199 BC Evacuation of Hattusa — reading note on the 3rd wave: Hattusa is abandoned; the Hittite Empire collapses (about 1200)
  • 1196 BC Anyang / Yinxu (殷墟), Yinxu (Anyang): ownerless interval ends
  • 1190 BC Kingdom of Ugarit (Gibala/Tell Tweini) - destruction by the Sea Peoples: Ugarit/Tell Tweini is destroyed (age of the Sea Peoples)
  • 1189 BC Ugarit (Ras Shamra): Kingdom of Ugarit period ends
  • 1179 BC Hattusa burned and was abandoned; the Hittite Empire collapsed
  • 1176 BC Ramesses III defeated the Sea Peoples on land (Djahy) and at sea in the Delta
  • 1174 BC At Carchemish Kuzi-Teshub continued the Hittite legacy with the title "Great King"
  • 1157 BC Shutruk-Nahhunte plundered Babylonia; he carried the stelae of Hammurabi and Naram-Sin to Susa
  • 1154 BC Nippur, Uruk, Ur: Kassite Babylonian Kingdom → ownerless interval
  • 1149 BC Nippur: ownerless interval ends
  • 1129 BC Waira-jirca ceramic phase (Huánuco basin, Jancao): Wairajirca pottery ends
  • 1109 BC Nebuchadnezzar I (Isin II) marched on Elam and recovered the statue of Marduk
  • 1105 BC Sanxingdui sacrificial pits (K3, K4, K6, K8) — burial event: the sacrificial pits (K3/K4/K6/K8) are buried (AMS)
