3rd century

  • 212 Constitutio Antoniniana: Roman citizenship was granted to all free inhabitants of the empire
  • 221 Chengdu: ownerless interval → Shu Han
  • 28 April 224 Battle of Hormozgan: Ardashir defeated the Parthian ruler Artabanus V — Isfahan entered the Sasanian core
  • 28 April 224 Babylon, Dura-Europos, Ecbatana, Hamadan, Ctesiphon, Seleucia (on the Tigris), Uruk: Parthian (Arsacid) Empire period ends
  • 231 Ardashir I attacked Roman Mesopotamia; the Roman-Sasanian wars began
  • 240 Hatra fell to the Sasanians (240-241)
  • 242 Urfa (Edessa / Orhai / Vodina): Kingdom of Osroene (Edessa) period ends
  • 244 Gordian III died near Ctesiphon; Philip the Arab made a heavy peace with Shapur
  • 256 Shapur I invaded Syria and plundered Antioch
  • 256 Dura-Europos was taken by the Sasanians
  • 260 Postumus founded the separatist Gallic Empire in Gaul
  • 260 Battle of Edessa: Shapur I captured the Roman emperor Valerian
  • 263 Chengdu: Shu Han → Cao Wei
  • 270 Palmyra (Zenobia) invaded Egypt; the Roman general Probus was defeated
  • 271 Aurelian evacuated Dacia: the area north of the Danube was abandoned
  • 272 Aurelian defeated the Palmyrene Empire: Zenobia's eastern lands returned to Rome
  • 274 Aurelian defeated the Gallic Empire: Rome was reunited
  • 297 Domitius Domitianus was proclaimed emperor in Egypt; Diocletian besieged Thebes and then Alexandria and suppressed the revolt
  • 298 Diocletian drew Egypt's southern border back to the Elephantine/Philae line; Lower Nubia was left to the Nobatae
  • 298 Treaty of Nisibis (Nusaybin): Galerius defeated Narseh; the land beyond the Tigris passed to Rome
