18th century BC

  • 1799 BC Shimao (Shenmu, Shaanxi): abandoned
  • 1799 BC Ban Non Wat: Neolithic 1 phase: rice farmers settle
  • 1794 BC Shamshi-Adad took Mari and installed his son Yasmah-Adad as king of Mari
  • 1793 BC Rim-Sin I, king of Larsa, conquered Isin; the Dynasty of Isin came to an end
  • 1791 BC Old Babylon (First Dynasty of Babylon, Hammurabi): Old Babylon (Babylon I): Hammurabi's reign begins (Middle Chronology)
  • 1775 BC Ashur: Kingdom of Upper Mesopotamia (Shamshi-Adad I) period ends
  • 1769 BC Waira-jirca ceramic phase (Huánuco basin, Jancao): Wairajirca pottery begins (Jancao)
  • 1762 BC Hammurabi took Larsa and defeated Rim-Sin I; southern Mesopotamia was bound to Babylon
  • 1762 BC Ur: ownerless interval → Old Babylonian (Amorite) Kingdom
  • 1760 BC Hammurabi took Mari; two years later he had its walls demolished
  • 1760 BC Mari: ownerless interval → Old Babylonian (Amorite) Kingdom
  • 1753 BC The stele of the Code of Hammurabi was erected
  • 1749 BC Anitta, king of Kussara, made Kanesh his capital and destroyed Hattusa
  • 1749 BC Erlitou (Erlitou culture, Yanshi): Erlitou culture Phase I begins
  • 1749 BC Troy V and the beginning of Troy VI (VIa): end of Troy V / beginning of VI (consensus value)
  • 1731 BC Uruk: ownerless interval → First Dynasty of the Sealand
