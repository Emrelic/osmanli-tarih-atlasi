20th century BC

  • 1999 BC Lothal: decline of Lothal
  • 1984 BC Amenemhat I founded the Twelfth Dynasty; the capital moved to Itj-tawy
  • 1971 BC Ashur (beginning of documents of the Old Assyrian period and the fall of 614 BCE): Ashur: the Old Assyrian eponym list begins (beginning of documentation, not foundation)
  • 1960 BC Naplanum founded the independent Amorite dynasty at Larsa
  • 1929 BC Troy 'Proto-IV' (hypothesis) and Troy IV: the Troy IV phase ends (or later)
  • 1924 BC Gungunum, king of Larsa, took Ur from Isin
  • 1915 BC Troy V and the beginning of Troy VI (VIa): Troy Va phase (single sample)
