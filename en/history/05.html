5th century

  • 31 December 406 The Vandals, Alans, and Suebi crossed the Rhine and entered Gaul
  • 409 The Vandals, Alans, and Suebi crossed into Spain: Hispania was partitioned
  • 418 Rome settled the Visigoths in Aquitania Secunda: the Visigothic kingdom centered on Toulouse
  • 433 The Western Roman Empire ceded Pannonia to the Huns; the Hungarian plain was joined to the Hun kingdom
  • 434 Treaty of Margus: the Huns received 700 pounds of gold annually from Rome
  • 435 Treaty of Hippo: Rome recognized the Vandals as foederati in Numidia and part of Mauretania
  • 441 Attila took the Danube fortresses: Viminacium and Singidunum (Belgrade) passed to the Huns
  • 442 Rome and the Vandals concluded a new treaty: Africa Proconsularis, Byzacena, and part of Numidia were left to the Vandals
  • 448 Peace of Anatolius: a broad strip south of the Danube was ceded to the Huns
  • 451 Battle of the Catalaunian Plains (Châlons): Aetius and Theodoric I drove Attila out of Gaul
  • 451 Battle of Avarayr: Yazdegerd II suppressed the Armenian revolt
  • 8 October 451 Council of Chalcedon: Dioscorus, Patriarch of Alexandria, was condemned; the Church of Egypt separated from the imperial Church
  • 452 Attila entered Italy; Aquileia was destroyed
  • 453 Attila died; the Hunnic state dissolved
  • 455 The Gepid Kingdom was founded: Ardaric defeated the Huns, the Tisza-Transylvania region came under Gepid rule
  • 475 Euric's Visigothic kingdom effectively separated from Rome: the Auvergne was ceded to the Visigoths
  • 480 Julius Nepos was murdered in Dalmatia: the last legitimate emperor of the Western Roman Empire
  • 481 Clovis succeeded his father Childeric as king of the Salian Franks; the centre of power was Tournai
  • 484 Peroz I died in war with the Hephthalites; the Sasanians began paying tribute to the Huns
  • 486 Clovis defeats Syagrius at the Battle of Soissons; the Roman rump principality south of the Somme passes to the Franks
  • 28 August 489 Theodoric led the Ostrogoths into Italy: Odoacer was defeated at the Isonzo and Verona
  • 5 March 493 Theodoric had Odoacer killed in Ravenna; Italy became the Ostrogothic Kingdom
  • 496 Kavad I was deposed over his pro-Mazdakite policy; he returned with Hephthalite help in 498-499
  • 25 December 496 Clovis is baptized by Bishop Remigius at Reims (Christmas)
