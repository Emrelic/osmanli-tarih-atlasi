23rd century BC

  • 2299 BC Under the Sixth Dynasty, a great campaign under Weni to the Canaanite frontier with Nubian troops
  • 2299 BC Sargon subjugated Elam (Awan, Susa, Parahshum)
  • 2299 BC Sargon defeated Lugalzagesi, king of Uruk, and bound the city-states of Sumer to Akkad
  • 2299 BC Shimao (Shenmu, Shaanxi): the city is founded
  • 2299 BC Umma, Ur: ownerless interval → Akkadian Empire
  • 2294 BC Mari: Mari City Kingdom (Mari II, Early Dynastic–Akkadian) period ends
  • 2279 BC The expeditions of Harkhuf, governor of Elephantine, to the land of Yam: diplomatic and commercial ties beyond Nubia
  • 2269 BC Cerro Sechín: Cerro Sechín: earliest calibrated dates
  • 2260 BC Naram-Sin ascended the Akkadian throne; he took the title "king of the four quarters of the world"
  • 2249 BC Naram-Sin suppressed the "Great Revolt": the coalition of city-states led by Kish and Uruk was defeated
  • 2242 BC Old Kingdom (Pyramid Age): ends
  • 2242 BC First Intermediate Period: begins
  • 2229 BC Naram-Sin defeated the Lullubi mountain people in the Zagros (Victory Stele)
  • 2203 BC Lothagam North: owner period ends — Turkana Pastoral Neolithic ('pillar sites' tradition)
