2nd century BC

  • 200 BC Peace after Zama: Carthage lost Hispania, the Mediterranean islands and her fleet, and undertook an indemnity of 10,000 talents
  • 200 BC Rome rewarded Masinissa with the kingdom of Syphax
  • 199 BC The Battle of Panion: Antiochus III defeated Scopas; Coele-Syria passed from the Ptolemies to the Seleucids
  • 199 BC Tepe Yahya: Period I (final occupation phase) ends
  • 196 BC Cynoscephalae: Rome defeated Philip V; Macedonia was forced to withdraw from Greece
  • 196 BC Two Roman provinces were founded in Hispania: Citerior and Ulterior
  • 195 BC Flamininus proclaimed the freedom of the Greeks at the Isthmian Games
  • 27 March 195 BC The Memphis synod issued the Rosetta Decree: Ptolemy V granted tax exemptions to the temples
  • 191 BC Sparta joined the Achaean League: after Nabis, Philopoemen bound Laconia to the League
  • 189 BC Magnesia ad Sipylum: Rome and Pergamum defeated Antiochus III
  • 188 BC Manlius Vulso defeated the Galatian tribes: Galatian independence came under Roman supervision
  • 188 BC Aetolia made peace with Rome: she lost Ambracia and Oeniadae and recognized Roman sovereignty
  • 187 BC Peace of Apamea: Eumenes II gained for Pergamum the Thracian Chersonese, Phrygia, Lydia and most of Mysia
  • 187 BC Treaty of Apamea: the Seleucids withdrew from the west of the Taurus Mountains and paid an indemnity
  • 185 BC The Theban revolt was suppressed: Komanos brought Upper Egypt back under Ptolemaic rule
  • 183 BC Maurya Empire: the Maurya dynasty ends
  • 183 BC Patna (Azimabad): ownerless interval ends
  • 170 BC Mithridates I ascended the Parthian throne; the era of Parthian imperial expansion began
  • 168 BC Antiochus IV attacked Egypt; in the Sixth Syrian War he advanced to Memphis and Naucratis
  • 167 BC The 'Day of Eleusis': the Roman envoy Popilius Laenas forced Antiochus IV to withdraw from Egypt
  • 167 BC Petra: Nabataean Kingdom period begins
  • 22 June 167 BC Pydna: Aemilius Paullus defeated Perseus; the Antigonid kingdom came to an end
  • 166 BC Rome ordered Rhodes to withdraw its governors from Lycia and Caria; the Lycian League (Patara) left Rhodian rule
  • 166 BC Epirus plundered by Rome: seventy towns destroyed
  • 166 BC Macedonia divided by Rome into four autonomous regions (merides)
  • 166 BC Rome took Lycia and Caria from Rhodes; Delos was declared a free port
  • 166 BC Maccabean Revolt: uprising in Judaea against the religious prohibitions of Antiochus IV
  • 149 BC Masinissa attacked Carthaginian territory: Numidian-Carthaginian War
  • 148 BC Rome declared war on Carthage: the Third Punic War began
  • 147 BC Revolt of Andriscus: the kingdom of Macedonia was briefly re-established and suppressed by Metellus
  • 147 BC Ecbatana, Hamadan: Seleucid Empire → Parthian (Arsacid) Empire
  • 145 BC Achaean War: the Achaean League was defeated by Rome and the leagues were dissolved
  • 145 BC The Third Punic War ended: Carthage was destroyed and the province of Africa was founded
  • 145 BC Macedonia became a Roman province
  • 145 BC Corinth: Corinth period ends
  • 140 BC Babylon, Seleucia (on the Tigris), Uruk: Seleucid Empire → Parthian (Arsacid) Empire
  • 137 BC The Parthians captured Demetrius II Nicator; the Seleucids definitively lost Babylonia and Mesopotamia
  • 132 BC Numantia fell: Celtiberia was added to Rome
  • 132 BC Bergama, Pergamon: Kingdom of Pergamon (Attalid) → Roman Republic
  • 131 BC Urfa (Edessa / Orhai / Vodina): Kingdom of Osrhoene (Edessa) period begins
  • 128 BC Aristonicus was defeated; the province of Asia was established from the lands of Pergamum
  • 128 BC Antiochus VII Sidetes died on campaign against Parthia; the Seleucids could not recover Mesopotamia
  • 124 BC At the call of Massalia, Rome entered Gaul
  • 120 BC Zhangye (Ganzhou): Western Han Dynasty period begins
  • 119 BC Mithridates VI Eupator ascended the throne of Pontus
  • 117 BC Narbo Martius was founded: Southern Gaul (Gallia Transalpina) became a Roman province
  • 112 BC Dura-Europos: Seleucid Empire → Parthian (Arsacid) Empire
  • 111 BC Jugurtha took Cirta: Rome intervened in Numidian affairs
  • 110 BC Dunhuang: Western Han Dynasty period begins
  • 108 BC Pairisades V left his kingdom to Mithridates VI: the Bosporus was bound to Pontus
  • 104 BC Bocchus surrendered Jugurtha to Rome; western Numidia was added to Mauretania
