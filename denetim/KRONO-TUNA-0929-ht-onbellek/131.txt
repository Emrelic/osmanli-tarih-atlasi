 Much of the information on early Slav settlements in southeastern Transylvania comes from Zoltán SZÉKELY's excavations and publications: 'Contribuţii la cultura slavă în sec. VII-VIII, în sud-estul Transilvaniei' ( SCIV 13: 1962), 'Die frühesten slawischen Siedlungen in Siebenbürgen' ( Slavia Antiqua 17: 1970). He reported on Fiatfalva in Aluta 6-7: 1974-75.
 An essential study of Bulgarian culture in Transylvania is that of Ž. N. VAŽAROVA, Slavjani i prabâlgari - Slaven und Protobulgaren (Sofia, 1976), which was published in both Bulgarian and German.
