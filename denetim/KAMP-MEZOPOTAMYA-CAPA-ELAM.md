# K1 — Elam phase anchors (attested events)

Chronology: Middle Chronology (MC) for the 3rd and 2nd millennium, as Vallat uses it (Sargon 2334-2279, Ur III 2112-2004, Hammurabi 1792-1750). For the 1st millennium the dates are absolute (eponym/Ptolemy-based).
Types: OLAY = dated event · SALTANAT = reign/synchronism bound · DONEM = scholarly convention.

## (a) Does Vallat tie each phase boundary to an event? (body text)
| Boundary | Vallat | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Start of 1 (Awan, 2400) | "it is only from the beginning of the Akkadian period that Elam really enters into history." The earliest name is Enmebaragesi (~2650). | NO event. 2400 = DONEM |
| 1→2 (2100) | "its last king, Puzur-Inšušinak, is thought to have reigned in the time of Ur-Nammu (2112-2095 B.C.E.)" + "Several years later Šulgi of Ur (2094-47) retook the city with the surrounding region" | SALTANAT synchronism + an undated Šulgi event |
| 2→3 (1970) | "In 2004 the Elamites, allied with the "Susianans" under the leadership of Kindattu, sixth king of Simaški, conquered Ur and led Ibbi-Sin away to Elam as a prisoner." / "When the Elamites ... brought an end to the empire of Ur in 2004 B.C.E., they annexed Susiana, where the Epartid, or sukkalmah, dynasty was founded by the ninth king of Simaški" | YES, OLAY 2004 (Vallat itself places 1970 after it and says the Simaškian and Epartid kings overlapped) |
| 3→4 (1600/1500) | Only "Kuk-Našur II, a contemporary of Ammiṣaduqa, king of Babylon (1646-26 B.C.E.)"; Susa and Haft Tepe show "a break between the period of the sukkalmahs and the Middle Elamite period" | NO event. The last bound is SALTANAT ~1646-1626 |

## (c) Anchors per phase

### 1. Awan (ca. 2400–2100)
- anchor_start: **bulunamadı** (2400 = DONEM). The earliest datable item is Sargon's campaign below. The Sumerian King List gives Awan 356 years, which AW says has "no chronographic worth".
- içerde çapa:
  - **Sargon vs Luh-ishan, king of Elam (MC 2334-2279, within the reign; OLAY).** S8 caption 5: "Luḫ'iš'an son of Ḫišibrasini, king of Elam" and caption 15: "Booty of Awan". AW: "Luḫ-iššan ... is identical with the eighth king of Awan named on the Susa list."
  - **Rimuš campaign (MC 2278-2270; OLAY).** R6: "Emahsini, king of Elam, he captured ... in between Awan and Susa, at the River Middle."
  - **Naram-Sin treaty (MC 2254-2218; OLAY).** V: "In the reign of the Akkadian Naram-Sin a treaty (König, 1965, no. 2) was concluded between Naram-Sin's vassal ruling at Susa and a king of Awan, perhaps Hita."
- anchor_end: **SALTANAT, MC ~2112-2095 (Ur-Nammu).** V: "Puzur-Inšušinak, is thought to have reigned in the time of Ur-Nammu (2112-2095 B.C.E.)". AW adds that this rests on unpublished text 1 B 1537 (Wilcke). The closing event is V's "Šulgi of Ur (2094-47) retook the city", which carries no year-name date. Treat it as a bound only.

### 2. Šimaški (ca. 2100–1970)
- anchor_start: **bulunamadı**. Vallat gives no founding event. The same Puzur-Inšušinak/Šulgi bound from phase 1 applies; V says Puzur-Inšušinak was "subduing also the king of Simaški".
- içerde çapa (Ur III year names, UR3; MC year = accession year − regnal year + 1):
  - **Šulgi 30 = 2065, OLAY:** "Year: The governor of Anšan took the king's daughter into marriage".
  - **Šulgi 34 = 2061, OLAY:** "Year: Anshan was destroyed".
  - **Šu-Suen 7 = 2031, OLAY:** "Year: Šu-Suen, the king of Ur, king of the four quarters, destroyed the land of Zabšali". Zabšali is a land of Šimaški (Vallat: "at least two expeditions").
  - **Ibbi-Suen 9 = 2020, OLAY:** "Year: Ibbi-Suen ... went with massive power to Huhnuri, the bolt to the land of Anšan".
  - **Ibbi-Suen 14 = 2015, OLAY:** "Year: Ibbi-Suen, the king of Ur, overwhelmed Susa, Adamdun and Awan like a storm, subdued them in a single day and seized the lords of their people".
- anchor_end: **OLAY, MC 2004, fall of Ur.** Quotes from V are in table (a). LSU, a literary text: "that Cimacki and Elam, the enemy, should dwell in their place; ... that Ibbi-Suen should be taken to the land Elam in fetters". Note: V says the Šimaškian kings continued at Susa after 2004 (Idaddu I, Tan-Ruhurater), so 2004 is the start of phase 3 more than the end of the Šimaški line.

### 3. Epartid / Sukkalmah (ca. 1970–1600)
- anchor_start: **OLAY, MC 2004.** V: "they annexed Susiana, where the Epartid, or sukkalmah, dynasty was founded". 1970 itself = DONEM.
- içerde çapa:
  - **Gungunum of Larsa, ~1928, OLAY (year name).** AN: "Still later in about 1,928 B.C., Gungunum, fifth king of Larsa, boasts of military victories in Anshan". V: "Several raids are known, particularly those of Gungunum of Larsa".
  - **SALTANAT synchronisms:** "Atta-hušu, contemporary with Sumu-abum (1894-81 B.C.E.)" and "Siruk-tuh ... contemporary with the Assyrian Šamši-Adad I (1813-1781 B.C.E.)" (V).
  - **Siwe-palar-huppak / Hammurabi, OLAY, MC ~1765-1764 (Mari era).** V: "Siwe-palar-huppak's suzerainty was broken by an alliance led by Hammurabi". The primary source, Hammurabi's year 30 year name, was not verified.
- anchor_end: **bulunamadı.** The last bound is V's "Kuk-Našur II, a contemporary of Ammiṣaduqa, king of Babylon (1646-26 B.C.E.)" (SALTANAT). AN: "cuneiform texts continue to mention Elamite rulers until the final quarter of the sixteenth century B.C." (no event).

### 4. Middle Elamite (ca. 1500–1100)
- anchor_start: **bulunamadı** (1500 = DONEM; V says only "a break").
- içerde çapa:
  - **Tepti-ahar × Kadašman-Enlil I (SALTANAT, ~1374-1360).** HT: "On one clay tablet the name of Kadashman Enlil is inscribed with an impression of the seal of King Tepti-ahar." V: "Pahir-iššan married the sister or daughter of Kurigalzu I, whose reign ended in 1374" (SALTANAT).
  - **Kurigalzu II vs Hurbatila at Dur-Šulgi (OLAY, Kurigalzu II 1332-1308).** ABC Chronicle 22 (Chronicle P): "Hurbatila, king of Elam, [wrote] to Kurigalzu (II) ... He went to conquer Elam ... He captured the king of Elam." Caveat: V holds that "Hurpatila was not an Elamite king but king of ... Elammat".
  - **Kidin-Hutran's raids (OLAY, ~1224 and 1222-1217).** ABC Chronicle 22 iv 14-15: "[At the time of] Enlil-nadin-shumi, the king, Kiten-Hutran, king of Elam, attacked. [He went into] action against Nippur (and) scattered its people." A second attack under Adad-šuma-iddina follows at iv 17.
  - **Untaš-Napiriša builds Čoğa Zanbil (building inscriptions, ~1250 relative; V: "built the famous ziggurat at Čoḡa Zanbīl (ca. 1250)").** OLAY, but without an absolute date.
  - **Šutruk-Nahhunte (OLAY, 1158).** V: "In 1158 B.C.E. he killed the Kassite king, Zababa-šuma-iddina, and placed his own eldest son, Kutir-Nahhunte, on the throne of Babylon." V also: "putting an end to the long Kassite dynasty by deposing Enlil-nadin-ahi (1157-55 B.C.E.)".
- anchor_end: **OLAY, Nebuchadnezzar I (1125-1104; the campaign itself has no year).** V's quote is in table (a). BBSt 6 (BM 90858), col. I 41-43: "he turned evil against the king of Elam, and destruction overtook him. And King Nebuchadnezzar triumphed, he captured the land of Elam, he plundered its possessions." AN: "The invasion of Elamite territories (ca. 1,110 B.C.) by Nebuchadrezzer I ... marked the effective end of Elam as an independent power for nearly 300 years."

### 5. Neo-Elamite (1100–539?)
- anchor_start: the same Nebuchadnezzar I event (c. 1110). There is no separate Elamite event. V: "No Elamite document from this first phase of two and a half centuries provides any historical information."
- içerde çapa:
  - **Šamši-Adad V (823-811): Elamite troops fought for Marduk-balassu-iqbi.** V; AN dates it "821 B.C.". The RIMA 3 primary text was not read.
  - **Humban-nikaš I accession (absolute, 743; OLAY).** ABC Chronicle 1 i 9-10: "The fifth year of Nabu-nasir: Humban-nikash (I) ascended the throne in Elam."
  - **Battle of Der (720; OLAY).** ABC Chronicle 1 i 33-35: "The second year of Merodach-baladan (II): Humban-nikash (I), king of Elam, did battle against Sargon (II), king of Assyria, in the district of Der, effected an Assyrian retreat".
  - **Sack of Susa (OLAY, 646 per V).** RINAP5 Ashurbanipal 011 (Prism A) v 126ff: "I conquered the city Susa, a great cult center, the residence of their gods ... I entered its palaces" and vi 27: "the ziggurrat of the city Susa ... I destroyed (it)". V quote is in table (a). This is the NE II/III boundary, not the end of the phase: V says "the Elamite kingdom rose from the ashes with Šutur-Nahhunte".
- anchor_end: **bulunamadı (539 = DONEM).** V: "perhaps by Cyrus in 539" and "These three individuals ruled in succession between 585 and about 539". Leads not yet verified: Elam appears as a province in Darius's Bisitun inscription (522/521, Açina and Martiya revolts). That is a "terminus ante quem" after the end, not the end itself.

## Summary
Vallat ties only these boundaries to an attested event: **2→3 (2004, fall of Ur)**, **4→5 (Nebuchadnezzar I, 1125-1104)** and, inside phase 5, **646 (Ashurbanipal)**. **1→2** rests only on the Ur-Nammu synchronism plus the undated Šulgi recapture of Susa. **Start of 1, 3→4 and end of 5** are conventions with no event. In-phase anchors were found for every phase.
