## 2) ② SALTANAT (yıl sabit değil — SAYILMAZ)

Kaynak: CDLI yıl adı sayfaları; harf (a, b, c…) ile numaralanmış olanların saltanat içindeki sırası bilinmiyor.

- Sargon (akkad): "mu szar-um-gi-ne2 elam{ki} / nim{ki} mu-hul-a — Year in which Sargon destroyed Elam"; "mu ma-ri2{ki} hul-a — Year in which Mari was destroyed"; "mu szar-um-gi-ne2 uruXa{ki} mu-hul-a — Year in which Sargon destroyed Uru'a"; "mu szar-um-gi si-mur-um{ki}-sze3 i3-gin-na-a — Sargon went to Simurrum". https://cdli.ox.ac.uk/wiki/doku.php?id=sargon_year-names
- Naram-Sin (akkad): (b) Maridaban destroyed; (ca) Azuhinum defeated; (d) Šabbunum destroyed; (f) "Year in which Uruk and Nagsu were defeated"; (j) "victorious against Subartu in Azuhunum and took prisoner Dahisz-atal"; (k) reached sources of Tigris/Euphrates, victorious against SzeNAMinda; (n) victory + cedar in Amanus; (q) "victorious against Simurrum in Kiraszeniwe and took prisoner Baba the governor of Simurrum and Dubul the ensi of Arame". https://cdli.ox.ac.uk/wiki/doku.php?id=naram-sin_year-names — Armanum/Ebla ve Magan için CDLI'da yıl adı yok (yalnızca kraliyet yazıtları) → ②.
- Akkad hanedanı, kral bilinmiyor: "mu lugal a-ka3-de3 ba-dab5-a — Year in which the king of Agade was seized"; "mu asz2-na-ak{ki} al-hul-a — Year in which Asznak was destroyed". https://cdli.ox.ac.uk/wiki/doku.php?id=king_unknown_akkad_dynasty_year-names
- Gudea (lagas-ii): yıl 6 "mu anšanki gištukul ba-sig3-a — Anšan was smitten by weapons" — Gudea'nın mutlak MC tarihi belirsiz (CDLI: "Gudea (vers 2120)") → ②. https://cdli.ox.ac.uk/wiki/doku.php?id=year_names_gudea
- Ur-Namma (ur-iii): (k) "mu gu-ti-umki ba-hul — The land of Guti was destroyed" (sıra bilinmiyor, "unattested"); (f) "The king received kingship from Nippur". CDLI notu: "Only the first year name can be securely assigned…". https://cdli.ox.ac.uk/wiki/doku.php?id=year_names_ur-namma
- Utu-hegal (uruk-utuhegal): Tirigan/Gutium yenilgisi yalnızca yazıtta (RIME 2.13.6.4); CDLI'da yalnız "mu {d}utu-he2-gal2 lugal" yıl adı → ②. https://cdli.ox.ac.uk/wiki/doku.php?id=year_names_utu-hegal
- Puzur-Inšušinak (elam-avan) Akkad/Ur-Namma çağı fetihleri – yıl yok → ② (açılmış birincil kaynak yok, yalnız not).

Harita değişimi sayılmayan ama yıllı bağlam (tabloya alınmadı): Š18 Marhaši'ye kraliyet evliliği (2077), Š30 Anšan valisiyle evlilik (2065), ŠS4 "bad3 mar-tu muriq-tidnim" Amurru suru (2034), İS5 Zabšali valisiyle evlilik (2024), Š19 "EZENxKU3ki bad3ki ki-be2 ba-ab-gi4 — … was restored" (2076; görevde verilen "Der restored" bu kalem olabilir; CDLI çevirisi şehri tanımlamıyor).
