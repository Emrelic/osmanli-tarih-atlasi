# K1: Babylon suzerainty (`v:`) segments, 732 to 626 BC

## Year convention (read first)
- Year `-X` means the Babylonian regnal year that **begins in Nisannu (spring) of X BC** and ends in Addaru of X-1 BC. Livius writes this as "X/X-1". For example, ABC 1's "The second year [of Nabû-nadin-zeri]: 732/731" is stored as -732.
- Each year belongs to exactly one segment. The status is the one for the ruler that the Babylonian sources credit with that regnal year. If a year has more than one ruler or status inside it, it is flagged `KARMA` in `not`.
- Coverage is -732 to -626 **inclusive = 107 years**. "732→626" spans 106 year differences but 107 year cells.
- The Ptolemaic Canon smooths gaps: it gives Tiglath-pileser 731–727, gives Kandalanu 647–626, and treats 704–703 as "Without kings". Where the Canon differs, I followed the chronicles (ABC 1, 2, 14, 15, 16) and Brinkman/Frame in RlA. The `not` field records each Canon difference.
