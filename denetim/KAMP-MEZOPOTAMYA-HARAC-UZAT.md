### Year conventions

**Fixed years:**
- An eponym year is converted to BC with the SAAS 2 list.
- Shalmaneser III regnal year n = 859 − n. This is confirmed by the eponymies he names himself: "eponymy of my own name" is 857, Aššur-bēla-ka''in is 856 and Daiian-Aššur is 853.
- Aššurnaṣirpal II year 1 = 883. The king's eponym year is 882 (SAAS 2), and ORACC Suhu calls the 878 Kudurru campaign his "sixth regnal year".

**Year markers in the tables:**
- **②** means the witness is fixed only to a reign, and the range is given.
- **②\*** means a conventional year exists but is not stated in the text itself.

### Classes

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| TRIBUTE/VASSAL | The tributary or vassal relationship continues. |
| REVOLT | Hostility toward Assyria, or a broken relationship. |
| ANNEXED/PROVINCE | The polity has become Assyrian territory. |
| NONE-FOUND | The polity is mentioned, but the text says nothing about the relationship. |

- First fixed witness: 857. Earlier reign-level witnesses fall under Aššurnaṣirpal II (883–859).
- Last fixed tributary witness: 831 (Sasi installed).
- Hostile episodes:
  - 858: the coalition, just before the relationship starts.
  - 831: a short usurpation, resolved within the same year.
  - 738: revolt followed by annexation.
- Between 831 and 738 there are no witnesses.
- Fixed-year TRIBUTE/VASSAL witnesses: 4 years (857, 853, 848, 831).
