# Source reading: Kiš, Kutha, Sippar, Dilbat, Ešnunna

Method: I downloaded the RlA page images from publikationen.badw.de/de/rla/a/<vol>.<img>.jpg and read them myself. The image number is not the printed page number. Offsets: vol. 2 = +9 (Dilbat) / +13 (Ešnunna); vol. 4 = +30; vol. 5 = +37; vol. 6 = +35; vol. 9 = +27; vol. 12 = +57; vol. 14 = +63. Every quote below gives the PRINTED page.
CDLI: I queried the JSON API (cdli.earth). I report only records whose provenience field has no "?". CDLI is a catalogue. Each record names the signed edition I cite.
Dates are astronomical years (BC n = -(n-1)).

## Kiš 8: -2999 → -0477

(a) Owner sub-slices
- **-0538 → -0477: ahameni**
  - M. Gibson, RlA 5 (1976–80) p. 619, Kiš B §6: "Both parts of Kiš continued into the Achaemenid Period, although Ḫursagkalama was clearly the dominant half. Many economic texts found at Mound W are dated by reference to Achaemenid kings. … Mound W yielded many economic documents dated by reference to the Achaemenid kings."
  - The dated documents behind this are G. J. P. McEwan, OECT 10 (1984), CDLI provenience "Kish (mod. Tell Ingharra)":
    - OECT 10, 122 (P385135): Cyrus 8.IV.10, "Written in: Hursagkalama" = 531 BC = -530
    - OECT 10, 125: Cambyses acc.
    - OECT 10, 134 (P385147): Darius I 2.XI, written in Hursagkalama = 520/19 BC
    - OECT 10, 173 (P385186): Xerxes 7.III.26, written in Hursagkalama = 479 BC = -478. This is inside the window.
    - More Cyrus, Cambyses and Darius I texts: OECT 10, 118–169.
- **Before -0538: KÜNYE GEREKİR (Neo-Babylonian kingdom; earlier Babylon I, Ur III and others)**
  - Edzard, RlA 5 p. 607: "Als Kiš unter der Regierung des Sumu-la-El von Babylon (1880—1845/1816—1781) dem aufstrebenden Staate Babylon einverleibt wurde, hatte es seine Rolle als selbständiger politischer Faktor auf immer eingebüßt."
  - Edzard, RlA 5 p. 613: "Nebukadnezar II. restaurierte das É-kišib-ba des Zababa".

(b) Existence
- Gibson, RlA 5 p. 615: "In the Neo-Bab. and Achaemenid eras, Ḫursagkalama seems to have been the major focus of occupation, with Mound W as the main living area."

(c) Verdict: **YAZILABİLİR** (ahameni, -538→-477). The pre-539 part is KÜNYE GEREKİR (Neo-Babylonian).

Uncertainties
- Edzard (RlA 5 p. 613) says "Von der Achämenidenzeit an sind keine schriftlichen Nachrichten mehr über Kiš überliefert". This statement is outdated. Gibson's article in the same volume (p. 619) and OECT 10 (1984) both contradict it.

## Kiš 9: -0429 → -0325

(a) Owner sub-slices
- **ahameni (weak)**
  - OECT 10, 209 (P385222, Ashm 1930-228i, Kish excavation): "Artaxerxes 34.X.20".
    - If this is Artaxerxes I, year 34 Tebētu falls in about January 430 BC = -0429-01, just inside the window opening. That conversion is my approximate calculation, not Parker–Dubberstein.
    - If it is Artaxerxes II, all of OECT 10, 189–232 (Artaxerxes years 2–34) fall inside the window.
    - CDLI does not number this Artaxerxes. The dossier follows the Xerxes series directly, which suggests Artaxerxes I.
- For makedon (-330-10-18 → -325): no city-named dated owner witness found.

(b) Existence
- Gibson, RlA 4 (1972–75) p. 521, Ḫursagkalama B: "Contemporary with the Neo-Babylonian temple, there was a sizable town at Mound W clearly referred to as Ḫ. This town thrived into and beyond the Achaemenid Period."

(c) Verdict: **YAZILABİLİR (zayıf)** for ahameni, resting on one borderline tablet. Without it, VARLIK.

## Kiš 10: -0315 → -0291

(a) Owner
- **selefki (very weak)**
  - OECT 10, 306 (P385319, Ashm 1929-800, Kish excavation): CDLI date "HE.SE.Seleucus.05+". The year is damaged (≥5), so the range is SE 5–31 = -306…-280. Most of that range falls inside the window.
  - Caveat: Babylonian dating by "Seleucus" regnal years at SE 5–6 is itself unusual.

(b) Existence
- Gibson, RlA 5 p. 619: "There is little or no excavated evidence of the Seleucid era".

(c) Verdict: **BULUNAMADI**, or YAZILABİLİR only if OECT 10, 306 is accepted after checking McEwan's edition.

## Kiš 11: -0271 → -0139

(a) No owner witness found.

(b) Existence
- Gibson, RlA 5 p. 619: "There is little or no excavated evidence of the Seleucid era, but there are Parthian sherds on many of the mounds and an excavated fortress (Tell Bandar; Mound V)."

## Kutha 30: -0454 → -0186

(a) Owner sub-slices
- **ahameni**
  - Jursa, RA 97 (2003):
    - BM 54671: Artaxerxes I 11.VII.20 = 454 BC = -453
    - BM 54136+: Artaxerxes I 14.XI.03
    - BM 54802+ (P522452), pp. 102–104: Artaxerxes I 30.VI.24 = 435 BC = -434
- **selefki**
  - Edzard/Gallery, RlA 6 p. 385: "There is only one tablet from Rassam's excavations (BM 42308+42323 …) which might indicate that Tall Ibrāhīm is K., since it is dated at K. in the reign of Antiochus (Walker, personal communication)."
    - Here "K." means Kutha, and Tall Ibrāhīm is the mound assumed to be Kutha.
    - The text is city-named and gives a king, but it does not say which Antiochus. Antiochus I–III are inside the window; Antiochus IV falls in Kutha 31.
  - BRM 1, 88 (P296448; McEwan FAOS 4, 21–23; van der Spek 1986, 236–241): Kutha, SE 125.IV.11 = about July 187 BC = **-0186**.
    - This falls exactly on the window boundary: "to" is -0186, and Kutha 31 starts at -0185. So it belongs to neither window.
- **makedon:** no witness found.

(b) Existence
- RlA 6 p. 384: "continuously into the Seleucid period".
- J. Oelsner (ZA 61, 1970/71, 166f.), cited in RlA 6 p. 385, attributes CT 49, 131; 155; 187 to Kutha. This is only a suggestion based on content.

(c) Verdict: **YAZILABİLİR** (ahameni firm; selefki via RlA "reign of Antiochus").

(b) Existence

(c) Verdict: **VARLIK.**

## Dilbat 28: -2699 → 0750

(a) Owner sub-slices
- **ahameni**
  - E. Unger, RlA 2 (1938) p. 220: "Aus der neubabylonischen Zeit sind die Kontrakte noch nicht sehr zahlreich, häufiger erst aus der persischen Periode, während sie aus der noch späteren Zeit ganz fehlen, so daß man annehmen möchte, daß in der griechischen und parthischen Epoche die Kenntnis der Keilschrift in D. bereits erloschen gewesen sei."
  - Dated texts, CDLI provenience "Dilbat (mod. Deilam)":

    - VS 3, 153 etc.: Darius I

- **Before -0538: KÜNYE GEREKİR (Neo-Babylonian, Assyria, Babylon I)**
  - RlA 2 p. 220: "da D. sich in den Händen der Assyrer befand, und zwar nach dem Kontrakte des Assurbânipal dd. Dilbat, vom 19. Jahre (= 650)".
- **makedon, selefki, part, sasani, Rashidun, Umayyad:** no witness found.

(b) Existence

(c) Verdict: **YAZILABİLİR** (ahameni sub-slice only). The rest of the window to 750 is BULUNAMADI.

## Overall uncertainties
1. **CDLI attributions.** For BM collections, CDLI provenience is often based on the collection or the text content, not on excavation. The Kish OECT 10 items (Ashmolean 1924–1933 numbers) come from the Oxford–Field Museum Kish excavation and are reliable.
2. **Boundary items.**
   - Kiš 9: OECT 10, 209 (≈ -429-01, if Artaxerxes I).
   - Kutha 30/31: BRM 1, 88 (SE 125.IV = -186) falls in the gap year.
   - Sippar 33/34: AD -330 (Oct 331 BC) falls in the gap.
3. **Kutha identification.** RlA 6 p. 385 says "very likely, though not definitely proven".
4. **Date conversions are mine.** No Parker–Dubberstein check was made on the -429 and -186 items.
5. **Not accessed:** van der Spek 1992, McEwan OECT 10 text volume, Jursa RA 97 text, Stolper Iraq 54 text. Their catalogue data was used through CDLI.
