The Military History of a Defeat: Ottoman Front of the 1877-
1878 Ottoman Russian War

Abstract 
The military narrative of the 1877-78 Ottoman-Russian War is not 
known in Turkey. Due to the emphasis on ep isodic victories, chiefly the 
defence of Plevna, most people simply do not know that the war had been 
fought in two independent fronts and plagued with a series of disasters 
from the beginning till the end. For this reason, this article provides a 
concise summary of the military story of the war putting emphasis on 
strategy, operational and tactical command problems.
