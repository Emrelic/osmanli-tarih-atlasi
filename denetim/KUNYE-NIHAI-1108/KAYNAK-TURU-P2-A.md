## 1. sih-misller (Sih Misl konfederasyonu)

Akademik: Hari Ram Gupta, *History of the Sikhs* II (1978); J.S. Grewal, *The Sikhs of the Punjab* (Cambridge NHI 1990) [BİLGİ, sayfa yok].
