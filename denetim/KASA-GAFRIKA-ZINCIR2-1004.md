## SPRINGBOK ZİNCİRİ — halka halka (kayda yazılacak biçim)
1. **Kasaba ∈ Little Namaqualand (iki kaynaklı, geometrik):**
   - GeoNames 3361142: S 29°39′51″ / E 17°53′11″.
   - EB1911 'Namaqualand': "…extending along the west coast over 600 m. from Damaraland (22° 43′ S.) on the north to 31° S., and stretching inland 80 to 350 m. It is divided by the lower course of the Orange river into two portions—Little Namaqualand to the south and Great Namaqualand to the north."

2. **Little Namaqualand ∈ Cape Colony:** EB1911 aynı madde: "Little Namaqualand forms part of Cape Colony".
