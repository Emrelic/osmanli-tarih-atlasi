Fransız Kanada nehir yolu — Champlain 1615 (Quebec – Sault St-Louis – Ottawa Nehri – Nipissing Gölü – Huron ülkesi)

4 Temmuz 1615 📍 Quebec

Güzergâh: Quebec → Sault Saint-Louis (Montréal) → Nipissing Gölü → Otoueacha (Huron köyü) → Carhagouha. Yolculuğun tamamı haritada seyahat çizgisi olarak gösterilir.

🗺 Haritada aç

Kaynakça

  • Samuel de Champlain, Voyages of Samuel de Champlain, cilt III (Gutenberg #6825, ham metin), 'Voyage made in 1615': 'I left Quebec on the fourth day of July', Riviere des Prairies ve Falls of St. Louis, Ottawa nehri ('Lake of the Algonquins'), 'entered the lake of the Nipissings ... on the twenty-sixth day of the month', Mer Douce (Georgian Bay), 'arrived in the country of the Attigouautan at a village called Otoueacha, on the first day of August', Carhagouha ('On the twelfth day of August')
