Cayatrasimha Nâsırüddin'in ordusunca yenildi (Minhaj)

A.D. 1259 (Majumdar V, Minhaj) 📍 Ranthambore

Cayatrasimha, Hammira-Mahakavya'ya göre Delhi sultanına haraç ödedi; Majumdar onu Minhaj'ın 1259'da Sultan Nâsırüddin'in ordusunca yenildiğini söylediği Ranthambhor kralı olarak tanımlar ('probably').

🗺 Haritada aç

Kaynakça

  • R. C. Majumdar (ed.), The History and Culture of the Indian People, Vol. V: The Struggle for Empire, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Bombay 1957 (archive.org: hcip-5-struggle-for-empire-ed.-r.-c.-majumdar_202512, tam metin okundu), «The Chahamanas of Ranastambhapura»: "he was probably the king of Ran- thambhor who, according to Minhaj, was defeated by the army of Sultan Nasir-ud-din in AD. 1259"
